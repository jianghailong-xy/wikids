  **Why was the Ambassador particularly lucky?（大使为什么特别幸运？）** 带着这个问题听录音、读课文，重点留意全篇是怎样复习 **各种动词形式**——被动语态（*set on fire / was shocked*）、使役结构（*get that fellow posted*）、间接引语（*where he was*）以及结尾那句 **与过去事实相反的虚拟条件句**（*If I had been ...*）。
</Callout>

## The Text 课文

When the Ambassador of Escalopia **returned home for lunch**, his wife **got a shock**. He looked pale and his clothes were **in a frightful state**.

'What has happened?' she asked. 'How did your clothes **get into such a mess**?'

'A fire extinguisher, my dear,' answered the Ambassador **drily**. 'University students **set the Embassy on fire** this morning.'

'**Good heavens**!' exclaimed his wife. 'And where were you at the time?'

'I was in my office as usual,' answered the Ambassador. 'The fire **broke out in the basement**. I went down immediately, of course, and that fool, Horst, **aimed a fire extinguisher at me**. He thought I was on fire. I must definitely **get that fellow posted**.'

The Ambassador's wife **went on asking questions**, when she suddenly noticed a big hole in her husband's hat.

'And how can you explain that?' she asked.

'Oh, that,' said the Ambassador. 'Someone **fired a shot** through my office window. Accurate, don't you think? Fortunately, I wasn't wearing it at the time. **If I had been, I would not have been able to get home for lunch**.'

## 课文注释 Notes on the text

<Callout type="info" title="8 个值得停下来嚼一嚼的点">
  1. **get into such a mess**（ll.4-5）—— “搞得这样糟糕 / 一塌糊涂”。**mess** 指“脏乱、糟糕的状态”；*get into a mess* = 陷入混乱、弄得乱七八糟。同型：*Don't get your room into such a mess. / The whole project got into a terrible mess.*
  2. **set ... on fire**（l.7）—— “放火烧……、点燃……”。这是固定搭配：*set + 宾语 + on fire*。被动则是 *be set on fire*。同型：*The candle set the curtain on fire. / The barn was set on fire by lightning.*
  3. **Good heavens!**（l.8）—— “天哪！”表示惊讶的感叹语，**heaven 用复数 heavens**。同型感叹：*Good heavens! / Heavens above! / For heaven's sake!*
  4. **The fire broke out in the basement.**（l.10）—— “地下室突然着火。” **break out** = （战争、火灾、疾病等坏事）突然爆发、发生，是不及物动词短语，**不能用被动**。同型：*War broke out in 1939. / A riot broke out in the city.*
  5. **aim ... at ...**（l.11）—— “用……瞄准 / 对准……”。*aim a fire extinguisher at me* = 把灭火器对准我。同型：*He aimed the gun at the target. / She aimed the hose at the flames.*
  6. **get that fellow posted**（l.12）—— “把那个家伙派走。”这里的 **post** 作动词，意为“委派、调任”。注意区别：*post a letter / parcel* 是“寄信、寄包裹”；而 *post a person* 则专指把人“派往”政府的某个新职位（多指外派、调离）。**get + 宾语 + 过去分词** 是使役结构 = “让……被……”，详见第 3 节。
  7. **Accurate, don't you think?**（l.16）—— “很准，是不是？”这是省略说法，完整的意思相当于：*Don't you think it was accurate?* 句末的 *don't you think?* 是反意 / 征询附加问句。
  8. **If I had been, I would not have been able to get home for lunch.**（ll.17-18）—— “如果（当时）真戴着它，我就回不了家吃午饭了。”这是 **与过去事实相反的虚拟条件句**（详见第 4 节）。*If I had been* 后面省略了 *wearing my hat*。
</Callout>

## 3. 关键句型 Review of verb forms 动词形式全景复习

<Callout type="tip" title="一分钟回顾">
  本课是教材里 **复习动词形式（Review of verb forms）** 的专项课——把第 60、69 课学过的句型拢到一篇短文里集中操练。一篇不到 200 词的对话里，藏着 5 类高频考点：

  | 考点 | 课文例句 | 要点 |
  |---|---|---|
  | **被动语态** | *University students **set the Embassy on fire**. / His wife **got a shock**（= was shocked）* | 动作的承受者作主语 |
  | **使役结构 get / have sth done** | *I must **get that fellow posted**.* | “让某事被做” = **get / have + 宾语 + 过去分词** |
  | **间接引语（宾语从句语序）** | *his wife wanted to know **where he was***（改写自 Where were you?） | 间接问句用 **陈述语序**，不倒装 |
  | **tried to do / lucky not to do** | *They tried **to burn** it. / He was lucky **not to be** wearing it.* | 动词后接不定式；否定词放 to 前 |
  | **与过去相反的虚拟条件句** | *If I **had been**, I **would not have been** able to get home.* | if 从句 had + 过去分词；主句 would have + 过去分词 |

  ### ⚠️ 4 个本课最容易踩的雷区

  1. **get sth done ≠ do sth**：*get that fellow posted* 是“（找人）把他调走”，强调“让别人去办”，**用过去分词 posted**，不是 *get that fellow to post*。
  2. **break out 无被动**：*The fire broke out*（火突然烧起来）—— break out 是不及物短语，不能说 *was broken out*。但 *set on fire* 是及物，可以被动 *was set on fire*。
  3. **间接引语要“拉直”语序**：直接问句 *Where were you?* 变成间接 *where you were / where he was* —— 主谓不再倒装，也不用问号。
  4. **第三类条件句两边时态要配套**：*had + V-ed*（if 从句）↔ *would have + V-ed*（主句）。少一个 *have* 就成了第二类（与现在相反），逻辑就乱了。
</Callout>

## 4. 难点：第三类条件句 + 间接引语 Special difficulties

<Callout type="info" title="两块硬骨头：与过去相反的假设 + 把对话改成报道">
  本课练习把对话改写成报纸报道（Exercise A），并以结尾那句虚拟句收尾——正好操练两大难点：

  ### A. 与过去事实相反的虚拟条件句（第三类条件句）

  | 部分 | 形式 | 课文例句 |
  |---|---|---|
  | **if 从句** | *if + 主语 + **had** + 过去分词* | *If I **had been** (wearing it) ...* |
  | **主句** | *主语 + would / could / might + **have** + 过去分词* | *I **would not have been** able to get home for lunch.* |

  含义：**假设过去发生了与事实相反的事**，并推测“那样的话会有什么结果”。事实是“我没戴帽子”，假设“假如戴了”，结果“就回不来吃午饭了（其实是没命了）”。

  ⚠️ 三类条件句别混：
  - 真实可能（一般将来）：*If it **rains**, I **will stay** home.*
  - 与现在 / 将来相反：*If I **were** you, I **would go**.*
  - **与过去相反（本课）**：*If I **had been** wearing it, I **would not have got** home.*

  ### B. 直接引语 → 间接引语（把对话写成报道）

  改写时三处要同步变：**人称、时态、语序**。
  - 一般疑问 → *whether / if* 引导：*Were you ...? → asked whether he was ...*
  - 特殊疑问 → 疑问词 + **陈述语序**：*Where were you? → asked where he was*
  - 时态后移（主句过去时）：*has happened → had happened*；*is → was*；*will → would*。

  例：直接 *'Where were you at the time?'* → 间接 *His wife asked where he had been at the time.*
</Callout>

## 11. 作文 Composition (约 150 词)

<Callout type="info" title="任务">
  In about **150 words**, write an imaginary account of **the scene in the basement when the Ambassador went down to investigate**. Use the ideas given below（想像大使下到地下室调查时的场景）：
</Callout>

<Callout type="warning" title="提示骨架（教材给的情节线）">
  - The Ambassador shouted —
  - Horst explained: the Ambassador **was on fire** —
  - the Ambassador denied it —
  - Horst insisted —
  - aimed the fire extinguisher at him —
  - the Ambassador very angry —
  - will send Horst to the South Pole —
  - Horst explained that Escalopia has broken off **diplomatic relations** with the South Pole —
  - the Ambassador went out angrily —
  - Horst was pleased with himself.
</Callout>

<Callout type="tip" title="写作脚手架">
  - **时态主线**：一般过去时叙事（*shouted, aimed, went out*）；插入对话用直接引语；背景用过去进行（*was burning / was pointing*）。
  - **“争辩”动词库**：*shouted, snapped, protested, insisted, retorted, denied, barked*。
  - **“喷射”动词库**：*aimed, sprayed, blasted, let off, doused him in foam, covered him from head to foot*。
  - **结构亮点**：用一句荒诞的逻辑反转收尾——*Horst calmly explained that Escalopia had broken off diplomatic relations with the South Pole, so the threat meant nothing to him.*（把威胁“化解”成笑点）。
</Callout>

<Callout type="tip" title="参考范文（自己写完再看）">
  The Ambassador rushed into the smoke-filled basement and shouted for someone to help put out the flames. Horst, his servant, took one look at him and announced that the Ambassador himself was on fire. The Ambassador angrily denied it, but Horst insisted and aimed the fire extinguisher straight at him, covering him from head to foot in white foam. Spluttering and furious, the Ambassador roared that he would send Horst to the South Pole. Quite unmoved, Horst calmly explained that Escalopia had broken off diplomatic relations with the South Pole years ago, so the threat meant nothing at all. The Ambassador, dripping and speechless, stormed out of the basement. Left alone among the hoses and the foam, Horst dusted off his hands and looked thoroughly pleased with himself. *(126 words)*
</Callout>

## 12. 书信写作 Letter writing (约 80 词)

<Callout type="info" title="任务">
  Write a letter to a friend **inviting him or her to come to a party**. Supply a suitable **Introduction** and **Conclusion**. Use the following ideas to write your **Purpose**（给朋友写信，邀请他/她来参加聚会）：

  ***Purpose:*** the reason for the party — time and place — many old friends will be there.
</Callout>

<Callout type="tip" title="写作脚手架">
  - **称呼**：*Dear + 名字*（写给朋友用名字，亲切自然）。
  - **开头 (Introduction)**：寒暄 + 切入主题 —— *I hope you're keeping well. I'm writing to ask whether you'd like to come to a little party.*
  - **主体 (Purpose)** —— 按 3 个要点串：
    1. **缘由**：*I'm throwing a party to celebrate moving into my new flat.*
    2. **时间地点**：*It's on Saturday the 5th, at eight o'clock, at my place — 12 Park Road.*
    3. **谁会来**：*Lots of our old friends from college will be there.*
  - **结尾 (Conclusion)**：*Do try to come — it wouldn't be the same without you. Let me know if you can make it. / Yours, / [你的名字]*
  - **邀请常用句**：*Would you like to ...? / I'd love it if you could come. / Do come if you can.*
</Callout>

<Callout type="tip" title="参考范文（自己写完再看）">
  Dear Tom,

  I hope you're keeping well. I'm writing to ask whether you'd like to come to a little party. I'm finally celebrating moving into my new flat, and I'd love you to be there. It's on Saturday the 5th, at eight o'clock, at 12 Park Road. Lots of our old friends from college are coming too, so it should be a cheerful evening.

  Do try to come — it wouldn't be the same without you. Let me know if you can make it.

  Yours,

  Anna *(83 words)*
</Callout>

## 13. 拓展练习 Going further

<Callout type="tip" title="自学者建议">
  - **影子跟读 (shadowing)**：用第 1 节的三个 SayIt 按钮跟读 3-5 遍。重点模仿大使那种 *drily*（冷冷的、不动声色）的语气——把惊险的内容用最平淡的腔调说出来，正是本文幽默感的来源。
  - **复述 (retell)**：合上课文，用 2 分钟把故事讲一遍。**叙事三段**：开场（大使狼狈回家 → 夫人吓一跳）→ 经过（火灾 → Horst 误喷灭火器 → 帽子上的洞 → 枪击）→ 收尾（“没戴帽子才幸运”的冷幽默点睛）。
  - **造句迁移**：用今天复习的 5 类动词形式造你自己的句子——
    1. 被动语态：*The old house **was set on fire** by accident.*
    2. 使役 get sth done：*I must **get my watch repaired**.*
    3. 间接引语：*She asked me **where I had been**.*
    4. tried to do / lucky not to do：*He was **lucky not to be** hurt.*
    5. 与过去相反的虚拟句：*If you **had told** me, I **would have helped** you.*
  - **延伸思考**：本课题为 *A fantasy*（纯属虚构）——荒诞的情节（接连遇险却镇定吃午饭）服务于一个语法目的：在一段对话里 **复习并对比多种动词形式**。下次写英文叙事时，问自己四个问题：“主线动作用一般过去时；‘让别人去办’用 get/have sth done；把对话写成报道用间接引语；‘假如过去不是这样’用 had + 过去分词 ↔ would have + 过去分词。”——把这几把“尺子”握在手里，叙事就既准确又有层次。另一个值得回味的点：好的幽默往往藏在 **轻描淡写（understatement）** 里——*I would not have been able to get home for lunch* 表面说“赶不上午饭”，实则说“早没命了”，把生死大事说得云淡风轻，这正是英式冷幽默的精髓。
</Callout>
