## The Text 课文

When a plane from London arrived at Sydney airport, workers began to **unload a number of wooden boxes** which contained clothing. No one could **account for the fact** that one of the boxes was extremely heavy. It suddenly **occurred to** one of the workers to **open up the box**. He **was astonished at what he found**. A man was lying in the box **on top of a pile of** woollen goods. He was **so surprised at being discovered that** he did not even try to run away. After he was arrested, the man **admitted hiding in the box** before the plane left London. He had had a long and uncomfortable trip, for he **had been confined to** the wooden box for over eighteen hours. The man **was ordered to pay** £3,500 for the cost of the trip. The normal price of a ticket is £2,000!

## 课文注释 Notes on the text

<Callout type="info" title="7 个值得停下来嚼一嚼的点">
  1. **a number of** (l.1) — “许多、一些”，后接**可数名词复数**，作主语时谓语用复数。课文 *a number of wooden boxes* = 一批木箱。⚠️ 区分 *the number of ...*（……的数目，谓语用**单数**）：*The number of boxes was large.*
  2. **account for** (l.2) — “说明（原因）；解释”。*No one could account for the fact that ...* = 谁也弄不清……这一事实（是怎么回事）。这里 **that** 引导的从句是 *the fact* 的**同位语从句**，进一步说明 fact 的内容。同型：*How do you account for your absence?*
  3. **It occurred to sb. to do sth.** (l.4) — “某人突然想到做某事”。**occur to** = 想起、想到。句中 **it** 是形式主语，真正的主语是后面的不定式 *to open up the box*。同型：*It never occurred to me to ask his name.*
  4. **be astonished at what he found** (l.5) — “对所见的情景大为吃惊”。**what** 引导**宾语从句**作介词 **at** 的宾语，*what* 相当于 *the thing(s) which*。同型：*She was astonished at what she heard.*
  5. **on top of** (l.6) — “在……上面”。课文 *on top of a pile of woollen goods* = 趴在一堆毛织品上面。**a pile of** = 一堆。
  6. **be surprised at being discovered** (l.6) — “因被发现而吃惊”。**at** 后接动名词；因为“他**被**发现”是被动关系，所以用**动名词的被动式 being discovered**。*so ... that ...* = “如此……以至于……”。
  7. **admit doing sth.** (l.8) — “承认做过某事”。**admit** 后面只接**动名词**作宾语，不接不定式。课文 *admitted hiding in the box* = 承认（曾）躲在箱子里。另注 **confine sb./sth. to ...**（把……关 / 限制在……），课文用被动 *had been confined to the wooden box* = 被关在木箱里。
</Callout>

## 3. 关键句型 Verbs followed by to, at, for and with 与介词连用的动词

<Callout type="tip" title="一分钟回顾">
  本课的语法主线是 **“动词 + 介词”的固定搭配**（教材 Key Structures：与 *to / at / for / with* 连用的动词）。同一个动作，配不同的介词，意思就变——介词不是“随便加的”，而是**词组的一部分**。把课文里的搭配先记牢：

  | 介词 | 课文 / 高频动词 | 例句 |
  |---|---|---|
  | **to** | occur **to**, be confined **to**, listen **to**, reply **to**, object **to**, belong **to**, amount **to**, be accustomed **to** | *It occurred **to** him to open it. / He was confined **to** the box.* |
  | **at** | be astonished **at**, be surprised **at**, look **at**, stare **at**, glance **at**, be shocked **at**, guess **at** | *He was astonished **at** what he found.* |
  | **for** | account **for**, pay **for**, ask **for**, search **for**, wait **for**, blame sb. **for**, thank sb. **for**, apologize **for** | *No one could account **for** it. / ordered to pay £3,500 **for** the trip.* |
  | **with** | agree **with**, comply **with**, cope **with**, quarrel **with**, communicate **with**, compare **with**, part **with** | *I agree **with** you. / comply **with** the rules.* |

  ### ⚠️ 5 个最容易踩的雷区

  1. **occur to sb.**（某人想到）千万别丢 **to**；occur 单独用是“发生”（*The accident occurred at noon.*）。
  2. **pay £3,500 for the trip**：**pay + 钱数**直接接（不加介词），**pay for + 所买之物**才加 for。课文两者都齐了。
  3. **be confined to**（被关 / 局限在……）—— 介词是 **to**，不是 in；课文用被动 *had been confined to*。
  4. **account for**（解释）≠ account（单独几乎不这么用）；*account for* 后接“要解释的事”。
  5. **agree** 三连：*agree **with** sb.*（同意某人）/ *agree **to** sth.*（同意某提议）/ *agree **on** sth.*（就……达成一致）—— 介词不同，含义不同。
</Callout>

## 4. 难点：动名词还是不定式？Gerund vs infinitive

<Callout type="info" title="哪些动词后接 -ing，哪些后接 to do">
  本课接连出现了好几个“非谓语动词”的考点，归纳成一张表，照着记就不会错：

  | 规则 | 课文例句 | 同型动词 / 结构 |
  |---|---|---|
  | **介词后用动名词（-ing）** | *surprised **at being discovered*** | be good at / be afraid of / object to + V-ing |
  | **admit + 动名词** | *admitted **hiding** in the box* | finish / enjoy / mind / avoid / suggest + V-ing |
  | **begin + to do / doing 均可** | *began **to unload*** the boxes | start / continue + to do / doing |
  | **It occurs to sb. + to do** | *occurred to him **to open** the box* | it + 形式主语 + 真主语不定式 |
  | **order / be ordered + to do** | *was ordered **to pay** £3,500* | tell / ask / advise / force sb. to do |
  | **try + to do（设法）** | *did not try **to run** away* | try to do（设法）≠ try doing（试着做做看） |

  ### ⚠️ 记忆口诀

  - **介词后一律 -ing**：at / of / to(介词) / about + **doing**。注意 *object to / be accustomed to* 里的 **to 是介词**，后面要用 -ing！
  - **“承认、完成、避免、建议、介意、喜欢”后接 -ing**（admit, finish, avoid, suggest, mind, enjoy）。
  - **“想要、决定、希望、设法、命令”后接 to do**（want, decide, hope, try, order）。
</Callout>

He found a man lying on top of a pile of woollen goods. The man was arrested. He had travelled in the box from London. He had to pay £3,500, though an ordinary ticket costs only £2,000. *(64 words)*
</Callout>

## 11. 作文 Composition 句子合并（连词改写）

<Callout type="info" title="任务">
  *Rewrite the following sentences using the joining words in parentheses.* 用括号中的连词改写以下句子：

  1. The man had had an uncomfortable trip. He was very hungry. *(not only ... but ... as well)*
  2. He had not eaten anything for thirteen hours. He had not drunk anything for thirteen hours. *(neither ... nor)*
  3. Woollen goods cannot be eaten. At least they are soft. The man had had a few hours' sleep. *(but ... so)*
</Callout>

<Callout type="warning" title="提示骨架">
  - **not only ... but ... as well**：把两件事并列，前句放 *not only*，后句用 *but ... as well* 收尾。注意 not only 置于句首时主谓常需**部分倒装**，但放在句中（如本题）则不倒装。
  - **neither ... nor**：合并两个**否定**句，nor 后接“没吃也没喝”的并列成分；neither A nor B 连接对等结构。
  - **but ... so**：先用 but 转折（不能吃，但很软），再用 so 引出结果（所以他睡了几小时）。
</Callout>

<Callout type="tip" title="参考答案（自己写完再看）">
  1. The man had **not only** had an uncomfortable trip **but** was very hungry **as well**.
  2. He had eaten **neither** food **nor** drink for thirteen hours. *(或：He had had **neither** anything to eat **nor** anything to drink for thirteen hours.)*
  3. Woollen goods cannot be eaten, **but** at least they are soft, **so** the man had had a few hours' sleep.
</Callout>

## 12. 书信写作 Letter writing 信封 / 地址格式

<Callout type="info" title="任务">
  1. Write your **home address** and the **date** in the way they would appear in a letter to a friend who lives abroad.（按书信的要求，在一封寄给国外朋友的信上写上你自己的家庭住址和日期。）
  2. Write the **address of a friend** who lives abroad in the way it would appear in a letter to you.（按书信的要求，写上国外朋友给你的信上对方的地址。）
</Callout>

<Callout type="tip" title="写作脚手架 + 范例">
  英式书信里，**写信人地址**写在信纸**右上角**，由小到大逐行排列，日期写在地址下方：

  ```
  15 Park Road
  Haidian District
  Beijing 100080
  China

  19th June, 2026
  ```

  **收信人地址（信封上）**同样由小到大：门牌 → 街道 → 城 / 区 → 邮编 → 国家。例如写给一位英国朋友：

  ```
  Mr. John Smith
  24 Bridge Street
  Cambridge CB2 1UF
  England
  ```

  - **顺序**：中文是“国—省—市—街—门牌”，英文恰好**相反**（门牌在前，国家在最后）。
  - **日期**：英式 *19th June, 2026*；美式 *June 19, 2026*。寄往国外时国家名不可省略。
</Callout>

## 13. 拓展练习 Going further

<Callout type="tip" title="自学者建议">
  - **影子跟读 (shadowing)**：用第 1 节的三个 SayIt 按钮跟读 3-5 遍。重点感受结尾两句的**反差语气**——*£3,500 ... The normal price of a ticket is £2,000!*，最后那个感叹号要读出“偷鸡不成蚀把米”的讽刺味。
  - **复述 (retell)**：合上课文，用 1 分钟把故事讲一遍。**叙事三段**：起（飞机到港、卸木箱）→ 承转（一箱超重 → 打开 → 发现偷渡客 → 抓获招供）→ 合（罚款 3,500 vs 票价 2,000 的反讽）。
  - **造句迁移**：用今天的 5 个高价值结构造你自己的句子——
    1. *No one could account for ...*（谁也说不清……）—— *No one could account for the strange noise.*
    2. *It occurred to sb. to do sth.*（某人想到做某事）—— *It occurred to me to call her first.*
    3. *be astonished / surprised at ...*（对……吃惊）—— *I was surprised at how quiet the city was.*
    4. *admit doing sth.*（承认做过某事）—— *He admitted breaking the window.*
    5. *be ordered to do sth.*（被责令做某事）—— *The driver was ordered to pay a fine.*
  - **延伸思考**：本课是“**动词 + 介词搭配**”的集中训练场——*account for / occur to / be surprised at / be confined to / pay for*。这些搭配没有“为什么”，**靠的是成组记忆**：把动词和它的“专属介词”当成一个整体背下来，写作时就不会再纠结该用 to 还是 at。另一个值得回味的点：好故事往往把**最有冲击力的信息留到最后一句**——本文直到结尾才用 £3,500 vs £2,000 的数字对比，把“偷渡省钱”彻底反转成“既昂贵又受罪”，标题的双关也在此刻才完全落地。
</Callout>
