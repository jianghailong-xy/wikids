## The Text 课文

My friend, Hugh, has always been fat, but things **got so bad** recently that he decided to **go on a diet**. He began his diet a week ago. **First of all**, he wrote out a long list of all the foods **which were forbidden**. The list included most of the things Hugh loves: butter, potatoes, rice, beer, milk, chocolate, and sweets. Yesterday I **paid him a visit**. I rang the bell and was not surprised to see that Hugh was still **as fat as ever**. He led me into his room and **hurriedly hid** a large parcel under his desk. **It was obvious that** he was very embarrassed. When I asked him what he was doing, he smiled **guiltily** and then put the parcel on the desk. He explained that his diet was **so strict that** he had to **reward himself occasionally**. Then he showed me the contents of the parcel. It contained five large bars of chocolate and three bags of sweets!

> The list included the things Hugh loves（清单上列的，正是 Hugh 最爱吃的东西）。

## 课文注释 Notes on the text

<Callout type="info" title="7 个值得停下来嚼一嚼的点">
  1. **Reward for virtue**（标题）—— virtue 作"美德"讲，此处指 Hugh"节食自律"这件事。标题是一句反讽：所谓对"美德"的奖赏，竟是大吃巧克力糖果。
  2. **but things got so bad ...** (l.1) —— "但情况变得如此糟糕"。**get** 在这里是系动词，作"变得、变成"讲（= become），后接形容词 bad。同型：*It is getting dark. / He got angry.*
  3. **go on a diet** (l.2) —— 实行节食、开始节食（= be on a diet）。**go on a diet** 偏"开始节食"这个动作，**be on a diet** 偏"正在节食"这个状态。
  4. **First of all** (l.3) —— "首先"。用来引出一系列动作的第一步，比单说 first 更郑重。
  5. **I paid him a visit.** (l.5) —— "我去拜访他。" **pay sb. a visit** = pay a visit to sb. = visit sb.（去看望某人）。
  6. **as fat as ever** (l.6) —— "像以往一样胖"。这里的 **as ever** 是状语从句 *as he ever was* 的省略形式，意思是"和他一向的样子一样（胖）"。同型：*as busy as ever / as cheerful as ever*。
  7. **It was obvious that he was very embarrassed.** (l.8) —— "显然他很尴尬。" **it** 是形式主语，真正的主语是后面 *that* 引导的主语从句。同型：*It is clear that he is right. / It was lucky that nobody got hurt.*
</Callout>

## 3. 关键句型 What happened? 一般过去时（KS27）

<Callout type="tip" title="一分钟回顾：用一般过去时讲一件已经发生的事">
  本课对应教材关键句型 **KS27 — What happened?（一般过去时）**。叙述"过去某个时间已经发生、并且已经结束"的动作，用 **一般过去时**：动词用过去式（规则动词加 -ed，不规则动词要背）。把课文里"昨天那场拜访"的动词圈出来，就是一条清晰的一般过去时主线：

  | 动词原形 | 课文过去式 | 句中位置 |
  |---|---|---|
  | decide | **decided** | he decided to go on a diet (l.2) |
  | begin | **began** | He began his diet a week ago (l.2) |
  | write | **wrote** (out) | he wrote out a long list (l.3) |
  | pay | **paid** | Yesterday I paid him a visit (l.5) |
  | ring | **rang** | I rang the bell (l.5) |
  | lead | **led** | He led me into his room (l.6) |
  | hide | **hid** | hurriedly hid a large parcel (l.7) |
  | put | **put** | put the parcel on the desk (l.9) |
  | show | **showed** | he showed me the contents (l.10) |

  ### ⚠️ 4 个本课最容易踩的雷区

  1. **a week ago / yesterday + 一般过去时**：时间状语 *ago / yesterday* 是过去时的"信号灯"，必须配过去式（*began / paid*），**绝不能用现在完成时**（不能说 *has begun ... a week ago*）。
  2. **疑问句借助 did + 动词原形**：*How long ago **did** he **begin** his diet?*——一旦用了助动词 did，后面的实义动词要还原成原形 begin，不再变过去式。
  3. **so + 形容词 + that 结果状语从句**：*things got **so** bad **that** he decided ... / his diet was **so** strict **that** he had to ...*——"如此……以致……"。注意是 *so + adj*，不是 *such + adj*。
  4. **be 动词的过去式**：人或物的"状态"用 was/were——*Hugh **was** still as fat as ever / It **was** obvious that ...*。
</Callout>

## 4. 难点：易混动词 Special difficulties — verbs often confused

<Callout type="info" title="三对最容易混的动词：raise/rise · lay/lie · beat/win">
  本课难点是几对"长得像、用法却相反"的动词。记住一条总纲：**带宾语的是及物动词（vt.），不带宾语的是不及物动词（vi.）。**

  | 及物 vt.（要带宾语） | 不及物 vi.（不带宾语） |
  |---|---|
  | **raise** raised raised 举起、抬高、提高（某物） | **rise** rose risen 升起、起立、上升（自己） |
  | **lay** laid laid 放置、摆放（某物）；lay the table 摆桌子 | **lie** lay lain 躺、平卧、处于（某状态） |
  | **beat** beat beaten 打败（某人 / 某队） | **win** won won 赢得（比赛 / 奖品） |

  - **raise / rise**：*That boy always **raises** his hand.*（举手，带宾语 hand）/ *I always **rise** at six o'clock.*（起床，无宾语）。
  - **lay / lie**：*Lay those parcels on the floor.*（把包放下，带宾语 parcels）/ *It's nicer to **lie** in bed.*（躺着，无宾语）。⚠️ 最坑的是：*lie* 的过去式偏偏是 **lay**——*I **lay** in bed till 10 o'clock*。
  - **beat / win**：宾语是"对手"用 **beat**（*Arsenal **beat** Manchester United*）；宾语是"比赛、奖品"用 **win**（*Arsenal **won** the game*）。
</Callout>

## 10. 摘要写作 Summary writing

<Callout type="info" title="任务（Answer these questions in not more than 65 words）">
  回答下列问题，将答案组成**一个段落，不超过 65 个单词**（括号里是建议用上的连接词）：

  1. Is Hugh fat or not? Has he gone on a diet or not? *(so ... that)*
  2. Has he forbidden himself all the foods he likes, or all the foods he does not like? Has he lost weight or not? *(but)*
  3. What did he hide under his desk when the writer visited him yesterday?
  4. Did the parcel contain chocolate and sweets, or did it contain biscuits?
  5. Why did Hugh say that he had to reward himself occasionally? *(because)*
</Callout>

<Callout type="tip" title="参考范文（自己写完再看）">
  Hugh is so fat that he has gone on a diet. He has forbidden himself all the foods he likes, but he has not lost any weight. When the writer visited him yesterday, Hugh hid a large parcel under his desk. It contained chocolate and sweets, not biscuits. Hugh said he had to reward himself occasionally because his diet was so strict. *(62 words)*
</Callout>

## 11. 作文 Composition

<Callout type="info" title="任务（Write two or three sentences using the ideas given below）">
  用下面给出的词组，写出 **2 至 3 个句子**：

  *I invited a friend to dinner — expensive restaurant — good meal — asked for the bill — not enough money — borrowed some from my guest.*
</Callout>

<Callout type="warning" title="提示骨架">
  - 用**一般过去时**串起整件事：invited → had → asked for → found → had to borrow。
  - 注意 *ask for the bill*（要账单）、*not enough money*（钱不够）、*borrow ... from sb.*（向某人借）几个关键搭配。
  - 结尾那一笔（向客人借钱）最好笑，可用感叹号收尾。
</Callout>

<Callout type="tip" title="参考范文（自己写完再看）">

</Callout>

## 12. 书信写作 Letter writing — how to begin a letter

<Callout type="info" title="规则：写给不太熟的人，称呼怎么写">
  给一个**不太熟悉**的人写信，开头（称呼）应这样写：

  - **Dear Mr. Brown,**
  - **Dear Miss Williams,**
  - **Dear Mrs. Smith,**

  ⚠️ 要点：① 用 *Dear + 头衔（Mr./Mrs./Miss）+ 姓氏*；② **姓名之后一定要加一个逗号**。
</Callout>

<Callout type="tip" title="练习 Exercise + 参考答案">
  **How would you begin a letter to: your sister, your friend Bill, your employer, your old headmaster?**（给下列人写信该怎样称呼？）

  - **your sister（你姐姐/妹妹）** → *Dear Mary,*（关系亲密，直呼其名即可）
  - **your friend Bill（你的朋友比尔）** → *Dear Bill,*（朋友用名字）
  - **your employer（你的老板）** → *Dear Mr. Brown,*（不太熟/正式，用 Mr. + 姓）
  - **your old headmaster（你的老校长）** → *Dear Mr. Smith,*（尊敬而正式，用 Mr. + 姓；也可 Dear Dr. Jones 等）

  小结：**关系越亲近，称呼越随意（直呼名字）；关系越正式，越要用"头衔 + 姓氏"**——但无论哪种，姓名后的逗号都不能少。
</Callout>

## 13. 拓展练习 Going further

<Callout type="tip" title="自学者建议">
  - **影子跟读 (shadowing)**：用第 1 节的三个 SayIt 按钮反复跟读，重点找 *so ... that* 的语流停顿，以及 *as fat as ever* 的轻读节奏。
  - **复述 (retell)**：合上课文，用一般过去时把这个小故事讲一遍。**叙事三段**：起因（Hugh 太胖 → 决定节食 → 开列禁吃清单）→ 经过（我登门 → 他还是那么胖 → 慌忙藏大包 → 我追问）→ 结局（揭开大包 → 巧克力 + 糖果 → "节食太严，得奖赏自己"）。
  - **造句迁移**：用今天的 5 个高价值结构造你自己的句子——
    1. *go on a diet*（开始节食）—— *After the holidays, I decided to go on a diet.*
    2. *so + adj + that ...*（如此……以致……）—— *The film was so boring that I fell asleep.*
    3. *as + adj + as ever*（像以往一样……）—— *After ten years, the old town looked as beautiful as ever.*
    4. *It was obvious that ...*（显然……）—— *It was obvious that she had been crying.*
    5. *reward oneself (with sth.)*（奖赏自己）—— *I rewarded myself with a cup of coffee after finishing the report.*
  - **延伸思考**：本课的笑点全在标题 *Reward for virtue* 的反讽上——"自律"的奖赏，竟是大吃特吃违禁的巧克力。它提醒我们：**英语幽默常常藏在标题与结尾的反差里**。语法上，本课是一次干净的"一般过去时阅兵"：一串不规则动词（began / wrote / paid / rang / led / hid / put / showed）把"昨天那场拜访"讲得一气呵成——把它们背熟，你就握住了英语叙事最常用的那条主线。
</Callout>
