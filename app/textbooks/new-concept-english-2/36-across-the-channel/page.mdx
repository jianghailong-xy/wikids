## The Text 课文

Debbie Hart **is going to swim** across the English Channel tomorrow. She **is going to set out** from the French coast at five o'clock in the morning. Debbie is only eleven years old and she **hopes to set up** a new world record. She is a strong swimmer and many people feel **that she is sure to succeed**. Debbie's father **will set out** with her in a small boat. Mr. Hart **has trained** his daughter for years. Tomorrow he **will be watching** her anxiously as she swims the long distance to England. Debbie **intends to take** short rests every two hours. She will have something to drink but she **will not eat any solid food**. Most of Debbie's school friends will be waiting for her on the English coast. **Among them will be Debbie's mother**, who swam the Channel herself when she was a girl.

## 课文注释 Notes on the text

<Callout type="info" title="5 个值得停下来嚼一嚼的点">
  1. **set out** (l.2) — \"出发；动身\"。强调\"开始一段旅程\"。课文 *is going to set out from the French coast* = 准备从法国海岸出发。同型：*We set out at dawn. / They set out on a long journey.* 近义还有 *set off / start out*。
  2. **set up** (l.3) — \"创立；建立\"。课文 *set up a new world record* = 创造一项新的世界纪录。注意和 set out 区分：**out 是\"出发\"，up 是\"立起 / 创建\"**。同型：*set up a company / set up a tent / set up a record*。
  3. **many people feel that she is sure to succeed** (l.4-5) — \"许多人都认为她肯定能成功\"。**be sure to do sth.** = \"必定会做……\"（说话人对结果有把握），不要和 *be sure of sth.*（对某事有把握）混淆。同型：*He is sure to win. / It is sure to rain.*
  4. **every two hours** (l.8) — \"每两小时\"。**every + 基数词 + 复数名词** 表示\"每隔……\"。同型：*every three days（每三天）/ every ten minutes（每十分钟）*。也可说 *every second hour*（每隔一小时）。
  5. **Among them will be Debbie's mother** (l.10-11) — \"他们当中还会有黛比的母亲\"。这是一个**完全倒装句**：地点状语 *Among them* 提到句首，谓语 *will be* 整体移到主语 *Debbie's mother* 前面，为的是与后面那个较长的定语从句 *who swam the Channel ...* 在结构上保持平衡。正常语序是 *Debbie's mother will be among them.* 同型：*On the hill stands a temple. / Here comes the bus.*
</Callout>

## 3. 关键句型 谈论未来的三件法宝：will / be going to / will be doing

<Callout type="tip" title="一分钟回顾">
  本课的关键句型 (KS12) 与练习 A (KS13) 把英语里**表示将来的三种主要方式**集中铺开了。一篇课文里三种形式同台演出：

  | 形式 | 课文例句 | 用途 |
  |---|---|---|
  | **be going to + V** | *Debbie **is going to swim** ... / **is going to set out** ...* | 事先有计划、有打算的将来 |
  | **will + V**（一般将来） | *Her father **will set out** ... / She **will have** ... / **will not eat** ...* | 单纯陈述将来、临时决定、预测 |
  | **will be + V-ing**（将来进行） | *Tomorrow he **will be watching** her ...* | 将来某时\"正在进行\"的动作 |

  ### going to 与 will 怎么换？

  教材关键句型给了一组对照——很多时候 **going to 可以替换 shall / will**：

  | Instead of saying | We can say |
  |---|---|
  | I shall travel by air. | I **am going to** travel by air. |
  | He will sell his car. | He **is going to** sell his car. |
  | They will move to a new house. | They **are going to** move to a new house. |
  | I intend to write to him. | I **am going to** write to him. |
  | She means to ask for an explanation. | She **is going to** ask for an explanation. |

  ### ⚠️ 4 个本课最容易踩的雷区

  1. **be going to + 动词原形**：是 *going to swim*，**不是** *going to swimming*。going to 后面永远跟原形。
  2. **将来进行时 = will be + V-ing**：别丢了 be，也别丢了 -ing。*will be watching* ✓ / *will watching* ✗ / *will be watch* ✗。
  3. **不能都换成 going to**：表示\"条件 + 结果\"的预测句要用 will——*You **will** enjoy yourself if you travel by sea.*（不能说 are going to enjoy）。
  4. **完全倒装**：地点状语 *Among them* 提前 → 谓语 *will be* 提到主语前 → *Among them **will be** Debbie's mother.*（主语后面拖着长长的定语从句，倒装读起来更顺）。
</Callout>

<Callout type="tip" title="句子结构小练（教材 Sentence structure）">

  *from the French coast / she / in the morning / is going to / at five o'clock / set out*

  **参考答案**：She is going to set out from the French coast at five o'clock in the morning.（语序：主语 + be going to + 动词 + 地点 + 时间点 + 时间段）
</Callout>

## 4. 难点：经常容易混淆的词 Special difficulties

<Callout type="info" title="两组最爱混的词">
  教材 *Special Difficulties* 挑出了两组近义词，记住它们各自的\"专属语境\"：

  ### a. Watch / Look at / Follow

  | 词 | 含义 | 例句 |
  |---|---|---|
  | **watch** | 观看\"正在发生 / 会动\"的事物 | *Tomorrow he **will be watching** her anxiously.*（l.6-7） |
  | **look at** | 看\"静止\"的对象 | *Look at the blackboard. / Look at this picture.* |
  | **follow** | 跟随、走在后面 | *I **followed** my mother into the kitchen.* |

  ### b. Solid / Firm / Stable

  | 词 | 含义 | 例句 |
  |---|---|---|
  | **solid** | 固体的（非流体的） | *She will not eat any **solid** food.*（l.9） |
  | **firm** | 稳固的（不松动）/ 坚决的（不动摇）/ 严格的 | *It is **firm** now. / a **firm** refusal / be very **firm** with that child* |
  | **stable** | 坚定的（常形容性格） | *He is a very **stable** person.* |

  > 一句话区分：**watch 看动的、look at 看静的、follow 跟在后**；**solid 是状态（固体）、firm 是态度 / 牢固、stable 是性格 / 平稳**。
</Callout>

  The man on the raft saw the boat (so) (but) he tried to send a signal. He (took off) (put out) his shirt (or) (and) (waved) (shook) it (but) (so) the men on the boat (neither) (either) saw (or) (nor) (heard) (listened to) him.
</Callout>

<Callout type="warning" title="选词思路">
  - **saw the boat ___ he tried to send a signal**：看见船是\"信号\"的**原因 / 结果** → 用 **so**（于是）。
  - **took off / put out his shirt**：脱下衬衫示意 → **took off**（脱下）。
  - **waved / shook it**：挥动衬衫当信号 → **waved**（挥动）。
  - **___ the men ... saw ... him**：转折——他发了信号，可对方没看见 → **but**。
  - **neither saw ___ heard him**：两个否定并列 → **neither ... nor ...**（既没看见也没听见）。
</Callout>

<Callout type="tip" title="参考范文（自己写完再看）">
  The man on the raft saw the boat, so he tried to send a signal. He took off his shirt and waved it, but the men on the boat neither saw nor heard him.
</Callout>

## 12. 书信写作 Letter writing (地址缩写)

<Callout type="info" title="规则">

  - **N.W.3** = North West 3

## 13. 拓展练习 Going further

<Callout type="tip" title="自学者建议">
  - **影子跟读 (shadowing)**：用第 1 节的三个 SayIt 按钮跟读 3-5 遍。重点体会 *is going to swim* 里 *going to* 在口语中常弱读成 \"gonna\"，以及 *will be watching* 的连读节奏。
  - **复述 (retell)**：合上课文，用 1 分钟把 Debbie 的横渡计划讲一遍。**三段**：人物（11 岁的游泳能手）→ 计划（明早 5 点从法国出发、每两小时休息、只喝不吃）→ 守望（父亲随船、同学与母亲在英国岸边）。
  - **造句迁移**：用今天的 5 个高价值结构造你自己的句子——
    1. *S + be going to + V*（有计划的将来）—— *I am going to visit my grandparents this weekend.*
    2. *S + will be + V-ing*（将来某时正在进行）—— *This time tomorrow I will be flying to Tokyo.*
    3. *intend to + V*（打算做）—— *We intend to leave early in the morning.*
    4. *every + 数词 + 复数名词*（每隔……）—— *The bus comes every ten minutes.*
    5. *Among them + will be + 主语*（完全倒装）—— *Among the guests will be the mayor himself.*
  - **延伸思考**：本课是一张\"**未来时态全景图**\"——同样是说\"明天的事\"，**be going to**（事先有计划）、**will**（单纯陈述 / 预测）、**will be doing**（强调那一刻正在进行）各有分工。下次写英文计划时，先问自己：这件事是\"早就定好的安排\"，还是\"临时的决定 / 纯预测\"，还是\"将来某刻持续进行的画面\"？三把尺子在手，你笔下的\"未来\"就会立刻清晰起来。另一处值得回味的是结尾那句**完全倒装**——把 *Among them* 提前，既呼应上文\"岸边等候的人群\"，又给\"也曾横渡海峡的母亲\"留出最郑重的压轴位置。
</Callout>
