## The Text 课文

During a bullfight, a drunk suddenly **wandered into** the middle of the ring. The crowd began to shout, but the drunk **was unaware of** the danger. The bull was busy with the matador at the time, but it suddenly **caught sight of** the drunk who was shouting rude remarks and waving a red cap. **Apparently sensitive to criticism**, the bull forgot all about the matador and **charged at** the drunk. The crowd suddenly **grew quiet**. The drunk, however, seemed quite **sure of himself**. When the bull got close to him, he clumsily **stepped aside** to **let it pass**. The crowd **broke into cheers** and the drunk bowed. By this time, however, three men **had come into** the ring and they quickly dragged the drunk to safety. Even the bull seemed to feel sorry for him, for it **looked on** sympathetically until the drunk was **out of the way** before once more turning its attention to the matador.

## 课文注释 Notes on the text

<Callout type="info" title="7 个值得停下来嚼一嚼的点">
  1. **be unaware of ...** (l.2) — 不知道，没有觉察……。**unaware** = not aware，后接 of + 名词 / 动名词。课文 *the drunk was unaware of the danger* = 醉汉没意识到危险。同型：*She was unaware of his presence. / He seemed unaware of what was happening.*
  2. **catch sight of ...** (l.4) — 突然看见……，瞥见……。固定搭配，sight 前不加冠词。课文 *it suddenly caught sight of the drunk*。同型：*I caught sight of her in the crowd.* 反义 *lose sight of*（看不见 / 忽略）。
  3. **Apparently sensitive to criticism, the bull ...** (ll.5-6) — 这句可以看作是省略了 **being** 的现在分词短语，作状语，说明原因，等于 *Because it was apparently sensitive to criticism, the bull ...*。**be sensitive to ...** = 对……敏感。同型：*Knowing the truth, she said nothing.（= Because she knew ...）*
  4. **charge at ...** (l.6) — 向……（猛冲）攻击。介词用 **at** 表示“冲着……去”这一攻击方向。同型：*The dog charged at the stranger. / He charged at the door and broke it open.*
  5. **break into cheers** (l.9) — 突然欢呼起来。**break into ...** 作“突然发出”“突然……起来”讲，后接表声音 / 动作的名词。同型：*break into laughter（突然大笑）/ break into tears（突然大哭）/ break into a run（撒腿就跑）/ break into song（突然唱起来）*。
  6. **look on** (l.11) — 旁观，作壁上观。这里 on 是副词，look on = 在一旁看着（不参与）。课文 *it looked on sympathetically* = 它（公牛）同情地在一旁看着。同型：*The crowd looked on as the firemen worked.*
  7. **out of the way** (l.11) — 不碍事；不挡路。课文 *until the drunk was out of the way* = 直到醉汉离场、不再碍事为止。**step aside / get out of the way** 都表示“让开”。反义 *in the way*（挡道、碍事）。
</Callout>

## 3. 关键句型 charge at · let + 动词原形 · by this time + 过去完成

<Callout type="tip" title="一分钟回顾">
  本课的 Structure 部分围绕“**几个看似简单、却最容易选错的小结构**”展开。把课文相关的句子圈出来，主线很清楚：

  | 结构 | 课文例句 | 要点 |
  |---|---|---|
  | **charge at / run at（冲向攻击）** | *the bull ... **charged at** the drunk* / *It ran straight **at** him.* | 表“冲着……攻击”，介词固定用 **at**（不用 to / against / for） |
  | **let + 宾语 + 动词原形** | *he ... stepped aside to **let it pass*** | let（make / see / hear）后接**不带 to 的动词原形** |
  | **过去时的“将来”用 would** | *The drunk seemed quite sure of himself.*（→ *He was sure he **would** be all right.*） | 站在“过去某刻”看将来，用 would（不用 will） |
  | **by this time / by then + 过去完成** | *By this time ..., three men **had come** into the ring.* | “到那时为止已经……”，主句用 *had + 过去分词* |
  | **before + 从句 / 动名词** | *before once more **turning** its attention to the matador* | before 后可接动名词（turning）或从句（before it turned） |
  | **现在分词短语作原因状语** | ***Apparently sensitive to criticism**, the bull ...*（省略 being） | = Because it was sensitive to criticism, ... |

  ### ⚠️ 4 个本课最容易踩的雷区

  1. **冲向攻击只能 charge / run + at**：*charge at him* ✓ / *run straight at him* ✓。写成 to / against / for 都不对——at 才有“瞄准目标冲过去”的攻击感。
  2. **let / make 后面接动词原形**：*let it pass* ✓（不是 to pass / passing）；同类还有 *make him laugh, see her come, hear them sing*。
  3. **主句过去 → 从句“将来”用 would**：*He was sure he **would** be all right.* 主句 was 是过去，从句里的“将来”要相应变成 would（不能用 will）。
  4. **by this time / by then 是过去完成的“信号词”**：表示“到那个过去时间点为止，事情早已发生”，主句用 *had + 过去分词*（had come），不能用一般过去 came。
</Callout>

## 4. 难点：与介词搭配的形容词 Adjectives + prepositions

<Callout type="info" title="与 for / with / of / to / at / from / in / on / about 连用的形容词（对比第 22、46 课）">
  本课的 *Key Structures* 是一张**“形容词 + 固定介词”速查表**——很多形容词后面跟哪个介词是**约定俗成、必须记牢**的。把它们按介词分组，记起来事半功倍：

  | 介词 | 常见形容词（课文 / 练习用到的） | 例 |
  |---|---|---|
  | **of** | afraid, aware, capable, careful, certain, conscious, fond, guilty, ignorant, jealous, sure, ahead | *unaware **of** the danger* / *jealous **of** his sister* / *guilty **of** a crime* |
  | **to** | close, cruel, equal, faithful, indifferent, obedient, polite, rude, sensitive, similar, useful, contrary | *sensitive **to** criticism* / *cruel **to** his dog* / *contrary **to** my expectations* |
  | **for** | eager, famous, fit, grateful, ready, responsible, sorry, sufficient, valid, qualified | *responsible **for** this mistake* / *grateful **for** your advice* / *valid **for** all countries* |
  | **with** | angry (with sb.), busy, consistent, content, familiar, patient, popular | *familiar **with** his novels* / *patient **with** him* / *consistent **with** the facts* |
  | **at** | bad, clever, efficient, good, indignant, quick, slow, skillful | *good **at** arithmetic* / *quick **at** understanding* / *efficient **at** her work* |
  | **from** | away, different, far, safe | *different **from** anyone else* |
  | **in** | deficient, fortunate, honest, weak, qualified | *fortunate **in** ...* / *qualified **in** languages* |
  | **on** | dependent, intent, keen | *keen **on** reading* / *dependent **on** their parents* / *intent **on** passing* |
  | **about** | curious, doubtful, enthusiastic, reluctant, uneasy | *uneasy **about** the results* / *doubtful **about** his chances* |

  ### ⚠️ 最容易混的几对

  1. **angry with sb.**（生某人的气）／ **angry at sth.**（对某事生气）——人用 with，事用 at。
  2. **afraid of / fond of / jealous of / capable of**——这几个高频形容词后面**永远是 of**。
  3. **sorry for what he did**（对所做的事感到抱歉）／ **angry with him**（生他的气）——同一句里可能两个介词，别张冠李戴。
  4. **good / bad / quick / slow + at**——“擅长 / 不擅长 / 反应快慢”一律用 at。
</Callout>

## 10. 摘要写作 Summary writing (≤ 75 词)

<Callout type="info" title="任务">
  *Answer these questions in not more than 75 words.* 回答下列问题，将答案组成一个段落，不要超过 75 个单词。

  1. Did a drunk suddenly wander into the middle of the ring during a bullfight or not? What did he do? *(and)*
  2. Did the bull ignore the matador or not? What did it do? Why did he step aside? *(Ignoring ... but)*
  3. Did the crowd cheer or not? What did the drunk do? *(and)*
  4. Did three men drag the drunk to safety just after this or not? Did the bull look on sympathetically or not? Did it once more turn its attention to the matador or not? *(Just after this ... while ... before it ...)*
</Callout>

<Callout type="tip" title="参考范文（自己写完再看）">
  During a bullfight, a drunk suddenly wandered into the middle of the ring and shouted rude remarks at the bull. Ignoring the matador, the bull charged at him, but he calmly stepped aside to let it pass. The crowd broke into cheers and the drunk bowed. Just after this, three men dragged him to safety, while the bull looked on sympathetically before it once more turned its attention to the matador. *(70 words)*
</Callout>

## 11. 作文 Composition

<Callout type="info" title="任务">
  *Rewrite the following sentences using the joining words in parentheses.* 用括号中的连词改写以下句子：

  1. The man became sober. It was possible to discover his identity. *(When)*
  2. He was Domingo Cordova. He had been a great matador in his youth. He had failed in the ring. He had taken to drinking. *(... a man who ... but having ...)*
  3. He had changed. No one recognized him. *(so much that)*
</Callout>

<Callout type="warning" title="提示骨架">
  - 第 1 句：用 **When** 把“变清醒”作时间状语放句首 —— *When the man ..., it was possible to ...*
  - 第 2 句：把同位语 **a man who ...** 嵌进主句，再用 **but having + 过去分词** 把“失败 → 酗酒”串成分词状语 —— *He was Domingo Cordova, a man who ... but having ..., had taken to ...*
  - 第 3 句：用 **so ... that ...**（如此……以至于……）连接“变化之大”和“没人认出他” —— *He had changed so much that ...*
</Callout>

<Callout type="tip" title="参考答案（自己写完再看）">
  1. **When** the man became sober, it was possible to discover his identity.
  2. He was Domingo Cordova, **a man who** had been a great matador in his youth **but having** failed in the ring, had taken to drinking.
  3. He had changed **so much that** no one recognized him.

  （原来这位“醉汉”竟是昔日名震一时的斗牛士 Domingo Cordova——失意之后借酒消愁，连身手都还在。难怪他能在牛角下从容一闪！）
</Callout>

## 12. 书信写作 Letter writing

<Callout type="info" title="任务">
  *Learn the following opening phrases by heart.* 熟记以下 3 个开头语：

  - **I have not forgotten that ...**（我没有忘记……）
  - **You will find it hard to believe, but ...**（你也许难以相信，但是……）
  - **I am afraid I ...**（恐怕我……）

  *Exercise:* Write sentences completing each of the above phrases. 用以上 3 个开头语写出完整的句子。
</Callout>

<Callout type="tip" title="写作脚手架">
  - **I have not forgotten that ...** 后接**宾语从句**，常用来唤起一段共同的回忆 —— *I have not forgotten that you helped me when I first arrived.*
  - **You will find it hard to believe, but ...** 用来抛出一件出人意料的事 —— 后半句直接陈述那个“惊人事实”。注意 but 之后是完整的句子。
  - **I am afraid I ...** 是**委婉道歉 / 表遗憾**的口吻 —— *I am afraid I won't be able to come to your party.* afraid 后可直接接从句（省略 that）。
  - 写完检查：从句语序正常（不用疑问倒装）、时态前后一致、句子完整有主谓。
</Callout>

<Callout type="tip" title="参考范文（自己写完再看）">
  1. I have not forgotten that you lent me your umbrella on that rainy afternoon, and I still owe you my thanks.
  2. You will find it hard to believe, but the quiet old man next door was once a famous matador.
  3. I am afraid I shall not be able to join you on Saturday, as I have to work late at the office.
</Callout>

## 13. 拓展练习 Going further

<Callout type="tip" title="自学者建议">
  - **影子跟读 (shadowing)**：用第 1 节的三个 SayIt 按钮跟读 3-5 遍。尤其留意那句 *Apparently sensitive to criticism, the bull ... charged at the drunk* —— 感受分词状语“先交代原因、再讲动作”的节奏，以及 *charge at* 里 at 的重音。
  - **复述 (retell)**：合上课文，用 1 分钟把这场闹剧讲一遍——**起因**（斗牛中醉汉闯入、喊话挥帽）→ **经过**（公牛被激怒撇下斗牛士冲来 → 全场静默 → 醉汉镇定一闪让牛扑空 → 观众欢呼、醉汉鞠躬）→ **收尾**（三人把他拖到安全地带；连公牛都“同情”地目送他离场）。
  - **造句迁移**：用今天的 5 个高价值结构造你自己的句子——
    1. *be unaware of ...*（没意识到）—— *He was unaware of the mistake until it was too late.*
    2. *charge at ...*（冲向攻击）—— *The dog suddenly charged at the postman.*
    3. *let + 宾语 + 动词原形*（让某物 / 某人做）—— *Open the window and let some fresh air in.*
    4. *By this time / by then + 过去完成*（到那时已经……）—— *By the time we arrived, the train had already left.*
    5. *形容词 + 固定介词*（如 sensitive to / fond of / good at）—— *She is very sensitive to other people's feelings.*
  - **延伸思考**：本课只有一百多词，却写得活灵活现——秘诀在于**“拟人 + 悬念 + 反转”**：作者把公牛写成一个“对批评敏感、会同情人”的角色（拟人），用“全场静默”制造紧张（悬念），再用醉汉从容一闪、观众欢呼来翻转预期（反转）。再配合作文里揭示的身世——这“醉汉”竟是落魄的名斗牛士 Domingo Cordova——一桩看似荒诞的闹剧，背后藏着一个失意者的故事。下次你写一个小场景时，不妨也试试：给“对手”一点人格，把最意外的信息留到最后。
</Callout>
