  **Why was the girl in hospital?**（这姑娘为什么会住进医院？）带着这个问题听录音、读课文，重点留意全篇是怎样用 **方向 / 位移的介词短语**（to / from / into / out of / for / towards / at）一步步把 “她从哪儿来、往哪儿去、最后到了哪里” 讲得清清楚楚。
</Callout>

## The Text 课文

Nearly a week passed before the girl was able to explain what had happened to her. One afternoon she **set out from the coast** in a small boat and **was caught in a storm**. Towards evening, the boat **struck a rock** and the girl **jumped into the sea**. Then she **swam to the shore** after **spending the whole night in the water**. During that time she **covered a distance of eight miles**. Early next morning, she saw **a light ahead**. She knew she was near the shore because the light was **high up on the cliffs**. **On arriving at the shore**, the girl **struggled up the cliff** towards the light she had seen. That was the last she remembered. When she woke up a day later, she **found herself in hospital**.

## 课文注释 Notes on the text

<Callout type="info" title="6 个值得停下来嚼一嚼的点">
  1. **set out** (l.2) — “出发，动身，启程”。是固定短语，相当于 *set off / start out*。后接目的地用 **for**（*set out **for** Australia* 动身去澳大利亚）；后接 *to do* 则变成 “着手做某事”。课文 *she set out **from** the coast* —— 用 from 强调 “从海岸这个起点出发”。
  2. **be caught in a storm** (l.3) — “遭遇暴风雨”。这里的 **catch** 不是 “抓住”，而是 “（风雨、麻烦等）冷不防地撞上、困住”，几乎只用**被动** *be caught in*。同型：*be caught in the rain*（淋雨）/ *be caught in a traffic jam*（堵在路上）。
  3. **cover a distance of eight miles** (ll.5-6) — “走过 / 游过八英里的路程”。**cover** 在这里作 “行经、走完（一段距离）” 讲，不是 “覆盖”。同型：*We **covered** thirty miles a day.*（我们每天行进 30 英里。）
  4. **a light ahead** (l.7) — “前面的一盏灯”。**ahead** 是表方位的副词，放在所修饰的名词**后面**作后置定语（= *a light that was ahead*）。同型：*the road **ahead***（前方的路）/ *the days **ahead***（未来的日子）。
  5. **On arriving at the shore** (l.8) — “一到岸边……”。**on + 动名词（V-ing）** 表示 “一……就……”，相当于由 *as soon as* 引导的时间状语从句（= *As soon as she arrived at the shore*）。同型：***On hearing** the news, she burst into tears.*（一听到消息她就哭了。）
  6. **That was the last she remembered** (l.9) — “她所记得的就是这些”（再往后就什么都不记得了）。**the last** 在句中作表语，后面 *she remembered* 是省略了关系代词 *that* 的定语从句（that 在从句里作 remembered 的宾语，可省）。同型：*That was the first (that) he noticed.*
</Callout>

## 3. 关键句型 方向与位移的介词短语 Key structures

<Callout type="tip" title="一分钟回顾：用介词短语回答 Where / Which direction">
  本课的 Key structures 复习的是**表示方向与位移的介词短语**——它们专门用来回答 *Where ...?* 或 *Which direction ...?* 这类问题。课文几乎每一步移动都靠它们标定：*set out **from** the coast → jumped **into** the sea → swam **to** the shore → struggled up the cliff **towards** the light*。

  | 方向 / 位移 | 介词短语 | 课文 / 例句 |
  |---|---|---|
  | 朝某处去（到达终点） | **to** | *swam **to** the shore* / *climbed **to** the top* |
  | 从某处（来 / 离开） | **from** | *set out **from** the coast* / *a letter **from** Alan* |
  | 进入…里面 | **into** | *jumped **into** the sea* / *gone **into** the kitchen* |
  | 从…里面出来 | **out of** | *sailed **out of** the harbour* / *threw it **out of** the window* |
  | 出发前往（目的地） | **for** / set out for | *set out **for** Australia* / *left **for** Scotland* |
  | 朝…（只表方向，未必到达） | **towards** | *swam **towards** the shore* / *the car came **towards** me* |
  | 朝…（瞄准 / 指向） | **at** | *aimed **at** the bird* / *point **at** people* |

  ### ⚠️ 4 个最容易踩的雷区

  1. **to vs towards**：**to** 强调 “到达终点”，**towards** 只强调 “朝那个方向”，不保证到达。*She swam **to** the shore*（游到了岸）≠ *She swam **towards** the shore*（朝岸游，未必到）。
  2. **out of 是一对、不能拆**：表 “从…里出来” 必须 *out **of***，前面也不再加 from（*~~from out the window~~*）。
  3. **set out for + 目的地**（出发前往），不用 *set out to a place*；*set out **to do** sth.* 则是 “着手做某事”。
  4. **be taken to hospital**：英式英语中 *go to / be in / be taken to hospital* 表 “就医” 时**不加冠词 the**。
</Callout>

## 4. 难点：passed / past 与 next / other Special difficulties

<Callout type="info" title="两组最容易混淆、误用的词">
  本课 *Special difficulties* 讲的是 “经常容易混淆和误用的词”，共两组：

  ### ① passed vs past —— 同音，词性完全不同

  | 词 | 词性 | 含义 | 例句 |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | **passed** | **动词**（pass 的过去式 / 过去分词） | 经过；通过；（时间）流逝 | *Nearly a week **passed** ...*（课文 l.1）/ *He **passed** my house this morning.* |
  | **past** | **介词** | 从…旁边经过 | *He walked **past** my house.* |
  | **past** | **形容词** | 过去的 | *He told me about his **past** experiences.* |
  | **past** | **名词** | 过去（the past） | *He seems to live in the **past**.* |

  一句话记牢：**只有作 “谓语动词” 时才用 passed**；其余 “经过（介词）/ 过去的（形容词）/ 过去（名词）” 一律用 **past**。

  ### ② next vs other —— 都译 “另 / 下一个”，参照点不同

  - **the next day** = “（过去某天的）第二天 / 紧接其后的那天”，需要一个**前面提到的参照点**。*We arrived in Paris on Tuesday evening. The **next** day we went sightseeing.*（= the day after，星期三）。课文 *Early **next** morning*（第二天一早）也是这种 “紧接其后” 的用法。
  - **the other day** = “前几天、几天前”，泛指过去**不太久**的某一天。*I saw Mary the **other** day.*（= a few days ago）。
</Callout>

## 10. 句型转换 Sentence structure

<Callout type="info" title="任务：用 On + V-ing 改写">
  把下面这句改写成以 **On** 开头的句子，然后对照课文第 8-9 行，核对你的答案：

  *When she arrived at the shore, the girl struggled up the cliff.*

  **On** ______________________________________
</Callout>

<Callout type="tip" title="参考答案（自己写完再看）">
  **On arriving at the shore, the girl struggled up the cliff (towards the light she had seen).**

  要点：*When + 主语 + 谓语过去式* → **On + 动名词（V-ing）**。两个动作的主语必须是同一个人（都是 the girl），才能这样省略主语、压缩成 *on doing* 结构，表示 “一……就……”。
</Callout>

## 12. 作文 Composition

<Callout type="info" title="任务：用正确的动词和连词改写">
  *Rewrite these sentences using the correct verbs and joining words*（从每对 / 每组括号中选出正确的动词与连接词，改写成通顺的一句话）：

  On Saturday I (wanted) (needed) to go to a football match. It (not only) (neither) rained all day (or) (but) it was cold as well, (but) (so) I (stayed) (waited) at home (and) (but) (watched) (looked) the match on television.
</Callout>

<Callout type="warning" title="选词思路">
  - **wanted / needed** → **wanted**（“想去看球” 表意愿，不是 “必须去”）。
  - **not only / neither** → **not only**（与后文 *... as well* 呼应，构成 “not only ... but ... as well”）。
  - **or / but** → **but**（“not only ... **but** ... as well” 固定框架）。
  - **but / so** → **so**（又下雨又冷，**因此**待在家——因果关系）。
  - **stayed / waited** → **stayed**（*stay at home* = 待在家；wait at home 语义不对）。
  - **and / but** → **and**（“待在家” 与 “看电视” 顺承并列）。
  - **watched / looked** → **watched**（*watch the match / watch television* 表 “观看”；look 不及物，须接 at）。
</Callout>

<Callout type="tip" title="参考答案">
  On Saturday I **wanted** to go to a football match. It **not only** rained all day **but** it was cold as well, **so** I **stayed** at home **and watched** the match on television.
</Callout>

## 14. 拓展练习 Going further

<Callout type="tip" title="自学者建议">
  - **影子跟读 (shadowing)**：用第 1 节的三个 SayIt 按钮各跟读 3-5 遍。重点感受介词短语连成的 “路线感”——*swam **to** the shore* / *struggled up the cliff **towards** the light* / *found herself **in** hospital*，让每个介词都 “指向” 一个清晰的方向或位置。
  - **复述 (retell)**：合上课文，用 2 分钟把姑娘的脱险经过讲一遍。**叙事链条**：set out from the coast → caught in a storm → struck a rock → jumped into the sea → swam to the shore（all night, eight miles）→ saw a light on the cliffs → struggled up towards it → woke up in hospital。
  - **造句迁移**：用今天的 5 个高价值结构造你自己的句子——
    1. *set out from / for ...*（从……出发 / 前往……）—— *We set out for the mountains at dawn.*
    2. *be caught in ...*（遭遇风雨 / 堵车等）—— *I was caught in the rain on my way home.*
    3. *swim / walk / run **to** vs **towards** ...*（到达 vs 朝向）—— *He ran towards me but stopped halfway.*
    4. *On + V-ing, 主句*（一……就……）—— *On hearing the news, she jumped for joy.*
    5. *find oneself + 地点 / 状态*（发觉自己处于……）—— *He woke up and found himself alone in the dark.*
  - **延伸思考**：本课是一篇 “**动线清晰**” 的微型历险记——不到 100 词，却用一连串方向 / 位移介词（from / into / to / towards / up / in）把人物的每一步移动都钉在了地图上。写英文叙事时，与其堆砌形容词，不如先把 “**人从哪儿来、往哪儿去、最后到哪里**” 用准介词交代清楚——方向对了，画面自然就有了。再回味一下标题 *Out of the darkness*：它既是 “冲出黑夜的海面”，也是 “走出绝境”——好标题往往就这样，一语既写实、又点题。
</Callout>
