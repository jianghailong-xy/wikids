  你的母校给你来信了。信上说，你当年的老校长 Stuart Page 先生下星期就要退休了。算一算，他在那所学校教书已经整整 **40 年**——从你父亲那一辈开始，到你自己，再到今天坐在教室里的孩子们。学生们决定送他一件礼物、一本签名簿、一场告别晚宴。而这位教了四十年书的老校长，退休后却打算去学一门**全新的手艺**——园艺。他说过一句话，也正是本课的标题：**人，永远不会太老而学不了新东西**。

  **How long has Mr. Page been teaching?** 带着这个问题读课文，特别留意英语是怎样用三种 **将来时态**（一般将来 / 将来进行 / 将来完成进行）在短短 8 句话里搭出一座时间的阶梯。
</Callout>

## The Text 课文

I have just received a letter from my old school, **informing** me that my former headmaster, Mr. Stuart Page, **will be retiring** next week. Pupils of the school, old and new, **will be sending** him a present to mark the occasion. All those who have **contributed towards** the gift will sign their names in a large album which will be sent to the headmaster's home. We shall all remember Mr. Page for his **patience** and understanding and for the kindly **encouragement** he gave us when we went so unwillingly to school. A great many former pupils **will be attending** a **farewell** dinner **in his honour** next Thursday. It is a curious **coincidence** that the day before his retirement, Mr. Page **will have been teaching** for a total of forty years. After he has retired, he will **devote** himself to gardening. For him, this will be an **entirely** new hobby. But this does not matter, **for**, as he has often remarked, one is never too old to learn.

## 课文注释 Notes on the text

<Callout type="info" title="8 个值得咀嚼的语言点">
  1. **informing me that ...** (l.1) — **现在分词短语作伴随状语**。等于 *and it informed me that ...*，但更紧凑。分词逻辑主语是 *a letter*。
  2. **will be retiring / will be sending / will be attending** — **将来进行时** (will be + V-ing)。表按计划将要发生的动作，语气比一般将来时更确定。
  3. **contribute towards** (l.5) — 为……捐助。contribute 后接 to 或 towards + 名词。
  4. **patience and understanding** (l.7) — 抽象名词并列作 for 的宾语。patience 来自形容词 patient。
  5. **in his honour** (l.9) — 为向某人表示敬意。英式拼 honour（美式 honor）。
  6. **will have been teaching** (l.10) — **将来完成进行时**。站在将来某时间点回头看持续到那时的动作。全课最高阶的时态。
  7. **devote oneself to + doing** (l.11) — 致力于。to 是介词，后接动名词，不是不定式！
  8. **for** (l.13) — 并列连词 = because（书面语，不能放句首）。结尾 **one is never too old to learn** 是 too ... to 结构：永远不会太老而不能学。
</Callout>

## 3. 关键句型 Three future tenses 三种将来时态

<Callout type="tip" title="一分钟回顾">
  本课是英语将来时态三种变体的绝佳示例——短短 8 句话里，三种将来时各司其职：

  | 时态 | 形式 | 课文例句 | 用途 |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | **一般将来时** | will + V 原形 | *will sign / will devote* | 单纯将来动作 |
  | **将来进行时** | will be + V-ing | *will be retiring / will be sending* | 已安排好的将来动作 |
  | **将来完成进行时** | will have been + V-ing | *will have been teaching for forty years* | 站将来回头看持续时长 |

  ### 雷区

  1. 将来进行时 vs 一般将来时：will retire（单纯宣布）vs will be retiring（计划已定）
  2. 将来完成进行时回答到那时为止持续了多久
  3. 时间状语从句用现在时表将来：After he has retired, not After he will have retired
  4. for 作并列连词 = because（书面语，从句只能后置）
</Callout>

## 4. 难点 Special difficulties: too...to / enough to / so...that / such...that

<Callout type="info" title="程度→结果句型家族">
  | 结构 | 接什么 | 含义 | 例句 |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | **too + adj/adv + to do** | 形容词/副词 → 不定式 | 太……而不能…… | *He is too tired to walk.* |
  | **adj/adv + enough + to do** | enough 后置 | 足够……能做…… | *She is old enough to vote.* |
  | **so + adj/adv + that 从句** | 形容词/副词 → that 从句 | 如此……以至于…… | *The tea was so hot that I burnt my tongue.* |
  | **such + (a/an) + adj + n. + that** | 名词短语 → that 从句 | 如此……的（东西）以至于…… | *It was such a long film that we left early.* |
  | **never too + adj + to do** | too...to 反转版 | 永远不会太……而不能…… | *You are never too old to learn.* |

  ### 高频错误

  1. too...to 已含否定，再加 not 是大错
  2. enough 修饰形容词/副词时后置：old enough ✓ / enough old ✗
  3. so 接形容词/副词，such 接名词短语：so unwillingly ✓ / such unwilling pupils ✓
</Callout>

## 10. 作文 Composition

<Callout type="info" title="任务">
  Write a short composition of about 120 words about a teacher you remember well. Describe who they were, one quality you remember them for, and what they do now or will do after retirement. Use at least three of: the future continuous, devote oneself to, in one's honour, and never too old to ...
</Callout>

<Callout type="warning" title="提示骨架">
  - 介绍老师：Who? What subject? How long did they teach?
  - 难忘的特质：What quality? Give one specific memory.
  - 现在/将来：Where are they now? Or imagine their retirement.
  - 收尾：End with a reflection on never too old to ...
</Callout>

<Callout type="tip" title="参考范文">
  Mrs. Chen taught English at my middle school for over thirty years. I shall always remember her for her endless patience. Once, when I was too shy to read aloud in class, she stayed after school and practised with me sentence by sentence. By the time she retired last year, she had been teaching for a total of thirty-two years. A great many former students attended a farewell party in her honour. After retiring, she told us she would devote herself to learning the piano — an instrument she had never touched before. After all, she said with a smile, one is never too old to learn. (about 130 words)
</Callout>

## 11. 拓展练习 Going further

<Callout type="tip" title="自学者建议">
  - **影子跟读**：用前面三个 SayIt 按钮跟读 3-5 遍。重点感受将来进行时的自然语流——will be retiring 读成 /wɪl bi rɪˈtaɪərɪŋ/，will 轻读，重音落在 retiring 第二音节。
  - **复述**：合上课文，用 2 分钟讲一遍 Mr. Page 退休的故事。叙事三段：来信通知退休 → 礼物与告别宴会 → 40 年巧合与园艺新爱好。
  - **时态转换**：把课文转成过去视角——假设一年后回忆：
    - I had just received a letter ... informing me that Mr. Page would be retiring the following week.
    - By the day before his retirement, he would have been teaching for forty years.
  - **造句迁移**：用 5 个高价值结构各造一个句子——
    1. will be + V-ing: I will be visiting my grandparents this weekend.
    2. will have been + V-ing: By next month, she will have been working here for ten years.
    3. After + 现在完成时, 将来时: After I have finished this report, I will call you.
    4. devote oneself to + doing: He devoted himself to helping refugees.
    5. never too + adj + to do: You are never too busy to call your mother.
  - **延伸思考**：NCE2 Lesson 85 把三种将来时态塞进了一段不到 180 词的短文里。将来进行时告诉你那个将来时刻事情正按计划运转；将来完成进行时告诉你到那天为止有件事已经持续了这么久。而标题那句 never too old to learn 则把 too...to 这个否定结构翻转为励志格言——学会这一句，就拿到了一个可以说一辈子的话。
</Callout>
