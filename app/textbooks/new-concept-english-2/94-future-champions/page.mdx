</Callout>

## The Text 课文

Experiments **have proved** that children **can be instructed in swimming** at a very early age. At a special swimming pool in Los Angeles, children **become expert at** holding their breath under water even before they can walk. Babies of two months old **do not appear to be reluctant** to enter the water. **It is not long before** they are **so accustomed to swimming that** they can pick up weights from the floor of the pool. A game that is very **popular with** these young swimmers is the underwater tricycle race. Tricycles **are lined up** on one side of the pool seven feet under water. The children **compete against each other** to reach the other end of the pool. Many pedal their tricycles, but most of them prefer to push or drag them. Some children can cover the whole length of the pool **without coming up for breath** even once. **Whether they will ever become future Olympic champions, only time will tell.** Meanwhile, they should encourage those among us who cannot swim five yards before they are **gasping for air**.

## 课文注释 Notes on the text

<Callout type="info" title="5 个值得停下来嚼一嚼的点">
  1. **hold one's breath** (l.3) — “屏住呼吸”。固定搭配，breath 是名词（不发 /ð/，读 /breθ/）；动词形式 *breathe* /briːð/ 才发浊音。课文 *become expert at holding their breath* = 变得擅长屏住呼吸。同型：*Hold your breath and count to ten.*
  2. **be popular with ...** (l.7) — “深受……的欢迎，为……所喜爱”。注意介词是 **with**（不是 among / for）。课文 *A game that is very popular with these young swimmers* = 一种深受这些小泳手喜爱的游戏。同型：*The teacher is popular with her students.*
  3. **compete against / with ...** (l.9) — “与……竞争，与……抗衡”。**against** 强调“对抗”，**with** 也可用。课文 *compete against each other* = 互相比赛。**each other = one another**（互相）。同型：*Our team competed against five others.*
  4. **come up for breath / air** (l.11) — “(从水下)升上水面换气”。**come up** = 浮上来；**for breath** = 为了换气。课文 *without coming up for breath even once* = 一次也不用浮出水面换气。
  5. **Whether they will ever become future Olympic champions, only time will tell.** (ll.11-12) — “他们将来是否能成为奥林匹克冠军，只能由时间来回答。” 这里 **whether 引导的从句其实是 tell 的宾语**，正常语序是 *Only time will tell whether ...*；把宾语从句提到句首，是为了 **强调** 那份悬念。**only time will tell** 是地道短语：“只有时间能给出答案 / 拭目以待”。
</Callout>

## 3. 关键句型 被动语态 · be accustomed to · It is not long before

<Callout type="tip" title="一分钟回顾">
  本课 Structure 题集中考 4 组高频结构，把它们一次理清：

  | 结构 | 课文 / 例句 | 要点 |
  |---|---|---|
  | **被动语态 be + 过去分词** | *children **can be instructed** in swimming* / *Tricycles **are lined up*** | 受动者作主语；情态动词后用 *be + V-ed* |
  | **be accustomed to + 名词 / 动名词** | *they are **so accustomed to swimming** that ...* | 这里 **to 是介词**，后接 -ing，不是 to do |
  | **so + adj. + that 从句** | *so accustomed to swimming **that** they can pick up weights* | “如此……以至于……” |
  | **It is not long before ...** | ***It is not long before** they are so accustomed ...* | “不久之后就……”；before 引导时间状语从句 |

  ### ⚠️ 4 个最容易踩的雷区

  1. **be accustomed / used TO + 动名词**：这里的 *to* 是 **介词**，后面跟 **-ing**。*be accustomed to swimming* ✓ / *be accustomed to swim* ✗。别和表过去习惯的 **used to do**（“过去常常”）混淆。
  2. **情态动词 + 被动**：*can be instructed* —— 情态动词后动词必用原形，被动就是 *be + 过去分词*；*can be instructed* ✓ / *can is instructed* ✗。
  3. **compete against / with each other**：*each other* = *one another*（互相），都对；不能写成 *the one against the other*。
  4. **It is not long before ...** 是固定句型，**before**（在……之前）不能换成 after/since；意思是“要不了多久就……”。
</Callout>

## 4. 难点：动词 / 形容词 + 介词搭配 Prepositions

<Callout type="info" title="本课最大的练习：介词搭配">
  教材本课的 *Key Structures* 练习是一张 35 句的“**填介词**”大表——这正是 NCE2 阶段最该攻克的硬功夫。介词不是“随便选一个”，而是和特定动词 / 形容词 **死死绑定** 的固定搭配。先记住课文里出现的几组：

  | 搭配 | 课文例句 | 中文 |
  |---|---|---|
  | **instruct sb. in sth.** | *children can be instructed **in** swimming* | 传授某人某事 |
  | **be expert at (doing)** | *become expert **at** holding their breath* | 擅长（做）…… |
  | **be reluctant to do** | *do not appear to be reluctant **to** enter* | 不愿做…… |
  | **be accustomed to (doing)** | *so accustomed **to** swimming* | 习惯于…… |
  | **be popular with sb.** | *popular **with** these young swimmers* | 受……欢迎 |
  | **compete against sb.** | *compete **against** each other* | 与……竞争 |

  ### ⚠️ 几组最易混的“形近搭配”

  1. **different from**（与……不同）/ **dependent on**（依赖于）/ **inferior to**（不如，次于）—— to 不能换成 than。
  2. **approve of**（赞成）/ **be capable of**（有能力）/ **be aware of**（意识到）/ **beware of**（提防）—— 都接 of。
  3. **rely on / depend on / count on**（依靠、指望）—— 都接 on。
  4. **cured of**（治愈某病）/ **guilty of**（犯有某罪）/ **ignorant of**（不知道）—— 都接 of。
</Callout>

## 10. 摘要写作 Summary writing (≤ 80 词)

<Callout type="info" title="任务">
  *In not more than 80 words*, describe **what goes on at the children's swimming pool in Los Angeles**. Write **two different paragraphs** using the points and connections given below.

  用不超过 **80 词**、分 **两段** 描述洛杉矶儿童游泳池里正在进行的事。按下列要点（POINTS）与连词（CONNECTIONS）串成文：

  **第一段**
  1. Swimming pool — Los Angeles. （**who** 引出下一点）
  2. Children taught — hold breath — walk.
  3. Begin learning: two months old. （**often begin**）
  4. Weights — bottom of pool. （**and, in time** / **Though**）
  5. Game they enjoy — race.

  **第二段**
  6. Takes place seven feet under water. （**which** / **This**）
  7. Pedal — push — pull. （**Some ... others** / **and**）
  8. A few get across — without coming up for air. （**and**）
</Callout>

<Callout type="tip" title="参考范文（自己写完再看）">
  At a special pool in Los Angeles, children are taught to hold their breath before they can walk. They often begin learning at two months old, and in time they can pick up weights from the bottom of the pool. The game they most enjoy is a race. This takes place seven feet under water. ## 11. 作文 Composition (约 150 词)

<Callout type="info" title="任务">
  Imagine witnessing the sort of underwater tricycle race described in the passage. Write **two paragraphs** in **about 150 words** using the ideas given below:

  假设你亲眼看见了课文中的水下三轮车比赛，按以下思路写两段、约 150 词：

  1. The children dived into the water — found their tricycles — started off.
  2. A child was pulling his tricycle — dropped it — hindered two others — got it out of the way — another child was pedalling hard — won the race — rose to surface dragging up his tricycle.
</Callout>

<Callout type="warning" title="提示骨架">
  - **第一段（起跑）**：一声哨响 → 孩子们鱼贯跳入水中 → 沉到池底找到各自的三轮车 → 出发。
  - **第二段（过程与结局）**：聚焦“一个拉车的孩子”——拉得吃力 → 三轮车脱手 → 挡住后面两人 → 好不容易挪开 → 镜头切到“拼命蹬车的另一个孩子” → 率先冲线 → 拖着三轮车浮出水面欢呼。
</Callout>

<Callout type="tip" title="写作脚手架">
  - **时态主线**：一般过去时叙事（*dived, found, started, won, rose*）；**过去进行时**作背景（*was pulling / was pedalling hard*）；过去完成偶用（*had dropped*）。
  - **入水动词库**：*dived in, plunged into the water, slipped beneath the surface, sank to the bottom, kicked off*。
  - **比赛动词库**：*pedalled furiously, pushed, dragged, lost his grip on, hindered, blocked, got it out of the way, shot ahead, surged forward, won the race*。
  - **浮出水面**：*broke the surface, rose to the surface, came up gasping, dragging his tricycle behind him*。
  - **结构亮点**：用 *As one child was pulling his tricycle, he dropped it and ...*（过去进行 + 主句）把“某一刻发生了意外”演活。
</Callout>

<Callout type="tip" title="参考范文（自己写完再看）">
  When the whistle blew, the children dived into the water one after another and sank straight to the bottom. There, lined up in a neat row, were their tricycles. Each child found his own, climbed on and started off across the pool. For a moment everything was a blur of legs and bubbles. As one boy was pulling his tricycle along, he suddenly lost his grip and dropped it. The machine rolled sideways and hindered two other children, who had to swim round it. By the time he had got it out of the way, he was hopelessly behind. Meanwhile, another child was pedalling hard near the far wall. She never once came up for air, and she won the race easily, touching the side a full length ahead of the rest. Then she rose to the surface, dragging up her tricycle and grinning from ear to ear. *(146 words)*
</Callout>

## 13. 拓展练习 Going further

<Callout type="tip" title="自学者建议">
  - **影子跟读 (shadowing)**：用第 1 节的三个 SayIt 反复跟读 3-5 遍，重点感受 *can be instructed* 的弱读和 *so accustomed to swimming that* 的连读节奏。
  - **复述 (retell)**：合上课文，用 1 分钟讲清这座洛杉矶泳池里发生的事——**三层**：① 婴儿很早就被传授游泳、能屏息捡重物；② 最受欢迎的游戏是水下三轮车赛；③ 他们能否成奥运冠军，只有时间能回答。
  - **造句迁移**：用今天的 5 个核心结构造你自己的句子——
    1. *S + can be + 过去分词*（被动）—— *The machine can be repaired in an hour.*
    2. *be accustomed to + 动名词* —— *She is accustomed to working late.*
    3. *It is not long before ...* —— *It is not long before he gives up.*
    4. *compete against + 人* —— *Our school competed against three others.*
    5. *Whether ..., only time will tell.* —— *Whether the plan succeeds, only time will tell.*
  - **介词专项**：把第 4 节的“动词 / 形容词 + 介词”做成自己的小卡片（approve of / different from / dependent on / capable of / inferior to ...），每天默写 5 组——NCE2 阶段，介词搭配过关，作文里的“硬伤”就少一大半。
  - **延伸思考**：本课是一篇 **说明性叙述**——先“摆事实”（实验证明……），再“给画面”（三轮车赛），最后“留悬念 + 升华”（only time will tell + 反衬普通人）。这种“事实 → 细节 → 点睛”的结构，正是写英文说明文最稳的骨架。
</Callout>
