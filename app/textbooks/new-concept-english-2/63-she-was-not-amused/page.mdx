## The Text 课文

Jeremy Hampden has **a large circle of friends** and is **very popular at parties**. Everybody **admires him for** his **great sense of humour** — everybody, that is, except his six-year-old daughter, Jenny. Recently, one of Jeremy's closest friends asked him to **make a speech** at a wedding reception. This is **the sort of thing that** Jeremy loves. He prepared the speech carefully and went to the wedding with Jenny. He had included a large number of funny stories in the speech and, of course, it was **a great success**. **As soon as** he had finished, Jenny told him she wanted to go home. Jeremy was a little disappointed by this but he **did as his daughter asked**. On the way home, he asked Jenny if she had enjoyed the speech. **To his surprise**, she said she hadn't. Jeremy asked her why this was so and she told him that she did not like to see **so many people laughing at him**!

## 课文注释 Notes on the text

<Callout type="info" title="5 个值得停下来嚼一嚼的点">
  1. **a large circle of friends** (l.1) — "一大群朋友；交际很广"。**circle** 在这里不是"圆圈"，而是"圈子、社交圈"。同型：*a circle of close friends / the family circle / business circles*（商界）。配 *is very popular at parties* 一起读，画出一个左右逢源的社交达人形象。
  2. **Everybody admires him for his great sense of humour** (l.2) — "人人都钦佩他那绝妙的幽默感"。**admire ... for ...** = "因……而钦佩 / 赞赏……"，**for** 后接钦佩的原因。**a sense of humour** = "幽默感"（sense of + 抽象名词，如 *a sense of direction / a sense of duty*）。注意 *everybody ... except ...* 的对照：先说"所有人"，再用破折号 + *that is, except ...* 抖出那个唯一的例外，制造反差。
  3. **but he did as his daughter asked** (l.9) — "但他还是按照女儿的要求做了"。**as** 在这里是连词，引导 **方式状语从句**，意为"按照、正如"。**did** 是替代动词，指代前文的 *go home*（=他照做了"回家"这件事）。同型：*Do as I say. / She acted as she had promised.*
  4. **To his surprise** (l.10) — "使他吃惊的是"。**to one's + 情感名词** 是固定结构，作句首状语。同型：*to my delight（令我高兴的是）/ to our astonishment / much to his relief*。
  5. **she did not like to see so many people laughing at him** (l.11) — "她不愿意看到那么多人嘲笑他"。**see sb. doing sth.** = "看见某人正在做某事"，其中 *laughing at him* 是 **现在分词短语**，作 *see* 的 **宾语补足语**，强调"正在进行、持续的画面"。**laugh at sb.** = "嘲笑某人"（这正是全文的反转：大家笑的是"故事"，孩子却以为是在"笑话爸爸"）。
</Callout>

## 3. 关键句型 间接引语 Reported speech（He said that... / He told me... / He asked...）

<Callout type="tip" title="一分钟回顾">
  本课的语法重点是 **间接引语（reported speech / indirect speech）**——把别人说的"原话"转述出来。转述时要做三件事：**换引导动词、调人称、退时态（与课文一致：主句过去 → 从句相应后退）**。把常见的四类原话和对应的转述句记牢：

  | 原话类型 | 直接引语 | 间接引语（转述） |
  |---|---|---|
  | **陈述句** | *"I want to go home."* | She told him (that) she **wanted** to go home. |
  | **一般疑问句** | *"Did you enjoy the speech?"* | He asked her **if / whether** she **had enjoyed** the speech. |
  | **特殊疑问句** | *"Why didn't you enjoy it?"* | He asked her **why** she **hadn't enjoyed** it. |
  | **祈使句 / 请求** | *"Open the door, please."* | She asked me **to open** the door. |
  | **祈使句 / 命令** | *"Don't make so much noise."* | She told them **not to make** so much noise. |
  | **建议 suggest** | *"Send him a fax."* | He suggested **that** I (**should**) send him a fax. |
  | **坚持 insist** | *"Stay to lunch."* | He insisted **that** I (**should**) stay to lunch. |

  ### ⚠️ 4 个本课最容易踩的雷区

  1. **间接疑问句用陈述语序，且去掉问号**：*He asked **why this was so***（√，主语 this 在前、动词 was 在后）；✗ *He asked why was this so?*（不能再倒装、不能加问号）。
  2. **tell / ask sb. to do**：转述命令或请求用 **不定式**。*told them **not to** make ...*（否定式 not 放 to 前）；*asked me **to** open ...*。
  3. **suggest / insist 不接 "sb. to do"**：要用 **that 从句 + (should) do**。✗ *suggested me to send* —— 这是中国学生最爱犯的错；√ *suggested that I should send*。
  4. **时态后退**：主句是过去时（asked / told），从句要相应后退——*did enjoy → had enjoyed*，*want → wanted*，*will → would*。课文 *asked Jenny **if she had enjoyed** the speech* 就是范例。
</Callout>

## 4. 难点：直接引语的标点 & 间接疑问句 Special difficulties

<Callout type="info" title="把“原话”标点标对：引号、逗号、问号">
  本课练习 A（参见第 26 课难点 **SD26**）要你给一段对话加上 **引号、逗号、问号**。规则记牢这 4 条：

  | 场景 | 规则 | 例 |
  |---|---|---|
  | **引号** | 说的"原话"前后各加一对引号 | **"** I want to go home **,"** Jenny told him. |
  | **逗号 vs 问号** | 原话是 **陈述句** → 引号内用 **逗号**；原话是 **疑问句** → 引号内用 **问号** | **"** No **,"** she said. ／ **"** Why didn't you enjoy it **?"** Jeremy asked. |
  | **称呼语 (vocative)** | 句中对某人的称呼前要加逗号 | Did you enjoy the speech **,** Jenny? |
  | **结束标点位置** | 逗号 / 问号放在 **引号之内**；转述部分（he said / she asked）首字母小写 | "..., " Jenny told him. |

  ### ⚠️ 把课文那句话标点还原一遍

  原话散开：*I do not like to see so many people laughing at you she told him* →
  标点后：**"**I do not like to see so many people laughing at you**,"** she told him.
  （陈述句 → 引号内逗号；she told him 紧跟其后，s 小写。）

  ### 间接疑问句（I wonder ...）三步走

  把"直接疑问句"改成"I wonder + 间接疑问句"：① 疑问句一律换成 **陈述语序**；② **去掉问号**；③ 一般疑问句用 **if / whether** 引导，特殊疑问句保留 **疑问词**。
  *Will it rain tomorrow?* → *I wonder **if it will** rain tomorrow.*　*Why didn't he mention this?* → *I wonder **why he didn't** mention this.*

## 10. 摘要写作 Summary writing (≤ 80 词)

<Callout type="info" title="任务">
  *Answer these questions in not more than 80 words.*（回答下列问题，把答案连成一段，不超过 80 词。）按这 4 个提示点串成一段：

  1. Is Jeremy Hampden greatly admired for his great sense of humour or not? What was he invited to do? Did he immediately agree to do so or not? *(When Jeremy ... who is ...)*
  2. Did the speech contain a lot of funny stories or not? Was it a great success or not? *(Since)*
  3. What did his six-year-old daughter, Jenny, want to do after his speech? Was Jeremy disappointed or not? *(When)*
  4. Why had she not enjoyed it? *(because)*
</Callout>

<Callout type="tip" title="参考范文（自己写完再看）">
  Jeremy Hampden, who is greatly admired for his great sense of humour, was invited to make a speech at a wedding reception and immediately agreed. Since the speech contained a lot of funny stories, it was a great success. When it was over, however, his six-year-old daughter Jenny wanted to go home, and Jeremy was disappointed, because she said she did not like to see so many people laughing at him. *(72 words)*
</Callout>

## 11. 作文 Composition (扩写)

<Callout type="info" title="任务">
  *Write two or three sentences using the ideas given below.*（用以下提示写出 2 至 3 个句子。）

  Jeremy was amused by Jenny's answer — he explained why everybody laughed — there was another reception some time later — Jeremy made a speech — not very funny — Jenny asked why no one laughed.
</Callout>

<Callout type="warning" title="提示骨架">
  - 第 1 句：Jeremy 被女儿的回答逗乐了 + 向她解释大家为什么笑（*be amused by ... and explained that ...*）。
  - 第 2 句：过了一阵又有一场招待会，Jeremy 又致辞，但这次不太好笑（*Some time later ... made another speech ... not very funny*）。
  - 第 3 句：这回 Jenny 反过来问"为什么没人笑"（*this time Jenny asked why no one was laughing*）—— 形成首尾呼应的反转。
</Callout>

<Callout type="tip" title="写作脚手架">
  - **时态主线**：一般过去时叙事（was, explained, made, asked）。
  - **"逗乐"词库**：*was amused by, was tickled by, could not help smiling at*。
  - **"解释"句式**：*explained that they were laughing at his stories, not at him*。
  - **呼应技巧**：开篇 Jenny 担心"大家笑爸爸"，结尾 Jenny 困惑"为什么没人笑"——同一个孩子、相反的处境，幽默感就出来了。
</Callout>

<Callout type="tip" title="参考范文（自己写完再看）">
  Jeremy was amused by Jenny's answer and gently explained that the guests had been laughing at his funny stories, not at him. Some time later, he was asked to speak at another reception, but this speech was not very funny at all. When he had finished, Jenny turned to him and innocently asked why no one had laughed this time. *(58 words)*
</Callout>

## 12. 书信写作 Letter writing

<Callout type="info" title="任务">
  *Learn the following opening phrases by heart.*（熟记以下两个开头语。）然后 *write two short paragraphs (of not more than two sentences each) completing the above phrases*（用这两个开头语各写一小段，每段不超过两句）：

  - You must be very annoyed with me for ...
  - I have just heard that ...
</Callout>

<Callout type="tip" title="写作脚手架">
  - **第 1 个开头语**（道歉口吻）：*You must be very annoyed with me for ...* 后接 **动名词**（for + V-ing），交代你让对方生气的事 + 致歉。例：*for not replying to your last two letters / for failing to return your book on time*。
  - **第 2 个开头语**（转告消息）：*I have just heard that ...* 后接 **that 从句**，传达一个刚得知的消息 + 你的反应。例：*that you have passed all your examinations / that you are getting married next month*。
  - **每段限两句**：第一句用给定开头语起头，第二句补一句解释或祝愿即可，别展开太长。
</Callout>

<Callout type="tip" title="参考范文（自己写完再看）">
  You must be very annoyed with me for not answering your last letter sooner. I have been snowed under with examinations, and simply could not find a free moment to write.

  I have just heard that you have won a scholarship to study in London. Many congratulations — you thoroughly deserve it, and I do hope we can meet before you leave.
</Callout>

## 13. 拓展练习 Going further

<Callout type="tip" title="自学者建议">
  - **影子跟读 (shadowing)**：用第 1 节的三个 SayIt 按钮跟读 3-5 遍。重点感受破折号处的停顿——*everybody admires him ... — everybody, that is, except ...*：先扬后抑，把"唯一例外"那份反差读出来；结尾 *laughing at him!* 重音落在 *him*，带出孩子心疼爸爸的语气。
  - **复述 (retell)**：合上课文，用 1 分钟讲完这个故事。**三段式**：人物（人见人爱、幽默感爆棚的 Jeremy，唯独女儿不买账）→ 经过（受邀致辞 → 笑话连篇 → 大获成功 → 刚讲完女儿就要回家）→ 反转（一问才知，她不忍看大家"嘲笑"爸爸）。
  - **造句迁移**：用今天的 5 个核心结构造你自己的句子——
    1. *admire sb. for sth.* —— *I admire her for her courage.*
    2. *as soon as + 从句* —— *As soon as the bell rang, the children rushed out.*
    3. *ask sb. if / whether ...*（间接疑问句）—— *She asked me if I had finished my homework.*
    4. *tell sb. (not) to do sth.* —— *The teacher told us not to be late.*
    5. *I wonder + 疑问词 + 陈述语序* —— *I wonder why he hasn't called.*
  - **延伸思考**：本课是 **间接引语** 的实战范本——把"原话"转述出来，时态、人称、语序都要相应调整，这是英语叙事的基本功。更妙的是它的"反转结构"：客观上是 *a great success*（大成功），主观上 Jenny *was not amused*（笑不出来）——**同一件事，因为视角不同，意义完全相反**。好的故事往往就藏在这种"误读"里：六岁孩子把善意的大笑听成了嘲笑，于是这份天真反而让人心头一暖。下次你写英文小故事时，不妨也留一个"出人意料、却又合情合理"的结尾。
</Callout>
