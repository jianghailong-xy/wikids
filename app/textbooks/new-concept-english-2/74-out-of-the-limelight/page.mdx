## The Text 课文

An ancient bus stopped by a dry river bed and a party of famous actors and actresses got off. **Dressed in dark glasses and old clothes**, they **had taken special precautions** so that no one would recognize them. But as they soon discovered, **disguises can sometimes be too perfect**.

'This is a wonderful place for a picnic,' said Gloria Gleam.

'**It couldn't be better**, Gloria,' Brinksley Meers agreed. 'No newspaper men, no film fans! **Why don't we come more often?**'

Meanwhile, two other actors, Rockwall Slinger and Merlin Greeves, **had carried** two large food baskets to **a shady spot** under some trees. When they **had all made themselves comfortable**, a stranger appeared. He looked very angry. 'Now you **get out of here**, all of you!' he shouted. 'I'm sheriff here. Do you see that notice? It says "No Camping" — **in case you can't read**!'

'Look, sheriff,' said Rockwall, '**don't be too hard on us**. I'm Rockwall Slinger and this is Merlin Greeves.'

'Oh, is it?' said the sheriff **with a sneer**. 'Well, I'm Brinksley Meers, and my other name is Gloria Gleam. Now you get out of here fast!'

## 课文注释 Notes on the text

<Callout type="info" title="6 个值得停下来嚼一嚼的点">
  1. **out of the limelight**（标题）— **limelight** 本义是19世纪舞台上用的"石灰光"，引申为"公众注意的中心 / 舞台"。**in the limelight** = 出风头、受人瞩目；**out of the limelight** = 躲开聚光灯、不在公众视线中。这群明星正是想 *stay out of the limelight*。
  2. **as they soon discovered**（l.4）— 这里的 **as** 是**关系代词**，代替它后面的整个主句 *disguises can sometimes be too perfect*，作动词 **discovered** 的宾语。这种 *as* 引导的非限定性从句意为"正如……"，可置于句首、句中或句末。同型：*As we all know, ... / He is late again, as is usual.*
  3. **Why don't we come more often?**（l.8）— "我们为什么不更经常来这里呢？"这是一个**否定形式的特殊疑问句**，字面是反问，实际**表示一种建议**（= Let's come more often / We should come more often）。同型：*Why don't you sit down? / Why not try again?*
  4. **get out of here**（l.11）— "从这里走开、滚开"。**get out of + 地点** = 离开、出去。课文里司法长官连说两次 *get out of here*，口气强硬。**get** 是本课难点，一词多义（见下方"难点"）。
  5. **in case you can't read**（l.12）— "免得 / 生怕你们不识字"，这里是带挖苦意味的"除非你们不识字"。**in case + 从句**表示"以防、免得"，强调预防。同型：*Take an umbrella in case it rains.*
  6. **don't be too hard on sb.**（l.13）— "别对某人太苛刻 / 别让某人太难堪"。**be hard on sb.** = 对某人严厉、苛刻。同型：*Don't be so hard on yourself. / The teacher was hard on the lazy students.*。结尾 **with a sneer**（冷笑着）一词，把司法长官的不屑表现得淋漓尽致。
</Callout>

## 3. 关键句型 现在进行时 vs 一般现在时 (KS2, 26, 50)

<Callout type="tip" title="一分钟回顾：What is happening? 还是 What always happens?">
  本课的关键句型复习第 2、26、50 课——**现在进行时**和**一般现在时**的对立。一句话抓住区别：

  | 时态 | 回答的问题 | 用途 | 课文 / 例句 |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | **一般现在时** | *What always happens?* | 习惯、普遍真理、客观事实 | *It **says** "No Camping". / Light **travels** fast.* |
  | **现在进行时** | *What is happening (now)?* | 此刻正在进行的动作、眼前正在发生的事 | *The police now **are investigating** the matter. / The bus **is coming**!* |

  ### ⚠️ 4 个本课最容易踩的雷区

  1. **状态动词 (stative verbs) 一般不用进行时**：表示"感知 / 情感 / 拥有 / 思维"的动词——**see, hear, resemble, love, want, prefer, contain, mean, believe, understand, matter, like, know**——通常**只用一般现在时**，不说 *is resembling / am wanting*。课文 *Do you **see** that notice?* 用一般现在，不是 *Are you seeing*。
  2. **"现在"不等于"正在"**：*Light travels at 186,000 miles a second*（光速是普遍真理）→ 一般现在；*The police now are investigating*（眼下正在查）→ 进行时。关键看是"恒常规律"还是"此刻动作"。
  3. **临时 / 眼前的动作用进行时**：*Hurry up! The bus **is coming**!*（车正过来）——眼前正在发生，必须进行时。
  4. **建议用否定疑问的一般现在**：*Why **don't** we come more often?*——这是建议，不是"正在来"，用一般现在时。
</Callout>

## 4. 难点：动词 get 的百变含义 + 公共告示

<Callout type="info" title="难点 a：一个 get，十几种意思">
  课文里司法长官吼的 *Now you **get out** of here!*（l.11）只是 **get** 的一种用法。**get** 是英语里最"百变"的动词，搭配不同小品词（out / into / on / off / over / through）意思天差地别。教材列举如下，建议整组记忆：

  | 短语 | 含义 | 例句 |
  |---|---|---|
  | **get out** | 出去、离开 | *The door is locked. I can't get out.* |
  | **get into** | 进入 | *I've lost my key. I can't get into the house.* |
  | **get on** | 进展（顺利） | *How is he getting on in his new job?* (= progressing) |
  | **get on with (work)** | 继续做 | *Get on with your work.* (= continue) |
  | **get on with (sb.)** | 与某人相处融洽 | *He's difficult. I can't get on with him.* (= be on good terms) |
  | **get off** | 逃脱（惩罚） | *Tom was punished, but Jim got off lightly.* (= escaped punishment) |
  | **get ... off** | 取下、拿掉 | *I can't get the cap of this pen off.* (= remove) |
  | **get over** | 从（病 / 打击中）恢复 | *He has now got over his illness.* (= recovered) |
  | **get ... over** | 把（讨厌的事）了结 | *I want to get this interview over as quickly as possible.* (= finish) |
  | **get through** | 通过（考试）；吃 / 用完 | *He got through his exams. / He got through a huge amount of food.* |
</Callout>

<Callout type="info" title="难点 b：公共告示怎么写">
  课文告示牌上写的是 *No Camping*（l.12），而不是 *Do not camp*。**在公共告示牌上，英语习惯用 "No + 动名词/名词" 的简洁形式**，而不用完整的祈使句：

  - *Do not smoke* → **No Smoking**
  - *Do not park* → **No Parking**
  - *Do not wait (on this side)* → **No Waiting (on this side)**
  - *Do not enter* → **No Entry** ／ *Do not camp* → **No Camping**
</Callout>

## 11. 作文 Composition (约 150 词，两段)

<Callout type="info" title="任务">
  Write **two paragraphs in about 150 words** using the ideas given below:

  1. A fine day — we prepared for a picnic — drove into the country — found a quiet spot — unpacked our hampers.
  2. There were a few wasps — then a great many — our efforts to drive them away — saw a wasp nest in a tree nearby — seized our things — returned to car pursued by wasps — drove away.
</Callout>

<Callout type="warning" title="提示骨架">
  - **第一段（铺垫，风和日丽）**：天气好 → 备好野餐 → 开车进乡间 → 找到僻静处 → 打开食品篮，本该是完美的一天。
  - **第二段（转折，黄蜂来袭）**：先来几只黄蜂 → 越聚越多 → 我们驱赶 → 发现旁边树上有蜂巢 → 抓起东西 → 被黄蜂追着逃回车里 → 开车走人。
</Callout>

<Callout type="tip" title="写作脚手架">
  - **时态主线**：一般过去时叙事（*drove, found, unpacked, seized*）；过去进行表背景（*the sun was shining, wasps were buzzing*）；过去完成表更早（*had prepared / had found*）。
  - **"野餐准备"词库**：*packed a hamper, spread a rug, set out the food, a quiet shady spot, the perfect day out*。
  - **"黄蜂来袭"词库**：*a few wasps, then dozens / swarms of them, buzzed around the food, waved our arms, tried to drive them away, a wasp nest hung in the tree, stung*。
  - **"狼狈撤退"词库**：*snatched up our things, fled to the car, pursued by an angry swarm, slammed the doors, drove off as fast as we could*。
  - **结构亮点**：第二段开头用 *At first there were only a few wasps, but before long ...*（先抑后扬的转折），把"灾难升级"写得有节奏。
</Callout>

<Callout type="tip" title="参考范文（自己写完再看）">
  It was a fine, sunny day, so we decided to have a picnic. We packed a large hamper with sandwiches, fruit and lemonade, and drove out into the country. After searching for a while, we found a quiet, shady spot beside a stream and unpacked our hampers, certain that nothing could spoil such a perfect afternoon.

  At first there were only a few wasps, but before long a great many appeared and buzzed angrily around the food. We waved our arms and did our best to drive them away, but it was no use. Then we noticed a wasp nest hanging in a tree close by. In a panic, we seized our things, abandoned the rest of the food, and ran back to the car, pursued by the furious swarm. We scrambled inside, slammed the doors and drove away as fast as we could. *(约 150 词)*
</Callout>

## 12. 拓展练习 Going further

<Callout type="tip" title="自学者建议">
  - **影子跟读 (shadowing)**：用第 1 节的三个 SayIt 按钮跟读 3-5 遍。重点模仿结尾 *Oh, is it? ... with a sneer* 那种**先扬后抑、轻蔑反问**的语调。
  - **复述 (retell)**：合上课文，用 1 分钟把故事讲一遍。**三幕结构**：明星乔装野餐（背景）→ 司法长官指着 "No Camping" 赶人（冲突）→ 自报家门反被冷笑（反转点题：disguises can be too perfect）。
  - **造句迁移**：用今天的 5 个高价值结构造你自己的句子——
    1. *S + be dressed in ...*（穿着……）—— *She was dressed in a long red coat.*
    2. *... so that no one would + V*（以便不让……）—— *He whispered so that no one would hear.*
    3. *Why don't we + V?*（提建议）—— *Why don't we take a break?*
    4. *get + 小品词*（一词多义）—— *I can't get over how cold it is. / Let's get on with it.*
    5. *No + V-ing*（公共告示）—— *No Smoking / No Parking / No Entry.*
  - **语法回火**：本课关键是 **What is happening? vs What always happens?**——把课文和练习里的动词分两栏：一栏"此刻正在发生"（is coming, are investigating），一栏"恒常 / 状态"（travels, resembles, sees, loves, prefers）。记牢**状态动词不用进行时**这条铁律，写作就不会再写出 *I am wanting / He is resembling* 这类中式英语。
  - **延伸思考**：这是个一页纸的小幽默，妙就妙在 **"too perfect" 的反讽**——为求隐身而精心伪装，结果连真实身份都无人采信。生活里很多"用力过猛"也是如此：伪装、客套、修饰一旦过头，反而离真实更远。好的写作，懂得给"完美"留一道缝。
</Callout>
