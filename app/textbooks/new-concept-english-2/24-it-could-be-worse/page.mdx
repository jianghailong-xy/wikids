## The Text 课文

I entered the hotel manager's office and sat down. **I had just lost £50** and I felt very **upset**. "I left the money in my room," I said, "and it's not there now." The manager **was sympathetic**, but he **could do nothing**. "**Everyone's losing money these days**," he said. He **started to complain** about this **wicked** world but **was interrupted by a knock at the door**. A girl came in and put an envelope on his desk. **It contained £50.** "I found this **outside this gentleman's room**," she said. "Well," I said to the manager, "**there is still some honesty in this world!**"

> 行号对照（练习里会用到）：l.1 *I entered ... I had just lost* · l.2 *£50 and I felt very upset* · l.3 *it's not there now ... do* · l.4 *nothing ... He started* · l.5 *to complain ... a knock* · l.6 *at the door ... It* · l.7 *contained £50 ... she* · l.8 *said ... honesty in* · l.9 *this world!*

## 课文注释 Notes on the text

<Callout type="info" title="4 个值得停下来嚼一嚼的点">
  1. **标题 It could be worse —— “不幸中之万幸”。** 这是一个**虚拟/假设**的省略说法，完整意思是 *Things could have been worse than they are*（事情本可能更糟）。课文的题目可以有几种不同的理解方式：“如果我丢失的不止 50 英镑”“如果经理没有任何同情心”“如果拾到钱的姑娘没有把钱送回来”——结局都会更糟糕。所以最后失而复得，是 **“不幸中之万幸”**。
  2. **Everyone's losing money these days.** (l.4) —— “现在大家都在丢钱。”这里用**现在进行时**（is losing）表示**不断重复**的动作，**其中常含有埋怨、不满的意思**。同型：*You're always losing your keys!*（你老是丢钥匙！）/ *He's forever asking for money.* —— 进行时 + always / forever / these days，读起来就有“抱怨”的语气。
  3. **was interrupted by a knock at the door** (l.5–6) —— **被动语态**：句子主语是“他（he）”，他是“被打断”的人，所以用 *was interrupted*；动作的发出者由 **by** 引出（*a knock at the door* 一阵敲门声）。对比 MCQ 第 5 题：如果主语换成 *A knock at the door*（敲门声本身），它是“施动者”，就要用**主动** *interrupted him*。
  4. **had just lost** (l.1) / **there is still some honesty** (l.8) —— 一前一后两处时态值得玩味。*had just lost* 是**过去完成时**：在“我走进办公室（过去）”这个时点**之前**，钱已经丢了，是“过去的过去”。结尾的 *there **is** still some honesty* 却切回**一般现在时**——因为这是作者由此悟出的、超越时间的**普遍道理**。
</Callout>

## 3. 关键句型 The past perfect 过去完成时（“过去的过去”）

<Callout type="tip" title="一分钟回顾">
  本课的语法主线是 **过去完成时（had + 过去分词）**——专门用来表示“**在某个过去时间点之前**就已经发生/完成的动作”，也就是“过去的过去”。课文和练习里它无处不在：

  | 句子 | 时间层次 |
  |---|---|
  | *I entered the office. I **had just lost** £50.* | 走进办公室是“过去”；丢钱**更早**（过去之过去）→ had lost |
  | ***Had** the writer's money **been stolen**?* | 过去完成时的**被动**（had been + 过去分词） |
  | *What **had** really **happened**?* | 站在“事后”回看更早的真相 |
  | *he **had lost** / someone **had stolen** / the girl **had stolen*** | MCQ 里都在推测“更早发生了什么” |
  | *Where **had** she **found** it?* | 摘要题：捡到钱在“她进门”之前 |

  ### ⚠️ 4 个本课最容易踩的雷区

  1. **过去完成时必须有“过去参照点”。** *I had just lost £50* 之所以用 had，是因为有 *I entered the office*（过去）作参照。没有参照点时只用一般过去时：*Yesterday I **lost** £50.* ✓（不能无端用 had lost）。
  2. **money / honesty 是不可数名词，作主语用单数动词。** *The money **was** in his room* ✓（不是 were）；*There **is** still some honesty* ✓。
  3. **否定句里用 anything，不用 nothing 再叠一个否定。** *He **could not do anything*** = *He could do **nothing***。两者择一，**别双重否定**（couldn't do nothing ✗）。
  4. **被动还是主动，看主语是“受动者”还是“施动者”。** 主语是 *he*（被打断的人）→ *he **was interrupted***；主语是 *a knock*（打断的来源）→ *a knock **interrupted** him*。
</Callout>

### B 感叹句 What ...! （SD2）

<Callout type="tip" title="结构：What + (a/an) + 形容词 + 名词 + 主语 + 谓语！">
  英语感叹句常用 **What** 引导，表示惊奇、愤怒、赞赏、喜悦。把陈述句改写成 What 感叹句：

  1. This is a wonderful garden! → **What a wonderful garden this is!**
  2. He is causing a lot of trouble! → **What a lot of trouble he is causing!**
  3. It is a tall building! → **What a tall building it is!**
  4. You are a clever girl! → **What a clever girl you are!**

  ⚠️ 主谓**不倒装**（this is，不是 is this）；可数单数名词前别忘了 **a/an**。
</Callout>

### C 直接宾语与间接宾语换位（SD3）

<Callout type="tip" title="give sb. sth. ↔ give sth. to sb. / buy sb. sth. ↔ buy sth. for sb.">
  *He lent me a book.* ↔ *He lent a book to me.* · *She bought me a tie.* ↔ *She bought a tie for me.* 用另一种形式改写：

  1. He handed me the prize. → **He handed the prize to me.**
  2. The waiter brought a bottle of beer to the man. → **The waiter brought the man a bottle of beer.**
  3. I've ordered some soup for you. → **I've ordered you some soup.**
  4. Bring that book to me please. → **Bring me that book please.**
  5. She promised a reward to the finder. → **She promised the finder a reward.**

  ⚠️ 多数动词换位用 **to**（hand/bring/give/promise）；表示“为某人（做）”的用 **for**（buy/order/make）。
</Callout>

### D there be 的两种否定回答（SD9）

<Callout type="tip" title="not ... any  =  no">
  *Is there any tea in the pot?* → *There **isn't any** tea ...* / *There's **no** tea ...* 用两种方式作否定回答：

  1. Have you any money? → **I haven't any money.** / **I have no money.**
  2. Did you go anywhere in the holidays? → **I didn't go anywhere ...** / **I went nowhere ...**
  3. Was there anybody present when the accident happened? → **There wasn't anybody present ...** / **There was nobody present ...**

  ⚠️ **not + any/anybody/anywhere** 与 **no/nobody/nowhere** 等值，二选一，**不要并用**（双重否定）。
</Callout>

### E 所有格撇号的位置（SD13）

<Callout type="tip" title="单数 + 's；复数（以 s 结尾）只加 '">
  回答时注意 ’s 的位置：

  1. Whose umbrella is this? (George) → It's **George's** umbrella.
  2. Whose is this handbag? (That woman) → It's **that woman's** handbag.
  3. Whose poetry do you like best? (Keats) → I like **Keats's** poetry best.
  4. Whose are these clothes? (The children) → They're **the children's** clothes.
  5. Whose are these uniforms? (The soldiers) → They're **the soldiers'** uniforms.

  ⚠️ children 不以 s 结尾 → **children's**；soldiers 以 s 结尾的复数 → 只加撇号 **soldiers'**。
</Callout>

<Callout type="tip" title="G 组：把斜体词的位置后移（代词宾语必须夹在中间）">

## 12. 拓展练习 Going further

<Callout type="tip" title="自学者建议">
  - **影子跟读 (shadowing)**：用第 1 节的三个 SayIt 按钮跟读 3–5 遍。重点体会 *Everyone's losing money these days* 里那种“无奈又埋怨”的语气，以及结尾 *there is still some honesty in this world!* 的释然上扬。
  - **复述 (retell)**：合上课文，用 1 分钟把故事讲一遍。**叙事三段**：起（丢了 £50，去找经理）→ 转（经理同情却无能为力，开始抱怨世道）→ 合（姑娘门外拾金归还，作者感叹“世上仍有诚实”）。
  - **造句迁移**：用今天的 5 个高价值结构造你自己的句子——
    1. *S + had + 过去分词 + when/before + 一般过去*（过去的过去）—— *The train **had** already **left** when I got to the station.*
    2. *S + could do nothing*（无能为力）—— *We **could do nothing** but wait.*
    3. *主语 + be 进行时 + these days/always*（含抱怨）—— *He's **always losing** his umbrella!*
    4. *S + be + interrupted + by ...*（被……打断）—— *Our meeting **was interrupted by** a phone call.*
    5. *There is still some + 不可数名词 + in ...*（……仍然存在）—— *There is still some **hope** in this situation.*
  - **延伸思考**：标题 **It could be worse**（不幸中之万幸）是一种典型的“虚拟语气式宽慰”——把目光从“损失”移到“本可能更糟”。课文用一连串**过去完成时**（had lost / had been stolen / had happened / had found）织出“事后回看”的视角：先让你以为钱被偷了，再用一只“门外捡到的信封”反转，最后落在一句一般现在时的格言上。下次写英文叙事，记住这把“时间尺”：**主线动作用一般过去；比它更早的用过去完成（had + 过去分词）；要点出超越时间的道理，再切回一般现在。**
</Callout>
