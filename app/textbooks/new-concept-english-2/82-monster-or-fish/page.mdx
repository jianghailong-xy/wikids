</Callout>

## The Text 课文

Fishermen and sailors sometimes **claim to have seen monsters** in the sea. Though people have often **laughed at stories told by seamen**, it **is now known** that many of these 'monsters' which **have at times been sighted** are simply strange fish. Occasionally, unusual creatures **are washed to the shore**, but they are rarely caught out at sea. Some time ago, however, **a peculiar fish was caught** near Madagascar. A small fishing boat **was carried miles out to sea** by the powerful fish as it pulled on the line. **Realizing that this was no ordinary fish**, the fisherman **made every effort not to damage it** in any way. When it **was eventually brought to shore**, it was found to be over thirteen feet long. It had a head like a horse, big blue eyes, shining silver skin, and a bright red tail. The fish, which **has since been sent to a museum** where it **is being examined** by a scientist, is called an oarfish. Such creatures **have rarely been seen alive** by man as they live at a depth of six hundred feet.

## 课文注释 Notes on the text

<Callout type="info" title="5 个值得停下来嚼一嚼的点">
  1. **claim to have seen monsters** (l.1) — "声称看见过海里的妖怪"。**claim + 不定式的完成式 (to have + 过去分词)** 表示"声称做过 / 经历过"的动作发生在 claim 之前。同型：*He claims to have met the President. / She claims to have finished the work.* 用完成式不定式，就把"看见"这个动作明确放到了"声称"之前。
  2. **at times** (l.3-4) — "有时，间或"，等于 *sometimes / occasionally*。同型：*At times I feel lonely. / The sea is dangerous at times.* 注意与 *at a time*（每次；一次）区分：*two at a time*（一次两个）。
  3. **out at sea** (l.5-6) — "在海上，在远海"。**out** 强调"远离海岸"。课文 *rarely caught out at sea* = 很少在远海被捕到。对比 *go out to sea*（出海）/ *at sea*（在海上；也可比喻"茫然不知所措"）。
  4. **no ordinary fish** (l.8-9) — "根本不是一条普通的鱼"。**no + 名词**比 *not a + 名词* 否定意味更强、更有强调色彩。*He is no fool.*（他绝不是傻瓜）远比 *He is not a fool* 有力。课文用 *no ordinary fish* 烘托这条鱼有多不寻常。
  5. **made every effort (not) to do** (l.9) — "尽了一切努力（不）去做……"。**make every effort to do** 是固定搭配，等于 *try one's best / do all one can*。同型：*We made every effort to arrive on time. / He made every effort not to wake the baby.* 课文 *made every effort not to damage it* = 千方百计不让它受到损伤。
</Callout>

## 3. 关键句型 The passive voice 被动语态全景

<Callout type="tip" title="一分钟回顾">
  本课的关键句型 (Key structures) 就是 **被动语态**：*A peculiar fish **was caught** near Madagascar.*（复习第 10、34、58 课）。整篇课文几乎是一场"被动语态阅兵"——鱼被捕、船被拖、鱼被运、被研究，重点都不在"谁做"，而在"对鱼发生了什么"。把课文里的被动结构按时态排一排：

  | 时态 / 形式 | 课文例句 | 结构 |
  |---|---|---|
  | **一般现在被动** | *unusual creatures **are washed** to the shore* | am / is / are + 过去分词 |
  | **一般过去被动** | *a peculiar fish **was caught** / a boat **was carried*** | was / were + 过去分词 |
  | **现在完成被动** | *monsters which have **been sighted** / have **been seen** alive* | have / has been + 过去分词 |
  | **现在进行被动** | *it **is being examined** by a scientist* | am / is / are being + 过去分词 |
  | **被动 + 不定式** | *it **was found to be** over thirteen feet long* | be + 过去分词 + to do |

  ### ⚠️ 4 个本课最容易踩的雷区

  1. **被动语态 = be 的相应时态 + 过去分词**。时态体现在 be 上，动词永远用**过去分词**：*was caught*（不是 was catch / was catched）。
  2. **现在进行被动**用 **is/are being + 过去分词**：*it is being examined*（正在被研究）。别漏掉 being，也别写成 is examining（那是主动"它在研究"）。
  3. **现在完成被动**用 **have/has been + 过去分词**：*have been sighted / has been sent*。三个词缺一不可。
  4. **by + 动作执行者**：当需要点明"被谁"时才加 *by ...*：*carried ... by the powerful fish / examined by a scientist*。不需要时就省略——这正是被动语态的好处。
</Callout>

## 4. 难点：易混词 laugh / laugh at · wash / wash up

<Callout type="info" title="一个介词 / 一个小词，意思全变">
  教材 *Special difficulties* 部分讲了两组"经常容易混淆的词"。差别就在一个介词或一个 up 上：

  **① laugh（笑）vs laugh at（嘲笑 / 取笑）**
  - *laugh*（不及物）= 发笑：*Everybody **laughed** when the circus clown made his appearance.*（小丑出场，大家都笑了。）
  - *laugh at sb./sth.*（及物短语）= 嘲笑、取笑某人某物 (= make fun of)：*Though people have often **laughed at** stories told by seamen ...*（人们常对水手讲的故事付之一笑……）
  - 区别：单纯"觉得好笑"用 laugh；带"拿……开玩笑、瞧不起"的味道，必须加 **at**。

  **② wash（洗）vs wash up（洗碗）**
  - *wash*（及物 / 反身）= 洗（手、衣、自己）：*I must **wash** my hands.* / *Have you ever seen a cat **washing** itself?*
  - *be washed (to / overboard)* = 被水冲（到某处 / 卷下船）：*Unusual creatures **are washed** to the shore.*（被水冲到岸上。）/ *The man **was washed** overboard by a big wave.*（被大浪从船上卷入水中。）
  - *wash up* = 洗餐具、洗碗 (= wash the dishes)：*I'll **wash up** tonight.*（今晚我洗碗。）
  - 区别：洗手洗脸洗东西用 wash；专指"饭后洗碗碟"用 **wash up**；"被水冲"用被动 **be washed**。

## 9. 句子合并 Sentence structure

<Callout type="info" title="任务">
  Join these two sentences, then check your answer against the text (ll.11-12).
  连接以下句子，然后对照课文第 11-12 行核对你的答案：

  *The fish is called an oarfish. It has since been sent to a museum. It is being examined by a scientist.*
</Callout>

<Callout type="tip" title="参考答案与思路（自己合完再看）">
  **The fish, which has since been sent to a museum where it is being examined by a scientist, is called an oarfish.**

  - 主句保留 *The fish ... is called an oarfish*（鱼叫桨鱼）。
  - 把"已被送往博物馆"降级为**非限定性定语从句** *which has since been sent to a museum*，前后用逗号夹住。
  - 再把"正在被研究"用 **where (= in which)** 接到 museum 后：*where it is being examined by a scientist*。
  - 三句话由此套成一句，主次分明——这正是课文原句的写法。
</Callout>

## 11. 摘要写作 Summary writing (≤ 80 词)

<Callout type="info" title="任务">
  *Give an account of what happened in not more than 80 words.* 用不超过 80 词描述所发生的事，按下面 10 个要点串成两段（教材给出的连词写在括号里）：

  1. Strange fish caught — Madagascar.
  2. Fishing boat — out to sea. *(Though ...)*
  3. Effort — damage. *(Making ...)*
  4. Fisherman — brought it to shore. *(after having pulled ...; On being brought ...)*
  5. Thirteen feet. *(The fish, which ...)*
  6. Head like horse. *(with ...; and ...)*
  7. Sent museum. *(Now that ...)*
  8. Examined — scientist. *(It ... who said ...)*
  9. Oarfish. *(It ...)*
  10. Six hundred feet. *(and ...)*
</Callout>

<Callout type="tip" title="参考范文（自己写完再看）">
  A strange fish was caught near Madagascar. Though it dragged the fishing boat out to sea, the fisherman made every effort not to damage it. After it had been brought to shore, the fish was found to be thirteen feet long, with a head like a horse. It has since been sent to a museum, where it is being examined by a scientist. It is called an oarfish, and such creatures live at a depth of six hundred feet. *(78 words)*
</Callout>

## 12. 作文 Composition (约 150 词)

<Callout type="info" title="任务">
  Write two paragraphs in about 150 words using the ideas given below. 按以下思路写出两段，约 150 词：

  1. A man in a bar — explaining to others how he caught a big fish — rough seas — great difficulty — boat carried out to sea.
  2. After several hours he pulled the fish up — never seen before — its size, appearance and colours — but it got away.
</Callout>

<Callout type="warning" title="提示骨架">
  - **第一段（铺垫）**：地点（酒吧）+ 听众 + 那天的天气（风浪大）+ 鱼上钩 + 力气大到把船拖出海。
  - **第二段（高潮 + 反转）**：搏斗几小时后把鱼拖出水面 → 从没见过的怪鱼 → 大小、模样、颜色 → 正要拉上船，鱼却挣脱跑了。
</Callout>

<Callout type="tip" title="写作脚手架">
  - **时态主线**：一般过去时叙事；过去进行表背景 (*the wind was blowing, the waves were rising*)；过去完成表更早 (*had been fighting for hours*)。
  - **"风浪"词库**：*rough seas, towering waves, a howling wind, the boat tossed about, spray lashed his face*。
  - **"搏斗"词库**：*the line went tight, the rod bent double, he held on with all his strength, the fish pulled the boat miles out to sea, fought for hours*。
  - **"怪鱼"词库**：*a head like a horse, shining silver skin, big blue eyes, a bright red tail, over thirteen feet long, like nothing he had ever seen*。
  - **"逃脱"反转**：*just as he reached for it, the line snapped / it gave one last twist, slipped free and vanished into the deep*。
</Callout>

<Callout type="tip" title="参考范文（自己写完再看）">
  One evening in a small harbour bar, an old fisherman told us how he had once hooked a giant fish. The seas had been rough that day, he said; a howling wind drove towering waves against his little boat. Suddenly the line went tight and the rod bent double. The fish was so powerful that it dragged the boat miles out to sea, and he could do nothing but hold on.

  He fought the fish for several hours. At last he managed to pull it up to the surface — and stared in amazement. It was like nothing he had ever seen: over thirteen feet long, with a head like a horse, shining silver skin, big blue eyes and a bright red tail. But just as he reached out to haul it aboard, the great fish gave one last twist, snapped the line and disappeared into the deep. *(146 words)*
</Callout>

## 13. 书信写作 Letter writing

<Callout type="info" title="任务">
  教材本课练习"结束语 (The Conclusion)"。先熟记这两句常用结束语：
  - *I shall be looking forward to hearing from / seeing you soon.*（盼早日回信 / 相见。）
  - *I hope you will soon settle down in your (new job, school, etc.).*（希望你早日适应你的新工作 / 新学校。）

  **Exercise:** 根据下面给出的结束语，为这封信写一段约 50 词的"目的 (Purpose)"。其结束语为：
  *'I hope you feel better soon.'*（希望你早日康复。）
</Callout>

<Callout type="tip" title="写作脚手架">
  - 既然结束语是"祝早日康复"，那么信的"目的"应是**写给一位生病 / 住院 / 出意外的朋友**——表达问候、关心、并送上鼓励。
  - **开头点明缘由**：*I was so sorry to hear that you ...*（听说你……我十分难过）。
  - **主体串 3 点**：① 怎么得知的（from your mother / from a mutual friend）；② 表达关心与不能去看望的歉意；③ 给点宽慰（好好休息、不要担心功课 / 工作）。
  - **时态**：现在完成"刚得知" (*have just heard*) + 一般现在表关心 (*I do hope*) + 一般将来 (*I'll come and see you as soon as ...*)。
</Callout>

<Callout type="tip" title="参考范文（约 50 词，自己写完再看）">
  I was very sorry to hear from your sister that you had been taken to hospital after your accident. It must have been a great shock. Please don't worry about your studies — I'll bring you all the notes and come to see you as soon as visitors are allowed. *(50 words)*
</Callout>

## 14. 拓展练习 Going further

<Callout type="tip" title="自学者建议">
  - **影子跟读 (shadowing)**：用第 1 节的三个 SayIt 按钮跟读 3-5 遍，重点感受被动结构的节奏——*was caught / was carried / has been sent / is being examined*，让 be 动词与过去分词连成一气。
  - **复述 (retell)**：合上课文，用 1-2 分钟把这条怪鱼的故事讲一遍。**叙事三段**：背景（水手讲"妖怪"，多是奇鱼）→ 经过（马达加斯加捕到怪鱼 → 拖船出海 → 竭力保全 → 拖上岸 13 英尺）→ 结局（送博物馆研究 → 名叫桨鱼 → 深居 600 英尺）。
  - **造句迁移**：用今天的 5 个高价值结构造你自己的句子——
    1. *claim to have done sth.*（声称做过……）—— *He claims to have met the author.*
    2. *S + be + 过去分词（被动）*—— *The window was broken by the storm.*
    3. *S + is being + 过去分词（现在进行被动）*—— *The road is being repaired now.*
    4. *Realizing / Knowing that ..., S + V*（现在分词作状语）—— *Realizing that he was late, he ran to the station.*
    5. *make every effort (not) to do*（尽一切努力去 / 不去做）—— *They made every effort to save the patient.*
  - **延伸思考**：本课是一篇典型的"破除迷信"小品——开头抛出"海里的妖怪"，结尾用科学（送博物馆、由科学家研究、定名 oarfish）给出答案。读完想一想：作者全篇大量使用**被动语态**，把焦点始终对准"那条鱼"而非"渔夫 / 科学家"，正是为了让"鱼"成为叙事的真正主角。下次你写说明性、报道性的英文段落时，也可以借助被动语态来**突出对象、淡化动作者**。
</Callout>
