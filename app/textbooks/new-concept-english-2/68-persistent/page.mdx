## The Text 课文

I crossed the street to **avoid meeting** him, but he saw me and came **running** towards me. **It was no use pretending** that I had not seen him, so I waved to him. I never **enjoy meeting** Nigel Dykes. He never has anything to do. **No matter how busy you are**, he always **insists on coming** with you. I had to think of a way of **preventing him from following** me around all morning.

'Hello, Nigel,' I said. '**Fancy meeting** you here!'

'Hi, Elizabeth,' Nigel answered. 'I was just **wondering how to spend** the morning — until I saw you. You're not **busy doing** anything, are you?'

'No, not at all,' I answered. 'I'm going to...'

'**Would you mind my coming** with you?' he asked, before I had finished **speaking**.

'Not at all,' I lied, 'but I'm going to the dentist.'

'Then I'll come with you,' he answered. 'There's always plenty to read in **the waiting room**!'

## 课文注释 Notes on the text

<Callout type="info" title="9 个值得停下来嚼一嚼的点">
  1. **avoid + -ing** (l.1) — "避免做……"。**avoid 后必须接动名词**（不是不定式！），与 enjoy, fancy, mind, finish, deny 等动词一样。课文 *to avoid meeting him* = 为了避开见他。同型：*avoid driving in the rain / avoid talking about it*。❌ 绝不能说 *avoid to meet*。

  2. **came + running** (l.1) — "跑着过来"。**running 是现在分词作方式状语**，描述"来"的伴随方式。类似的 **go/come + -ing** 结构在英文里极其高频：*go swimming / go shopping / come dancing* ——都是用一个动词（来 / 去）搭一个 -ing（干什么）。本课关键结构 §d 专门讲这个。

  3. **It was no use + -ing** (l.2) — "做……是没有用的"。**it** 是形式主语，真正主语是后面的动名词短语 *pretending that I had not seen him*。同型：*It's no use crying over spilt milk.*（覆水难收）/ *It's no use trying to change his mind.*。同类结构：**It's not worth + -ing**（不值得做……）—— 练习第 4 题考的就是这个。

  4. **No matter + 疑问词 + 从句** (l.4) — "不管……"。**no matter** 后接 **how / when / where / what / who** 等疑问词，构成**让步状语从句**。课文 *No matter how busy you are* = 不管你多忙。同型：*No matter what you say, I won't believe you. / No matter where you go, I'll follow you.* 这个结构等价于 **However / Whatever / Wherever + 从句**。本课句型改写题考的就是 *It doesn't matter whether ...* → *No matter ...*。

  5. **insist on + -ing** (l.5) — "坚持要做……"。**insist** 后接介词 **on**，再接动名词。同型：*He insists on paying the bill. / She insisted on coming with us.*。注意 **insist** 还可以接 **that 从句**（用 should + 原形，或省略 should 用虚拟语气）：*He insisted that she (should) go.* —— 练习第 6 题考的就是这个替代用法。

  6. **prevent sb. from + -ing** (l.5-6) — "阻止某人做……"。**from 可以省略** —— *prevent him (from) following me* 也正确。同类搭配：**stop sb. from doing / keep sb. from doing**（阻止某人做）。注意与 **forbid sb. to do** 的区别（forbid 接不定式，不是 -ing）。

  7. **Fancy + -ing!** (l.7) — "真想不到……！"。**fancy** 在这里不是"想象、喜欢"，而是**感叹用法**——表"惊讶"。后接**动名词**。课文 *Fancy meeting you here!* = 真想不到在这儿遇见你！相当于 *Imagine meeting you here!* —— 练习第 11 题考的就是这个同义替换。

  8. **be busy + -ing** (l.9) — "忙着做……"。**busy** 后接 **(in) + 动名词**（in 常省略）。课文 *You're not busy doing anything, are you?* 是个**反义疑问句**——前面否定 (*not busy*)，后面用肯定反问 (*are you?*)。同型：*He's busy preparing for the exam. / I'm busy writing a report.*

  9. **Would you mind + 所有格 + -ing?** (l.11) — "你介意我……吗？"——这是**征求意见**的经典句型。**my** 是动名词 **coming** 的**逻辑主语**（= Would you mind if I come with you?）。动名词的逻辑主语通常用**所有格**（my / your / his / her），但口语中也可用**宾格**（me / you / him / her）。本课关键结构 §b 专门比较：*Would you mind opening the window?*（你介意开窗吗？）vs *Would you mind my opening the window?*（你介意我开窗吗？）。
</Callout>

## 3. 关键句型：动名词的 8 种用法 Gerunds

<Callout type="tip" title="一分钟回顾">
  本课是英语教材里**动名词（Gerund）**的经典训练课——一篇不到 200 词的短文中出现了 **10+ 处 -ing 形式**，覆盖了动名词的几乎所有核心用法。把课文里所有 -ing 动词圈出来，你会发现一张完整的"动名词用法地图"：

  | 用法 | 课文例句 | 规则 |
  |---|---|---|
  | **① 作动词宾语** | *avoid meeting / enjoy meeting / mind my coming / finished speaking* | avoid / enjoy / fancy / mind / finish / deny 等动词**必须接动名词**（不接不定式） |
  | **② 介词 + 动名词** | *insists **on coming** / way **of preventing** / prevent him **from following*** | 介词后**只能用 -ing**（不能接 to do） |
  | **③ 形式主语 it + -ing** | ***It was no use pretending*** that I had not seen him. | it 作形式主语，真正主语是后面的 -ing 短语 |
  | **④ 现在分词作状语** | *came **running** towards me* | 描述"来"的伴随方式（方式状语） |
  | **⑤ Go / Come + -ing** | *go shopping / come swimming / go dancing* | 表示"去做某项活动"，-ing 是现在分词的非谓语用法 |
  | **⑥ 感官动词 + 宾语 + -ing** | *I **saw him coming**. / I **heard her singing**.* | see / hear / smell + 宾语 + -ing 表示"看到/听到……正在做" |
  | **⑦ 动名词的逻辑主语** | *Would you mind **my coming** with you?* | 所有格（my / your / his）或宾格（me / you / him）放在 -ing 前，说明**谁**在做 |
  | **⑧ It's worth + -ing** | *It's **not worth worrying** about.* | 同 "it's no use + -ing" 模式，worth 后必须接 -ing |

  ### ⚠️ 5 个本课最容易踩的雷区

  1. **avoid to do** ❌——这是中国学习者最高频的错误之一。记住：**avoid + doing** ✓，**never avoid + to do**。
  2. **insist on to go** ❌——介词 on 后只能接 -ing，不能接不定式。**insist on going** ✓。
  3. **动名词逻辑主语的位置**——*Would you mind my coming?* 里的 **my**（不是 me / I / mine）用在正式文体中。口语中可以用 me，但**所有格 my 是"教科书标准"**。注意对比：*Would you mind opening the window?*（你开窗；my 不存在） vs *Would you mind **my** opening the window?*（我开窗；my 是 coming 的逻辑主语）——有没有 my 意思完全不同！
  4. **It's no use to cry** ❌ —— 必须是 **It's no use + -ing**。同理：**It's (not) worth + -ing**（不是 to do）。
  5. **感官动词 + 宾语 + 动词原形 vs -ing**：*I saw him cross the street*（看到全过程——他穿过了马路）vs *I saw him crossing the street*（看到进行中的片段——他正在穿马路）。本课 *I saw him coming* 用的是 -ing——强调"正在过来"这个**进行中的画面**。
</Callout>

## 4. 难点：感官动词 + 宾语 + -ing ｜ Go/Come + -ing

<Callout type="info" title="两类特殊 -ing 结构">

  ### See / Hear / Smell + 宾语 + -ing ——"感官正在捕捉"
  | 例句 | 中文 | 要点 |
  |---|---|---|
  | *I **saw him coming**.* | 我看见他过来了。 | see + 宾语 + 现在分词 → "正在做"的片段 |
  | *He **heard me opening** the door.* | 他听见我开门。 | hear + 宾语 + -ing |
  | *I can **smell something burning**.* | 我闻到有东西烧焦了。 | smell + 宾语 + -ing |

  ⚠️ 感官动词还可以接**动词原形**（不带 to）：*I saw him come*（看见他来了——全过程）vs *I saw him coming*（看见他正在过来——进行中的画面）。-ing 强调"当时正在发生"的动态感。

  ### Go / Come + -ing ——"去 / 来做某事"
  | 例句 | 中文 |
  |---|---|
  | *Let's **go dancing**.* | 我们去跳舞吧。 |
  | *I **went shopping** this morning.* | 今天早上我去购物了。 |
  | *Come **swimming** with me tomorrow.* | 明天跟我一起去游泳吧。 |

  这个结构里的 -ing 大多是**体育 / 休闲 / 日常活动**类动词：shopping, swimming, dancing, fishing, skiing, hiking, running, climbing, sightseeing……不是所有动词都能套。

  ### ⚠️ 4 个常见雷区

  1. 感官动词 + 宾语 + **to do** ❌ —— 中文"我看见他去做某事"容易误导，英文是 see sb. **do / doing**，不是 to do。
  2. *go to shop* ≠ *go shopping*——go to shop 是"去商店"，go shopping 是"去购物（一种活动）"——含义完全不同。
  3. *come to dance*（来是为了跳舞——不定式表目的）≠ *come dancing*（来跳舞——-ing 表活动方式）——虽然都通，但语感不同。
  4. 不是所有动词都能套 go/come + -ing —— go working / go writing 不自然（work 和 write 不是休闲活动类动词）。
</Callout>

## 11. 作文 Composition

<Callout type="info" title="任务">
  Rewrite the sentences below using the joining words in parentheses:

  1. There was a dentist nearby. She knew him well. Elizabeth decided to go there. She would explain what had happened. **(As ... whom ... in order to)**

  2. Nigel was in the waiting room. Elizabeth spoke to the dentist. He told her to leave at once through another door. **(While ... and)**

  3. Elizabeth left. The dentist went into the waiting room. 'Do you want to have your teeth examined?' he asked Nigel. **(After ... and asked Nigel if...)**
</Callout>

<Callout type="warning" title="提示骨架">
  - 第 1 句：**As** there was a dentist nearby + **whom** she knew well → Elizabeth decided to go there **in order to** explain what had happened.
  - 第 2 句：**While** Nigel was in the waiting room → Elizabeth spoke to the dentist **and** he told her to leave at once through another door.
  - 第 3 句：**After** Elizabeth left → the dentist went into the waiting room **and asked Nigel if** he wanted to have his teeth examined.
</Callout>

<Callout type="tip" title="写作脚手架">
  - **时态主线**：一般过去时叙事（knew, decided, spoke, told, left, went, asked）。
  - **从句嵌套**：第 1 句是最复杂的——as 引导原因状语 + whom 引导定语从句 + in order to 表目的——三个连接词把四个简单句串成一句"高级复合句"。
  - **直接引语 → 间接引语**：第 3 句把 Nigel 被问的原句变成 *asked Nigel if he wanted to...*（间接疑问句，陈述语序）。
</Callout>

<Callout type="tip" title="参考范文（自己写完再看）">
  1. As there was a dentist nearby whom she knew well, Elizabeth decided to go there in order to explain what had happened.

  2. While Nigel was in the waiting room, Elizabeth spoke to the dentist and he told her to leave at once through another door.

  3. After Elizabeth left, the dentist went into the waiting room and asked Nigel if he wanted to have his teeth examined.
</Callout>

## 12. 书信写作 Letter writing

<Callout type="info" title="任务">
  Write four sentences beginning with each of the following:

  - **You must be very annoyed with me for ...**
  - **I have just heard that ...**
  - **I really hate to complain, but ...**
  - **Some time ago, I ...**
</Callout>

<Callout type="tip" title="写作脚手架">
  - 这四句是**道歉 / 抱怨信**的经典开头句型。想象你写信给一位朋友，为自己最近的行为道歉或解释。
  - 时态混搭：*You must be...*（情态动词表推测，现在）/ *I have just heard...*（现在完成时——刚听说）/ *I really hate to complain...*（一般现在时——表明态度）/ *Some time ago, I...*（一般过去时——讲述过去的事）。
  - 每句都是一个独立的情景开场句——后面可以各自展开一个段落。
</Callout>

<Callout type="tip" title="参考范文（自己写完再看）">
  You must be very annoyed with me for not replying to your letter sooner — I have been away on business for the past two weeks.

  I have just heard that you passed the driving test on your first attempt. Congratulations!

  I really hate to complain, but the parcel you sent arrived with its contents badly damaged.

  Some time ago, I promised that I would visit you in Cambridge this summer.
</Callout>

## 13. 拓展练习 Going further

<Callout type="tip" title="自学者建议">
  - **影子跟读 (shadowing)**：用第 1 节三个 SayIt 按钮跟读 3-5 遍。重点感受几个 -ing 形式的自然连读——*came**running***、*avoid**meeting***、*insists on**coming***、*Would you mind my**coming***——这些 -ing 短语是英语节奏的"呼吸点"，跟读时不要断开。
  - **复述 (retell)**：合上课文，把 Elizabeth 和 Nigel 的故事用 1 分钟讲一遍。**叙事结构**：躲 → 被发现 → 挥手 → 内心吐槽 → 撒谎说去牙医 → Nigel 跟去。重点感受**第一人称内心独白**（*I never enjoy meeting Nigel Dykes. He never has anything to do...*）如何在一篇叙事短文中建立"读者同盟"（reader alignment）——我们被拉进 Elizabeth 的视角，和她一起感到被 Nigel 纠缠的无奈。
  - **造句迁移**：用今天的 5 个 -ing 结构造你自己的句子——
    1. *avoid + -ing* —— *I always avoid driving during the rush hour.*（教材 Key Structures 例句）
    2. *It's no use + -ing* —— *It's no use arguing with him. He never changes his mind.*
    3. *prevent sb. from + -ing* —— *The heavy rain prevented us from going out.*
    4. *Would you mind + 所有格 + -ing* —— *Would you mind my sitting here?*
    5. *go / come + -ing* —— *Let's go hiking this weekend!*
  - **延伸思考**：本课是一篇**第一人称叙事的幽默小品**——全篇不到 200 词，却包含了完整的"情景喜剧式"结构：Setup（Nigel 的性格特征）→ Conflict（Elizabeth 被黏上）→ Attempted Solution（撒谎说去牙医）→ Punchline（Nigel 说候诊室有书看，跟定了）。而本课的语法主线——**动名词**——恰恰是构成这种**轻快叙事节奏**的语法工具：*-ing 形式*本身带有"进行中 / 持续中"的语感，让整篇读起来有种"事情正在发生"的动态画面感。下次你写英文叙事时，试试像本课这样——**用 -ing 形式串联全篇**，让你的文字"跑起来"。
</Callout>
