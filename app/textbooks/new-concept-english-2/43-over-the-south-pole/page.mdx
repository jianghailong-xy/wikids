  1929 年，美国探险家 **R.E. Byrd**（伯德）要做一件从没有人做成过的事——**驾飞机飞越南极**。起飞、拍照都还顺利，可飞机很快撞上了一道难关：前方是连绵群山，飞机**必须爬升到 10,000 英尺才能翻越**，否则就要坠毁。千钧一发之际，Byrd 下了一道命令……最后飞机竟在**离山头仅 400 英尺**的高度擦了过去。他到底做了什么，让一架眼看要坠毁的飞机重新爬升？
  **How was the plane able to clear the mountains?**（飞机是怎样得以越过群山的？）带着这个问题读课文，并特别留意全篇是怎样用 **can / could / be able to** 这一组"能力"表达，去区分"有没有能力"和"最终有没有做成"。
</Callout>

## The Text 课文

In 1929, three years after his **flight over the North Pole**, the American explorer, R.E. Byrd, successfully **flew over the South Pole** for the first time. Though, at first, Byrd and his men **were able to take** a great many photographs of the mountains that lay below, they soon **ran into serious trouble**. At one point, **it seemed certain** that their plane would crash. **It could only get over the mountains if it rose to 10,000 feet.** Byrd **at once ordered his men to throw out** two heavy food sacks. The plane **was then able to rise** and it **cleared the mountains by 400 feet**. Byrd now knew that he **would be able to reach** the South Pole which was 300 miles away, for there were **no more mountains in sight**. The aircraft **was able to fly over** the endless white plains without difficulty.

## 课文注释 Notes on the text

<Callout type="info" title="7 个值得停下来嚼一嚼的点">
  1. **the North Pole / the South Pole** (title, l.1) — "北极 / 南极"。专有名词，**两个极都要加 the 并大写**。同型：*the Equator*（赤道）、*the Antarctic*（南极地区）、*the Arctic*（北极地区）。
  2. **a great many + 可数名词复数** (l.4) — "大量的、许许多多的"。**a great many** 后只接**可数名词复数**：*a great many photographs / a great many people*。注意区别 *a great deal of + 不可数*（a great deal of money）。
  3. **run into serious trouble** (l.5) — "陷入严重困境"。**run into** = 偶然/突然遇上（不好的事），相当于 *meet with / encounter*。同型：*run into difficulties / run into debt / run into an old friend*（偶遇老友）。
  4. **at one point** (l.5) — "（一度）在某一刻 / 某个地方"。这里指时间上的"一度、有那么一会儿"。
  5. **it seemed certain that ...** (l.5) — "看来肯定会……"。**seem + 形容词 + that 从句**，*it* 是形式主语。同型：*It seems likely that ... / It seemed clear that ...*。
  6. **get over the mountains** (l.6) — "越过群山"。**get over** 在这里是"翻越、越过"；它还有"克服（困难）/（病）痊愈"的意思：*get over a difficulty / get over an illness*。
  7. **clear the mountains by 400 feet** (l.8) — "在离山头 400 英尺的高度飞越过去"。**clear** 作动词 = "（不接触地）越过、避开"；**by + 数量**表示"相差……、以……之差"。同型：*The horse cleared the fence by an inch. / He missed the train by two minutes.*
</Callout>

<Callout type="tip" title="本课语法主线：can / could / be able to">
  全篇像一张 *can vs be able to* 的"用法地图"，把课文里的"能力"动词圈出来：

  | 课文原句 | 形式 | 含义 |
  |---|---|---|
  | Byrd and his men **were able to take** photographs | was/were able to | 过去**成功做成**了某事（= managed to） |
  | It **could only get** over the mountains **if** it rose ... | could + 条件句 | **假设性**的能力（还没做到） |
  | The plane **was then able to rise** | was able to | 真的**成功**升起来了（完成） |
  | he **would be able to reach** the South Pole | would be able to | 站在过去看"将来能够……" |
  | The aircraft **was able to fly** over the plains | was able to | 顺利**飞过去了**（完成） |

  ⚠️ **关键区别**：表示"过去某一次具体地、成功地做成了某事"时，**必须用 was/were able to（或 managed to），不能用 could**。*could* 只表示"曾具备某种能力"，不保证"做成了"。
</Callout>

## 3. 关键句型 Can / Could / Be able to

<Callout type="tip" title="一分钟回顾：能力的三种说法">
  英语里表"能力 / 能够"主要有三套工具，课文几乎全用上了：

  | 表达 | 用在哪 | 例句 |
  |---|---|---|
  | **can / could** | 一般能力；could 表过去的"曾能" | *Can I use your telephone? / He could swim at five.* |
  | **be able to** | 各种时态都能用，**没有时态限制** | *He will come if he **is able to**. / I **wasn't able to** understand him.* |
  | **was/were able to** | **过去某一次成功做成**（= managed to） | *I **was able to** persuade him.（最终说服了他）* |

  ### ⚠️ 全课最容易踩的雷区：could ≠ was able to

  | 想表达 | 对 ✓ | 错 ✗ |
  |---|---|---|
  | 过去**具体一次成功**做成 | The plane **was able to** rise. | ~~The plane **could** rise.~~ |
  | 过去**普遍的能力** | When young, Byrd **could** fly any plane. | （这里 could 正确） |
  | 否定句（没做到） | I **couldn't** understand him. ✓ = I **wasn't able to** ... ✓ | 否定时两者都行 |

  口诀：**肯定句 + 一次性成功 → 只能 was/were able to；could 表"曾有能力"，不保证"做成了"。**

  ### 顺带一个"间接命令"句型（教材 Sentence structure 练习）

  把直接命令改成间接命令：*"Throw out two heavy food sacks at once!" Byrd told his men.* → **Byrd at once ordered his men to throw out two heavy food sacks.**（*order sb. to do sth.* 命令某人做某事）
</Callout>

## 4. 难点：含有 at 的词组 Phrases with "at"

<Callout type="info" title="一串高频 at 短语，一次记牢">
  课文里有 *at first*（起初）和 *at once*（立刻）两个 **at 短语**。教材的 *Special difficulties* 把这一族短语集中讲了一遍——它们多是固定搭配，**意思不能从字面拼**：

  | 短语 | 意思 | 例句 |
  |---|---|---|
  | **at first** | 起初、最初 | *At first Byrd and his men were able to take photographs.* |
  | **at once** | 立刻、马上 | *Byrd at once ordered his men to throw out two food sacks.* |
  | **at present** | 目前、现在 | *Billy is not at home at present.* |
  | **at home** | 在家 | *He's not at home; he's at school.* |
  | **at school** | 在上学、在学校 | *He's at school.* |
  | **at last** | 终于、最终 | *We arrived at the village at last.* |
  | **at any rate** | 无论如何、至少 | *At any rate you'll be able to hear it on the radio.* |
  | **at heart** | 内心里、本质上 | *He's a very pleasant person at heart.* |
  | **at least** | 至少 | *At least you could have telephoned me.* |
  | **at times** | 有时、间或 | *He behaves very strangely at times.* |
  | **at a loss** | 不知所措、茫然 | *I'm completely at a loss.* |

## 11. 作文 Composition：连词改写

<Callout type="info" title="任务">
  *Rewrite the following sentences using the joining words (conjunctions) in parentheses.*（用括号中的连词改写下列句子。）

  1. Byrd stayed in the Antarctic for a year. He made many more flights. *(and)*
  2. He went back to America in 1930. He returned to the Antarctic in 1946. *(but)*
  3. This time he had 4,000 men with him. He had thirteen ships and seventeen aeroplanes. *(not only ... but ... as well)*
</Callout>

<Callout type="warning" title="提示骨架">
  - **and**：两句主语相同（he/Byrd）时，第二句可省略主语，直接 *and made ...*。
  - **but**：表转折，连接两个对照的事实——\"先回美国，后又重返南极\"。
  - **not only ... but ... as well**：*not only* 接第一组信息，*but ... as well* 接第二组（= but also）；注意 *as well* 放句末。
</Callout>

<Callout type="tip" title="参考答案（自己写完再看）">
  1. Byrd stayed in the Antarctic for a year **and** made many more flights.
  2. He went back to America in 1930 **but** returned to the Antarctic in 1946.
  3. This time he had **not only** 4,000 men with him **but** thirteen ships and seventeen aeroplanes **as well**.
</Callout>

<Callout type="tip" title="写作脚手架：英文信封 / 信头地址的写法">
  **英语地址书写顺序与中文相反——由小到大**：门牌号 + 街名 → 城市/区 → 邮编 → （国名）。寄信人地址写在信纸**右上角**，下面紧跟日期。

  - **本国朋友（信头地址，右上角）**：
    > 15 Park Road
    > Cambridge CB2 1RX
    >
    > 12th May, 2026

  - **寄往国外的收信人地址（信封）**——最后一行写**国名**（大写）：
    > Mr. John Smith
    > 24 Bridge Street
    > Oxford OX1 2EP
    > ENGLAND

  ⚠️ 要点：① 街名/门牌**门牌号在前**（15 Park Road，不是 Park Road 15）；② **由小到大**层层放大；③ 寄往国外**必须写国名**且常大写；④ 日期英式写法 *12th May, 2026*，美式 *May 12, 2026*。
</Callout>

## 13. 拓展练习 Going further

<Callout type="tip" title="自学者建议">
  - **影子跟读 (shadowing)**：用第 1 节的三个 SayIt 按钮跟读 3-5 遍。重点感受让步句 *Though, at first, ... they soon ran into serious trouble* 的语调起伏——前半\"顺利\"读得平稳，*soon ran into serious trouble* 读出转折的下沉感。
  - **复述 (retell)**：合上课文，用 1 分钟把伯德飞越南极的故事讲一遍。**叙事三段**：背景（1929 年，飞越北极 3 年后第一次飞越南极）→ 危机（拍照顺利 → 群山挡路 → 需升到 10,000 英尺否则坠毁 → 扔袋减重 → 飞机升起，离山头 400 英尺越过）→ 结局（前方无山，毫无困难飞过白色原野）。
  - **造句迁移**：用今天的 5 个高价值结构造你自己的句子——
    1. *was/were able to + V*（过去一次性成功）—— *After hours of work, we were able to fix the engine.*
    2. *Though, at first, ..., S soon ...*（先扬后抑的让步）—— *Though, at first, the plan worked, we soon ran into trouble.*
    3. *S could only ... if ...*（条件性能力）—— *We could only finish in time if everyone helped.*
    4. *order sb. to do sth. at once*（立刻命令）—— *The captain at once ordered the men to lower the boats.*
    5. *clear ... by + 数量*（以……之差越过/避开）—— *The plane cleared the trees by just ten metres.*
  - **延伸思考**：本课是 *can / could / be able to* 的活教材。下次写英文叙事，遇到\"过去做成了某件难事\"，先问自己：**是\"曾有能力\"还是\"那一次真的做成了\"？** 前者用 *could*，后者用 *was/were able to*。一字之差，读者就能分清\"有本事\"和\"成了事\"。另外留意全篇怎样用 *at first → soon → then → now → without difficulty* 这串时间副词，把一次惊险飞行的节奏一层层推进——这正是叙事英语\"有镜头感\"的秘诀。
</Callout>
