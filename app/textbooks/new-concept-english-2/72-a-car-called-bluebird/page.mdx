  **What mistake was made? 出了什么差错？** 带着这个问题听录音、读课文，重点留意全篇是怎么用 **过去完成时（含被动）had been built / had been made / had been 301 ...** 来标记“比过去更早”的那一层时间的。
</Callout>

## The Text 课文

The great racing driver, Sir Malcolm Campbell, **was the first man to drive at over 300 miles per hour**. He **set up a new world record** in September 1935 at Bonneville Salt Flats, Utah. *Bluebird*, the car he was driving, **had been specially built for him**. It was **over 30 feet in length** and had a **2,500-horsepower engine**. Although Campbell reached a speed of over 304 miles per hour, he **had great difficulty in controlling the car** because **a tyre burst during the first run**. After his attempt, Campbell was **disappointed to learn** that his average speed had been 299 miles per hour. However, a few days later, he was told that **a mistake had been made**. His average speed had been 301 miles per hour. Since that time, racing drivers **have reached speeds of over 600 miles an hour**. **Following in his father's footsteps** many years later, Sir Malcolm's son, Donald, also set up a world record. Like his father, he was driving **a car called *Bluebird***.

## 课文注释 Notes on the text

<Callout type="info" title="6 个值得停下来嚼一嚼的点">
  1. **the first man to drive at over 300 miles per hour** (l.1-2) — “第一个以每小时 300 多英里速度驾车的人”。**the first / the last / the only + 名词 + to do** 是固定句式，用**不定式**修饰前面的名词（不用定语从句）。**drive at + 速度** = “以……速度行驶”，介词用 **at**。同型：*She was the first woman to swim the Channel. / He drove at 120 kilometres an hour.*
  2. **had been specially built for him** (l.4) — **过去完成时的被动语态 had been + 过去分词**。说话的“过去时间点”是 1935 年那次冲刺；车“被造出来”在更早，所以用“过去之过去”。同型：*The house had been painted before they moved in. / The letter had been posted the day before.*
  3. **over 30 feet in length** (l.4) — “在长度上超过 30 英尺”，即 *30 feet long*。**in length / in width / in height / in depth** 是表“在……方面”的固定搭配，前面放具体数字。同型：*The river is 200 metres in width. / The wall is two metres in height.*
  4. **the first run** (l.7) — “开始的那一趟行程”。**run** 在这里是**名词**，指“一次行驶 / 一次冲刺”。**a tyre burst** 里 **burst** 是不规则动词，三态同形 *burst-burst-burst*，这里是**过去式**。
  5. **a mistake had been made** (l.9-10) — 又一个**过去完成被动**：“（在被告知之前）已经出了一个差错”。注意全篇 *had been 299 / had been 301* 用的都是过去完成——因为“冲刺当时”是过去，而“平均速度是多少”这件事在更早就已确定。这就是本课的语法核心。
  6. **Following in his father's footsteps** (l.11) — “踏着父亲的足迹”。固定短语 **follow in someone's footsteps** = 继承某人的事业 / 步某人后尘。这里 **Following ...** 是**现在分词短语作状语**，逻辑主语是后面的 Donald。同型：*She followed in her mother's footsteps and became a doctor.*
</Callout>

## 3. 关键句型 过去完成时与“the first ... to do”

<Callout type="tip" title="一分钟回顾">
  本课的语法主角是**过去完成时（past perfect）had + 过去分词**，以及它的**被动形式 had been + 过去分词**。它的唯一职责：在一句以**过去**为基准的叙述里，标出“**比那个过去还要早**”的动作。

  | 句法 | 课文例句 | 解读 |
  |---|---|---|
  | **过去完成被动** | *Bluebird ... **had been specially built** for him.* | 1935 年开车（过去）之前，车早就被造好了 |
  | **过去完成被动** | *he was told that **a mistake had been made**.* | 被告知（过去）之前，差错早已发生 |
  | **过去完成** | *his average speed **had been** 299 ... **had been** 301* | “当时算出的平均速度是多少”——比叙述时间更早就定下了 |
  | **the first + n. + to do** | *was **the first man to drive** at over 300 mph* | 用不定式修饰名词，不用定语从句 |
  | **现在完成（对照）** | *racing drivers **have reached** speeds of over 600 ...* | 从过去持续到“现在”，和过去完成区分开 |

  ### ⚠️ 4 个本课最容易踩的雷区

  1. **过去完成 ≠ 一般过去**：只有在“以过去为参照、动作更早”时才用 had done。课文 *a mistake had been made*（差错早就出了）→ had been made；如果只是平铺直叙“出了差错”，就用一般过去 *was made*。
  2. **被动语态别丢 been**：过去完成被动是 **had + been + 过去分词**，三件套缺一不可。*had specially built* ✗ / *had been specially built* ✓。
  3. **the first man to drive**：first / last / only / next + 名词后面跟**不定式**，不要写成 *the first man who drives* 这种啰嗦从句，也不要用 *driving*。
  4. **drive at + 速度**：表“以……速度”用介词 **at**，不是 *with / in / to*。*drive at 300 miles per hour* ✓。
</Callout>

## 4. 难点：易混词复习 Special difficulties (Review SD50-71)

<Callout type="info" title="本课难点：复习第 50–71 课的常容易混淆的词">
  Lesson 72 是 Unit 3 的复习课，难点部分回顾了前面若干课反复出现的**易混词**。下面把最高频的几组先理清楚——它们几乎每年都在考：

  | 易混对 | 区别 | 例句 |
  |---|---|---|
  | **miss / lose** | miss = 错过（赶不上）；lose = 丢失 | *You'll **miss** the train.*（赶不上车） |
  | **raise / rise** | raise = 抬高（及物）；rise = 升起（不及物） | *Please **raise** the picture.* |
  | **lie / lay** | lie = 躺（lying，不及物）；lay = 放置（laying，及物） | *I love **lying** in bed.* |
  | **beat / win** | beat + 人（击败某人）；win + 比赛/奖（赢得） | *He always **beats** me at chess.* |
  | **quiet / quite** | quiet = 安静；quite = 完全 | *Are you **quite** ready?* |
  | **hard / hardly** | hard = 努力地；hardly = 几乎不 | *He works too **hard**.* |
  | **late / lately** | late = 迟；lately = 最近 | *The train arrived **late**.* |
  | **practise / practice** | practise = 动词；practice = 名词（英式） | *How many hours do you **practise**?* |

  口诀：**形容词 / 副词别混**（hard ≠ hardly，late ≠ lately，near ≠ nearly）；**及物 / 不及物别混**（raise/rise，lie/lay）；**词性别混**（practise 动词 / practice 名词）。
</Callout>

## 10. 摘要写作 Summary writing (≤ 75 词)

## 11. 作文 Composition

<Callout type="info" title="任务">
  Write two or three sentences using the ideas given below. 用以下词组写出 2 至 3 个句子来：

  *During a car race — leading car skidded — overturned — the driver was not hurt — got his car back on the course — won the race.*
</Callout>

<Callout type="warning" title="提示骨架">
  - **背景**：During a car race（在一场赛车比赛中）——用作时间状语开头。
  - **意外**：the leading car skidded and overturned（领先的赛车打滑并翻了车）——两个动词并列。
  - **转折**：Fortunately, the driver was not hurt（万幸车手没受伤）——用 Fortunately 转折。
  - **逆转**：he got his car back on the course and won the race（他把车重新开回赛道并赢得了比赛）。
</Callout>

<Callout type="tip" title="参考范文（自己写完再看）">
  During a car race, the leading car skidded and overturned. Fortunately, the driver was not hurt. He got his car back on the course and went on to win the race.
</Callout>

## 12. 书信写作 Letter writing

<Callout type="info" title="任务">
  Write sentences completing each of the following phrases. 将以下书信的开头语写成完整的句子：

  - I have not forgotten that ...
  - You will find it hard to believe, but ...
  - I am afraid I ...
  - I wonder how you learnt that ...
  - You will never guess who/what ...
</Callout>

<Callout type="tip" title="写作脚手架">
  - **I have not forgotten that ...** 后接一件过去发生、至今记得的事（宾语从句，常用一般过去时）。
  - **You will find it hard to believe, but ...** 后接一个令人意外的消息（用 but 引出反差）。
  - **I am afraid I ...** 用来委婉地说“恐怕我（不能/做不到）”（含歉意）。
  - **I wonder how you learnt that ...** 后接对方“是怎么知道某事的”（宾语从句）。
  - **You will never guess who/what ...** 后接一个让对方猜不到的人或物（设悬念）。
</Callout>

<Callout type="tip" title="参考范文（自己写完再看）">
  - I have not forgotten that you once lent me your bicycle and that I never thanked you properly.
  - You will find it hard to believe, but I have just passed my driving test at the very first attempt.
  - I am afraid I shall not be able to come to your party next Saturday after all.
  - I wonder how you learnt that I was planning to move abroad next year.
  - You will never guess who I ran into at the railway station yesterday afternoon.
</Callout>

## 13. 拓展练习 Going further

<Callout type="tip" title="自学者建议">
  - **影子跟读 (shadowing)**：用第 1 节的三个 SayIt 按钮反复跟读，重点抓两个数字的清晰发音——*two hundred and ninety-nine* 与 *three hundred and one*，再连读结尾长句 *Following in his father's footsteps ...*。
  - **复述 (retell)**：合上课文，用 1 分钟把故事讲一遍。**三段法**：纪录（1935 年盐滩 + 蓝鸟 + 30 英尺 / 2,500 马力）→ 波折（轮胎爆裂 → 被告知 299 → 失望）→ 反转（差错 → 其实 301 → 父子两代蓝鸟）。
  - **造句迁移**：用今天的 5 个核心结构造你自己的句子——
    1. *S + was the first + 名词 + to do*（第一个做某事的人）—— *She was the first woman to fly across the Atlantic.*
    2. *S + had been + 过去分词*（过去完成被动）—— *The room had been cleaned before the guests arrived.*
    3. *数字 + in length / height / width*—— *The bridge is 500 metres in length.*
    4. *drive / run / travel at + 速度*—— *The train was travelling at 200 kilometres an hour.*
    5. *follow in one's footsteps*—— *He followed in his father's footsteps and joined the navy.*
  - **延伸思考**：本课最妙的是那个“乌龙“——一个**过去完成被动**（*a mistake had been made*）把”失望“瞬间翻转成”纪录到手“。当你写英文叙事时，记住：**主线用一般过去**，要回头交代”更早发生、且常常是被动的事“，就切到 **had been + 过去分词**。两代人开同一个名字的车（*a car called Bluebird*）这个收尾，也是一种含蓄而有力的呼应——好故事往往在最后一句留一个回味。
</Callout>
