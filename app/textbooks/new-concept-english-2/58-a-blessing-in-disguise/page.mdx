## The Text 课文

The tiny village of Frinley **is said to possess** a 'cursed tree'. Because the tree **was mentioned** in a newspaper, the number of visitors to Frinley **has now increased**. The tree **was planted** near the church fifty years ago, but **it is only in recent years that** it **has gained an evil reputation**. **It is said that** if anyone touches the tree, he will have bad luck; if he picks a leaf, he will die. Many villagers believe that the tree **has already claimed a number of victims**. The vicar **has been asked to have the tree cut down**, but so far he has refused. He **has pointed out** that the tree is a **useful source of income**, as tourists **have been coming** from all parts of the country to see it. **In spite of all that has been said**, the tourists **have been picking** leaves and cutting their names on the tree-trunk. So far, **not one of them has been struck down** by sudden death!

## 课文注释 Notes on the text

<Callout type="info" title="5 个值得停下来嚼一嚼的点">
  1. **a blessing in disguise**（标题）—— “坏事变好事，因祸得福”。字面是“伪装起来的福气”：表面看是灾祸（cursed tree），其实暗藏好处（财源）。标题末尾的问号是作者的反讽——它真的是因祸得福吗？同型：*Losing that job was a blessing in disguise.*（丢了那份工作反倒是因祸得福。）
  2. **The tiny village of Frinley**（l.1）—— “弗林利这个小村庄”。介词 **of** 在这里表示**同位关系**（of 前后指同一事物），不是“……的”。同型：*the city of London（伦敦城）/ the month of May（五月）*。
  3. **it is only in recent years that it has gained an evil reputation**（ll.4-5）—— “只是近几年才得了个坏名声”。这是 **强调句型**：结构是 **it is (was) + 被强调部分 + that (who) + 从句**。这里强调的是时间状语 *only in recent years*。把强调框架 *it is ... that* 去掉，句子依旧成立：*it has only gained an evil reputation in recent years*。
  4. **to have the tree cut down**（l.8）—— “把这棵树砍掉”。**have + 宾语 + 过去分词**（have sth done）表示“叫别人去做某事”（树是被砍的，所以用过去分词 cut down 作宾语补足语）。同型：*have my hair cut（理发）/ have the car repaired（把车送修）*。
  5. **In spite of all that has been said**（l.10）—— “尽管有上述种种说法”，是**介词短语作状语**。其中 *that has been said* 是**定语从句**，修饰 *all*；关系代词 **that 在从句中作主语，不能省略**（作主语的关系代词永远不能省）。
</Callout>

## 3. 关键句型 被动语态 + "be said to do / It is said that"

<Callout type="tip" title="一分钟回顾：传闻怎么说？">
  本课的语法主线是 **被动语态**，尤其是表达“据说、传说”的两套句式。课文几乎句句被动：*is said / was mentioned / was planted / has been asked / has been said / has been struck down*。

  ### “据说……” 的两种说法（Key structures）

  | 主动 | 被动① It 作形式主语 | 被动② 真主语提前 |
  |---|---|---|
  | People say **he is a genius**. | **It is said that** he is a genius. | He **is said to be** a genius. |
  | People say **there is oil under the North Sea**. | **It is said that** there is oil ... | There **is said to be** oil ... |

  两条规律：
  - **It is said / believed / thought + that 从句**——that 后接**完整的句子**。
  - **主语 + is said / believed + to do**——to 后接**不定式**；从句的主语被提到句首。
  - 课文开篇 *Frinley **is said to possess** a 'cursed tree'* 用的就是第二种。

  ### 双宾语动词的被动（give / offer 等）

  *She gave me a pen.* → 两种被动都行：**I was given a pen.**（人作主语，更常用）/ **A pen was given to me.**（物作主语）。同理 *The manager offered him the post* → **He was offered the post.** / **The post was offered to him.**

  ### ⚠️ 4 个易错雷区

  1. **be said + 不定式带 to**：*is said **to** possess* ✓，不能写成 *is said possess*。
  2. **It is said + that 从句**，不是 “It is said sth to do”：✗ *It is said the tree to be cursed* → ✓ *It is said that the tree is cursed* / *The tree is said to be cursed*。
  3. **被动 = be + 过去分词**：*was planted / has been asked / has been struck down*——别丢了 be 动词，也别把 struck 写成原形 strike。
  4. **现在完成进行时 have been + V-ing**：*tourists have been coming / picking*——强调动作从过去一直持续，别误用过去分词（*have been come* ✗）。
</Callout>

## 4. 难点：易混动词 increase/grow · gain/earn · pick/cut

<Callout type="info" title="三组“看着像、用着不一样”的动词">
  本课 *Special difficulties* 专门挑出三组容易混的动词：

  | 词组 | 区别 | 例句 |
  |---|---|---|
  | **increase / grow** | increase 偏“数量、程度增大”（抽象）；grow 偏“生长、长大”（人、植物、也可数量） | The number of visitors **has increased / grown**. ／ She **has grown** so much she is nearly as tall as I am.（人长高只能用 grow） |
  | **gain / earn** | gain = 获得（名声、时间、重量，不一定靠出力）；earn = 靠工作 / 努力挣得（钱、尊重） | It has **gained / earned** an evil reputation.（名声两者皆可）／ He is **earning** a lot of money.（挣钱用 earn）／ travel by air to **gain** time（赢得时间用 gain） |
  | **pick / cut** | pick = 采、摘（花、果、叶）；cut = 切、割、刻 | The tourists have been **picking** the leaves and **cutting** their names on the tree-trunk. ／ She has **picked** a lot of flowers. ／ She **cut** the apple into two. |

  ### ⚠️ 一句话记牢
  - “数量”增长 increase / grow 通用；但**人、动物、植物长大**只用 **grow**。

  - **摘**花摘叶 → **pick**；**切 / 刻** → **cut**（cut 的过去式仍是 cut）。
</Callout>

## 10. 摘要写作 Summary writing (≤ 85 词)

<Callout type="info" title="任务">
  *Answer the questions in not more than 85 words.* 回答下列问题，把答案组成一个段落，不要超过 85 个单词。括号里是给出的连接词，要用上：

  1. Has the number of visitors to the village of Frinley increased or not? Is there said to be a 'cursed tree' near a church or not? *(because)*
  2. Do the villagers believe that if anyone picks a leaf he will die? What have they asked the vicar to do? *(Since)*
  3. Is the tree a useful source of income or not? Has the vicar agreed to have the tree cut down, or has he refused to do so? *(As)*
  4. Have tourists been picking leaves or not? Have any of them come to harm or not? *(Meanwhile, though ...)*
</Callout>

Meanwhile, tourists have been picking leaves, though none of them has come to any harm. *(74 words)*

## 11. 作文 Composition：连词改写句子

<Callout type="info" title="任务">
  *Rewrite the following sentences using the joining words in parentheses.* 用括号内的连词把每组句子合并改写成一句：

  1. A village well was said to be cursed. It was bought by a man. *(which)*
  2. Tourists came to see it. There was not even any water in it. *(though)*
  3. The tourists could look into the well. They had to throw a coin in first. *(Before)*
</Callout>

<Callout type="warning" title="提示骨架">
  - **(which)**：把第二句变成定语从句嵌入第一句——*A village well which was said to be cursed ...*，再接主句动词。
  - **(though)**：though 引导让步状语从句，可放句中：“虽然里面连一滴水都没有”。
  - **(Before)**：Before 大写说明它领起句首的时间状语从句——“在……之前，他们必须先……”。
</Callout>

<Callout type="tip" title="参考答案（自己写完再看）">
  1. A village well **which** was said to be cursed was bought by a man.
  2. Tourists came to see it, **though** there was not even any water in it.
  3. **Before** the tourists could look into the well, they had to throw a coin in first.
</Callout>

## 12. 书信写作 Letter writing

<Callout type="info" title="任务">
  *Learn the following opening phrases by heart, then write two short paragraphs (of not more than two sentences each) completing the phrases.* 熟记下面两个开头语，再用它们各写出一段（每段不超过两句话）：

  - You will be glad to hear that ...
  - Thank you for letting me know that ...
</Callout>

<Callout type="tip" title="写作脚手架">
  - **You will be glad to hear that ...**（你听了一定会高兴……）—— 用来报告**好消息**：考试通过、找到工作、即将团聚。第二句补一个细节或下一步打算。
  - **Thank you for letting me know that ...**（谢谢你告诉我……）—— 用来**回应对方来信里的消息**：平安到达、近况、邀请。第二句表达你的感受或回应。
  - 时态小贴士：报告已发生的好消息用 **现在完成时**（*have passed / have found*）；表达将来打算用 **be going to / will**。
</Callout>

<Callout type="tip" title="参考范文（自己写完再看）">
  You will be glad to hear that I have passed all my examinations. I am going to celebrate by coming home next weekend, so do keep Saturday evening free for me.

  Thank you for letting me know that you arrived safely. I had begun to feel a little anxious, as I had not heard a word from you for over a fortnight.
</Callout>

## 13. 拓展练习 Going further

<Callout type="tip" title="自学者建议">
  - **影子跟读 (shadowing)**：用第 1 节的三个 SayIt 按钮跟读 3-5 遍。重点体会结尾那句 *not one of them has been struck down by sudden death!* 的**反讽语气**——句末感叹号读出来要带一点“看吧，根本没事”的揶揄。
  - **复述 (retell)**：合上课文，用 2 分钟把故事讲一遍。**三段式**：背景（小村有“诅咒之树”+ 报纸一报道游客暴增）→ 矛盾（村民信诅咒、要砍树 vs 牧师看中财源、坚决不砍）→ 收尾（游客照样摘叶刻名，却无一暴死——因祸得福）。
  - **造句迁移**：用今天的 5 个核心结构造你自己的句子——
    1. *主语 + is said to + do*（据说……）—— *This temple is said to be over a thousand years old.*
    2. *It is said that + 从句*（据说……）—— *It is said that the bridge is haunted.*
    3. *have + 宾语 + 过去分词*（让人做某事）—— *I must have my watch repaired.*
    4. *In spite of + 名词, 主句*（尽管……）—— *In spite of the rain, the match went on.*
    5. *现在完成进行时 have been + V-ing*（一直在……）—— *Tourists have been arriving all morning.*
  - **延伸思考**：本课是一次**被动语态阅兵**——*is said / was mentioned / was planted / has been asked / has been said / has been struck down* 一字排开。为什么作者爱用被动？因为全篇讲的都是**传闻**：是谁说的、谁种的、谁请求的，都不重要、也无从查证；被动恰好把“动作的发出者”隐去，留下一团人人转述、却无人负责的“流言”。读到最后那句反讽，你会心一笑：所谓“诅咒”，多半就是被动语态织出来的一场空。再回味标题里的问号——*A blessing in disguise?*——坏事究竟有没有变成好事，作者把判断留给了你。
</Callout>
