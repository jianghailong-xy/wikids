## The Text 课文

In 1858, a French engineer, Aimé Thomé de Gamond, **arrived in England with a plan** for a twenty-one-mile tunnel under the English Channel. He said that it would be possible to build a platform in the centre of the Channel. This platform would **serve as a port and a railway station**. The tunnel would be **well-ventilated** if tall chimneys were built **above sea level**. In 1860, a better plan was **put forward** by an Englishman, William Low. **He suggested that a double railway-tunnel should be built.** This would **solve the problem of ventilation**, for if a train entered this tunnel, it would **draw in fresh air behind it**. Forty-two years later a tunnel was actually begun. If, at the time, **the British had not feared invasion, it would have been completed**. The world had to wait almost another 100 years for the Channel Tunnel. It was **officially opened** on March 7, 1994, finally **connecting Britain to the European continent**.

## 课文注释 Notes on the text

<Callout type="info" title="5 个值得停下来嚼一嚼的点">
  1. **a plan for a twenty-one-mile tunnel under the English Channel** (l.2) — "一项建造长 21 英里、穿越英吉利海峡的隧道的计划"。**a plan for + 名词**表示"……的计划 / 方案"；后面的 *under the English Channel* 是介词短语作定语，修饰 *tunnel*。注意 **twenty-one-mile** 作定语时用**连字符连成一个形容词**，且 mile 用**单数**（a twenty-one-mile tunnel，不是 *miles*）。同型：*a five-year plan / a ten-minute walk*。
  2. **serve as ...** (l.4) — "用作……；充当……"，相当于 *be used as / function as*，也可说 *serve for*。课文 *This platform would serve as a port and a railway station* = 这个平台将用作港口和火车站。同型：*The sofa can serve as a bed. / She served as our guide.*
  3. **put forward** (l.6) — "提出（计划、建议、方案）"，相当于 *propose / suggest / advance*。课文是**被动语态** *a better plan was put forward by ...* = "一项更好的计划由……提出"。同型：*put forward a proposal / a theory / a name*。
  4. **He suggested that a double railway-tunnel should be built.** (ll.6-8) — "他建议应当修建一条双轨隧道。"**suggest / demand / insist / recommend / propose** 等表示"建议、要求、坚持"的动词，后面的 **that 宾语从句要用虚拟语气**：谓语用 **(should) + 动词原形**，其中 **should 常被省略**。所以本句也可写成 *...that a double railway-tunnel **be** built*。⚠️ suggest **不接不定式**——不能说 *suggest to build*。同型：*I suggest that he (should) leave at once. / They demanded that the rule (should) be changed.*
  5. **If, at the time, the British had not feared invasion, it would have been completed.** (ll.10-11) — "如果当时英国人不害怕入侵，隧道早就建成了。"这是**与过去事实相反的虚拟语气（第三类条件句）**：条件从句用**过去完成时** *had not feared*，主句用 **would have + 过去分词** *would have been completed*。它表达的不是事实，而是对**已经发生（或没发生）的过去**的一种假设——事实上英国人确实害怕入侵，所以隧道当时**没有**建成。同型：*If you had told me earlier, I would have helped you.*
</Callout>

## 3. 关键句型 条件句与虚拟语气 Conditionals

<Callout type="tip" title="一分钟回顾：三类条件句">
  本课的语法主线是**条件句（if 句）**。把它们按"真不真实"分成三档，是英语叙事最常用的一把尺子。课文里同时出现了**第二类**和**第三类**——这正是它被选作 Key Structures 范例的原因。

  | 类型 | 结构 | 含义 | 例句 |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | **真实条件句** | if + **一般现在时**, **will / shall** + V | 将来很可能发生 | *If it **is** fine tomorrow, we **shall** go for a swim.* |
  | **第二类（与现在 / 一般事实相反）** | if + **过去式**, **would** + V | 对现在 / 将来的假设 | *If a train **entered** this tunnel, it **would draw** in fresh air.* |
  | **第三类（与过去事实相反）** | if + **过去完成时**, **would have** + 过去分词 | 对过去的假设（已无法改变） | *If the British **had not feared** invasion, it **would have been completed**.* |

  ### ⚠️ 4 个本课最容易踩的雷区

  1. **真实条件句的 if 从句里用一般现在时表将来**，不能用 will：*If it **is** fine tomorrow ...* ✓ / *If it ~~will be~~ fine ...* ✗。
  2. **第二类条件句**主句必须用 **would / could / might + 动词原形**，不能用 will：*If a train entered ..., it **would** draw ...* ✓。be 动词在虚拟里常用 **were**（各人称通用）：*If father **were** alive ...*。
  3. **第三类条件句**专门假设**已经过去、再也无法改变**的事：从句 **had + 过去分词**，主句 **would have + 过去分词**。课文的标题句就是它。
  4. **suggest / demand / insist** 等后的 that 从句用 **(should) + 动词原形**（与时态无关的"虚拟"）：*He suggested that a double railway-tunnel **(should) be** built.* —— 别写成 *suggested to build*。
</Callout>

## 4. 难点：动词 draw 的多义 Special difficulties

<Callout type="info" title="一个 draw，五种意思">
  课文 *it would **draw in** fresh air* 里的 draw 是"吸入、抽进"。但 **draw** 是个"百变动词"，搭配不同小品词意思天差地别——教材 Special Difficulties 专门拎出来对比：

  | 短语 | 含义 | 例句（教材原例） |
  |---|---|---|
  | **draw in** | 吸入、抽进 | *If a train entered this tunnel, it would **draw in** fresh air behind it.* (l.9) |
  | **draw back** | （因恐惧等）退缩、后退 | *The dog **drew back** in terror when it saw the snake.* |
  | **draw up** | （车辆）停下 | *A taxi **drew up** outside the bank.*（停下） |
  | **draw off** | 开走、离去 | *Two men got out and then the taxi **drew off**.*（开走了） |
  | **draw up** | 起草、拟定（文件 / 协议 / 计划） | *A new trading agreement was **drawn up** between Holland and Denmark.* |

  ⚠️ 注意 **draw up** 一词两用：说车是"**停下**"，说文件 / 计划是"**起草、拟定**"——靠上下文区分。
</Callout>

## 10. 摘要写作 Summary writing (≤ 85 词)

<Callout type="info" title="任务">
  Answer these questions *in not more than 85 words*（把答案组成一个连贯的段落，不超过 85 词）：

  1. Who planned to build a tunnel under the English Channel in 1858? How would it be ventilated? *(The tunnel, which ...)*
  2. Who suggested a better plan two years later?
  3. How would passing trains solve the problem of ventilation in his proposed double railway-tunnel? *(because they would)*
  4. Did work begin forty-two years later? Why was it stopped? *(Though ... because)*
  5. When was the Channel Tunnel officially opened? *(However)*
</Callout>

<Callout type="tip" title="参考范文（自己写完再看）">
  In 1858, a French engineer, de Gamond, planned a tunnel under the English Channel. The tunnel, which would have a platform in the centre, would be ventilated by tall chimneys above sea level. Two years later, an Englishman, William Low, suggested a better plan, because passing trains would draw in fresh air behind them. Though work began forty-two years later, it was stopped because the British feared invasion. However, the Channel Tunnel was finally opened officially on March 7, 1994. *(80 words)*
</Callout>

## 11. 句子合并 Composition

<Callout type="info" title="任务">
  Rewrite the following sentences using the joining words in parentheses（用括号里的连接词把两句合成一句）：

  1. The English Channel separates Britain from Europe. The country has not been invaded since 1066. *(Thanks to ... which)*
  2. Modern warfare is far more complex. Such fears no longer exist. *(However, now that ...)*
  3. Britain benefits enormously from a Channel Tunnel. Europe benefits enormously from a Channel Tunnel. *(Both ... and)*
</Callout>

<Callout type="tip" title="参考答案（自己写完再看）">
  1. **Thanks to** the English Channel, **which** separates Britain from Europe, the country has not been invaded since 1066.
  2. **However, now that** modern warfare is far more complex, such fears no longer exist.
  3. **Both** Britain **and** Europe benefit enormously from a Channel Tunnel.

  小提示：① *Thanks to + 名词*（多亏了……）后接非限定性定语从句 which，用一对逗号夹住；② *now that*（既然 / 由于现在）引导原因状语从句，句首的 *However* 表与上文转折；③ *Both A and B + 复数动词*（benefit，不是 benefits）。
</Callout>

## 14. 拓展练习 Going further

<Callout type="tip" title="自学者建议">
  - **影子跟读 (shadowing)**：用第 1 节的三个 SayIt 按钮跟读 3-5 遍，重点体会条件句里 *would / would have* 的弱读，以及 *had not feared* 的连读节奏。
  - **复述 (retell)**：合上课文，用 2 分钟把海峡隧道的故事讲一遍。**三段式**：① 1858 年 de Gamond 的平台 + 烟囱方案 → ② 1860 年 William Low 的双轨隧道（火车通风）→ ③ 42 年后动工、因怕入侵而停工，直到 1994 年才正式开通。
  - **造句迁移**：用今天的 5 个核心结构造你自己的句子——
    1. *If + 一般现在, will / shall + V*（真实条件）—— *If it rains tomorrow, we will stay at home.*
    2. *If + 过去式, would + V*（与现在相反）—— *If I knew his number, I would call him.*
    3. *If + 过去完成, would have + 过去分词*（与过去相反）—— *If you had asked me, I would have helped you.*
    4. *suggest that + (should) + V*（建议从句）—— *I suggest that we leave early.*
    5. *serve as ...*（用作 / 充当）—— *This room can serve as a study.*
  - **延伸思考**：这篇说明文真正的"主角"不是隧道，而是**条件句**——de Gamond 的"如果建烟囱"（设想）、Low 的"如果火车驶入"（设想）、英国人"如果不怕入侵"（与过去相反的遗憾）。三种"如果"对应三种现实关系：**可能、假设、再也无法改变**。下次你写英文议论或叙事时，先问自己一句：我这个"如果"，是真有可能，还是纯属假设，还是早已无法挽回？——把这三把尺子分清，条件句就再也不会用错了。
</Callout>
