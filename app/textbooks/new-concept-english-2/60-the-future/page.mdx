## The Text 课文

At a village fair, I **decided to visit** a fortune-teller called Madam Bellinsky. I went into her tent and she **told me to sit down**. **After I had given her some money**, she **looked into a crystal ball** and said: 'A **relation of yours** is coming to see you. She **will be arriving** this evening and **intends to stay** for a few days. **The moment you leave this tent**, you **will get a big surprise**. A **woman you know well** will **rush towards you**. She will speak to you and then she **will lead you away** from this place. That is all.'

**As soon as I went outside**, I **forgot all about** Madam Bellinsky because my wife **hurried towards** me. 'Where have you been hiding?' she asked **impatiently**. 'Your sister will be here **in less than an hour** and we must be at the station to meet her. **We are late already**.' As she walked away, I followed her out of the fair.

## 课文注释 Notes on the text

<Callout type="info" title="4 个值得停下来嚼一嚼的点">
  1. **a relation of yours** (l.4) —— “您的一个亲戚”。这是**名词双重所有格**（double genitive）的又一个例子：*of + 物主代词的名词性形式 / 名词所有格*。课文里 *a relation of yours* = *one of your relations*，比 *your relation* 更强调“众多亲戚中的某一个”。同型：*a friend of mine（我的一个朋友）/ a book of my brother’s（我哥哥的一本书）*。
  2. **the moment you leave this tent** (l.5) —— **the moment** 在这里作**连词**，引导**时间状语从句**，意思是“一……就……”，相当于 *as soon as*。注意：从句虽指将来，却**用一般现在时 leave** 代替 will。同型连词还有 *the minute / the instant / as soon as / immediately*。课文 *The moment you leave this tent, you will get a big surprise* = 你一走出这帐篷，就会大吃一惊。
  3. **a woman you know well** (l.6) —— “一个你很熟悉的女人”。*you know well* 是省略了关系词的**定语从句**，修饰前面的名词 *a woman*，被省略的引导词是 *whom / that*（充当 know 的宾语，故可省）。补全即 *a woman (whom) you know well*。同型：*the book (that) I bought / the man (whom) we met*。
  4. **That is all.** (l.8) —— “就这些。”算命人收尾的固定说法，干脆利落。口语里也说 *That’s all / That will be all*（служ员、店员结束服务时也常用）。
</Callout>

## 3. 关键句型 将来时态 & “时间/条件状语从句用现在时表将来”

<Callout type="tip" title="一分钟回顾：本课的核心铁律">
  本课题为 *The future*，整篇就是一次**“将来时”的集中操练**。两条主线务必记牢：

  ### 主线一：表“将来”的几种说法

  | 形式 | 课文 / 教材例句 | 用途 |
  |---|---|---|
  | **will + V** | *you **will get** a big surprise / she **will speak** to you* | 最通用的将来；预言、临时决定 |
  | **be going to + V** | *I **am going to** travel by air.*（KS36） | 打算、有迹象的将来 |
  | **be V-ing（现在进行表将来）** | *He **is arriving** tomorrow. / She **is leaving** in two days’ time.* | 已**安排好**的活动、行程 |
  | **will be V-ing（将来进行）** | *She **will be arriving** this evening.* | 将来某时“正在进行”/ 礼貌地谈安排 |
  | **intend / plan / be due to + V** | *She **intends to** stay for a few days.* | 计划、预定要做的事 |

  ### 主线二：⚠️ 状语从句里“主将从现”

  当 **when / the moment / as soon as / until / before / after / if** 引导**时间或条件状语从句**、而主句指将来时，**从句要用一般现在时代替将来时**（俗称“主将从现”）：

  | 连词 | 教材例句 | 从句动词形式 |
  |---|---|---|
  | **the moment** | *The moment he **arrives**, I shall let you know.* | 现在时 arrives（≠ will arrive） |
  | **as soon as** | *As soon as the rain **stops**, we shall go out.* | 现在时 stops |
  | **until** | *I shall wait here until he **comes**.* | 现在时 comes |
  | **before** | *You must finish your dinner before you **leave** the table.* | 现在时 leave |
  | **after** | *We’ll go into the living room after we **finish** dinner.* | 现在时 finish |
  | **when** | *I’ll give him the message when he **returns**.* | 现在时 returns |
  | **if（条件）** | *If it **rains** tomorrow we shall stay at home.*（KS16） | 现在时 rains |

  课文原句 *The moment you **leave** this tent, you **will get** a big surprise.* 就是这条规则的范本：主句 *will get*，从句 *leave*（绝不能写成 will leave）。
</Callout>

## 4. 难点：名词作定语 / 复合名词 Special difficulties

<Callout type="info" title="用“名词 + 名词”替换“of / in / for + 名词”">
  本课难点（对比第 13 课 SD13）讲的是：英语里常把 **“X + of / in / for / about / to + Y”** 这种后置短语，**改写成更简洁的“Y + X”名词修饰结构**（名词当形容词用，或构成复合名词）。

  | 啰嗦说法 *Instead of saying* | 更地道 *It is better to say* |
  |---|---|
  | We went to the fair **in the village**. | We went to the **village fair**. |
  | a story **about ghosts** | a **ghost story** |
  | a present **for my birthday** | a **birthday present** |
  | the key **to the front door** | the **front-door key** |

  ⚠️ 规律：**作定语的名词通常用单数**（*a ghost story* 不是 a ghosts story），且**重音多落在前一个名词**上（GHOST story / BIRTHDAY present）。
</Callout>

## 10. 摘要写作 Summary writing (≤ 80 词)

<Callout type="info" title="任务">
  *Answer these questions in not more than 80 words.* 回答下列问题，将答案组成一个段落，**不要超过 80 个单词**：

  1. Whom did the writer visit at a village fair? Did she tell him that a relation was coming to see him or not? *(who)*
  2. Who would speak to him when he left the tent? Would he follow her out of the fair or not? *(She added that ... and)*
  3. Did this come true or not? Did his wife speak to him when he went outside, or did a strange woman speak to him? *(because)*
  4. Whom did she tell him they had to meet at the station? Did she lead him out of the fair or not? *(and)*
</Callout>

She added that a woman he knew well would speak to him when he left the tent, and he would follow her out of the fair. This came true, because his wife spoke to him when he went outside. She told him they had to meet his sister at the station, and she led him out of the fair. *(78 words)*
</Callout>

## 11. 作文 Composition

<Callout type="info" title="任务">
  *Rewrite the following sentences using the joining words in parentheses.* 用括号中的连词把下列句子连成一句：

  1. Fortune-tellers always tell the truth. They speak in general terms. *(because)*
  2. They say you will go on a journey. You will meet a friend. They are right. We often do these things. *(If ... or ... because)*
</Callout>

<Callout type="warning" title="提示骨架">
  - 第 1 句：*because* 引导**原因状语从句**——“算命人之所以‘总说对’，是因为他们说得很笼统”。把第二句变成 because 从句，接到第一句后面。
  - 第 2 句：用 *If ... or ...* 把两个预言（去旅行 / 遇朋友）并成条件；再用 *because* 接上原因（我们本来就常做这些事）。注意条件从句**“主将从现”**：*if you go ... or meet ...*（现在时），主句 *they are right*。
</Callout>

<Callout type="tip" title="参考范文（自己写完再看）">
  1. Fortune-tellers always tell the truth **because** they speak in general terms.

  2. **If** they say you will go on a journey **or** (that you will) meet a friend, they are right, **because** we often do these things.
</Callout>

## 12. 书信写作 Letter writing

<Callout type="info" title="任务">
  *Address an envelope to a married couple who live abroad.* 给一对寄居国外的已婚夫妇写**信封上的地址**。
</Callout>

<Callout type="tip" title="写作脚手架 + 范例">
  英文信封地址要点：
  - **收件人称呼**：写给一对已婚夫妇，传统写法用 *Mr and Mrs + 丈夫的姓*（如 *Mr and Mrs J. Brown*）。
  - **地址自上而下、由小到大**：门牌号 + 街道 → 城市/邮区 → 邮编 → **国家（寄往国外必须写国名，且常大写）**。
  - 每行左对齐，国名单独一行放最后。

  范例：

  ```
  Mr and Mrs J. Brown
  25 Hill Road
  Cambridge CB2 1RT
  ENGLAND
  ```
</Callout>

## 13. 拓展练习 Going further

<Callout type="tip" title="自学者建议">
  - **影子跟读 (shadowing)**：用第 1 节的三个 SayIt 按钮各跟读 3–5 遍。重点体会 *will be arriving* 这类“将来进行”读起来轻而连贯，以及 *The moment you leave this tent...* 一句里 from-clause 到 main-clause 的语调起伏。
  - **复述 (retell)**：合上课文，用 1 分钟把故事讲一遍。**两幕结构**：帐篷内（付钱 → 水晶球 → 四条预言）→ 帐篷外（妻子冲来 → 催促接姐姐 → 预言“全应验”的反讽）。
  - **造句迁移**：用今天的 5 个核心结构造你自己的句子——
    1. *The moment + 一般现在, 主句 + will*（一……就……）—— *The moment the bell rings, the children will run out.*
    2. *as soon as / until / when + 一般现在表将来*（主将从现）—— *I’ll call you as soon as I arrive.*
    3. *be V-ing 表将来安排* —— *We are leaving for Shanghai tomorrow morning.*
    4. *a + 名词 + of + 物主代词性*（双重所有格）—— *a friend of mine / a relation of yours*.
    5. *名词作定语 / 复合名词* —— *a village fair, a ghost story, a birthday present*.
  - **延伸思考**：本课表面是“算命笑话”，骨子里是一堂**“将来时”精读课**。两件事请刻进肌肉记忆：① 凡 *when / the moment / as soon as / until / before / after / if* 引导**时间或条件状语从句**且主句表将来，**从句一律用一般现在时**——这是中国学生最常踩、考试最常考的雷区；② “神预言”之所以可笑，是因为它**说得足够笼统**，于是怎样都能“算准”——这也提醒我们写作时：**越具体的承诺越难兑现，越笼统的话越‘永远正确’**。
</Callout>
