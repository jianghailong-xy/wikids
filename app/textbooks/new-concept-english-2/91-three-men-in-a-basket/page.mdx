## The Text 课文

A pilot noticed a balloon which **seemed to be making for** a Royal Air Force Station nearby. He informed the station at once, but no one there **was able to** explain the mystery. The officer in the control tower was very angry when he heard the news, because balloons **can be a great danger** to aircraft. He said that someone **might be spying** on the station and the pilot was ordered to **keep track of** the strange object. The pilot **managed to** circle the balloon for some time. He **could make out** three men in a basket under it and one of them was holding **a pair of binoculars**. When the balloon was over the station, the pilot saw one of the men taking photographs. Soon afterwards, the balloon **began to descend** and it landed near an airfield. The police were called in, but they **could not arrest** anyone, for the basket contained two Members of Parliament and the Commanding Officer of the station! As the Commanding Officer explained later, **one half of the station did not know what the other half was doing!**

## 课文注释 Notes on the text

<Callout type="info" title="4 个值得停下来嚼一嚼的点">
  1. **which seemed to be making for a Royal Air Force Station nearby** (l.1) — 好像正朝附近的一座皇家空军基地飞去。这是 **which 引导的定语从句**修饰 *a balloon*。**seem + 不定式的进行式 (to be doing)** 表示“似乎正在做某事”；**make for** 是固定短语，意为“走向、驶向、朝……去”。同型：*The ship was making for the harbour. / He seemed to be telling the truth.*
  2. **might be spying on the station** (l.6) — **might + 不定式的进行式 (be doing)** 表示对“正在进行的动作”的推测：“也许正在……”。**spy on sb./sth.** = 暗中监视、侦察。同型：*She might be waiting for us. / They were spying on their neighbours.*
  3. **keep track of** (l.6) — “跟踪、监视、掌握……的动向”。**track** 本义是“踪迹、轨迹”，*keep track of* 即“盯住……的行踪”。反义：*lose track of*（失去……的线索 / 跟丢）。同型：*It's hard to keep track of all the changes.*
  4. **make out** (l.8) — 在本课意为“看清、辨认出”（费力地看清）。*make* 是本课难点词，含义众多（见第 4 节）：*make out* 还可表“理解、听懂”和“写出、开出”。同型：*I could just make out a figure in the fog.*
</Callout>

## 3. 关键句型：Can / Be able to / Manage to (KS19, 43, 67)

<Callout type="tip" title="一分钟回顾：三种“能”怎么分工">
  本课是“能力 / 做成”系列句型的复习课（复习第 19、43、67 课）。一段不长的课文里，三种“能”全到齐了——把它们圈出来，分工一目了然：

  | 形式 | 含义 | 课文例句 |
  |---|---|---|
  | **can / could** | 一般能力、可能性；感官动词前 | *balloons **can** be a great danger* / *he **could** make out three men* |
  | **be able to** | 能够（尤指“那一次（没）做成”），可用于各种时态 | *no one there **was able to** explain the mystery* |
  | **manage to** | 设法做成（含“克服困难才办到”的意味） | *The pilot **managed to** circle the balloon* |

  ### ⚠️ 4 个本课最容易踩的雷区

  1. **could vs was able to（最核心的区分）**：表示“过去某一次、经过努力终于做成了某件具体的事”，要用 **was/were able to** 或 **managed to**，**不用 could**。例：*They **were able to** jump into the sea before the boat sank.*（在船沉前成功跳海）✓；*They **could** jump ...* ✗（could 只表“一般有跳的能力”，不表“那次做成了”）。
  2. **could 的例外**：在**否定句**（could not）和**感官动词**（could see / hear / make out / understand）里，could 表“做成”是没问题的。课文 *He could make out three men* ✓。
  3. **manage to do**（接不定式）才是“设法做成”。*managed circling* ✗（接 V-ing 改变了搭配与含义）。
  4. **请求用 Can/Could you ...?**：can 是情态动词，疑问句直接前置，**不能再加 do/are**：*Can you show me the way?* ✓；*Do you can show me ...?* ✗。
</Callout>

## 4. 难点：动词 make 的多副面孔 Special difficulties

<Callout type="info" title="一个 make，七种意思">
  本课难点是动词 **make** 在不同搭配里的不同含义。课文用了两处（*make for* / *make out*），教材又补了几处——记住这张表，*make* 的“变脸”就不再迷惑人：

  | 搭配 | 含义 | 例句（教材原例） |
  |---|---|---|
  | **make for** | 走向、朝……去 | *A balloon seemed to be **making for** a Royal Air Force Station.*（似乎正驶向……） |
  | **make out** | 看清、辨认出 | *He could **make out** three men in a basket.*（能看见） |
  | **make out** | 理解、听懂 | *I could not **make out** what he said.*（我无法理解他说的话） |
  | **make out** | 写出、开出 | *Please **make out** a fresh copy.*（请重写 / 开一份） |
  | **make up** | 编造 | *He **made up** a story about two men and a horse.*（他编造了……） |
  | **make up** | 化妆 | *She spends hours **making up** in front of the mirror.*（化妆） |
  | **make up for** | 补偿、弥补 | *I must **make up for** the time I lost this morning.*（把损失的时间补上） |

  ### ⚠️ 别混了
  - **make for**（朝……去）≠ **make out**（看清 / 听懂 / 写出）≠ **make up**（编造 / 化妆）。
  - **make up**（编造、化妆）和 **make up for**（补偿）只差一个 *for*，意思天差地别：*make up a story*（编故事）vs *make up for lost time*（弥补失去的时间）。
</Callout>

## 10. 摘要写作 Summary writing (≤ 85 词)

<Callout type="info" title="任务">
  *In not more than 85 words*, explain **what happened from the time the pilot was ordered to keep track of the balloon**. 用不超过 85 个单词，描述从飞行员受命监视气球开始所发生的一切，写成两段。可借助教材给的提示词组：

  - **While circling** — Pilot circled balloon. Saw three men — basket. A pair of binoculars. (**one of whom** ...)
  - **When** — Flew over station. Took photographs. **Then** — Landed — airfield.
  - **but** — Police — could not arrest. **because** — Two Members of Parliament and Commanding Officer.
  - **Afterwards** — One half — the other half. (The mystery was explained when ...)
</Callout>

<Callout type="tip" title="参考范文（自己写完再看）">
  Ordered to keep track of the balloon, the pilot managed to circle it. Under it was a basket containing three men, one of whom was holding a pair of binoculars. When the balloon flew over the station, one man took photographs. Then it descended and landed near an airfield. The police were called in, but they could not arrest anyone, because the basket contained two Members of Parliament and the station's own Commanding Officer. *(70 words)*
</Callout>

## 11. 作文 Composition (约 150 词，两段)

<Callout type="info" title="任务">
  Imagine that **the police did not believe the three men and arrested them**. Write two paragraphs in about **150 words** using the ideas given below.
</Callout>

<Callout type="warning" title="提示骨架（每个破折号都得自己填）">
  - **第 1 段**：The men struggled — they explained who they were — they were not believed — taken to the police station.
  - **第 2 段**：The police questioned the men — they refused to answer — the Commanding Officer telephoned the station — a senior officer arrived — he identified the C.O. — the police apologized.
</Callout>

<Callout type="tip" title="写作脚手架">
  - **时态主线**：一般过去时叙事（struggled / explained / arrested）；过去完成时表更早的事（*had said / had arranged*）；过去进行时铺背景（*were questioning / was telephoning*）。
  - **“拒捕 / 申辩”动词库**：*struggled, protested, insisted, demanded to be released, refused to say a word, kept silent.*
  - **“核实身份”动词库**：*telephoned the station, sent for a senior officer, identified the Commanding Officer, confirmed their story, recognised him at once.*
  - **“道歉收尾”句式**：*The police apologized profusely and let them go. / Red-faced, the officers released the three men with their apologies.*
</Callout>

<Callout type="tip" title="参考范文（自己写完再看）">
  The three men struggled as the policemen seized them, protesting that they were not spies at all. They explained exactly who they were — two Members of Parliament and the Commanding Officer of the station — but the officers simply laughed. Nobody believed a word of it, and the men were bundled into a van and driven straight to the police station.

  At the station the police questioned them for an hour, but the three prisoners, by now thoroughly annoyed, refused to answer a single question. In the end the Commanding Officer was allowed to telephone the station. A senior officer arrived within minutes, took one look at the prisoners and identified the C.O. immediately. The police apologized profusely, released all three men, and drove them home themselves. *(126 words)*
</Callout>

## 12. 书信写作 Letter writing

<Callout type="tip" title="写作脚手架">
  - **开头信的两种语气**：① 责备 / 想念（对方久未来信）；② 致歉（自己久未回信）；③ 致谢（对方款待）；④ 祝贺（对方喜讯）。

  - **致谢**：*Thank you so much for ... / I can't tell you how much I enjoyed ...*
  - **祝贺**：*Congratulations on ... / I was delighted to hear that ... / Well done!*
</Callout>

## 13. 拓展练习 Going further

<Callout type="tip" title="自学者建议">
  - **影子跟读 (shadowing)**：用第 1 节的三个 SayIt 按钮跟读 3–5 遍，重点感受最后一句反转的语气——*one half of the station did not know what the other half was doing!*，重音落在 *one half / other half* 的对照上。
  - **复述 (retell)**：合上课文，用 2 分钟把故事讲一遍。**叙事三段**：误会（气球飞向基地 → 疑似间谍 → 命令跟踪）→ 侦察（绕飞 → 看清三人 + 望远镜 + 拍照）→ 反转（落地 → 警察来 → 竟是两名议员和指挥官）。
  - **造句迁移**：用今天的 5 个高价值结构造你自己的句子——
    1. *S + was/were able to + V*（过去那一次做成了）—— *We were able to finish the project on time.*
    2. *S + managed to + V*（设法克服困难做成）—— *She managed to repair the engine by herself.*
    3. *make for + 地点*（朝……去）—— *Seeing the storm, the boats made for the harbour.*
    4. *make out + 宾语*（看清 / 听懂）—— *In the fog I could just make out a figure ahead.*
    5. *keep track of + 宾语*（跟踪、掌握动向）—— *A good diary helps you keep track of your spending.*
  - **延伸思考**：本课是一篇典型的“**反转幽默**”小品——前文步步把读者引向“间谍”的紧张联想，结尾一句却揭穿：篮子里全是“自己人”。语言上，它把 **can / be able to / manage to** 这三种“能”和 **make** 的多义用得淋漓尽致。读完不妨想想：作者是如何只用最后两句话，就把一桩“国家安全事件”变成一则“内部信息不通”的笑话的？这正是英语叙事里 **anticlimax（突降 / 虎头蛇尾式幽默）** 的妙处。
</Callout>
