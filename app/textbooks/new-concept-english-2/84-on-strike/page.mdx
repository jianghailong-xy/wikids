## The Text 课文

Busmen **have decided to go on strike** next week. The strike **is due to begin** on Tuesday. No one knows how long it **will last**. The busmen have stated that the strike **will continue until** general agreement **is reached** about pay and working conditions. Most people believe that the strike will last for at least a week. Many owners of private cars **are going to offer** 'free rides' to people on their way to work. This will **relieve pressure** on the trains **to some extent**. Meanwhile, a number of university students **have volunteered to drive** buses **while the strike lasts**. All the students are expert drivers, but **before they drive** any of the buses, they **will have to pass** a special test. The students **are going to take** the test in two days' time. Even so, people are going to **find it difficult** to get to work. But so far, the public **has expressed its gratitude** to the students in letters to the Press. Only one or two people **have objected that** the students will drive too fast!

## 课文注释 Notes on the text

<Callout type="info" title="5 个值得停下来嚼一嚼的点">
  1. **go on strike** (l.1) — “举行罢工”。**strike** 既是名词（一次罢工）也是动词；**on strike** 表示“处于罢工状态”。课文还出现了 *go on strike*（开始罢工）这个动词短语。同型：*The workers went on strike. / The miners have been on strike for a month.* 注意它的“开始”味儿来自 **go on**——“进入……状态”。
  2. **be due to + 动词原形** (l.2) — *... is due to begin* 中的 **due** 意为“定于（某时做某事）、预定”，后接**不定式**。同型：*The train is due to arrive at six. / She is due to give a speech tomorrow.* 别和 *due to + 名词*（“由于”）混淆——后者后面跟名词，如 *The delay was due to fog.*
  3. **free ride** (l.6) — “免费乘车、搭便车”。课文用单引号 *'free rides'* 表示这是一种“被善意提供的免费搭载”。引申义 *get a free ride* 还可指“不劳而获、坐享其成”。
  4. **to some extent** (l.7) — “在某种程度上”。是程度的“留有余地”说法，等于 *partly / to a certain degree*。课文说免费乘车只能 *relieve pressure to some extent*——言下之意：缓解有限，并不能完全解决。同型：*I agree with you to some extent.*
  5. **Only one or two people have objected that ...** (l.12) — **only** 在句首**修饰主语** *one or two people* 时，句子**不用倒装**（谓语照常 *have objected*）。对比：当 only 在句首**修饰状语**（时间、地点、方式）时，主句要**部分倒装**，如 *Only then did I understand.* 一句之差，全看 only 修饰的是“主语”还是“状语”。
</Callout>

## 3. 关键句型 一般将来时 will / be going to（本课语法重点）

<Callout type="tip" title="一分钟回顾：四种“说未来”的方式">
  本课是 NCE2 复习**一般将来时**的经典课（教材标注 *KS12, 36, 60*）。一篇短文里，几乎每句话都在讲“下周将发生什么”，把“说未来”的工具一次性铺开了：

  | 形式 | 课文例句 | 用法 |
  |---|---|---|
  | **will + 动词原形** | *the strike **will continue** / **will last** / they **will have to** pass a test* | 对未来的预测、客观将来事实 |
  | **be going to + 动词原形** | *car owners **are going to offer** ... / students **are going to take** the test / people **are going to find** it difficult* | 已有打算 / 有迹象的将来 |
  | **be due to + 动词原形** | *The strike **is due to begin** on Tuesday.* | 按计划、时刻表“定于……” |
  | **现在时表将来**（在 while / before / until 从句中） | *while the strike **lasts** / before they **drive** ... / until agreement **is reached*** | 时间 / 条件状语从句里用现在时代替将来时 |

  ### ⚠️ 4 个本课最容易踩的雷区

  1. **时间 / 条件状语从句不用 will**：*before they **drive*** ✓ / *before they **will drive*** ✗；*until agreement **is reached*** ✓ / *until agreement **will be reached*** ✗；*while the strike **lasts*** ✓。“主将从现”——主句一般将来，从句一般现在。
  2. **be due to + 不定式**：*due* 后接 **to do**（is due to begin），不接 -ing，也不是 *due to + 名词*（那是“由于”）。
  3. **be going to vs will**：有**预先打算 / 现有迹象**多用 *be going to*（车主们已经准备好了、考试已定档）；纯粹**预测 / 临时决定**多用 *will*（罢工会持续多久）。本课两者交替出现，留心区别。
  4. **will have to**：表示“将来必须”——*they will have to pass a special test*。must 没有将来形式，要用 *will have to* 补位。
</Callout>

## 4. 难点：用 it 作形式宾语 Special difficulties

<Callout type="info" title="find it difficult to do —— “it” 是个占位的形式宾语">
  教材 *Special difficulties* 专讲一种语序：当“真正的宾语”是一个**不定式短语**或 **that 从句**、而后面还跟着**形容词 / 名词作宾语补足语**时，英语习惯先放一个**形式宾语 it** 占位，把又长又重的真宾语挪到句末。

  | 结构 | 教材例句 | 真正的宾语 |
  |---|---|---|
  | **find it + adj + to do** | People are going to **find it difficult to get to work**. (ll.10-11) | to get to work |
  | **think it + adj + to do** | He **thought it easy to pass the examination**. | to pass the examination |
  | **consider it + adj + that 从句** | He **considered it wrong that she should have to wait**. | that she should have to wait |
  | **feel it + adj + that 从句** | I **feel it right that he should be punished**. | that he should be punished |
  | **think it + adj + for sb to do** | I **think it wrong for people to behave like that**. | (for people) to behave like that |

  ### ⚠️ 3 条铁律

  1. **不能省掉 it**：*find difficult to do* ✗ → *find **it** difficult to do* ✓。
  2. **it 之后是“宾语补足语”**——用**形容词**（difficult / easy / right / wrong），不是副词（difficultly ✗）。
  3. 真宾语若是**人做的动作**，可加 *for sb*：*think it wrong **for people** to behave like that*。
</Callout>

## 11. 作文 Composition (约 150 词)

<Callout type="info" title="任务">
  Write **two paragraphs in about 150 words** using the ideas given below（按以下两组思路各写一段，共约 150 词）：

  1. The strike began — the students drove badly — the buses were seldom on time — often crowded — the public complained — the busmen were pleased.
  2. The students threatened to go on strike — they did so — this angered the busmen who returned to work.
</Callout>

<Callout type="warning" title="提示骨架">
  - **第 1 段（学生顶班却帮了倒忙）**：罢工一开始 → 学生开得糟 → 车几乎不准点 → 又常常拥挤 → 公众开始抱怨 → 司机们暗自高兴（“看吧，我们没那么好替代”）。
  - **第 2 段（学生也撂挑子）**：学生威胁要罢工 → 真的罢了 → 惹恼了原来的司机 → 司机们复工。
</Callout>

<Callout type="tip" title="写作脚手架">
  - **时态主线**：全文一般过去时叙事（*began / drove / complained / returned*）；更早的事可用过去完成（*had promised / had praised*）；背景持续用过去进行（*were driving / were getting worse*）。
  - **“开得糟”动词库**：*drove badly / clumsily, stalled the engine, missed stops, ran late, could not keep to the timetable*。
  - **“抱怨”动词库**：*complained, grumbled, protested, wrote angry letters, demanded the busmen back*。
  - **连接词**：*At first ... / Before long ... / Worse still ... / In the end ...* 串起情节起伏。
</Callout>

<Callout type="tip" title="参考范文（自己写完再看）">
  When the strike began, the university students took over the buses, but they soon proved far less skilful than they had promised. They drove badly, and the buses were seldom on time. Worse still, they were often dangerously crowded, since the students could not keep to the timetable. Before long the public, who had praised the students so warmly, began to complain bitterly. The busmen, watching from the sidelines, were secretly pleased: their work no longer seemed so easy to replace.

  After a few days the students grew tired of the criticism and the long hours. They threatened to go on strike themselves, and a week later they actually did so. This angered the busmen, who felt that amateurs were making fun of a serious dispute. Rather than let the city's transport collapse for good, the busmen finally agreed to fresh talks and returned to work. *(about 145 words)*
</Callout>

## 12. 书信写作 Letter writing — The Conclusion 结束语

<Callout type="info" title="任务">
  *The Conclusion.* Complete the following sentences（完成以下书信**结束语**）：

  1. I shall be looking ...
  2. I am very sorry for ...
  3. I hope you will ...
  4. I wish you ...
  5. Please give my ...
</Callout>

<Callout type="tip" title="参考补全 + 用法（自己先写再看）">
  - 1. I shall be looking **forward to hearing from you soon.**（盼回信——*look forward to* 后接动名词）
  - 2. I am very sorry for **not having written sooner.**（为没早点写信致歉——*sorry for + 动名词*）
  - 3. I hope you will **come and stay with us next summer.**（表达邀请 / 期望）
  - 4. I wish you **a speedy recovery.** / **every success in your new job.**（祝愿——wish sb + 名词）
  - 5. Please give my **kind regards to your family.**（代问候——*give one's regards to sb*）

  **小贴士**：这些都是书信收尾的常用“客套句”。寒暄要与正文语气一致——给朋友用 *looking forward to hearing from you*，正式信件可用 *I look forward to your early reply.*
</Callout>

## 13. 拓展练习 Going further

<Callout type="tip" title="自学者建议">
  - **影子跟读 (shadowing)**：用第 1 节的三个 SayIt 按钮各跟读 3-5 遍，重点体会 *while the strike lasts / before they drive / until ... is reached* 这些从句的连读节奏。
  - **复述 (retell)**：合上课文，用 1 分钟把“下周将发生什么”讲一遍——**三条线**：罢工本身（定于周二、至少一周、为工资和工作条件）→ 应对（私车免费乘车、学生顶班但要先考试）→ 反响（公众感激、个别人嫌开太快）。全程练习**一般将来时**。
  - **造句迁移**：用今天的 5 个高价值结构造你自己的句子——
    1. *be due to + 原形*（定于）—— *The meeting is due to start at nine.*
    2. *主将从现*（时间从句用现在时）—— *I will call you when I get home.*
    3. *be going to + 原形*（打算 / 有迹象）—— *Look at those clouds — it is going to rain.*
    4. *will have to + 原形*（将来必须）—— *You will have to take an exam before you can drive.*
    5. *find it + adj + to do*（形式宾语）—— *I find it hard to wake up early.*
  - **延伸思考**：本课是一张“**未来时态全景图**”——同一件事，*will* 偏“预测 / 客观将来”，*be going to* 偏“打算 / 有迹象”，*be due to* 偏“按计划”，而到了 while / before / until 从句里，未来又要“退回”一般现在时。下次写英语计划或新闻预告时，问自己：“这是预测、打算，还是定好的安排？从句里该不该收起 will？”——四把尺子在手，你笔下的“未来”就会既准确又地道。
</Callout>
