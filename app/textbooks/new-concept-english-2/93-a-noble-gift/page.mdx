## The Text 课文

One of the most famous monuments in the world, the Statue of Liberty, **was presented to** the United States of America in the nineteenth century by the people of France. The great statue, which **was designed by** the sculptor Auguste Bartholdi, **took ten years to complete**. The actual figure **was made of copper** supported by a metal framework which **had been especially constructed** by Eiffel. Before it **could be transported** to the United States, a site **had to be found** for it and a pedestal **had to be built**. **The site chosen** was an island at the entrance of New York Harbour. By 1884, a statue which was 151 feet tall **had been erected** in Paris. The following year, it **was taken to pieces** and sent to America. By the end of October 1886, the statue **had been put together** again and it **was officially presented** to the American people by Bartholdi. Ever since then, the great monument **has been a symbol of liberty** for the millions of people who have passed through New York Harbour to make their homes in America.

## 课文注释 Notes on the text

<Callout type="info" title="4 个值得停下来嚼一嚼的点">
  1. **the site chosen** (l.8) — = *the site which was chosen*，“被选中的那块场地”。这里用 **过去分词 chosen 作后置定语**，与定语从句的作用相同，但更简洁。规则：当被修饰的名词与分词是 **被动关系**（场地“被”选）时，用 **过去分词**；主动关系才用现在分词。同型：*the money paid*（= which was paid）/ *the bridge built last year*（= which was built）。
  2. **take ... to pieces** (l.11) — “把……拆卸开”。pieces 用复数，强调拆成一块一块。课文 *it was taken to pieces*（它被拆成了大块）是其 **被动形式**。反义即第 3 条。
  3. **put ... together** (l.12) — “把……装配 / 组装起来”。课文 *the statue had been put together again*（雕像被重新组装起来）。put together 是 **可分动词短语**，宾语短可放中间（put it together），也可放后面。
  4. **make their homes in America** (l.15) — “在美国安家落户”。**make one's home in + 地点** 是固定表达，强调“定居、长住”，比 *live in* 更有“安顿下来”的意味。同型：*Many immigrants made their homes in the New World.*
</Callout>

## 3. 关键句型 The passive voice 被动语态全景

<Callout type="tip" title="一分钟回顾：本课是被动语态的“密集训练场”">
  本课的 **关键句型（Key structures）** 是：*The Statue of Liberty **was presented to** the United States of America.*（复习第 21、45、69 课）。为什么通篇用被动？因为这篇的 **主角是“雕像”**——我们关心的是“它被怎样设计、制造、运输、组装、赠送”，而 **谁** 做这些事反而次要。被动语态的公式永远是 **be 动词（随时态变） + 过去分词（V-ed）**，把动作的“承受者”顶到主语位置。

  把课文动词圈出来，你会看到被动在 **各种时态** 里的样子：

  | 形式 | 课文例句 | 结构 |
  |---|---|---|
  | **一般过去时被动** | *was presented / was designed / was made / was taken / sent* | was/were + V-ed |
  | **过去完成时被动** | *had been especially constructed / had been erected / had been put together* | had been + V-ed |
  | **情态动词被动** | *could be transported* | could be + V-ed |
  | **“had to” + 被动不定式** | *a site had to be found / a pedestal had to be built* | had to be + V-ed |
  | **过去分词作后置定语** | *the site chosen*（= which was chosen） | V-ed 修饰名词 |
  | **by + 动作发出者** | *by the people of France / by Eiffel / by Bartholdi* | by + 施动者 |

  ### ⚠️ 4 个本课最容易踩的雷区

  1. **被动一定要 be + 过去分词**：是 *was **taken*** 不是 was *took*；是 *had been **erected*** 不是 had been *erect*。漏掉过去分词或写成原形 / 过去式都是最常见的错。
  2. **“被……制成”用 made of / made from，不用 made by**：*made **of** copper*（看得出原料是铜）。made by 后面接的是“制造者”（made by Eiffel），不是“原料”。
  3. **had to be + V-ed**：情态意义“必须”的被动——“某事必须被做”。*A site **had to be found**.*（必须找一块场地）。不要写成 had to be *find* / had to *found*。
  4. **过去分词作定语 vs 定语从句**：*the site chosen* = *the site **which was** chosen*。被动关系（场地“被”选）才用过去分词；若是主动（the man choosing the site），就得用现在分词。

  教材练习 B 要求给课文里的动词填上正确形式，标准答案正是这串被动链：*was presented · was designed · was supported · had been (especially) constructed · could be transported · to be found · to be built · chosen · had been erected · was taken · (was) sent · had been put · was (officially) presented*。
</Callout>

## 4. 难点：重音 Word stress 名词 / 动词的重读移位

<Callout type="info" title="同一个词，重音一移、词性就变">
  本课的 **Special difficulties（难点）** 讲的是 **重音（stress）**。英语里有一批词，**拼写完全相同**，但 **重音在前** 时是名词、**重音在后** 时是动词——读错重音，听者就理解错词性。课文里的 **present** 就是典范：

  | 例句 | 词性 | 重音 | 读音 |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | The Statue of Liberty was a **present** from the French people. | 名词（礼物） | 第 1 音节 | /ˈprezənt/ |
  | It was **presented** to the United States of America. | 动词（赠送） | 第 2 音节 | /prɪˈzent/ |

  规律口诀：**“名词重前，动词重后”**（noun first, verb second）。下面这串词都遵守同一规律——

  *accent, conduct, contest, contrast, export, import, increase, insult, produce, protest, record, transport.*

  ### ⚠️ 怎么判断该读哪个重音？

  看 **它在句中的角色**：前面有 **the / a / this / 形容词** 的，多半是 **名词**（重前）；前面有 **助动词 / 主语，后面带宾语** 的，多半是 **动词**（重后）。例如 *the **RE**cord*（名词，唱片 / 纪录）vs *to re**CORD** a song*（动词，录制）。

## 10. 摘要写作 Summary writing (≤ 80 词)

<Callout type="info" title="任务">
  *In not more than 80 words* describe **how the Statue of Liberty came to be built in New York Harbour**. 用不超过 80 个单词，写 **两段**，串起下面 9 个要点（括号里是教材给的连接词提示）：

  1. Statue — presented U.S.A.
  2. French people — 19th century.（用 *which*）
  3. Designed — Bartholdi.（用 *and / Though*）
  4. Built on metal frame — Eiffel.
  5. Site chosen — island — New York Harbour.（用 *where*）
  6. Pedestal built.（用 *and*）
  7. The statue erected Paris 1884.（*The statue was first ...*）
  8. Re-erected two years later — America.（用 *but*）
  9. October 1886 — presented.（用 *and*）
</Callout>

<Callout type="tip" title="参考范文（自己写完再看）">
  The Statue of Liberty was presented to the United States by the French people in the nineteenth century. It was designed by Bartholdi and built on a metal frame made by Eiffel. The site chosen was an island in New York Harbour, where a pedestal was built.

  The statue was first erected in Paris in 1884, but two years later it was re-erected in America, and in October 1886 it was presented to the American people. *(75 words)*

## 11. 作文 Composition (约 150 词)

<Callout type="info" title="任务">
  Imagine you are entering New York Harbour by ship. Write **two paragraphs in about 150 words** using the ideas below. 假设你正乘船进入纽约港，按下面思路写两段：

  1. Sailing into New York Harbour between Long Island and Staten Island: the Statue of Liberty: first impressions.
  2. A first glimpse of New York — Manhattan — skyscrapers: the Empire State Building — the United Nations building — great ships in the harbour — bridges, traffic and people.
</Callout>

<Callout type="warning" title="提示骨架（每段一个画面）">
  - **第 1 段（驶入港口 + 女神像）**：船从 Long Island 与 Staten Island 之间缓缓驶入 → 海风、海鸥、晨雾 → 第一眼望见高举火炬的自由女神 → 它在你心里激起的感受（自由、希望、渺小感）。
  - **第 2 段（第一眼纽约）**：曼哈顿的天际线 → 摩天大楼丛林（帝国大厦、联合国大楼）→ 港湾里穿梭的巨轮 → 大桥、车流、人潮 → 一座永不停歇的城市扑面而来。
</Callout>

<Callout type="tip" title="写作脚手架">
  - **时态主线**：现在时 / 现在进行时写“正在驶入”的临场感（*Our ship is gliding ... the city is rising ...*），让读者身临其境。
  - **“望见”动词库**：*catch sight of, make out, loom into view, come into focus, tower above, rise out of the haze*。
  - **港口画面词库**：*skyline, harbour, ferries, tugboats, ocean liners, suspension bridges, skyscrapers glinting in the sun*。
  - **情绪词库**：*awe-struck, dwarfed by, overwhelmed, a lump in my throat, a sense of arrival*。
  - **结构亮点**：每段用一个长句铺开全景，再用一个短句收束情绪（*Then, suddenly, there she was — Liberty herself.*）。
</Callout>

<Callout type="tip" title="参考范文（自己写完再看）">
  As our ship glides slowly into New York Harbour, passing between Long Island and Staten Island, a salty wind sweeps across the deck and gulls wheel overhead. Then, out of the morning haze, a green figure looms into view: the Statue of Liberty, her torch held high above the water. Everyone falls silent. For the millions who once arrived this way, she must have meant hope; even today, she makes a newcomer feel both small and strangely welcome.

  Beyond her, Manhattan rises like a wall of glass and steel. Skyscrapers crowd the skyline — the Empire State Building piercing the clouds, the United Nations building gleaming by the river. Great ships move slowly through the harbour, while bridges stretch from shore to shore, loaded with traffic. As we draw nearer, the roar of the city reaches us: horns, voices, the endless rush of people. New York never sleeps, and it has clearly been awake for hours. *(150 words)*
</Callout>

## 12. 书信写作 Letter writing

<Callout type="info" title="任务">
  Write **five sentences** which could be used to **begin letters to friends**. 写 5 句可以用作给朋友写信开头的句子。

  好的“开场白”要做到三件事之一：**寒暄问候、解释为什么写、回应对方的上一封信**。语气要 **亲切、口语化**（写给朋友，不是写给公司）。
</Callout>

<Callout type="tip" title="5 句参考开场白（自己先写再看）">
  1. It seems ages since I last wrote to you, so I thought I'd drop you a line at last.
  2. Thank you so much for your long letter — it arrived just when I needed cheering up.
  3. I'm sorry I've taken so long to reply; things have been incredibly busy at this end.
  4. You'll never guess what has happened since we last met — I simply had to tell you straight away.
  5. I hope this letter finds you and the family well; we've all been thinking of you lately.
</Callout>

<Callout type="tip" title="写作脚手架">
  - **常用开场公式**：*It's been a while since ... / Thank you for ... / Sorry I haven't written sooner ... / I've got some news ... / I hope you're well ...*
  - **口语缩写更自然**：*I'd / I'm / I've / you'll / we've* —— 写给朋友尽量用缩写，显得轻松。
  - **避免太正式**：朋友信别用 *Dear Sir / I am writing to inform you*——那是商务信的口吻。
</Callout>

## 13. 拓展练习 Going further

<Callout type="tip" title="自学者建议">
  - **影子跟读 (shadowing)**：用第 1 节的三个 SayIt 按钮跟读 3–5 遍。重点感受 **被动长句的节奏**——*was preSENTED to ... by the PEOPLE of FRANCE*，被动结构里 be 动词轻读、过去分词重读。
  - **复述 (retell)**：合上课文，用 2 分钟把女神像的“一生”讲一遍。**叙事四步**：法国送礼（设计 + 铜身 + 铁架）→ 选址建座 → 巴黎先立、拆运赴美 → 1886 重组揭幕、成为自由象征。
  - **被动改写练习**：把下面主动句改成课文式被动——① The French people presented the statue. ② Bartholdi designed it. ③ Eiffel constructed the framework. ④ They erected it in Paris. ⑤ Workers put it together again.（答案：It was presented / It was designed by Bartholdi / The framework was constructed by Eiffel / It was erected in Paris / It was put together again.）
  - **重音迁移练习**：把 record / export / increase / conduct / present 各造一对句子——一句当名词（重前），一句当动词（重后），大声读出区别。
  - **延伸思考**：为什么这篇通篇用被动？因为它在写 **一件“物”的命运**，而不是某个人的功劳。被动语态把镜头牢牢对准“雕像”，让“谁设计、谁建造、谁赠送”都退为背景里的 *by ...*。下次你写“产品说明、工艺流程、历史文物”这类客观介绍时，被动语态就是你最顺手的工具——它让叙述显得 **客观、正式、聚焦于事物本身**。
</Callout>
