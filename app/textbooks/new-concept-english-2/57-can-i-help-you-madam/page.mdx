## The Text 课文

A woman in jeans **stood at the window** of an expensive shop. Though she **hesitated for a moment**, she finally went in and asked to see a dress that was in the window. The assistant who served her did not like the way she was dressed. **Glancing at her scornfully**, he told her that the dress was sold. The woman **walked out of the shop** angrily and decided to **punish the assistant** next day. She returned to the shop the following morning **dressed in a fur coat**, with a handbag in one hand and a long umbrella in the other. After **seeking out** the rude assistant, she asked for the same dress. **Not realizing who she was**, the assistant was **eager to serve** her this time. **With great difficulty**, he climbed into the shop window to get the dress. As soon as she saw it, the woman said she did not like it. She **enjoyed herself making** the assistant bring almost everything in the window before finally buying the dress she had first asked for.

## 课文注释 Notes on the text

<Callout type="info" title="6 个值得停下来嚼一嚼的点">
  1. **Can I help you, Madam?** (标题) —— "您要买什么，夫人？"这是售货员招呼顾客的常用语。**madam** /ˈmædəm/ 是对妇女的尊称，常用于正式场合或服务行业。口语中也常说 *Can I help you? / May I help you?* 注意：单独写信称呼时 Madam 首字母大写（*Dear Madam*）。

  2. **A woman in jeans** (l.1) —— "一位穿着牛仔裤的妇女"。**in + 衣物** 是英语中极为常见的"穿着"表达法，比 wearing 更简洁。同型：*a man in a black suit*（穿黑西装的男子）/ *the woman in the blue coat*（穿蓝外套的那个女人）/ *a girl in red*（红衣女孩）。注意：这里 in 表"穿着"，不是表地点。

  3. **dressed in a fur coat ... with a handbag in one hand and a long umbrella in the other** (ll.6-8) —— "穿了一件裘皮大衣，一手拎着一只手提包，另一只手拿着一把长柄伞"。三个结构打包在一起，形成了一幅极其完整的"贵妇形象"画面：① **dressed in a fur coat** 是**过去分词短语作伴随状语**，相当于 *She was dressed in ...*，但更紧凑；② **with a handbag in one hand and a long umbrella in the other** 是 **with + 名词 + 介词短语** 的复合结构，也称"独立主格"。同型：*He stood there with his hands in his pockets.* / *She walked in with a baby in her arms.*

  4. **seek out** (l.8) —— "找出、找到"。与单纯的 *look for*（寻找）不同，**seek out** 强调"主动地、有目的地找到（某个人或东西）"。课文 *After seeking out the rude assistant* = 她找到（特地去揪出）那个无礼的售货员。同型：*She sought out the best restaurant in town.* / *He sought out his old friend in the crowd.*

  5. **Not realizing who she was** (l.9) —— "没有认出她是谁"。这是**现在分词短语的否定形式**：**Not + V-ing** 作原因 / 伴随状语。相当于 *Because he did not realize who she was*，但用分词后叙事更流畅。同型：*Not knowing the way, she asked a policeman.* / *Not having finished his homework, he couldn't go out to play.*

  6. **enjoy oneself (in) doing sth.** (ll.11-12) —— "（在做某事中）玩得开心、自得其乐"。**enjoy oneself** 是固定搭配 = "玩得开心"，后接 **(in) + V-ing** 作方式状语（in 常省略）。课文 *She enjoyed herself making the assistant bring almost everything* = 她开心地迫使售货员把几乎所有东西都拿出来。注意这里的 **make sb. do sth.**（使某人做某事，宾补用不带 to 的不定式）。同型：*We enjoyed ourselves swimming in the lake.* / *The children enjoyed themselves playing in the park.*
</Callout>

## 3. 关键句型：At, In, Off and With 介词精讲

<Callout type="tip" title="一分钟回顾：介词虽小，用错意思全变">
  本课 **Key Structures** 集中讲 **at / in / off / with** 四大介词的用法——它们不是"背出来的"，而是**看搭配和场景**来的。

  ### 一、at —— 精确"定位点"

  at 的核心含义是**一个点**（不强调内部/范围），常用于：地址、站点、门口。

  | 例句 | 含义 | 为什么用 at |
  |---|---|---|
  | He lives **at** 27 West Street. | 他住在西大街 27 号。 | 精确门牌号 → 一个"点" |
  | A large crowd was waiting **at** the bus stop. | 一大群人在公共汽车站等候。 | 公交站 → 一个"点" |
  | I'll see you **at** the station. | 我将到车站送你。 | 车站作为碰头"点" |
  | On the way home, we stopped **at** a small village called Puddleton. | 在回家的路上，我们在一个叫普都顿的小村庄停留。 | 村庄作为途经"点" |
  | Someone is **at** the door. | 有人敲门 / 门口有人。 | 门口 → 一个"点" |

  课文例句：*A woman in jeans stood **at** the window of an expensive shop.* —— 她站在橱窗"那一点"（不是在里面 in，也不是在旁边 by）。

  ### 二、in —— 在"空间内部" / 穿着

  in 的核心含义是**在……里面**（空间 / 范围内部）；也可以表**穿着**。

  | 例句 | 含义 | 为什么用 in |
  |---|---|---|
  | There were a lot of people **in** the street. | 街上有许多人。 | 在街道这个空间里 |
  | I would like to live **in** a warm country. | 我想住在一个温暖的国家。 | 在国家 / 区域内部 |
  | He lives **in** Berlin. | 他住在柏林。 | 在城市内部 |
  | Let's go for a walk **in** the park. | 让我们到公园去散散步。 | 在公园范围内 |
  | Where's Tom? He's **in** his room. | 汤姆在哪里？他在自己的房间里。 | 在房间内部 |

  课文例句：*asked to see a dress that was **in** the window* —— 衣服在橱窗**里面**（陈列着）。*A woman **in** jeans* —— **in + 衣物** = "穿着"（把人"包裹"在衣服"里"的隐喻）。

  ### 三、off —— "脱离、离开表面"

  off 的核心含义是**从……表面脱开 / 离开**。

  | 例句 | 含义 | 为什么用 off |
  |---|---|---|
  | The lid came **off** easily. | 这个盖子很容易掉。 | 盖子从容器上"脱开" |
  | Take your coat **off**. | 把你的外套脱下来。 | 衣服从身体上"离开" |
  | The pencil rolled **off** the table. | 铅笔从桌子上滚下去了。 | 铅笔离开桌面 |

  ### 四、with —— "带有 / 伴随着"

  with 的核心含义是**带有、伴随**。可以接：名词（with + n.）、复合结构（with + n. + 介词短语）。

  | 例句 | 含义 |
  |---|---|
  | That man **with** long hair is supposed to be a poet. | 那个留着长发的男人应该是个诗人。 |
  | The police are looking for a man **with** a scar on his face. | 警察正在搜寻一个脸上有疤的男人。 |
  | The woman **with** the brown handbag and long umbrella is a famous novelist. | 那个拎着棕色手提包和长柄伞的女士是一位著名的小说家。 |

  课文例句：*with a handbag in one hand and a long umbrella in the other* —— **with + n. + 介词短语** 复合结构，描绘"伴随的装备"。

  ### ⚠️ 4 个最易踩的雷区

  1. **at vs in —— 大地方用 in，小地点 / 精确点用 at**：*in London*（在城市内部）vs *at 27 West Street*（精确门牌号点）。但车站、门口等"功能点"用 at。
  2. **in + 衣物 = "穿着"**：*the woman in the blue coat* = 穿蓝外套的女人。不要用 *the woman with the blue coat*（那可能是"拿着"蓝外套，不一定是"穿着"）。
  3. **off 表"脱离"，out of 表"从内部出来"**：*walk out of the shop*（从店内走到店外）≠ *the lid came off*（脱离表面）。课文两处都用到了：*walked out of the shop* + climbed **into** the shop window。
  4. **with 复合结构 = "带着……处于某种状态"**：*with a handbag in one hand* —— with + 名词 + 介词短语，极其常用的书面描写结构。
</Callout>

## 4. 难点：Make and Let 使役动词

<Callout type="info" title="make sb. do vs let sb. do——「使」和「让」的语法区别">
  本课 **Special Difficulties** 聚焦两个高频使役动词：**make** 和 **let**。它们的共同点是：后面都接**不带 to 的不定式**（bare infinitive）作宾补。

  ### make —— "迫使、使……（不得不）"

  有一种**强迫 / 导致**的含义。主语使宾语发出动作（宾语可能不愿意，但不得不做）。

  | 例句 | 含义 |
  |---|---|
  | She enjoyed herself **making** the assistant **bring** almost everything in the window. (ll.11-12) | 她开心地**迫使**售货员把橱窗里几乎所有东西都**拿出来**。 |
  | I can't **make** him **change** his mind. | 我无法**使**他**改变**主意。 |
  | The teacher **made** the boy **write** the exercise again. | 教师**叫**那男孩把练习**重做**一遍。 |

  **make sb. do sth.** —— 注意：do 是**不带 to 的不定式**（不是 doing，不是 to do）。

  ### let —— "允许、让（听任）"

  有一种**允许 / 放任**的含义。

  | 例句 | 含义 |
  |---|---|
  | Don't **let** him **persuade** you. | 不要**让**他**说服**你。 |
  | **Let** me **try**. | **让**我**试一试**。 |
  | Don't **let** the children **touch** anything in this room, please. | 请别**让**孩子们**动**这个房间里的任何东西。 |

  **let sb. do sth.** —— 同样是**不带 to 的不定式**。

  ### ⚠️ 3 个关键雷区

  1. **make + sb. + do**（不带 to），但**被动语态要把 to 加回来**：*I was made **to** wait outside.*（被动 → 恢复 to）。主动不用 to：*They made me wait.*
  2. **let 一般不用被动**；要用被动表达"被允许"时改用 *be allowed to*：*We were allowed to go home early.*（不说 *We were let go home.*）
  3. **make sb. do = 强迫（较强硬）** vs **have sb. do = 安排 / 请某人做（较委婉）** vs **get sb. to do = 说服 / 劝某人做（用 to do）**——别记混了。
</Callout>

## 10. 摘要写作 Summary writing (≤ 80 词)

<Callout type="info" title="任务">
  *Answer these questions in not more than 80 words.* 回答下列问题，把答案串成**一个段落**，总词数不超过 80：

  1. Did the woman in jeans hesitate for a moment or not? Did she enter an expensive shop or not? What did she ask to see? (Though ... and)
  2. What did an assistant tell her? When did the woman return? Was she dressed in a fur coat or not? (On being told by)
  3. What was the assistant eager to do this time?
  4. What did she make him bring her? What did the woman finally buy? (After making)
</Callout>

<Callout type="tip" title="参考范文（自己写完再看）">
  Though the woman in jeans hesitated for a moment, she entered an expensive shop and asked to see a dress in the window. On being told by an assistant that the dress was sold, she returned the following morning dressed in a fur coat. Not recognizing her, the assistant was eager to serve her. After making him bring her almost everything in the window, the woman finally bought the dress she had first asked for. *(78 words)*
</Callout>

## 11. 作文 Composition

<Callout type="info" title="任务">
  Write **two or three sentences** using the ideas given below（用以下词组写出 2 至 3 个句子）：

  *The owner of a shop wanted to see how polite his assistants were — dressed as a tramp — went into his shop — asked to see a suit — was thrown out.*

  店主想检验自己店员是否礼貌 → 扮成流浪汉 → 走进店里 → 要求看一套西装 → 被赶了出去。
</Callout>

<Callout type="tip" title="写作脚手架">
  - **时态**：一般过去时叙事。
  - **第一句**：交代老板动机 + 乔装（*dressed as a tramp* 过去分词作状语）。
  - **第二 / 三句**：进店 → 要求 → 被赶（*be thrown out* 被动语态）。
  - **收尾**：结局可以点题：店主 thus learned that his assistants were not as polite as he had thought.
</Callout>

<Callout type="tip" title="参考范文（自己写完再看）">
  The owner of a shop once wanted to see how polite his own assistants were. Dressed as a tramp, he went into his shop and asked to see an expensive suit. The assistant glanced scornfully at the shabby figure and, without a word, threw him out. The "tramp" — who was, in fact, the boss — thus discovered the ugly side of his staff. *(62 words)*
</Callout>

## 12. 书信写作 Letter writing

<Callout type="info" title="任务">
  熟记以下两个开头语（opening phrases），然后用每个开头语各写一段不超过两句话的短文：

  1. **What a surprise it was to ...**（……是多么令人吃惊啊！）
  2. **Forgive me for not writing earlier, but ...**（请原谅我没有早点写信，但是……）
</Callout>

<Callout type="tip" title="写作脚手架">
  - **What a surprise it was to ...** 后面接动词原形（to + V），是一个感叹句。可接：*see him again / hear from you / find the shop still open after all these years*。
  - **Forgive me for not writing earlier, but ...** → *forgive sb. for (not) doing sth.* 请原谅某人（没）做某事 → but 后面解释原因：*I have been terribly busy with my exams / I was away on a business trip*。
</Callout>

<Callout type="tip" title="参考范文（自己写完再看）">
  **What a surprise it was to** run into my old teacher in that little bookshop in Paris! I had not seen him since I left school ten years ago, yet he remembered my name instantly.

  **Forgive me for not writing earlier, but** I have been preparing for my university entrance exams these past three months. Now that they are finally over, I can at last sit down and tell you all my news.
</Callout>

## 13. 拓展练习 Going further

<Callout type="tip" title="自学者建议">
  - **影子跟读 (shadowing)**：用第 1 节的三个 SayIt 按钮各跟读 3-5 遍。重点感受：① *Glancing at her scornfully* 中 scornfully 的重音落在第一音节，语调带"高冷、不屑"；② *with a handbag in one hand and a long umbrella in the other* 这句话像"用英语画画"——逐个细节叠加，读时节奏均匀、不赶；③ 最后一句 *enjoyed herself making ...* 要读出"幸灾乐祸"的得意感。
  - **复述 (retell)**：合上课文，把这两天的"换装复仇"讲一遍。**三步走**：① 第一天（牛仔裤 → 被冷遇 → 借口 sold）→ ② 第二天变身（裘皮大衣 + 手包 + 长伞 → 售货员没认出 → 殷勤爬窗）→ ③ 复仇（说不喜欢 → 一件件往外拿 → 最后才买最初那件）。
  - **造句迁移**：用今天的 5 个高价值结构造你自己的句子——
    1. *V-ing ..., 主语 + V*（现在分词作伴随状语）—— *Smiling at the customer, the waiter took her order.*
    2. *dressed in + 衣物*（过去分词作状语）—— *Dressed in a black suit, he walked into the interview room.*
    3. *with + n. + 介词短语*（复合结构 / 独立主格）—— *He stood at the door with a book in his hand.*
    4. *make sb. do sth.*（迫使某人做）—— *The joke made everyone laugh.*
    5. *Not + V-ing ..., 主语 + V*（分词否定作原因状语）—— *Not wanting to be late, she took a taxi.*
  - **介词意识**：本课集中训练了 **at / in / off / with** 的用法。下次用这些介词时，心里默念"坐标"：at = 一个点（门口 / 站台 / 橱窗前）；in = 空间内部 + 穿着；off = 脱离表面；with = 带有 / 伴随。"以貌取人"的故事本身也是深刻的文化主题——**judging people by their appearance**（以貌取人）既在售货员身上得到体现，也值得学习者自省。
  - **延伸思考**：本课的两个分词结构——**Glancing at her scornfully**（现在分词主动）和 **dressed in a fur coat**（过去分词被动）——刚好构成"分词作状语"的一对经典示例。判断用哪个的关键在于**主语是主动还是被动**：主语发出动作 → 现在分词 V-ing；主语承受状态 → 过去分词 V-ed。一课两招，吃透了以后英文写作的信息密度会上一个台阶。
</Callout>
