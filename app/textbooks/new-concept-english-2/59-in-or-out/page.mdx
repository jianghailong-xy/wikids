  **Why did Rex run away? 雷克斯为什么离家出走了？** 带着这个问题读课文，重点留意全篇是怎样用 **used to / would（过去的习惯）**、**so that（目的状语）** 和 **as soon as + 一般现在时（时间状语从句）** 把一只狗的"作妖"过程讲得活灵活现。
</Callout>

## The Text 课文

Our dog, Rex, **used to sit** outside our front gate and bark. Every time he wanted to come into the garden he **would bark** until someone opened the gate. As the neighbours **complained of the noise**, my husband **spent weeks training him** to **press his paw on the latch** to **let himself in**. Rex soon **became an expert at opening the gate**.

However, when I went shopping last week, I noticed him in the garden near the gate. This time he was **barking so that someone would let him out**! Since then, he **has developed another bad habit**. **As soon as he opens the gate** from the outside, he comes into the garden and waits until the gate shuts. Then he sits and barks until someone lets him out. After this he immediately lets himself in and begins barking again. Yesterday my husband removed the gate and Rex **got so annoyed we have not seen him since**.

## 课文注释 Notes on the text

<Callout type="info" title="4 个值得停下来嚼一嚼的点">
  1. **every time** (l.1) —— 此处是**连词**，作"每当""无论何时"讲，引出**时间状语从句**，相当于 *whenever*。课文 *Every time he wanted to come into the garden he would bark ...* = 每当它想进花园，就汪汪叫。同型连词还有 *each time / the moment / the minute*。
  2. **become an expert at + doing** (l.5) —— "成了……能手"。注意介词用 **at**（不是 in / of），后接动名词。课文 *Rex soon became an expert at opening the gate* = 雷克斯很快成了开门的专家。同型：*She is an expert at solving puzzles.*
  3. **so that someone would let him out** (ll.7-8) —— **so that** 是连词，引导**目的状语从句**，作"为的是、以便"讲，从句里常配 **would / could / might** 等情态动词。课文 *This time he was barking so that someone would let him out!* = 这次它叫，是为了让人把它放出去。
  4. **got so annoyed (that) we have not seen him since** (l.11) —— 句中藏着一个 **so ... that ...** 的**结果状语从句**，表示"如此……以至于……"，口语里 **that 常被省略**。*Rex got so annoyed (that) we have not seen him since* = 雷克斯气坏了，以至于此后我们再没见过它。*since* 在这里是副词，作"自那以后"讲。
</Callout>

## 3. 关键句型 过去的习惯 + 目的状语 + 时间状语从句

<Callout type="tip" title="一分钟回顾">
  本课是一篇"叙述习惯 + 讲一件新鲜事"的短文，语法重心落在三组结构上。把课文动词圈出来，你会看到这样的分布：

  | 结构 | 课文例句 | 用法 |
  |---|---|---|
  | **used to + 动词原形** | *Rex **used to sit** outside our front gate and bark.* | 过去经常（如今不再）——可表过去的**动作或状态** |
  | **would + 动词原形** | *Every time he wanted to come in he **would bark** ...* | 过去**反复发生的动作**（不能表过去的状态） |
  | **so that + 从句** | *he was barking **so that** someone would let him out* | **目的**状语从句（"为的是"），常配 would / could / might |
  | **as soon as + 一般现在时** | ***As soon as he opens** the gate ..., he **comes** into the garden* | 时间状语从句"主将从现"——这里表**反复习惯**，全用一般现在 |
  | **spend + 时间 + (in) doing** | *my husband **spent weeks training** him ...* | 花（时间）做某事 |
  | **so ... (that) ...** | *Rex got **so annoyed** (that) we have not seen him since* | **结果**状语从句（that 可省略） |

  ### ⚠️ 4 个本课最容易踩的雷区

  1. **used to do ≠ be used to doing**：*used to sit* = 过去常坐（现在不坐了）；*be used to sitting* = 习惯于坐（强调现在的习惯）。一个看 **to + 原形**，一个看 **to + doing**，别混。
  2. **would 只表"反复动作"，不表"状态"**：*He would bark*（反复的动作）✓；想说"过去他很高/这里曾有座桥"这类**状态**，只能用 *used to*，不能用 would。
  3. **so that（目的）vs so ... that（结果）**：*barking **so that** someone would let him out*（目的，配情态动词）；*got **so** annoyed **that** ...*（结果，接事实陈述）。注意后者 so 后面跟形容词 / 副词。
  4. **时间状语从句"主将从现"**：*As soon as he **opens** the gate ..., he **comes** in.* —— as soon as / when / until / every time 引导的从句，即便指将来或习惯，也用**一般现在时**，不用 will。
</Callout>

## 4. 难点：表示目的的几种方式 Special difficulties

<Callout type="info" title="To / in order to / so as to / so that / in order that —— 五种“为了”">
  课文的 *Special difficulties* 把"表示目的"的几种连接方式排在一起对比。关键看**后面接什么**、**前后主语是否一致**：

  | 方式 | 后接 | 主语关系 | 教材例句 |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | **to + 原形** | 动词原形 | 前后**同一主语** | *He ran to the station **to** catch the train.* |
  | **in order to + 原形** | 动词原形 | 前后**同一主语**（比 to 更正式） | *I told him about it **in order to** help you.* |
  | **so as to + 原形** | 动词原形 | 前后**同一主语**（否定 so as not to） | *I opened the door quietly **so as not to** disturb him.* |
  | **so that + 从句** | 主语 + may/might/would/could | 前后**可不同主语** | *He was barking **so that** someone would let him out.* (ll.7-8) |
  | **in order that + 从句** | 主语 + may/might/should | 前后**可不同主语**（更正式） | *He works hard **in order that** he may pass his exams.* |
  | **for + 宾语 + to do** | 名词/代词 + 不定式 | 引出**动作的执行者** | *He sent a fax **for** his mother **to** learn the good news.* |

  ### ⚠️ 选用口诀

  - **同主语**、想简洁 → **to / in order to / so as to + 原形**。
  - **换了主语**（"为了让别人……"）→ 只能用 **so that / in order that + 从句**，且从句里要有情态动词。
  - **否定目的**（"为了不……"）→ 首选 *so as not to + 原形* 或 *so that ... would not ...*。
  - 课文 *barking **so that** someone would let him out* —— 主句主语是 he（狗），目的从句主语是 someone（人），**主语不同**，所以必须用 so that，不能用 to。
</Callout>

## 10. 摘要写作 Summary writing (≤ 80 词)

<Callout type="info" title="任务">
  *Answer these questions in not more than 80 words.* 回答下列问题，将答案组成**一个段落**，不超过 **80 个单词**。括号内是建议使用的连接词：

  1. Why did our dog, Rex, use to sit outside the front gate and bark? *(so that)*
  2. Did my husband train him to open the gate himself or not? Has Rex developed another bad habit or not? *(Ever since)*
  3. Why does he bark when he is in the garden? *(so that)*
  4. Does he let himself in after this or not? Does he bark until someone opens the gate again or not? *(and)*
  5. Has my husband removed the gate or not? Has Rex disappeared or not? *(Now that)*
</Callout>

<Callout type="tip" title="参考范文（自己写完再看）">
  Rex used to sit outside the front gate and bark so that someone would let him in. My husband trained him to open the gate himself, but ever since, Rex has developed another bad habit. When he is in the garden, he barks so that someone will let him out, and after this he lets himself in and barks until someone opens the gate again. Now that my husband has removed the gate, Rex has disappeared. *(74 words)*
</Callout>

## 11. 作文 Composition

<Callout type="info" title="任务">
  Write **two or three sentences** using the ideas given below 用以下词组写出 2 至 3 个句子来：

  *My husband put the gate back — kept it locked — Rex returned — delighted at first — could not open the gate — dissatisfied — disappeared.*
</Callout>

<Callout type="warning" title="提示骨架">
  - 先讲丈夫的"对策"：把门装回去 + 上锁（*put the gate back and kept it locked*）。
  - 再写 Rex 的"反应曲线"：回来 → 起初高兴 → 打不开门 → 不满 → 又消失（*returned → delighted at first → could not open → dissatisfied → disappeared*）。
  - 时态主线用**一般过去时**叙事；表"先后"可用过去完成（*had locked*）。
</Callout>

<Callout type="tip" title="参考范文（自己写完再看）">
  My husband put the gate back, but this time he kept it locked. Rex returned home and was delighted at first, but when he found that he could not open the gate, he soon grew dissatisfied. A few days later, he disappeared once more. *(43 words)*
</Callout>

## 12. 书信写作 Letter writing

<Callout type="info" title="任务">
  *How would the following appear in a letter?* 在书信里，下面这些日期、地址词该怎么写（含缩写形式）：

  *August the first; the third of December; July the second; Avenue; Place; Street; London, East Central 4.*
</Callout>

<Callout type="tip" title="写作脚手架">
  - **日期**：英式书信常写成"日 + 月"或"月 + 日"——
    - August the first → **1st August** / **August 1st**（亦可写 1 August）
    - the third of December → **3rd December** / **December 3rd**
    - July the second → **2nd July** / **July 2nd**
  - **街道类词的缩写**：
    - Avenue → **Ave.**
    - Place → **Pl.**
    - Street → **St.**
  - **邮政区**：London, East Central 4 → **London, E.C.4**（伦敦东中心 4 区）。
  - 写信地址一般**靠右上角**、按"门牌号 + 街名 → 城市 + 邮区 → 日期"自上而下排列。
</Callout>

  Mary

## 13. 拓展练习 Going further

<Callout type="tip" title="自学者建议">
  - **影子跟读 (shadowing)**：用第 1 节的三个 SayIt 按钮跟读 3–5 遍。重点感受 *so that someone would let him **OUT**!* 句末那个略带无奈的强调重音。
  - **复述 (retell)**：合上课文，用 90 秒把 Rex 的故事讲一遍。**三段式**：旧毛病（蹲门外叫）→ 训练（学会按门闩自己进来）→ 新花样（进来、等门关、叫着出去、再进来……循环）→ 结局（门被拆，气得离家出走）。
  - **造句迁移**：用今天的 5 个核心结构造你自己的句子——
    1. *S + used to + 原形*（过去常常）—— *I used to walk to school every day.*
    2. *Every time + 从句, S + would + 原形*（每当……就……）—— *Every time it rained, he would forget his umbrella.*
    3. *... so that + S + would/could + 原形*（为了……）—— *She spoke slowly so that everyone could understand.*
    4. *As soon as + 一般现在, 主句一般现在 / 将来*（一……就……）—— *As soon as the bell rings, the children run out.*
    5. *spend + 时间 + (in) doing*（花时间做）—— *We spent an hour tidying the room.*
  - **延伸思考**：本课表面是个"狗作妖"的笑话，骨子里却把 **used to / would（过去习惯）** 与 **as soon as ... 一般现在（当下习惯）** 这两套"讲习惯"的时态并排展示——前半段讲"以前的老毛病"，后半段切到"现在的新花样"。下次描写某人/某物的习惯时，问自己一句：这是**过去**的常态（used to / would），还是**现在**的常态（一般现在时）？时态一选对，叙事的"时间层次"就立住了。
</Callout>
