## The Text 课文

The former Prime Minister, Mr. Wentworth Lane, **was defeated** in the recent elections. He **is now retiring** from political life and **has gone abroad**. My friend, Patrick, **has always been a fanatical opponent** of Mr. Lane's Radical Progressive Party. After the elections, Patrick went to the former Prime Minister's house. When he **asked if Mr. Lane lived there**, the policeman on duty told him that since his defeat, the ex-Prime Minister **had gone abroad**. On the following day, Patrick went to the house again. The same policeman **was just walking slowly past the entrance, when** Patrick asked the same question. **Though a little suspicious this time**, the policeman gave him the same answer. The day after, Patrick went there once more and asked exactly the same question. This time, the policeman **lost his temper**. 'I told you yesterday and the day before yesterday,' he shouted, 'Mr. Lane was defeated in the elections. He has retired from political life and gone to live abroad!'

'I know,' answered Patrick, 'but I love to hear you say it!'

## 课文注释 Notes on the text

<Callout type="info" title="6 个值得停下来嚼一嚼的点">
  1. **the elections** (title / l.1) — \"大选\"。在英语里 **election 常用复数形式 elections**，后接表示复数的动词，用来指 **全国性的选举**（议会改选往往同时选出许多席位，故用复数）。课文 *was defeated in the recent elections* / *Mr. Lane was defeated in the elections* 都用复数。
  2. **Prime Minister** (l.1) — \"首相\"。注意构词家族：**former Prime Minister**（前首相，former = 从前的）、**ex-Prime Minister**（前首相，**ex-** 是前缀\"前……\"，用于名词前，如 *ex-wife / ex-president*）—— 三种说法本课都出现了，互为同义。
  3. **He is now retiring from political life** (l.2) — \"他现在正退出政界\"。这里用 **现在进行时表\"正在 / 即将发生\"** 的变化（retire from = 从……退下来 / 退出）。同型：*She is leaving the company next month.*（现在进行表最近计划）。
  4. **asked if Mr. Lane lived there** (l.5-6) — \"询问莱恩先生是否住在那里\"。**间接引语（reported question）**：① 一般疑问句变间接引语用 **if / whether** 引导；② 语序变成\"陈述句语序\"（主 + 谓）；③ 主句 *asked* 是过去时，从句动词随之\"退一格\"成 **lived**（时态后移）。
  5. **was just walking ... when ...** (l.8-9) — \"正慢慢走着……，这时……\"。**过去进行时（背景动作）+ when + 一般过去时（突发动作）**：长动作做\"背景\"，短动作\"打断\"它。**just** 加强\"正好那一刻\"。同型：*I was having a bath when the phone rang.*
  6. **Though a little suspicious this time** (l.10) — \"这一次虽然有点疑心\"。这是一个 **让步状语从句的省略**：主语和系动词都省掉了，完整应是 *Though the policeman was a little suspicious this time*。当从句主语与主句主语一致且含 be 动词时，常这样\"瘦身\"。另：**lost his temper**（l.12）= 发脾气、发火（固定搭配，下面\"难点\"会专门讲 temper 与 mood 的区别）。
</Callout>

## 3. 关键句型 Review of verb forms 时态全景复习

<Callout type="tip" title="一分钟回顾">
  本课是 **复习课**（教材 Key structures: *Review KS74-82*）。一段不到 200 词的短文，像\"时态阅兵\"一样把多种时态 / 语态依次走了一遍。把课文动词圈出来，分布如下：

  | 时态 / 形式 | 课文例句 | 用途 |
  |---|---|---|
  | **一般过去 + 被动** | *Mr. Lane **was defeated** in the elections.* | 过去发生、受动者作主语 |
  | **现在进行（计划 / 变化）** | *He **is now retiring** from political life.* | 正在 / 即将发生 |
  | **现在完成** | *He **has gone** abroad. / Patrick **has always been** an opponent.* | 过去到现在、对现在有影响 |
  | **过去完成** | *since his defeat, the ex-Prime Minister **had gone** abroad.* | 过去之前的\"更早\" |
  | **过去进行 + when** | *The policeman **was just walking** ... **when** Patrick **asked** ...* | 长背景被短动作打断 |
  | **间接引语（时态后移）** | *He asked **if** Mr. Lane **lived** there.* | 转述疑问句 |
  | **现在完成（持续到现在）** | *He **has been** abroad for some time now.*（练习题） | for + 一段时间 + now |

  ### ⚠️ 4 个本课最容易踩的雷区

  1. **has gone vs went**：*has gone abroad*（现在完成）强调\"现在人不在国内\"这个 **当前结果**；若只叙述过去动作用 *went*。课文先用 *has gone*（说现状），转述时又用 *had gone*（过去的更早）。
  2. **still + 现在时**：*He has always been a fanatical opponent and he still **is**.* —— 承前省略 *an opponent*，时态从\"现在完成\"切回\"一般现在\"表 **如今依旧**，所以用 **is**，不是 has / was。
  3. **be opposed / suspicious + 介词**：固定搭配 **be opposed TO sth.**（反对）、**be suspicious OF sb.**（对……起疑）。介词错了整句就破。
  4. **was walking ... when ...**：\"过去进行\"做背景、\"一般过去\"做打断，**两个动作不能都用进行时**，也不能都用一般过去（那样就读不出\"打断\"的层次）。
</Callout>

## 4. 难点：temper 与 mood Special difficulties

<Callout type="info" title="经常容易混淆的词：temper 和 mood">
  课文 *the policeman **lost his temper*** 把 **temper** 推到台前。教材 *Special difficulties* 专门辨析 **temper（脾气，多与\"怒\"相关）** 与 **mood（心境 / 情绪，未必关乎怒）**：

  | 短语 | 含义 | 是否\"生气\" |
  |---|---|---|
  | **lose one's temper** | 发脾气、发火 | ✅ 生气了（= got angry） |
  | **keep your temper!** | 别发火！沉住气！ | 提醒\"别生气\" |
  | **in a good temper** | 脾气好、没在气头上 | ❌ 不生气（= not angry） |
  | **in a (very) bad temper** | 脾气很坏、正在气头上 | ✅ 生气（= is angry） |
  | **in a good mood** | 心情好、兴致高 | ❌ 单纯\"高兴\"（= cheerful） |
  | **in a bad mood** | 心情不好 | ❓ 不开心，但 **未必生气** |
  | **in the mood for sth.** | 有兴致做某事、想做某事 | 表\"想做\"（= feel like） |

  ### ⚠️ 一句话记住区别
  **temper 围着\"怒\"打转**（好脾气=没发火，坏脾气=正发火）；**mood 是更宽的\"心境\"**——*in a bad mood* 只是\"不开心\"，不一定是\"生气\"。所以 *lost his temper* 必然是\"动了肝火\"，而 *in a bad mood* 可能只是\"提不起劲\"。

## 9. 句子结构改写 Sentence structure

<Callout type="info" title="教材原题：改写后对照课文第 9-10 行">
  把 *just as*（正当……时）引导的句子，改写成本课地道的 **was just doing ... when ...**（正做着……，这时……）结构：

  **原句：** Just as the same policeman was walking slowly past the entrance, Patrick asked the same question.

  **改写（= 课文原句）：** *The same policeman **was just walking** slowly past the entrance, **when** Patrick asked the same question.*

  要点：① 主语 *The same policeman* 提前；② \"正走着\"用 **过去进行 was (just) walking** 作背景；③ \"问\"这个突发动作用 **when + 一般过去 asked** 引出。这是 NCE 里描写\"长动作被短动作打断\"的看家句型。
</Callout>

## 11. 摘要写作 Summary writing (≤ 80 词)

<Callout type="info" title="任务">
  *Give an account of what happened **in not more than 80 words**.* 用 **不超过 80 词** 写出所发生的事情。教材给了 9 个要点 + 两套连词，要求 **写成两段**：

  **第一段（用左侧连词 After having / who / and / whether / When 串起）：**
  1. Defeated — elections（落选）
  2. Prime Minister retired — abroad（退出政界、出国）
  3. Patrick — fanatical opponent — Party（狂热反对者）
  4. Went — house — three times（三次上门）
  5. Asked policeman（问警察）
  6. Mr. Lane's house（莱恩的住处）

  **第二段（用右侧连词 Since / As / Each time / if / he was told / but 串起）：**
  7. Third time — angry policeman（第三次：警察发火）
  8. Told — defeat, retirement（被告知落选、退隐）
  9. Patrick said — something he loved to hear（帕特里克说：这正是他爱听的）
</Callout>

<Callout type="tip" title="参考范文（自己写完再看）">
  After having been defeated in the elections, the former Prime Minister, Mr. Lane, retired from political life and went abroad. Patrick, who was a fanatical opponent of Mr. Lane's Party, went to his house three times and each time asked the policeman whether Mr. Lane lived there.

  Since Patrick kept returning, the policeman grew suspicious, and the third time he lost his temper. As he was told yet again that Mr. Lane had been defeated and had retired abroad, Patrick was delighted — for that was exactly what he loved to hear. *(78 words)*
</Callout>

## 12. 作文 Composition (约 150 词)

<Callout type="info" title="任务">
  Write **two paragraphs in about 150 words** using the ideas given below：

  1. A politician was giving a pre-election speech: big promises — more houses, schools etc. — better foreign policy.
  2. Members of the audience asked rude questions — the politician lost his temper — said that the audience did not deserve more houses, etc. — walked off angrily — defeated in the election.
</Callout>

<Callout type="warning" title="提示骨架">
  - **第一段（竞选演说）**：政客登台 → 慷慨许诺（盖更多房子 / 学校、更好的外交政策）→ 听众起初被打动。
  - **第二段（翻车现场）**：听众抛出无礼的问题 → 政客 **lost his temper** → 反唇相讥说\"你们不配有更多房子\" → 气冲冲离场 → 结果大选落败。
</Callout>

<Callout type="tip" title="写作脚手架">
  - **时态主线**：一般过去时叙事（*promised / asked / lost / walked off / was defeated*）；过去进行作背景（*was giving a speech / while he was speaking*）。
  - **\"许诺\"动词库**：*promised, pledged, vowed, guaranteed, assured the crowd that ...*。
  - **\"发火\"表达库**：*lost his temper, flew into a rage, snapped at the audience, retorted angrily, stormed off the platform*。
  - **结构亮点**：用 *No sooner had he begun than ...* / *Hardly had he finished when ...* 制造\"刚……就……\"的戏剧节奏。
</Callout>

<Callout type="tip" title="参考范文（自己写完再看）">
  Last week, a politician was giving a pre-election speech in the town hall. He made one big promise after another: he would build more houses and better schools, lower taxes and bring in a wiser foreign policy. For a while the audience listened politely, and a few even cheered.

  Then some members of the audience began to ask rude questions about his past record. At first the politician tried to smile, but soon he lost his temper. He shouted that such an ungrateful audience did not deserve more houses or schools at all, and walked off the platform angrily, leaving the hall in uproar. Not surprisingly, when the votes were counted a week later, he was heavily defeated in the election. *(118 words)*
</Callout>

## 13. 书信写作 Letter writing

<Callout type="info" title="任务">
  **The Conclusion 结束语** —— 熟记以下结束语，并据此写一段约 50 词的\"写信目的（Purpose）\"，使该信可以用这句话收尾：

  > *I am very sorry for all the trouble this has caused you. I wish you good luck / every success in …*

  Exercise: *In about 50 words write the Purpose for a letter which has one of the above phrases as its conclusion.* 写出约 50 词的\"目的\"段落。
</Callout>

<Callout type="tip" title="写作脚手架">
  - **判断信的性质**：能用\"为给您添的麻烦致歉 + 祝您好运\"收尾的，多半是 **道歉信 / 辞别信 / 退订信**——先在\"目的\"段交代\"出了什么岔子\"或\"为何要分别\"。
  - **目的段三步走**：① 点明事由（*I am writing to apologize for ... / to let you know that I shall be leaving ...*）；② 简述经过 / 原因（*Owing to ... I am no longer able to ...*）；③ 表达歉意 / 祝愿，自然接上结束语。
  - **时态混搭**：*I am writing*（现在进行表当下意图）/ *has caused*（现在完成表已造成的影响）/ *I wish you*（一般现在表祝愿）。
</Callout>

<Callout type="tip" title="参考范文（自己写完再看）">
  I am writing to let you know that I shall have to cancel the music lessons we arranged for my son this term. He has been unwell for several weeks, and the doctor has advised a long rest, so he cannot possibly keep to the timetable we agreed on.

  I am very sorry for all the trouble this has caused you. I wish you every success in the new term. *(63 words)*
</Callout>

## 14. 拓展练习 Going further

<Callout type="tip" title="自学者建议">
  - **影子跟读 (shadowing)**：用第 1 节的三个 SayIt 按钮各跟读 3-5 遍。重点体会结尾那句 *but I love to hear you say it!* 的俏皮语气——重音落在 **love** 和 **say**，把\"明知故问\"的得意感读出来。
  - **复述 (retell)**：合上课文，用 1 分钟把故事讲一遍。**三段法**：背景（前首相落选出国 + Patrick 是死硬反对者）→ 经过（连续三天上门问同一问题 → 警察从平静到疑心到发火）→ 点睛（Patrick：我知道，但就爱听你说）。
  - **造句迁移**：用今天的 5 个高价值结构造你自己的句子——
    1. *was just doing ... when ...*（正做着……，这时……）—— *I was just leaving the office when the phone rang.*
    2. *ask if / whether + 陈述语序*（间接疑问）—— *She asked me whether I knew the way.*
    3. *had + 过去分词*（过去之前的更早）—— *When I arrived, the train had already left.*
    4. *be opposed to / be suspicious of*（介词搭配）—— *They were strongly opposed to the plan.*
    5. *lose one's temper*（发火）—— *Don't lose your temper over such a small thing.*
  - **延伸思考**：本课是 NCE2 里少见的 **\"复习课\"**——它不靠新词新句，而是把前几课的时态在一段小故事里 **一次性巡演**。下次写英文叙事时，记住这\"四把尺子\"：主线动作用一般过去；插入回顾用过去完成；正在发生的背景用过去进行；持续到现在的状态用现在完成。把时态用准，平淡的小事也能写出层次。最后别忘了——好故事常常把\"包袱\"留到最后一句：*I know, but I love to hear you say it!* 一句话，人物的执拗与得意尽显。
</Callout>
