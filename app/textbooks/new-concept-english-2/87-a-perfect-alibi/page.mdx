<Callout type="info" title="Listen first 听前思考">
  一桩凶杀案。一名嫌疑人被带到探长面前——他有一个看似**天衣无缝的不在犯罪现场证据 (a perfect alibi)**：案发当时，他正坐在 **8 点钟开往伦敦的火车上**，雇主能证明他按时到了公司。听上去无懈可击。可探长不动声色地问了几个细节：几点到的车站？有没有注意到什么异常？然后他抛出一句 *“I suggest that you are not telling the truth.”* ——原来，**案发那天早晨，8 点钟的那班车根本没有发车**……
  **What was wrong with the man's story?（这个人的说法到底错在哪里？）** 带着这个问题听录音、读课文，重点留意全篇是怎样用**对话**展开，以及课后如何把这段对话改写成**间接引语 (reported speech)** 的新闻报道——*He said that ... / He asked if ... / He added that ...*。
</Callout>

## The Text 课文

'**At the time the murder was committed**, I **was travelling on the 8 o'clock train** to London,' said the man.

'Do you always **catch such an early train**?' asked the inspector.

'Of course I do,' answered the man. 'I **must be at work** at 10 o'clock. **My employer will confirm** that I was there on time.'

'Would a later train **get you to work on time**?' asked the inspector.

'I suppose it would, but I never catch a later train.'

'At what time did you arrive at the station?'

'**At ten to eight.** I bought a paper and waited for the train.'

'And you didn't notice anything unusual?'

'Of course not.'

'I suggest,' said the inspector, 'that **you are not telling the truth**. I suggest that you did not catch the 8 o'clock train, but that **you caught the 8.25** which would still get you to work on time. You see, on the morning of the murder, the 8 o'clock train **did not run at all**. It **broke down** at Ferngreen station and **was taken off the line**.'

## 课文注释 Notes on the text

<Callout type="info" title="4 个值得停下来嚼一嚼的点">
  1. **At the time the murder was committed**（l.1）—— “在凶杀发生的时候”。*the murder was committed* 可以看作是一个**定语从句修饰 time**（省略了关系副词 when）。**at the time** 意为 *at the moment when something happened*（在某事发生的那一刻）。注意 **commit** 与“罪、错”搭配：*commit a murder / a crime / a mistake*。
  2. **on time**（l.5）—— “准时，按时”（不早不晚，恰在规定时间）。要与 **in time** 区分：*in time* 是“及时、来得及”（赶在某事之前）。课文 *I was there on time*（我按时到岗）；对比 *We arrived just in time to catch the train.*（我们正好赶上火车）。
  3. **And you didn't notice anything unusual?**（l.11）—— “难道你没有注意到什么异常情况吗？”这是一个由**陈述句语序 + 句末问号**构成的问句（口语里读**升调**），常用来表示追问、惊讶或确认。同型：*You're coming, then? / He said that?*
  4. **the 8.25**（l.14）—— 指“8 点 25 分的那班火车”。英语里用 **the + 时间数字**指代某一班车／航班：*the 8 o'clock train*（8 点钟那班车）/ *catch the 9.15*（赶 9 点 15 的车）。读作 *the eight twenty-five*。
</Callout>

## 3. 关键句型 间接引语 Reported speech（KS 15 / 39 / 63）

<Callout type="tip" title="一分钟回顾">
  本课课文是一段**对话（直接引语）**，而课后练习要你把它改写成一篇**新闻报道**——这就是教材点名复习的 **间接引语 (reported speech)**：*He said that ... / He told me ... / He asked ...*。改写时盯紧三件事：**时态后移、人称变化、问句变陈述语序**。

  ### A. 陈述句 → say / tell + that 从句（主句过去 → 从句时态后移）

  | 直接引语 | 间接引语 | 变化 |
  |---|---|---|
  | 'I **am** travelling ...' | He said that he **was** travelling ... | 现在 → 过去 |
  | 'My employer **will** confirm ...' | He said that his employer **would** confirm ... | will → would |
  | 'I **must** be at work ...' | He said that he **had to** be at work ... | must → had to |
  | 'I never **catch** a later train.' | He said that he never **caught** a later train. | 现在 → 过去 |

  ⚠️ **say vs tell**：*say (that) ...*（后面**不直接跟人**）/ *tell **sb.** (that) ...*（后面**必须跟人**）。课文 *said the man* ✓；若要带人则 *He told the inspector that ...*。

  ### B. 疑问句 → ask + 连接词 + 陈述句语序（去掉助动词 do/does/did）

  | 直接引语（问句） | 间接引语 | 规则 |
  |---|---|---|
  | 'Do you always catch ...?' | He asked **if/whether** he always **caught** ... | 一般疑问 → **if/whether** + 陈述语序 |
  | 'At what time did you arrive ...?' | He asked **what time** he **had arrived** ... | 特殊疑问 → **疑问词** + 陈述语序 |
  | 'Would a later train get you ...?' | He asked **if** a later train **would get** him ... | 助动词回归陈述位置 |

  ### C. “I suggest (that) ...” —— 课后改写练习

  教材最后的 *Sentence structure* 让你把 *In my opinion you are not telling the truth.* 改写成课文第 13 行的句式：**I suggest (that) you are not telling the truth.**（that 可省略）。这里 *suggest* = “（委婉地）指出、提醒”。

  ### ⚠️ 4 个本课最容易踩的雷区

  1. **间接疑问句不再用 do/does/did，也不倒装**：*He asked **what time he arrived*** ✓；*He asked what time did he arrive* ✗。
  2. **一般疑问句要加 if / whether**：*He asked **if** he always caught ...* ✓；少了 if 就接不上从句。
  3. **时态后移**：主句是过去时 (said/asked)，从句一般要后移一个时态（is→was, will→would, must→had to）。
  4. **人称随说话人改**：直接引语里的 *I / you / my* 在报道里通常变成 *he / him / his*。
</Callout>

## 4. 难点：选词与搭配 Word choice & collocations

<Callout type="info" title="本课多项选择里反复考的 5 组词">
  本课没有单设 *Special Difficulties*，但课后 *Structure* 与 *Vocabulary* 单选题密集地考了一批**搭配与近义词辨析**。它们错起来往往不是“语感”，而是**固定搭配**问题——记住规则就一劳永逸：

  | 考点 | 正确 | 易错点 |
  |---|---|---|
  | **说谎** | *tell a lie* | 不能说 say / talk / speak a lie；说真话是 *tell the truth* |
  | **赶/误车** | *catch a train* / *miss a train* | 没赶上车用 **miss**（不是 lose / didn't find）|
  | **在某城市工作** | *work **in** London* | 城市用 in，不用 at/to/on |
  | **在几点钟** | *at 8 o'clock* | 钟点用 **at**；on 用于日期，in 用于月份/年份 |
  | **证实** | *confirm / certify that ...* | certify = 正式证明；assure 要接人 (*assure me that*) |

  ### A. 过去“不可能”的推测：couldn't have + 过去分词

  课后第 7 题：*It broke down so the man **couldn't have caught** it.* —— 车根本没发，所以“他**绝不可能**赶上那趟车”。**couldn't have + 过去分词** 表示对**过去**的“有把握的否定推测”（=肯定没发生）。对比 *mightn't have caught*（也许没赶上，只是“可能”，语气太弱，与“铁定没发车”矛盾）。

  ### B. “异常的”怎么换说法

  课后第 10 题：*anything unusual* = *anything **out of the ordinary***（不同寻常的）。不要被 *unused*（没用过的）/ *unaccustomed*（不习惯的）/ *inquisitive*（好奇的）这些形近或近义干扰项带偏。

  ### C. “当然不”：Certainly not

  课后第 11 题：*Of course not* 的同义回答是 ***Certainly** not*。*Surely not* 表“难道不成”（不相信的口吻）；*Doubtlessly / Truly not* 都不地道。
</Callout>

## 10. 摘要写作 Summary writing (≤ 80 词)

<Callout type="info" title="任务">
  *In not more than 80 words show how the inspector proved that the man's alibi was false.*（用不超过 80 个单词，说明探长是如何证明该人的不在犯罪现场证据是假的。）教材要求**用下列要点和连词写成两段**。

  | 连词 | 要点 (POINTS) |
  |---|---|
  | At / that | 1. Time of murder — 2. Man claimed: travelling — 3. on the 8 o'clock train to London |
  | and / When | 4. Arrived work on time — 5. Inspector asked: a later train, work on time? |
  | but | 6. Man agreed it would — 7. but always travelled early |
  | because / so | 8. Inspector suggested: lying — 9. 8 o'clock train — broke down — 10. Man caught the 8.25 |
</Callout>

<Callout type="tip" title="参考范文（自己写完再看）">
  **段一：** At the time of the murder the man claimed that he had been travelling on the 8 o'clock train to London and had arrived at work on time. When the inspector asked whether a later train would still get him to work on time, the man agreed that it would, but said he always travelled early.

  **段二：** The inspector suggested that he was lying, because the 8 o'clock train had broken down that morning and had not run at all. The man must therefore have caught the 8.25. *(79 words)*
</Callout>

## 11. 作文 Composition (约 150 词续写对话)

<Callout type="info" title="任务">
  *Continue the conversation begun in the passage. Write about 150 words using the ideas given below.*（继续课文的对话，按以下思路写约 150 个词的对话。）

  *The man suddenly 'remembered' that he had caught the later train — didn't he notice anything unusual? — not unusual for a train to be late — how did he spend the time? — waited on the platform for 25 minutes — read a newspaper — the inspector suggested that the man was lying — a neighbour saw him leave the house at 8.15, just after the murder — the man was arrested.*
</Callout>

<Callout type="warning" title="提示骨架（每个破折号都得自己填）">
  - 那人突然“想起”自己其实赶的是晚一点的车 —— *Suddenly the man 'remembered' that ...*
  - 探长追问：那你没注意到异常？—— *'And you noticed nothing unusual?'*
  - 那人辩解：火车晚点又不稀奇 —— *'It's not unusual for a train to be late.'*
  - 探长问：那这 25 分钟你干什么了？—— *'How did you spend the time?'*
  - 那人答：在站台等了 25 分钟，看报纸 —— *'I waited on the platform ... I read a newspaper.'*
  - 探长收网：邻居 8 点 15 看见他出门，正好在凶案之后 —— *a neighbour saw him leave the house at 8.15*
  - 结局：那人被捕 —— *The man was arrested.*
</Callout>

<Callout type="tip" title="写作脚手架">
  - **时态主线**：对话整体一般现在/过去；那人“声称做过的事”用**过去完成** *had caught / had waited*；探长揭露的“更早事件”也用过去完成 *had seen / had left*。
  - **追问句库**：*'How do you explain that?' / 'And then what?' / 'Can anyone confirm that?'*
  - **辩解句库**：*'There's nothing strange about that.' / 'Trains are often late.' / 'I simply waited.'*
  - **收网句库**：*'I'm afraid that won't do.' / 'A neighbour of yours tells a different story.' / 'You left your house at a quarter past eight — just after the murder.'*
</Callout>

<Callout type="tip" title="参考范文（自己写完再看）">
  Suddenly the man 'remembered'. 'Now I think of it, I did catch the later train that morning,' he said. 'And you still noticed nothing unusual?' asked the inspector. 'No. It is not unusual for a train to be late.' 'Then how did you spend the time?' 'I waited on the platform for about twenty-five minutes and read my newspaper.' The inspector leaned forward. 'I suggest you are still not telling the truth. A neighbour of yours saw you leave your house at a quarter past eight — just after the murder was committed. You never went near the station at all.' The man said nothing. A moment later he was arrested. *(108 words)*
</Callout>

## 12. 书信写作 Letter writing

<Callout type="info" title="任务">
  *The Signature.* How you sign your name depends on how well you know the person you are writing to. You may use your full name, your first name, or even a nickname. Your signature must be readable. It must come under the letter-ending.（**签名**：怎样签名取决于你与收信人的熟悉程度——全名、名字、甚至昵称皆可，但必须清楚易认，且写在结束语的下面。）

  *Write suitable letter-endings and signatures to the following:*（为下列收信人写出合适的结束语和签名：）**your sister; your wife or husband; your employer; a close friend.**
</Callout>

<Callout type="tip" title="参考写法（结束语 + 签名）">
  - **给姐妹 (your sister)** —— 亲切随意：
    *With love,* / *Your loving brother,* —— **Tom**（用名字即可）
  - **给配偶 (your wife / husband)** —— 最亲密：
    *All my love,* / *Yours ever,* —— **Tom**（名字或昵称，如 **Tommy**）
  - **给雇主 (your employer)** —— 正式：
    *Yours sincerely,* —— **Thomas Hardwick**（用全名，签名须清楚）
  - **给好友 (a close friend)** —— 轻松友好：
    *Yours,* / *All the best,* / *Cheers,* —— **Tom**（名字或昵称）

  ⚠️ 规律：**关系越正式，结束语越拘谨、签名越完整**（陌生/正式 → *Yours faithfully / sincerely* + 全名；亲近 → *With love / Yours ever* + 名字或昵称）。结束语后加**逗号**，签名另起一行写在其**正下方**。
</Callout>

## 13. 拓展练习 Going further

<Callout type="tip" title="自学者建议">
  - **影子跟读 (shadowing)**：用第 1 节的三个 SayIt 按钮跟读 3-5 遍。重点模仿探长**全程不动声色、最后一句才收网**的语气——前面平静地问，*'I suggest that you are not telling the truth ...'* 这句放慢、加重，体会那种“证据在手、稳操胜券”的从容。
  - **复述 (retell)**：合上课文，用 2 分钟把这桩“审讯”讲一遍。**叙事三段**：嫌疑人的不在场证明（案发时在 8 点的火车上 → 雇主可证明按时上班）→ 探长的盘问（几点到站？有无异常？晚车也能按时上班吗？）→ 收网反转（8 点的车那天根本没发 → 他撒了谎）。
  - **改写练习 (reported speech)**：把整段对话**改写成一篇新闻报道**（教材 Exercise A 的任务）。开头可以这样起笔——*At the time the murder was committed, the man said that he had been travelling on the 8 o'clock train to London. The inspector asked him if he always caught such an early train. The man answered that he did ...* 一路用 *He said that / He asked if / He added that* 串下去。
  - **造句迁移**：用今天的 5 个关键结构造你自己的句子——
    1. *He said that he was doing ...*（间接引语 + 过去进行）—— *She said that she was waiting for a friend.*
    2. *He asked if / whether ...*（间接一般疑问）—— *I asked him whether he had finished the work.*
    3. *He asked + 疑问词 + 陈述语序*（间接特殊疑问）—— *They asked me where I had put the keys.*
    4. *couldn't have + 过去分词*（对过去的有把握否定推测）—— *He couldn't have taken the bus; it wasn't running.*
    5. *I suggest (that) you ...*（委婉点破/提醒）—— *I suggest that you check your facts again.*
  - **延伸思考**：本课是一篇**“逻辑推理小品”**——没有动作、没有追逐，全靠一段对话和一个细节（8 点的车那天抛锚）就把谎言拆穿。它给写作的启示是：**间接引语**能把一段鲜活的对话“压缩”成简洁的报道，而**一个无可辩驳的事实**往往比千言万语更有力量。下次写推理/纪实类文字时不妨想想——把直接引语改成 *He said that ...*，叙述会立刻显得客观、紧凑、像一篇真正的报道。
</Callout>
