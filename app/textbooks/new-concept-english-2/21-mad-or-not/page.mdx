## The Text 课文

Aeroplanes are slowly **driving me mad**. I live near an airport and passing planes **can be heard** night and day. The airport **was built** years ago, but for some reason it **could not be used** then. Last year, however, it **came into use**. Over a hundred people **must have been driven away** from their homes by the noise. I am **one of the few people left**. Sometimes I think this house **will be knocked down** by a passing plane. I **have been offered** **a large sum of money** to go away, but I am **determined to stay** here. Everybody says I **must be mad** and they are probably right.

> *Everybody says I must be mad.*（插图说明）

## 课文注释 Notes on the text

<Callout type="info" title="4 个值得停下来嚼一嚼的点">
  1. **Aeroplanes are slowly driving me mad.**（l.1）—— “飞机正在逐渐把我逼疯。” **drive someone mad** 是固定搭配，意为 “把某人逼疯 / 逼得发狂”。这里 **drive** 不是 “驾驶”，而是 “驱使、逼迫（某人进入某种状态）”。同型：*The noise is driving me crazy. / Her constant complaints drove him mad.*

  2. **passing planes can be heard night and day**（l.2）—— “过往飞机日夜不绝于耳。” 句中 **passing** 用来修饰名词 **planes**，起形容词作用，是一个 **现在分词**（= planes that are passing）。**can be heard** 是 “情态动词 + be + 过去分词” 构成的 **被动语态**——主语 planes 是 “被听到” 的对象。同型：*passing cars / a passing remark / the passing years*。

  3. **came into use**（l.4）—— “开始使用、投入使用”。**come into use** 是固定短语，主语是 “物”（机场、新词、新方法等）。对照课文：*The airport was built years ago, but ... it came into use* 只是去年的事。反向短语：*go out of use*（不再使用）。

  4. **Over a hundred people must have been driven away from their homes by the noise.**（l.4-5）—— “有 100 多人肯定是被噪音逼得离家而去。” **情态动词 must + 完成式被动 (have been + 过去分词)** 表示 **对过去事情的肯定推测**：猜测某件事 “肯定已经发生”。**drive away** = 赶走、逐走。同型：*The thief must have been caught by now.（贼现在肯定已经被抓住了。）*
</Callout>

## 3. 关键句型：被动语态 The passive voice

<Callout type="tip" title="一分钟回顾：主动句怎样变被动句">
  本课的关键句型是 **被动语态**（参见第 10 课关键句型）。一句话抓住要点：**当我们更关心 “动作落在谁/什么身上” 而不是 “谁发出动作” 时，就把承受者提到句首，用 “be + 过去分词” 收尾。**

  教材给出一组对照——**第 1 句以 “人” 为主语（Who，谁来做）**，**第 2 句以 “物” 为主语（What/Which，哪件事被做）**：

  | 主动（Who 谁来做） | 被动（What 哪件事被做） |
  |---|---|
  | He **will repair** your watch. | Your watch **will be repaired**. |
  | He **can repair** your watch. | Your watch **can be repaired**. |
  | They **must test** this new car. | This new car **must be tested**. |
  | You **have to write** this letter again. | This letter **has to be written** again. |
  | I told you it **could be done**. | （情态 could + be + done） |
  | Someone **has stolen** it. | It **has been stolen**. |
  | Someone **must have stolen** it. | It **must have been stolen**. |

  ### 公式速记

  | 时态 / 情态 | 主动 | 被动（be 形式 + 过去分词） | 课文例句 |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | 一般过去 | built | **was / were built** | The airport **was built** years ago. |
  | 情态 can | can hear | **can be heard** | passing planes **can be heard** |
  | 情态 could（否定） | could not use | **could not be used** | it **could not be used** then |
  | 情态 will | will knock down | **will be knocked down** | this house **will be knocked down** |
  | 现在完成 | have offered | **have been offered** | I **have been offered** ... money |
  | 情态 + 完成式 | must have driven | **must have been driven** | people **must have been driven** away |

  ### ⚠️ 4 个最容易踩的雷区

  1. **be 动词不能丢**：被动必须有 be（is/are/was/be/been/being）。*The airport built years ago* ✗ → *was built* ✓。
  2. **情态动词后用 be 原形**：*can be heard / will be knocked down / must be tested*——情态后面永远跟 **be**，不跟 is/are。
  3. **“情态 + 完成式被动” 三层叠**：*must have been driven*（情态 must + have + been + 过去分词），一个都不能少，专门表示 “对过去的肯定推测”。
  4. **by 短语可省**：施动者不重要或不言自明时省略 *by ...*；课文 *was built / could not be used* 都没写 by。
</Callout>

## 5. 难点：drive 的多义 & home / house Special difficulties

<Callout type="info" title="一、drive 这个动词的词义很多">
  教材 Special Difficulties 专门点出 **经常容易混淆和误用的词**。第一个就是 **drive**——它远不止 “开车”：

  | 用法 | 例句（教材原例） | 释义 |
  |---|---|---|
  | drive sb + 形容词 | Aeroplanes are slowly **driving** me mad. (l.1) | 把某人逼成（疯/狂） |
  | drive（开车） | He **drives** his car very badly. | 驾驶（开得很糟） |
  | drive + 牲畜 + into | The farmer **drove** the cattle into the field. | 把（牛群）赶进…… |
  | drive + 敌人 + back | Our army **drove** the enemy back. | 把（敌人）撵回去 |
  | be driven out of | During the war, many people **were driven out of** their homes. | 被赶出（家园） |

  记忆线索：drive 的核心是 “**驱使、驱赶**” —— 驱使方向盘 = 开车，驱赶牛群 = 赶，驱使人进入某种状态 = 逼疯。课文里用的正是 “逼疯（drive sb mad）” 和 “逼走（drive away / be driven away）”。
</Callout>

<Callout type="info" title="二、home（家·副词感）vs house（房子·实体）">
  教材辨析的第二组是 **home (l.5)** 和 **house (l.6)**：

  - **home** = “家” —— 偏向 “归属感 / 抽象的家”，常 **不加介词/冠词**：*go home（回家）、stay at home（待在家）、There is no place like home（没有比家更好的地方）*。
  - **house** = “房子” —— 指 **具体的建筑物**，可数：*build new houses（盖新房）、a new house（一栋新房）、Houses are very expensive（住宅很贵）*。

  课文里两词都出现：*this house will be knocked down*（具体的房子会被撞）vs 邻居们 *driven away from their homes*（被赶离家园——这里 home 强调 “家” 的归属感）。
</Callout>

## 12. 拓展练习 Going further

<Callout type="tip" title="自学者建议">
  - **影子跟读 (shadowing)**：用第 1 节的三个 SayIt 按钮各跟读 3–5 遍，重点抓住被动结构的弱读——*can be HEARD / must have been DRIVEN away / have been OFFERED*，重音落在过去分词上，be / been 一带而过。
  - **复述 (retell)**：合上课文，用 3 句话把故事讲完——背景（住机场旁，飞机日夜吵）→ 经过（邻居被逼走、他被开价劝离）→ 结局（他决心留下，自嘲 “也许真疯了”）。
  - **造句迁移**：用今天的 5 个核心结构造你自己的句子——
    1. *主语 + can be + 过去分词*（被动）—— *The music can be heard from the street.*
    2. *主语 + was/were + 过去分词*（过去被动）—— *The bridge was built in 1990.*
    3. *主语 + must have been + 过去分词*（对过去的肯定推测被动）—— *The window must have been broken by the storm.*
    4. *drive sb + 形容词*（把某人逼成……）—— *The endless meetings are driving us crazy.*
    5. *be determined to do*（决心做……）—— *She is determined to pass the exam.*
  - **延伸思考**：本课是一篇 “被动语态实战范本”——短短九行用了 6 处被动（*can be heard / was built / could not be used / must have been driven / will be knocked down / have been offered*）。为什么作者偏爱被动？因为全文的主角是 “**被噪音、被机场、被金钱推着走的小人物**”——被动语态恰好把这种 “身不由己” 写进了语法里。下次你想表达 “某人被环境裹挟、无可奈何” 时，不妨也让句子 “被动” 起来。最后一句 *they are probably right* 的自嘲，则让一篇抱怨变成了带笑的小品——好文章往往在结尾轻轻一转，把怨气化成幽默。
</Callout>
