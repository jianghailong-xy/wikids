## The Text 课文

The secretary **told me that** Mr. Harmsworth **would see** me. I **felt very nervous** when I went into his office. He **did not look up** from his desk when I entered. **After I had sat down**, he said that business was very bad. He **told me that** the firm **could not afford to pay** **such large salaries**. Twenty people **had already left**. I knew that **my turn had come**.

‘Mr. Harmsworth,’ I said **in a weak voice**.

‘**Don’t interrupt**,’ he said.

Then he smiled and told me I **would receive an extra thousand pounds a year**!

## 课文注释 Notes on the text

<Callout type="info" title="3 个值得停下来嚼一嚼的点">
  1. **look up** (l.2) —— “抬头看”。课文 *He did not look up from his desk when I entered* = 我进去时他连头也没从桌上抬起来。注意 **look up** 还可表“查阅（词典 / 资料）”：*look up a word in the dictionary*。
  2. **could not afford to ...** (l.4) —— “（在钱 / 时间上）负担不起做……”。**afford** 表“有能力支付 / 承担”，几乎总是和 **can / could / be able to** 连用（多用于否定句和疑问句）。课文 *the firm could not afford to pay such large salaries* = 公司支付不起这么庞大的工资。同型：*I can't afford to buy a car. / We can't afford to waste time.*
  3. **I knew that my turn had come.** (l.5) —— “我知道这次该轮到我了。”**my turn** 承接上文“有人被公司解雇”这件事——“我”以为下一个被裁的就是自己。**had come** 是 **过去完成时**：相对于“我知道（knew）”这个过去时刻，“轮到我”这件事被看作“更早 / 已然”的事实。
</Callout>

## 3. 关键句型 间接引语 Reported speech (He said that ... / He told me ...)

<Callout type="tip" title="一分钟回顾：把“直接说的话”转述出来">
  本课通篇都是别人“说”的话。当我们不照原话引用、而是 **转述** 时，就用 **间接引语（reported speech）**：在 **say / tell** 后面接一个由 **that** 引导的从句（that 常可省略）。

  ### ① say 还是 tell？看后面有没有“告诉的对象”

  | 动词 | 后面是否直接跟“人” | 例句 |
  |---|---|---|
  | **tell** | **必须** 跟一个“人”（tell **sb.** that ...） | *He **told me** that business was bad.* |
  | **say** | **不** 直接跟人（say that ... / say **to sb.** that ...） | *He **said** that business was bad. / He **said to me** that ...* |

  一句口诀：**tell 后面要带人，say 后面不带人**（要带得加 to）。课文里两种都有：*The secretary **told me** that ... / he **said** that business was very bad.*

  ### ② 主句是过去时，从句要“往后退一格”（tense backshift）

  当转述动词是过去时（said / told）时，从句里的时态、情态动词通常 **后退一格**：

  | 直接引语 | → 间接引语 | 课文 / 例句 |
  |---|---|---|
  | am / is / are（一般现在） | → was / were（一般过去） | *‘I am busy.’ → He said that he **was** busy.* |
  | 一般现在（works） | → 一般过去（worked） | *‘I never work on Sundays.’ → She said she never **worked** on Sundays.* |
  | 现在完成（have finished） | → 过去完成（had finished） | *‘I have just finished.’ → He said he **had** just finished.* |
  | 一般过去（broke） | → 过去完成（had broken） | *‘I broke that plate.’ → He said he **had broken** that plate.* |
  | will | → would | *‘Mr. Jones will see you.’ → She said Mr. Jones **would** see me.* |
  | can | → could | *‘You can go now.’ → The teacher said I **could** go.* |

  课文 *Mr. Harmsworth **would** see me*（原话 will）、*the firm **could** not afford*（原话 can）、*I **would** receive*（原话 will）——全是这条规则的活例。**人称**也要随之改变（I → he，you → me 等）。

  ### ⚠️ 4 个本课最容易踩的雷区

  1. **tell 一定要带宾语**：*He **told me** that ...* ✓ / *He **told** that ...* ✗（缺“人”）。
  2. **say 不直接带人**：*He **said** that ...* ✓ / *He **said me** that ...* ✗（要带人得用 *said to me*）。
  3. **主句过去 → 从句后退一格**：*will → would，can → could，have done → had done*。课文没有一个 will / can 被原样保留。
  4. **过去 → 过去完成**：*‘I broke it.’ → He said he **had broken** it.*（更早的动作用过去完成）。
</Callout>

## 4. 难点：经常容易混淆和误用的词 Words often confused and misused

<Callout type="info" title="3 组本课要分清的词">
  教材 *Special Difficulties* 辨析了三组词——

  ### a. nervous vs irritable

  | 词 | 含义 | 例句 |
  |---|---|---|
  | **nervous** | 紧张不安的（restless or uneasy） | *Examinations make me **nervous**.*（考试使我紧张。） |
  | **irritable** | 易怒的（easily made angry） | *He is such an **irritable** person, you can hardly speak to him.*（他这人很易怒，简直没法跟他讲话。） |

  口诀：**nervous 是“慌”，irritable 是“躁”**——一个心里发虚，一个一点就着。

  ### b. office vs study vs desk

  | 词 | 含义 | 例句 |
  |---|---|---|
  | **office** | 办公室（上班的地方） | *There are six people in our **office**.*（我们办公室有 6 个人。） |
  | **study** | 书房（家里读书 / 工作的房间） | *I often read in the **study** when I want peace and quiet.*（想清静时我常在书房看书。） |
  | **desk** | 书桌、办公桌（一件家具） | *My **desk** is covered with books.*（我的书桌上铺满了书。） |

  ### c. afford —— “负担得起”

  **afford** 几乎只跟 **can / could / be able to** 连用，多见于否定句和疑问句；既能“付得起钱”，也能“抽得出时间”：

  - *Will you buy this car? I can’t **afford** it. I can’t **afford** £7,000.*（我买不起，掏不出 7,000 英镑。）
  - *You can **afford** this model. It’s not very expensive.*（你买得起这种型号，它不很贵。）
  - *I haven’t been to the cinema lately, I can’t **afford** the time.*（最近没去电影院，我没时间。）
</Callout>

## 10. 摘要写作 Summary writing (≤ 55 词)

<Callout type="info" title="任务">
  *Answer these questions in not more than 55 words.* 回答下列问题，把答案串成 **一个段落**，总词数不超过 55：

  1. Who wanted to see you?
  2. How did you feel about this?
  3. Where did you go?
  4. Did he say that business was bad, or did he say that it was good?
  5. Could the firm pay such large salaries or not?
  6. How many people had left already?
  7. Did he ask you to leave as well or not?
  8. What did he offer you?
</Callout>

<Callout type="tip" title="参考范文（自己写完再看）">
  The secretary told the writer that Mr. Harmsworth wanted to see him. Feeling very nervous, he went into the office. Mr. Harmsworth said business was bad and the firm could not afford such large salaries. Twenty people had already left. But instead of dismissing him, he offered him an extra thousand pounds a year. *(54 words)*
</Callout>

## 11. 拓展练习 Going further

<Callout type="tip" title="自学者建议">
  - **影子跟读 (shadowing)**：用第 1 节的三个 SayIt 按钮各跟读 3-5 遍。把 *told me that / could not afford to / would receive* 连读起来——尤其体会句末 *an extra thousand pounds a year!* 那种“先抑后扬、反转上扬”的语气。
  - **复述 (retell)**：合上课文，用 1 分钟把这场“虚惊”讲一遍。**三步走**：起（秘书通知老板要见我、我很紧张）→ 承（老板说生意差、付不起工资、二十人已走，我以为要被裁）→ 转（他却微笑着说要给我加薪 1,000 英镑）。
  - **造句迁移**：用今天学的 5 个结构造你自己的句子——
    1. *He told me that + 从句*（间接引语，tell 带人）—— *He told me that he was tired.*
    2. *She said that + 从句*（间接引语，say 不带人）—— *She said that she would come.*
    3. *can’t / couldn’t afford to do sth.*（负担不起）—— *I can’t afford to buy a new phone.*
    4. *such + 形容词 + 复数名词*（这么……的……）—— *I have never seen such tall buildings.*
    5. *had + 过去分词*（过去之过去）—— *The film had already started when we arrived.*
  - **语法意识**：本课是“**间接引语（reported speech）**”的入门课。记牢三条铁律——① **tell 后面要带人（tell sb. that），say 不直接带人（say that / say to sb.）**；② 主句是过去时（said / told），从句时态、情态动词 **后退一格**（will→would，can→could，have done→had done，do→did）；③ **人称** 随说话立场改变（I→he，you→me）。课文里 *would see / could not afford / would receive* 全是这套规则的现成范例——把它们当模板，转述别人的话就不会出错了。
</Callout>
