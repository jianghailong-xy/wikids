<Callout type="info" title="Listen first 听前思考">
  每年一次，一群古董老爷车要凑到一起“赛”一场。报名的车很多，开赛前人人都激动得不行。最漂亮的是一辆 **罗尔斯-罗伊斯“银鬼”**，最稀奇的是一辆只有 **三个轮子**、造于 **1885 年** 的奔驰。发令枪没响——倒是先来了一阵噼啪的“爆炸”声。很多车半路 **抛锚**，有些司机钻在车底下的时间比坐在车里还长……可冠军车竟跑出了 **时速 40 英里**！标题却戏称它 *Faster than sound*（比声音还快）——这正是作者的幽默：40 英里在今天慢得可怜，却被故意吹成“超音速”。
  **How fast did the winning car go? 获胜的那辆车到底跑了多快？** 带着这个问题读课文，重点留意全篇是怎么用 **a lot of / a great deal of / a great many / a few / different from** 这一串“数量 + 比较”表达把热闹场面写活的。
</Callout>

## The Text 课文

Once a year, **a race is held for old cars**. A lot of cars **entered for this race** last year and there was **a great deal of excitement** just before it began. One of the most **handsome** cars was a Rolls-Royce Silver Ghost. The most **unusual** car was a Benz which had only three wheels. **Built in 1885**, it was the oldest car **taking part**. After a great many loud explosions, the race began. Many of the cars **broke down** on the course and some drivers spent more time *under* their cars than *in* them! A few cars, however, **completed the race**. The winning car **reached a speed of forty miles an hour** — much faster than any of its **rivals**. It **sped downhill** at the end of the race and its driver **had a lot of trouble trying to stop it**. The race gave everyone a great deal of pleasure. It was **very different from modern car races but no less exciting**.

<Callout type="tip" title="一句话抓主线">
  *It was different from modern car races* —— 一群快散架的老爷车，一场又吵又慢又狼狈的比赛，却让在场每个人乐开了花。题眼是结尾的 *but no less exciting*（但激动人心的程度毫不逊色）。
</Callout>

## 课文注释 Notes on the text

<Callout type="info" title="7 个值得停下来嚼一嚼的点">
  1. **once a year** —— “每年一次”。**once / twice / three times + a + 时间单位** 表频率：*once a week*（每周一次）/ *twice a month*（每月两次）/ *three times a year*（每年三次）。课文开头 *Once a year, a race is held ...* 用的是 **一般现在时的被动语态**，表“惯常发生的安排”。
  2. **A lot of cars entered for this race.** —— “去年许多车报名参加了这项比赛。”**enter for** = 报名参加（比赛 / 考试），后接 *a race / a competition / an exam*。注意区别：**enter（不加 for）= 进入**（*enter the room*）。
  3. **a great deal of** —— “大量的”，后面 **只接不可数名词**（excitement, pleasure, trouble）。课文出现两次：*a great deal of excitement* / *a great deal of pleasure*。可数名词要用 *a great many*（如 *a great many loud explosions*）。
  4. **break down** —— “（机器、车辆）抛锚、出故障”。课文 *Many of the cars broke down on the course*。同型：*The lift broke down. / My computer keeps breaking down.* 另一层意思是“（人）情绪崩溃”。
  5. **some drivers spent more time *under* their cars than *in* them!** —— “有些驾驶员花在车底下的时间比坐在车里还长！”句中 **under** 和 **in** 都用斜体，是为了 **形成对比、需要重读**——一个钻在底下修，一个本该坐在上面开，幽默尽出。
  6. **its driver had a lot of trouble trying to stop it** —— “驾驶员费了九牛二虎之力才把车停下。”**have trouble (in) doing sth.** = 做某事很费劲 / 有麻烦，**doing** 前的 in 常省略。同型：*I had trouble finding your house.*
  7. **but no less exciting** —— “但激动人心的程度毫不逊色。”这是 **省略句**，补全为 *but it was no less exciting than modern car races*。**no less ... than** = “不亚于、同样……”。
</Callout>

## 3. 关键句型 数量与比较表达 a lot of / a great deal of / a great many / different from

<Callout type="tip" title="一分钟回顾">
  本课 Key structures 复习的是 **“数量词 + 比较关系”** 的一整套表达。关键是分清 **可数 vs 不可数**，以及 **like / the same as / different from** 这组比较短语：

  | 表达 | 接什么 | 课文 / 例句 |
  |---|---|---|
  | **a lot of / lots of** | 可数复数 **和** 不可数都行 | *A lot of cars entered for this race.* |
  | **a great deal of** | **只接不可数** | *a great deal of excitement / pleasure / trouble* |
  | **a great many** | **只接可数复数** | *a great many loud explosions* |
  | **a few** | 可数复数（“几个、一些”，肯定意味） | *A few cars completed the race.* |
  | **much / little** | 不可数 | *There isn't much I can do.* |
  | **the same as** | “与……相同” | *My jacket is the same as yours.* |
  | **different from** | “与……不同” | *It was very different from modern car races.* |

  ### ⚠️ 4 个本课最容易踩的雷区

  1. **a great deal of 只配不可数**：*a great deal of excitement* ✓；*a great deal of cars* ✗（cars 可数，要用 *a great many cars* 或 *a lot of cars*）。
  2. **a lot of 后不能省 of**：*a lot of cars* ✓；*lot of cars* ✗。单用作代词时用 *a lot*：*There isn't a lot I can do.*
  3. **different from**（英式标准）：不要写成 *different than*（美式口语）或 *different to*。课文用的是 **from**。
  4. **the same as** 而非 *the same with / the same like*：*the same as yours* ✓。否定就用 *different from*。
</Callout>

## 4. 难点：经常容易混淆和误用的词 Words often confused and misused

<Callout type="info" title="3 组课文里的“形近 / 义近”词">
  本课 *Special difficulties* 讲了 3 组最容易用错的词：

  | 组 | 用法要点 | 例句 |
  |---|---|---|
  | **handsome / beautiful / pretty / good-looking** | **handsome** 多指男子或大件物（车、建筑）的“英俊、美观”；**beautiful** 指（女子 / 景物）“美丽”；**pretty** 指（女孩 / 小物）“清秀、小巧可爱”；**good-looking** 中性，“好看的”，男女皆可 | *He is a very handsome young man. / She is now a beautiful woman.* |
  | **reach / arrive in / arrive at** | **reach + 宾语**（及物，无介词）“到达 / 够到 / 达到”；**arrive in + 大地点**（国家、城市）；**arrive at + 小地点**（车站、机场等） | *We arrived in New York. We arrived at the station. / I can't reach it.* |
  | **take part / take place** | **take part (in)** = 参加（活动）；**take place** = （事情）发生、举行 | *It was the oldest car taking part. / The next race will take place in a year's time.* |

  ### ⚠️ 3 个最常见的错

  1. **arrive 不及物**：*arrive London* ✗ → *arrive in London* ✓；到站用 *arrive at the station*。**reach 及物**：*reach London* / *reach the station*（不加介词）。
  2. **take place 不能用于“人参加”**：“他参加了比赛”是 *He took part in the race.*，不是 *He took place in the race.*。
  3. **take place 没有被动语态**（本身就含“发生”义）：*The race will take place* ✓；*The race will be taken place* ✗。
</Callout>

## 11. 作文 Composition

<Callout type="info" title="任务">
  Rewrite the following sentences using the joining words in parentheses. Where necessary omit the words in italics. 用括号中的连词把下列各组句子连成一句，必要时省略斜体词。

  1. There was a loud explosion. My old car stopped. *(and)*
  2. I got out. *I* wanted to have a look at the engine. *(to)*
  3. Then a man passed me. *He* was driving a large modern car. *(a man who)*
  4. I waved to him. He did not stop. *(but)*
  5. I passed the large car later. Its driver was changing a wheel. *(When)*
</Callout>

<Callout type="warning" title="提示骨架">
  - **and** —— 把两件事顺接：“砰的一声……（然后）车就停了”。
  - **to** —— 省略 *I wanted*，用不定式表目的：“我下了车去看看引擎”。
  - **a man who** —— 把第二句变成定语从句，省略 *He*：“一个开着大型现代汽车的人”。
  - **but** —— 转折：“我朝他挥手，但他没停”。
  - **When** —— 时间状语从句置于句首，记得加逗号：“当我后来超过那辆大车时，它的司机正在换轮胎”。
</Callout>

<Callout type="tip" title="参考范文（自己写完再看）">
  1. There was a loud explosion and my old car stopped.
  2. I got out to have a look at the engine.
  3. Then a man who was driving a large modern car passed me.
  4. I waved to him but he did not stop.
  5. When I passed the large car later, its driver was changing a wheel.
</Callout>

## 12. 书信写作 Letter writing

<Callout type="info" title="任务">
  Learn the following opening phrases by heart, then write two short paragraphs (of not more than two sentences each) completing them. 熟记下面两个开头语，然后各写一个不超过两句话的小段落把它们补完整。

  - *I am sorry it has taken me so long to write, but ...*
  - *I was glad to hear from you at last and to learn that ...*
</Callout>

<Callout type="tip" title="写作脚手架">
  - **第一句开头（道歉 + 解释为何拖了这么久）**：*but ...* 后接你“一直很忙”的原因 —— *I have been extremely busy with my new job since the summer.*
  - **第二句开头（高兴收到对方信 + 转述对方的好消息）**：*to learn that ...* 后接一个 that 从句，复述对方信里的内容 —— *you have settled into your new flat and that the children are doing well at school.*
  - **时态搭配**：道歉用现在完成 *it has taken me so long*；解释近况用现在完成（进行）*I have been ...*；转述对方消息用一般现在 / 现在完成。
</Callout>

<Callout type="tip" title="参考范文（自己写完再看）">
  I am sorry it has taken me so long to write, but I have been extremely busy with my new job ever since the summer and have hardly had a free evening.

  I was glad to hear from you at last and to learn that you have settled happily into your new flat and that both children are doing well at their new school.
</Callout>

## 13. 拓展练习 Going further

<Callout type="tip" title="自学者建议">
  - **影子跟读 (shadowing)**：用第 1 节的三个 SayIt 按钮各跟读 3-5 遍。重点感受 *much faster than any of its rivals* 里 **much** 的重读，以及破折号前后的停顿节奏。
  - **复述 (retell)**：合上课文，用 1 分钟把这场古董车赛讲一遍。**叙事三段**：开赛（每年一次 + 报名踊跃 + 漂亮 / 稀奇的车）→ 比赛（爆炸声 → 纷纷抛锚 → 少数跑完）→ 冠军 + 结语（40 英里 → 冲下坡停不住 → different but no less exciting）。
  - **造句迁移**：用今天的 5 个高价值结构造你自己的句子——
    1. *Once a + 时间单位, ... is held ...*（每……一次，举办……）—— *Once a year, a book fair is held in our city.*
    2. *a great deal of + 不可数*（大量的……）—— *We had a great deal of fun at the party.*
    3. *break down*（抛锚 / 出故障）—— *My car broke down on the motorway.*
    4. *much + 比较级 + than*（比…………得多）—— *This phone is much cheaper than that one.*
    5. *very different from ... but no less ...*（与……不同，但毫不逊色）—— *Her new film is very different from her last one but no less moving.*
  - **延伸思考**：本课的“笑点”全在 **反讽与夸张**——标题 *Faster than sound!* 明明只有 40 英里；*spent more time under their cars than in them* 把“修车比开车久”写得活灵活现。下次你写英文记叙时，不妨学这一手：**用一个夸张的对比或一句轻描淡写的反语，把平淡的事写出幽默感**。语言点上，记住一条主线：**可数用 a great many / a lot of，不可数用 a great deal of / much；“相同” the same as，“不同” different from**——这四组词组掌握了，描述“数量与异同”就不再卡壳。
</Callout>
