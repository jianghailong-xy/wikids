</Callout>

## The Text 课文

Haroun Tazieff, the Polish scientist, **has spent his lifetime studying** active volcanoes and deep caves **in all parts of the world**. In 1948, he went to Lake Kivu in the Congo to observe a new volcano which he later named Kituro. Tazieff **was able to** set up his camp very close to the volcano while it **was erupting violently**. Though he **managed to take** a number of brilliant photographs, he **could not stay** near the volcano for very long. He noticed that **a river of liquid rock** was coming towards him. It **threatened to surround** him completely, but Tazieff **managed to escape just in time**. He waited until the volcano became quiet and he **was able to return** two days later. This time, he managed to climb into the mouth of Kituro so that he could take photographs and measure temperatures. Tazieff has often **risked his life** in this way. He has been able to tell us more about active volcanoes than **any man alive**.

## 课文注释 Notes on the text

<Callout type="info" title="6 个值得停下来嚼一嚼的点">
  1. **has spent his lifetime studying ...** (l.1) — “把毕生的精力用于研究……”。**spend + 时间/精力 + (in) doing sth.** 是固定结构，介词 *in* 通常省略。课文 *has spent his lifetime studying* = 一辈子都在研究。同型：*She spent the whole afternoon (in) reading. / Don't spend too much time (in) playing games.*
  2. **in all parts of the world** (l.2) — “世界各地”。这个介词短语在课文里修饰前面 *active volcanoes and deep caves* 两个名词，意为“世界各地的（活火山和深洞）”。同义：*all over the world / throughout the world / around the world.*
  3. **a river of liquid rock** (ll.7-8) — “一股岩浆”。这是一种修辞手法——**暗喻 (metaphor)**：把缓缓流动的熔岩比作一条“河”。同型的“of + 物质”暗喻：*a sea of faces（人头攒动）/ a flood of letters（雪片般的来信）。*
  4. **just in time** (l.9) — “及时；恰好赶上（差一点就来不及）”。注意与 *in time*（及时）相比，*just* 加强了“千钧一发”的语气。区别 *on time*（准时，按点）：*The train left on time（火车准点开）* vs *I caught it just in time（我险些没赶上）。*
  5. **risked his life** (l.11) — “冒着生命危险”。**risk + 名词 / risk doing sth.** = 拿……去冒险。同型：*He risked his life to save the child. / Don't risk losing all your money.*
  6. **any man alive** (l.12) — “任何在世的人 / 任何活着的人”。**alive** 是表语形容词，一般不放在名词前面，但可以 **后置作定语**（放在名词之后）：*any man alive / the happiest man alive / no one alive*。不能说 *an alive man*（要用 *a living man*）。
</Callout>

## 3. 关键句型 could / was able to / managed to

<Callout type="tip" title="一分钟回顾：三种“能”怎么分">
  本课是练 **“能力 vs 成功”** 的经典课文。同样译成中文的“能 / 得以 / 设法”，英语里却分三种，差别全在“是不是某一次真的做成了”：

  | 形式 | 用法 | 课文例句 |
  |---|---|---|
  | **could**（肯定） | 表“过去一段时期一直具备的能力”，**不**表“某一次成功做成” | *so that he **could** take photographs*（具备拍照的条件/能力） |
  | **could not**（否定） | 表“过去没能做到”，任何场合都能用 | *he **could not** stay near the volcano for very long* |
  | **was / were able to** | 表“过去某一次具体地、确实做成了某事” | *Tazieff **was able to** set up his camp ... / he **was able to** return two days later* |
  | **managed to** | 和 was able to 同义，但更强调“**费了劲、克服了困难**才做成” | *he **managed to** take ... / **managed to** escape just in time / **managed to** climb into the mouth* |

  ### ⚠️ 4 个最容易踩的雷区

  1. **肯定句里表“某一次成功”绝不能用 could**。✗ *He could escape just in time.* → ✓ *He **managed to / was able to** escape just in time.*（could escape 只表“当时有逃跑的能力”，不表“真的逃成了”。）
  2. **could / can 后面接动词原形，不加 to**；而 **be able 后必须加 to**（was able **to** return）。
  3. **manage / be able 后接 to do**：*manage to do sth. / be able to do sth.*。
  4. **否定的“没能做到”三者都行**：*could not / was not able to / didn't manage to* —— 这时区别消失。
</Callout>

## 4. 难点：say 与 tell 的短语 Special difficulties

<Callout type="info" title="say 还是 tell？一张表分清">
  课文 *He has been able to **tell us** more about volcanoes ...* (ll.11-12) 用的是 tell。教材的 Special difficulties 专门梳理了 **say** 和 **tell** 的固定短语——两者都是“说”，搭配却完全不同：

  | 动词 | 后面接什么 | 常见固定搭配 |
  |---|---|---|
  | **say** | 说的“**内容**”（不直接跟“人”） | *say nothing / say so / say a good word for sb / say goodbye / say one's prayers / say no more* |
  | **tell** | 先接“**人**”，再接内容 | *tell sb more / tell sb the truth / tell a lie / tell the time / tell the difference / tell a story / tell a secret* |

  ### ⚠️ 三条铁律

  1. **tell 后常直接跟人**（tell **me**, tell **us**）；**say 后不直接跟人**——要带对象得用 *say sth **to** sb*（say something **to** me）。
  2. 几个只能用 tell 的固定搭配要背死：**tell the time**（看钟点 / 报时）、**tell the difference**（分辨、区分）、**tell a lie**（撒谎）、**tell the truth**（说实话）、**tell a story**（讲故事）。
  3. 几个只能用 say 的固定搭配：**say a good word for sb**（为某人美言）、**say one's prayers**（做祷告）、**say so**（这么说）、**say goodbye**（道别）。
</Callout>

## 10. 摘要写作 Summary writing (≤ 80 词)

<Callout type="info" title="任务">
  *Answer these questions in not more than 80 words.* 回答下列问题，把答案串成一个段落，不要超过 80 个单词：

  1. Where did Tazieff, the Polish scientist, go in 1948? Why did he go there? What did he call it? *(to ... which)*
  2. Did he take photographs or not? Did he have to leave almost at once or not? Did a river of liquid rock threaten to surround him or not? *(After taking ... because)*
  3. Did he escape just in time or not? When did he return? Had the volcano become quiet, or was it still active? *(but ... when)*
  4. Where did he climb this time? Why did he do so? *(in order to)*
</Callout>

## 11. 作文 Composition

<Callout type="info" title="任务">
  *Write three or four sentences using the ideas given below.* 用以下提示写出 3-4 个句子：

  **Tazieff went into the mouth of Kituro — saw the boiling red centre — ash — lumps of rock shooting up — in great danger — took photographs — returned to camp.**
</Callout>

<Callout type="warning" title="提示骨架">
  - **进入**：When Tazieff climbed into the mouth of Kituro, ...（用 when 引出时间状语，主线一般过去时）
  - **所见**：he saw the boiling red centre of the volcano below him.（看到沸腾的红色中心）
  - **危险**：Ash filled the air and lumps of rock were shooting up, so he was in great danger.（灰尘弥漫、石块飞迸——用过去进行 were shooting up 渲染背景）
  - **行动 + 撤离**：All the same, he calmly took a number of photographs and then returned to his camp.（尽管如此，他还是从容拍照后返回营地）
</Callout>

<Callout type="tip" title="写作脚手架">
  - **时态主线**：一般过去时叙事（climbed, saw, took, returned）；用 **过去进行时**渲染当时的背景动作（ash was filling the air / lumps of rock were shooting up）。
  - **“危险”词库**：*in great danger, at great risk, a wall of heat, choking ash, molten rock, boiling red centre, lumps of rock shooting up.*
  - **“沉着”副词**：*calmly, steadily, without panic, all the same（尽管如此）.*
</Callout>

<Callout type="tip" title="参考范文（自己写完再看）">
  When Tazieff climbed into the mouth of Kituro, he saw the boiling red centre of the volcano far below him. Ash filled the air and lumps of rock were shooting up all around him, so he was in great danger. All the same, he calmly took a number of photographs. Then, before the volcano could erupt again, he returned safely to his camp. *(62 words)*
</Callout>

## 12. 书信写作 Letter writing

<Callout type="info" title="任务">
  *Write six sentences beginning with each of the following.* 用下列开头语各写出一句完整的话：

  **Thank you ... ; What a ... ; Forgive ... ; I have not ... ; I am sorry ... ; It was very ... .**
</Callout>

<Callout type="tip" title="写作脚手架">
  - **Thank you ...** —— 用来道谢：*Thank you + for + 名词 / V-ing*。
  - **What a ...** —— 感叹句：*What a + (形容词) + 单数名词 + 主谓!*。
  - **Forgive ...** —— 请求原谅：*Forgive me for + V-ing*。
  - **I have not ...** —— 现在完成时：*I have not + 过去分词 ... since / for ...*。
  - **I am sorry ...** —— 表达歉意 / 遗憾：*I am sorry + (that) 从句 / to do*。
  - **It was very ...** —— 评价过去某事：*It was very + 形容词 + of you to do*。
</Callout>

<Callout type="tip" title="参考范文（自己写完再看）">

  2. **What a** pleasant surprise it was to meet you at the station yesterday!
  3. **Forgive** me for not writing to you sooner, but I have been extremely busy.
  4. **I have not** heard from you since you moved to Bristol last spring.
  5. **I am sorry** to tell you that I shall not be able to come to your party.

</Callout>

## 13. 拓展练习 Going further

<Callout type="tip" title="自学者建议">
  - **影子跟读 (shadowing)**：用第 1 节的三个 SayIt 按钮各跟读 3-5 遍。重点感受 *managed to escape just **in time*** 句末的紧迫感，以及 *than any man **alive*** 的收尾重音。
  - **复述 (retell)**：合上课文，用 1 分钟把 Tazieff 的故事讲一遍。**叙事三段**：第一次靠近（搭营 → 拍照 → 岩浆逼近 → 及时逃脱）→ 两天后返回（火山平静 → 爬进火山口 → 拍照测温）→ 总结（经常冒险 → 贡献最大）。
  - **造句迁移**：用今天的 5 个核心结构造你自己的句子——
    1. *managed to do*（克服困难做成）—— *After hours of trying, I finally managed to fix the bug.*
    2. *was able to do*（某一次做成）—— *Thanks to the map, we were able to find the village before dark.*
    3. *could not do*（没能做到）—— *I could not open the door because I had lost the key.*
    4. *threaten to do*（眼看就要）—— *The black clouds threatened to spoil our picnic.*
    5. *tell sb the difference / the time / a lie*（tell 接人）—— *He is too young to tell the time.*
  - **延伸思考**：本课的语法主线是 **could / was able to / managed to** 三者之别——它们都译作“能”，但只有 was able to 和 managed to 才表示“那一次真的做成了”，而 managed to 还额外强调“费了劲、有难度”。写英文叙事时记住这把尺子：**讲“某一次具体的成功”用 was able to / managed to，讲“一贯的能力”才用 could，讲“失败 / 没做到”三者都行但常用 could not。**另一处值得回味的是 say 与 tell 的分工：**tell 后面先接“人”，say 后面接“说的内容”**——一旦把它们记成两套固定搭配（tell the time / tell a lie / say a good word / say one's prayers），就再也不会用错。
</Callout>
