## The Text 课文

I love **travelling in the country**, but I don't like **losing my way**. I **went on an excursion** recently, but my trip took me longer than I **expected**.

'I'm going to Woodford Green,' I said to the **conductor** as I **got on** the bus, 'but I don't know where it is.'

'I'll tell you where **to get off**,' answered the conductor.

I sat **in the front of** the bus to **get a good view** of the countryside. After some time, the bus stopped. **Looking round**, I realized **with a shock** that I was **the only passenger** left on the bus.

'**You'll have to get off here**,' the conductor said. '**This is as far as we go.**'

'Is this Woodford Green?' I asked.

'**Oh dear**,' said the conductor suddenly. 'I **forgot to put you off**.'

'**It doesn't matter**,' I said. 'I'll get off here.'

'**We're going back now**,' said the conductor.

'Well, **in that case**, I **prefer to stay on the bus**,' I answered.

## 课文注释 Notes on the text

<Callout type="info" title="7 个值得停下来嚼一嚼的点">
  1. **go on an excursion** (l.2) — "做一次远足 / 短途旅行"。**excursion** 比 *trip* 更正式，通常指"有组织的短途出游"。同型：*go on a trip / go on a picnic / go on holiday*。注意搭配 **go on** 一个 excursion（不是 make 或 do）。
  2. **the conductor** (l.4) — "售票员"。英式英语中 bus conductor 不是"指挥 / 导演"，而是在公交车上卖票、报站的乘务员。而 bus driver 才指"司机"——两者不要混淆。
  3. **I'll tell you where to get off.** (l.5) — "我告诉你在哪儿下车。" **where + 不定式 to get off** 作动词 **tell** 的直接宾语。同型：*I don't know what to do. / She asked me how to get there. / Can you show me which button to press?*——疑问词 + to do 是非常常用的宾语结构。
  4. **get a good view** (l.7) — "饱览好风光 / 获得好视野"。**view** 作"视野、景色"解时是可数名词；**get a good view of ...** 是固定搭配。而同义词 **scenery**（风景，总体不可数）不能用 *a scenery*。同型：*The room has a view of the sea. / There is beautiful scenery along the road.*
  5. **Looking round** (l.7) — "环顾四周"。**现在分词短语作伴随状语**——*Looking round, I realized ...* = 环顾四周时，我意识到……。主动用 V-ing（Looking），被动用 V-ed。同型：*Turning around, she saw her friend. / Hearing the noise, he ran outside.*
  6. **put sb. off** (l.13) — "让某人下车 / 让某人下去"。**put off** 除了常见的"推迟、拖延"义，这里特指"让……下公交车"。同型：*Could you put me off at the next stop?*（你能让我在下一站下车吗？）—— 英式公交用语的典型表达。另外注意 **forget to do**（忘了去做……）与 **forget doing**（忘了做过……）的区别：*I forgot to put you off*（忘了让你下车——还没让你下）= 不定式表未做的事。
  7. **in that case** (l.16) — "既然这样 / 既然如此"。固定短语，表示"从前面所说的情况来看，那么……"，常用于引出决定或结论。同型：*In that case, I'll take the train. / 'It might rain.' 'In that case, bring an umbrella.'*
</Callout>

## 3. 关键句型 Verbs that tell us what always happens 习惯性动作动词

<Callout type="tip" title="一分钟回顾">
  本课的 Key Structures 训练的是**表示习惯性动作 / 状态的动词**——这类动词通常用**一般现在时**（而不用现在进行时），因为它们描述的不是"此刻正在发生"的动作，而是**长期的状态、喜好、看法**。

  课文中出现的习惯性动词：
  - **love** (l.1) —— *I love travelling ...*（喜爱——不是"此刻正在爱"，是持续的状态）
  - **don't like** (l.1) —— *I don't like losing ...*（不喜欢——看法 / 偏好）
  - **don't know** (l.4) —— *I don't know where it is.*（不知道——精神状态）
  - **prefer** (l.16) —— *I prefer to stay ...*（宁愿——偏好）

  教材列举了以下**常用于一般现在时、不用于进行时的动词**：

  | 类别 | 动词 | 例句 |
  |---|---|---|
  | **心理状态** | know, believe, think (认为), understand, remember, forget, mean | *I **don't understand** a thing.* |
  | **喜恶** | love, like, dislike, hate, detest, prefer, desire, hope, wish, want | *She **hates** writing letters.* |
  | **所属 / 构成** | belong to, consist of, contain, own, possess | *This box **contains** 48 matches.* |
  | **感官** | see, hear, smell, taste | — |
  | **其他** | matter, need, mind, seem, appear | *It **doesn't matter**.* |

  ### ⚠️ 4 个最容易踩的雷区

  1. **love / like + V-ing**：*I love travelling*（不是 I am loving travelling）。现在进行 *I'm loving it* 是口语广告语，正式写作中不用于描述持续习惯。
  2. **"我不知道" 永远是 I don't know**，不是 ~~I am not knowing~~——know 这类状态动词**几乎从不**用进行时。
  3. **matter 是不及物动词**：*It doesn't matter*（不是 ~~It doesn't matter me~~）。如果要带"对我而言"，用 *It doesn't matter **to** me*。
  4. **prefer 的两种搭配**：*prefer **to** do*（一次性的选择）和 *prefer **doing** to doing*（泛指偏好）。课文 *I prefer **to stay on** the bus* = 这一次，我宁愿留在车上。
</Callout>

## 4. 难点 Special difficulties

### 4a. Lose, Loose, Miss 三个"丢"一把抓

<Callout type="info" title="形近词辨析">
  这三个词中国学生极容易混淆——拼写只差一两个字母，意思全不同：

  | 词 | 词性 | 核心义 | 例句 |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | **lose** /luːz/ | v. | 丢失、输掉、迷失 | *Take care not to **lose** your passport.*（小心别**丢**了护照。） |
  | **loose** /luːs/ | adj. | 松的、松动的 | *Several screws have come **loose**.*（几颗螺丝**松**了。） |
  | **miss** /mɪs/ | v. | 错过、想念 | *We must hurry or we'll **miss** the train.*（快一点，不然要**误**火车了。） |

### 4b. Expect 与 Wait for —— 看似都像"等"，差别非常大

<Callout type="info" title="Expect vs Wait for">
  | 词 | 核心义 | 典型搭配 | 例句 |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | **expect** /ɪkˈspekt/ | 心理上的"预期、料想、期待"（某事会发生/某人会来）——不强调"原地等待"的动作 | expect sb. to do / expect that | *I am **expecting** Jack to arrive at six.*（我**预计/期待**杰克 6 点到。） |
  | **wait for** /weɪt fɔː/ | 行为上的"等待"——在某个地方花时间等，直到某人/某事出现 | wait for sb./sth. | *I shall not **wait for** him if he is not here by 6.15.*（如果他 6:15 还不到，我就不**等**他了。） |

  ⚠️ **一句话记住**：**expect** = 心里觉得会发生（心态）；**wait for** = 待着不动地等（动作）。你可以 *expect* 某人来，但不一定 *wait for* 他（比如边做别的事边等）；反之你也可以 *wait for* 某人，但心里并不 *expect* 他真会来。

### 4c. My trip took me longer than I expected —— 用 It takes / It took 表达"花时间"

<Callout type="info" title="It takes + sb. + 时间 + to do">

  | 句型 | 例句 | 要点 |

  | **主语 + take + sb. + 时间** | *The trip **took** me two hours.* | 主语是"事"（trip, journey, task） |

## 10. 摘要写作 Summary writing (≤ 65 词)

<Callout type="info" title="任务">
  *Answer these questions in not more than 65 words.* 把下面 4 组问题的答案连成一段话，不超过 65 词：

  1. Where did the writer want to go? Did he know the way or not? What did the conductor promise to do? *(but as)*
  2. Did they arrive at the bus terminus or not? What did the writer ask? *(When)*
  3. What did the conductor realize then? *(... that)*
  4. Why did the writer stay on the bus? *(because)*
</Callout>

<Callout type="tip" title="参考范文（自己写完再看）">
  The writer wanted to go to Woodford Green, but as he did not know the way, the conductor promised to tell him where to get off. When they arrived at the bus terminus, the writer asked if it was Woodford Green. The conductor then realized that he had forgotten to put him off. The writer stayed on the bus because it was going back. *(65 words)*
</Callout>

## 11. 作文 Composition

<Callout type="info" title="任务">
  *Rewrite the following sentences using the joining words in parentheses.* 用括号里的连词把下列句子合并：

  1. I went into the kitchen. I turned on the light. It was dark. *(On going ... because)*
  2. My brother shouted angrily. The lights went on. *(when)*
  3. I spoilt a film. He was developing it. *(which)*
</Callout>

<Callout type="warning" title="提示骨架">
  - **第 1 句**：**On going into the kitchen**（一进厨房就……）用 *on + V-ing* 表"一……就……"，后半句用 **because** 接原因。
  - **第 2 句**：用 **when** 连接时间先后——"当……时，……"。
  - **第 3 句**：用 **which** 引导定语从句——"我把（他正在冲洗的）那个胶卷弄坏了"。注意 which 已充当宾语，不再加 it。
</Callout>

<Callout type="tip" title="参考答案（自己写完再看）">
  1. On going into the kitchen, I turned on the light because it was dark.
  2. When my brother shouted angrily, the lights went on.
  3. I spoilt a film which he was developing.
</Callout>

## 12. 书信写作 Letter writing — How to begin a letter

<Callout type="info" title="任务">
  **How to begin a letter** —— 写信如何开头。

  教材给出了两条规则：

  a. 给**朋友**写信要称呼他们的**名字**，如 *Dear Fred*——**决不要**以 *Dear Friend*（亲爱的朋友）开头。

  b. 给**亲戚**写信可以这样开头：*Dear Mother, Dear Father, Dear Uncle Fred, Dear Aunt Alice*，但**决不可**用 *Dear Cousin* 或 *Dear Cousin Fred*。

  **Exercise（练习）**：How would you begin letters to:

  - your grandmother —— 你的祖母
  - your cousin Elizabeth —— 你的表姐伊丽莎白
  - your friend Jack —— 你的朋友杰克
  - your uncle Tom —— 你的叔叔汤姆
</Callout>

<Callout type="tip" title="参考答案（自己写完再看）">
  - Grandmother: **Dear Grandmother**（祖母属于直系亲属，可以写 Dear Grandmother）
  - Cousin Elizabeth: **Dear Elizabeth**（不可用 Dear Cousin 或 Dear Cousin Elizabeth——只用名字）
  - Friend Jack: **Dear Jack**（朋友用名字即可）
  - Uncle Tom: **Dear Uncle Tom**（叔叔可以加 Uncle）
</Callout>

## 13. 拓展练习 Going further

<Callout type="tip" title="自学者建议">
  - **影子跟读 (shadowing)**：用第 1 节的三个 SayIt 按钮跟读 3–5 遍。重点感受结尾句 *"... in that case, I prefer to stay on the bus"* 不紧不慢的淡定节奏——这种"遇到乌龙不炸毛、将错就错"的语气正是地道的英式幽默。
  - **复述 (retell)**：合上课文，用 1 分钟把故事讲一遍。**叙事三段**：起因（作者爱旅行 → 做短途游 → 上了去 Woodford Green 的车但不知在哪下 → 售票员答应提醒）→ 经过（车到终点只剩他一人 → 售票员承认忘了让他下 → 发现这不是目的地）→ 结局（车子要原路返回 → 作者干脆坐着兜回去）。
  - **造句迁移**：用今天的 5 个核心结构造你自己的句子——
    1. **love + V-ing**（习惯性喜好）—— *She loves reading before bed.*
    2. **where + to do**（疑问词 + 不定式作宾语）—— *Can you tell me how to reach the station?*
    3. **现在分词作伴随状语**—— *Walking home, I ran into an old friend.*
    4. **forget to do vs forget doing**（不定式 vs 动名词）—— *I forgot to lock the door. / I'll never forget meeting her.*
    5. **It takes + sb. + 时间 + to do**（花时间）—— *It took me three hours to finish the homework.*
  - **词义辨析速记**：
    - **lose（丢/输）vs loose（松的）vs miss（错过/想念）**—— 口诀：*"If you **lose** your key, the door won't come **loose**; you'll **miss** the bus and feel sad."*
    - **expect（心理预期）vs wait for（行为等待）**—— 口诀：*"I **expect** rain, but I won't **wait for** it."*（我预料会下雨，但我不会站在那等它下。）
  - **延伸思考**：本课最妙的一层是标题 *Taken for a ride* 的**双关**。在英式俚语中，*take sb. for a ride* 既是"带某人兜风"，又指"欺骗/愚弄某人"（比如故意把某人带去没人的地方、或让人白跑一趟）。作者确实是"被带去兜了风"（多坐了一大段往返），但他又是被**无意**地"耍"了一下（售票员忘了喊人，并非恶意）。所以这个标题既是字面的"乘车兜风"，又暗合"被摆了乌龙"的无奈感——笑得淡淡的。下次写标题时问自己："这个词，还有第二层意思吗？"——好标题的秘密往往就藏在这层一语双关里。
</Callout>
