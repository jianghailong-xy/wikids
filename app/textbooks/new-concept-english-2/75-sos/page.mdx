## The Text 课文

When a light passenger plane **flew off course** some time ago, it **crashed in the mountains** and its pilot was killed. The only passengers, a young woman and her two baby daughters, **were unhurt**. It was the middle of winter. **Snow lay thick on the ground**. The woman knew that **the nearest village was miles away**. When it grew dark, she **turned a suitcase into a bed** and put the children inside it, **covering them with all the clothes she could find**. During the night, it got terribly cold. The woman **kept as near as she could to** the children and even tried to get into the case herself, but it was too small. Early next morning, she **heard planes passing overhead** and wondered how she could send a signal. Then she had an idea. She **stamped out the letters 'SOS'** in the snow. Fortunately, a pilot saw the signal and sent a message by radio to the nearest town. **It was not long before** a helicopter **arrived on the scene** to rescue the survivors of the plane crash.

## 课文注释 Notes on the text

<Callout type="info" title="6 个值得停下来嚼一嚼的点">
  1. **SOS** (l.11) = “Save Our Souls”（拯救我们的灵魂），是国际通用的呼救信号。它无所谓单词拼写，是约定俗成的求救符号——在莫尔斯电码里就是 “···— — —···”，好认、好发。
  2. **fly off course** (l.1) — “偏离航线 / 航向”。**off** 在这里是介词，表 “离开、脱离”；**course** 指 “航线、航向”。同型：*The ship was blown off course by the storm.*（船被暴风刮离了航线。）
  3. **Snow lay thick on the ground.** (l.4) — “地上积着厚厚的雪。” 这里的 **lay** 是 **lie**（躺、平展）的过去式，作**系动词**；**thick** 是**表语形容词**，描述主语 snow 的状态（而不是修饰动词，所以不用副词 thickly）。同型：*The dust lay thick on the shelves.*
  4. **she heard planes passing overhead** (l.9-10) — “她听见头顶上有飞机飞过”。**hear sb./sth. doing** = 听见某物正在做某事；**passing overhead** 是**现在分词短语作宾语补足语**，强调 “飞过” 这个动作正在进行。对比 *hear sb. do*（听见全过程）：*She heard the planes pass.*
  5. **stamp out** (l.10) — “跺 / 踩出（来）”。**out** 是副词，与一些动词连用时常带 “（使）显现出来、被看见 / 被听见” 的意味，如 *write out*（写出来）、*tap out*（敲打出）、*ring out*（响起）。课文里她用脚把 SOS “踩” 进雪地，让它能被空中看见。
  6. **It was not long before ...** (l.11-12) — “不久（之后）就……”。这里的 **before** 是**连词**，引导**时间状语从句**；**long** 是形容词，作主句 *It was ...* 的表语。⚠️ 千万别把它和词组 **long before**（很久以前）或 **before long**（不久以后）混淆——那两个里的 long 是另一种用法。
</Callout>

## 3. 关键句型 一般过去时 The simple past — 叙事的主线

<Callout type="tip" title="一分钟回顾">
  本课的 Key structures 点名复习 **一般过去时（What happened?，KS 3 / 27 / 51）**。一篇讲 “发生了什么” 的记叙文，主干动词几乎全是一般过去时——它像一条时间轴，把一个个动作按先后钉在上面：

  | 动作 | 课文原句 | 形式 |
  |---|---|---|
  | 坠机 | *the plane **crashed** in the mountains* | 规则动词 + -ed |
  | 遇难（被动） | *its pilot **was killed*** | was/were + 过去分词 |
  | 积雪 | *Snow **lay** thick on the ground* | lie → **lay**（不规则） |
  | 求生 | *she **turned** a suitcase into a bed and **put** the children inside* | turn → turned / put → **put**（原形不变） |
  | 听见 | *she **heard** planes passing overhead* | hear → **heard** |
  | 求救 | *She **stamped** out the letters 'SOS'* | stamp → stamped |
  | 获救 | *a helicopter **arrived** on the scene* | arrive → arrived |

  ### ⚠️ 4 个本课最容易踩的雷区

  1. **lay vs laid vs lain**：*Snow **lay** thick*——这里是 **lie（躺/平展）的过去式 lay**，不及物、不接宾语。别和 *lay（放置）→ laid → laid*（及物，要接宾语）搞混。**lie（说谎）** 又是另一个词（lie-lied-lied）。
  2. **不规则过去式**：本课 *flew / was / lay / knew / grew / put / kept / heard / saw / sent* 全是不规则。put 的过去式还是 *put*（不变形），最易被错写成 putted。
  3. **被动语态也用过去式**：*its pilot **was** killed*——“被杀” 用 *was/were + 过去分词*，主语是承受动作的人。
  4. **过去时 ≠ 都加 -ed**：考试里最常见的失分是 “知道这是过去但写成现在”，或 “把不规则动词硬加 -ed（knowed / putted ✗）”。
</Callout>

## 4. 难点：表示 “变成” 的系动词 Verbs meaning *become*

<Callout type="info" title="一组都表示 “变成” 的动词">
  课文 *When it **grew** dark ...*（天黑下来时）里的 **grow**，用的不是 “生长” 而是 “**变得**” 的意思。教材的 Special Difficulties 把这一类动词集中起来——它们都相当于 **become**，后面接形容词（表语）：

  | 动词 | 教材例句 | 中文 |
  |---|---|---|
  | **grow / get** | *He **grew** (or **got**) angry when I told him about it.* | （逐渐）变得生气 |
  | **turn / get** | *The leaves **are turning** (or **getting**) yellow.* | 树叶正在变黄 |
  | **go** | *This apple **has gone** bad.* | 这只苹果坏了 |
  | **get** | *Coal **got** scarce last winter.* | 去年冬天煤变得很缺乏 |
  | **come** | *Everything you said **came** true.* | 你说过的都成真了 |
  | **fall** | *She **fell** ill while she was on holiday.* | 她度假时病倒了 |

  ### ⚠️ 怎么挑对那个 “变”？

  - **grow / get / turn**：表示**逐渐**变化（颜色、天气、情绪、年龄）。get 最口语、最百搭。
  - **go**：常用于**变坏 / 变糟**（go bad / go sour / go mad / go grey）。
  - **come**：常用于**朝好的、如愿的方向**变（come true / come right / come loose 也算 “松开来”）。
  - **fall**：固定搭配 **fall ill / fall asleep / fall silent / fall vacant**（职位空出）。
  - 这些都是**系动词**，后面跟**形容词**，不是副词：*grew dark* ✓ / *grew darkly* ✗。
</Callout>

## 10. 摘要写作 Summary writing (≤ 80 词)

<Callout type="info" title="任务">
  *In not more than 80 words*, describe **what happened**. 用教材给的 11 个要点（points）和连接词（connections）串成**两段**：

  1. Plane crashed — mountains. （When ...）
  2. Only passengers — woman, baby daughters not hurt. （but）
  3. Mid-winter — extremely cold. （and / However）
  4. Put children in case. （so / After putting）
  5. Covered them — clothes. （However, and）
  6. Kept close all night. （and / the woman）
  7. Planes — next morning. （When / On hearing）
  8. Stamped 'SOS' — snow. （the woman）
  9. Seen by pilot. （This / which）
  10. Message sent. （who）
  11. Rescued — helicopter. （and）
</Callout>

<Callout type="tip" title="参考范文（自己写完再看）">
  When a light plane crashed in the mountains, the pilot was killed, but the only passengers, a woman and her two baby daughters, were not hurt. It was mid-winter and extremely cold. The woman put the children in a suitcase and covered them with clothes, then kept close to them all night. When she heard planes the next morning, she stamped 'SOS' in the snow. This was seen by a pilot, who sent a message, and the survivors were rescued by helicopter. *(80 words)*
</Callout>

## 11. 作文 Composition (约 150 词，两段)

<Callout type="info" title="任务">
  Write two paragraphs in **about 150 words** using the ideas given below:

  1. A light aeroplane with a heavy cargo — sudden storm — high winds — the pilot made a crash-landing in the snow.
  2. The pilot was unhurt — roped the plane to a rock — spent the night in a tent — next morning — found that the plane had been swept away by the wind — smashed to pieces — cargo and wreckage in the snow.
</Callout>

<Callout type="warning" title="提示骨架">
  - **第 1 段（坠落）**：起飞 → 货重 → 突遇暴风 → 狂风把飞机吹离航线 → 被迫雪地迫降。
  - **第 2 段（次日）**：人没事 → 用绳把飞机系在岩石上 → 在帐篷里熬过一夜 → 清晨却发现飞机被风刮走 → 撞得粉碎 → 货物与残骸散落雪中。
</Callout>

<Callout type="tip" title="写作脚手架">
  - **时态主线**：一般过去时叙事（took off, ran into, made）；**过去完成时**表 “更早发生” —— *found that the plane **had been swept away***。
  - **“天气 / 风暴” 词库**：*a sudden storm broke, gale-force winds, was buffeted by the wind, visibility dropped, was forced down*。
  - **“固定 / 防护” 词库**：*roped the plane to a rock, lashed it down, took shelter in a tent, huddled inside*。
  - **“损毁” 词库**：*swept away, smashed to pieces, scattered across the snow, nothing but wreckage was left*。
</Callout>

<Callout type="tip" title="参考范文（自己写完再看）">
  A light aeroplane carrying a heavy cargo took off in fine weather, but a sudden storm soon broke over the mountains. High winds buffeted the little plane and drove it off course. Unable to climb above the gale, the pilot was forced to make a crash-landing on a snow-covered slope.

  Luckily, the pilot was unhurt. Fearing that the wind might move the plane, he roped it firmly to a rock and spent the night in a small tent nearby. When he woke the next morning, however, he was astonished to find that the plane had been swept away by the wind. He searched the slope and discovered that it had been smashed to pieces. Cargo and wreckage lay scattered across the snow, and there was nothing he could do but wait for rescue. *(128 words)*
</Callout>

## 12. 书信写作 Letter writing

<Callout type="info" title="任务">
  Write a suitable ***Purpose*** in **about 50 words** to follow this introductory paragraph:

  *Forgive me for not writing earlier to thank you for the lovely scarf you sent me for my birthday, but I have been in bed with 'flu.*
</Callout>

<Callout type="tip" title="写作脚手架">
  - **承上**：先把 “生病” 这条线接住——病好了 / 还在恢复，再转入正题。
  - **目的（Purpose）要点**：① 围巾很合心意（颜色 / 用处）② 谢意 ③ 顺带聊近况 / 邀约见面。
  - **常用句**：*I am glad to say I am much better now. / The scarf is exactly the colour I love and has kept me warm through this cold spell. / The moment I am back on my feet, I'd love to meet up and thank you properly.*
</Callout>

<Callout type="tip" title="参考范文（自己写完再看）">
  I am glad to say I am much better now, though still a little weak. I simply had to write and tell you how much I love the scarf — it is exactly the shade of blue I had been looking for, and it has kept me beautifully warm in bed. As soon as I am well again, I hope we can meet so that I can thank you in person. *(67 words — 可再精简到 50 左右)*
</Callout>

## 13. 拓展练习 Going further

<Callout type="tip" title="自学者建议">
  - **影子跟读 (shadowing)**：用第 1 节的三个 SayIt 按钮跟读 3-5 遍，重点找准 *Snow lay thick* 里 lay 的发音，以及 *It was not long before ...* 的句子重音。
  - **复述 (retell)**：合上课文，用 1 分钟把故事讲一遍。**三段式**：背景（坠机 → 母女幸存 → 隆冬大雪）→ 经过（搭床御寒 → 熬夜 → 清晨听见飞机 → 踩出 SOS）→ 结局（飞行员发现 → 无线电报信 → 直升机获救）。
  - **句型默写 — 句子结构 (Sentence structure)**：教材最后一题是把四个短句合并成课文第 5-7 行的长句。试着自己写出 *When ...* 开头的版本，再对照课文：

    *When it grew dark, she turned a suitcase into a bed and put the children inside it, covering them with all the clothes she could find.*
  - **造句迁移**：用今天的 5 个高价值结构造你自己的句子——
    1. *S + flew / lay / put ...*（不规则动词过去式）—— *The cat lay quietly by the fire all evening.*
    2. *grow / get / go / fall + 形容词*（“变成”）—— *It grew dark before we reached home.*
    3. *hear sb./sth. doing*（感官动词 + 现在分词）—— *I heard someone calling my name.*
    4. *not ... enough*（enough 后置）—— *The rope was not long enough to reach the ground.*
    5. *It was not long before ...*（不久就……）—— *It was not long before the rain stopped.*
  - **延伸思考**：本课是一篇标准的 “**What happened?**” 记叙文——主干几乎全是一般过去时，像电影分镜一样把动作一格一格排好。下次你写英文叙事，先问自己：“主线动作用一般过去时；更早发生的回溯用过去完成时（had + 过去分词）；背景 / 正在发生用过去进行时。” 三把尺子在手，故事就有了清晰的时间层次。另一个值得记住的细节：当人没有高科技设备时，**SOS** 这样一个最简单的约定符号，配上 “被看见” 的智慧（踩进雪地里），就能跨越语言、跨越距离把命救回来——好的求救，往往胜在 “让对方一眼就懂”。
</Callout>
