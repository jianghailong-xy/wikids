## The Text 课文

The whole village soon learnt that a large sum of money **had been lost**. Sam Benton, the local butcher, **had lost his wallet while taking his savings** to the post office. Sam was sure that the wallet **must have been found** by one of the villagers, but **it was not returned to him**. Three months passed, and then one morning, Sam found his wallet outside his front door. **It had been wrapped up in newspaper** and it contained half the money he had lost, together with a note which said: 'A thief, yes, but only 50 per cent a thief!' Two months later, **some more money was sent to Sam** with another note: 'Only 25 per cent a thief now!' **In time, all Sam's money was paid back** in this way. The last note said: 'I am 100 per cent honest now!'

## 课文注释 Notes on the text

<Callout type="info" title="3 个值得停下来嚼一嚼的点">
  1. **had lost his wallet while taking his savings to the post office** (l.2) — "在把存款送往邮局的途中把钱包丢了"。**while taking his savings** 是 *while* 引导的时间状语从句的**省略形式**，完整说法是 *while he was taking his savings*。当从句主语与主句主语一致、且谓语含 *be* 时，可省去"主语 + be"，只留 **while + V-ing**。同型：*He hurt his hand while playing football.*（= while he was playing football）。
  2. **the wallet must have been found by one of the villagers** (l.3) — "钱包肯定是被某个村民捡到了"。**must** 在这里表**推测**（"一定……"）；因为是对**过去**事情的推测，所以用 **must have + 过去分词**；又因为钱包是"被捡到"，所以再叠加**被动语态** → **must have been found**。一条公式：*must have been + p.p.* = "（过去）一定被……"。同型：*The window is broken — it must have been smashed by the storm.*
  3. **in time** (l.9) — "最后；终于；久而久之"。注意它 **不等于** *on time*（准时）。*in time* 强调"经过一段时间后终归如此"，相当于 *eventually / sooner or later*。同型：*Don't worry, you'll learn it in time.* 而 *on time* 才是"按点、不迟到"：*The train arrived on time.*
</Callout>

## 3. 关键句型 被动语态 The passive voice

<Callout type="tip" title="一分钟回顾：本课是被动语态的复习专题（KS10 + KS21 + KS34）">
  整篇课文的"主角"其实是**钱包和钱**——做动作的人（捡钱的村民）始终匿名。当我们**关心"东西怎么了"而不关心"谁做的"**时，英语就用**被动语态**：*be + 过去分词 (p.p.)*。本课把被动语态在**各种时态/情态**下的形式来了一次大检阅：

  | 形式 | 课文例句 | 结构 |
  |---|---|---|
  | **一般过去被动** | *some more money **was sent** to Sam* | was/were + p.p. |
  | **过去完成被动** | *a large sum of money **had been lost*** / *It **had been wrapped up*** | had been + p.p. |
  | **情态被动（推测）** | *the wallet **must have been found*** | must have been + p.p. |
  | **一般过去被动（否定）** | *it **was not returned** to him* | was not + p.p. |
  | **一般过去被动（结果）** | *all Sam's money **was paid back*** | was + p.p. |

  教材在 *Key structures* 里专门复习了三课的被动句型：

  | 复习课 | 例句 |
  |---|---|
  | **KS10** | *This bridge was built in 1942. / The thief was arrested by the police.* |
  | **KS21** | *I can't find my bag. It must have been stolen. / I must be paid for this.* |
  | **KS34** | *I was told to wait for him. / He never expected the bicycle to be found.* |

  并给出"化主动为被动"的范例：

  | Instead of saying（主动） | We can say（被动） |
  |---|---|
  | I found out that *someone had sent the parcel* to the wrong address. | I found out that *the parcel had been sent* to the wrong address. |
  | He told me *the police had arrested the thief*. | He told me *the thief had been arrested* (by the police). |

  ### ⚠️ 4 个本课最容易踩的雷区

  1. **被动 = be 的相应形式 + 过去分词**。改写时**时态体现在 be 上**，主动词一律变 p.p.：*has prepared → has been prepared*；*will translate → will be translated*；*must send → must be sent*；*had put out → had been put out*。
  2. **不及物动词没有被动**。*disappear, happen, arrive* 等不能说 *was disappeared*。但 *lose / find / send / pay back / wrap up* 是及物的，都能变被动。
  3. **by + 动作发出者**常可省略。当"谁做的"不重要或不知道时，*by ...* 整段省掉：*all Sam's money was paid back*（没人关心是谁还的）。
  4. **双宾语动词**（give / send / tell …）变被动，通常用**间接宾语作主语**：*Some more money was sent **to Sam*** → *Sam **was sent** some more money*；*Someone gave the cat some milk* → *The cat **was given** some milk*。
</Callout>

## 4. 难点：steal / rob / pay back 经常混淆的词

<Callout type="info" title="偷、抢、还——三个最容易用错的动作">
  教材 *Special Difficulties* 专挑了三个和"钱"有关、却最容易混的词：

  | 词 | 搭配 | 含义 | 例句 |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | **steal** | steal **sth** (from sb/somewhere) | 偷**东西**（宾语是"被偷的物"） | A thief broke in and **stole** some money from the safe. |
  | **rob** | rob **sb/sth** (of sth) | 抢劫**人/机构**（宾语是"被抢的对象"） | Two thieves **robbed** him **of** all his money. / the men who **robbed the bank** |
  | **pay back** | pay sb back / pay back sth | 偿还（钱）；（口语）报复 | All Sam's money **was paid back**. (ll.9-10) / I'll **pay you back** for what you did. |

  ### ⚠️ 一句话记牢
  - **steal 后面跟"东西"**（steal *money / a car / pictures*）；**rob 后面跟"人或地方"**（rob *a man / a bank*），要说"抢走某物"得用 **rob sb of sth**。一句口诀：*steal 偷物，rob 抢人*。
  - **pay back** 既是"把钱还回去"（= repay），也能在口语里表示"报复"：*I'll pay you back!*（我会报复你的！）——本课是前一个意思。
</Callout>

## 10. 摘要写作 Summary writing (≤ 70 词)

## 11. 改写句子 Composition (joining words)

<Callout type="info" title="任务">
  *Rewrite the following sentences using the joining words in parentheses.* 用括号内的连词改写以下句子。

  1. Sam told everybody about the wallet. He did not try to find the thief. *(but)*
  2. The man was not really a thief. He needed money badly. *(but)*
  3. He paid back the money. He bought himself a clear conscience. *(not only ... but also)*
</Callout>

<Callout type="warning" title="提示骨架">
  - **but** 连接两个**相反/转折**的分句，前后通常是完整的句子，第二个分句可省略重复的主语：*..., but did not ...*。
  - **not only ... but also ...** 连接**并列**成分（这里连两个谓语动词短语）；*not only* 后接动词原形，*but also* 后再接一个动词短语，时态保持一致。
</Callout>

<Callout type="tip" title="参考答案（自己写完再看）">
  1. Sam told everybody about the wallet, **but** he did not try to find the thief.
  2. The man was not really a thief, **but** he needed money badly.
  3. He **not only** paid back the money **but also** bought himself a clear conscience.
</Callout>

## 12. 书信写作 Letter writing

<Callout type="info" title="任务">
  *Address an envelope to a married lady who lives abroad.* 为写给居住在国外的一位已婚女士的信配一个信封（写出信封上的收信人地址）。
</Callout>

<Callout type="tip" title="信封格式脚手架">
  英文信封"收信人"信息**右下偏中**书写，从小到大、逐行缩进：

  1. **称呼 + 姓名**：已婚女士用 **Mrs.**（再加丈夫或本人姓氏）—— *Mrs. J. Brown*
  2. **门牌号 + 街名**：*24 Banbury Road*
  3. **城市 + 邮编**：*Oxford OX2 6NN*
  4. **国家（寄往国外必写，常大写）**：*ENGLAND*

  示例：

  ```
                              Mrs. J. Brown
                              24 Banbury Road
                              Oxford OX2 6NN
                              ENGLAND
  ```

  - 寄往国外时，**国家名单独成行、通常全大写**，方便各国邮局识别。
  - 已婚女士传统上用 **Mrs.**；若不确定婚姻状况可用 **Ms.**。
</Callout>

## 13. 拓展练习 Going further

<Callout type="tip" title="自学者建议">
  - **影子跟读 (shadowing)**：用第 1 节的三个 SayIt 按钮跟读 3–5 遍，重点体会**被动句**里 *had been / was / must have been* 这些助动词的**弱读**——它们读得又轻又快，过去分词才是重音落点。
  - **复述 (retell)**：合上课文，用 1 分钟把 Sam 的故事讲一遍。**三段式**：丢钱（屠户去邮局途中丢了装着全部存款的钱包）→ 失而复得（三个月后钱包出现在门口，分期还钱）→ 点睛（字条良心指数 50%→25%→100%，问心无愧）。
  - **被动语态造句迁移**：用今天复习的 5 类被动各造一句——
    1. *一般过去被动*：*The window **was broken** last night.*
    2. *过去完成被动*：*By the time I arrived, the cake **had been eaten**.*
    3. *情态被动（推测）*：*The keys **must have been left** at home.*
    4. *将来被动*：*The report **will be finished** tomorrow.*
    5. *双宾语变被动*：*I **was given** a second chance.*
  - **steal / rob 辨析迁移**：各写一句——steal 后跟"东西"(*They stole my bike.*)；rob 后跟"人/地方"且配 of (*They robbed me of my phone.*)。
  - **延伸思考**：本课用一连串**被动语态**把一桩"匿名还钱"的小事讲得克制而有悬念——因为全篇真正的"动作者"始终隐身，读者的注意力就被牢牢锁在"钱怎么样了"。当你**不想（或不必）点明是谁做的**，被动语态就是最得体的选择。再回味标题 *A clear conscience*：那个捡钱的人，用三笔分期付款，最终给自己**买回了一颗问心无愧的心**——这正是英语用 *pay back* 一词巧妙的双关（既"还钱"又"心安"）。
</Callout>
