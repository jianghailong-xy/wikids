## The Text 课文

Last Christmas, the circus owner, Jimmy Gates, **decided to take** some presents to a children's hospital. **Dressed up as Father Christmas** and **accompanied by** a 'guard of honour' of six pretty girls, he **set off down** the main street of the city riding a baby elephant called Jumbo. He **should have known** that the police would never allow this sort of thing. A policeman **approached** Jimmy and told him he **ought to have gone along a side street** as Jumbo was **holding up the traffic**. Though Jimmy **agreed to go at once**, Jumbo **refused to move**. Fifteen policemen **had to push** very hard to get him off the main street. The police had a difficult time, but they were most amused. 'Jumbo **must weigh** a few tons,' said a policeman afterwards, 'so **it was fortunate that** we didn't have to carry him. Of course, we **should arrest** him, but as he has a good record, we shall **let him off** this time.'

## 课文注释 Notes on the text

<Callout type="info" title="7 个值得停下来嚼一嚼的点">
  1. **Dressed up as Father Christmas and accompanied by a 'guard of honour' of six pretty girls** (ll.2-3) —— “扮成圣诞老人，在 6 位漂亮姑娘组成的‘仪仗队’陪同下”。这是两个**过去分词短语作状语**（*dressed up ...* / *accompanied by ...*），说明主语 he 出发时的**伴随状态**。注意分词的逻辑主语就是主句主语 he —— 是 he 被打扮、被陪同。
  2. **He should have known** (l.5) —— “他本该知道”。**should + have + 过去分词** 表示**过去本应发生却没有发生**的事，常含**责备 / 遗憾**语气。言下之意：他当时其实没料到。
  3. **he ought to have gone along a side street** (l.7) —— “他本该走一条小路”。**ought to + have + 过去分词** 与 *should have done* 同义，都表示“过去该做而没做”。**ought** 是情态助动词，后面带 **to**。
  4. **hold up the traffic** (l.8) —— “阻碍交通、使交通堵塞”。**hold up** 在这里是“拦阻、使停顿”，不是“举起”。
  5. **get him off the main street** (ll.9-10) —— “把它弄离 / 推离主干道”。**get + 宾语 + off / out / in** 表示“使……离开 / 出来 / 进入”。
  6. **as he has a good record** (l.13) —— “由于它一贯表现良好”。**record** 这里指“（过往的）记录、履历”，尤其带点“警方档案”的味道——警察故意用对待“惯犯 / 良民”的口吻打趣大象。
  7. **let him off** (l.14) —— “放过它、饶恕它、不予处罚”。**let sb. off** = 不惩罚某人。这是动词 **let** 的众多含义之一（参见下文“难点”）。
</Callout>

## 3. 关键句型 Must / Have to / Should / Ought to

<Callout type="tip" title="一分钟回顾：义务与推测的情态动词">
  本课是 NCE2 复习 **情态动词** 的经典一课，集中操练 *must / have to / should / ought to* 这一组，并重点引入它们的**完成式** *should have done / ought to have done*。

  | 形式 | 含义 | 课文 / 例句 |
  |---|---|---|
  | **must + V** | 必须（说话人主观认为有义务） | I must leave now. |
  | **have (got) to + V** | 不得不（外部规定 / 客观必要） | Fifteen policemen **had to** push very hard |
  | **had to + V** | 过去“不得不”（must 没有过去式！） | we didn't **have to** carry him |
  | **should / ought to + V** | 应该（建议、义务，语气比 must 弱） | we **should** arrest him |
  | **should have + 过去分词 / ought to have + 过去分词** | 本该做却没做（含责备 / 遗憾） | He **should have known** / he **ought to have gone** |
  | **must + V（推测）** | 一定（对现状的有把握推断） | Jumbo **must weigh** a few tons |

  ### ⚠️ 4 个本课最容易踩的雷区

  1. **must 没有过去式**：表“过去不得不”只能用 **had to**（课文 *had to push*）。错写成 *must push yesterday* ✗。
  2. **must 的两副面孔**：既能表“必须”（义务），也能表“一定”（推测）。课文 *Jumbo must weigh a few tons* 是**推测**（≈ probably weighs）。
  3. **didn't have to ≠ mustn't**：*didn't have to carry* = “不必抬”（没必要，也没抬）；*mustn't carry* = “不许抬”（禁止）。意思天差地别。
  4. **should / ought to have + 过去分词** = “本该……（但没做）”。*He should have known* 的潜台词是“他其实没料到”；千万别理解成“他知道了”。
</Callout>

## 4. 难点：let 的多义 / agree vs accept / dress up vs dress

<Callout type="info" title="3 组教材点名的易混点">
  本课 *Special Difficulties* 讲了三组词，全是写作高频坑：

  ### ① 动词 let 在不同句子里意思不同
  | 句子 | let 的含义 |
  |---|---|
  | we shall **let him off** this time (ll.11-12) | 放过、不处罚（= We shall not punish him.） |
  | he **let me down** | 使我失望（= He failed to do what I expected.） |
  | don't **let him in** | 让……进来（= allow to enter） |
  | Who **let the dog out**? | 把……放出去 |

  ### ② Agree vs Accept
  - **agree to do sth.** = 同意（去做某事）：*Though Jimmy **agreed to go** at once, Jumbo refused to move.* (ll.8-9)
  - **accept sth.** = 接受（某物 / 道歉 / 邀请）：*We are not allowed to **accept** presents from customers.*（注意 accept 后**直接接名词**，不接不定式。）

  ### ③ Dress up vs Dress
  - **dress up (as ...)** = 盛装打扮、乔装（成）：***Dressed up** as Father Christmas ...* (ll.2-3) = wearing fancy dress.
  - **dress** = （给自己 / 别人）穿衣（日常动作）：*I got up at 6 o'clock, washed, shaved, **dressed** and had breakfast.*
</Callout>

## 10. 摘要写作 Summary writing (≤ 80 词)

<Callout type="info" title="任务">
  *Answer these questions in not more than 80 words.*（用不超过 80 词回答下列问题，连成 2-3 个段落 / 一段连贯叙述。）

  1. What did he decide to take to a children's hospital? How did the circus owner, Jimmy Gates, dress up? Did he set off down the main street of the city, or did he go down a side street? Was he riding an elephant called Jumbo or not? *(After having ... and ... riding)*
  2. Was he told that he was holding up the traffic? Did Jimmy agree to go at once, or not? Did Jumbo agree to go, or did he refuse to move? How many policemen had to push him off the main street? *(On being ... but ... so ...)*
  3. Did he have a good record or not? Was Jumbo arrested or not? *(As ... however, ...)*
</Callout>

<Callout type="tip" title="参考范文（自己写完再看）">

On being told that he was holding up the traffic, Jimmy agreed to go at once, but Jumbo refused to move, so fifteen policemen had to push him off the main street. As Jumbo had a good record, however, he was not arrested. *(67 words)*

## 11. 作文 Composition

<Callout type="info" title="任务">
  *Write three or four sentences using the ideas given below:*（用下列提示写出 3 到 4 个句子。）

  The children were waiting at the hospital — Jimmy and the guard of honour arrived — a great welcome — Jimmy gave presents to the children — they rode on Jumbo — time to leave — Jumbo refused to move.
</Callout>

<Callout type="warning" title="提示骨架">
  - **到达**：孩子们在医院等候 → Jimmy 和“仪仗队”到了。
  - **欢迎 + 送礼**：受到热烈欢迎 → Jimmy 给孩子们发礼物 → 孩子们骑上 Jumbo。
  - **离开 + 转折**：到了该走的时候 → Jumbo 又一次拒绝移动（呼应课文的笑点）。
</Callout>

<Callout type="tip" title="写作脚手架">
  - **时态主线**：一般过去时叙事（waited / arrived / gave / rode / refused）；用过去进行表背景（*were waiting*）。
  - **“欢迎”词库**：*gave them a warm welcome, cheered loudly, greeted them with delight, crowded round the elephant*。
  - **“发礼物”词库**：*handed out presents, gave each child a present, shared out the gifts*。
  - **衔接词**：*When ... / As soon as ... / At last / But when it was time to leave ...*。
</Callout>

<Callout type="tip" title="参考范文（自己写完再看）">
  The children were waiting excitedly at the hospital when Jimmy and his guard of honour arrived on Jumbo. They gave the visitors a warm welcome, cheering as Jimmy handed out presents to every child. A few lucky ones were even allowed to ride on Jumbo's back. But when it was time to leave, Jumbo, as usual, refused to move, and everyone laughed as the nurses tried in vain to coax him out of the ward. *(73 words)*
</Callout>

## 12. 书信写作 Letter writing

<Callout type="info" title="任务">
  *Learn the following opening phrases by heart:*（把下面两个开头句熟记于心。）

  - **I really hate to complain, but ...**（我实在不想抱怨，但是……）—— 投诉信常用的“委婉开场”。
  - **Some time ago, I ...**（前些时候，我……）—— 交代事情起因的开场。

  *Exercise:* Write two short paragraphs (of not more than two sentences each) completing the above phrases.（用以上两个开头各写一小段，每段不超过两句。）
</Callout>

<Callout type="tip" title="写作脚手架">
  - **第一句（投诉口吻）**：先用 *I really hate to complain, but ...* 引出不满，再补一句具体说明。
    - *I really hate to complain, but the new washing machine you delivered last week has already broken down twice.*
  - **第二句（交代起因）**：用 *Some time ago, I ...* 讲事情的来龙去脉。
    - *Some time ago, I ordered a set of bookshelves from your shop, and I am still waiting for them to arrive.*
  - **语气提示**：投诉信要“客气但坚定”——hate to complain 表客气，but 之后把问题说清楚；动词时态多用现在完成（*has broken down*）+ 一般过去（*ordered*）。
</Callout>

<Callout type="tip" title="参考范文（自己写完再看）">
  I really hate to complain, but the heater I bought from your shop last month has stopped working altogether. I should be grateful if you could send someone to repair it as soon as possible.

  Some time ago, I left a coat to be cleaned at your branch in the High Street. When I called to collect it yesterday, however, I was told that it had been mislaid.
</Callout>

## 13. 拓展练习 Going further

<Callout type="tip" title="自学者建议">
  - **影子跟读 (shadowing)**：用第 1 节的三个 SayIt 按钮跟读 3-5 遍，重点体会 *should have known / ought to have gone* 里 **have** 弱读成 /əv/ 的连读节奏。
  - **复述 (retell)**：合上课文，用 2 分钟把故事讲一遍。**叙事三段**：起因（圣诞节送礼 → 扮圣诞老人骑象上街）→ 经过（堵车 → 警察拦下 → Jumbo 拒不移动 → 15 人合力推走）→ 结局（警察打趣“记录良好，这次饶了它”）。
  - **造句迁移**：用今天的 5 个核心结构造你自己的句子——
    1. *should have + 过去分词*（本该……却没）—— *I should have set off earlier.*
    2. *ought to have + 过去分词*（本该……却没）—— *You ought to have told me sooner.*
    3. *had to + V*（过去不得不）—— *We had to wait for two hours.*
    4. *must + V（推测）*（一定）—— *He must be at least sixty.*
    5. *let sb. off*（放过 / 饶恕）—— *The judge let the young man off with a warning.*
  - **延伸思考**：本课的语言核心是 **一组“义务与推测”的情态动词**——*must / have to / should / ought to*。记住三把尺子：① 过去“不得不”只能用 **had to**（must 没有过去式）；② **should / ought to have done** = “本该做却没做”，自带遗憾或责备；③ **must** 既能“必须”，也能“一定”（推测）。另一个值得回味的点：作者把 **arrest / good record / let off** 这些**司法术语**安到一头大象身上，制造出“一本正经地胡说”的英式幽默——好的叙事，常常在最后一句用一个出人意料的措辞，把平常事写出会心一笑。
</Callout>
