## The Text 课文

I **used to travel** by air a great deal when I was a boy. My parents **used to live** in South America and I **used to fly** there from Europe in the holidays. A flight attendant **would take charge of** me and I never had an unpleasant experience. I **am used to travelling** by air and **only on one occasion have I ever felt frightened**. After **taking off**, we **were flying low** over the city and slowly **gaining height**, when the plane suddenly turned round and flew back to the airport. While we **were waiting** to land, a flight attendant told us to keep calm and to get off the plane quietly **as soon as it had touched down**. Everybody **on board** was worried and we were curious to find out what had happened. Later we learnt that there was a very important person on board. The police **had been told** that a bomb **had been planted** on the plane. After we had landed, the plane was searched thoroughly. Fortunately, nothing was found and five hours later we were able to take off again.

## 课文注释 Notes on the text

<Callout type="info" title="7 个值得停下来嚼一嚼的点">
  1. **take charge of me** (l.3) — “照看我、负责管我”。**take charge of + sth./sb.** = 接管、负责。课文 *A flight attendant would take charge of me* = 一位空中乘务员总会照看我。同型：*She took charge of the whole project. / Who is in charge of the class?*
  2. **I am used to travelling by air** (l.4) — “我（现在）已习惯了乘飞机旅行”。**be used to + 名词 / 动名词** 表“习惯于……”，这里的 **to 是介词**，后面必须接 V-ing，不能接动词原形。注意和 **used to + 动词原形**（过去常常）区分。同型：*I am used to getting up early. / She is used to the noise now.*
  3. **only on one occasion have I ever felt frightened** (l.5) — “只有一次我感到过害怕”。**only + 状语**放到句首，主句要**部分倒装**——把助动词 have 提到主语 I 前面。同型：*Only then did I understand. / Only in this way can we succeed.*
  4. **take off** (l.5) — （飞机）起飞。本课难点专讲 take 的多义，take off 还可表“脱下、模仿、发射”。
  5. **gain height** (l.6) — （飞机）爬升、升高。*slowly gaining height* 是现在分词短语作伴随状语。
  6. **touch down** (l.9) — （飞机）着陆、降落。*as soon as it had touched down* = 飞机一着陆。
  7. **on board** (l.9) — 在（飞机 / 船 / 火车）上。*Everybody on board was worried* = 飞机上的人都很着急。同型：*a very important person on board / welcome on board*。
</Callout>

## 3. 关键句型 used to / would / be used to 过去的习惯三件套

<Callout type="tip" title="一分钟回顾">
  本课的 Key structures 复习的是**表过去发生 / 过去正在进行 / 过去经常发生**的动词形式（KS 7, 31, 55）。三个长得很像的表达，一定要分清：

  | 形式 | 含义 | 课文例句 | 关键限制 |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | **used to + 原形** | 过去常常（现在不再） | *I **used to travel** by air ... My parents **used to live** in ...* | 可表过去的**动作或状态** |
  | **would + 原形** | 过去反复发生的**动作** | *A flight attendant **would take charge of** me* | **只表反复动作**，不表状态（×would live） |
  | **be used to + 名词 / 动名词** | 习惯于…… | *I **am used to travelling** by air* | to 是**介词**，后接 V-ing |
  | **过去进行 was/were + V-ing** | 过去某刻正在进行 | *we **were flying** low ... when the plane suddenly turned round* | 常被 when + 一般过去“打断” |
  | **过去完成 had + 过去分词** | 过去之前已完成 | *a bomb **had been planted** ... after it **had touched down*** | 用作“过去的过去” |

  ### ⚠️ 4 个本课最容易踩的雷区

  1. **used to travel** vs **be used to travelling**：前者“过去常常乘飞机（现在不一定）”，后者“（现在）已习惯乘飞机”。一个看动词原形，一个看 V-ing，差一个 -ing 意思全变。
  2. **would 不能表过去的状态**：*My parents would live in South America* ✗——live 是状态动词，过去的居住状态只能用 *used to live*。would 只接“反复的动作”（would take / would fly）。
  3. **only + 状语置于句首要倒装**：*Only on one occasion **have I** ever felt frightened*（助动词 have 提前），不是 *only on one occasion I have ...*。
  4. **过去进行被打断**：背景动作（正在飞）用 were flying，突发动作（掉头）用一般过去 turned——*were flying ... when ... turned round*。
</Callout>

## 4. 难点：动词 take 的多义 Special difficulties

<Callout type="info" title="一个 take，十种意思">
  课文的 *Special difficulties* 部分指出：动词 **take** 在不同搭配里意思完全不同。把这一组 take 短语记牢，做填空就稳了：

  | 短语 | 含义 | 例句 |
  |---|---|---|
  | **take off** | 起飞；脱下；模仿；发射 | *After **taking off**, we were flying over the city. / He **took off** his coat. / He is always **taking** his teacher **off**. / The rocket will **take off**.* |
  | **take after** | （长得 / 性格）像（长辈） | *Young Tom **takes after** his father.* |
  | **take up** | 占据（空间 / 时间）；开始学（某门学问） | *That wardrobe **takes up** a lot of space. / He has **taken up** French.* |
  | **take to** | 染上（习惯）；喜欢上 | *When his wife died, he **took to** drinking.* |
  | **take in** | 欺骗 | *He was so persuasive that I **was taken in**.* |
  | **take down** | 记下、写下 | *The reporter **took down** everything I said.* |
  | **take over** | 接管、接手 | *That business was doing badly until Jones **took over**.* |

  ⚠️ 同一个 **take off** 在课文里是“起飞”，在 *take his hat off* 里是“脱下”，在 *take his teacher off* 里是“模仿”，在 *the rocket will take off* 里是“发射”——靠**宾语和上下文**判断。
</Callout>

<Callout type="warning" title="提示骨架">
  - **第一段（起因）**：飞机刚起飞 → 一名乘客点了烟 → 把烟头弹进通风口 → 误以为那是烟灰缸。
  - **第二段（经过与结局）**：通风口冒烟 → 机舱一阵恐慌 → 飞机折返机场 → 消防车、救护车待命 → 无人受伤 → 火被扑灭 → 飞机重新起飞。
</Callout>

<Callout type="tip" title="写作脚手架">
  - **时态主线**：一般过去时叙事；更早的动作用过去完成（*had thrown / had mistaken*）；背景用过去进行（*smoke was pouring out / passengers were screaming*）。
  - **“冒烟 / 恐慌”动词库**：*smoke poured out of the vent, filled the cabin, passengers panicked, screamed, leapt from their seats, the alarm sounded*。
  - **“处置”动词库**：*the captain turned back, fire engines and ambulances raced onto the runway, the crew put out the flames, brought the fire under control*。
  - **收尾**：*Luckily, no one was hurt, and once the fire had been put out, the plane took off again.*
</Callout>

<Callout type="tip" title="参考范文（自己写完再看）">
  Shortly after the plane had taken off, one of the passengers lit a cigarette. Looking around for somewhere to put out the match, he noticed a small opening in the wall beside him. Mistaking the air vent for an ash tray, he carelessly dropped his lighted cigarette into it and went on reading his newspaper.

  Moments later, smoke began to pour out of the vent and quickly filled the cabin. The passengers panicked and a flight attendant struggled to keep them calm. The captain immediately turned round and flew back to the airport. As the plane landed, fire engines and ambulances were already racing onto the runway. Fortunately, no one was hurt. The crew soon put out the fire, and once everything had been checked, the plane was able to take off again. *(128 words)*
</Callout>

## 12. 书信写作 Letter writing

<Callout type="info" title="任务">
  Write a suitable **Purpose** of about **50 words** to follow this introductory paragraph（按下面这段引言的思路，写一段约 50 词的“目的”）：

  *You will be surprised to hear that your uncle Peter has unexpectedly returned from South America. He is staying with us at present and I know that he would very much like to see you.*
</Callout>

<Callout type="tip" title="写作脚手架">
  - **承接引言**：引言已交代“Peter 叔叔意外回国、正住在我们这儿、很想见你”，**Purpose 段要把“写信目的”说清**——邀请对方来相聚。
  - **要点**：① 提出邀请（来我们家住几天）；② 给出时间（趁叔叔还没回南美）；③ 说明缘由（多年未见 / 叔叔有许多见闻要分享）；④ 请对方尽快回信确认。
  - **常用句式**：*I am writing to ask whether you could ... / It would be wonderful if you could come and stay ... / Do let us know as soon as possible ...*
</Callout>

<Callout type="tip" title="参考范文（自己写完再看）">
  I am therefore writing to ask whether you could come and stay with us for a few days while he is still here. He has to fly back to South America at the end of the month, so there is not much time. We have all missed you, and it would be a perfect chance to meet again. Do let us know as soon as you can. *(54 words)*
</Callout>

## 13. 拓展练习 Going further

<Callout type="tip" title="自学者建议">
  - **影子跟读 (shadowing)**：用第 1 节的三个 SayIt 按钮各跟读 3-5 遍。重点感受 *were flying ... when ... turned round* 里“平稳叙述被一个 when 突然打断”的节奏，以及 *only on one occasion **have I** ever felt frightened* 里倒装句的语气重音。
  - **复述 (retell)**：合上课文，用 1-2 分钟把故事讲一遍。**叙事三段**：背景（从小常飞、习惯了、有乘务员照看）→ 那一次（刚起飞就折返、乘务员要大家镇静、悄悄下机）→ 真相（机上有要人、接报有炸弹、彻查无果、5 小时后再起飞）。
  - **造句迁移**：用今天的 5 个核心结构造你自己的句子——
    1. *S + used to + 原形*（过去常常）—— *I used to walk to school every day.*
    2. *S + be used to + V-ing*（习惯于）—— *She is used to working at night.*
    3. *S + would + 原形*（过去反复的动作）—— *On Sundays my grandfather would tell us stories.*
    4. *was/were + V-ing + when + 一般过去*（进行被打断）—— *I was cooking when the lights went out.*
    5. *Only + 状语 + 倒装*—— *Only then did I realize my mistake.*
  - **延伸思考**：本课是 **used to / would / be used to** 三个“过去习惯”表达的集中训练场。记住三把尺子——**used to** 看“过去常常（动作或状态，现在不再）”，**would** 看“过去反复的**动作**（不表状态）”，**be used to** 看“**现在**已习惯（后接 V-ing）”。再叠加 **过去进行**（正在做的背景）和 **过去完成**（更早发生的事），你就能像本文这样，把一段往事的“惯例—那一次—更早的隐情”三层时间线写得清清楚楚。
</Callout>
