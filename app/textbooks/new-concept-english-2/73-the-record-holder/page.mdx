  **Did the boy go where he wanted to?（这个男孩去到他想去的地方了吗？）** 带着这个问题听录音、读课文，重点留意全篇是如何用 **被动语态**（*be put to shame / be picked up / be sent back*）、**过去完成时**（*the boat had ... travelled to Calais*）和各种 **连词**（who / while / when / as / and / but）把“简单句”织成“并列句、复合句”、把一段越走越远的旅程讲得层层分明的。
</Callout>

## The Text 课文

Children who **play truant from school** are unimaginative. A quiet day's fishing, or eight hours in a cinema seeing the same film over and over again, **is usually as far as they get**. **They have all been put to shame** by a boy who, **while playing truant**, travelled 1,600 miles. He hitchhiked to Dover and, towards evening, went into a boat to find somewhere to sleep. When he woke up next morning, he discovered that **the boat had, in the meantime, travelled to Calais**. No one noticed the boy **as he crept off**. From there, he hitchhiked to Paris in a lorry. The driver gave him a few biscuits and a cup of coffee and left him just outside the city. The next car the boy stopped **did not take him into the centre of Paris as he hoped it would**, but to Perpignan on the French-Spanish border. There he **was picked up by a policeman** and sent back to England by the local authorities. He **has surely set up a record** for the thousands of children who **dream of evading school**.

## 课文注释 Notes on the text

<Callout type="info" title="6 个值得停下来嚼一嚼的点">
  1. **play truant from school** (l.1) — 逃学，也可以只说 **play truant**。*truant* 本是名词“逃学的学生”，*play truant* 是固定短语。同型：*He played truant and went fishing.*
  2. **as far as they get** (l.3) — “他们至多也就到那种程度”。这里 *as far as* 作“至多达到、最远到达（某程度）”解，引出他们逃学能耐的“天花板”。同型：*A cup of coffee is as far as my cooking gets.*
  3. **They have all been put to shame ...** (l.3) — 这是一个**被动结构**。*put sb. to shame* = 使某人相形见绌 / 使某人自愧不如（也可译“使……黯然失色”）。这里指那个走了 1,600 英里的男孩，让所有“只会钓鱼看电影”的逃学孩子都显得太没出息。同型：*Her courage put us all to shame.*
  4. **while playing truant** (l.4) — **分词短语作时间状语**，相当于一个状语从句 *while he was playing truant*（当他逃学时）。主句主语与分词逻辑主语一致（都是 the boy）时，可这样省略。同型：*While waiting for the bus, he read a book.*
  5. **in the meantime** (l.7) — 在此期间，与此同时（= meanwhile）。课文指“就在男孩睡觉的这段时间里”，船已经渡海到了加来。同型：*I'll phone the doctor. In the meantime, lie down and rest.*
  6. **did not take him into the centre of Paris as he hoped it would** (l.10) — 没有像他希望的那样把他带到巴黎市中心。*as he hoped it would* 是**方式状语从句**，作“像……那样”解；*would* 后面省略了与前面相同的谓语 *take him into the centre of Paris*（避免重复）。
</Callout>

## 3. 关键句型 简单句、并列句和复合句 · 连词

<Callout type="tip" title="一分钟回顾：把简单句“连”成并列句、复合句">
  本课的 *Key structures* 专门复习 **简单句（Simple）、并列句（Compound）、复合句（Complex）**——核心就在 **连词（joining words）**。把课文里的连词圈出来，你会看到一段旅程是怎样被“连”起来的：

  | 连词 | 课文例句 | 把句子连成 |
  |---|---|---|
  | **who**（关系代词） | *Children **who** play truant from school are unimaginative.* | 复合句（定语从句） |
  | **while**（从属连词 / 分词） | *a boy who, **while** playing truant, travelled 1,600 miles* | 复合句（时间状语） |
  | **and**（并列连词） | *He hitchhiked to Dover **and** ... went into a boat* | 并列句 |
  | **when**（从属连词） | ***When** he woke up next morning, he discovered ...* | 复合句（时间状语） |
  | **that**（从属连词） | *he discovered **that** the boat had ... travelled to Calais* | 复合句（宾语从句） |
  | **as**（从属连词） | *No one noticed the boy **as** he crept off.* | 复合句（时间状语） |
  | **but**（并列连词） | *did not take him into the centre of Paris ... **but** to Perpignan* | 并列（转折） |

  ### ⚠️ 3 个本课最容易踩的雷区

  1. **关系代词 who 不再重复主语**：*Children **who** play truant ...* ——从句里 who 已经做了主语，不能再写 *who they play truant*。
  2. **such ... that 与 so ... that 别混**：*He gave me **such a fright that** I knocked the teapot over.* —— **such + a + 名词**；如果是形容词 / 副词则用 *so*（*so frightened that ...*）。
  3. **not only ... but ... as well**：两件事并列时，*not only* 引出第一件，*but ... as well* 收束第二件——*not only reached the top **but** spent the night there **as well***，注意别和 *but also* 叠用成 *but also ... as well*。
</Callout>

## 4. 难点：被动语态 · after being done · 否定 + anyone · how far

<Callout type="info" title="教材 Structure 多项选择题里藏着的 4 组高频考点">
  本课的 *Structure* 多项选择题（第 3-7 题）集中训练 4 个语法点，恰好都来自课文。逐个拆开看：

  | 考点 | 课文 / 改写 | 要点 |
  |---|---|---|
  | **be + 过去分词（被动）** | *He **was given** a few biscuits.*（由 The driver gave him a few biscuits 变来） | 间接宾语 him 变主语 He，动词变被动 |
  | **after + being + 过去分词** | *After **being picked up** by a policeman, he was sent back.* | 介词 after 后接动名词；被动用 being done |
  | **否定 + anyone** | *He wasn't noticed by **anyone** as he crept off.* | 否定句里用 anyone，不用 no one / none |
  | **how far（问距离）** | ***How far** did he travel? 1,600 miles.* | 问距离用 how far，不是 how long（时长 / 长度） |

  ### ⚠️ 还有一道“句子结构”改写题（对照课文 ll.6-7）

  把分词开头的句子改成 **When 引导的时间状语从句**：

  - 原句：*On waking up next morning, he discovered that the boat had, in the meantime, travelled to Calais.*
  - 改写：***When he woke up** next morning, he discovered that the boat had, in the meantime, travelled to Calais.*

  要点：*On + 动名词*（On waking up）= *When + 主语 + 一般过去*（When he woke up）。补出主语 he、把 waking 还原成谓语 woke up 即可。
</Callout>

## 10. 摘要写作 Summary writing (≤ 80 词)

<Callout type="info" title="任务">
  *In not more than 80 words, describe the boy's experiences.* 用不超过 80 个单词描述这个孩子的经历，并用下面给出的**要点 (points)** 和**连词 (connections)** 写成**两个段落**。

  **要点 Points：**
  1. Boy played truant. — 2. Travelled 1,600 miles. — 3. Hitchhiked — Dover. — 4. Slept — boat. — 5. Found himself — Calais — morning.
  6. Lorry driver: lift and something to eat. — 7. Boy got off near Paris. — 8. Stopped another car. — 9. Not taken Paris — Perpignan. — 10. Picked up — policeman. — 11. Sent home — authorities.

  **连词 Connections：** who / and / where / only to find // and / On getting off / which ... not to ... / but to ... / There / and
</Callout>

<Callout type="tip" title="参考范文（自己写完再看）">
  A boy who played truant travelled 1,600 miles. He hitchhiked to Dover and slept in a boat, only to find himself in Calais next morning.

  A lorry driver gave him a lift and something to eat. On getting off near Paris, he stopped another car, which took him not to Paris but to Perpignan. There he was picked up by a policeman and sent home by the authorities. *(68 words)*
</Callout>

## 11. 作文 Composition (约 150 词，两段)

<Callout type="info" title="任务">
  *Write two paragraphs in about 150 words using the ideas given below.* 按以下思路写出 150 个词左右的两段文字：

  1. The boy returned — sick, cold and hungry — his parents were worried — met him at the harbour — their reactions.
  2. The boy returned to school — he was afraid he would be punished — the boys gave him a hero's welcome — asked questions — the headmaster punished him by making him give a talk to the whole school about his experiences abroad.
</Callout>

<Callout type="warning" title="提示骨架">
  - **第一段（回家）**：先写状态（sick, cold and hungry）→ 父母的担心（had been worried sick）→ 在码头接他（met him at the harbour）→ 他们的反应（relief + scolding 交织：又心疼又生气）。
  - **第二段（回校）**：他怕受罚（afraid he would be punished）→ 同学们却当英雄欢迎（a hero's welcome）→ 围着问个不停（asked him questions）→ 校长的“惩罚”出人意料：罚他给全校做一次关于这趟国外经历的演讲（made him give a talk）。
</Callout>

<Callout type="tip" title="写作脚手架">
  - **时态主线**：一般过去时叙事（returned / met / gave）；更早的事用过去完成（had been worried / had run away）；“害怕将来会被罚”用过去将来（he would be punished）。
  - **“狼狈”词库**：*pale, exhausted, soaked, starving, trembling with cold*。
  - **“情绪”词库**：*relief, anxiety, scolded him gently, hugged him tightly, half-angry, half-glad*。
  - **“热闹”词库**：*crowded round him, fired questions at him, treated him like a hero, cheered*。
  - **结构亮点**：用 *No sooner had he stepped off the boat than ...* 或 *make sb. do sth.*（the headmaster made him give a talk）让句子更地道。
</Callout>

<Callout type="tip" title="参考范文（自己写完再看）">
  When the boy finally returned, he was sick, cold and hungry. His parents, who had been worried sick for days, were waiting for him at the harbour. The moment they saw his pale, exhausted face, their anger melted into relief. His mother hugged him tightly while his father scolded him gently, half-angry and half-glad to have him home at last.

  Back at school, the boy was afraid he would be punished. To his surprise, the other boys gave him a hero's welcome, crowding round him and firing questions about France. The headmaster, however, found a cleverer punishment: instead of caning him, he made the boy stand up and give a talk to the whole school about his experiences abroad — a lesson far more uncomfortable than any detention. *(126 words)*
</Callout>

## 12. 书信写作 Letter writing

<Callout type="info" title="任务">
  *A letter contains three main parts: the Introduction, the Purpose and the Conclusion.* 每封信都包含 3 个主要部分：**引言 (Introduction)、目的 (Purpose)、结束语 (Conclusion)**。其中最重要的是**目的**——你必须说清楚“为什么写这封信”。

  *Exercise:* Write a short **Introduction** of a letter to a friend beginning: 用以下句子开头，给朋友写一段简短的引言：

  **‘You will never guess what ...’**
</Callout>

<Callout type="tip" title="写作脚手架">
  - 这是一句**制造悬念**的口语开场白，专门用来在信 / 聊天里抛出一件让人意想不到的事。
  - **‘You will never guess what ...’** 后面接一个 **what 引导的名词性从句**（陈述语序，不要写成疑问句倒装），把“最意外的那件事”留到后面慢慢揭晓。
  - 引言只需 1-3 句：先抛悬念，再用一句话承上启下，引出后面要详述的“目的”。
  - 写完检查：从句时态前后一致；悬念句之后别急着把全部细节倒完，给“目的段”留余地。
</Callout>

<Callout type="tip" title="参考范文（自己写完再看）">
  Dear Tom,

  You will never guess what happened to me last week! I set out one ordinary morning meaning to skip just a few dull lessons — and three days later I found myself in the south of France, with a policeman's hand on my shoulder. I simply have to tell you the whole extraordinary story from the very beginning. *(两到三句的引言，悬念已抛出，正文留待“目的”段展开)*
</Callout>

## 13. 拓展练习 Going further

<Callout type="tip" title="自学者建议">
  - **影子跟读 (shadowing)**：用第 1 节的三个 SayIt 按钮跟读 3-5 遍。重点感受那句 *the boat **had**, in the meantime, **travelled** to Calais* 里 had travelled 的“过去完成”节奏，以及结尾 *who **dream of evading** school* 的轻读收束。
  - **复述 (retell)**：合上课文，用 2 分钟把这趟“失控之旅”讲一遍——**起点**（逃学 → 搭车到多佛 → 上船过夜）→ **越走越远**（醒来到加来 → 卡车到巴黎城外 → 拦车却被带到佩皮尼昂）→ **结局**（警察抓住 → 当局送回 → 创下逃学纪录）。
  - **造句迁移**：用今天的 5 个高价值结构造你自己的句子——
    1. *be put to shame by ...*（被……比得相形见绌）—— *His diligence put us all to shame.*
    2. *while + 动名词*（在……期间）—— *While waiting for the train, I read the news.*
    3. *had + 过去分词*（过去的过去）—— *When I arrived, the film had already started.*
    4. *be picked up by ...*（被……带走 / 抓住）—— *The hikers were picked up by a rescue team.*
    5. *dream of + 动名词*（梦想做……）—— *She dreams of travelling around the world.*
  - **延伸思考**：本课用“连词”这把钥匙，把一连串**简单句**拼成了**并列句、复合句**——who 交代“哪一类孩子”，when / as 标出“什么时候”，and / but 串起“一步接一步又陡然转折”的行程。同样一段经历，若全用简单句会读得支离破碎；正是这些连词，让“越走越远、越陷越深”的层次感跃然纸上。下次你写英文叙事时，不妨先用简单句把事件列清，再用连词把它们“焊”成有主有次的长句——这正是从“写对”走向“写顺”的关键一步。
</Callout>
