## The Text 课文

We have just moved into a new house and I **have been working** hard all morning. I **have been trying** to **get my new room in order**. This **has not been** easy because I own over a thousand books. **To make matters worse**, the room is rather small, so I **have temporarily put** my books on the floor. At the moment, they **cover every inch of floor space** and I **actually** have to walk on them to get in or out of the room. A short while ago, my sister **helped me to carry** one of my old bookcases up the stairs. She went into my room and **got a big surprise** when she saw all those books on the floor. 'This is **the prettiest carpet I have ever seen**,' she said. She **gazed at** it for some time then added, 'You don't need bookcases at all. You can sit here **in your spare time** and read the carpet!'

## 课文注释 Notes on the text

<Callout type="info" title="4 个值得停下来嚼一嚼的点">
  1. **I have been working hard all morning.**（ll.1-2）—— “我辛辛苦苦地干了整整一上午。” 句中用了**现在完成进行时（have been doing）**，用来强调动作**一直在进行**，而且这个动作**对现在仍有影响 / 结果**（活儿还没干完，人已经累坏了）。对照一下：*I have worked*（强调“做完了”这个结果）vs *I have been working*（强调“一直在做”这个过程）。这正是本课的核心语法。
  2. **get ... in order**（l.2）—— “把……整理好、收拾停当”。**in order** = 井然有序、归置妥当；反义 **out of order**（出故障 / 失序）。同型：*Please get your papers in order before the meeting.*
  3. **To make matters worse**（ll.3-4）—— “更糟糕的是……”。这是一个**独立的插入语**（不定式短语作状语），放在句首引出“雪上加霜”的新情况，后面用逗号隔开。同型：*To make matters worse, it began to rain. / To be honest, I forgot.*
  4. **in your spare time**（ll.10-11）—— “在你空闲的时候”。**spare time** = 空闲时间、业余时间（= free time）；**spare** 作形容词意为“多余的、备用的”，如 *a spare key*（备用钥匙）、*spare parts*（备用零件）。
</Callout>

## 3. 关键句型 现在完成时 vs 现在完成进行时

<Callout type="tip" title="一分钟回顾">
  本课的语法主线是 **What has happened?（现在完成时）** 和 **What has been happening?（现在完成进行时）** 的对比。两者都把“过去”和“现在”连起来，但**关注点不同**：

  | 形式 | 结构 | 关注点 | 课文 / 教材例句 |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | **现在完成时** | have / has + 过去分词 | **结果**：动作完成了、留下了影响 | *We **have** just **moved** into a new house. / I **have read** “Oliver Twist” five times.* |
  | **现在完成进行时** | have / has + been + 现在分词 | **过程**：动作一直在持续（也许还在进行） | *I **have been working** hard all morning. / It **has been raining** since yesterday.* |

  ### ⚠️ 4 个本课最容易踩的雷区

  1. **“次数 / 完成”用完成时，“一直在做”用完成进行时**：*He **has rung** me up five times since 12 o'clock*（数得清的五次 → 完成时）vs *He **has been ringing** me up all morning*（强调“一上午没停” → 完成进行时）。
  2. **状态动词（know, own, be, have“拥有”…）一般不用进行时**：说“我有 1000 多本书”只能 *I **own** over a thousand books* / *I **have known** him for years*，不能说 ~~have been owning / have been knowing~~。
  3. **just / already / ever / never / for + 次数** 多配**完成时**；**all morning / since.../ for the last three weeks**（强调持续时段）多配**完成进行时**——但二者常可并存，看你想突出“结果”还是“过程”。
  4. **现在完成（进行）时不与明确的过去时间点连用**：可以说 *I have worked hard this morning*（this morning 还没结束），但 *yesterday / two days ago* 要用一般过去时。
</Callout>

## 4. 难点：形容词还是副词？Special difficulties

<Callout type="info" title="adjective vs adverb：别再张冠李戴">
  课文里 *I have been working **hard***、*She **actually** has to walk* 都在示范一件事：**修饰动词要用副词，修饰名词 / 作表语才用形容词**。本课难点专门操练几组最容易认错的副词。

  ### 一、大多数副词 = 形容词 + ly

  | 形容词（修饰名词 / 作表语） | 副词（修饰动词 / 形容词） | 例句 |
  |---|---|---|
  | a **beautiful** voice | sings **beautifully** | She has a beautiful voice. She sings beautifully. |
  | a **slow** worker | works **slowly** | He is a slow worker. He works slowly. |
  | a **hard** worker | works **hard** | He is a hard worker. He works hard. |
  | a **fast** driver | drives **fast** | He is a fast driver. He drives fast. |

  ⚠️ **hard** 和 **fast** 的副词**与形容词同形**（没有 ~~hardly / fastly~~ 这种“努力地 / 快地”的写法）。

  ### 二、最坑人的“双胞胎”副词

  | 词 | 含义 A | 含义 B（意思全变！） |
  |---|---|---|
  | **hard / hardly** | hard = 努力地、用力地（He worked very **hard**.） | hardly = 几乎不（He **hardly** ever does any work.） |
  | **late / lately** | late = 迟、晚（The train arrived very **late**.） | lately = 最近、近来（Have you travelled by train **lately**?） |
  | **high / highly** | high = 高高地（位置）（The plane flew very **high**.） | highly = 高度地（评价 / 程度）（He thinks very **highly** of me.） |
  | **near / nearly** | near = 靠近（He went **near**.） | nearly = 几乎、差点儿（He was **nearly** run over by a car.） |

  记住一句话：**hardly / lately / highly / nearly 不是“hard / late / high / near 的副词”，而是另一个意思的词！**
</Callout>

## 10. 摘要写作 Summary writing (≤ 75 词)

<Callout type="info" title="任务">

A short while ago his sister helped him to carry an old bookcase up the stairs. ## 11. 作文 Composition (连词改写)

<Callout type="info" title="任务">
  *Rewrite the following sentences using the joining words in parentheses.*（用括号中的连词改写以下句子。）

  1. I moved into a new room. Three workmen brought my things upstairs. *(When)*
  2. There were several cases of clothes. There were hundreds of books. *(not only ... but ... as well)*
  3. The pile was as high as the ceiling. I could not get into my room. *(Since)*
</Callout>

<Callout type="warning" title="改写要点">
  - **When** 引导时间状语从句，放句首时从句末尾加逗号；两个分句共用主语 “I”。
  - **not only ... but ... as well**：not only 接第一项，but + 第二项 + as well（= 不但……而且……）；注意 *as well* 放句末。
  - **Since** 在此表“因为、既然”（引出原因），从句在前、主句在后，中间加逗号。
</Callout>

<Callout type="tip" title="参考答案（自己写完再看）">
  1. **When** I moved into a new room, three workmen brought my things upstairs.
  2. There were **not only** several cases of clothes **but** hundreds of books **as well**.
  3. **Since** the pile was as high as the ceiling, I could not get into my room.
</Callout>

## 12. 书信写作 Letter writing — 怎样开头称呼

<Callout type="info" title="任务">
  *How would you begin letters to: your cousin Ted, your bank manager, your friend Mary, your grandfather?*（给下列人写信，你会如何开头？你的表弟特德、你的银行经理、你的朋友玛丽、你的祖父。）
</Callout>

<Callout type="tip" title="四种称呼的分寸">
  英文信件的称呼（**salutation**）讲究“亲疏有别”——越亲近越用名字，越正式越用头衔：

  | 收信人 | 开头称呼 | 结尾呼应 |
  |---|---|---|
  | **表弟 Ted**（亲戚、随意） | **Dear Ted,** | Love, / Yours, / Best wishes, |
  | **银行经理**（公事、正式） | **Dear Sir,**（不知名）或 **Dear Mr. + 姓,**（知名） | Yours faithfully,（配 Dear Sir）/ Yours sincerely,（配 Dear Mr. ...） |
  | **朋友 Mary**（熟人、亲切） | **Dear Mary,** | Yours, / Best wishes, / Love,（关系很好时） |
  | **祖父**（家人、亲昵） | **Dear Grandfather,** 或 **Dear Grandad,** | With love, / Your loving grandson / granddaughter, |

  ⚠️ 三条铁律：① 称呼后用**逗号**；② **Dear Sir** 配 **Yours faithfully**，**Dear Mr. 某某** 配 **Yours sincerely**（英式惯例，别配错）；③ 写给亲友才用 Love / With love，写给银行经理这类正式对象绝不能用。
</Callout>

## 13. 拓展练习 Going further

<Callout type="tip" title="自学者建议">
  - **影子跟读 (shadowing)**：用第 1 节的三个 SayIt 跟读 3-5 遍。重点体会 *I **have been working** hard all morning* 那种“没完没了忙活”的连贯语流，以及收尾妙语 *read the carpet!* 的俏皮上扬。
  - **复述 (retell)**：合上课文，用 1 分钟把这段“搬家趣事”讲一遍。**三段式**：起因（刚搬家、书有一千多本、房间太小）→ 经过（书只好暂时摊地上，多到要踩着进出 → 妹妹帮抬旧书橱上楼）→ 结局（妹妹推门一看，非但不嫌乱，反而打趣“这是最漂亮的地毯，空闲时可以坐着读”）。
  - **造句迁移**：用今天 5 个高价值结构造你自己的句子——
    1. *have been doing*（现在完成进行，强调持续）—— *I **have been studying** English for two hours.*
    2. *get ... in order*（把……收拾整齐）—— *Please **get** your desk **in order** before you leave.*
    3. *to make matters worse, ...*（更糟糕的是）—— *I missed the bus; **to make matters worse**, it started to rain.*
    4. *help sb (to) do sth.*（帮某人做某事）—— *My friend **helped me to move** the sofa.*
    5. *in one's spare time*（在空闲时）—— *She paints **in her spare time**.*
  - **延伸思考**：本课表面是一段轻松的搬家小插曲，骨子里是 **What has happened? vs What has been happening?** 的实战演练——通读一遍，把每个 *have/has + 过去分词* 和 *have/has been + V-ing* 圈出来问自己：这一处是看重**结果**（have moved / has not been easy），还是强调**过程**（have been working / have been trying）？再顺手把 *hard / actually / temporarily* 这些**副词**和它们容易混淆的“双胞胎”（hardly / late–lately / high–highly / near–nearly）一并复习掉。把“完成时看结果、完成进行时看过程”这把尺子练顺，你的英文叙事就会立刻清晰许多。
</Callout>
