## The Text 课文

When he **had killed** the guard, the prisoner of war quickly **dragged him into** the bushes. **Working rapidly in the darkness**, he soon **changed into** the dead man's clothes. Now, **dressed in a blue uniform** and **with a rifle over his shoulder**, the prisoner **marched boldly up and down** in front of the camp. He could hear shouting in the camp itself. **Lights were blazing** and men **were running here and there**: they **had just discovered** that a prisoner **had escaped**. At that moment, a large black car **with four officers inside it** stopped at the camp gates. The officers got out and the prisoner **stood to attention and saluted as they passed**. When they had gone, the driver of the car came towards him. The man obviously wanted to talk. He was rather **elderly with grey hair and clear blue eyes**. The prisoner felt sorry for him, but there was nothing else he could do. As the man came near, the prisoner **knocked him to the ground with a sharp blow**. Then, **jumping into the car**, he drove off **as quickly as he could**.

## 课文注释 Notes on the text

<Callout type="info" title="5 个值得停下来嚼一嚼的点">
  1. **When he had killed the guard, the prisoner of war quickly dragged him into the bushes.** (l.1) — 那个战俘杀死卫兵之后，迅速地把（卫兵的尸体）拖进灌木丛。这是一个含**时间状语从句**的复合句。**先“杀”后“拖”**——“杀”发生在“拖”之前，所以从句用**过去完成时 had killed**，主句用**一般过去时 dragged**。注意：根据英语习惯，**人称代词宾格 him** 放在主句里（指代从句中的 the guard）。同型：*When he had finished dinner, he washed the dishes.*
  2. **the prisoner of war** (l.1) — 战俘，常缩写为 **POW** (= Prisoner Of War)。介词 **of** 表所属/身份，整体是固定说法，注意不要漏掉 *of*。
  3. **up and down** (l.5) — 来回，前后。固定副词短语，描述“在一个范围内反复走动”。同型：*pace up and down*（来回踱步）/ *look me up and down*（上下打量我）。
  4. **here and there** (l.6) — 到处，四处。固定短语，强调“零零散散、各个方向”。课文 *men were running here and there* = 人们东奔西跑。同型：*Books were scattered here and there.*
  5. **stand to attention** (l.9) — 立正。军事口令式固定搭配，**attention 前用介词 to**（不是 *at*）。反义：*stand at ease*（稍息）。课文 *the prisoner stood to attention and saluted* = 战俘立正并敬礼——正是这套“标准动作”让他骗过了军官。
</Callout>

## 3. 关键句型 At / In / To / With + 非谓语动词

<Callout type="tip" title="一分钟回顾：本课的两条语法主线">
  本课 *Key structures* 复习 **At, In, To, With 等介词** 的用法（复习第 9、33、57 课），而课文又密集地使用了 **非谓语动词**（分词、动名词）。两条线交织，正是这篇“脱逃”读起来又快又紧的原因。

  ### 主线一：介词精确制导（into / in / with / at / to）

  | 介词 | 课文例句 | 用法 |
  |---|---|---|
  | **into** | dragged him **into** the bushes / changed **into** the clothes / jumping **into** the car | 表“进入”或“（状态/衣着）转变”，强调“动作的方向与结果” |
  | **in** | Working rapidly **in** the darkness / dressed **in** a blue uniform | 表“在……之中 / 穿着……” |
  | **with** | **with** a rifle over his shoulder / a car **with** four officers inside it / **with** a sharp blow | 表“带着、伴随、用（手段）” |
  | **at** | stopped **at** the camp gates / **At** that moment | 表“在某一点（地点/时刻）” |
  | **to** | knocked him **to** the ground / stood **to** attention | 表“朝向某终点” / 固定搭配 *stand to attention* |

  ### 主线二：非谓语动词织出“同时发生的动作层”

  | 形式 | 课文例句 | 作用 |
  |---|---|---|
  | **现在分词作状语** | ***Working** rapidly in the darkness, he ...* / *Then, **jumping** into the car, he drove off* | 主语主动发出的“伴随/方式”动作 |
  | **过去分词作状语** | *Now, **dressed** in a blue uniform, ...* | 主语“被穿上”——被动、状态 |
  | **过去进行时作背景** | *Lights **were blazing** and men **were running** ...* | 铺陈“正在进行”的紧张背景 |
  | **过去完成时表更早** | *had killed / had just discovered / had escaped* | “过去之前”已完成的动作 |

  ### ⚠️ 3 个最容易踩的雷区

  1. **stand to attention**：立正用 **to**，不是 *at*（*at ease* 才是“稍息”）。
  2. **现在分词 vs 过去分词**：主语“主动做” → 现在分词 (*working / jumping*)；主语“被……” → 过去分词 (*dressed*)。判断口诀：“他主动地工作 working；他被穿上军装 dressed”。
  3. **change into ＝ 换上（衣服）/ 变成**，介词必须是 **into**；*change in* 意思全然不同。
</Callout>

## 4. 难点：易混词 Words often confused

<Callout type="info" title="三组“长得像、意思差很远”的词">
  课文的 *Special Difficulties* 部分挑出三组**经常容易混淆的词**。它们形近或义近，一不留神就用错：

  | 组 | 词 | 辨析 | 例句 |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | **a** | **cloth** | 布料（不可数，做衣服的料子） | *I bought three and a half yards of cloth.* 我买了 3 码半布料。 |
  |  | **clothing** | 衣物（不可数，统称，较正式） | *You should give all this old clothing away.* 把这些旧衣物送人吧。 |
  |  | **clothes** | 衣服（永远复数，具体穿的） | *He soon changed into the dead man's clothes.* (ll.2-3) |
  | **b** | **salute** | （行军礼）敬礼，对上级/国旗 | *He stood to attention and saluted as they passed.* (l.9) |
  |  | **greet** | 迎接、问候（对亲友） | *He went to the station to greet his friend.* 他去车站接朋友。 |
  | **c** | **clear** | 清澈的 / 清楚的 / 畅通的 | *He was rather elderly with clear blue eyes.* (ll.10-11) · *The road is clear.* 路上没车。 |
  |  | **clean** | 干净的（无污渍） | *She keeps her house very clean.* 她把房子收拾得干干净净。 |

  ### ⚠️ 记忆要点

  1. **cloth → clothes → clothing** 三连：一块“布 (cloth)”做成具体的“衣服 (clothes，恒复数)”，统称“衣物 (clothing，不可数)”。*clothes* 没有单数、不能加 a。
  2. **salute（敬礼，对上级）vs greet（问候，对亲友）**：对军官行军礼用 salute；去车站“接”朋友用 greet。
  3. **clear（清澈/清楚/畅通）vs clean（干净）**：眼睛明亮、河水清澈、道理清楚、马路畅通都用 *clear*；只有“无污渍、洁净”才是 *clean*。
</Callout>

### 句子结构 Sentence structure

<Callout type="tip" title="连句练习（对照课文第 9 行核对）">
  把下面这四个短句**合并成课文里那一句**，体会 *and ... and ... as ...* 是怎样把“下车—立正—敬礼—经过”四个动作连成一气的：

  *The officers got out. The prisoner stood to attention. He saluted. They passed.*

  **参考答案（课文 l.9）**：*The officers got out and the prisoner stood to attention and saluted as they passed.*

  要点：前两个并列动作用 **and** 连接；“敬礼”与“经过”**同时发生**，用 **as**（= while）引导时间状语，比生硬地写四个短句自然得多。
</Callout>

## 10. 摘要写作 Summary writing (≤ 85 词)

<Callout type="info" title="任务">
  *In not more than 85 words* describe **what the prisoner did**. 用不超过 85 个单词，按教材给的 11 个要点，分**两段**复述这场脱逃：

  **要点 (points)**：1 Prisoner of war killed guard. 2 Dragged into bushes. 3 Changed into clothes. 4 Rifle over shoulder. 5 Marched — camp. 6 Four officers — car. 7 Attention — saluted. 8 Driver — towards him. 9 Knocked out. 10 Jumped into car. 11 Drove away.

  **可用连接词**：*After having ... and ... Then he ... A short time afterwards ... so ... and ... When they had gone ... and after ...*（第一段）；*As soon as he ... and ... With ... when ... and ... When the driver ... Then, jumping ...*（第二段）。
</Callout>

<Callout type="tip" title="参考范文（自己写完再看）">
  **Paragraph 1.** After having killed the guard, the prisoner of war dragged him into the bushes and changed into his clothes. Then he picked up the rifle and marched in front of the camp.

  **Paragraph 2.** A short time afterwards a car with four officers stopped, so the prisoner stood to attention and saluted. When they had gone, the driver came towards him. The prisoner knocked him out, jumped into the car and drove away. *(64 words)*
</Callout>

## 11. 作文 Composition (约 150 词)

<Callout type="info" title="任务">
  Write **two paragraphs in about 150 words** using the ideas given below:

  1. **Dark night** — a prisoner of war who had escaped was dressed as a guard — he was still in the camp — took part in the search for the 'missing' prisoner.
  2. He went out in a lorry with the other guards — into the countryside — the guards went into the forest — the prisoner escaped.
</Callout>

<Callout type="warning" title="提示骨架（每点都要扩成完整句）">
  - **第一段（伪装混入搜捕）**：夜黑 → 越狱者已换上卫兵装 → 仍在营内 → 故意加入搜捕“失踪俘虏”的队伍，以洗清自己。
  - **第二段（借机脱身）**：他和其他卫兵坐卡车出营 → 开进乡间 → 卫兵下车钻进森林搜寻 → 战俘趁机溜走。
</Callout>

<Callout type="tip" title="写作脚手架">
  - **时态主线**：一般过去时叙事；**过去完成时**表更早（*had escaped / had broken out*）；**过去进行时**作背景（*the guards were searching / the lorry was bumping along*）。
  - **“伪装/混入”动词库**：*disguised himself as, slipped in among, joined the search party, pretended to look for, kept his head down*。
  - **“行进/出营”动词库**：*climbed into the lorry, rumbled out of the gates, drove into the countryside, jolted along the track*。
  - **“脱身”动词库**：*seized his chance, slipped away, melted into the trees, doubled back, made off under cover of darkness*。
</Callout>

<Callout type="tip" title="参考范文（自己写完再看）">
  It was a dark, moonless night. The prisoner of war who had escaped was now dressed as a guard, and to everyone's surprise he was still inside the camp. Instead of hiding, he boldly took part in the search for the 'missing' prisoner, shouting orders and pretending to hunt through the huts with the others.

  Before long he climbed into a lorry with a party of guards and rumbled out of the gates into the countryside. When the lorry stopped, the guards jumped down and spread out into the forest, searching every bush and ditch for the man who was standing among them. As soon as their backs were turned, the prisoner quietly slipped away in the opposite direction, melted into the trees and disappeared into the darkness. By the time the guards realised that one of their number had gone, he was already miles away. *(146 words)*
</Callout>

## 12. 书信写作 Letter writing：结束语 The Conclusion

<Callout type="info" title="教材要点">
  *The last paragraph of a letter should take the form of a polite wish.* 书信的**最后一段应是有礼貌的问候/祝愿**。熟记以下两句“收尾”套语：

  - *Please give my love / regards to ...*（替我向……问好）—— **love** 用于亲近的人，**regards** 较为正式。
  - *I hope you feel better soon.*（祝你早日康复。）
</Callout>

<Callout type="info" title="练习 Exercise">
  Write **five opening sentences** which could be used in letters to friends or relations. 写出 5 句给朋友或亲属的信的**开头语**。
</Callout>

<Callout type="tip" title="参考答案（开头语 5 句）">
  1. *Thank you very much for your letter, which arrived this morning.*（非常感谢今早收到你的来信。）
  2. *I'm sorry I haven't written for so long, but I've been terribly busy.*（抱歉这么久没写信，实在太忙了。）
  3. *It was lovely to hear from you again after all this time.*（隔了这么久又收到你的消息，真高兴。）
  4. *I hope this letter finds you and all the family well.*（希望你和全家都安好。）
  5. *I've been meaning to write to you for ages, and I'm finally getting round to it.*（我早就想给你写信，现在总算动笔了。）
</Callout>

## 13. 拓展练习 Going further

<Callout type="tip" title="自学者建议">
  - **影子跟读 (shadowing)**：用第 1 节的三个 SayIt 按钮各跟读 3–5 遍。重点体会本课“动作连珠”的节奏感——*dragged ... into / changed into / jumping into* 这一串 **into** 把镜头一推到底。
  - **复述 (retell)**：合上课文，用 2 分钟把脱逃过程讲一遍。**叙事三幕**：伪装（杀卫兵 → 拖尸 → 换装 → 扛枪踱步）→ 应对（黑车停下 → 立正敬礼 → 骗过军官）→ 脱身（司机搭话 → 一拳打倒 → 跳车开走）。
  - **造句迁移**：用今天的 5 个高价值结构造你自己的句子——
    1. *When he had + 过去分词, 主句一般过去*（先后两动作）—— *When he had finished work, he went home.*
    2. *Working / Dressed + ..., 主句*（现在/过去分词作状语）—— *Dressed in his best suit, he walked into the office.*
    3. *with + 名词 + 介词短语*（伴随状语）—— *He stood there with his hands in his pockets.*
    4. *a + 名词 + with ... inside it*（后置修饰）—— *a box with a present inside it*
    5. *Then, jumping into ..., 主句 as quickly as 主语 could*（分词收尾 + 比较）—— *Then, grabbing his bag, he ran off as fast as he could.*
  - **延伸思考**：本课是一篇“**镜头感极强的微型惊悚片**”——作者几乎不用形容词渲染情绪，而是靠**精确的动词 + 介词 + 非谓语动词**把动作一帧一帧拍出来。下次写英文叙事时记住：**让动作说话**比堆砌形容词更有力。另一个值得回味的点：*The prisoner felt sorry for him, but there was nothing else he could do* —— 一句话给冷峻的逃亡添了一抹人性的犹豫，正是这种“同情与不得已”的张力，让这个故事比单纯的“打斗逃跑”更耐读。
</Callout>
