  **What was the joke? 这则新闻的“笑点”到底在哪里？** 带着这个问题读课文，并重点感受全篇是怎样用 **现在完成时（has won / has begun）** 与 **现在完成进行时（has been working / has been expecting）** 织出“持续到现在”的真实感——正是这种“以假乱真”的时态，把一个愚人节恶作剧讲得有鼻子有眼。
</Callout>

## The Text 课文

'To end our special news bulletin,' said the voice of the television announcer, 'we're going over to the macaroni fields of Calabria. **Macaroni has been grown** in this area for over six hundred years. Two of the leading growers, Giuseppe Moldova and Riccardo Brabante, tell me that they **have been expecting** a splendid crop this year and harvesting earlier than usual. Here you can see two workers who, **between them**, **have just finished** cutting three cartloads of golden brown macaroni stalks. The whole village **has been working** day and night gathering and threshing this year's crop before the September rains. On the right, you can see Mrs. Brabante herself. She **has been helping** her husband for thirty years. Mrs. Brabante is talking to the manager of the local factory where the crop **is processed**. This last scene shows you what will happen at the end of the harvest: the famous Calabrian macaroni-eating competition! Signor Fratelli, the present champion, **has won** it every year since 1991. And that ends our special bulletin for today, Thursday, April 1st. We're now going back to the studio.'

## 课文注释 Notes on the text

<Callout type="info" title="6 个值得停下来嚼一嚼的点">
  1. **April Fools' Day**（标题）— 愚人节，4 月 1 日。按西方习俗，这一天人们以善意地愚弄别人取乐。本课正是一则著名的“通心粉长在树上”恶作剧（原型是 1957 年 BBC 的电视玩笑）。全文用一本正经的新闻播报口吻，把“假”讲得像“真”，结尾才用日期 *Thursday, April 1st* 抖出包袱。
  2. **Macaroni has been grown ... for over six hundred years** (l.3) — **现在完成时的被动语态**（has been + 过去分词）。通心粉是被人“种”的，所以用被动；*for + 时间段* 强调“从过去一直持续到现在”。同型：*Tea has been drunk in China for thousands of years.*
  3. **have been expecting / has been working / has been helping** (ll.5, 8-9, 11) — **现在完成进行时**（have/has been + V-ing），强调动作“持续不断或反复进行”且延续到此刻。配 *day and night*、*for thirty years* 这类“时间跨度”词最自然。
  4. **between them** (l.7) — 字面“在他们俩之间”，这里是注释里说的“**由于他们俩共同协力的结果**”。**between** 用于“两者”；若是三者以上要用 *among*（见难点部分）。
  5. **the September rains** (l.9) — 9 月雨季。**rains 用复数**指“雨季 / 连绵的降雨”，与单数 *rain*（雨）语感不同。*before the September rains* = 赶在 9 月雨季到来之前。
  6. **has won it every year since 1991** (l.13) — **现在完成时 + since + 过去时间点**，表示“自 1991 年起（年年）一直保持”。注意：*since* 接“时间点 / 从句”，*for* 接“时间段”——这正是本课练习反复考的对比。
</Callout>

## 3. 关键句型 现在完成时 vs 现在完成进行时

<Callout type="tip" title="一分钟回顾：What has happened? What has been happening?">
  本课关键句型（复习第 4、28、52 课）就是这一对：**现在完成时**回答“做完了什么（结果）”，**现在完成进行时**回答“一直在做什么（过程 / 持续）”。把课文的动词圈出来，分布一目了然：

  | 形式 | 课文例句 | 用途 |
  |---|---|---|
  | **现在完成时** have/has + 过去分词 | *Signor Fratelli **has won** it ... / harvesting **has begun** ... / they **have just finished** cutting* | 强调动作的**完成、结果**；常配 since / already / yet / just |
  | **现在完成进行时** have/has been + V-ing | *they **have been expecting** ... / the whole village **has been working** ... / she **has been helping** ...* | 强调动作的**持续 / 反复进行**；常配 for + 时间段、how long |
  | **现在完成时被动** has been + 过去分词 | *Macaroni **has been grown** in this area ...* | “被……”且延续到现在（无“完成进行被动”，故用此式） |

  ### ⚠️ 4 个本课最容易踩的雷区

  1. **for + 时间段 / since + 时间点**：*for thirty years*、*for over six hundred years*（时间段用 for）；*since 1991*、*since she was a young woman*（时间点 / 从句用 since）。
  2. **完成 vs 完成进行**：*has won*（每年夺冠，重在“结果”）≠ *has been winning*（重在“一直在赢的过程”）。本课讲“战绩”，所以用 *has won*。
  3. **不及物动词没有被动**：*work* 在 *has been working* 里是不及物的——不能说 *has been worked*。只有及物动词（grow、process）才有被动。
  4. **than usual ≠ than usually**：*earlier than usual*（比平时早）里 **usual 是形容词**，不能写成 usually。
</Callout>

## 4. 难点：经常容易混淆的词 Words often confused

<Callout type="info" title="三组本课必须分清的词">
  课文的 *Special Difficulties* 部分挑了三组形近 / 义近词。记住它们各自的“词性”和“适用范围”，填空就不会错：

  | 组别 | 区别 | 例句（教材原例） |
  |---|---|---|
  | **usual / usually** | usual 是**形容词**（than usual、as usual）；usually 是**副词**，修饰动词 | *Harvesting has begun earlier than **usual**.* (l.6) / *He **usually** gets up at six o'clock.* |
  | **between / among** | between 用于**两者**之间；among 用于**三者或三者以上**之中 | *two workers who, **between** them, have just finished ...* (ll.6-8) / *The Prime Minister was **among** those present.* |
  | **manager / director / headmaster** | manager 经理（公司、商店、工厂）；director 主管、董事（公司、机构）；headmaster 校长（学校） | *Mrs. Brabante is talking to the **manager** of the local factory.* (ll.10-11) / *Mr. Jones is the **director** of several companies.* / *Who is the **headmaster** of this school?* |

  ### ⚠️ 两个小提醒

  1. **as usual**（像往常一样）、**than usual**（比平时）都是固定搭配，里面永远是 usual，不是 usually。

</Callout>

## 11. 作文 Composition (约 150 词)

<Callout type="info" title="任务">
  Write an imaginary account of a macaroni-eating competition. Write **two paragraphs in about 150 words** using the ideas given below.

  按下面思路写一个想象中的“吃通心粉大赛”，分两段，约 150 词。
</Callout>

<Callout type="warning" title="提示骨架">
  1. The appearance of the six competitors — all very fat — their past achievements — huge quantity of macaroni prepared, weighed and served.
  2. Quantity, not speed, was the important thing — competitors eating for three hours — only one man left — he asked for more!
</Callout>

<Callout type="tip" title="写作脚手架">
  - **时态主线**：一般过去时叙事（shuffled / began / ate）；过去完成表更早（had prepared / had eaten）；过去进行作背景（were watching）。
  - **“块头”词库**：*enormously fat, round, well-padded, a champion eater, a long list of past triumphs*。
  - **“吃”动词库**：*ate steadily, worked through plate after plate, swallowed, polished off, showed no sign of slowing down*。
  - **“结局”亮点**：*to the astonishment of the crowd, he wiped his mouth, smiled broadly, and calmly asked for more.*
</Callout>

<Callout type="tip" title="参考范文（自己写完再看）">
  The six competitors shuffled to the table, and not one of them was thin: each was enormously fat, and each had a long list of past triumphs. In front of them the cooks had prepared a huge quantity of macaroni, which was carefully weighed and then served on great steaming plates. At a signal, the contest began.

  Here, quantity — not speed — was what mattered, so the men ate slowly and steadily, plate after plate, hour after hour. After three full hours, five of them had given up, groaning and clutching their stomachs. Only one man was left at the table. He had eaten more macaroni than anyone could count, yet he did not look in the least bit tired. To the astonishment of the crowd, he wiped his mouth, smiled broadly, and calmly asked the waiter for more! *(137 words)*
</Callout>

## 13. 拓展练习 Going further

<Callout type="tip" title="自学者建议">
  - **影子跟读 (shadowing)**：用第 1 节的三个 SayIt 按钮跟读 3-5 遍，模仿新闻播音那种“一本正经、娓娓道来”的腔调——正是这种郑重其事，才让愚人节玩笑更可笑。
  - **复述 (retell)**：合上课文，用 1 分钟把这则“假新闻”讲一遍。**三步**：背景（卡拉布里亚“种”了 600 年通心粉）→ 经过（全村抢收、加工、吃通心粉大赛、冠军年年夺魁）→ 抖包袱（日期是 4 月 1 日，原来全是玩笑）。
  - **造句迁移**：用本课 5 个核心结构造你自己的句子——
    1. *S + has/have been + V-ed + for + 时间段*（被动持续）—— *English has been taught here for fifty years.*
    2. *S + has/have been + V-ing + for/since ...*（完成进行）—— *I have been learning the piano since I was six.*
    3. *S + has/have won/done ... since + 时间点*（完成 + since）—— *She has lived here since 2018.*
    4. *between the two / among the three*（两者 vs 三者以上）—— *Share it between the two of you.*
    5. *earlier / warmer than usual*（than usual 固定搭配）—— *He arrived earlier than usual today.*
  - **延伸思考**：这是一篇“用真时态讲假故事”的范文——**现在完成时**给人“结果摆在眼前”的可信感，**现在完成进行时**给人“一直在发生”的现场感。当这两种“延续到现在”的时态被用来描述一件根本不存在的事，假新闻就显得无比逼真。下次你读到任何“言之凿凿”的报道，不妨也留个心眼：**语气越笃定，越要看清它落款的那个日期。**
</Callout>
