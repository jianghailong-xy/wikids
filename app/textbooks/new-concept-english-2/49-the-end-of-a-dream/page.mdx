## The Text 课文

**Tired of sleeping on the floor**, a young man in Teheran **saved up** for years to buy a real bed. **For the first time in his life**, he became **the proud owner of** a bed **which had springs and a mattress**. **Because** the weather was very hot, he carried the bed **on to the roof** of his house. He slept very well for the first two nights, but on the third night, a storm **blew up**. **A gust of wind swept the bed off the roof and sent it crashing into the courtyard below**. The young man **did not wake up until** the bed had struck the ground. **Although** the bed was **smashed to pieces**, the man was **miraculously unhurt**. **When** he woke up, he was still on the mattress. **Glancing at** the bits of wood and metal **that lay around him**, the man sadly picked up the mattress and carried it into his house. **After** he had put it on the floor, he **promptly** went to sleep again.

> 配图说明：*miraculously unhurt*（奇迹般地毫发无伤）——床摔得粉碎，人却还安稳地躺在床垫上。

## 课文注释 Notes on the text

<Callout type="info" title="5 个值得停下来嚼一嚼的点">
  1. **Tired of sleeping on the floor** (l.1) — \"对睡地板感到厌倦\"。**be tired of (doing) sth.** = 对……感到厌倦/厌烦。这里省略了 *being*（完整是 *Being tired of ...*），现在分词短语作**原因状语**，相当于 *Because he was tired of sleeping on the floor*。⚠️ 区分 **tired of**（厌倦）与 **tired from / with**（因……而疲劳）。
  2. **save up** (l.2) — \"积攒（钱）\"。**up** 在这里表\"积累、攒够\"的语气，比单用 *save* 更强调\"为某目标一点点攒\"。同型：*save up for a car / save up enough money*。
  3. **blow up** (l.6) — 这里指 (暴风雨) \"骤起、刮起、加剧\"。*a storm blew up* = 风暴突然刮了起来。⚠️ **blow up** 还有\"爆炸、把……炸毁、给（轮胎）打气、发脾气\"等义，需看语境。
  4. **A gust of wind swept the bed off the roof and sent it crashing into the courtyard below.** (ll.6-7) — \"一阵大风把床从屋顶上刮了下来，把它摔进了下面的院子里。\" **sweep ... off the roof** = 把……从屋顶刮下来；**send sth. + 现在分词（crashing）** = 使某物（哗啦一声地）……，现在分词作**宾语补足语**。同型：*The blow sent him reeling. / The news sent her crying.* 另外 **below** 在此作副词后置修饰 *courtyard*（下面的院子）。
  5. **The young man did not wake up until the bed had struck the ground.** (ll.7-8) — \"直到床撞到地上，年轻人才醒过来。\" **not ... until ...** = 直到……才……。主句一般过去否定（*did not wake up*），从句用**过去完成**（*had struck*）标出\"更早发生\"的动作。
</Callout>

## 3. 关键句型 Word order in complex statements 复合句的语序与连词

<Callout type="tip" title="一分钟回顾">
  本课的语法重点（Key structures）是**复合句的语序**——把几个简单句用**连词**接成一个有层次的复合句。常用连词：*when, until, after, as soon as, while, before, because, as, since, to, in order to, although, who, which, that*。

  | 连接方式 | 例句（课文/教材原例） | 要点 |
  |---|---|---|
  | **because** 原因 | *Because the weather was very hot, he carried the bed on to the roof.* | 后接**句子**；从句在前要加逗号 |
  | **until / not...until** 时间 | *He did not wake up **until** the bed had struck the ground.* | \"直到……才……\"；主否定 + 从句 |
  | **although** 让步 | ***Although** the bed was smashed to pieces, the man was unhurt.* | \"虽然……\"；**不能**再配 but |
  | **which** 定语 | *a bed **which** had springs and a mattress* | 引导定语从句，which 已作主语，不再加 it |
  | **分词作状语** | ***Glancing** at the bits ..., the man picked up the mattress.* / ***Tired** of sleeping ..., a young man saved up.* | 主动用 V-ing，被动/状态用 V-ed |
  | **so...that** 结果 | *I was **so** tired **that** I went to sleep immediately.* | \"如此……以致……\" |

  ### ⚠️ 4 个中国学生最容易踩的雷区

  1. **Although 与 but 不能同时出现**：*Although he ran fast, **but** he failed.* ✗ —— 中文\"虽然……但是……\"，英文只能留一个。
  2. **Because（连词）vs Because of（介词）**：Because + **句子**；Because of + **名词/动名词**。*Because of the hot weather, ...* ✓ / *Because the weather was hot, ...* ✓。
  3. **定语从句的 which/that 已作主语就别再加 it**：*a bed which **it** had springs* ✗。
  4. **从句置于句首，末尾必须加逗号**：*When he woke up**,** he was still on the mattress.*
</Callout>

<Callout type="tip" title="句子结构 Sentence structure（被动 → 主动）">
  教材最后一题：把下面这句**改写**，再对照课文第 6-7 行核对——

  *The bed was swept off the roof by a gust of wind.*（被动）

  → **A gust of wind swept the bed off the roof (and sent it crashing into the courtyard below).**（主动）

  把 by 后面的 *a gust of wind* 提到句首作主语，谓语由 *was swept* 改为主动的 *swept*，宾语 *the bed* 跟在动词后即可。
</Callout>

## 10. 摘要写作 Summary writing (≤ 80 词)

<Callout type="info" title="任务">
  *Answer these questions in not more than 80 words.* 把下面 5 个问题的答案串成一个段落，不超过 80 词：

  1. What did a young man in Teheran buy for the first time in his life?
  2. Was the weather hot or cold? Did he sleep on the roof of his house or not? *(Because)*
  3. Was the bed swept off the roof during a storm three nights later or not?
  4. Was the man unhurt, or was he seriously injured? Was he still on his mattress or not? *(not only ... but)*
  5. Was the bed in pieces or not? Did he carry the mattress indoors or not? Where did he put it? Did he go back to sleep or did he stay awake? *(As ... and)*
</Callout>

<Callout type="tip" title="参考范文（自己写完再看）">
  A young man in Teheran bought a real bed for the first time in his life. Because the weather was hot, he slept on the roof of his house. Three nights later a storm swept the bed off the roof. The man was not only unhurt but still on his mattress. As the bed was in pieces, he carried the mattress indoors, put it on the floor and went back to sleep. *(72 words)*
</Callout>

## 11. 作文 Composition

<Callout type="info" title="任务">
  Write two or three sentences using the ideas given below. 用下列词组写出 2 至 3 个句子：

  *The man gathered the pieces next morning — repaired the bed — put it on the roof — tied it down — enjoyed many comfortable nights' sleep.*
</Callout>

<Callout type="warning" title="提示骨架">
  - **第二天早上**：gather up the pieces（把碎片捡起来）→ 用过去时叙事 *gathered / repaired*。
  - **修好后**：put it back on the roof，但这次 **tied it down**（拴牢、固定住）以防再被刮跑。
  - **结果**：from then on，enjoyed many comfortable nights' sleep（从此睡了许多安稳觉）。
  - 注意 **nights'** 的所有格撇号位置（复数 nights + 撇号）。
</Callout>

<Callout type="tip" title="参考范文（自己写完再看）">
  The next morning the man gathered up the broken pieces and patiently repaired the bed. Once it was as good as new, he carried it back up on to the roof, but this time he tied it down firmly so that no gust of wind could move it. From then on he enjoyed many comfortable nights' sleep under the stars.
</Callout>

## 12. 书信写作 Letter writing

<Callout type="info" title="任务">
  Most letters begin with the word *Dear* followed by a name, placed against the **left-hand margin**. 大多数书信都以 *Dear* 开头、后接姓名，且要在信纸**左侧顶格**。

  **Exercise（练习）**：按下面的格式，写下你自己的**地址、日期**和给一位朋友的**信件开头**。
</Callout>

<Callout type="tip" title="格式范例 + 脚手架">
  教材示范的英式书信抬头（**寄信人地址靠右上、日期在地址下方；称呼 Dear + 名字靠左顶格**）：

  ```
                                          24 Clayton Ave.,
                                          St. Albans.
                                          Herts.
                                          England.
                                          17th June, 19—

  Dear Tom,
  ```

  - **地址**：由小到大，逐行缩进，每行末尾标点（逗号或句号）。
  - **日期**：英式写法 *17th June, 19—*（日 + 月 + 年）。
  - **称呼**：*Dear + 朋友名字,*（顶格，后加逗号），随后另起一段写正文。
</Callout>

## 13. 拓展练习 Going further

<Callout type="tip" title="自学者建议">
  - **影子跟读 (shadowing)**：用第 1 节的三个 SayIt 按钮跟读 3-5 遍。重点感受 *sent it **crashing** into the courtyard below* 这种\"动词 + 现在分词\"的连贯节奏。
  - **复述 (retell)**：合上课文，用 1-2 分钟把故事讲一遍。**叙事三段**：起因（厌倦睡地板 → 攒钱买床 → 天热搬上屋顶）→ 经过（第三夜风暴 → 大风刮床 → 摔进院子）→ 结局（人奇迹般没伤 → 捡起床垫 → 很快又睡着）。
  - **造句迁移**：用今天的 5 个连词/结构造你自己的句子——
    1. *Because + 从句, 主句*（原因状语前置）—— *Because it was raining, we stayed at home.*
    2. *主句 + not ... until + 从句*（直到……才……）—— *I did not realize my mistake until he told me.*
    3. *Although + 从句, 主句*（让步，不配 but）—— *Although he was tired, he kept working.*
    4. *send sth./sb. + V-ing*（使……）—— *The explosion sent everyone running.*
    5. *分词短语作状语*（V-ing / V-ed 开头）—— *Glancing at his watch, he hurried off.*
  - **延伸思考**：本课的语法主线是\"**把简单句焊成复合句**\"。同一串事实，用不同连词连接，强调的重点就不同：用 *because* 突出因果，用 *although* 突出反差，用 *when / after / until* 标定时间先后。下次写英文叙事时，先把零碎的简单句列出来，再问自己\"它们之间是**因果、转折，还是先后**？\"——选对连词，句子就有了层次。另一个值得回味的点：标题 *The end of a dream* 既指\"床（多年攒钱的梦）被毁\"，也暗合\"一夜好梦被风暴打断\"——好标题往往就藏着这样的一语双关。
</Callout>
