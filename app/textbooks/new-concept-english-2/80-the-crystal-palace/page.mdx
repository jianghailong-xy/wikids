  1851 年，伦敦海德公园里立起了一座**前所未见的建筑**——它不是砖砌的，也不是石造的，而是**用铁和玻璃搭成的**，阳光照进来，整座大楼晶莹剔透，于是人们叫它 **the Crystal Palace（水晶宫）**。它是为当年的 **the Great Exhibition（世界博览会）** 而建：来自世界各地的商品和机器在这里展出，最奇妙的展品是内史密斯的蒸汽锤。当时旅行远不如今天方便，可汽船还是把成千上万的人从欧洲大陆运过了英吉利海峡……
  **How many people visited the Great Exhibition of 1851? 1851 年的世界博览会有多少人参观？** 带着这个问题读课文，重点留意全篇是怎样用 **形容词与副词的比较级和最高级**（the most extraordinary / one of the biggest ... of all time / not as easy as ... / one of the most famous）一层层把“水晶宫到底有多了不起”讲清楚的。
</Callout>

## The Text 课文

Perhaps **the most extraordinary building** of the nineteenth century was the Crystal Palace, **which was built in Hyde Park** for the Great Exhibition of 1851. The Crystal Palace **was different from all other buildings** in the world, for **it was made of iron and glass**. It was **one of the biggest buildings of all time** and a lot of people from many countries came to see it. **A great many goods** were sent to the exhibition from various parts of the world. There was also **a great deal of machinery on display**. The most wonderful piece of machinery **on show** was Nasmyth's steam hammer. Though in those days, travelling was **not as easy as** it is today, steam boats carried thousands of visitors across the Channel from Europe. **On arriving in England**, they were taken to the Crystal Palace by train. There were **six million visitors in all**, and the profits from the exhibition were used to build museums and colleges. Later, the Crystal Palace was moved to South London. It remained **one of the most famous buildings** in the world until **it was burnt down** in 1936.

## 课文注释 Notes on the text

<Callout type="info" title="7 个值得停下来嚼一嚼的点">
  1. **which was built in Hyde Park ...** (l.2) — 这是一个**非限定性定语从句**，前面用逗号隔开，补充说明 *the Crystal Palace*（在哪里、为什么而建）。去掉它，主句 *Perhaps the most extraordinary building ... was the Crystal Palace* 依然成立。同型：*London, which lies on the Thames, is an old city.*
  2. **the Great Exhibition** (l.2-3) — 特指 1851 年在伦敦举办的“**世界博览会**”，是历史上第一届世博会，水晶宫正是为它而建。专有名词，首字母大写。
  3. **it was made of iron and glass** (l.4) — “它是用铁和玻璃建成的”。**be made of + 原材料**：成品仍看得出原料、**没有发生化学变化**（铁还是铁，玻璃还是玻璃）；若原料“面目全非”则用 **be made from**（如 *Paper is made from wood.* 纸由木头制成）。
  4. **of all time** (l.5) — “有史以来的、空前的”，常跟在**最高级**后面把范围拉到“整段历史”。课文 *one of the biggest buildings of all time* = 有史以来最大的建筑之一。同型：*the greatest player of all time*。
  5. **on display = on show** (l.8 / l.9) — 两个短语意思相同，都表示“**展出、陈列**”。课文先用 *machinery on display*，紧接着换说 *machinery on show*，是写作里避免重复的小技巧。
  6. **On arriving in England** (l.10-11) — **on + 动名词(V-ing)** = “一……就……”，相当于 *as soon as they arrived in England*。同型：*On hearing the news, she burst into tears.*
  7. **in all** (l.11) — “**总共、合计**”，用于在句末或句中对数目作总结。课文 *six million visitors in all* = 参观者总共 600 万。同型：*There were twenty of us in all.*
</Callout>

## 3. 关键句型 The best and the worst 比较级与最高级

<Callout type="tip" title="一分钟回顾">
  本课的关键句型是**形容词与副词的比较级和最高级**（复习第 8、32、56 课）。课文像一条“最高级项链”，一颗颗把水晶宫的“最”串起来：*the most extraordinary building / one of the biggest buildings of all time / one of the most famous buildings*，中间还夹了一个同级比较 *not as easy as it is today*。

  | 形式 | 规则 | 课文 / 例句 |
  |---|---|---|
  | **短词加 -er / -est** | 单音节 + 部分双音节：big → bigger → **biggest**；easy → easier → easiest | *one of the **biggest** buildings of all time* |
  | **长词用 more / most** | 三音节及以上：extraordinary、famous、wonderful | *the **most** extraordinary building* / *the **most** famous buildings* |
  | **不规则变化** | good → better → best；bad → worse → worst；many/much → more → most | *the **best** and the **worst*** |
  | **同级比较 as ... as** | 中间夹**原级**（形容词 / 副词原形）；否定可用 not so ... as | *travelling was **not as easy as** it is today* |
  | **one of the + 最高级 + 复数名词** | “最……的……之一”，名词必须用复数 | *one of the biggest **buildings** / one of the most famous **buildings*** |
  | **最高级的范围** | of all time（有史以来）/ in the world（全世界）来圈定比较范围 | *the biggest ... **of all time** / the most famous ... **in the world*** |

  ### ⚠️ 4 个本课最容易踩的雷区

  1. **不能“双重比较 / 双重最高级”**：✗ *more bigger* / ✗ *most biggest* / ✗ *most extraordinariest*。要么加词尾，要么用 more/most，二选一。
  2. **one of the 最高级后名词用复数**：*one of the biggest **buildings*** ✓，不是 *one of the biggest building* ✗。
  3. **as ... as 之间放原级**：*not as **easy** as* ✓，不是 *not as easier as* ✗。
  4. **biggest 的拼写**：big 重读闭音节、末尾辅音字母**双写**再加 -est → bigg**est**（同理 hot → hottest）。
</Callout>

## 4. 难点：带 on 的短语 Phrases with on

<Callout type="info" title="一串必须背下来的 on 短语（对比第 43 课）">
  本课难点是**带 on 的固定短语**。课文里出现了 *on display / on show*，教材借此把一整串 on 短语拉出来一起记。它们大多**不能按字面直译**，要当成整体记忆：

  | 短语 | 意思 | 例句 |
  |---|---|---|
  | **on display / on show** | 展出、陈列 | *There was a great deal of machinery **on display**.* |
  | **on business** | 出差、办公事 | *He has gone to Frankfurt **on business**.* |
  | **on foot** | 步行 | *I didn't catch the bus. I came here **on foot**.* |
  | **on duty** | 值班、当值 | *I asked to see the officer **on duty**.* |
  | **on the whole** | 总起来说、大体上 | ***On the whole**, it has been a very successful year.* |
  | **on fire** | 着火 | *That building must be **on fire**.* |
  | **on purpose** | 故意地 | *He did it **on purpose**.* |
  | **on the average** | 平均说来 | ***On the average**, I make six telephone calls a day.* |
  | **on any account** | （否定句中）无论如何（不） | *You mustn't **on any account** sign it before you read it.* |
  | **on second thoughts** | 重新考虑后、转念一想 | *...but **on second thoughts**, you should stay at home.* |

  ### ⚠️ 别混淆
  - **on the whole**（大体上）≠ **as a whole**（作为一个整体）；
  - **on purpose**（故意）↔ **by accident / by chance**（偶然）是一对反义；
  - **on any account** 只用于**否定句**表“无论如何都不”，肯定时用 *on every account*（在各方面）。
</Callout>

## 10. 摘要写作 Summary writing (≤ 85 词)

<Callout type="info" title="任务">
  *Give an account of the history of the Crystal Palace in not more than 85 words.* 用不超过 **85 个单词**讲述水晶宫的历史，**分两段**，按下面教材给的 12 个要点（POINTS）和提示连词（CONNECTIONS）来串：

  **第一段（建造与展品）**：The Crystal Palace → built — exhibition（用 *which*）→ different — others（用 *because*）→ iron and glass（用 *Made of ... it*）→ goods — world → machinery（用 *as well as / not only ... but ... as well*）。

  **第二段（参观、用途与结局）**：visitors — boat, train（用 *So many ... that it was possible ...*；*This attracted ... who*）→ colleges — museums（用 *Later / from*）→ profits → moved — South London（用 *After the exhibition / The Crystal Palace*）→ remained（用 *where / and ... until*）→ burnt down 1936（用 *when*）。
</Callout>

<Callout type="tip" title="参考范文（自己写完再看）">
  The Crystal Palace, which was built for the Great Exhibition of 1851, was different from all other buildings because it was made of iron and glass. Goods from all over the world were shown there, as well as a great deal of machinery.

  So many visitors came by boat and train that the profits could be used to build museums and colleges. After the exhibition, the Crystal Palace was moved to South London, where it remained until it was burnt down in 1936. *(81 words)*
</Callout>

## 11. 作文 Composition (约 150 词)

<Callout type="info" title="任务">
  Write two paragraphs in **about 150 words** using the ideas given below:

  1. **A visit to a modern exhibition** — how I got there — the exhibition attracted large crowds.
  2. **My first impressions** — the things on display — a walk round the exhibition hall — the exhibits that I liked best — tired at the end of the day.
</Callout>

<Callout type="warning" title="提示骨架（每点都自己填）">
  - 第一段：哪一场展览、为什么去 → 怎么到的（地铁 / 公交 / 步行）→ 人山人海（*The exhibition had attracted large crowds*）。
  - 第二段：进门第一印象 → 看到的展品 → 绕展厅走一圈 → 最喜欢的展品 → 结尾“一天下来累坏了”。
</Callout>

<Callout type="tip" title="写作脚手架">
  - **时态主线**：一般过去时叙事（*went / got there / walked round / liked*）；过去完成时表更早的事（*the exhibition had attracted large crowds*）。
  - **“到达”动词库**：*made my way to, got there by underground, arrived to find ...*。
  - **“人多”表达库**：*was packed with people, drew enormous crowds, we had to queue for an hour*。
  - **“展品”词库**：*on display / on show, exhibits, stands, the latest models, a working model of ...*。
  - **“最喜欢”句式**：*What I liked best was ... / The exhibit that impressed me most was ...*。
  - **结尾**：*By the end of the day I was tired out, but it had been well worth the visit.*
</Callout>

<Callout type="tip" title="参考范文（自己写完再看）">
  Last month I went to a modern science exhibition that everyone in town was talking about. I made my way there by underground, and when I came up into the street I found the hall packed with people. The exhibition had attracted such large crowds that we had to queue for almost an hour before we could get in.

  My first impression was one of noise and light. There was a great deal on display — robots, electric cars and even a working model of a space station. I walked slowly round the hall, stopping at every stand. The exhibits that I liked best were the robots, which could talk and play chess. By the end of the day I was tired out and my feet ached, but it had been one of the most interesting days I had spent for a long time. *(143 words)*
</Callout>

## 12. 书信写作 Letter writing

<Callout type="info" title="任务">
  Write a suitable ***Purpose*** of about **50 words** to follow this introductory paragraph:

  *I have some wonderful news for you. I have at last managed to get two tickets for the Cup Final.*

  即：承接上面这段“开头（Introduction）”，写出约 50 词的“目的（Purpose）”——告诉朋友你想邀他一起去看决赛，约定时间地点，并让对方尽快回复。
</Callout>

<Callout type="tip" title="写作脚手架">
  - **目的段（Purpose）= 写信的核心意图**：紧扣“两张票”往下写——邀请、安排、请对方确认。
  - **邀请句**：*I am writing to ask whether you would like to come with me. / I would love you to join me.*
  - **安排细节**：日期、碰头地点、出发时间 —— *The match is on Saturday the 12th. We could meet outside the stadium at two o'clock.*
  - **催回复**：*Do let me know as soon as you can, as I must confirm the seats. / Drop me a line by Friday.*
  - **时态**：将来安排用一般现在 / be going to（*The match is on Saturday*）；委婉邀请用 would。
</Callout>

<Callout type="tip" title="参考范文（自己写完再看）">
  I am writing at once to ask whether you would like to come with me. I know how keen you are on football, so I could not think of a better person to take. The match is on Saturday the 12th. We could meet outside the main gate at two o'clock. Do let me know by Friday so that I can confirm everything. *(58 words)*
</Callout>

## 13. 拓展练习 Going further

<Callout type="tip" title="自学者建议">
  - **影子跟读 (shadowing)**：用第 1 节的三个 SayIt 按钮反复跟读，重点感受 *not as **easy** as* 里 as 的弱读、以及 *one of the most **famous** buildings* 重音落在 famous 上的节奏。
  - **复述 (retell)**：合上课文，用 1 分半钟讲水晶宫的“一生”——**为何而建**（1851 世博会、海德公园）→ **为何特别**（铁和玻璃、有史以来最大之一）→ **展出什么**（各国商品、机器、蒸汽锤）→ **怎么来的人**（汽船过海峡 + 火车，共 600 万）→ **结局**（利润建馆校 → 迁往伦敦南部 → 1936 焚毁）。
  - **造句迁移**：用今天的 5 个高价值结构造你自己的句子——
    1. *one of the + 最高级 + 复数名词*（……之一）—— *The Great Wall is one of the most famous places in China.*
    2. *not as + 原级 + as*（不像……那样）—— *This winter is not as cold as last winter.*
    3. *be made of ...*（用……制成，物理变化）—— *The bridge is made of steel.*
    4. *on + V-ing*（一……就……）—— *On hearing the bell, the students stood up.*
    5. *带 on 的短语*（on duty / on purpose / on the whole）—— *On the whole, the trip was a success.*
  - **延伸思考**：水晶宫是“工业革命的橱窗”——一座用**铁与玻璃**预制、像搭积木一样建起的大楼，本身就是 19 世纪最了不起的“展品”。课文用**一连串最高级**（most extraordinary / biggest ... of all time / most famous）层层加码，让“它有多了不起”不靠形容词堆砌，而靠**精确的比较范围**（of all time、in the world）支撑——这正是英文写作里“把‘最’说得让人信服”的诀窍：先圈定范围，再下最高级。
</Callout>
