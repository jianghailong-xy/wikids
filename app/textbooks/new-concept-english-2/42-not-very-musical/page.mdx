## The Text 课文

As we **had had a long walk** through one of the markets of Old Delhi, we stopped at a square to **have a rest**. After a time, we noticed a **snake charmer** with two large baskets at the other side of the square, so we went to **have a look at him**. As soon as he saw us, he **picked up** a long pipe which **was covered with coins** and opened one of the baskets. When he began to play a tune, we **had our first glimpse** of the snake. It **rose out of** the basket and began to **follow the movements** of the pipe. We **were very much surprised** when the snake charmer suddenly began to play jazz and modern pop songs. The snake, however, **continued to 'dance'** slowly. It obviously **could not tell the difference between** Indian music and jazz!

## 课文注释 Notes on the text

<Callout type="info" title="4 个值得停下来嚼一嚼的点">
  1. **标题 Not very musical** — “并非很懂音乐”。**musical** 作形容词除了“音乐的”，还可指人“精通音乐、有音乐天赋的”，或指乐器“音色优美的”。课文用它来打趣那条蛇：它“跳舞”只是跟着笛子的物理摆动，根本分不清乐种——所以 *not very musical*（其实一点也不懂音乐）。
  2. **have + 名词（动作名词）** — 本课的灵魂句式。课文里 *had a long walk*（散步 / 走了很长一段路）、*have a rest*（休息）、*have a look at him*（看看他）、*had our first glimpse*（第一次瞥见）全都是“**have / had + 名词**”替代一个普通动词。这种说法在英式口语里极常见，比单用动词更自然、更轻巧。详见下面第 3 节。
  3. **It rose out of the basket** (l.7) — **rise** 是**不及物动词**（rise–rose–risen），意为“（自己）升起、起身”，后面**不接宾语**。注意别和及物动词 **raise**（raise–raised–raised，“举起、抬起 *某物*”）混淆。蛇是自己“升起来”，所以用 rose。
  4. **tell the difference between A and B** (l.10) — “分辨出 A 与 B 之间的不同”。这里的 **tell** 不是“告诉”，而是“辨别、看出”；常与 *can / could* 连用：*can't tell the difference / could not tell ... apart*。同型：*Can you tell the difference between butter and margarine?*
</Callout>

## 3. 关键句型 Have：用 have + 名词替代普通动词

<Callout type="tip" title="一分钟回顾">
  本课关键句型是 **have / have got** 表“拥有”，以及更妙的一招——**have + 名词** 代替一个普通动词，让英语更地道、更口语化。

  ### A. have 表“拥有”（= own / possess），还可换成 have got

  | 普通说法 | 地道说法 |
  |---|---|
  | He **owns** a new house. | He **has** a new house. / He **has got** a new house. |
  | He **possesses** a lot of money. | He **has** a lot of money. / He **has got** a lot of money. |

  ### B. have + 名词 = 一个动作（本课重点）

  很多“做某动作”都能说成 **have a + 动作名词**，比直接用动词更自然：

  | 用普通动词 | 用 have + 名词 |
  |---|---|
  | I **walked** in the garden. | I **had a walk** in the garden. |
  | He wanted to **drink** a glass of water. | He wanted to **have a drink** of water. |
  | We will **talk** about it tomorrow. | We will **have a talk** about it tomorrow. |
  | **Look** at this. | **Have a look** at this. |
  | I **took a bath** before dinner. | I **had a bath** before dinner. |
  | I **enjoyed myself** at the party. | I **had a good time** at the party. |

  ### ⚠️ 课文里 have 替代普通动词的 4 处

  1. *we **had had a long walk*** = we had walked（散步 / 走了很长一段路）
  2. *to **have a rest*** = to rest（休息）
  3. *to **have a look** at him* = to look at him（看看他）
  4. *we **had our first glimpse** of the snake* = we first glimpsed / first saw the snake（第一次瞥见那条蛇）

  > 小心：have a walk / have a rest / have a look 里的名词前**几乎都带 a**；而表“拥有”的 have 后面跟的是被拥有的东西，不一定带 a。
</Callout>

## 4. 难点：动词 pick 的多义 Special difficulties

<Callout type="info" title="一个 pick，四种意思（参见第 6、7 两课难点）">
  课文 l.4-5 的 *he **picked up** a long pipe* 只是 pick 的一种用法。**pick** 与不同小品词搭配，意思各不相同：

  | 短语 | 含义 | 例句 |
  |---|---|---|
  | **pick up** | 拿起、捡起 | He **picked up** a long pipe which was covered with coins. (ll.4-5) |
  | **pick ... up** | （开车）去接某人 | I'll **pick you up** in the car this evening.（今晚我开车去接你） |
  | **pick up** | （无意中）学会、习得 | I **picked up** a lot of English while I was in England.（= I learnt.） |
  | **pick out** | （从许多中）挑出、选出 | There are so many beautiful cards, I can't **pick out** the ones I like best.（= I can't choose.） |

  > 记忆钩子：**up** 多与“向上 / 拿起 / 半路接走 / 顺手学到”相关；**out** 强调“从一堆里挑出来”。
</Callout>

## 9. 句子合并 Sentence structure

<Callout type="info" title="把短句连成课文里的长句（对照课文第 4-6 行）">
  教材 *Sentence structure* 要求把下面几个短句合并，再对照课文核对：

  > He saw us. He picked up a long pipe. It was covered with coins. He opened one of the baskets.

  **参考答案（即课文 l.4-6 原句）：**

  *As soon as he saw us, he picked up a long pipe **which was covered with coins** and opened one of the baskets.*

  合并要点：① “一……就……”用 **As soon as** 引导时间状语从句；② “缠满硬币”用 **which was covered with coins** 这个定语从句修饰 pipe；③ 后两个动作 *picked up ... and opened ...* 用 **and** 并列，共用主语 he。
</Callout>

## 11. 作文 Composition

<Callout type="info" title="任务">
  *Rewrite the following sentences using the joining words in parentheses.* 用括号中的连词改写以下句子：

  1. The snake charmer opened his basket. He started to play a tune. The snake refused to move. *(and ... but)*
  2. The snake charmer shook the basket. The snake obeyed him. *(Then ... and)*
  3. Everybody was frightened. These snakes are not dangerous. *(but)*
</Callout>

<Callout type="warning" title="提示骨架">
  - **and** 连接“同一主语的连续动作”；**but** 连接“出乎意料的转折”。
  - **Then** 放句首表“接着、然后”，多用逗号断开：*Then he ..., and ...*。
  - 注意时态统一：题目都是一般过去时叙事，*opened / started / refused / shook / obeyed* 全用过去式。
</Callout>

<Callout type="tip" title="参考范文（自己写完再看）">
  1. The snake charmer opened his basket **and** started to play a tune, **but** the snake refused to move.
  2. **Then** the snake charmer shook the basket **and** the snake obeyed him.
  3. Everybody was frightened, **but** these snakes are not dangerous.
</Callout>

<Callout type="tip" title="写作脚手架">
  英文信头（寄信人地址 + 日期）写在右上角，顺序是**由小到大**：门牌街道 → 城镇 → 郡 / 州 → 国家，最后**单独一行写日期**。地址各行行末通常省略逗号（现代写法），日期写作 “日 月 年”。
  - **英国地址**：先街道、再城镇、再郡、再国家。Buxton 在 Derbyshire 郡，故顺序为 *街道 → Buxton → Derbyshire → England*。
  - **美国地址**：街道 → 城市 → 州，国家可选。
</Callout>

## 13. 拓展练习 Going further

<Callout type="tip" title="自学者建议">
  - **影子跟读 (shadowing)**：用第 1 节的三个 SayIt 按钮跟读 3-5 遍。重点练 *had had a long walk* 的连读、*which was covered with coins* 的轻读，以及结尾 *Indian music and jazz!* 的语调收束。
  - **复述 (retell)**：合上课文，用 1 分钟把故事讲一遍。**三步叙事**：背景（逛集市走累了，歇脚 → 看见耍蛇人）→ 经过（举笛、开筐 → 蛇随笛起舞）→ 反转（改吹爵士乐，蛇照旧慢舞 → 显然不懂音乐）。
  - **造句迁移**：用本课 5 个高价值结构造你自己的句子——
    1. *have / had + a + 动作名词*（用 have 代普通动词）—— *Let's have a swim before lunch.*
    2. *which was covered with ...*（定语从句）—— *He showed me a wall which was covered with photographs.*
    3. *rise out of ...*（不及物 rise）—— *The sun rose out of the sea.*
    4. *follow the movements of ...* —— *The kitten followed the movements of the string.*
    5. *can't tell the difference between A and B* —— *I can't tell the difference between the twins.*
  - **延伸思考**：标题 *Not very musical* 的妙处在于**反讽**——蛇看似“随乐起舞”，实则只跟着笛子的物理摆动。本课语法主线则是 **have + 名词**：英语里把“散步、休息、看一眼、瞥一下、洗个澡、聊一会儿”都说成 *have a walk / have a rest / have a look / have a glimpse / have a bath / have a talk*，既轻巧又地道。下次开口前不妨问自己一句：“这个动作，能不能用 *have a ...* 来说？”
