## The Text 课文

A Festival for the Dead **is held** once a year in Japan. This festival is a cheerful **occasion**, for on this day, the dead **are said to return** to their homes and they **are welcomed by the living**. As they **are expected to be hungry** after their long journey, food **is laid out** for them. Specially-made lanterns **are hung** outside each house to help the dead to find their way. **All night long**, people dance and sing. In the early morning, the food that **had been laid out** for the dead **is thrown** into a river or into the sea as **it is considered unlucky** for anyone living to eat it. In towns that are near the sea, the tiny lanterns which **had been hung** in the streets the night before, are placed into the water when the festival is over. Thousands of lanterns slowly **drift** out to sea guiding the dead on their return journey to the other world. This is **a moving spectacle**, for crowds of people stand on the shore watching the lanterns drifting away until they can be seen no more.

## 课文注释 Notes on the text

<Callout type="info" title="4 个值得停下来嚼一嚼的点">
  1. **the dead are said to return to their homes** (ll.2-3) — "据说死去的人要回到他们的家里来"。两层语言点：① **the + 形容词 = 一类人**，**the dead** = 死人 / 亡灵（同型：*the rich* 富人 / *the poor* 穷人 / *the living* 活着的人——课文下一行就出现了）。② **sb. be said to do sth.** 是"报道式被动"，相当于 *People say that the dead return ...*。同型：*He is said to be very rich. / She is said to have left the country.*
  2. **lay out** (l.4) — "摆开，摆设，陈列"。课文 *food is laid out for them* = 把食物摆放出来。注意动词变化 **lay - laid - laid**（及物，"放置"），别和 **lie - lay - lain**（不及物，"躺/位于"）混淆。同型：*She laid out the documents on the table. / The shop laid out its goods in the window.*
  3. **all night long** (l.6) — "整夜，通宵"。**名词 + long** 表"整段时间"的固定结构。同型：*all day long*（一整天）/ *all year long*（一年到头）/ *all summer long*（整个夏天）。课文 *All night long, people dance and sing* = 人们通宵跳舞唱歌。
  4. **the other world** (l.11) — "另一个世界，即阴间"。注意 **the other**（特指两者中的"另一个"）≠ *another*（泛指"再一个"）。课文里活人的世界与亡灵的世界两两相对，所以用 **the other world**。同型：*the other side of the river*（河的对岸）。
</Callout>

## 3. 关键句型 The passive voice 被动语态全景

<Callout type="tip" title="一分钟回顾">
  本课是英语教材里训练**被动语态**的范本——通篇描述一种"集体仪式"，动作的**执行者并不重要**（是"人们"在做），重要的是"被怎样对待"，所以一连串用了被动。把课文动词圈出来，你会看到这样的分布：

  | 形式 | 课文例句 | 用途 |
  |---|---|---|
  | **一般现在被动** | *A Festival ... **is held** / they **are welcomed** / food **is laid out** / lanterns **are hung** / it **is thrown** / are **placed*** | 反复发生的仪式、客观陈述 |
  | **be said to do**（报道式被动） | *the dead **are said to return** to their homes* | "据说……"，回避说话人 |
  | **be expected to do** | *they **are expected to be** hungry* | "预料……会……" |
  | **it is + 过去分词 + that / to** | ***it is considered unlucky** for anyone living to eat it* | "人们认为……"，形式主语 it |
  | **过去完成被动** | *the food that **had been laid out** / the lanterns which **had been hung*** | 比主线更早发生的"被动动作" |
  | **can + be + 过去分词** | *until they **can be seen** no more* | 情态动词的被动 |

  ### ⚠️ 4 个本课最容易踩的雷区

  1. **被动 = be + 过去分词**：*is held*（hold-held-held）、*is laid out*（lay-laid-laid）、*is thrown*（throw-threw-thrown）——别用动词原形或一般过去式。
  2. **be said to do** 不是 *be said do*：to 不能丢。整句 = *People say that the dead return ...* 的同义改写。
  3. **形式主语 it**：*it is considered unlucky ... to eat it* ——开头的 it 是形式主语，真正的主语是后面的 *to eat it*；句末的 it 才指"食物"。
  4. **真实条件句**：*If anyone **eats** the food, he **will be** unlucky.* ——if 从句用一般现在时，主句用 will，绝不能写成 *If anyone will eat*。
</Callout>

## 4. 难点：易混词 + 动词短语 Special difficulties

<Callout type="info" title="本课难点 = 复习第 74-91 课（Review SD 74-91）">
  教材本课的 *Special Difficulties* 是一次**总复习**，分两块：
  - **A 经常容易混淆的词**：在 *as usual / among / manager / clothes / greet / clean / wash up / temper / too / fairly / enough / single / queue* 之间选对的那个。
  - **B 动词短语**：用 **get / keep / take / run / make** 加上 *into, in, after, out, out of, down, up, away, for, over, from* 等小品词，组成 *take in（欺骗）/ run out of（用完）/ make up（编造）/ get over（康复）/ make for（驶向）/ take over（接管）/ make out（辨认）/ take up（开始从事）* 等地道搭配。

  ⚠️ 记忆窍门：动词短语的意思 = "动词 + 小品词"的**整体**，不能逐字直译。*take in* 不是"拿进"而是"欺骗/领会"；*run into* 不是"跑进"而是"偶遇"。
</Callout>

## 10. 摘要写作 Summary writing (≤ 80 词)

  按教材给的 9 个要点 + 连词串成两段：

  2. **As** dead return home — **for** ...

  7. Some places: lanterns — sea.
  8. **and** People watch — shore.

## 11. 作文 Composition (约 150 词)

<Callout type="info" title="任务">
  Imagine witnessing the sort of festival described in the passage. Write **two paragraphs in about 150 words** using the ideas given below.（设想你亲眼目睹了亡灵节，按以下思路写两段。）
</Callout>

<Callout type="warning" title="提示骨架">
  - **第一段 Preparations**：the making of the lanterns; preparing food — description of the streets at night; people dancing and singing.
  - **第二段 The following morning**：food was thrown away — lanterns on the sea — people watching — the lanterns disappeared — the sun rose.
</Callout>

<Callout type="tip" title="写作脚手架">
  - **时态主线**：第一段写"那一晚的所见"——一般过去时为主，背景用过去进行（*people were dancing / lanterns were glowing*）。
  - **"准备"动词库**：*made / folded / lit / hung up / set out / spread / prepared*。
  - **"画面"形容词库**：*glowing, flickering, festive, crowded, hushed, shimmering*。
  - **第二段的"先后"**：用 *first ... then ... at last ...* 或过去完成 *had been thrown away* 标清顺序，最后用 *as the sun rose* 收尾，呼应灯笼"消失"。
  - **结构亮点**：把课文的被动句"搬"进作文——*the food was thrown away / the lanterns were placed on the water*——既贴合本课语法，又显得客观庄重。
</Callout>

<Callout type="tip" title="参考范文（自己写完再看）">
  On the evening of the festival, the whole town was busy. In every doorway people had made bright paper lanterns and hung them up to glow in the dark. Food was being set out on little tables for the dead, and the narrow streets shimmered with hundreds of flickering lights. All night long, crowds danced and sang, and a happy, restless noise filled the air until dawn.

  The following morning everything was quieter. The uneaten food was carried down to the shore and thrown away into the sea. Then the tiny lanterns were placed gently on the water. Slowly they began to drift out, and crowds of people stood watching in silence. One by one the little lights grew fainter, until at last they disappeared over the horizon. As the sun rose, the sea was empty again, and the festival was over for another year. *(146 words)*
</Callout>

## 12. 书信写作 Letter writing (约 80 词)

<Callout type="info" title="任务">
  Write a letter to a friend **refusing an invitation to a party**. Supply a suitable **Introduction** and **Conclusion**. Use the following ideas to write your *Purpose*:（给朋友写信谢绝聚会邀请，配上恰当的引言与结束语。）

  ***Purpose:*** you regret you cannot come — reason why — you bought tickets for a play a month ago — you have arranged to go with several friends.
</Callout>

<Callout type="tip" title="写作脚手架">
  - **称呼**：*Dear + 名字*（写给朋友用名字，亲切）。
  - **开头 (Introduction)**：先谢邀 —— *Thank you so much for inviting me to your party.*
  - **主体 (Purpose)** 按 3 个要点串：
    1. **表遗憾 + 给理由**：*I'm really sorry, but I'm afraid I won't be able to come.*
    2. **原因**：*A month ago I booked tickets for a play on that very evening.*
    3. **已有安排**：*I've arranged to go with several friends, so I can't let them down.*
  - **结尾 (Conclusion)**：*I do hope the party goes well — let's meet up soon. / Best wishes,*
  - **时态混搭**：*Thank you for inviting*（动名词）/ *I booked / I've arranged*（一般过去 + 现在完成）/ *I won't be able to*（将来）。
</Callout>

<Callout type="tip" title="参考范文（自己写完再看）">
  Dear Helen,

  Thank you so much for inviting me to your birthday party on Saturday. I'm really sorry, but I'm afraid I won't be able to come. A month ago I booked tickets for a play that very evening, and I've arranged to go with several friends, so I simply can't let them down.

  I'll be thinking of you, and I do hope the party goes wonderfully. Let's meet up soon so I can wish you a happy birthday in person.

  Best wishes,

  Anna *(80 words)*
</Callout>

## 13. 拓展练习 Going further

<Callout type="tip" title="自学者建议">
  - **影子跟读 (shadowing)**：用第 1 节的三个 SayIt 跟读 3-5 遍。重点感受被动句里 *is held / are welcomed / had been laid out* 的轻读节奏——被动语态里 be 动词常弱读，过去分词才重读。
  - **复述 (retell)**：合上课文，用 2 分钟把亡灵节讲一遍。**叙事三段**：起（一年一次的欢乐节日 → 亡灵归家、活人迎接）→ 承（摆食、挂灯、通宵歌舞）→ 转/合（次日倒掉食物 → 把灯笼放进水里 → 漂向大海，看不见为止）。
  - **被动语态造句迁移**：用今天的 5 个被动结构造你自己的句子——
    1. *S + is / are + 过去分词*（一般现在被动）—— *The exam is held twice a year.*
    2. *S + be said to do*（据说……）—— *He is said to speak five languages.*
    3. *It is + 过去分词 + that / to do*（人们认为……）—— *It is believed that the bridge is over 800 years old.*
    4. *S + had been + 过去分词*（过去完成被动）—— *When I arrived, the room had been cleaned.*
    5. *can + be + 过去分词*（情态被动）—— *The stars can be seen clearly tonight.*
  - **延伸思考**：本课为什么几乎句句被动？因为它写的是一种**集体仪式**——"谁在做"并不重要（是"人们"、是"传统"），重要的是事物"被怎样对待"。被动语态把焦点从"人"移到"事"，让文字显得客观、庄重，正合一篇"风俗志"的笔调。下次你写说明文、实验报告或介绍流程时，不妨多用被动——*The water is heated to 100°C / The lanterns are placed on the sea*——立刻就有了一种"客观叙述"的分量。
</Callout>
