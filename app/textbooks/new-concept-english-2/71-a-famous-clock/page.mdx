<Callout type="info" title="Listen first 听前思考">
  到伦敦，你最先看到的东西之一，往往就是那座钟——**Big Ben（“大本”钟）**。它的钟声透过 **B.B.C.（英国广播公司）** 的广播传遍全世界。你也许不知道：要不是 1834 年那场大火把 **国会大厦（the Houses of Parliament）** 烧成了灰，这座钟根本就不会被造出来。它体型巨大，走时却极准——格林尼治天文台每天派人来校两次。它几乎从不出错……可“几乎”不等于“从不”。有那么一回，它真的把时间报错了——而罪魁祸首，竟是一位油漆工和一桶油漆。
  **Has Big Ben ever gone wrong?（“大本”钟出过差错吗？）** 带着这个问题听录音、读课文，重点留意全篇是如何用 **与过去事实相反的虚拟语气**（*if ... had not been ..., ... would never have been ...*）、**“have + 宾语 + 过去分词”的“使役 / 让别人做”结构**，以及 **过去进行时的被动语态** 把一座钟的“身世”讲得层层分明的。
</Callout>

## The Text 课文

When you visit London, one of the first things you will see is Big Ben, the famous clock which **can be heard** all over the world on the B.B.C. **If the Houses of Parliament had not been burned down in 1834, the great clock would never have been erected.** Big Ben **takes its name from** Sir Benjamin Hall who **was responsible for** the making of the clock **when the new Houses of Parliament were being built**. **It is not only of immense size, but is extremely accurate as well.** Officials from Greenwich Observatory **have the clock checked twice a day**. On the B.B.C. you can hear the clock when it is actually striking because microphones **are connected to** the clock tower. Big Ben **has rarely gone wrong**. Once, however, it **failed to** give the correct time. A painter **who had been working** on the tower hung a pot of paint on one of the hands and **slowed it down**!

## 课文注释 Notes on the text

<Callout type="info" title="7 个值得停下来嚼一嚼的点">
  1. **Big Ben** (l.1) — 大钟的名字，译成“大本”。一说得名于负责工程的 Sir Benjamin Hall（本杰明·霍尔爵士），他身材高大，人称 “Big Ben”。
  2. **the B.B.C.** (l.2) — 是英国广播公司（**B**ritish **B**roadcasting **C**orporation）的缩写。注意每个字母后面都带句点，读作 /ˌbiː biː ˈsiː/。
  3. **If the Houses of Parliament had not been burned down in 1834, the great clock would never have been erected.** (ll.3-4) — 如果不是国会大厦在 1834 年被烧毁的话，这座大钟永远也不会建造。这是一个**表示与过去事实相反的虚拟语气**结构：*if + had (not) + 过去分词*（条件），主句 *would (never) have + 过去分词*（结果）。同型：*If you had told me, I would have helped you.*
  4. **take its name from** (l.4) — 以……命名，得名于……。同型：*The river takes its name from a Roman fort. / This dish takes its name from the city where it was invented.*
  5. **when the new Houses of Parliament were being built** (l.6) — 注意这个从句中**过去进行时的被动结构**：*were/was being + 过去分词*，表示“（当时）正在被建造”。同型：*The bridge was being repaired when I passed by.*
  6. **It is not only of immense size, but is extremely accurate as well.** (ll.6-7) — 它不仅外型巨大，而且走时极准。“**to be of + 名词**”用来表示人或物的特征，*be of immense size* = be immensely large（体型巨大）。同型：*The matter is of great importance. = The matter is very important.* 另外 **not only ... but ... as well**（不仅……而且……）是一对常用关联结构。
  7. **have the clock checked** (l.9) — “**have + 宾语 + 过去分词**”表示“让别人做某事 / 使某事被做”（使役结构）。*have the clock checked twice a day* = 让人每天检查校正此钟两次。同型：*I had my hair cut. / She had her car repaired.* 还有一处需留意：**Big Ben has rarely gone wrong**（l.11）——“大本”钟很少出差错，**rarely** 本身含否定意味。
</Callout>

## 3. 关键句型 虚拟语气 · have sth done · when + 一般现在表将来

<Callout type="tip" title="一分钟回顾">
  本课是“**多种时态 / 语态的一次小检阅**”。把课文动词圈出来，你会看到这样几条主线：

  | 结构 | 课文例句 | 要点 |
  |---|---|---|
  | **与过去相反的虚拟语气** | *If the Houses of Parliament **had not been burned down**, the great clock **would never have been erected**.* | *if + had (not) + 过去分词*，主句 *would have + 过去分词* |
  | **have + 宾语 + 过去分词（使役）** | *Officials ... **have the clock checked** twice a day.* | “让别人做 / 使某事被做” |
  | **过去进行时的被动** | *when the new Houses of Parliament **were being built*** | *was/were being + 过去分词* |
  | **过去完成进行时** | *A painter **who had been working** on the tower ...* | 强调动作在“过去某点”之前一直在进行 |
  | **when + 一般现在表将来** | *one of the first things you **will see** is Big Ben ... when you **visit** London* | 时间状语从句里用一般现在代替将来 |
  | **be of + 名词（表特征）** | *It is **of immense size**.* | = It is immensely large |
  | **形容词最高级** | *Big Ben is the **most famous** clock ...* | 多音节形容词加 most |

  ### ⚠️ 4 个本课最容易踩的雷区

  1. **虚拟语气“两条腿都要变”**：条件句 *had not been burned down*（过去完成），主句 *would never have been erected*（would + have + 过去分词）。**两边都用过去完成 / would have**，少变一边就错。
  2. **have sth done ≠ 自己做**：*have the clock checked* 是“让人来检查（钟被检查）”，不是“官员亲自检查”。过去分词 *checked* 表“被动 + 完成”。
  3. **时间状语从句不用 will**：*When you visit London, you will see ...* —— 主句用 will，*when* 从句用**一般现在 visit**（不能 will visit）。
  4. **of immense size 是固定说法**：表“体型巨大”用 *be of + 名词*，不要写成 *be immense size*（少了 of）或 *be in immense size*。
</Callout>

## 4. 难点：经常容易混淆的词 Words often confused

<Callout type="info" title="两组最容易混的词：official / employee / shop assistant 和 hang 的两种过去式">
  教材 *Special Difficulties* 部分挑了两组词，专治“看着差不多、用起来全错”：

  ### ① official / employee / shop assistant —— 都是“干活的人”，但身份不同

  | 词 | 含义 | 教材例句 |
  |---|---|---|
  | **official** | （政府 / 机构的）**官员、行政人员** | *Officials from Greenwich Observatory have the clock checked twice a day.*（ll.8-9）/ *The Customs official asked me several questions.* 那个海关官员问了我好几个问题。 |
  | **employee** | （公司 / 工厂的）**雇员、职工** | *Over a thousand factory employees went on strike.* 上千名工厂工人罢工了。 |
  | **shop assistant** | （商店里的）**售货员、店员**（英式；美式多说 clerk / salesperson） | *She works as a shop assistant in a clothing store.* 她在一家服装商店当店员。 |

  ### ② hang 的两个过去式：hung 还是 hanged？

  | 含义 | 过去式 / 过去分词 | 教材例句 |
  |---|---|---|
  | **悬挂（东西）** | **hang – hung – hung** | *A painter hung a pot of paint on one of the hands.*（ll.11-12）/ *When the sun came out, she hung the washing on the line.* 当太阳出来的时候，她把洗好的衣服晾在绳子上。 |
  | **绞死（人）** | **hang – hanged – hanged** | *The murderer was hanged.* 杀人犯被绞死了。 |

  ### ⚠️ 一句话记牢

  - **挂东西用 hung，绞死人用 hanged**——油漆工 *hung* 油漆桶（挂东西）；杀人犯被 *hanged*（绞刑）。
  - **官员 official，雇员 employee，店员 shop assistant**——别一概用 worker。
</Callout>

## 10. 摘要写作 Summary writing (≤ 75 词)

<Callout type="info" title="任务">
  *Answer these questions in not more than 75 words.* 回答下列问题，将答案组成一个段落，不要超过 75 个单词。

  1. When were the Houses of Parliament burnt down? Who was made responsible for the construction of a huge clock? Did it become known as Big Ben or not? *(After ... which ...)*
  2. Why is it very accurate despite its immense size? *(for)*
  3. Has this clock often gone wrong, or has it rarely gone wrong? Why can it be heard on the B.B.C. when it is striking? *(This clock which ... because ...)*
</Callout>

<Callout type="tip" title="参考范文（自己写完再看）">
  After the Houses of Parliament were burnt down in 1834, Sir Benjamin Hall, who was made responsible for the construction of a huge clock, gave it his name, so it became known as Big Ben. It keeps very accurate time, for officials from Greenwich have it checked twice a day. This clock, which has rarely gone wrong, can be heard on the B.B.C. when it is striking because microphones are connected to the tower. *(73 words)*
</Callout>

## 11. 作文 Composition

<Callout type="info" title="任务">
  *Write two or three sentences using the ideas given below.* 用以下内容写出 2 至 3 个句子：

  Big Ben tells correct time — it also tells us when Parliament is in session — there is a light in the clock tower — it is kept on until the House closes — sometimes it is on all night.
</Callout>

<Callout type="warning" title="提示骨架">
  - 第一句：先说“报时准” + “还告诉我们议会是否在开会”（用 not only ... but also / as well 把两点并起来）。
  - 第二句：解释“怎么告诉”——钟塔上有一盏灯（there is a light），议会开会时亮着。
  - 第三句：补充细节——灯一直亮到议会散会（is kept on until ...），有时通宵不灭（all night）。
</Callout>

<Callout type="tip" title="参考范文（自己写完再看）">
  Big Ben does not only tell the correct time; it also tells us when Parliament is in session. There is a light in the clock tower which is kept on until the House closes. Sometimes, when the debate runs late, it stays on all night. *(43 words)*
</Callout>

## 12. 书信写作 Letter writing

<Callout type="info" title="任务">
  *Learn the following phrases by heart.* 熟记以下两个开头语：

  - **I wonder how you learnt that ...**（我想知道你是怎么知道那件事的……）
  - **You will never guess who / what ...**（你绝对猜不到谁 / 什么……）

  *Exercise:* Write two short paragraphs (of not more than two sentences each) completing the above phrases. 用以上开头语各写一小段话，每段不超过两句。
</Callout>

<Callout type="tip" title="写作脚手架">
  - 两个开头语都是**口语化的“制造悬念”句**，常用于写信 / 聊天里抛出八卦或惊喜。
  - **I wonder how you learnt that ...** 后面接一个**宾语从句**（that 引导），暗示“连我自己都没告诉过你”。
  - **You will never guess who / what ...** 后面接 **who / what 引导的从句**，把“最意外的那个人 / 物”留到句末。
  - 写完检查：从句语序要正常（不要用疑问句倒装），时态要前后一致。
</Callout>

<Callout type="tip" title="参考范文（自己写完再看）">
  I wonder how you learnt that I had changed my job. I only told two people, and I asked them both to keep it a secret!

  You will never guess who I met at the airport last week. It was our old teacher, Mr. Reed, now flying off to teach English in Japan! *(两段各两句)*
</Callout>

## 13. 拓展练习 Going further

<Callout type="tip" title="自学者建议">
  - **影子跟读 (shadowing)**：用第 1 节的三个 SayIt 按钮跟读 3-5 遍。尤其留意那句虚拟语气 *If the Houses of Parliament **had not been burned down** ..., the great clock **would never have been erected*** —— 感受 had / would have 这一对“过去虚拟”的呼应节奏。
  - **复述 (retell)**：合上课文，用 1 分钟把“大本”钟的来历讲一遍——**身世**（火灾 → 建钟 → 得名 Sir Benjamin Hall）→ **特点**（体型巨大 + 走时极准 + 每天校两次 + 钟声经麦克风上 B.B.C.）→ **趣闻**（油漆工挂油漆桶把钟弄慢）。
  - **造句迁移**：用今天的 5 个高价值结构造你自己的句子——
    1. *If ... had (not) + 过去分词, ... would (never) have + 过去分词*（与过去相反的虚拟）—— *If I had not missed the bus, I would not have been late.*
    2. *have + 宾语 + 过去分词*（让别人做）—— *I had my photo taken in the park.*
    3. *take its name from ...*（得名于）—— *The town takes its name from a famous bridge.*
    4. *be of + 名词*（表特征）—— *This decision is of great importance.*
    5. *When + 一般现在, ... will ...*（时间状语从句表将来）—— *When you arrive, I will show you around.*
  - **延伸思考**：本课用一座钟串起了**虚拟语气、使役被动、过去进行被动、过去完成进行**好几种结构——它们都不是“炫技”，而是各司其职：虚拟语气交代“本不该存在的钟为何存在”，have sth done 交代“准时靠谁维护”，过去完成进行交代“出错前发生了什么”。下次你介绍一件事物的来历时，不妨也问问自己：哪一句该用虚拟、哪一句该让“别人来做”、哪一句该往更早的过去回溯——把时态当作“讲故事的镜头”，叙述就会立刻有层次。
</Callout>
