## The Text 课文

The Hubble telescope **was launched** into space by NASA on April 20, 1990 **at a cost of** over a billion dollars. **Right from the start** there was trouble with the Hubble. The pictures it sent us were very disappointing because its main mirror was **faulty**! NASA is now going to **put the telescope right**, so it **will soon be sending up** four astronauts to repair it. The shuttle Endeavour **will be taking** the astronauts to the Hubble.

A robot-arm from the Endeavour **will grab** the telescope and hold it while the astronauts **make** the necessary repairs. Of course, the Hubble is above the earth's atmosphere, so it **will soon be sending** us the clearest pictures of the stars and distant galaxies that we **have ever seen**. The Hubble **will tell** us **a great deal** about the age and size of the universe. **By the time** you read this, the Hubble's **eagle eye** **will have sent** us thousands and thousands of wonderful pictures.

## 课文注释 Notes on the text

<Callout type="info" title="5 个值得停下来嚼一嚼的点">
  1. **at a cost of** (l.2) — “以……的花费 / 代价”，**cost** 在此为名词，指“成本 / 费用”。同型：*The bridge was built at a cost of £50 million. / He succeeded, but at a cost of his health.* 注意区分：**at the cost of** 强调“以牺牲……为代价”（偏抽象牺牲），**at a cost of** 偏“具体费用数额”。

  2. **right from the start** (l.2) — “从一开始就……”，**right** 在此是副词作强调 = exactly / just。同型：*Right from the beginning, I knew it was a mistake. / He was right in the middle of his speech when the power went out.*

  3. **put ... right** (l.4) — “把……修好 / 纠正”，注意这里的 **right** 是形容词“正确的 / 正常的”，**put + 宾语 + right** 是惯用结构。同型：*The mechanic put the engine right in an hour. / If I'm wrong, put me right.* 课文 *put the telescope right* = 把望远镜修好。

  4. **be above ...** (l.9) — “在……上方（不接触）”，对比：**above**（上方，不接触 / 海拔更高 / 超越）vs **over**（正上方——通常覆盖 / 越过）。课文 *above the earth's atmosphere* 强调“在大气层之外的上方”。同型：*The plane was flying above the clouds. / His work is above average.*

  5. **By the time ..., ... will have done** (l.11) — “到你读到这篇文章时，哈勃的'鹰眼'将已经给我们发来了成千上万张精彩照片。” 这句是本课的**语法核心**——**将来完成时**：**will have + 过去分词**，表示“站在未来某时刻回头看，某事在那时已经完成”。**By the time + 一般现在时从句** = “到……的时候”。同型：*By the time you get home, I will have cooked dinner. / By next summer, they will have completed the project.*
</Callout>

## 3. 关键句型 The future: 将来时态全家福

<Callout type="tip" title="一分钟回顾">
  本课的语法核心是**将来时态的三种高级形式**——它们不是简单的 "will do"，而是用来**精确标定未来两个时间点之间的先后关系**：

  | 时态形式 | 结构 | 课文例句 | 含义 |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | **将来进行时** | will be + V-ing | *The shuttle Endeavour **will be taking** the astronauts to the Hubble.* | 未来某时**正在做**——像“未来某一刻的快照” |
  | **将来完成时** | will have + V-ed | *By the time you read this, the Hubble's eagle eye **will have sent** us thousands and thousands of wonderful pictures.* | 到未来某时**已经做完**——“回头看，它已经在” |
  | **将来完成进行时** | will have been + V-ing | *By Friday midday, they **will have been working** on it for seven days.* | 到未来某时**已经持续做了多久**——“回头看，一直在做” |

  ### 三种时态的“画面感”

  把“修哈勃”这个任务想象成一场电影：
  - **将来进行时**：画面定格在“奋进号正载着宇航员飞向哈勃”那一帧 → *will be taking*。
  - **将来完成时**：镜头切到“你读到这篇文章的那一刻”，旁白说“到此刻，哈勃已经发回了成千上万张照片” → *will have sent*。
  - **将来完成进行时**：镜头切到“周五中午”，旁白说“到此时，他们已经连续干了 7 天” → *will have been working*。

  ### ⚠️ 3 个本课最容易踩的雷区

  1. **by the time 从句用一般现在时，主句用将来完成时** —— *By the time you **read** (NOT will read!) this, the Hubble **will have sent** us ...*。时间 / 条件从句中**绝不用 will**。

  2. **will be doing vs will have done** —— *Soon it **will be sending** us pictures*（将来某时正在发送） vs *By next year it **will have sent** thousands of pictures*（到明年已经发送了）。前者的焦点是“在做的动作”，后者的焦点是“已经完成的结果”。

  3. **will have done vs will have been doing** —— 前者强调**完成**（修好了），后者强调**持续时长**（已经修了多久了）。课文习题 b 中把 *Soon the astronauts will have repaired the telescope* 与 *By Friday midday, they will have been working on it for seven days* 对比，正是在区分“完成”与“持续”。
</Callout>

## 4. 难点：Cost, Price, Value 三个“钱”词 Special difficulties

<Callout type="info" title="Cost / Price / Value —— 三个词，三种角度">
  本课的 *Special Difficulties* 把 **cost、price、value** 放在一起辨析——它们都和“钱”有关，但**视角完全不同**：

  | | cost（成本 / 代价） | price（价格 / 标价） | value（价值） |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | **名词核心意** | 做一件事要“**付出**”的 | 买卖中“**标 / 叫**”的 | 东西本身“**值**”的 |
  | **课文例句** | *The Hubble was launched **at a cost of** over a billion dollars.*（花费 10 亿多美元） | *Let's ask the **price**.*（问问价格） | *The stolen items were of sentimental, rather than financial, **value**.*（情感价值，而非金钱价值） |
  | **动词核心意** | “**花掉**某人多少钱” | “给……**标价**” | “**珍视**” |
  | **课文例句** | *What does this blouse **cost**?* | *I want to **price** the bed linen ... before I buy any.* | *I **value** your advice a great deal.* |

  ### ⚠️ 快速辨认法

  - **cost → “花了多少”** —— 主语是**事 / 物**：*The repair **cost** $500.*
  - **price → “标多少”** —— 主语是**人**，宾语是**商品**：*They **priced** the car at £8,000.*
  - **value → “看得多重”** —— 可以是钱可以是感情：*I really **value** your opinion.*

  ### 最常见的错误

  “生活成本上升了”：
  - ✓ *There has been a sharp rise in the **cost** of living.*
  - ✗ *There has been a sharp rise in the **price** of living.* —— price 是“标价”，生活不能被“标价”。
</Callout>

## 10. 摘要写作 Summary writing (≤ 60 词)

<Callout type="info" title="任务">
  *Answer these questions in **not more than 60 words**.*

  按下面两个提示点串成一段：

  1. When was the Hubble telescope launched into space? What were the pictures it sent us like? Why?
     *(When the Hubble ... was launched ..., the pictures ..., because ...)*
  2. What will the four astronauts from the shuttle Endeavour be doing? What will the pictures from the Hubble tell us?
     *(..., and eventually ...)*
</Callout>

<Callout type="tip" title="参考范文（自己写完再看）">
  When the Hubble telescope was launched into space on April 20, 1990, the pictures it sent us were very disappointing because its main mirror was faulty. Now the shuttle Endeavour will be taking four astronauts to repair it. Once repaired, the Hubble will soon be sending us the clearest pictures of stars and distant galaxies ever seen. Eventually it will tell us a great deal about the age and size of the universe. *(74 words — try to trim further!)*
</Callout>

## 12. 书信写作 Letter writing

<Callout type="info" title="任务">
  **Learn the following opening phrases by heart:**

  - *I have not heard from you for some time, so ...*
  - *It was very kind of you to ...*

  **Exercise:** Write **two short paragraphs** (of not more than two sentences each) completing the above phrases.
</Callout>

<Callout type="tip" title="写作脚手架">
  - **Phrase 1: *I have not heard from you for some time, so ...***
    用“好久没收到你的来信了，所以……”开头——后面跟一个“忍不住写信问候”的下文。例：*...so I thought I'd drop you a line to see how you are getting on.*

  - **Phrase 2: *It was very kind of you to ...***
    用“你真是太好了……”开头——感谢对方做过的某件具体的事。例：*...send me that book on astronomy. I have been reading it with great interest.*

  - **时态提示**：第一句因为“从过去到现在没收到信”，适合现在完成时 + 一般现在 / will；第二句是感谢过去的事，用一般过去时。
</Callout>

<Callout type="tip" title="参考范文（自己写完再看）">
  **Paragraph 1:** I have not heard from you for some time, so I thought I would write and tell you about the Hubble repair mission I'm following in the news. *(1 sentence)*

  **Paragraph 2:** It was very kind of you to lend me that telescope last summer. Seeing the craters on the moon through it was what first got me interested in space exploration. *(2 sentences)*
</Callout>

## 13. 拓展练习 Going further

<Callout type="tip" title="自学者建议">
  - **影子跟读 (shadowing)**：用第 1 节的三个 SayIt 按钮跟读 3–5 遍。重点感受这句长句的节奏切分：*The Hubble is above the earth's atmosphere, || so it will soon be sending us || the clearest pictures of the stars and distant galaxies || that we have ever seen.* —— 四个意群，读时在 **||** 处微微停顿。

  - **复述 (retell)**：合上课文，用 1 分钟把哈勃的故事讲一遍。**叙事三段**：问题（花了 10 亿美元 → 主镜有缺陷 → 图片令人失望）→ 解决（NASA 派出奋进号 + 四名宇航员 → 机器手抓住望远镜 → 在轨修复）→ 展望（大气层之上 → 最清晰图像 → 宇宙的年龄与大小 → 成千上万张精彩照片）。

  - **造句迁移**：用今天的 5 个高价值结构造你自己的句子——
    1. **at a cost of + 金额**（以……的花费）—— *The new library was built at a cost of £20 million.*
    2. **right from the start**（从一开始）—— *Right from the start, I knew this job was going to be difficult.*
    3. **put ... right**（修复 / 纠正）—— *The IT team put the server right within half an hour.*
    4. **将来进行时 will be + V-ing**（未来某时正在做）—— *This time next week, I will be lying on a beach in Spain.*
    5. **By the time ..., ... will have done**（将来完成时）—— *By the time you arrive, I will have finished cooking dinner.*

  - **延伸思考**：本课是 NCE2 中**“将来时态全集”的经典展示**——一段不到 200 词的短文蕴含了三种将来时态形式。注意：不是“三种都可以随便用”——每种都精确地表达了一种**时间关系**。将来进行时让你“看到”动作在未来的那一刻**正在进行**；将来完成时让你“站在未来回头”确认某事**已经完成**。这种时间层次感，是英文写作和中文写作最大的区别之一。下次你写未来计划时，问自己：我是在描述“那个时刻正在做的事”（will be doing），还是在宣布“到那时已经达成的结果”（will have done）？四个字：**时态即逻辑**。
</Callout>
