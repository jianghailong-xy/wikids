## The Text 课文

Some plays are **so successful that** they **run for years on end**. In many ways, this is unfortunate for the poor actors who are required to **go on repeating** the same lines night after night. One would expect them to **know their parts by heart** and never have cause to **falter**. Yet **this is not always the case**.

A famous actor in a highly successful play was once **cast in the role of** an aristocrat who had been imprisoned in the Bastille for twenty years. In the last act, a gaoler would always come on to the stage with a letter which he would hand to the prisoner. Even though the noble was expected to read the letter at each performance, he always **insisted that it should be written out in full**.

One night, the gaoler decided to **play a joke on** his colleague to find out if, after so many performances, he **had managed to** learn the contents of the letter by heart. The curtain went up on the final act of the play and revealed the aristocrat sitting alone behind bars in his dark cell. Just then, the gaoler appeared with the precious letter in his hands. He entered the cell and presented the letter to the aristocrat. But the copy he gave him had not been written out in full as usual. It was simply a blank sheet of paper. The gaoler looked on eagerly, anxious to see if his fellow actor had at last learnt his lines. The noble stared at the blank sheet of paper for a few seconds. Then, **squinting his eyes**, he said: "The light is dim. Read the letter to me." And he promptly handed the sheet of paper to the gaoler. Finding that he could not remember a word of the letter either, the gaoler replied: "The light is indeed dim, sire. I must get my glasses." With this, he hurried off the stage. **Much to the aristocrat's amusement**, the gaoler returned a few moments later with a pair of glasses and the usual copy of the letter which he **proceeded to** read to the prisoner.

## 课文注释 Notes on the text

<Callout type="info" title="10 个值得停下来嚼一嚼的点">
  1. **run for years on end** (l.1) — "连演好几年"。**on end** = "连续不断地"，不是"在末端"。同型：*It rained for days on end.* / *He worked for hours on end without a break.* **run** 在此不是"跑"，而是戏剧/电影的"连演、连映"。

  2. **go on repeating** (l.3) — "继续重复"。**go on + 现在分词** = "继续做（同一件事）"。区别于 **go on + to do** = "接着做（另一件事）"。同型：*He went on talking for another hour.*（继续说）vs *After the break, he went on to talk about the budget.*（转而谈预算）。

  3. **know ... by heart** (l.4) — "把……背熟、牢记在心"。**by heart** = "凭记忆"，固定短语。同型：*She knows the poem by heart.* / *Learn these phrases by heart.* 后文复习了这个说法。

  4. **falter** (l.4) — "支吾、结巴"，指说话不流畅、因紧张或忘记而停顿。同型：*He began to falter when the teacher asked him a difficult question.*

  5. **cast in the role of ...** (ll.6-7) — "被选派扮演……角色"。**cast**（过去式/过去分词 cast）在此是"选派演员扮演某角色"的意思。同型：*She was cast as Juliet in the school play.*

  6. **insist that + (should) do** (l.9) — "坚持要求……"。**insist** 后接 that 从句时，常用**虚拟语气**：从句谓语用 **should + 动词原形**，**should** 常省略（英式英语）。这是本课的核心语法点之一。同型：*He insisted that she (should) stay for dinner.* / *I insisted that he (should) apologize.* 注意：insist 还有"坚称（某事实）"的意思，此时不用虚拟语气：*He insisted that he was right.*

  7. **play a joke on sb.** (l.10) — "开某人的玩笑、捉弄某人"。同型：*The children love playing jokes on their teacher.* 近似表达 **play a trick on sb.** 也是"捉弄"，稍带恶作剧色彩，是本课 Vocabulary 第 9 题的考点。

  8. **squinting his eyes** (l.16) — "眯起眼睛"。**squint** = "眯着眼看"，多因光线太强或太暗而眯眼。这里贵族在昏暗的牢房里故意眯眼，装作看不清——实则是为了把信递给狱卒。现在分词短语作伴随状语，描述与主句动作同时进行的状态。

  9. **sire** (l.18) — "陛下"。古代仆人对国王的尊称，现在仅用于故事、剧本或历史题材中。注意不是"先生"（sir）——sire 是王族专用。课文中狱卒这样称呼牢里的"贵族"，暗示这部剧设定在古代。

  10. **Much to the aristocrat's amusement** (l.19) — "使贵族感到非常可笑的是……"。**much to + sb.'s + 名词** 是常见状语短语，意思是"令某人非常……的是"。同型：*Much to my surprise, he arrived on time.* / *Much to her relief, the test was easy.* 这个结构把情感反应放在句首，非常洒脱。
</Callout>

## 3. 关键句型 so... that / insist that / would & used to

<Callout type="tip" title="一分钟回顾">
  本课的核心语法围绕三类句型展开，正好对应教材 Key Structures 的全部练习：

  | 句型 | 课文例句 | 用途 |
  |---|---|---|
  | **so... that** | *Some plays are **so successful that** they run for years on end.* | "如此……以致"——so + 形容词/副词 + that 从句 |
  | **such... that** | — | such + 名词 + that：*It was such a success that ...* |
  | **such a... that** | — | such a(n) + 形容词 + 单数名词 + that |
  | **would (过去习惯)** | *a gaoler **would always come** on to the stage* | 过去反复发生的习惯动作 |
  | **used to (过去状态/习惯)** | — | 过去的持续状态或习惯（现已不……） |
  | **insist that + (should) do** | *He always **insisted that it should be written** out in full.* | 坚持要求做……（虚拟语气） |
  | **suggest / demand that + (should) do** | — | 同样用虚拟语气，should 常省略 |
  | **managed to** | *he **had managed to** learn the contents* | 设法做成（强调成功）= succeeded in doing |

  ### ⚠️ 5 个本课最容易踩的雷区

  1. **so... that** vs **such... that**：so 修饰**形容词/副词**（so successful that）；such 修饰**名词**（such a success that）。He was **so** tired **that** he fell asleep. / It was **such a** good film **that** we saw it twice.
  2. **insist that + 虚拟语气**：insist 作"坚持要求"时，that 从句用 (should) + 动词原形。但 insist 作"坚称（事实）"时不用虚拟：*He insisted that he was innocent.*
  3. **would** vs **used to**：would 只用于**反复发生**的过去动作（不用于状态）；used to 可用于**动作 + 状态**。*He would walk / used to walk to school.* ✓ 但 *He used to be shy.* ✓ vs *He would be shy.* ✗（状态不能用 would）。
  4. **managed to** vs **could**：managed to = "设法办成了"（强调成功结果）；could = "有能力做"（不强调是否做成）。*He managed to escape.*（成功了）vs *He could escape if he wanted to.*（有能力但不一定做了）。
  5. **suggest/insist/demand that + (should) do**：三个动词后从句都用虚拟语气（should + 动词原形，should 可省略）。英式英语尤其倾向于省略 should：*He suggested that she go to the doctor.*（不是 goes）。
</Callout>

## 4. 难点：句法结构串讲 Special difficulties

<Callout type="info" title="教材 Key Structures 练习全解">
  本课 Key structures 给出了 8 个改写/造句练习，覆盖 5 个核心语言点：

  ### 1. so... that / such... that / such a... that（练习 1）

  | 结构 | 公式 | 示例 |
  |---|---|---|
  | **so... that** | so + 形容词/副词 + that 从句 | *The play was **so successful that** it ran for years.* |
  | **such... that** | such + 复数/不可数名词 + that 从句 | *They were **such good actors that** the show never got stale.* |
  | **such a... that** | such a + 形容词 + 单数名词 + that 从句 | *It was **such a success that** tickets were impossible to get.* |

  ### 2. would vs used to（练习 2）

  - **would**：过去**反复**的动作（不用于状态）
  - **used to**：过去的状态或习惯（已不再……）
  - 替换 would 的词：**used to**

  *A gaoler would always come on to the stage.* → *A gaoler always **used to** come on to the stage.*

  ### 3. 连句：现在分词（练习 3 & 6）

  *The noble was expected to read the letter + He always insisted that it ...* → **Although** the noble was expected to read the letter at each performance, he always insisted that it should be written out in full.（课文已用 even though 连接）

  *He entered the cell + He presented the letter* → **He entered the cell and presented the letter**（课文用 and 并列）

  ### 4. insist / suggest / demand that + (should) do（练习 4）

  *He suggested that she (should) leave early.* / *He insisted that the work (should) be finished.* / *He demanded that everyone (should) be present.*

  ### 5. managed to vs could not（练习 5）

  *He **managed to** learn the contents.* = He **succeeded in** learning. ←→ *He **could not** learn the contents.*（反义）

  ### 6. throw at / point at（练习 7）

  **throw at** = 朝……扔（常含敌意）；**point at** = 指着（指方向/对象）。比较：*He threw the ball **to** me.*（传球给我）vs *He threw the ball **at** me.*（拿球砸我）

  ### 7. must → have to（练习 8）

  *I **must** get my glasses.* → *I **have to** / **need to** get my glasses.* （must 的主观必要性 → have to 的客观需要）
</Callout>

## 10. 摘要写作 Summary writing (≤ 80 词)

<Callout type="info" title="任务">
  *In not more than 80 words* describe **what happened after the curtain went up on the final act of the play**. **Do not include anything that is not in the last paragraph**（只用第三段——即最后一幕发生的事情）。

  按以下提示点串成一段：

  1. 幕布升起后展现在观众面前的场景是什么？
  2. 狱卒登场后做了什么（递信——但信的内容不同于往常）？
  3. 贵族如何反应（盯着白纸 → 眯眼 → 说光线太暗 → 请狱卒读）？
  4. 狱卒又是什么反应（记不住 → 说要去拿眼镜 → 匆匆下台）？
  5. 故事的结尾是什么（狱卒回来 → 拿着眼镜和真信 → 念给囚犯听）？
</Callout>

<Callout type="tip" title="参考范文（自己写完再看）">
  After the curtain rose on the final act, the aristocrat sat alone in his dark cell. The gaoler entered and gave him a letter — only this time it was a blank sheet. After staring at it briefly, the noble squinted and said the light was too dim, asking the gaoler to read it instead. The gaoler, unable to remember a word himself, excused himself to fetch his glasses. He returned moments later with the real letter and read it aloud to the prisoner. *(79 words)*
</Callout>

## 11. 作文 Composition (约 250 词)

<Callout type="info" title="任务">
  Write a composition of about **250 words** using the ideas given below. Do not write more than four paragraphs.

  **Title:** *Six Short Weeks*

  **Introduction:** A new play called *The World Tomorrow* to be shown. Highly advertised — public interest — cast of famous actors.

  **Development:** First night — play not well received — supposed to be funny — nobody laughed — people walked out — bad reviews.

  **Conclusion:** The play ran for six weeks — last performance — small audience — actors struggled through — the audience found the last line of the play very funny: *"Our six short weeks have hastened to their end."* Even the actors laughed.
</Callout>

<Callout type="warning" title="提示骨架">
  - **开头 (Introduction)**：介绍新剧 *The World Tomorrow* —— 大肆宣传、万众期待、名演员阵容。
  - **主体 1 (First night)**：首演之夜 —— 剧院满座 → 本以为要笑，结果全场寂静 → 有人中途离场 → 剧评人的贬斥。
  - **主体 2 (The run)**：硬撑了六周 —— 观众越来越少 → 演员越演越丧 → 大家都在数着什么时候"熬到头"。
  - **结尾 (Conclusion)**：最后一场 —— 稀稀拉拉的观众席 → 演员勉强撑完全场 → 最后一句台词 "*Our six short weeks have hastened to their end.*" 所有人都笑了，连演员自己都笑了——**最大的反讽**。
</Callout>

<Callout type="tip" title="写作脚手架">
  - **时态主线**：一般过去时叙事。介绍剧时用过去完成时/过去时；首演描述用过去时。
  - **"宣传/轰动"词库**：*highly advertised, heavily promoted, much anticipated, public curiosity, star-studded cast*。
  - **"失败"词库**：*fell flat, a flop, poorly received, not a single laugh, disappointed faces, people slipped out early, savage reviews, panned by critics*。
  - **"苦撑"词库**：*struggled through, limped along, dwindling audiences, half-empty rows, going through the motions, counting the days*。
  - **结构亮点**：标题 *Six Short Weeks* 本身就是一句台词——在结尾处做"点睛之笔"，正与课文 *"dear" 的双关* 异曲同工。用 **反讽 (irony)** 收尾——六周像六年一样漫长，最后一句台词却说 "六周短暂"。
</Callout>

<Callout type="tip" title="参考范文（自己写完再看）">
  *The World Tomorrow* was the most talked-about play of the season. Weeks before the opening, the papers were full of it. It had everything — a famous director, a star-studded cast, and a publicity campaign that promised "the funniest night you will ever spend in a theatre." On the first night, the house was packed.

  Then the curtain went up. The first scene was received in silence. A joke that was supposed to bring the house down produced not a single laugh. By the interval, people were already slipping out. The next morning, the reviews were savage: "A disaster," one critic wrote, "as funny as a wet weekend in November."

  Yet the play ran on — for six long weeks. Audiences grew thinner by the night; by the third week whole rows stood empty. The actors, once so full of fire, were now merely going through the motions, their eyes fixed on the calendar. Every performance felt like a sentence being served.

  On the final night, a tiny audience sat scattered through the stalls. The actors dragged themselves through the last act like swimmers against a current. Then came the closing line: "Our six short weeks have hastened to their end." A ripple of laughter ran through the house — the first real laugh the play had ever got. And the actors, exhausted and relieved, found themselves laughing too. Six short weeks? They had felt like six long years. *(251 words)*
</Callout>

## 12. 书信写作 Letter writing

<Callout type="info" title="任务">
  Write **five sentences** which could be used to **begin** a letter and **five sentences** which could be used to **end** one.
</Callout>

<Callout type="tip" title="写作脚手架">
  ### 5 种开头句式 (Opening sentences)

  | # | 句式 | 适用场景 |
  |---|---|---|
  | 1 | *Thank you so much for your letter — it was lovely to hear from you.* | 回信（朋友间） |
  | 2 | *I hope this letter finds you well.* | 正式/半正式开篇寒暄 |
  | 3 | *I am writing to let you know that ...* | 直接告知某事 |
  | 4 | *Forgive me for not writing sooner — I have been terribly busy.* | 久未回信的歉意 |
  | 5 | *I was so sorry to hear about ...* | 表达同情/慰问 |

  ### 5 种结尾句式 (Closing sentences)

  | # | 句式 | 适用场景 |
  |---|---|---|
  | 1 | *I look forward to hearing from you soon.* | 正式/半正式 |
  | 2 | *Do write back when you have a moment.* | 朋友间 |
  | 3 | *Once again, thank you for everything.* | 再次致谢 |
  | 4 | *Take care of yourself and give my love to the family.* | 温暖亲切 |
  | 5 | *I had better stop here — the post is about to go.* | 轻松随性收尾 |
</Callout>

<Callout type="tip" title="参考范文（自己写完再看）">
  **Opening sentences:**

  1. Thank you so much for your letter — I was so pleased to hear your news.
  2. I hope this letter finds you well and enjoying the summer.
  3. I am writing to tell you about something rather exciting that happened last week.
  4. Forgive me for not having written sooner; the last few weeks have been a whirlwind.
  5. I was so sorry to hear that you had been unwell, and I do hope you are feeling better now.

  **Closing sentences:**

  1. I look forward to hearing from you when you have a spare moment.
  2. Do write back soon — I'm dying to know how it all turned out.
  3. Once again, thank you from the bottom of my heart for all your help.
  4. Take care of yourself, and give my warmest regards to your parents.
  5. I had better stop here as the dinner bell is about to ring!
</Callout>

## 13. 拓展练习 Going further

<Callout type="tip" title="自学者建议">
  - **影子跟读 (shadowing)**：用第 1 节三个 SayIt 按钮跟读 3-5 遍。尤其注意末句台词的语气——*"The light is indeed dim, sire."* 中 indeed 拉长 + sire 稍带恭敬/讽刺的混合语调，是英式戏剧腔的精华。
  - **复述 (retell)**：合上课文，用 2 分钟把故事讲一遍。**四段叙事线**：背景（名剧连演、演员厌烦）→ 设局（狱卒决定考贵族——给白纸）→ 反转（贵族以光暗为由把信递回）→ 结局（狱卒也记不住、溜去拿眼镜回来后乖乖念信）。
  - **造句迁移**：用今天的 5 个高价值结构造你自己的句子——
    1. *so... that*（如此……以致）—— *The film was so dull that half the audience left before the interval.*
    2. *insist that + (should) do*（坚持要求……）—— *The teacher insisted that every student (should) hand in the assignment on time.*
    3. *would (过去习惯)* —— *Every Sunday, my grandfather would sit in that chair and tell us stories.*
    4. *managed to do*（设法做成了）—— *After three attempts, she managed to start the car.*
    5. *Much to + sb.'s + n.*（令某人非常……的是）—— *Much to my relief, the exam was far easier than I had expected.*
  - **延伸思考**：本课是一部**迷你喜剧**——篇幅短但起承转合俱全。从写作角度看，这篇文章教会我们三点：① **"设局—反转"是叙事最容易出彩的结构**——狱卒是"设局者"→ 反转成"局中人"；② **一句话的台词推着整个情节走**——*"The light is dim. Read the letter to me."* 一句就让立场逆转，干净利落；③ **语法为故事服务**——*insist that ... (should) be written out in full* 这个虚拟语气不是空洞的规则，而是塑造了这位演员"固执又依赖"的性格：明明每场都念同一封信，就是不背。下一次你用英文写故事时，记住这三条：**用转折结构设悬念、用一句台词推情节、用语法细节塑造人物**。
</Callout>
