## The Text 课文

The Scandinavian countries are much admired all over the world for their enlightened social policies. Sweden **has evolved an excellent system** for protecting the individual citizen from high-handed or incompetent public officers. The system has worked so well that it **has been adopted** in other countries too.

The Swedes were the first to recognize that public officials like civil servants, police officers, health inspectors or tax-collectors can make mistakes or **act over-zealously** **in the belief that** they are serving the public. As long ago as 1809, the Swedish Parliament introduced a scheme to **safeguard the interest of the individual**. A parliamentary committee representing all political parties appoints a person who is suitably qualified to investigate private grievances against the State. The official title of the person is "Justitieombudsman", but the Swedes commonly refer to him as the "J.O." or "Ombudsman". The Ombudsman **is not subject to** political pressure. He investigates **complaints large and small** that come to him from all levels of society. As complaints must be made **in writing**, the Ombudsman receives an average of 1,200 letters a year. He has eight lawyer assistants to help him and he examines every single letter in detail. There is nothing secretive about the Ombudsman's work, for his correspondence is open to public inspection. If a citizen's complaint is justified, the Ombudsman will **act on his behalf**. The action he takes **varies according to** the nature of the complaint. He may petition parliament an official or even suggest to parliament that a law be altered. The following case is a typical example of the Ombudsman's work.

A foreigner living in a Swedish village wrote to the Ombudsman complaining that he **had been ill-treated** by the police, simply because he was a foreigner. The Ombudsman immediately wrote to the Chief of Police in the district asking him to send a record of the case. There was nothing in the record that showed that the foreigner's complaint was justified and the Chief of Police strongly denied the accusation. It was impossible for the Ombudsman to take action, but when he received a similar complaint from another foreigner in the same village, he immediately sent one of his lawyers to investigate the matter. The lawyer ascertained that a policeman had indeed **dealt roughly with** foreigners on several occasions. **The fact that** the policeman was prejudiced against foreigners could not be recorded in the official files. It was only possible for the Ombudsman to find this out by sending one of his representatives to check the facts. The policeman in question was severely reprimanded and was informed that **if any further complaints were lodged against him, he would be prosecuted**. The Ombudsman's prompt action at once **put an end to** an unpleasant practice which might have gone unnoticed.

## 课文注释 Notes on the text

<Callout type="info" title="7 个值得停下来嚼一嚼的点">
  1. **in the interest(s) of ... / act on sb's behalf** (l.9, l.17) — \"为了……的利益 / 代表某人\"。*safeguard the interest of the individual* = 维护个人利益；*act on his behalf* = 代他（公民）出面。本课标题 *In the public interest* 即\"为了公众的利益\"。同型：*in the interest of safety*（为了安全）/ *speak on behalf of the team*（代表全队发言）。
  2. **in the belief that ...** (l.8) — \"以为……、自以为……\"。**belief 后接 that 同位语从句**，解释\"信念的内容\"。课文 *act over-zealously in the belief that they are serving the public* = 自以为在为公众服务而过分热心。同型：*He lied in the belief that no one would find out.*
  3. **be subject to ...** (l.12) — \"受……支配 / 易遭受……\"。**subject 在此是形容词**。课文 *is not subject to political pressure* = 不受政治压力左右。同型：*Prices are subject to change. / The plan is subject to approval.*
  4. **complaints large and small** (l.13) — **形容词作后置定语**。large and small 放在名词后，等于 *complaints, both large and small*（大大小小的投诉）。这种\"形容词成对、后置\"的写法很书面。同型：*problems old and new / people young and old*。
  5. **in writing** (l.13) — \"以书面形式\"。固定短语，介词用 **in**。同型：*put it in writing*（写下来）/ *confirm in writing*（书面确认）。课文用它解释\"为什么 Ombudsman 一年收 1,200 封信\"——因为投诉必须书面提交。
  6. **vary according to ...** (l.17) — \"随……而变化 / 视……而定\"。课文 *The action he takes varies according to the nature of the complaint* = 他采取的行动视投诉的性质而定。同型：*Prices vary according to season.*
  7. **put an end to ...** (l.30) — \"结束、制止……\"。**end 前用 an，介词用 to**，后接名词。课文 *put an end to an unpleasant practice* = 制止了一桩不愉快的恶习。同型：*put an end to the rumour / bring sth. to an end*。
</Callout>

## 参考译文 Chinese reference

<Callout type="tip" title="先读原文，遇到读不懂的句子再回来对照">
  斯堪的纳维亚半岛各国实行开明的社会政策，受到全世界的推崇。瑞典已经建立了一整套保护个人不受专横或不称职的政府官员伤害的卓越制度。这种制度行之有效，已经被其他国家采纳。

  瑞典人最先认识到，政府官员，如公务员、警官、卫生稽查员、税务人员等，也会犯错误，或者自以为在为公众服务而采取过激行为。早在 1809 年，瑞典议会就建立了一个保护个人利益的制度。一个代表各政党的议会委员会任命一位有资格调查公民对国家的不满的人。这个人的正式职称是\"司法特派员\"，但瑞典人一般称之为\"J.O.\"或\"Ombudsman\"。司法特派员不受任何政治压力的制约。他受理民间各种大大小小的投诉。由于投诉均须用书面形式，司法特派员每年平均收到 1,200 封信。他有 8 名律师助手协助工作，每封信他都逐封仔细审阅。司法特派员的工作没有什么秘密可言，因为他的来往信件是公开供公众检查的。如果某位公民的投诉合情合理，司法特派员就会出面为他说话。他所采取的行动则根据投诉的性质而定。他可以向议会检举某位官员，甚至建议议会修改某项法律。下面的案例就是司法特派员工作的一个典型例子。

  一个住在瑞典乡村的外国人写信给司法特派员，抱怨说他受到警察的虐待，只是因为他是个外国人。司法特派员立即写信给当地警察局局长，请他寄送有关此案的材料。材料中没有任何文字说明这个外国人的投诉是合情合理的，警察局局长矢口否认这一指控。司法特派员无法采取行动，但是，当他又收到同一村庄另一个外国人的类似投诉时，他立即派出一位律师前去调查。律师证实那个警察确实曾几次粗暴地对待外国人。警察对外国人的偏见是无法记录在官方档案中的，司法特派员只有派他的代表去核实事实真相才能弄清这一情况。当事的警察受到严厉斥责，并被告知，如果再有人投诉他，他将受到起诉。司法特派员及时采取的行动迅速制止了这一起不愉快的事件，不然这件事可能因未得到人们的注意而不了了之。
</Callout>

## 3. 关键句型 被动 · 条件句 · 强调句 The grammar engine of this lesson

<Callout type="tip" title="一分钟回顾">
  本课是一篇\"制度说明文\"，要客观、要严谨，于是密集使用了三大句法工具。把它们认全，本课的语法题就拿下了一大半：

  | 句型 | 课文例句 | 要点 |
  |---|---|---|
  | **被动语态** | *it **has been adopted** ... / complaints **must be made** in writing / he **had been ill-treated** / could not **be recorded** / he **would be prosecuted*** | 强调\"动作承受者\"、不点明施动者——制度性文本的标配 |
  | **第二类条件句（与现在/将来相反或一般条件）** | *If a citizen's complaint **is** justified, the Ombudsman **will** act ... / **if** any further complaints **were lodged**, he **would be** prosecuted* | if + 过去式 → 主句 would + 动词原形 |
  | **第三类条件句（与过去相反）** | *If the Ombudsman **hadn't acted** promptly, this practice **might have continued*** | if + had done → 主句 might/would have done |
  | **强调句 / It was ... who ...** | ***It was the Swedes who** first recognized ...*（由 *The Swedes were the first to recognize* 改写） | It was + 被强调部分 + who/that + 其余 |
  | **the fact that + 从句** | ***The fact that** the policeman was prejudiced ... could not be recorded*（由 *The policeman's prejudice ...* 改写） | 名词 + that 同位语从句，把短语\"升级\"成从句作主语 |
  | **suggest / petition that + (should) + 动词原形** | *suggest to parliament that a law **be altered*** | 建议类动词后从句用虚拟（动词原形） |

  ### ⚠️ 4 个本课最容易踩的雷区

  1. **be altered 不是漏了助动词**：*suggest that a law (should) be altered* —— suggest / demand / insist / order 等\"建议、要求\"类动词后的 that 从句用 **(should) + 动词原形**，这里省略了 should，所以是 *be altered* 而不是 *is altered*。
  2. **第三类条件句的标志是 had done + would/might have done**：*If the Ombudsman hadn't acted ..., this practice might have continued*。千万别写成 *might continue*（那是第二类）或 *will continue*（那是真实条件）。
  3. **the fact that ≠ the fact which**：同位语从句用 **that**（that 不充当成分、不可省）；不能用 which。*The fact that he was late* ✓ / *The fact which he was late* ✗。
  4. **was prejudiced against**：prejudice 作动词常用被动 *be prejudiced against sb.*（对……抱有偏见），介词是 **against**，不是 to。
</Callout>

## 4. 难点：名词短语 ⇄ 从句 的互改 Special difficulties

<Callout type="info" title="把“名词短语”拆成“从句”——书面英语的核心功力">
  教材 Special difficulties 让你把 *The policeman's **prejudice against** foreigners could not be recorded* 改写成 ***The fact that** the policeman **was prejudiced against** foreigners could not be recorded*。这考的是一项关键功夫：**名词短语 ⇄ that 从句**的双向转换。掌握它，你就能在\"简洁\"和\"清楚\"之间自由切换。

  | 名词化短语（名词 + of / 's） | 还原成 the fact that + 从句 |
  |---|---|
  | the policeman's **prejudice** against foreigners | the fact that the policeman **was prejudiced** against foreigners |
  | his **refusal** to apologize | the fact that he **refused** to apologize |
  | the **disappearance** of the box | the fact that the box **had disappeared** |
  | their **arrival** late at night | the fact that they **arrived** late at night |

  ### ⚠️ 三步还原法

  1. 找到**核心名词**（prejudice / refusal / arrival …），换回它的**动词或形容词**形式（be prejudiced / refuse / arrive）。
  2. 把短语里的**所有格 / of 短语**还原成**主语**（the policeman's → the policeman；of the box → the box）。
  3. 在前面补上 **the fact that**（或直接用 that 从句作主语），时态按语境补全。

  ### 反向：把从句\"压缩\"成名词短语（让句子更紧凑）

  *That the policeman was prejudiced surprised no one.* → ***The policeman's prejudice** surprised no one.* —— 正式书面、新闻标题尤其爱用名词化。
</Callout>

  按事件顺序串成一段：
  1. 外国人来信投诉什么？
  2. Ombudsman 先做了什么（写信给谁、要什么）？

  4. 什么事让局面改变（第二封类似投诉）？
  5. 他派谁去、查到了什么？
  6. 警察最终受到什么处理？
</Callout>

<Callout type="tip" title="参考范文（自己写完再看）">

## 11. 作文 Composition (约 300 词)

<Callout type="info" title="任务">
  Write a composition in about **300 words** on one of the following:

  - **(a)** Explain whether the Ombudsman institution **is or would be useful in your country**.
  - **(b)** Describe **the work of any one** of the following: a policeman, a civil servant, a health officer, a social worker.
</Callout>

<Callout type="warning" title="提示骨架（以 (a) 为例）">
  - **开头**：用一句话点出问题——普通公民对抗庞大的官僚机器时往往无能为力；引出 Ombudsman 这一\"申冤人\"角色。
  - **主体 1（它解决什么痛点）**：列举本国公民可能遇到的\"high-handed or incompetent\"官员问题——办事拖拉、推诿、徇私、偏见。
  - **主体 2（它如何运作的优点）**：独立、不受政治压力、免费、书面受理、公开透明、可调查可建议改法律。
  - **主体 3（可能的障碍 / 反方）**：是否会与现有信访、监察、法院制度重叠？人手是否够？官员是否配合？
  - **结尾（你的判断）**：在……条件下它会有用 / 需要哪些配套才能落地。
</Callout>

<Callout type="tip" title="写作脚手架">
  - **\"独立公正\"词库**：*impartial, independent, free from political pressure, answerable to no party, safeguard the interest of the individual*。
  - **\"官员失职\"词库**：*high-handed, incompetent, over-zealous, red tape, abuse of power, prejudice, negligence*。
  - **\"受理与调查\"动词库**：*investigate grievances, act on a citizen's behalf, look into a complaint, ascertain the facts, reprimand, recommend that a law be altered*。
  - **句型亮点**：① 真实条件 *If a citizen's complaint is justified, the body will act on his behalf.* ② the fact that *The fact that an official is biased is hard to prove on paper.* ③ 虚拟建议 *I would suggest that such an office be set up.* ④ 第三类条件 *If such a system had existed earlier, many wrongs might have been put right.*
</Callout>

<Callout type="tip" title="参考范文（自己写完再看）">
  An ordinary citizen who is wronged by a careless or high-handed official often feels powerless. Courts are slow and expensive, and complaints to the department concerned are usually handled by the very people who caused the trouble. For this reason, I believe an Ombudsman would be genuinely useful in my country.

  The greatest value of such an office lies in its independence. Because the Ombudsman is not subject to political pressure and answers to no single party, citizens can trust that their grievances will be judged on the facts alone. Complaints would be free and made in writing, so there would be a clear record. The Ombudsman could investigate red tape, negligence and the abuse of power, act on the citizen's behalf, and, where necessary, recommend that a law be altered. Cases that no court would bother with — a rude clerk, a lost file, a small injustice — would finally have somewhere to go.

  There are, of course, difficulties. Such an office might overlap with bodies that already exist, and a single Ombudsman could never read 1,200 letters a year without a team of trained assistants. Officials might also resent being investigated and could be slow to hand over records, exactly as the Chief of Police was in the Swedish case.

  Even so, the experience of Sweden, where the institution has worked since 1809, suggests these problems can be overcome. The fact that the system has since been adopted in many other countries is itself strong evidence. If such an office were set up here, properly staffed and given real authority, it would protect the individual and remind every official that power is held in the public interest, not above it. *(about 280 words)*
</Callout>

## 12. 书信写作 Letter writing (约 100 词)

<Callout type="info" title="任务">
  Write an imaginary letter of about **100 words** to an Ombudsman **complaining about postal services in your district**. Begin **\"Dear Sir\"** and end **\"Yours faithfully\"**.
</Callout>

<Callout type="tip" title="写作脚手架">
  - **称呼 / 落款**：正式投诉、收信人不具名 → 用 **Dear Sir** 开头、**Yours faithfully** 结尾（与 Dear Sir 配对，不能用 Yours sincerely）。
  - **第 1 段（缘由）**：表明身份 + 投诉对象 —— *I am writing to complain about the poor postal service in ...*
  - **第 2 段（具体事实，越具体越有力）**：举 1-2 个例证 + 时间/频率 —— 信件延误数日、包裹丢失、投递不到、邮局排长队。注意用**被动**显得客观：*letters are frequently delayed / parcels have been lost*。
  - **第 3 段（诉求）**：请求调查 + 改进 —— *I should be grateful if you would look into the matter and ensure that ...*
  - **时态**：现状用一般现在（被动）；已发生的具体事用现在完成 / 一般过去；诉求用 *would / should be grateful*。
</Callout>

<Callout type="tip" title="参考范文（自己写完再看）">
  Dear Sir,

  I am writing to complain about the postal service in the Riverside district, which has become unacceptably poor over the past three months. Letters posted locally now take five or six days to arrive, and on two occasions registered parcels addressed to me have been lost altogether. When I raised the matter at the local post office, my complaint was politely noted but nothing was done.

  As this service affects every household in the area, I should be grateful if you would investigate the matter and ensure that deliveries are made promptly and reliably in future.

  Yours faithfully,

  J. Lindqvist *(about 100 words)*
</Callout>

## 13. 拓展练习 Going further

<Callout type="tip" title="自学者建议">
  - **影子跟读 (shadowing)**：用第 1 节的三个 SayIt 按钮各跟读 3-5 遍。本课句子长、从句多，重点练\"意群停顿\"——在每个逗号、每个 that/which 之前自然换气，比如 *He investigates complaints large and small | that come to him | from all levels of society.*
  - **复述 (retell)**：合上课文，用 2 分钟讲清两件事。**第一段**：Ombudsman 制度是什么（为保护个人、1809 年设立、各党委员会任命、不受政治压力、书面受理、公开透明）。**第二段**：那桩外国人案怎么办成的（投诉 → 要档案 → 查无实据 → 第二封投诉 → 派人核实 → 警察被严厉斥责 + 警告起诉）。
  - **造句迁移**：用今天的 5 个高价值结构造你自己的句子——
    1. *S + have/has + been + 过去分词*（被动 + 现在完成）—— *This method has been adopted in many schools.*
    2. *If + 一般现在, S + will + 动词原形*（真实条件）—— *If your complaint is justified, we will act at once.*
    3. *If + had done, S + might/would have done*（第三类条件）—— *If we had checked earlier, the mistake might have been avoided.*
    4. *The fact that + 从句 + 谓语*（同位语从句作主语）—— *The fact that he apologized surprised everyone.*
    5. *suggest / insist + that + (should) + 动词原形*（虚拟）—— *I suggest that the rule be changed.*
  - **延伸思考**：本课语言上是\"**被动语态 + 条件句 + 名词化从句**\"的范本——这正是一切**正式、客观、制度性**英语（法律、报告、新闻、学术）的底色。当你想让文字显得\"不带个人情绪、只陈述事实\"时，就多用被动（*complaints must be made*）、多用 the fact that 把判断\"客观化\"。内容上更值得回味：一个真正\"开明\"的制度，不是假设官员永远正确，而是**坦然承认\"官员也会犯错\"，并为此预留一条申冤的通道**——这就是标题 *In the public interest（为了公众的利益）* 的全部分量。
</Callout>
