## The Text 课文

In 1908 Lord Northcliffe offered a prize of £1,000 to the first man **who would fly across the English Channel**. Over a year passed before the first attempt was made. On July 19th, 1909, in the early morning, Hubert Latham took off from the French coast in his plane the 'Antoinette IV'. **He had travelled only seven miles across the Channel when his engine failed** and he was forced to land on the sea. The 'Antoinette' floated on the water until Latham was picked up by a ship.

Two days later, Louis Bleriot arrived near Calais with a plane called 'No. XI'. **Bleriot had been making planes since 1905** and this was his latest model. A week before, **he had completed a successful overland flight during which he covered twenty-six miles**. Latham, however, did not give up easily. He, too, arrived near Calais on the same day with a new 'Antoinette'. **It looked as if there would be an exciting race across the Channel.** Both planes were going to take off on July 25th, but Latham failed to get up early enough. After making a short test flight at 4.15 a.m., Bleriot set off half an hour later. His great flight lasted thirty-seven minutes. When he landed near Dover, the first person to greet him was a local policeman. Latham made another attempt a week later and **got within half a mile of Dover**, but he was unlucky again. His engine failed and he landed on the sea for the second time.

## 课文注释 Notes on the text

<Callout type="info" title="6 个值得停下来嚼一嚼的点">
  1. **the English Channel** (l.2) — \"英吉利海峡\"。大西洋延伸的部分，长约 350 英里（约 560 公里），将英法两国领土分开。**Channel** 首字母大写专指英吉利海峡；小写 channel = 频道 / 水道 / 渠道。
  2. **Over a year passed before the first attempt was made** (l.2-3) — \"一年多时间之后，才有人进行第一次尝试\"。**Over** = more than（超过）；**before** 在这里译作"才"——\"在……之前过去了一年多\" = \"过了一年多才……\"。这是英语的**反向叙述**：A passed before B happened = B 直到 A 之后才发生。
  3. **pick up** (l.7) — 此处作 \"(从海里) 救起\" 解。**pick up** 是高频多义动词：捡起 / 接 (人) / 学会 (一门语言) / 听到 (信号) / 整理 / 救起。课文 *Latham was picked up by a ship* = 被一艘路过的船救起。
  4. **did not give up easily** (l.10) — \"不轻易作罢\"。**give up** 当 \"放弃\" 讲，是 phrasal verb。后接 V-ing：*give up smoking / give up trying*。**easily** 修饰整个动词词组——\"不会轻易就放弃\"。
  5. **It looked as if there would be an exciting race across the Channel** (l.11-12) — \"看起来似乎有一场精彩的飞越英吉利海峡的比赛\"。**as if 引导的从句**作起系动词作用的 look 的表语，其中谓语动词 **would be** 表示**与未来事实不相符的虚拟结构**——\"看上去会……（但未必真发生）\"。
  6. **got within half a mile of Dover** (l.15) — \"到了离多佛不到半英里的地方\"。**within + 距离 + of + 地点** = 距某地……以内。同型：*within a stone's throw of / within walking distance of / within sight of*。
  7. **Dover** (l.14) — 多佛港，英吉利海峡北岸一英国港口，对面正是法国港口 **Calais**（加来）。多佛与加来相距 21 英里（34 公里），是英吉利海峡最窄的部分——所以两位飞行员都选这条路线。
</Callout>

## 3. 关键句型 Past perfect tense 过去完成时（含进行体 + 被动）

<Callout type="tip" title="一分钟回顾">
  本课延续上一组课文的**过去完成时**主题，新增**过去完成进行时**（had been V-ing）——表示"持续到过去某一刻"的动作或状态。课文里 5 次 had-结构都指向"那个时间点之前已经发生 / 已经持续的事"：

  - *He **had travelled** only seven miles across the Channel when his engine failed.*（飞了 7 英里**到**引擎熄火那一刻——过去完成）
  - *Bleriot **had been making** planes since 1905 and this was his latest model.*（从 1905 年**持续做**飞机到 1909 年——过去完成进行）
  - *A week before, he **had completed** a successful overland flight ...*（更早一周已完成——过去完成）
  - *during which he **covered** twenty-six miles*（在那次飞行中飞了 26 英里——一般过去）

  ### 过去完成时 vs 过去完成进行时

  | 形式 | 强调 | 例子 |
  |---|---|---|
  | **had + V-ed** | 在过去某点**之前完成** | *He **had completed** the flight by then.* |
  | **had been + V-ing** | 在过去某点之前**持续进行** | *He **had been making** planes since 1905.* |

  ### "before / when" 翻译陷阱

  课文 l.2-3：*Over a year passed **before** the first attempt was made.*
  字面：在第一次尝试之前过了一年多。
  自然译：**过了一年多才有人进行第一次尝试**。

  英语 \"A happened before B\" 在中文常常要翻成 \"**B 之前 A 已经过去 / B 才 ……**\"——别死译成\"在 B 之前 A\"。

  ### ⚠️ 4 个常见雷区

  1. **持续 vs 完成**：since 1905 一定要配**完成**（进行）时——*had been making since 1905* / *had made twenty planes since 1905*；不能用一般过去时 *made since 1905*。
  2. **被动语态**：had + been + V-ed（had been built / had been damaged）；这与过去完成进行时 *had been V-ing* 长得很像，**靠后面是 V-ed 还是 V-ing 区分**。
  3. **when 在课文中是 "结果\"而非 \"当……时\"**：*He had travelled only seven miles when his engine failed* = 他**才**飞了 7 英里**就**……（when ≈ and then）。这是英语高级表达。
  4. **as if + would V**：*It looked as if there **would be** an exciting race* —— 表"与未来事实不相符的虚拟"，而非 \"will be\"。
</Callout>

## 10. 摘要写作 Summary writing (≤ 80 词)

<Callout type="info" title="任务">
  **In not more than 80 words** describe **the attempts made by Bleriot and Latham to fly across the Channel from the time when they both arrived at Calais**. **Do not include anything that is not in the last paragraph**（仅用 l.8 起的内容）。

  按下面 9 个提示点串成一段：

  1. On what date did Bleriot and Latham arrive at Calais?
  2. Did it look as if there would be a race or not?
  3. When would it take place?
  4. Why did Latham not take part in the race?
  5. Did Bleriot make a short test flight before setting out or not?
  6. How long did it take him to fly across the Channel?
  7. Who greeted him when he arrived at Dover?
  8. How near to Dover did Latham fly the following week?
  9. Why did he have to land on the sea for the second time?
</Callout>

<Callout type="tip" title="参考范文（自己写完再看）">
  On July 21st, 1909, Bleriot and Latham both arrived near Calais, and it looked as if there would be a race across the Channel on July 25th. Latham failed to get up early enough. After a short test flight at 4.15 a.m., Bleriot set off and reached Dover in thirty-seven minutes, where a local policeman greeted him. A week later Latham got within half a mile of Dover, but his engine failed again and he landed on the sea. *(80 words)*
</Callout>

## 11. 作文 Composition (约 200 词扩写)

<Callout type="info" title="任务">
  **Imagine yourself in Bleriot's position.** In not more than **200 words**, write a **first-person account of the flight across the Channel**. Use the ideas given below. **Do not write more than three paragraphs.**

  **Title:** *My flight across the Channel.*
</Callout>

<Callout type="warning" title="提示骨架（每个省略号都得自己填）">
  - **Introduction**: Early morning — no sign of Latham — test flight — all well.
  - **Development**: Started off — could no longer see ship following below — suddenly alone — worried about direction — sea and sky — high winds — engine very hot — it began to rain — rain cooled engine — land ahead.
  - **Conclusion**: Flew in a circle — looked for a place to land — on field — two minutes later: policeman: bonjour!
</Callout>

<Callout type="tip" title="写作脚手架">
  - **时态主线**：一般过去时叙事（I took off / I climbed / I landed）；过去完成时表更早事件（the test flight **had gone** well）；过去进行时表背景（the sea **was darkening** below）。
  - **天空与海词库**：*the grey dawn / a low mist over the Channel / the sea darkening below / the horizon a thin silver line / sky and sea blending into one*。
  - **机械的紧张感**：*the engine roared / the engine grew warmer and warmer / oil splattered my goggles / the propeller hummed steadily / a faint trembling in the wings*。
  - **第一人称内心戏**：*I had no compass to guide me / I felt utterly alone / for one terrible moment I thought I was lost / then a dark line on the horizon — England!*
  - **结尾的反高潮**：飞越历史性的一刻，迎接你的不是欢呼而是一位警察的 \"Bonjour\"——把这份**英式幽默**留到最后一句。
</Callout>

<Callout type="tip" title="参考范文（自己写完再看）">
  Early on July 25th, I rose before dawn. There was no sign of Latham — perhaps he had overslept. At 4.15 a.m. I made a short test flight: the engine ran sweetly, the rudder answered, all was well. Half an hour later I lifted 'No. XI' off the French coast and turned her nose towards England.

  At first I could see a small ship steaming below to mark my course, but within minutes she had vanished behind me and I was suddenly alone above the grey water. I had no compass, and the wind tossed me from side to side. The engine grew dangerously hot; I began to fear it would seize at any moment. Then, almost as a miracle, it started to rain — heavy drops that cooled the cylinders and steadied my fears. Far ahead, a dark line appeared on the horizon. Land!

  I circled twice over the cliffs, searching for a flat patch of grass, and at last brought her down in a meadow not far from Dover. The propeller stopped, the wind dropped, the world went quiet. Two minutes later a tall policeman strode towards me through the wet grass and said simply, \"Bonjour, monsieur.\" *(199 words)*
</Callout>

## 13. 拓展练习 Going further

<Callout type="tip" title="自学者建议">
  - **影子跟读 (shadowing)**：用第 1 节的三个 SayIt 按钮跟读 3-5 遍。重点感受 *had travelled* / *had been making* / *would be* 这三种**复合时态**的连读——\"had been making\" 几乎吞成 \"d-bin-MAKE-ing\"，整体像一个单词。
  - **复述 (retell)**：合上课文，用 3 分钟把 *Pioneer pilots* 讲一遍。**叙事三段**：背景（1908 年悬赏 + Latham 首飞失败）→ 竞争（Bleriot 到达加来 + Latham 也到 + 准备 7 月 25 日比赛）→ 结局（Bleriot 37 分钟横渡成功 + 警察迎接 + Latham 第二次失败）。
  - **造句迁移**：用今天的 5 个高价值结构造你自己的句子——
    1. *S + had + V-ed + when + 一般过去时*（才……就……）—— *I had walked only ten steps when it started to rain.*
    2. *S + had been + V-ing + since + 起点*（持续到过去某点）—— *She had been studying English since 2010 when she went to the U.S.*
    3. *A passed before B happened*（过了 A 才 B）—— *Three months passed before he heard from her.*
    4. *It looked as if + would V*（看似将……）—— *It looked as if it would rain at any moment.*
    5. *the first / only / last + N + to V*（第一个 / 唯一 / 最后……的）—— *She was the only person to remember my birthday.*
  - **延伸思考**：本课与上一组的 *Loss of the Titanic* 一起，构成新概念三册讲**过去完成时**的"姊妹篇"——一篇是 \"had + V-ed\"（事件之前的事件），一篇是 \"had been + V-ing\"（持续到过去某点的动作）。下次你写英文叙事时，**先画一条时间轴**：主线事件用一般过去时，主线之前已完成的用 had + V-ed，主线之前一直在进行的用 had been + V-ing。三条时间线交织，你的英文就有了**电影般的层次感**。
  - **历史小贴士**：Louis Bleriot 1909 年的这次横渡只用了 **37 分钟**，从此打破了\"英国是岛国，靠海与欧洲隔绝\"的军事神话——一战前的英国军方据说在飞机降落后立即重新评估了国防战略。一架小小的单翼机，改变了一个帝国的战略思维——这就是 *pioneer* 一词最重的含义。
</Callout>
