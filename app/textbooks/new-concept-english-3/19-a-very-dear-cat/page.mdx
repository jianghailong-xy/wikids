## The Text 课文

Kidnappers are rarely interested in animals, but they recently **took considerable interest** in Mrs. Eleanor Ramsay's cat. Mrs. Eleanor Ramsay, a very wealthy old lady, **has shared** a flat with her cat, Rastus, for a great many years. Rastus leads an orderly life. He usually **takes a short walk** in the evenings and is always **home by seven o'clock**. One evening, however, he failed to arrive. Mrs. Ramsay got very worried. She looked everywhere for him but could not find him.

Three days after Rastus' disappearance, Mrs. Ramsay **received an anonymous letter**. The writer stated that Rastus **was in safe hands** and **would be returned** immediately if Mrs. Ramsay **paid a ransom** of £1,000. Mrs. Ramsay **was instructed** to place the money in a cardboard box and to leave it outside her door. At first, she decided to go to the police, but **fearing that she would never see Rastus again** — the letter made that quite clear — she **changed her mind**. She withdrew £1,000 from her bank and followed the kidnapper's instructions. The next morning, the box **had disappeared** but Mrs. Ramsay was sure that the kidnapper would keep his word. Sure enough, Rastus arrived **punctually at seven o'clock** that evening. He looked very well, though he was rather thirsty, for he drank half a bottle of milk. The police were astounded when Mrs. Ramsay told them what she had done. She explained that Rastus was very **dear** to her. **Considering the amount she paid, he was dear in more ways than one!**

## 课文注释 Notes on the text

<Callout type="info" title="6 个值得停下来嚼一嚼的点">
  1. **share ... with ...** (l.2) — \"与……合住 / 合用……\"。**share** 后接\"被分享的东西\"，**with** 后接\"分享对象\"。课文 *has shared a flat with her cat* = 与她的猫合住一间公寓。同型：*share a room with my sister / share the same opinion with him*。**has shared + for a great many years** 是**现在完成时**——动作从过去持续到现在。
  2. **be in safe hands** (l.11) — \"安全；落在可靠的人手里\"。**hand 复数 + safe** 是固定隐喻，相当于 *be safe / be well looked after*。同型：*The company is in good hands. / Leave it in my hands.*。课文里绑架者用这个短语来\"安抚\"——一种讽刺。
  3. **be instructed to + V** (l.12) — \"被指示去做……\"。**instruct** 比 *tell* 更正式，常含权威色彩。课文是**被动语态**——绑架者发号施令，Mrs. Ramsay 只能服从。同型：*The students were instructed to remain silent. / I was instructed to wait outside.*
  4. **fearing that ...** (l.13) — **现在分词短语作原因 / 伴随状语**——\"由于担心……\"。等于 *because she feared that ...* 但更紧凑。课文 *fearing that she would never see Rastus again* 解释了她为什么改变主意。同型：*Knowing he was wrong, he apologized. / Hoping for the best, she signed the contract.*
  5. **keep one's word** (l.15) — \"信守诺言\"。固定搭配。同型：*break one's word*（食言）/ *give one's word*（许诺）/ *be as good as one's word*（言出必行）。
  6. **considering ... + 主句** (l.17) — **considering** 在这里是介词 / 连词，作\"考虑到……\"或\"就……而言\"解，常作**原因状语**。后接名词或从句（含 the amount she paid 这种**省略 that 的定语从句**）。**dear** 一语双关——既是\"心爱的\"，又是\"昂贵的\"（dear = expensive 是英式英语）。**in more ways than one** = \"在不止一个层面上\"。同型：*Considering his age, he ran fast. / Considering she's new, she's done well.*
</Callout>

## 3. 关键句型 Review of verb forms 动词时态全景复习

<Callout type="tip" title="一分钟回顾">
  本课是英语教材里复习**动词时态**的经典案例——一篇短文里涵盖了 **8 种时态/语态**，是一次\"小型时态阅兵\"。把课文动词圈出来，你会看到这样的分布：

  | 时态 / 形式 | 课文例句 | 用途 |
  |---|---|---|
  | **一般现在时** | *Kidnappers **are** rarely interested ... / Rastus **leads** an orderly life.* | 普遍事实、习惯 |
  | **现在完成时** | *Mrs. Ramsay **has shared** a flat with her cat ... for a great many years.* | 过去到现在持续 |
  | **一般过去时** | *Mrs. Ramsay **got** very worried. / He **failed** to arrive.* | 主线叙事 |
  | **过去完成时** | *The next morning, the box **had disappeared** ...* | 过去之过去 |
  | **过去将来时** | *Rastus would be returned ... she **would never see** Rastus again ...* | 过去时间点之后的事 |
  | **被动语态** | *was instructed / would be returned / were astounded* | 受动者作主语 |
  | **不定式 / 动名词** | *decided **to go** to the police / kept his word* | 非谓语 |
  | **现在分词作状语** | ***Fearing** that she would never see Rastus again, she ...* / ***Considering** the amount she paid, ...* | 原因 / 伴随 |

  ### ⚠️ 4 个本课最容易踩的雷区

  1. **has shared + for ... years**：现在完成时配 *for + 时间段* —— 强调\"持续到现在\"。如果用一般过去 *shared*，就暗示\"现在不再合住了\"。
  2. **would + V 原形**（过去将来时）：从\"过去时间点\"看\"之后的动作\"。绑架信里的 *Rastus would be returned* / 改变主意时的 *she would never see Rastus again*——都是站在\"那一刻\"看未来。
  3. **fearing / considering** 作**现在分词短语作状语**：主语必须与主句主语一致。*fearing that ..., **she** changed her mind* ✓；*Considering ..., **he** was dear* ✓（语义上"我们考虑到……他显得贵"，但课文里 considering 已经介词化，主语限制松动）。
  4. **过去完成时只在需要\"过去之过去\"时才用**：课文 *the box had disappeared* —— 因为是\"第二天早晨\"发现的，而盒子消失发生在\"早晨之前的某个时间点\"，所以用 had + V-ed。
</Callout>

## 4. 难点：The comma 逗号的用法

<Callout type="info" title="5 类必须打逗号的场景">
  课文的 *Special Difficulties* 部分讲了 5 种**必须用逗号**的句式。逗号不是\"语感停顿\"，而是**句法标记**——错一个就读错一层意思：

  | 类型 | 例句（教材原例） | 规则 |
  |---|---|---|
  | **状语从句在前** | *After we **had visited** the market, we returned home.* | 主从句倒置时，**从句末尾必须加逗号** |
  | **同位语** | *Mr. Griffiths, **the Prime Minister**, said ...* | 同位语前后用**两个逗号**（像一对夹子） |
  | **列举三项以上** | *I bought pens, pencils and paper.* | 项与项之间用逗号；末项前**英式可不加**逗号（牛津逗号是美式风格） |
  | **插入语 / 转折语** | *There were, **however**, hundreds of people there.* | however / therefore / for example 等插入语**前后**各加一逗号 |
  | **非限定性定语从句** | *The small boat, **which took eleven weeks to cross the Atlantic**, arrived ...* | which / who 引导的非限定性从句**前后**各加一逗号；限定性从句则不加 |

  ### ⚠️ 课文里的高密度逗号示例

  *Mrs. Eleanor Ramsay**,** a very wealthy old lady**,** has shared a flat with her cat**,** Rastus**,** for a great many years.*

  这一句里 **4 个逗号**：第 1-2 对夹同位语 *a very wealthy old lady*；第 3-4 对夹同位语 *Rastus*（猫的名字是\"她的猫\"的同位语）。

  ### 5 个本课常见雷区

  1. 限定性 vs 非限定性 — *The boy who came yesterday is my brother*（不加逗号；限定 \"哪个男孩\"） vs *My brother, who came yesterday, is a doctor*（加逗号；插入说明）。
  2. 插入语 *however* 前后**必须双逗号**；句首单 however 后只加一个逗号。
  3. 列举不能用逗号代替 and 连接最后一项：*pens, pencils**,** paper* ✗（错，应是 *pens, pencils and paper*）。
  4. 主谓之间**绝不能加逗号**：*Mrs. Ramsay**,** has shared ...* ✗。
  5. 时间 / 条件状语**在句中**可不加逗号，但**置于句首时必须加**。
</Callout>

## 10. 摘要写作 Summary writing (≤ 80 词)

<Callout type="info" title="任务">
  *In not more than 80 words* describe **how Mrs. Ramsay's cat, Rastus, was returned to her**. **Do not include anything that is not in the last paragraph**（仅用第二段开始的内容）。

  按下面 9 个提示点串成一段：

  1. When did Mrs. Ramsay receive an anonymous letter?
  2. How much money did the kidnapper demand for the return of the cat?
  3. What would happen if she went to the police?
  4. Where did she have to put the money?
  5. Where did she have to put the box?
  6. How much did she draw from the bank?
  7. Did she act on the kidnapper's instructions or not?
  8. Had the money disappeared the following morning or not?
  9. When did Rastus return to Mrs. Ramsay?
</Callout>

<Callout type="tip" title="参考范文（自己写完再看）">
  Three days after Rastus disappeared, Mrs. Ramsay received an anonymous letter demanding a ransom of £1,000. If she went to the police, she would never see her cat again. She was told to leave the money in a cardboard box outside her door. She withdrew £1,000 from the bank and followed the instructions. The next morning the box had gone. Sure enough, at seven o'clock that evening, Rastus arrived home punctually, thirsty but unharmed. *(76 words)*
</Callout>

## 11. 作文 Composition (约 150 词扩写)

<Callout type="info" title="任务">
  Describe **how the kidnapper came to know that Mrs. Ramsay was so fond of her cat and how he stole it**. Expand the following into a paragraph of **about 150 words**.
</Callout>

<Callout type="warning" title="提示骨架（每个省略号都得自己填）">
  - In a bar one night, Mr. X was talking to a workman who told him that ...
  - The workman added that Mrs. Ramsay ...
  - Every day, Mr. X stood outside ...
  - As the cat ... that it had regular habits.
  - He also found out as much as he could about Mrs. Ramsay and learnt that ...
  - One evening, as the cat was leaving the block of flats ...
  - He took the cat to ...
  - During the next three days, he walked past Mrs. Ramsay's flat on several occasions and noticed that ...
  - Now that he was sure ... he wrote ... in which he ...
</Callout>

<Callout type="tip" title="写作脚手架">
  - **时态主线**：一般过去时叙事；过去完成时表更早事件（*had been seen / had told*）；过去进行时表背景（*was talking / was leaving*）。
  - **\"侦察\"动词库**：*watched, observed, noted, kept an eye on, hung around, lingered near, paced past, waited in the shadows*。
  - **\"打听\"动词库**：*learnt that, gathered that, found out that, picked up the rumour that, was told that, overheard that*。
  - **\"动手\"动词库**：*lured the cat with a piece of fish, scooped it up, bundled it into a sack, slipped it under his coat, made off into the alley*。
  - **结构亮点**：开头一句用 *In a bar one night, Mr. X was talking to a workman who told him that ...*（过去进行 + 主从套叠）—— 是非常\"小说式\"的开篇。
</Callout>

<Callout type="tip" title="参考范文（自己写完再看）">
  In a bar one night, Mr. X was talking to a workman who told him that an elderly tenant in Kingsley Court doted on her cat as though it were her own child. The workman added that Mrs. Ramsay was rumoured to keep a small fortune in the bank. Every day for a week, Mr. X stood outside the block of flats, watching the entrance. As the cat slipped out at the same hour each evening, he soon noticed that it had regular habits. He also found out as much as he could about Mrs. Ramsay and learnt that she lived entirely alone. One evening, as the cat was leaving the block of flats, Mr. X scooped it up and tucked it under his coat. He took the cat to a friend's lock-up garage on the other side of town. During the next three days, he walked past Mrs. Ramsay's flat on several occasions and noticed that the curtains were drawn and the lights burned all night. Now that he was sure she was desperate, he wrote a short anonymous letter in which he demanded one thousand pounds. *(190 words)*
</Callout>

## 12. 书信写作 Letter writing

<Callout type="info" title="任务">
  Write a letter of about **80 words** to a former teacher telling him briefly **what you have been doing since you left school**. Supply a suitable **Introduction** and **Conclusion**. Use the following information:

  ***Purpose:*** further studies since leaving school — how you got your present job — whether you like it and why — what you hope to do in the future.
</Callout>

<Callout type="tip" title="写作脚手架">
  - **称呼**：*Dear Mr. / Mrs. + 姓*（写给老师用 Mr. / Mrs. 比 Dear Sir 更亲切）。
  - **开头 (Introduction)**：寒暄 + 写信缘由 —— *I hope this letter finds you well. I have been meaning to write to you since I left school three years ago.*
  - **主体 (Body)** —— 按 4 个要点串：
    1. **进修**：*After leaving school, I went on to read English at the University of ...*
    2. **找到工作**：*Last summer I joined a small publishing house in London as an editorial assistant.*
    3. **喜欢与否**：*I enjoy the work because every manuscript brings a new world to discover.*
    4. **未来计划**：*In the long run, I hope to ...*
  - **结尾 (Conclusion)**：*Do let me know how things are with you. I look forward to hearing from you. / With very best wishes, / Yours sincerely, / [你的名字]*
  - **时态混搭**：*have been doing*（现在完成进行——\"自……以来一直在做\"）/ *went on to read*（一般过去）/ *enjoy / am hoping*（一般现在 / 现在进行）。
</Callout>

<Callout type="tip" title="参考范文（自己写完再看）">
  Dear Mrs. Hartley,

  I hope this letter finds you well. I have been meaning to write to you since I left St. Andrew's three years ago. After taking my A-levels, I went on to read English at Bristol. Last summer I joined a small publishing house in London as an editorial assistant — I got the job after a chance meeting at a book fair. I enjoy it enormously because no two manuscripts are ever alike. In the long run, I hope to become a literary editor.

  Do please let me know how things are with you.

  Yours sincerely,

  Anna *(82 words)*
</Callout>

## 13. 拓展练习 Going further

<Callout type="tip" title="自学者建议">
  - **影子跟读 (shadowing)**：用第 1 节的三个 SayIt 按钮跟读 3-5 遍。重点感受英式句末上扬的悬念感（如 *... he was dear in more ways than one!*）—— 整句轻读 \"in more ways than ONE\" 的重音落在最后。
  - **复述 (retell)**：合上课文，把 Rastus 的故事用 2 分钟讲一遍。**叙事三段**：背景（富婆 + 规律的猫）→ 经过（失踪 → 匿名信 → 改主意 → 取钱 → 猫回家）→ 结局（警察震惊 + dear 的双关点睛）。
  - **造句迁移**：用今天的 5 个高价值结构造你自己的句子——
    1. *S + has + V-ed + for + 时间段*（持续到现在）—— *I have lived in Beijing for ten years.*
    2. *fearing that ..., 主语 + V*（现在分词作原因状语）—— *Fearing that I would be late, I took a taxi.*
    3. *be instructed to + V*（被指示去……）—— *We were instructed to remain seated.*
    4. *had + V-ed + when + 一般过去*（过去之过去）—— *The train had left when I arrived.*
    5. *Considering ..., S + V*（考虑到……）—— *Considering his age, he runs remarkably fast.*
  - **延伸思考**：本课是\"**时态全景图**\"——一段不到 200 词的短文用了 8 种时态形式。每种时态都不是\"装饰\"，而是**精确标定事件之间的先后**。下次你写英文叙事时，问自己：\"主线动作是一般过去时；插入回顾用过去完成；从\'那一刻\'看未来用 would；持续到现在用现在完成。\"——四把尺子在手，英文叙事就有了**电影般的层次**。另一个值得回味的点：**\"dear\" 的一语双关**让本文从\"小报奇闻\"升级为\"文学小品\"——好的写作往往就是在最后一句话里，把读者从字面意思推到象征意义。
</Callout>
