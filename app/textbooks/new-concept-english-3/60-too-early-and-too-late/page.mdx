<Callout type="info" title="Listen first 听前思考">
  准时（**punctuality**）在文明社会里几乎是一种"必需的习惯"——没有它，什么事都办不成，一切都会乱套。可作者偏偏要替两种人说话：一种是埋头钻研深奥问题、被允许赴宴迟到的"知识分子"；另一种则是反过来——**早到得太离谱**的人，比客人提前半小时登门，简直成了最大的"麻烦"。文章后半段，一个**第一次独自出门**的小姑娘，把"宁可早到也别迟到"奉为信条，提前二十分钟进了站，却因为看错了时刻表上一个**针尖大的小 "o"**，眼睁睁看着火车把她甩在身后……
  **Why did the young girl miss the train?** 带着这个问题读课文，重点留意作者是如何用 **than 比较结构、had better / would rather、倒装、间接引语、非谓语动词** 把"早"与"晚"这对矛盾讲得妙趣横生。
</Callout>

## The Text 课文

Punctuality is a **necessary habit** in all public affairs in civilized society. Without it, nothing could ever **be brought to a conclusion**; everything would be **in a state of chaos**. Only in a sparsely-populated rural community is it possible to disregard it. In ordinary living, there can be **some tolerance of unpunctuality**. The intellectual, who is working on some abstruse problem, has everything coordinated and organized **for the matter in hand**. He is therefore forgiven if late for a dinner party. But people are often **reproached for unpunctuality** when their only fault is **cutting things fine**. It is hard for energetic, quick-minded people to waste time, so they are often **tempted to finish a job** before setting out to keep an appointment. If no accidents occur on the way, like punctured tyres, diversions of traffic, sudden descent of fog, they will be on time. They are often more industrious, useful citizens than those who are never late. The over-punctual can be **as much a trial to others as** the unpunctual. The guest who arrives **half an hour too soon** is the greatest nuisance. Some friends of my family had this irritating habit. The only thing to do was ask them to come half an hour later than the other guests. Then they arrived just when we wanted them.

If you are catching a train, it is always **better to be comfortably early than** even a fraction of a minute too late. Although being early may mean wasting a little time, this will be less than if you miss the train and have to wait an hour or more for the next one; and you avoid the frustration of arriving at the very moment when the train is **drawing out of the station** and being unable to get on it. An even harder situation is to be on the platform **in good time** for a train and still to see it go off without you. Such an experience befell a certain young girl the first time she was travelling alone.

She entered the station twenty minutes before the train was due, since her parents **had impressed upon her** that it would be unforgivable to miss it and cause the friends with whom she was going to stay to make two journeys to meet her. She gave her luggage to a porter and showed him her ticket. **To her horror** he said that she was two hours too soon. She felt in her handbag for the piece of paper on which her father had written down all the details of the journey and gave it to the porter. He agreed that a train did come into the station at the time on the paper and that it did stop, but only to **take on mail**, not passengers. The girl asked to see a timetable, feeling sure that her father could not have made such a mistake. The porter went to fetch one and arrived back with the station master, who produced it **with a flourish** and pointed out a microscopic 'o' beside the time of the arrival of the train at his station; this little 'o' indicated that the train only stopped for mail. Just as that moment the train came into the station. The girl, **tears streaming down her face**, begged to be allowed to slip into the guard's van. But the station master **was adamant**: rules could not be broken. And she had to watch that train disappear towards her destination while she **was left behind**.

## 课文注释 Notes on the text

<Callout type="info" title="7 个值得停下来嚼一嚼的点">
  1. **Only in a sparsely-populated rural community is it possible to disregard it.** (l.3-4) — **only + 状语置于句首，主句必须部分倒装**（助动词 / be 动词提到主语前）。这里 *is it possible* 就是把 *it is* 倒过来。同型：*Only then did I understand. / Only by working hard can you succeed.* 还原成正常语序就是 *It is possible to disregard it only in ...*。
  2. **for the matter in hand** (l.6-7) — "为手头 / 眼下的事"。**in hand** = 正在处理的、手头的。同型：*the job in hand（手头的活）/ have the situation in hand（掌控局面）*。
  3. **He is therefore forgiven if late for a dinner party.** (l.7-8) — *if* 后省略了 **he is**。规则：在 **if / when / though / while** 引导的状语从句里，**当从句主语与主句主语一致、且谓语含 be 动词时，可同时省去"主语 + be"**。还原：*if (he is) late for a dinner party*。同型：*When (you are) in Rome, do as the Romans do. / Though (he was) tired, he kept working.*
  4. **cut things fine** (l.8-9) — "把时间 / 余量掐得死死的，不留一点余地"。**fine** 在这里是副词"勉强地、紧巴巴地"。同型：*cut it fine（掐得太紧）/ a fine margin（极小的余量）*。课文里 *their only fault is cutting things fine* = 他们唯一的"毛病"就是不留富余时间。
  5. **The over-punctual can be as much a trial to others as the unpunctual.** (l.12) — **as much a trial ... as ...** = "和……一样地令人难受"。**a trial** 这里作可数名词，意为"一件烦人 / 磨人的事"。注意 *as much **a** trial* 这种 **as + much + a + 单数名词** 的语序。同型：*He is as good a teacher as his father.*
  6. **half an hour too soon** (l.13) = **half an hour earlier**，"早到半个小时"。**too soon / too early** 都带"早过头了"的贬义。
  7. **in good time** (l.20) — 作"**及时地、从容地（留有余地地）**"讲，相当于 *early enough, with time to spare*。不要理解成"美好时光"。同型：*We arrived in good time for the meeting.*
</Callout>

  如果你要赶火车，那么从容地早到总比哪怕迟到一丁点儿都要好。虽然早到可能意味着浪费一点时间，但这比误了车、再等上一个小时或更久去坐下一班要强；而且你可以免去这样的懊恼：恰好在火车正驶离车站时赶到，却上不去。更难受的情形是：从从容容地等在站台上，眼看着火车却把你扔下开走了。一位小姑娘第一次独自出门，就碰上了这样的事。

## 3. 关键句型 "宁早勿晚"——比较结构 + had better + 倒装

<Callout type="tip" title="一分钟回顾">
  本课 Structure 4 题集中考"如何把'早 / 晚'这对矛盾说清楚"。核心是四组结构：

  | 结构 | 课文 / 改写例句 | 要点 |
  |---|---|---|
  | **better to do A than (to do) B** | *It is always **better to be** comfortably early **than** ... too late.* | "与其 B 不如 A"；than 两边形式要平行 |
  | **had better (not) do** | *If you are catching a train, you **had better be** comfortably early ...* | "最好……"；后接**动词原形**，没有 to |
  | **only + 状语 → 部分倒装** | ***Only*** in a sparsely-populated community ***is it*** possible to disregard it. | only 引导的状语置于句首，助动词 / be 提前 |
  | **it would be impossible (ever) to do** | *Without it, it would be impossible **ever to bring anything** to a conclusion.* | impossible 后接**不定式**作真正主语 |

  ### ⚠️ 4 个本课最容易踩的雷区

  1. **had better + 动词原形**，不是 *had better to do*，更不是 *had better being*。否定式是 *had better **not** do*。
  2. **better ... than ...** 里，than 后若承前省略，要保持与前面**同样的形式**：*better to be early than (to be) late* ✓。
  3. **only + 状语在句首才倒装**；如果 only 修饰主语（*Only she knows*）则**不**倒装。
  4. **as much a trial as** 这种 *as + much + a + 单数名词 + as* 的语序别写错成 *as a much trial as*。
</Callout>

## 4. 难点：直接引语 → 间接引语 Reported speech

<Callout type="info" title="把“原话”变成“转述”的三步走">
  本课 *Key structures* 的改写题是：把 *"May I see the timetable, please?" the girl asked.* 改成 *The girl asked ...*（参照 l.27-28 课文里 *The girl asked to see a timetable* 的处理）。把直接引语变间接引语，记住三步：

  | 要变的 | 规则 | 例子 |
  |---|---|---|
  | **句式** | 一般疑问句 → *ask + if / whether + 陈述语序*；特殊疑问句 → *ask + 疑问词 + 陈述语序* | *"Are you ready?" → He asked **if I was** ready.* |
  | **人称** | 跟着转述者的视角变 | *"May **I** ...?" the girl asked → asked if **she** might ...* |
  | **时态** | 主句过去 → 从句"时态后移"（may→might, is→was, will→would）| *may → might* |

  本题最自然的间接引语是：**The girl asked if she might see the timetable.**（语气更礼貌可用 *might*；用 *could* 亦可。）课文则把它进一步简化为不定式结构 *The girl asked **to see** a timetable*——"ask to do sth." 表"请求做某事"，更精炼。

  ### ⚠️ 3 个易错点

  1. 间接引语里**语序变陈述句**（主语 + 谓语），**不再用疑问语序**，句末也**不加问号**。
  2. *please* 在转述时通常**去掉**（它属于口语礼貌词）。
  3. *may / can* 表"请求许可"转述后多用 *might / could*（时态后移 + 更委婉）。
</Callout>

## 10. 摘要写作 Summary writing (≤ 80 词)

<Callout type="info" title="任务">
  Give an account *in not more than 80 words* of **the girl's experience on the railway station, when she was not allowed to get on the train**. **Do not include anything that is not in the last paragraph**（只用最后一段的内容）。Use your own words as far as possible.

  按时间顺序串起 6 个要点：
  1. 她什么时候进的站？为什么这么早？
  2. 搬运工告诉她什么？
  3. 父亲那张纸 / 时刻表说明了什么？
  4. 那个针尖大的 "o" 是什么意思？
  5. 火车进站时她想做什么？
  6. 站长的态度如何，结果怎样？
</Callout>

  Eager not to miss her train, the girl reached the station twenty minutes early. To her dismay, the porter said she was two hours too soon. When it pulled in, she begged in tears to climb aboard, yet the station master refused to break the rules, and she watched it leave without her. *(75 words)*
</Callout>

## 11. 作文 Composition (250–350 词)

<Callout type="info" title="任务">
  Write a composition on **one** of the following subjects; the length should be between **250 and 350 words**.
  - **a** Write a short story beginning *"A piece of paper was blowing in the wind ..."*
  - **b** What science has done to make our lives easier and more comfortable.
</Callout>

<Callout type="warning" title="提示骨架（以 b 为例）">
  - **开篇**：一句话立论——科学如何重塑了日常生活（不要泛泛喊口号，落到"省时、省力、舒适"三个维度）。
  - **主体段 1 · 家居与舒适**：电、暖气 / 空调、冰箱、洗衣机 —— 把人从家务里解放出来。
  - **主体段 2 · 交通与通信**：汽车 / 高铁 / 飞机缩短距离；手机 / 互联网让信息瞬间可达。
  - **主体段 3 · 健康与医疗**：疫苗、麻醉、影像诊断 —— 延长寿命、减轻痛苦。
  - **辩证一笔**：便利也带来代价（久坐、信息过载、对设备的依赖），点到为止显成熟。
  - **结尾**：回扣"easier and more comfortable"，给出你的态度。
</Callout>

<Callout type="tip" title="写作脚手架">
  - **时态主线**：以**现在完成时**为骨干（*science **has transformed / has made / has given***——"至今为止的成就"），穿插一般现在时陈述普遍事实。
  - **"便利"动词库**：*has revolutionized, has simplified, has automated, has eased the burden of, has done away with, has put ... at our fingertips, has spared us the trouble of*。
  - **"领域"词库**：*household chores, transport and travel, communication, medicine and health, food preservation*。
  - **关联与让步**：*Thanks to ..., As a result, Moreover, On the other hand, Convenient as it is, ...*。
  - **结构亮点**：开头用 *Few things have changed our daily lives as profoundly as science has.*（as ... as 比较 + 完成时）。
</Callout>

<Callout type="tip" title="参考范文（自己写完再看）">
  Few things have changed our daily lives as profoundly as science has. Within a single century it has eased almost every burden our grandparents once took for granted, and the comfort we now enjoy would have seemed pure magic to them.

  Begin at home. Electricity lights our rooms at the touch of a switch; central heating and air-conditioning keep us comfortable in any season; refrigerators preserve our food, and washing machines have done away with hours of tiring labour. Tasks that once filled an entire day are now finished while we read a book.

  Travel and communication have been transformed just as dramatically. Cars, high-speed trains and aircraft have shrunk distances that once took weeks to cross, so that a journey across a continent is now a matter of hours. Meanwhile the telephone and the internet have put the whole world at our fingertips: we can speak to a friend on the other side of the globe, or find the answer to almost any question, in seconds.

  Perhaps most important of all is what science has done for our health. Vaccines and antibiotics have conquered diseases that once killed millions; anaesthetics have taken the terror out of surgery; and modern scanners allow doctors to see inside the body without a single cut. As a result, people live longer and suffer far less pain than ever before.

  Convenient as all this is, it is not without its price. We sit more and walk less; we are flooded with information; and we have grown so dependent on our machines that a single power cut can bring everything to a halt. Science gives, but it also asks us to use its gifts wisely.

  On balance, however, there is no doubt that science has made our lives both easier and more comfortable. The challenge now is not to invent more, but to live sensibly with what we already have. *(310 words)*
</Callout>

## 12. 书信写作 Letter writing (80–100 词)

<Callout type="info" title="任务">
  Write a letter of between **80 and 100 words** on **one** of the following subjects. Make the beginning and ending like those of an ordinary letter, but the address is not counted.
  - **a** Write a letter informing your employer (or the Principal of your school) that you have to be absent for a few days and explaining why this is necessary.
  - **b** A friend with several young children has fallen ill. Write offering help or suggesting a way in which help can be obtained.
</Callout>

<Callout type="tip" title="写作脚手架（以 a 为例）">
  - **称呼**：写给上司 / 校长用 *Dear Mr. / Mrs. + 姓*，或正式场合 *Dear Sir / Madam*。
  - **开头**：开门见山说明来意 —— *I am writing to inform you that I shall have to be absent from ... for a few days.*
  - **正文 3 句**：① 缺勤的具体日期；② 原因（如家人住院 / 须回老家奔丧 / 本人需做小手术）；③ 已做的安排（把工作交接给同事 / 会随时电话联系）。
  - **结尾**：致歉 + 感谢谅解 —— *I am sorry for any inconvenience this may cause and thank you for your understanding.*
  - **落款**：正式信用 *Yours sincerely / Yours faithfully*。
  - **时态**：将来 *I shall be away / I will return*；现在完成 *I have arranged for ...*。
</Callout>

<Callout type="tip" title="参考范文（自己写完再看）">
  Dear Mr. Davidson,

  I am writing to inform you that I shall have to be absent from work from Monday to Wednesday next week. My mother has been taken into hospital for an operation, and as I am her only child, I must go home to look after her.

  I have asked Mr. Lin to take over my urgent duties, and I will be reachable by telephone at any time should anything arise.

  I am sorry for the inconvenience this may cause and thank you for your understanding.

  Yours sincerely,

  Li Hua *(88 words)*
</Callout>

## 13. 拓展练习 Going further

<Callout type="tip" title="自学者建议">
  - **影子跟读 (shadowing)**：用第 1 节的三个 SayIt 按钮各跟读 3-5 遍。重点感受 *Only in a sparsely-populated rural community **is it** possible ...* 这种倒装句的语调——句首重音落在 Only，倒装部分一气呵成。
  - **复述 (retell)**：合上课文，分两块讲。**议论块**：准时为何重要 → 哪两种人可被"豁免"（钻研深奥问题的知识分子 / 偏远乡村居民）→ 早到过头的人同样讨厌。**故事块**：小姑娘提前进站 → 搬运工说早了两小时 → 时刻表上的针尖小 "o" → 火车进站却只装邮件 → 站长不通融 → 被撇下。
  - **造句迁移**：用今天的 5 个高价值结构造你自己的句子——
    1. *Only + 状语 + 倒装*（only + 助动词 / be 提前）—— *Only after the exam did I relax.*
    2. *had better (not) do*（最好……）—— *You had better leave now, or you'll miss the bus.*
    3. *better to do A than (to do) B*（与其 B 不如 A）—— *It is better to ask than to guess.*
    4. *impress upon sb. that ...*（使某人铭记）—— *My teacher impressed upon us that practice makes perfect.*
    5. *watch sb./sth. do, leaving ...*（感官动词 + 伴随分词）—— *I watched the bus pull away, leaving me alone in the rain.*
  - **延伸思考**：本课的妙处在于一个"反差结构"——开篇郑重论证"准时是文明的必需"，转而调侃"早到过头同样恼人"，再用一个**因循守旧、被一个针尖小 "o" 击败**的小姑娘故事收尾。它提醒我们：守规矩固然重要，但**死抠字面（那个小 "o"）与机械执规（不肯通融的站长）**也会把人逼入绝境。写英文叙事时不妨学这种**"先立论、再用一个具体故事反衬"**的章法——抽象的道理一旦落到细节上，文字就有了温度与幽默。
</Callout>
