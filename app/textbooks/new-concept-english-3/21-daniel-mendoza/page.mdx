  **How many unsuccessful attempts did Mendoza make before becoming Champion of all England?（门多萨在成为全英冠军之前，失败过几次？）** 带着这个问题读课文，重点留意全篇是怎样用 **被动语态**（were known as / could be killed / was adored by / was defeated / was sent to prison）把一个人的浮沉“客观地”铺陈出来。

## The Text 课文

Boxing matches were very popular in England two hundred years ago. In those days, boxers fought with bare fists for prize money. Because of this, they **were known as** 'prizefighters'. However, boxing was very crude, for there were no rules and a prizefighter **could be seriously injured or even killed** during a match.

One of the most colourful figures in boxing history was Daniel Mendoza, who was born in 1764. **The use of gloves was not introduced until 1860**, when the Marquis of Queensberry drew up the first set of rules. Though he was technically a prizefighter, Mendoza **did much to change** crude prizefighting **into** a sport, for he brought science to the game. In his day, Mendoza enjoyed **tremendous popularity**. He **was adored by rich and poor alike**.

Mendoza **rose to fame** swiftly after a boxing match when he was only fourteen years old. This attracted the attention of Richard Humphries who was then the most eminent boxer in England. He offered to train Mendoza and his young pupil was quick to learn. In fact, Mendoza soon became so successful that Humphries **turned against** him. The two men quarrelled bitterly and it was clear that the argument could only be settled by a fight. A match was held at Stilton where both men fought for an hour. The public **bet a great deal of money on** Mendoza, but he was defeated. Mendoza met Humphries in the ring on a later occasion and he lost for a second time. **It was not until his third match in 1790 that** he finally beat Humphries and became Champion of England. Meanwhile, he founded a highly successful Academy and even Lord Byron became one of his pupils. He earned enormous sums of money and **was paid as much as £100** for a single appearance. Despite this, he was so extravagant that he was always in debt. After he was defeated by a boxer called Gentleman Jackson, he was quickly forgotten. He **was sent to prison for failing to pay his debts** and died in poverty in 1836.

## 课文注释 Notes on the text

<Callout type="info" title="6 个值得停下来嚼一嚼的点">
  1. **be known as ...** (l.3) — “被称作……；以……著称”。课文 *they were known as 'prizefighters'* = 他们被称作“职业拳击手”。注意区分三个形近短语：**be known as**（作为……而著称，后接身份 / 称号）/ **be known for**（因……而著称，后接原因 / 特点）/ **be known to**（为……所知，后接人）。例：*He is known as a poet. / He is known for his courage. / He is known to the police.*
  2. **The use of gloves was not introduced until 1860 ... the Marquis of Queensberry** (ll.7-8) — “直到 1860 年才开始使用拳击手套……昆斯伯里侯爵”。**not ... until** = “直到……才”。**The Marquis of Queensberry**（1844—1900）昆斯伯里侯爵，他制定的规则沿用至今。**drew up** 是 **draw up** 的过去式，“起草、制定（规则 / 文件 / 计划）”。
  3. **do much to change ... into ... / bring ... to ...** (ll.9-10) — *did much to change crude prizefighting into a sport* = “为把粗野的职业拳击变成一项运动出了大力”。**do much to do sth.** 为做某事出大力；**change A into B** 把 A 变成 B；**bring science to the game** 把科学引入这项运动（bring ... to ... 把……引入……）。
  4. **in his day / be adored by rich and poor alike** (ll.10-11) — **in his day** “在他的全盛时期”（day 此处指“一生中最辉煌的阶段”）。*He was adored by rich and poor alike*：**rich and poor** = the rich and the poor（形容词前加 the 或并列省略 the，泛指“富人和穷人”两类人）；**alike** 是副词，“一样地、同样地”，放在所修饰的两类人之后。
  5. **rise to fame / turn against sb.** (ll.11-13) — **rise to fame** “一举成名”（rise 不及物动词，过去式 **rose**，切勿与及物的 raise 混淆）。**turn against sb.** “与某人反目、转而敌对”。课文 *Mendoza soon became so successful that Humphries turned against him* —— so ... that ... “如此……以至于……”。
  6. **It was not until ... that ... / bet ... on ... / be sent to prison for failing to ...** (ll.17-21) — **It was not until his third match in 1790 that he finally beat Humphries** 是 **not ... until** 的**强调句**：“直到 1790 年第三次比赛，他才终于击败汉弗莱斯”（强调被推迟到的时间）。**bet money on sb.** 把赌注押在某人身上。**He was sent to prison for failing to pay his debts**：be sent to prison “被关进监狱”；**for + 动名词**（failing）表原因；**fail to do** “未能做到”。
</Callout>

  两百年前，拳击比赛在英国非常盛行。当时，拳击手不戴手套，为争夺奖金而搏斗。因此，他们被称作“职业拳击手”。不过，那时的拳击十分粗野，因为没有任何规则，职业拳击手有可能在比赛中受重伤，甚至被打死。

  拳击史上最引人注目的人物之一是丹尼尔·门多萨，他生于 1764 年。直到 1860 年昆斯伯里侯爵第一次为拳击制定了规则，拳击比赛才开始使用手套。虽然严格来讲门多萨只是个职业拳击手，但他对于把粗野的职业拳击变成一项体育运动作出了重大贡献，因为是他把科学引进了这项运动。门多萨在他的全盛时期享有极高的声望，无论穷人还是富人都对他崇拜备至。

  门多萨 14 岁时参加一场拳击赛后一举成名。这引起了当时英国最负盛名的拳击手理查德·汉弗莱斯的注意。汉弗莱斯主动提出训练门多萨，而年少的门多萨一学就会。事实上，门多萨很快便名声大振，致使汉弗莱斯与他反目为仇。两人争吵不休，显然，只有较量一番才能解决他们之间的争端。于是两人在斯蒂尔顿设下赛场，厮打了一个小时。公众把大笔赌注押在门多萨身上，但他却输了。后来，门多萨与汉弗莱斯再次在拳击场上较量，他又输了第二场。直到 1790 年第三次较量时，门多萨才终于击败汉弗莱斯，成为全英拳击冠军。其间，他还办起了一所很受欢迎的拳击学校，连拜伦勋爵也成了他的学生。门多萨挣来大笔大笔的钱，每次出场费高达 100 英镑。尽管如此，他还是挥霍无度，经常债台高筑。他被一个叫绅士杰克逊的拳击手击败后很快被人遗忘。他因无力偿还债务而被投入监狱，最后于 1836 年在贫困中死去。
</Callout>

## 3. 关键句型 The passive voice 被动语态全景

<Callout type="tip" title="一分钟回顾">
  本课是一篇**人物传记**，作者要“客观地”叙述门多萨的浮沉，于是大量使用**被动语态**——施动者要么不重要、要么不言自明，重心落在“承受动作的人 / 物”上。把课文的被动句圈出来，会看到一条清晰的脉络：

  | 被动形式 | 课文例句 | 说明 |
  |---|---|---|
  | **一般过去被动** be + V-ed | *they **were known as** 'prizefighters' / he **was defeated** / he **was quickly forgotten*** | 主线叙事，施动者略去 |
  | **情态动词 + be + V-ed** | *a prizefighter **could be** seriously injured or even **killed*** | “可能被……”，情态被动 |
  | **被动 + by 引出施动者** | *He **was adored by** rich and poor alike. / he **was defeated by** a boxer called Gentleman Jackson* | by 后是“动作的发出者” |
  | **not ... until + 被动** | *The use of gloves **was not introduced until** 1860* | “直到……才被……” |
  | **be + V-ed + for + V-ing** | *He **was sent to prison for failing** to pay his debts* | 被动 + 介词短语表原因 |

  ### ⚠️ 4 个本课最容易踩的雷区

  1. **could be killed 是“情态 + 被动”**：modal（can / could / may / must）后接 **be + 过去分词**。*could be injured / killed* = “可能被打伤 / 打死”。错写成 *could killed* / *could be kill* 都不对。
  2. **被动里的施动者用 by**：*adored **by** rich and poor* / *defeated **by** Gentleman Jackson*。介词是 **by**（不是 with / from）。with 引出的是“工具”（fought **with** bare fists）。
  3. **not ... until 的强调句**：普通句 *He didn't beat Humphries until 1790.* → 强调句 ***It was not until** 1790 **that** he beat Humphries.* 结构是 **It was not until + 时间 + that + 句子**，that 后面用**肯定**形式（否定已经前移到 not until 里）。
  4. **owe sb. sth.（双宾语，主动）vs be paid（被动）**：*he was paid as much as £100*（他被支付……）是被动；而 *he owed people money*（他欠人家钱）是主动双宾语，**不加 to**（owe sb. sth.，不是 owe to sb.）。
</Callout>

## 4. 难点：rise / raise 与一词多义、动词 + 介词

<Callout type="info" title="3 组教材 Special Difficulties 必考点">
  教材 *Key structures and Special difficulties* 部分点出本课三类高频考点：

  **① rise vs raise（最爱考的一对）**

  | 词 | 及物性 | 词义 | 变化 | 例句 |
  |---|---|---|---|---|
  | **rise** | **不及物**（后不带宾语） | （自己）上升 / 升起 / 成名 | rise–rose–risen | *Mendoza **rose** to fame. / The sun **rises** in the east. / Prices have **risen**.* |
  | **raise** | **及物**（后必带宾语） | 举起 / 提高 / 抚养 | raise–raised–raised | *He **raised** his hand. / They **raised** prices. / She **raised** three children.* |

  一句话记牢：**rise 自己起来（无宾语），raise 把东西举起来（有宾语）**。

  **② 一词多义：clear / clean**

  - *it was **clear** (that ...)* —— 这里 clear = “显然的、清楚的”（*It was clear that the argument could only be settled by a fight.*）。
  - clear 还可表：晴朗的（a **clear** sky）、清澈的（**clear** water）、畅通的（a **clear** road）、（动词）清除（**clear** the table）。
  - 别和 **clean（干净的、清洁的）** 混：*Keep your hands **clean**.*

  **③ 动词 / 形容词 + 介词 + 动名词**

  课文 *He was sent to prison **for failing** to pay his debts* —— **for + V-ing** 表原因。把下面这组“介词后接动名词”的搭配记牢：**afraid of** doing / **without** doing / **apologize for** doing / **congratulate sb. on** doing。

<Callout type="info" title="任务">
  In not more than **250 words**, write an imaginary account of **the first fight between Mendoza and Humphries**. Provide a suitable title. Your composition should be in **four paragraphs**.

  ***Ideas***: The quarrel — Mendoza and Humphries: bets from supporters — atmosphere just before the fight — the fight itself — Mendoza's defeat — effect on him and his supporters.
</Callout>

<Callout type="warning" title="提示骨架（四段对应四个画面）">
  - **第 1 段 · 起因**：师徒为何反目 —— the pupil had outshone the master; pride and jealousy; only the ring could settle it.
  - **第 2 段 · 赛前**：押注与气氛 —— supporters poured into Stilton; huge bets were laid on Mendoza; the crowd roared, the air was thick with excitement.
  - **第 3 段 · 比赛**：一小时的搏斗 —— bare fists, no gloves; round after round; Mendoza's science vs Humphries's experience.
  - **第 4 段 · 失败与影响**：门多萨落败及其影响 —— against all expectation Mendoza was defeated; his backers lost a fortune; yet he vowed to return.
</Callout>

<Callout type="tip" title="写作脚手架">
  - **时态主线**：一般过去时叙事；过去完成时交代更早的背景（*the pupil **had outgrown** the master*）；过去进行时铺垫气氛（*the crowd **was roaring**, money **was changing** hands*）。
  - **“押注”词库**：*laid bets on, wagered, staked a fortune on, backed Mendoza heavily, the odds favoured ...*。
  - **“气氛”词库**：*the air was electric, tension mounted, the crowd hushed, a roar went up, all eyes were fixed on the ring*。
  - **“搏斗”词库**：*traded blows, ducked and weaved, landed a punch, staggered, the bare fists thudded, neither man would yield*。
  - **结构亮点**：用一个 **not ... until** 收尾呼应课文 —— *It was not the science of the younger man but the experience of the elder that won the day.*（强调句变体）。
</Callout>

  Once Mendoza had grown more famous than his teacher, the old friendship soured. ## 12. 书信写作 Letter writing

<Callout type="info" title="任务">
  On a full page, show the **exact position** of each of the following parts of a letter:
  the **address and date**; the **beginning of the letter**; the **Introduction**; the **Purpose**; the **Conclusion**; the **letter-ending**; the **signature**; the **postscript**. Supply all necessary **full stops and commas**.
</Callout>

<Callout type="tip" title="写作脚手架：一封信的 8 个位置（从上到下）">
  - **1 地址 + 日期 (Address and date)**：写在**右上角**。地址由小到大逐行排列，日期另起一行：*15 Park Road, / Cambridge. / 21st March, 2024.*
  - **2 称呼 (Beginning / Salutation)**：左对齐，后接**逗号** —— *Dear Mr. Humphries,*
  - **3 引言 (Introduction)**：第一段寒暄、说明写信背景 —— *I hope you are keeping well. It has been a long time since we last met.*
  - **4 正文 / 目的 (Purpose)**：核心段，把写信的真正目的讲清楚 —— *I am writing to ...*
  - **5 结语 (Conclusion)**：最后一段收束、表达期待 —— *I look forward to hearing from you soon.*
  - **6 结束语 (Letter-ending / Complimentary close)**：右下方，首字母大写、后接**逗号** —— *Yours sincerely,*（熟人）/ *Yours faithfully,*（Dear Sir 开头时用）。

  - **8 附言 (Postscript)**：临时补充，签名之后另起，以 **P.S.** 开头 —— *P.S. Do bring the photographs you promised.*
  - ⚠️ **标点要点**：地址每行末尾、日期、称呼后、结束语后都要有**逗号或句号**；P.S. 两个字母后各加一个点。

## 13. 拓展练习 Going further

<Callout type="tip" title="自学者建议">
  - **影子跟读 (shadowing)**：用第 1 节的三个 SayIt 按钮跟读 3-5 遍。重点练**被动语态**的弱读——*was a**dored** by* / *was de**feated*** 里 was 轻读、过去分词重读；以及强调句 *It was not until... **that**...* 中 not until 的下压与 that 后的提速。
  - **复述 (retell)**：合上课文，用 2 分钟把门多萨的一生讲一遍。**三段式**：时代背景（无规则、为奖金搏命的 prizefighting）→ 崛起（14 岁成名 → 拜师 Humphries → 反目 → 三战封王 → 办学收徒）→ 结局（挥霍 → 负债 → 入狱 → 贫困而终）。
  - **造句迁移**：用今天的 5 个本课结构造你自己的句子——
    1. *S + be + 过去分词 + by + 施动者*（被动 + 施动者）—— *The novel was written by a teenager.*
    2. *S + could be + 过去分词*（情态被动）—— *Such mistakes could easily be avoided.*
    3. *It was not until ... that ...*（强调句）—— *It was not until midnight that the work was finished.*
    4. *rise to fame / rise–rose–risen*（不及物，自己上升）—— *She rose to fame overnight.*
    5. *be sent to prison for + V-ing*（介词 + 动名词表原因）—— *He was fined for driving too fast.*
  - **延伸思考**：本课通篇是**被动语态的“客观叙事”**——作者很少说“某人做了什么”，而多说“某人**被**怎样了”（was adored / was defeated / was forgotten / was sent to prison）。这种写法把一个传奇人物放在**命运的承受端**，读来格外有“浮沉无常”的宿命感。下次你写人物小传时，不妨问自己：哪些句子该用主动（突出“他主动出击”），哪些该用被动（突出“他被时代 / 命运推着走”）？**主动与被动的切换，本身就是一种叙事的镜头语言。**另一个值得回味的点：门多萨 *was paid as much as £100*（被动，钱“被付给”他）却 *always owed people money*（主动，他主动欠债）——同一个人，进账是被动、败家是主动，一句话的语态对比，把“挣得多、留不住”写得入木三分。
</Callout>
