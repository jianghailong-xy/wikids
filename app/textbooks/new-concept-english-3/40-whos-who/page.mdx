## The Text 课文

It has never been explained why university students seem to **enjoy practical jokes** more than anyone else. Students **specialize in** a particular type of practical joke: the hoax. Inviting the fire brigade to **put out** a nonexistent fire is a crude form of deception which no self-respecting student would ever **indulge in**. Students often create amusing situations which are funny to everyone except the victims.

When a student recently saw two workmen using a pneumatic drill outside his university, he immediately telephoned the police and informed them that two students **dressed up as** workmen were tearing up the road with a pneumatic drill. As soon as he had hung up, he went over to the workmen and told them that if a policeman ordered them to go away, they **were not to take him seriously**. He added that a student had dressed up as a policeman and was playing all sorts of silly jokes on people. Both the police and the workmen were **grateful to** the student **for** this piece of advance information.

The student hid in an archway nearby where he could watch and hear everything that went on. Sure enough, a policeman arrived on the scene and politely asked the workmen to go away. When he received a very rude reply from one of the workmen, he threatened to remove them by force. The workmen told him to do as he pleased and the policeman telephoned for help. Shortly afterwards, four more policemen arrived and **remonstrated with** the workmen. As the men refused to stop working, the police attempted to seize the pneumatic drill. The workmen struggled fiercely and one of them **lost his temper**. He threatened to call the police. At this, the police pointed out ironically that this would hardly be necessary as the men were already **under arrest**. Pretending to speak seriously, one of the workmen asked if he might make a telephone call before being taken to the station. Permission was granted and a policeman accompanied him to a pay phone. **Only when** he saw that the man was actually telephoning the police **did he realize** that they had all been the victims of a hoax.

## 课文注释 Notes on the text

<Callout type="info" title="9 个值得停下来嚼一嚼的点">
  1. **It has never been explained why ...** (l.1) — "谁也弄不清为什么……"。这个句子的真正主语是以 **why** 引导的**主语从句**（*why university students seem to enjoy ...*），句首的 **It** 是**形式主语**。同型：*It has never been explained how the prisoner escaped. / It remains a mystery why she left.*

  2. **specialize in** (l.2) — "专门从事、擅长"。**in** 是固定搭配介词，后接名词或动名词。同型：*The restaurant specializes in seafood. / He specializes in making people laugh.*

  3. **Inviting ... fire** (ll.3-4) — "请消防队来扑灭……大火"。**Inviting the fire brigade to put out a nonexistent fire** 是**动名词短语作主语**——整个"请消防队来"这件事，被当成一个名词来用。**put out** = "扑灭、熄灭"（fire / cigarette / light）。同型：*Put out the candle before you go to bed. / It took firefighters three hours to put out the blaze.*

  4. **indulge in** (ll.4-5) — "沉迷于、纵情于"。**indulge + in + 名词/动名词** 是固定搭配。课文 *no self-respecting student would ever indulge in* = "有自尊心的大学生决不会沉溺于……"。同型：*On weekends, I indulge in a long bath and a good book. / He indulged in too much criticism of his colleagues.*

  5. **dress up as** (l.8) — "装扮成、化装成"。**dress** = 穿衣服；**dress up** = 精心打扮 / 乔装。**as** 引出装扮的角色。同型：*The children dressed up as pirates for the party. / He dressed up as Santa Claus.* 注意与 plain **dress** 的区别（参见 §4 难点）。

  6. **be not to do** (l.10) — "不要 / 不该做……"（指令、禁止）。**be + not + to do** 表达一种"事先约定的规则"或"上级的指令"。课文 *they were not to take him seriously* = "他们不要把他当回事"。同型：*You are not to leave the room without permission. / She was told she was not to speak to the press.* **take ... seriously** = "把……当真 / 当回事"。

  7. **be grateful to sb. for sth.** (l.12) — "对某人因某事表示感谢"。**to + 人**，**for + 事**——两个介词不能搞混。课文 *grateful to the student for this piece of advance information*。同型：*I'm grateful to you for your help. / We are grateful to our teachers for their patience.* **advance information** = 事先通报的信息，**advance** 此处是形容词（预先的）。

  8. **as he pleased** (ll.15-16) — "随他的便、悉听尊便"。**as** 引导方式状语从句，后面省略了 **to do**。完整形式是 *do as he pleased to do*。同型：*You may come and go as you please. / They spent the money as they pleased.*

  9. **Only when ... did he realize ...** (ll.22-23) — "只有当……的时候，他才意识到……"。**only + 状语从句** 放在句首时，主句的主语和谓语要用**部分倒装**（把助动词移到主语前面）。正常语序：*He realized ... only when ...*。倒装后：*Only when ... **did** he realize ...*。这是本课最核心的语法结构，同型：*Only when the rain stopped did we leave the house. / Only after you've tried it yourself will you understand how hard it is.* 倒装规则参见 §3 关键句型。
</Callout>

## 3. 关键句型 倒装与"Only + 状语" Inversion after "only"

<Callout type="tip" title="一分钟回顾">
  本课的语法发动机有两台：一台是**"only + 状语从句 → 主句倒装"**（贯穿最后一句，也是 Structure 第 8 题的焦点），另一台是**动词短语的固定搭配**（put out / dress up / lose one's temper / be under arrest 等，分布在全篇里）。

  | 结构 | 课文例句 | 用途 |
  |---|---|---|
  | **Only + 状语从句 + 倒装主句** | *Only when he saw that ... did he realize that ...* | 强调"直到那一刻才……"，书面色彩浓 |
  | **be not to do** | *they were not to take him seriously* | 表达"不该/不允许做"——指令或禁止 |
  | **seem + to do** | *seem to enjoy practical jokes* | "似乎……"——主观判断 |
  | **be grateful to sb. for sth.** | *grateful to the student for this piece of advance information* | 因某事感谢某人 |

  ### ⚠️ 4 个本课最容易踩的雷区

  1. **Only + 状语放在句首时，主句必须倒装**。*Only when he saw ... **did** he realize ...* ——助动词 **did** 必须提到主语 **he** 前面。写成 *Only then he realized* 是错的。注意：如果 only 修饰的不是整个状语从句而只是主语本身（如 *Only John knew the answer*），则**不倒装**。
  2. **dress vs dress up**：**dress** = 穿衣服（日常动作）；**dress up** = 精心打扮 / 乔装（有"特意/伪装"的意味）。课文 *dressed up as workmen* = 乔装成工人，"特意装扮"的意思——不能用 plain *dressed as* 完全替代。
  3. **put out / put up / put off / put up with**：四个短语各差一个词，意思完全不同。put out（扑灭）、put up（举起/搭建/留宿）、put off（推迟）、put up with（容忍）。这是教材 §4 难点专门练的。
  4. **lose one's temper** 不是"失去温度"——temper 在这里是"脾气、情绪"，*lose one's temper* = "发脾气、发火"。对应的正面表达是 *keep one's temper*（克制脾气）或 *in a good temper*（心情好）。
</Callout>

## 4. 难点：短语动词与近义词辨析 Special difficulties

<Callout type="info" title="教材 Key structures 五组练习">
  本课 Special difficulties 给出了五组需要区分的结构，分别来自第二册的：

  1. **put 短语家族**（ISD 27）：**put out**（扑灭）、**put up**（举起/搭建/留宿）、**put someone up**（留某人住宿）、**put up with**（容忍）、**put off**（推迟）。
  2. **"除了"三兄弟**（ISD 14b）：**except**（排除同类）、**except for**（排除不同类的细节修正）、**apart from**（可兼表"除……之外"与"除……之外还"，最灵活）。
  3. **dress vs dress up**（ISD 65c）：**dress** = 穿衣服（日常）；**dress up** = 精心打扮 / 乔装。
  4. **watch vs follow**（ISD 36c）：**watch** = 注视、观察（视觉）；**follow** = 跟随、追踪（移动 / 理解）。
  5. **temper / mood 表达**（ISD 83）：**in a good temper**（脾气好）、**in a good mood**（心情好）、**in a bad temper**（脾气坏）、**in the mood**（有心情做……）。
</Callout>

## 11. 作文 Composition (≤ 250 词)

<Callout type="info" title="任务">
  Imagine that the policeman who accompanied the workman to the pay phone **did not realize** that they had all been victims of a practical joke. In not more than **250 words**, describe what happened. Use the ideas given below. Do not write more than **four paragraphs**.

  **Title: Arrest the police!**

  - **Introduction**: Policeman and worker returned — the other workman still quarrelling with police — resisting arrest.
  - **Development**: More police arrived — workmen told them that the first lot of policemen were students — the second lot of police threatened to arrest the first lot — asked for identity cards — the first lot said that the workmen were students — workers had to prove their identity.
  - **Conclusion**: Realized they were victims of a hoax.
</Callout>

<Callout type="warning" title="提示骨架">
  - **第 1 段（引子）**：陪工人打完电话的警察回来了，一看——另一个工人还在跟其他警察吵，仍然拒绝交出风钻。气氛绷紧了。
  - **第 2 段（升级）**：又一批警察到了。工人们对第二批警察说：\"这些人是假警察——是学生装的！\"第二批警察立刻对第一批警察说：\"出示你们的证件。\"第一批警察反驳：\"不，是这些工人才是学生装的。\"工人们被迫掏出身份证、工资条自证身份。
  - **第 3 段（转折）**：有人想起那个报假警的电话，一查——发现真相：一个学生导演了这一切。
  - **第 4 段（收尾）**：所有人面面相觑。没有谁是罪犯，所有人都是被骗的。风钻继续在路面突突响着，但已经没人知道该逮捕谁了。
</Callout>

<Callout type="tip" title="写作脚手架">
  - **时态主线**：全篇用**一般过去时**叙事（was / returned / told / arrived / realized）；中间对话可穿插直接引语制造紧张感。
  - **冲突动词库**：*quarrel, resist, threaten, demand, insist, accuse, protest, prove, reveal*。
  - **\"证明身份\"词库**：*identity card, warrant card, pay slip, show proof, verify, establish one's identity*。
  - **结构亮点**：教材给了\"双向误解\"的框架——第二批警察以为第一批是学生，第一批以为工人是学生。用交叉对话把这个\"谁才是真的\"的罗生门感写出来。
</Callout>

<Callout type="tip" title="参考范文（自己写完再看）">
  **Arrest the police!**

  The policeman and the workman returned from the pay phone to find the scene in chaos. The other workman, his face red with fury, was still shouting at the remaining policemen and refusing to hand over the pneumatic drill. Two officers had him by the arms.

  At that moment, a second police car arrived with three more officers. Before the first group could explain, the workman who had made the call pointed at them. \"These men aren't real police — they're students in disguise!\" he cried. \"We have it on good authority.\" The new arrivals immediately surrounded the first lot of policemen. \"Identity cards, please. Now.\"

  The first group were stunned. \"Don't be ridiculous,\" one of them protested. \"Those workmen are the students! A young man rang us to say so.\" The workmen, meanwhile, had pulled out driving licences, union cards and pay slips — proof enough that they were genuine road workers. An uneasy silence fell. One of the new arrivals walked over to the pay phone and dialled the police station himself. The desk sergeant confirmed that no officers had been dismissed or impersonated that day.

  Slowly, the truth sank in. The real police looked at one another, then at the workmen, then back at the silent archway where the student had been hiding. There was no one to arrest. They had all — every single one of them — been the victims of the same hoax. *(246 words)*
</Callout>

## 12. 书信写作 Letter writing (≤ 100 词)

<Callout type="info" title="任务">
  A friend has written to you offering you a place in his car on a long trip he intends to make, **providing that you are willing to share expenses**. In not more than **100 words**, write a letter **accepting his offer**. Do not write more than **three paragraphs**.
</Callout>

<Callout type="tip" title="写作脚手架">
  - **称呼**：*Dear + 名*（朋友间用名字）。
  - **第 1 段**：感谢邀请 + 接受 —— *Thank you for your letter and the offer of a place in your car. I would love to come.*
  - **第 2 段**：关于费用分担的回应 —— 表示乐意分摊（*happy to share the petrol / tolls / accommodation*）+ 提一个具体建议（*Shall we agree to split everything 50/50?*）。
  - **第 3 段**：确认细节 + 期待 —— *Let me know the departure date and what I should bring. I'm really looking forward to it.*
  - **结束语**：*Best wishes, / Yours, / [你的名字]*。
  - **时态要点**：**providing that** = on condition that（条件是……）；acceptance 用 *I'd love to* / *I'd be delighted to*；未来安排用一般现在时/现在进行时/I'll。
</Callout>

<Callout type="tip" title="参考范文（自己写完再看）">
  Dear Tom,

  Thank you so much for your letter — I was thrilled to hear about your planned trip, and even more thrilled by your offer of a place in the car. I would love to come.

  Of course I am more than happy to share the expenses. Petrol, road tolls, meals on the way — let's just split everything down the middle. If you keep the receipts, I'll settle my half at the end of each day. That seems the fairest way, but I'm open to whatever arrangement suits you best.

  Do let me know the date you plan to set off, and what sort of things I should pack. I am already looking forward to it.

  Yours,

  Jack *(约 100 词)*
</Callout>

## 13. 拓展练习 Going further

<Callout type="tip" title="自学者建议">
  - **影子跟读 (shadowing)**：用第 1 节的三个 SayIt 按钮跟读 3-5 遍。特别注意 **Only when ... did he realize** 这句——读的时候在 **did** 上给一个微弱的语调上扬，模仿英语母语者在倒装句中的自然节奏。
  - **复述 (retell)**：合上课文，用 90 秒把这场恶作剧的完整逻辑链讲一遍。**五步因果**：学生看见工人 → 两面打电话（骗警察 + 骗工人）→ 躲起来看戏 → 警察到场 / 冲突升级 / 工人发火扬言叫警察 → 警察讽刺说\"你已经被捕了\" → 工人去打真电话 → 真相大白。每步保持过去时。
  - **造句迁移**：用今天的 5 个高价值结构造你自己的句子——
    1. *It has never been explained why ...*（谁也弄不清为什么……）—— *It has never been explained why the flight was delayed for six hours.*
    2. *be grateful to sb. for sth.*（因某事感谢某人）—— *I am deeply grateful to my colleague for covering my shift last week.*
    3. *refuse to do / attempt to do*（拒绝做……/ 试图做……）—— *He refused to comment and attempted to leave the room.*
    4. *lose one's temper*（发脾气）—— *The customer lost his temper when he was kept waiting for an hour.*
    5. *Only when ... did ...*（直到……才……）—— *Only when I saw the exam paper did I realize how little I had studied.*
  - **短语对比卡**：今天 §4 的五组短语（put out/up/up with/off；dress vs dress up；watch vs follow；temper vs mood；except vs except for vs apart from）找一个小本本做成速查卡片，正面写中文，反面写英文短语 + 一个例句——NCE3 越是往后学，这种\"长得像但意思不一样\"的短语越多，早积累早轻松。
  - **延伸思考**：这个故事表面上是一个\"笑到最后\"的恶作剧，骨子里却藏着一个挺妙的叙事技法——**全知视角 + 有限视角的切换**。我们读者从一开始就知道真相（学生捣鬼），但警察和工人一直被蒙在鼓里——这就是\"戏剧反讽\"（dramatic irony）：我们比角色知道得更多，所以越看越有味。下次你写叙事的时候，试试先让读者知道真相，再写角色慢慢发现——这种\"读者替角色着急\"的张力，比\"读者和角色一起蒙在鼓里\"更抓人。
</Callout>
