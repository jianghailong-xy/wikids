## The Text 课文

I stopped to let the car cool off and to study the map. **I had expected to be near my objective by now**, but everything still **seemed alien** to me. I was only five when **my father had taken me abroad**, and that was eighteen years ago. When my mother **had died after a tragic accident**, he did not quickly recover from the shock and loneliness. Everything around him was full of her presence, continually reopening the wound. So he decided to emigrate. In the new country he **became absorbed in** making a new life for the two of us, so that he gradually ceased to grieve. He did not marry again and I **was brought up without a woman's care**; but I lacked for nothing, for he was both father and mother to me. He always meant to go back one day, but not to stay. His roots and mine **had become too firmly embedded** in the new land. But he wanted to see the old folk again and to visit my mother's grave. He became **mortally ill** a few months before we had planned to go and, when he knew that he was dying, he **made me promise to go on my own**.

I hired a car the day after landing and bought a **comprehensive** book of maps, which I found most helpful on the cross-country journey, but which I did not think I should need on the last stage. It was not that I actually remembered anything at all. But my father **had described over and over again** what we should see at every milestone, after leaving the nearest town, so that **I was positive I should recognize it as familiar territory**. Well, I had been wrong, for I was now lost.

I looked at the map and then at the **milometer**. I had come ten miles since leaving the town, and at this point, according to my father, I should be looking at farms and cottages in a valley, with the **spire** of the church of our village showing in the far distance. I could see no valley, no farms, no cottages and no church spire — only a lake. I decided that I must have **taken a wrong turning** somewhere. So I drove back to the town and began to **retrace the route**, taking frequent glances at the map. I landed up at the same corner. The curious thing was that the lake was not marked on the map. I felt as if I had stumbled into a nightmare country, as you sometimes do in dreams. And, as in a nightmare, there was nobody in sight to help me.

Fortunately for me, as I was wondering what to do next, there appeared on the horizon a man on horseback, riding in my direction. I waited till he came near, then I asked him the way to our old village. He said that there was now no village. I thought he must have misunderstood me, so I repeated its name. This time he pointed to the lake. The village no longer existed because **it had been submerged**, and all the valley too. The lake was not a natural one, but **a man-made reservoir**.

## 课文注释 Notes on the text

<Callout type="info" title="7 个值得停下来嚼一嚼的点">
  1. **milometer** (l.19) — \"里程表\"，也拼作 **mileometer**（英式）/ **odometer**（美式），记录汽车行驶的总英里数。词根 **mile（英里）+ -meter（计量器）**。课文里"我"正是靠它读出"已开了十英里"才发现位置不对。
  2. **be / become absorbed in ...** (l.8) — \"全神贯注于……；埋头于……\"。**absorb** 本义\"吸收\"，被动 *be absorbed in* 引申为\"被……吸引、沉浸其中\"。课文 *he became absorbed in making a new life* = 他一头扎进为我们俩重建生活。同型：*She was absorbed in her book. / He is absorbed in his work.*
  3. **Everything around him was full of her presence** (l.6) — 直译\"他周围的一切都充满了她的存在\"，即\"触景生情、处处都是她的影子\"。**presence**（在场、存在）在这里指\"亡妻留下的气息与痕迹\"，与下文 *continually reopening the wound*（不断揭开伤口）呼应。
  4. **lack for nothing** (l.10) — \"什么都不缺\"。**lack** 作动词时通常直接接宾语（*lack money*），但在 *lack for nothing* 这一固定否定表达里带 **for**，意为\"应有尽有、一无所缺\"。课文 *I lacked for nothing, for he was both father and mother to me* = 我什么都不缺，因为他既当爹又当妈。
  5. **It was not that ...** (l.14) — \"并不是说……\"，用来**否定一个可能的误解 / 原因**，后常接 *but ...* 给出真正情况。课文 *It was not that I actually remembered anything* = 并不是我真的记得什么（言下之意：我靠的是父亲的描述）。同型：*It was not that I disliked it, but I had no time.*
  6. **over and over again** (l.16) — \"一遍又一遍地；反反复复地\"，强调**重复多次**。课文 *my father had described over and over again* = 父亲反反复复地描述过。近义：*repeatedly / time and again / again and again*。
  7. **take a wrong turning** (l.22) — \"拐错弯、走错路\"（英式 **turning** = 路口、岔道；美式多用 *turn*）。课文 *I must have taken a wrong turning somewhere* 中的 **must have + 过去分词** 表\"对过去的肯定推测\"——\"我准是在哪里拐错了弯\"。
</Callout>

## 3. 关键句型 过去完成时 & 过去将来时

<Callout type="tip" title="一分钟回顾">
  本课是 NCE3 复习**\"过去之过去\"**的经典篇目。叙述者站在"此刻迷路"这个**过去主线**上，不断回望**更早**发生的往事——这就需要**过去完成时（had + 过去分词）**；而站在"那时"看"那之后会发生的事"，则用**过去将来时（should / would + 动词原形）**。

  | 形式 | 课文例句 | 用途 |
  |---|---|---|
  | **一般过去时**（主线） | *I **stopped** to let the car cool off. / I **was** now lost.* | 旅途此刻的主线叙事 |
  | **过去完成时**（更早） | *my father **had taken** me abroad / my mother **had died** / his roots **had become** embedded / he **had described** ...* | 比主线更早发生 |
  | **过去完成被动** | *the village ... **had been submerged*** | 更早 + 受动者作主语 |
  | **过去将来时** | *what we **should see** / I **should recognize** it / I **should be looking** at farms* | 站在过去看其后的事 |
  | **must have + 过去分词** | *I **must have taken** a wrong turning / he **must have misunderstood** me* | 对过去的肯定推测 |

  ### ⚠️ 4 个本课最容易踩的雷区

  1. **had + 过去分词只在需要"过去之过去"时才用**：主线是 *I was lost*（一般过去）；十八年前\"被带出国\"更早 → *my father **had taken** me abroad*。两件过去事有先后，才动用过去完成。
  2. **英式 should = 美式 would（过去将来）**：第一人称 I / we 后，英式常用 **should** 表过去将来（*I should recognize / we should see*）；换成 would 也对。别把这个 should 当成\"应该\"。
  3. **过去完成的被动**：*had been + 过去分词*。课文 *it **had been submerged*** = 它（早就）被淹没了——\"更早\" + \"被动\"两层叠加。
  4. **must have done ≠ must do**：*I must have taken a wrong turning* 是\"对过去的推测（准是拐错了弯）\"，不是\"必须\"。否定 / 疑问推测则用 *can't have done / could he have ...*。
</Callout>

## 4. 难点：否定的多种说法 No / not...any / 强调否定

<Callout type="info" title="教材改写题：把 it wasn't possible... 改写成 I could see...">
  教材 *Key structures* 给的改写题是：

  > 原句：*It wasn't possible for me to see any valley, any farms, any cottages and any church spire — only a lake.*
  > 改写：***I could see no valley, no farms, no cottages and no church spire — only a lake.***

  这两句意思**完全相同**，但风格不同。把握三组等价关系，否定句就能自由切换：

  | 说法 | 例句 | 语感 |
  |---|---|---|
  | **not ... any + 名词** | *I could **not** see **any** valley.* | 中性、最常见 |
  | **no + 名词** | *I could see **no** valley.* | 更凝练、更书面、语气更重 |
  | **it isn't possible to do** | ***It wasn't possible** for me to see ...* | 强调\"做不到\"这件事本身 |

  课文连用四个 **no**（*no valley, no farms, no cottages and no church spire*）形成**否定排比**，再用破折号甩出 *only a lake* —— 把"什么都没有、只有一片湖"的荒凉与错愕推到极致。

  ### ⚠️ 否定句 4 个雷区

  1. **一个分句里只能有一个否定词**：*I could **not** see **no** valley* ✗（双重否定，英语口语里=肯定或被视为不规范）。要么 *not ... any*，要么 *no*，二选一。
  2. **no = not a / not any**：*no valley* = *not a valley* / *not any valley*；*no farms* = *not any farms*。
  3. **not ... anywhere / nowhere 二选一**：*I could **not** find it **anywhere*** ✓ = *I could find it **nowhere*** ✓；但 *not ... nowhere* ✗。
  4. **no longer**（不再）≠ no（没有）：课文 *The village **no longer** existed* = 村子\"不再\"存在（曾经有、现在没了），与 *no village* 的\"压根没有\"侧重不同。
</Callout>

## 10. 摘要写作 Summary writing (≤ 80 词)

<Callout type="info" title="任务">
  Give an account *in not more than 80 words* of **the author's search for the village**, as narrated in the **last paragraph**. Use your own words as far as possible. **Do not include anything that is not in the paragraph**（只用最后一段的内容）。

  抓住最后一段的事件链：① 看地图 + 里程表 → ② 按父亲的话，本该看到山谷、农舍、教堂尖顶 → ③ 却只有一片湖、地图上还没标 → ④ 折回原路、又回到同一拐角 → ⑤ 出现骑马人 → ⑥ 他说村子没了、指向湖 → ⑦ 村子连同山谷被淹、湖是人造水库。
</Callout>

</Callout>

## 11. 作文 Composition (250–350 词)

<Callout type="info" title="任务">
  Write a composition on **one** of the following subjects; the length should be between **250 and 350 words**.

  - **a.** The house you wish to own.（你想拥有的房子）
  - **b.** A day spent by the sea or by a lake or river.（在海边 / 湖边 / 河边度过的一天）

  下面以 **b** 为例搭脚手架——正好与课文那片\"湖\"形成温柔的呼应。
</Callout>

<Callout type="warning" title="提示骨架（写 b 题）">
  - **开头（设景）**：哪一天、和谁、去了哪片水域、天气如何 —— 一句话把读者\"放\"到水边。
  - **上午（动作）**：到达、第一眼的印象（光、风、水声）、做了什么（游泳 / 划船 / 钓鱼 / 沿岸散步）。
  - **中午（细节）**：野餐、与当地人 / 同伴的一个小插曲、一个意想不到的小发现。
  - **下午（高潮）**：一件最难忘的事（一场骤雨、一次落日、一条上钩的鱼、一段安静的独处）。
  - **结尾（升华）**：黄昏离开时的心情 —— 这一天给你留下了什么。
</Callout>

<Callout type="tip" title="写作脚手架">
  - **时态主线**：一般过去时叙事（*we set off / I swam / the rain came*）；过去完成表更早（*the sun had already risen*）；过去进行表背景（*the water was glittering / birds were calling*）。
  - **写"水"的名词库**：*ripples, shallows, the far bank, the surface, a breeze off the water, reeds, pebbles, the wake of a boat*。
  - **写"动作"的动词库**：*waded in, struck out across, drifted, cast a line, skimmed stones, basked on the warm rocks, dozed off, packed up*。
  - **写"光与色"的形容词库**：*glassy, shimmering, golden, amber at dusk, still as a mirror, dappled with sunlight*。
  - **结构亮点**：用一句\"以景结情\"收尾 —— *As we drove away, the lake was already turning to amber, and I knew I would carry that stillness home with me.*
</Callout>

<Callout type="tip" title="参考范文（自己写完再看）">
  Last summer my family spent a whole day beside a quiet mountain lake, two hours from the city. We set off early, while the air was still cool, and by the time we arrived the sun had already climbed above the far hills. The water lay glassy and still, dappled with sunlight, and a faint breeze carried the smell of pine across the shallows.

  In the morning my brother and I waded in and struck out towards a little wooded island. The water was colder than I had expected, but wonderfully clear; I could see pebbles glinting far below. Afterwards we lay basking on the warm rocks until our father called us back for lunch. We ate sandwiches under a willow, sharing our crumbs with a pair of bold ducks that paddled right up to our feet.

  In the afternoon the weather changed. Clouds gathered, and a sudden shower sent us scrambling for shelter beneath the trees. We watched the rain stipple the surface of the lake, and when it passed, everything seemed washed and new. My father, who had brought a rod, cast a line and, to our delight, landed a small silver fish, which he gently returned to the water.

  As the light softened, we packed up slowly, reluctant to leave. The lake had turned to amber in the setting sun, and the only sound was the lapping of tiny waves against the shore. Driving home in the dusk, tired and sunburnt, I felt that I had carried a little of that stillness away with me — a perfect, unhurried day by the water. *(266 words)*
</Callout>

## 12. 书信写作 Letter writing (80–100 词)

<Callout type="info" title="任务">
  Write a letter between **80 and 100 words** on **one** of the following subjects. Make the **beginning and ending** like those of an ordinary letter. Write the **postal address in full** at the head of your letter, but do not count it in the word total.

  - **a.** You are ill and cannot go to work for a few days. Write explaining your absence and making arrangements for your work while you are absent.（生病请假并安排工作）
  - **b.** You have received a gift from an old friend abroad. Write a letter of thanks, say how you will make use of the gift and briefly give news of yourself.（感谢国外老友的礼物）

  下面以 **b** 为例。
</Callout>

<Callout type="tip" title="写作脚手架（写 b 题）">
  - **地址 + 日期**：右上角写完整通信地址，下面写日期（不计入词数）。
  - **称呼**：*Dear + 名*（老友用名字，亲切）。
  - **开头（致谢）**：*I was delighted to receive the beautiful ... you sent me. It was so kind of you to remember ...*
  - **主体**：① 礼物如何 / 多喜欢 → ② 打算怎么用它（*I shall ... / It will take pride of place on ...*）→ ③ 简单报近况（工作 / 学习 / 生活）。
  - **结尾**：*Do write soon and tell me your news. / With love and many thanks, / Yours, / [名字]*
  - **时态混搭**：*was delighted / sent*（过去）+ *shall use / will*（将来）+ *am working / things are going well*（现在）。
</Callout>

<Callout type="tip" title="参考范文（自己写完再看）">
  17 Maple Road, Oxford OX2 6QT

  10th June

  Dear Tomas,

  I was delighted to receive the beautiful hand-painted box you sent for my birthday — it arrived safely last week. It was so thoughtful of you to remember the date. I have already placed it on my desk, where I shall keep my letters and photographs, so a little of Prague will sit beside me every day.

  With love and many thanks,

  Anna *(92 words)*
</Callout>

## 13. 拓展练习 Going further

<Callout type="tip" title="自学者建议">
  - **影子跟读 (shadowing)**：用第 1 节的三个 SayIt 按钮各跟读 3-5 遍。重点体会两处：① *... and that was eighteen years ago* 句末的下沉感（回忆的份量）；② 结尾 *... it had been submerged, and all the valley too* 里 *and all the valley too* 作为补充被轻轻\"甩\"在后面的语调。
  - **复述 (retell)**：合上课文，用 2 分钟讲完整个故事。**叙事三段**：缘起（母亲惨死 → 父亲移民 → 临终托\"我\"回乡扫墓）→ 寻路（租车 + 地图 + 凭父亲描述找路 → 迷路）→ 真相（骑马人 → 村庄连山谷被淹成人造水库 → 母亲的坟也沉在水下）。
  - **造句迁移**：用今天的 5 个高价值结构造你自己的句子——
    1. *S + had + 过去分词 + when / before + 一般过去*（过去之过去）—— *The film had already started when we arrived.*
    2. *S + must have + 过去分词*（对过去的推测）—— *He must have forgotten our appointment.*
    3. *no + 名词 (= not ... any)*（凝练否定）—— *There was no bread and no milk in the house.*
    4. *become absorbed in + 名词 / V-ing*（埋头于）—— *She soon became absorbed in her new project.*
    5. *it had been + 过去分词*（过去完成被动）—— *By the time I got there, the door had been locked.*
  - **延伸思考**：本课最动人的地方在于**\"过去完成时\"承载的情感重量**——叙述者站在\"迷路的此刻\"，一层层往回看：父亲十八年前带他出走、母亲更早死于车祸、村庄不知何时被淹。每一个 *had done* 都像往时间深处投下一块石头。结尾的反转——千里归乡，故土却沉在湖底，母亲的坟再也找不到——把\"乡愁\"推向了\"连可凭吊之物都已不存\"的更深的失落。下次写英文叙事时记住：**用一般过去推进主线，用过去完成铺垫往昔，用过去将来交代\"那时所期待的未来\"**——三层时间叠在一起，故事才有了纵深。
</Callout>
