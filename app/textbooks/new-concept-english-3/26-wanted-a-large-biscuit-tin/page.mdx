## The Text 课文

**No one can avoid being influenced by** advertisements. **Much as we may pride ourselves on** our good taste, we **are no longer free to choose** the things we want, for advertising **exerts a subtle influence on** us. In their efforts to persuade us to buy this or that product, advertisers have **made a close study of** human nature and have classified all our little weaknesses.

Advertisers discovered years ago that all of us **love to get something for nothing**. An advertisement which begins with the magic word FREE **can rarely go wrong**. These days, advertisers not only offer free samples, but free cars, free houses, and free trips round the world as well. They devise hundreds of competitions which will enable us to win huge sums of money. Radio and television have **made it possible for** advertisers **to capture the attention of** millions of people.

During a radio programme, a company of biscuit manufacturers once asked listeners to bake biscuits and send them to their factory. They offered to pay $10 a pound for the biggest biscuit baked by a listener. **The response to this competition was tremendous**. Before long, biscuits of all shapes and sizes began arriving at the factory. One lady brought in a biscuit on a wheelbarrow. It weighed nearly 500 pounds. A little later, a man came along with a biscuit which occupied the whole boot of his car. All the biscuits that were sent were carefully weighed. The largest was 713 pounds. It seemed certain that this would win the prize. But just before the competition closed, a lorry arrived at the factory with a truly colossal biscuit which weighed 2,400 pounds. It had been baked by a college student who had used over 1,000 pounds of flour, 800 pounds of sugar, 200 pounds of fat, and 400 pounds of various other ingredients. It was so heavy that a crane **had to be used to remove it** from the lorry. The manufacturers had to pay **more money than they had anticipated**, for they bought the biscuit from the student for $24,000.

## 课文注释 Notes on the text

<Callout type="info" title="5 个值得停下来嚼一嚼的点">
  1. **No one can avoid being influenced by advertisements** (l.1) — “没有人能避免受广告的影响”。**avoid + 动名词**（avoid 后只接 V-ing，不接不定式）；**being influenced** 是**动名词的被动式**（being + 过去分词），因为主语是“被影响”的一方。同型：*avoid being seen / avoid being caught*。
  2. **Much as we may pride ourselves on ...** (l.2) — “尽管我们可以自夸……”。**much as** = although / however much，引导**让步状语从句**；**pride oneself on sth.** = 以……自豪（介词是 **on**，不是 of）。这是一种把表语 / 状语提前的**倒装让步**结构。同型：*Hard as he tried, he failed.（= Although he tried hard）*
  3. **get something for nothing** (l.7) — “免费得到东西；不劳而获”。for nothing = 免费地 / 白白地。课文 *all of us love to get something for nothing* 道破了广告利用的人性弱点。
  4. **can rarely go wrong** (l.8) — “很少会出错 / 出岔子”。**go wrong** = 出毛病、出差错（go 作系动词，后接形容词，表“变得……”）。同类系动词：*go bad（变质）/ turn yellow（变黄）/ grow quiet（变安静）*。
  5. **made it possible for advertisers to capture the attention of ...** (l.11) — “使广告商得以吸引……的注意力”。**make it + 形容词 + for sb. + to do**：**it 是形式宾语**，真正的宾语是后面的不定式 *to capture ...*。同型：*The Internet has made it easy for us to learn anything.*
</Callout>

## 3. 关键句型 The gerund 动名词作宾语

<Callout type="tip" title="一分钟回顾">
  本课开篇 *No one can **avoid being influenced** by advertisements* 就是一记“动名词重拳”。**动名词（V-ing 当名词用）**最常见的考点，是“哪些词后面只能跟 V-ing、不能跟不定式”。把它们打包记牢：

  | 用法 | 触发词 / 句型 | 课文 / 例句 |
  |---|---|---|
  | **特定动词后** | avoid / enjoy / mind / finish / keep / practise / give up / can't help / consider | *avoid **being** influenced；can't help **laughing*** |
  | **介词后** | on / in / of / for / without / before / after + V-ing | *pride ourselves **on** having...；good at **swimming*** |
  | **固定句型** | It's no use / It's not worth / There's no point in / Would you mind ...? | *It's no use **crying**；Would you mind **opening** the window?* |
  | **被动动名词** | being + 过去分词 | *avoid **being seen**；enjoy **being praised*** |

  ### ⚠️ 4 个本课最容易踩的雷区

  1. **avoid / enjoy / mind / finish / can't help 后只能跟动名词**，绝不能跟不定式：*avoid being influenced* ✓ / ~~avoid to be influenced~~ ✗。
  2. **被动动名词 = being + V-ed**：主语是动作的承受者时用。*No one can avoid **being influenced***（人是“被影响”的一方）。
  3. **介词后一律用 V-ing**：*pride ourselves on **having** good taste*；*love + V-ing*（love getting something for nothing）。注意 *want / hope / decide / wish / refuse* 后接**不定式**，别混。
  4. **It's no use / not worth + V-ing**：固定搭配，主语用形式主语 *it*。*It's no use **arguing** with him.* / *The film is worth **seeing**.*
</Callout>

## 4. 难点：让步结构与系动词 + 形容词

<Callout type="info" title="2 组教材 Special Difficulties 必考点">
  **① 让步状语：Much as / adj-as-主语-may-be（倒装让步）**

  课文 *Much as we may pride ourselves on our good taste, we are no longer free ...* 用的是一种**把强调词提到 as 之前**的让步结构，比 although 更有文采：

  | 形式 | 含义 | 例句 |
  |---|---|---|
  | **Much as + S + V** | 尽管很…… | *Much as I like it, I won't buy it.* |
  | **adj + as + S + be** | 虽然（很）…… | ***Proud as** we may be, we still get fooled.* |
  | **n + as + S + be** | 虽然是…… | ***Child as** he was, he was brave.（尽管还是个孩子）* |
  | **V + as + S + may** | 无论怎样…… | ***Try as** he might, he failed.（无论怎么努力）* |

  ⚠️ 这种结构**自带“虽然”**，前面**不能再加 although / though**；后半句也**不能再加 but**。

  **② 系动词 + 形容词：go / turn / grow / become 表“变得……”**

  课文 *can rarely **go wrong*** 里的 go 是**系动词**，“变得出错”。这类“变化类系动词”后接形容词：

  - **go** + （多为不好的变化）：*go wrong（出错）/ go bad（变质）/ go mad（发疯）*
  - **turn** + （颜色 / 性质突变）：*turn yellow（变黄）/ turn pale（变苍白）/ turn sour（变酸）*
  - **grow** + （渐变）：*grow quiet（变安静）/ grow old（变老）/ grow dark（天渐黑）*
  - ⚠️ 系动词后跟**形容词**（不是副词）：*go bad* ✓ / ~~go badly~~（“做得糟”是另一意思）。
</Callout>

## 10. 摘要写作 Summary writing (≤ 80 词)

<Callout type="info" title="任务">
  *In not more than 80 words* write an account of **the competition organized by a company of biscuit manufacturers**. Use your own words as far as possible. **Do not include anything that is not in the last paragraph**（只写最后一段：从竞赛规则到学生夺魁）。

  按下面 6 个提示点串成一段：

  1. What did the biscuit company ask listeners to do, and what did they offer?
  2. How big was the response?
  3. What kinds of biscuits started arriving, and how?
  4. Which biscuit seemed certain to win at first?
  5. What arrived just before the competition closed, and who had baked it?
  6. How much did the manufacturers finally have to pay, and why?
</Callout>

<Callout type="tip" title="参考范文（自己写完再看）">
  On a radio programme, a biscuit company offered ten dollars a pound for the biggest biscuit a listener could bake. The response was tremendous, and biscuits of every shape and size soon arrived. A 713-pound biscuit seemed sure to win, until a student delivered a colossal one of 2,400 pounds by lorry. A crane was needed to unload it. Buying it at ten dollars a pound, the manufacturers had to pay him twenty-four thousand dollars — far more than expected. *(79 words)*
</Callout>

## 11. 作文 Composition (约 250 词)

<Callout type="info" title="任务">
  In not more than **250 words** describe **a radio quiz show organized by a firm of soap manufacturers**. Provide a suitable title. Do not write more than **two paragraphs**.

  ***Introduction***: A member of the audience is asked up to ten questions. Each time he answers correctly, he may take the money or ask for a harder question. The minimum prize is £2 for the first; it keeps doubling, up to a maximum of £2,048 for ten correct answers.

  ***Development & Conclusion***: A man is questioned in front of the audience — questions of all kinds (spelling, general knowledge, etc.). Excitement mounts as he answers question after question until he reaches the last one.
</Callout>

<Callout type="warning" title="提示骨架（两段对应两个画面）">
  - **第 1 段 · 规则与开场**：介绍节目与奖金规则 —— *Each Friday evening, the makers of Sunbright Soap fill the studio for "Double or Decide". The rules are simple but cruel: answer, and either pocket the money or gamble it on a harder question — two pounds, then four, eight, sixteen ... up to two thousand and forty-eight.*
  - **第 2 段 · 紧张与高潮**：聚焦一位选手层层闯关 —— from easy spelling to a fiendish final question; the audience holds its breath; will he take the money or risk it all on the tenth question?
</Callout>

<Callout type="tip" title="写作脚手架">
  - **时态主线**：一般现在时描述节目惯例（*Each week a contestant is invited ...*）；用一般过去时讲某一场具体比赛（*Last Friday, a young teacher walked up ...*）。
  - **“紧张气氛”词库**：*the audience held its breath, tension mounted, you could hear a pin drop, hearts raced, all eyes were on him, the clock ticked*。
  - **“答题闯关”词库**：*sailed through the first questions, hesitated, took a gamble, doubled his money, risked the lot, played safe and pocketed it*。
  - **结构亮点**：用**倍增的奖金**做节奏——*two pounds became four, four became eight, and still he would not stop.*（三连递进，把“贪欲与紧张”写活）。
</Callout>

<Callout type="tip" title="参考范文（自己写完再看）">
  **Double or Decide**

  Every Friday evening, the makers of Sunbright Soap pack a studio full of hopeful contestants for their radio quiz, "Double or Decide". The rules are simple but merciless. A member of the audience is asked up to ten questions. The first correct answer is worth a mere two pounds; but each time the contestant answers, he must decide whether to take the money or stake it all on a harder question. Two pounds become four, four become eight, and so the prize doubles its way towards a glittering maximum of two thousand and forty-eight.

  Last Friday a quiet young teacher walked up to the microphone. He spelt impossible words, named forgotten rivers, and dated ancient battles, while the audience held its breath. Question after question fell to him, and still he would not stop. By the ninth he had won over a thousand pounds. "Take it and go home," whispered his wife from the front row. But the tenth question glittered before him like the biggest biscuit of all. He hesitated, smiled, and said, "I'll risk it." The whole studio leaned forward. You could have heard a pin drop as the quizmaster, slowly and cruelly, began to read out the final question. *(198 words)*
</Callout>

## 12. 书信写作 Letter writing

<Callout type="info" title="任务">
  Write a letter of about **100 words** in **three paragraphs** informing a friend that **you will be moving into his neighbourhood**. Ask him to **help you find accommodation**.
</Callout>

<Callout type="tip" title="写作脚手架">
  - **第 1 段（开头 / 报喜）**：寒暄 + 宣布消息 —— *I have some news that I think will please you: I am moving to your part of town next month!*
  - **第 2 段（说明 / 请求）**：交代缘由 + 提出请求 —— *I have taken a new job in the city centre. Since you know the area so well, could you keep an eye out for a small flat to rent, somewhere near you?*
  - **第 3 段（具体要求 + 结尾）**：列条件 + 表感谢 —— *Ideally, two rooms, not too expensive, and within walking distance of the station. I'd be very grateful for any help. I look forward to being neighbours at last!*
  - **称呼 / 结束语**：*Dear + 名字, ... Yours, / Best wishes, + 你的名字*。
  - **时态混搭**：*will be moving / am moving*（将来）；*have taken*（现在完成，已定下的事）；*could you ...?*（委婉请求）。
</Callout>

<Callout type="tip" title="参考范文（自己写完再看）">
  Dear Tom,

  I have some news that I think will delight you: next month I am moving into your neighbourhood at last! I have just been offered a job in the city centre, and your side of town is perfect for it.

  As you know the area far better than I do, I wonder if you could help me find somewhere to live. I am looking for a small flat to rent — two rooms would be enough — that is reasonably cheap and within walking distance of the station.

  Do let me know if you hear of anything. I can't wait to be neighbours!

  Best wishes,

  Daniel *(101 words)*
</Callout>

## 13. 拓展练习 Going further

<Callout type="tip" title="自学者建议">
  - **影子跟读 (shadowing)**：用第 1 节的三个 SayIt 按钮跟读 3-5 遍。重点练**动名词的弱读**（avoid be**ing** influenced 里 being 轻读）和**让步从句的语调**（*Much as we may pride ourselves...* 前半句语调上扬“留悬念”，逗号后落下）。
  - **复述 (retell)**：合上课文，用 2 分钟把全文讲一遍。**两段式**：先讲“广告如何操纵人心”（研究弱点 → 利用“免费” → 借电台电视）；再讲“饼干大赛”这个反讽案例（征集 → 巨型饼干压轴 → 多付 $24,000）。
  - **造句迁移**：用今天的 5 个本课结构造你自己的句子——
    1. *avoid / enjoy / mind + 动名词*（动名词作宾语）—— *I couldn't help laughing at the joke.*
    2. *Much as / adj-as-S-may-be*（倒装让步）—— *Tired as he was, he kept working.*
    3. *pride oneself on + 动名词*（介词后用 V-ing）—— *She prides herself on speaking three languages.*
    4. *make it + adj + for sb. + to do*（形式宾语 it）—— *The app makes it easy for students to revise.*
    5. *so + adj + that + 结果*（结果状语从句）—— *The box was so heavy that I couldn't lift it.*
  - **延伸思考**：本课是一篇**“软批判”的说明文**——先冷静地分析广告如何“研究人性、利用弱点”，再用一个**饼干大赛搬起石头砸自己脚**的趣闻收尾，让“贪小便宜”的讽刺意味不言自明。好的论说文往往就是这样：**道理讲一半，留一个故事让读者自己笑出来。**下次你写“利弊分析”类作文时，不妨学这一手——观点 + 一个反转的小例子，比一味说教有力得多。另一个值得回味的点：全文反复出现 **free / for nothing / something for nothing**——广告许诺的“免费”，最后却让厂商**付出 $24,000**，“免费”二字的双关，正是这篇小品的题眼。
</Callout>
