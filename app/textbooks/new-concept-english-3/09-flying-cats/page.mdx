## The Text 课文

Cats **never fail to** fascinate human beings. They can be friendly and affectionate towards humans, but they lead mysterious lives of their own as well. They never become submissive **like** dogs and horses. As a result, humans have learned to respect feline independence. Most cats remain suspicious of humans all their lives. One of the things that fascinates us most about cats is the popular belief that they have nine lives. Apparently, there is a good deal of truth in this idea. A cat's ability to survive falls is **based on** fact.

Recently the New York Animal Medical Centre made a study of 132 cats over a period of five months. All these cats had one experience **in common**: they had fallen off high buildings, yet only eight of them died from shock or injuries. Of course, New York is the ideal place for **such an interesting study**, because there is no shortage of tall buildings. There are plenty of high-rise windowsills to fall from! One cat, Sabrina, fell 32 storeys, yet only suffered from a broken tooth. 'Cats behave like well-trained paratroopers,' a doctor said. It seems that **the further cats fall, the less they are likely to injure themselves**. In a long drop, they reach speeds of 60 miles an hour and more. At high speeds, falling cats have time to relax. They stretch out their legs **like** flying squirrels. This increases their air-resistance and reduces the shock of impact when they hit the ground.

## 课文注释 Notes on the text

<Callout type="info" title="6 个值得停下来嚼一嚼的点">
  1. **never fail to + V** (l.1) — **双重否定表肯定**：never（从不）+ fail to（未能）= 总是能。课文 *Cats never fail to fascinate human beings* = 猫**总是**能引起人们的极大兴趣。同型：*She never fails to call on Sundays.*（她周日总会打电话）。
  2. **be based on + 名词** (l.8) — “以……为基础、有……为依据”。课文 *A cat's ability ... is based on fact* = 这种说法是**有事实依据**的。常出现在学术 / 新闻报道里："The conclusion **is based on** evidence from ..."。
  3. **have ... in common** (l.10) — “有……共同之处”。课文 *All these cats had one experience **in common*** = 这些猫有**一个共同的**经历。常见搭配：*have a lot in common with sb. / have nothing in common*。
  4. **the further ..., the less ...** (l.14) — **“the + 比较级, the + 比较级”** 结构，表达“**越……越……**”的因果关系。课文 *The further cats fall, the less they are likely to injure themselves*。同型经典：*The more, the merrier. / The harder you work, the more you earn.*
  5. **like + 名词** (l.13, l.15) — *like* 在课文里**两次**作介词，意思“像……一样”：*behave like well-trained paratroopers / stretch out their legs like flying squirrels*。后接**名词**或**代词宾格**（不接句子）。**别和 as 混**：*as + 句子* = 按照……；*like + 名词* = 像……。
  6. **be likely to + V** (l.14) — “很可能……”。**likely 是形容词**，结构 *S + be + likely + to V*。⚠️ 别说成 ~~S is possible to V~~（中式英语）——possible 不能直接接 S+to V，要 *It is possible that S + V*。
</Callout>

## 3. 关键句型 Simple present for habits & general truths 一般现在时表习惯与普遍真理

<Callout type="tip" title="一分钟回顾">
  本课全篇用**一般现在时**讨论“猫这种动物的本性”——这是英语里讲述**普遍真理 / 习惯 / 状态**的最自然时态。第一段几乎每个动词都是一般现在时：

  *Cats **fascinate** ... They **lead** mysterious lives ... They **never become** submissive ... Most cats **remain** suspicious ... they **have** nine lives ... a cat's ability **is based on** fact.*

  ### 一般现在时的 4 大用法

  | 用法 | 例子 |
  |---|---|
  | **普遍真理 / 自然规律** | Cats **have** nine lives. / Water **boils** at 100°. |
  | **习惯 / 反复动作**（常配 always, usually, often, every day） | They **stretch out** their legs in mid-air. |
  | **状态、特征**（常用状态动词 be, have, know, seem） | Cats **are** suspicious of humans. |
  | **科学叙述 / 新闻标题** | Air-resistance **increases**, and the shock **is reduced**. |

  ### ⚠️ 三个最容易踩的雷

  1. **第三人称单数 + s**：cats fascinate（无 s）/ a cat **fascinates**（加 s）/ 第三人称单数动词加 -s / -es / -ies。
  2. **状态动词不用进行时**：~~Cats are remaining suspicious~~ → Cats **remain** suspicious。状态动词包括 *know, believe, like, love, contain, belong, seem, appear*。
  3. **一般现在 vs 现在完成**：普遍真理用一般现在；强调"至今为止经历过"用现在完成（*Humans **have learned** to respect feline independence* —— 这是“积累至今的认识”用完成时）。

  ### Exercise：判断时态

  课文练习 B 要求填出下列动词的正确形式：
  - Most cats **remain** suspicious of humans all their lives.
  - One of the things that **fascinates** us most about cats is the popular belief that they **have** nine lives.
  - Apparently, there **is** a good deal of truth in this idea.
  - A cat's ability to survive falls **is based** on fact.

  规律一致：**普遍事实 → 全部用一般现在时**（被动也是 is + V-ed 而不是 has been V-ed）。
</Callout>

## 4. 难点：so vs such 形容词强化结构

<Callout type="tip" title="一图看懂 so vs such">
  两个词都表“如此 / 这么”，但**搭配的中心词不同**：

  | 模式 | 接什么 | 例子 |
  |---|---|---|
  | **so + adj/adv** | 形容词 / 副词 | This study **is so interesting**. / He drives **so fast**. |
  | **such + (a/an) + (adj) + 名词** | 名词短语 | It's **such an interesting study**. / They are **such interesting studies**. |

  ### 对比记忆 4 组

  | 单数可数 | 复数 / 不可数 |
  |---|---|
  | The study is **so** interesting. | The studies are **so** interesting. |
  | It's **such an** interesting study. | They are **such** interesting studies. |

  ### so + many/much/few/little + 名词

  例外！**so**（不是 such）+ many / much / few / little + 名词：
  - **so many** people（这么多的人）
  - **so much** time（这么多的时间）
  - **so few** mistakes（这么少的错误）
  - **so little** food（这么少的食物）

  ### so / such ... that ... 结果状语从句

  - It was **so** cold **that** we stayed indoors.
  - It was **such** a cold day **that** we stayed indoors.

  ### 课文里另一个有趣的句型

  *They found it **so difficult** to study the behaviour of cats.*
  *They had **such difficulty** studying the behaviour of cats.*

  - **so + adj + to V**：找它（it 形式宾语）多难——形容词

## 10. 摘要写作 Summary writing (≤ 80 词)

<Callout type="info" title="任务">
  In not more than **80 words** describe **the study of cats made by the New York Animal Medical Centre**. Do not include anything that is not in the last paragraph（仅用 l.9 起的内容）。

  按下面 6 个提示点串成一段：

  1. How many cats did the New York Animal Medical Centre make a study of?
  2. What had all these cats done?
  3. How many of these cats had been killed?
  4. What happened to a cat called Sabrina?
  5. What do cats do when falling at speeds of 60 miles an hour or more?
  6. What effect does this have?
</Callout>

<Callout type="tip" title="参考范文（自己写完再看）">
  The New York Animal Medical Centre studied 132 cats over five months. All had fallen from tall buildings, yet only eight died from shock or injuries. One cat, Sabrina, fell 32 storeys and escaped with no more than a broken tooth. At speeds of 60 mph or more, falling cats appear to relax and stretch out their legs like flying squirrels. This greatly increases their air-resistance and softens the impact when they finally hit the ground. *(76 words)*
</Callout>

## 11. 作文 Composition (约 150 词扩写)

<Callout type="info" title="任务">
  Write an account of **cats as pets**. Expand the skeleton below into a paragraph of about **150 words**.
</Callout>

<Callout type="warning" title="提示骨架（每个省略号都得自己填）">
  - Human beings like to keep dogs and **...** Dogs are **...**, but cats like to **...**
  - Cats are especially lovable when they are still kittens. As kittens they **...**
  - That's how they learn to **...** A lot of people keep cats so they **...**
  - As kittens grow into cats, they like to **...** They like regular meal times and enjoy **...**
  - However, they also **...** During the day time and at night they like to **...**
  - They often have fights with other cats. Sometimes during the night **...**
  - Cats are very clean animals. You often see them **...** Human beings **...**
</Callout>

<Callout type="tip" title="写作脚手架">
  - **时态主线**：**全篇一般现在时**——讲普遍特征 / 习惯。这点本课课文已示范。
  - **猫的行为词库**：*purr, knead with their paws, pounce on, stalk, prowl, slink along the wall, curl up, doze in the sun, groom themselves*。
  - **对比 dogs vs cats**：*Dogs are loyal companions, but cats prefer to keep their own company. / Dogs come when called; cats come when they feel like it. / Dogs depend on us; cats merely tolerate us.*
  - **结尾的小升华**：可以扣回课文里 *humans have learned to respect feline independence*——以“我们其实是被猫养着”作幽默收尾。
</Callout>

<Callout type="tip" title="参考范文（自己写完再看）">
  Human beings like to keep dogs and cats as pets. Dogs are loyal and obedient companions, but cats prefer to live very much on their own terms. Cats are especially lovable when they are still kittens. As kittens they pounce on anything that moves — a ball of wool, a shoelace, even a beam of sunlight. That is how they learn to hunt. A lot of people keep cats so that mice and rats are kept away. As kittens grow into cats, they like to roam. They enjoy regular meal times and a warm lap to curl up on. However, they also disappear for hours on end. During the day and at night they like to prowl the rooftops, and they often have fights with other cats. Sometimes during the night you hear their cries from the garden. Cats are very clean animals. You often see them grooming themselves for hours, while human beings, in turn, must learn to share their homes on the cat's terms. *(159 words)*
</Callout>

## 12. 书信写作 Letter writing

<Callout type="info" title="任务">
  You borrowed a book from a friend but your baby tore some of the pages. Write a letter of **about 80 words** **offering to replace it**. Supply a suitable Introduction and Conclusion. Use the following ideas to write the **Purpose**:
  - Very sorry for what has happened
  - Had left book on low table — baby got hold of it — tore several pages
  - You have bought another book and are sending it
</Callout>

<Callout type="tip" title="结构模板：道歉 + 补偿信三段式">
  | 段落 | 作用 | 常用句式 |
  |---|---|---|
  | **Introduction** | 切题道歉 | *I am writing with a rather awkward confession ... / I owe you an apology — and an explanation ... / I have some bad news about the book you lent me ...* |
  | **Purpose** | 说清事情经过 + 提出补偿 | *I'm afraid I had left it on the coffee table, and before I knew it the baby had got hold of it and torn several pages. / I went straight to the bookshop and bought a fresh copy, which I am posting along with this letter.* |
  | **Conclusion** | 再致歉 + 期待原谅 | *Once again, my sincerest apologies. / I do hope the new copy makes up for it. / Please forgive the mishap — and the baby!* |
</Callout>

<Callout type="tip" title="参考范文（自己写完再看）">
  **Dear Susan,**

  I owe you both an apology and a small parcel. I'm afraid the book you so kindly lent me last month came to a rather sad end.

  I had left it on the low table by the sofa for a moment, and before I knew what was happening, baby Sam had got hold of it and torn out several of the middle pages. I felt dreadful — I know how much that copy meant to you.

  I went straight to Foyles this morning and bought a fresh copy, which I am posting along with this letter. I do hope it makes up for the damage, at least a little.

  Once again, my sincerest apologies — and please give Sam a chance to grow out of his book-eating phase!

  **With love,**
  **Helen** *(135 words — over the 80-word target; for the exam version, trim to the Purpose middle paragraph alone)*
</Callout>

## 13. 拓展练习 Going further

<Callout type="tip" title="自学者建议">
  - **影子跟读 (shadowing)**：用第 1 节的三个 SayIt 按钮跟读 3-5 遍。重点感受 *the further ... the less ...* 的**对称节奏**——两个 the 都重读，比较级位置在两个分句对应位置。
  - **复述 (retell)**：合上课文，**两段**复述。第一段 = 猫的本性（mysterious / independent / suspicious / 9 lives）；第二段 = 纽约研究（132 cats / 5 months / only 8 died / Sabrina 32 storeys / paratrooper analogy / 60 mph / air-resistance）。
  - **造句迁移**：用今天的 5 个高价值结构造你自己的句子——
    1. *S + never fail to + V*（双重否定表肯定）—— *He never fails to keep his promise.*
    2. *S + be based on + n.*（基于）—— *The film is based on a true story.*
    3. *S + have ... in common*（有共同点）—— *Twins often have a lot in common.*
    4. *The + 比较级 ..., the + 比较级 ...*（越……越……）—— *The older I get, the more I appreciate silence.*
    5. *S + behave / look + like + n.*（像……一样）—— *He behaves like a child when he is tired.*
  - **延伸思考**：本课展示了**科普写作**的典型套路——先抛出**通俗信念**（猫有九条命）→ 引用**机构权威研究**（New York Animal Medical Centre）→ 给出**具体数字**（132 cats / 8 died / 32 storeys）→ 用**专家引语**强化（doctor said）→ 最后揭示**机理**（air-resistance）。这种 “belief → study → numbers → quote → mechanism” 的写法是 *Scientific American*、*BBC Science* 的标配，背下来比单纯背单词更有用。
</Callout>
