## The Text 课文

There was a time when the owners of shops and businesses in Chicago **had to pay** large sums of money to gangsters **in return for** 'protection'. If the money was not paid promptly, the gangsters would quickly **put a man out of business** by destroying his shop. Obtaining 'protection money' is not a modern crime. **As long ago as** the fourteenth century, an Englishman, Sir John Hawkwood, made the remarkable discovery that **people would rather pay large sums of money than have their life work destroyed** by gangsters.

Six hundred years ago, Sir John Hawkwood arrived in Italy with a band of soldiers and settled near Florence. He soon **made a name for himself** and came to be known to the Italians as Giovanni Acuto. Whenever the Italian city-states **were at war with** each other, Hawkwood used to hire his soldiers to princes who were willing to pay the high price he demanded. **In times of peace**, when business was bad, Hawkwood and his men would march into a city-state and, after burning down a few farms, would offer to go away if protection money was paid to them. Hawkwood made large sums of money in this way. In spite of this, the Italians regarded him as **a sort of hero**. When he died at the age of eighty, the Florentines gave him a state funeral and **had a picture painted** which was dedicated to the memory of 'the most valiant soldier and most notable leader, Signor Giovanni Haukodue'.

## 课文注释 Notes on the text

<Callout type="info" title="6 个值得停下来嚼一嚼的点">
  1. **in return for ...** (l.2) — "作为……的回报 / 报答"。课文 *pay large sums of money to gangsters **in return for** 'protection'* = 付钱给歹徒以换取"保护"。同型：*She helped me in return for a favour. / I'll give you my old bike in return for two hours of homework help.* 注意 **in return for + 名词 / V-ing**，不接 to V。
  2. **out of business** (l.4) — "倒闭、破产"。*put sb. out of business* = 让某人破产 / 关门；*go out of business* = （自己）倒闭。课文 *put a man out of business by destroying his shop* = 砸店把人逼破产。**out of** 在这里表"脱离（某种状态）"——同型：*out of work*（失业）、*out of school*（辍学）、*out of fashion*（过时）。
  3. **as long ago as** (l.5) — "**早在……（之久）**"——表示**追溯到一个令人惊讶的久远时间点**。注意要与 *as long as* (只要 / 长达) 分开！课文 *As long ago as the fourteenth century* = 早在 14 世纪。同型：*As long ago as 1066, William the Conqueror invaded England.* 而 *as long as* 是：*As long as you pay, we'll leave.*（只要你付钱）/ *He stayed as long as a week.*（长达一周）。
  4. **would rather pay ... than have ... destroyed** (ll.7-8) — **本课题眼之一！would rather A than B** = "宁愿 A 也不愿 B"。课文 *people would rather pay large sums of money **than** have their life work destroyed* = 人们宁愿支付大笔钱也不愿毕生心血被毁。**A 和 B 必须是同一时态/同一形式**——这里 A = pay (V 原形)，B = have ... destroyed (V 原形 + 复合宾语)。
  5. **made a name for himself** (l.10) — "**出了名 / 闯出名号**"——固定搭配。*make a name for oneself* = 闯出名声（凭本事）。同型：*She made a name for herself as a violinist by age 20.* 注意 for **oneself** 不能省——*made a name* 单独说不完整。
  6. **had a picture painted** (l.16) — **本课题眼之二！have + 宾语 + 过去分词**（**使役**结构）= "**让别人为自己做某事**"。课文 *had a picture painted* = (找人) 画了一幅画（不是自己画的）。同型：*I had my hair cut. / She had her car repaired. / He had his teeth checked.* 注意与主动结构对比：*I painted a picture*（我自己画）vs. *I had a picture painted*（我让人画）。本课开头 *have their life work destroyed* (l.8) 也是同型——只是从"主动找人做"变成"被动遭受"（让……被毁）。
</Callout>

## 3. 关键句型 would rather ... than ... + have sth. done (IKS 90)

<Callout type="tip" title="一分钟回顾">
  本课题眼是 **would rather A than B** 与 **have + 宾语 + 过去分词**——课文 l.7-8 *people **would rather** pay large sums of money **than** have their life work destroyed* 一句话把两个结构合二为一。

  ### 结构一：would rather A than B（宁愿 A 也不愿 B）

  | 形式 | 例子 |
  |---|---|
  | **would rather + V 原形 + than + V 原形** | I'd rather **stay** at home than **go** out. |
  | **would rather not + V** | I'd rather **not go** out tonight. |
  | **would rather + 主语 + V 过去式**（虚拟） | I'd rather **he came** tomorrow. |
  | **would rather + 主语 + V 过去式（否定）** | I'd rather **he didn't tell** anyone. |

  **关键规则**：A 和 B **必须是同一时态/同一形式**——课文 *would rather **pay** ... than **have** ... destroyed* 中两个动词都是 V 原形。**would rather** 后面**不接 to**——*I'd rather to wait* (✗) / *I'd rather wait* (✓)。同义替换：**would sooner**（更正式略古）/ **prefer to ... rather than ...**（用 prefer 时要小心搭配）。

  ### 结构二：have + 宾语 + 过去分词（使役 / 被动）

  | 用法 | 例子 |
  |---|---|
  | **找别人做某事**（主动\"安排\"） | I **had** my hair **cut**. (我去理了发) |
  | **遭受某事**（被动\"经历\"） | He **had** his wallet **stolen**. (钱包被偷) |
  | **课文** l.8 (被动遭受) | have their life work **destroyed** by gangsters |
  | **课文** l.15-16 (主动安排) | the Florentines **had** a picture **painted** |

  ### ⚠️ 最关键的陷阱

  **would rather** 不要跟 **would like to** 混！would like to **后接 to V**，would rather **后接 V 原形**：
  - *I'd **like to** go.* (✓) / *I'd **rather** go.* (✓)
  - *I'd **like** go.* (✗) / *I'd **rather to** go.* (✗)
</Callout>

## 4. 难点 People would rather pay large sums of money than ... (Special Difficulties)

<Callout type="info" title="规则速览">
  课文 l.7-8 *People **would rather** pay large sums of money **than** have their life work destroyed by gangsters* 是本课难点的核心——**would rather** 在不同句型中的变形。教材给了 4 组核心对照：

  ### would rather 4 种用法对照

  | 想表达的意思 | 用 prefer 时 | 用 would rather 时 |
  |---|---|---|
  | 我想等在这儿（肯定） | I prefer to wait here. | I **would rather wait** here. / I'd sooner wait here. |
  | 我不想等在这儿（否定） | I prefer not to wait here. | I **would rather not wait** here. / I'd sooner not wait here. |
  | 最好他在这儿等（建议他） | It would be better if he waited here. | I'd rather **he waited** here. |
  | 最好他别在这儿等（不建议他） | It would be better if he didn't wait here. | I'd rather **he didn't wait** here. |

  ### ⚠️ 4 大易错点

  1. **would rather 后不接 to**：*I'd rather **wait*** (✓) / *I'd rather **to** wait* (✗)
  2. **主语自己**做事用 V 原形：*I'd rather **go***
  3. **建议别人**做事用 V 过去式（虚拟）：*I'd rather **he went***（不是 he goes / he go）
  4. **否定**直接加 not 在原形 / 过去式前：*I'd rather **not go*** / *I'd rather **he didn't go***（注意：用助动词 didn't，不是 hadn't / wouldn't）

  ### 教材练习 6 题填空精解

  *Give the correct form of the verbs in parentheses:*
  1. *I'd rather **go** (go) to the cinema.* —— 主语自己，用原形
  2. *I'd rather he **left** (leave) earlier.* —— 建议他人，用过去式
  3. *I'd rather you **didn't speak** (not speak) to him.* —— 否定他人，didn't + 原形
  4. *I'd rather **not speak** (not speak) about it.* —— 自己不做，not + 原形
  5. *I'd rather my father **settled** (settle) the account.* —— 建议他人，过去式
  6. *She'd rather you **didn't tell** (not tell) anyone about it.* —— 否定他人，didn't + 原形

  规则一句话：**自己做事原形，建议别人过去式；否定都用助动词或直接 not**。
</Callout>

## 11. 作文 Composition (约 200 词扩写)

<Callout type="info" title="任务">
  In not more than **200 words** write an **imaginary account** of one of Sir John Hawkwood's exploits. Use the ideas given below. Do not write more than three paragraphs.

  **Title:** *Hawkwood defeated.*
</Callout>

<Callout type="warning" title="提示骨架（按三段展开）">
  - **Introduction:** News that Hawkwood and his men were approaching — panic — villagers prepared to defend farms.
  - **Development:** Farmers fought — poorly armed — many killed — Hawkwood destroyed farms — sent message to prince of city-state — demanded money — refused — battle followed — Hawkwood invaded city — many buildings were destroyed — people killed.
  - **Conclusion:** Hawkwood was driven off — never attacked this city again — later became the prince's friend.

<Callout type="tip" title="参考范文（自己写完再看）">
  *Hawkwood defeated*

  The news that Hawkwood and his men were approaching threw the small valley into panic. Villagers buried what gold they had, drove their cattle into the woods, and gathered with pitchforks and old swords at the outskirts of their farms, determined to defend their life work.

  The farmers fought bravely but they were poorly armed; many were killed in the first hour. Hawkwood, furious at the resistance, burned down every farm in sight and sent a curt message to the prince of the city-state: pay five hundred florins by sundown or face invasion. The prince, who would rather fight than have his subjects bullied any longer, refused outright. A pitched battle followed in the narrow streets. Hawkwood's seasoned soldiers invaded the city; many fine buildings were destroyed and dozens of citizens killed.

  But the prince had ordered the gates of the inner citadel sealed, and from there his archers picked off Hawkwood's men one by one. By dawn the gangster-knight had lost a third of his band and was driven off in disorder. He never attacked that city again — and, in one of history's stranger twists, became the prince's loyal friend three years later. *(199 words)*

## 13. 拓展练习 Going further

<Callout type="tip" title="自学者建议">
  - **影子跟读 (shadowing)**：用第 1 节的三个 SayIt 按钮跟读 3-5 遍。重点感受 *would rather ... than ...* 的节奏——**rather** 和 **than** 都要**清晰发音**，不能含糊。课文 l.7-8 是 *would rather **pay** large sums of money **than** have their life work destroyed*——朗读时在 rather 后略停、than 前略停，让两个选项的对比鲜明。**had a picture painted** 这种使役结构里，**painted** 的 -ed 不能吞——它承载了"被画"的被动语义。
  - **复述 (retell)**：合上课文，把 Hawkwood 的故事用 2 分钟讲一遍。**叙事三段**：开场（14 世纪的英国人 → 带兵到意大利 → 在佛罗伦萨附近定居 → 出名）→ 经过（战时把士兵租给君主 → 和平时进城烧农场收保护费 → 挣了大笔钱）→ 结局（意大利人视他为英雄 → 80 岁过世 → 国葬 + 题献画像）。
  - **造句迁移**：用今天的 5 个高价值结构造你自己的句子——
    1. *would rather A than B*（宁愿 A 不愿 B）—— *I'd **rather walk home than wait** another hour for the bus.*
    2. *have + 宾语 + 过去分词*（找人做某事）—— *I had my watch **repaired** last week.*
    3. *in return for*（作为……的回报）—— *She gave me her old laptop **in return for** two months of free tutoring.*
    4. *as long ago as*（早在……）—— ***As long ago as** 1903, the Wright brothers flew the first powered aircraft.*
    5. *make a name for oneself*（出名）—— *He **made a name for himself** as a chess prodigy at the age of ten.*
  - **延伸思考**：本课的喜剧效果来自一个**英语写作大师级反讽**——课文标题 *A noble gangster*（贵族歹徒）本身就是矛盾修辞 (oxymoron)——**noble**（高贵的、骑士爵位的）与 **gangster**（街头流氓）正面冲撞。课文从头到尾都在玩这个对照：他是 **Sir** John Hawkwood（爵士头衔），却干 **gangster** 的勾当；他得了 **state funeral**（国葬，最高荣誉），墓志铭却写着 *the most **valiant** soldier*（最英勇的战士，正面褒义）——而他真正"英勇"的事迹是**烧农场、勒索保护费**。下次你写英文叙事时，试试用一个**矛盾修辞**做标题或主线——*a kindly tyrant / an honest liar / a gentle storm*——一句话就把读者拉进认知冲突里。再深一层：本课暗示**\"保护费\"不是现代发明，而是人类社会反复出现的模式**——这种**用具体故事撑起抽象命题**的写法，是英文议论文最常用的开头法之一，值得反复揣摩。
</Callout>
