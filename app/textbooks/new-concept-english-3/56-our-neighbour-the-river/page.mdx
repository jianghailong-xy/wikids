<Callout type="info" title="Listen first 听前思考">
  一条河可以是边界、是水源，也可以是一家人全部生活的中心。本课作者用深情的笔触，讲述一家农户与一条河之间堪称"共生"的关系——河水灌溉农田、接纳秘密、见证四季的庆典；但同样的河，在一个坏冬天也能悄然漫过堤岸，威胁下游新来的邻居。课文从日常写到危机，从四季风俗写到一场像"诺亚方舟"般的洪水中救援。全文充满了英语里那种*含蓄又温暖的叙事节奏*——不是呐喊式的感动，而是"我们至今还觉得那是奇迹"。
  **Why had the neighbours left their farm?** 带着这个问题读课文，重点留意作者怎样用 **定语从句**、**分词状语** 和 **时间状语从句**（As the floods had put ... / before it became exhausted ...）来编织一个紧张又有层次的叙事。
</Callout>

## The Text 课文

The river which forms the eastern boundary of our farm has always **played an important part in** our lives. Without it we could not **make a living**. There is only enough **spring water** to supply the needs of the house, so we have to pump from the river for farm use. We tell the river all our secrets. We know instinctively, just as beekeepers with their bees, that misfortune might **overtake** us if the important events of our lives were not related to it.

We have special river birthday parties in the summer. Sometimes we go **upstream** to a favourite **backwater**, sometimes we have our party at the **boathouse**, which a **predecessor** of ours at the farm built in the **meadow hard by** the deepest pool for swimming and diving. **In a heat wave** we choose a midnight birthday party and that is the most exciting of all. We welcome the seasons by the riverside, **crowning** the youngest girl with flowers in the spring, holding a summer festival on Midsummer Eve, giving thanks for the harvest in the autumn, and throwing a **holly wreath** into the current in the winter.

After a long period of rain the river may overflow its banks. This is **a rare occurrence** as our climate seldom **goes to extremes**. We are lucky in that only the lower fields, which make up a very small **proportion** of our farm, are affected by flooding, but other farms are less favourably **sited**, and flooding can sometimes **spell disaster** for their owners.

One bad winter we watched the river creep up the lower meadows. All the cattle had been moved into **stalls** and we **stood to lose** little. We were, however, worried about our nearest neighbours, whose farm was **low lying** and who were **newcomers** to the district. As the floods had **put the telephone out of order**, we could not find out how they were managing. From an **attic** window we could get a **sweeping** view of the river where their land joined ours, and **at the most critical juncture** we took turns in watching that point. The first sign of disaster was a dead sheep floating down. Next came a horse, swimming bravely, but we were afraid that the strength of the current would prevent its landing anywhere before it became exhausted. Suddenly a **raft** appeared, looking rather like **Noah's ark**, carrying the whole family, a few hens, the dogs, a cat, and a bird in a cage. We realized that they must have become **unduly** frightened by the rising flood, for their house, which had sound **foundations**, would have **stood stoutly** even if it had been almost **submerged**. The men of our family waded down through our flooded meadows with **boathooks**, in the hope of being able to **grapple** a corner of the raft and pull it out of the current towards our bank. We still think it a miracle that they were able to do so.

## 课文注释 Notes on the text

<Callout type="info" title="6 个值得停下来嚼一嚼的点">
  1. **Without it we could not make a living.** (ll.1-2) —— "没有它我们就无法谋生"。**Without + 名词** 在这里引出一个**隐含的虚拟条件**（= If we didn't have it, we could not ...），主句谓语用 **could not**（虚拟语气）。**make a living** = 谋生。同型：*Without water, no living thing could survive. / Without your help, I would have failed.*

  2. **hard by** (l.9) —— "紧挨着、在……近旁"。这是个有点古雅的介词短语，相当于 *close to / right next to*。课文 *built in the meadow **hard by** the deepest pool* = 建在最深水潭旁边的草地上。同型：*The cottage stood hard by the old church.*

  3. **in a heat wave** (l.10) —— "在大热天 / 热浪中"。**heat wave** = 热浪、酷暑期。*in a heat wave we choose a midnight birthday party* = 大热天里我们选午夜开生日会。同型：*In a cold snap, the pipes may freeze.*

  4. **stand to lose** (l.18) —— "很可能遭受损失"。**stand to do** = be in a position where one is likely to do or experience something（处于某种可能做/经历某事的境地）。*we **stood to lose** little* = 我们几乎不会有什么损失。同型：*If we don't act now, we stand to lose everything. / The company stands to make a huge profit.*

  5. **put ... out of order** (l.20) —— "使……出故障、失灵"。*the floods had **put the telephone out of order*** = 洪水把电话弄坏了。**out of order** 也可以单独用作表语：*The lift is out of order.* 同型：*The storm put the power lines out of order.*

  6. **Noah's ark** (l.25) —— "诺亚方舟"（《圣经》里诺亚为躲避大洪水而造的方舟）。作者把邻居自制的木筏比作诺亚方舟，既写实（载着一家老小和动物）又带着一丝幽默和温情。注意这个比喻的张力：一边是《圣经》级别的宏大典故，一边是*"a few hens, the dogs, a cat, and a bird in a cage"*的具体、日常的细节。
</Callout>

## 3. 关键句型 定语从句 · 分词状语 · 时间状语从句

<Callout type="tip" title="一分钟回顾">
  本课是一篇叙事文，句子偏长但层次分明。作者主要靠三样工具来把信息"折叠"进一个句子里：**定语从句 (which / whose / who)**、**分词状语 (crowning ... / holding ... / giving ... / throwing ... / carrying ...)**、以及**时间/原因状语从句 (As the floods had put ... / before it became exhausted ... / even if it had been ...)**。

  | 结构 | 课文例句 | 用途 |
  |---|---|---|
  | **which 定语从句** | *The river **which forms the eastern boundary** of our farm ...* | 补出先行词的身份/属性 |
  | **whose 定语从句** | *neighbours, **whose farm was low lying** and **who were newcomers*** | whose 表所属、who 表人 |
  | **分词状语（主动）** | ***crowning** the youngest girl with flowers in the spring ...* | 四个 -ing 分词并列，描写"如何迎接四季" |
  | **分词状语（主动伴随）** | *a raft appeared, **looking** rather like Noah's ark, **carrying** the whole family ...* | 木筏出现时的"样子"和"载着的东西" |
  | **As / before / even if 状语从句** | ***As** the floods had put the telephone out of order, we could not find out ...* | As = 因为（= because/since）；before = 在…之前；even if = 即使 |
  | **for 原因从句** | *... for their house ... would have stood stoutly* | for 引出原因（比 because 更正式、更书面） |

  ### ⚠️ 5 个本课最容易踩的雷区

  1. **定语从句中 whose ≠ who's**：*whose farm was low lying*（whose = 他们的农场），不是 *who is* 的缩写。
  2. **分词状语的主语必须与主句主语一致**：*crowning the youngest girl ...* → 主语必须是 *we*（课文前文有 *We welcome the seasons*），否则分词就成了 dangling modifier（悬垂分词）。
  3. **spell** 在这里不是"拼写"：*spell disaster* = 带来/招致灾难。这是 spell 的"老义项"（= to bring about, to mean）。
  4. **stand to do** 不是"站着去做"：*stood to lose little* = 处于很可能损失很少的状态。stand to do = be likely to do。
  5. **unduly** ≠ **un duly**：unduly = excessively（过分地），不要把它拆成 *un + duly* 去理解——它是固定一个词。同族词：due / duly / undue / unduly。
</Callout>

## 4. 难点：for / as / because / since 表"原因" Special difficulties

<Callout type="info" title="本课难点：因为 有四种说法">
  教材 Key structures 改写题要求把 *for* 改为 *As*（ll.20-21）。英语里表"原因"至少四种说法，语气和正式度各不相同：

  | 词 | 例句 | 特点 |
  |---|---|---|
  | **for** (并列连词) | *... for their house would have stood stoutly.* | 正式、书面；常用于对前句做**补充解释**而非直接因果；前面常有逗号。 |
  | **as** (从属连词) | ***As** the floods had put the telephone out of order, we could not ...* | 原因较明显、"既然、由于"的语气；从句常放句首。 |
  | **because** (从属连词) | *We were worried **because** their farm was low lying.* | 最直接、最强烈的因果；回答 why 的首选。 |
  | **since** (从属连词) | ***Since** you are here, let's begin.* | 类似 as，"既然"——原因已是已知信息。 |

  ⚠️ **Because / As / Since 不能与 so 一起用**（一个从属连词 + 一个并列连词 → 结构冲突）。要么 *Because ..., ...*，要么 *..., so ...*，二选一。
</Callout>

## 11. 作文 Composition (250–350 词)

<Callout type="info" title="任务">
  Write a composition on **one** of the following subjects; the length should be between **250 and 350 words**.

  **a.** What the world would be like without newspapers, radio, films and television.

  **b.** "It was a dark and gloomy street." Continue the story of an adventure you had in this street.
</Callout>

<Callout type="warning" title="提示骨架（以 a 题为例）">
  - **开篇（假设）**：Imagine waking up tomorrow to a world without any mass media — no news, no broadcasts, no screens.
  - **信息真空（第 1 层影响）**：Without newspapers and radio, how would we learn what was happening? Back to word of mouth, rumour, and the town crier. News would travel at walking speed.
  - **娱乐消失（第 2 层影响）**：Without films and television, evenings would change — more reading aloud, more conversation, more live music at home. Is this necessarily worse?
  - **社会连接断裂（第 3 层影响）**：Shared cultural references would fragment. No "everyone watched last night" — each town, each family would have its own isolated culture.
  - **权力与真相（第 4 层影响）**：Mass media can manipulate, but its absence might be worse — only the powerful would have access to information. The printing press, after all, was a democratising force.
  - **结尾（平衡判断）**：We complain about media, but a world without it would be smaller, slower and perhaps more easily controlled. The question is not whether to have media, but how to make it wise.
</Callout>

<Callout type="tip" title="写作脚手架">
  - **时态主线**：用**假设语气**贯穿——*Imagine / Suppose + 过去式 → ... would have to ... / ... would be ... / ... might become ...*（与课文 \"would have to be / would not be suitable\" 的推演笔法一致）。
  - **\"信息传播\"动词库**：*broadcast, transmit, circulate, spread, leak out, filter through, reach*。
  - **\"媒体缺失\"词库**：*word of mouth, rumour mill, information vacuum, isolated communities, the town crier, the printing press, local knowledge, shared experience*。
  - **结构亮点**：用 *Without newspapers, radio, films and television* 开篇，直接与课文 *Without it we could not make a living* 异曲同工。每段以\"Without ...\"或\"What if ...\"推进假设。
</Callout>

<Callout type="tip" title="参考范文（自己写完再看）">
  Imagine waking up tomorrow to find every newspaper gone, every radio silent, every screen dark. No news. No broadcasts. No films. For the first few days, it might feel like a holiday — a sudden, delicious silence. But as the days turned into weeks, the silence would deepen into something more troubling: a world in which nobody knew what was happening beyond the horizon of their own village.

  Without newspapers and radio, information would travel at walking speed. A flood in the next valley might not be known here for three days; a change in government, for three weeks. Word of mouth would become the only news service, and with it would come rumour, exaggeration and fear. The town crier might return, but he would bring as much gossip as truth.

  Without films and television, evenings would empty out. We would rediscover conversation — families reading aloud, neighbours singing, children inventing their own stories. That part of the world might be richer, not poorer. But without a shared screen culture, the common references that hold a nation together — the comedy everyone quotes, the drama everyone argued about — would fragment. Each town would become its own little island of stories.

  The deepest loss, however, might be political. Mass media can be manipulated — it always has been — but its absence might be worse. Without the printing press, the powerful have always controlled what is known. A world without media might be a world in which only the rich and the connected know what is really happening. The question, perhaps, is not whether we would be better off without media, but how to make media wise enough to deserve its power. *(280 words)*
</Callout>

## 13. 拓展练习 Going further

<Callout type="tip" title="自学者建议">
  - **影子跟读 (shadowing)**：用第 1 节的三个 SayIt 按钮跟读 3-5 遍。第一句注意定语从句的一气呵成（中间不停顿）；第二句（四季长句）注意四个分词状语之间的节奏——每个 *-ing* 短语后自然停顿换气；第三句注意 *looking rather like Noah's ark, carrying ...* 的叠加画面感。
  - **复述 (retell)**：合上课文，用 2 分钟把本文的故事讲出来。**四层叙事**：河与人的日常共生（第 1-2 段）→ 河也会泛滥（第 3 段：低概率但真有风险）→ 那个坏冬天（第 4 段：从担心到观察到的灾难信号）→ 诺亚方舟般的救援（高潮 +  miracle 收尾）。特别注意最后一句话 *We still think it a miracle* 把整个紧张的故事收在一个温和、感恩的调子上——好的叙事文结尾就是这样的。
  - **造句迁移**：用今天的 5 个高价值结构造你自己的句子——
    1. *play an important part in ...* —— *My grandmother played an important part in my upbringing.*
    2. *stand to lose / gain* —— *If the deal goes through, the company stands to make millions.*
    3. *put ... out of order* —— *Vandals had put the ticket machine out of order.*
    4. *in the hope of + doing* —— *She applied to twenty universities, in the hope of getting at least one offer.*
    5. *think it a miracle that ...* —— *Given the traffic, I think it a miracle that you arrived on time.*
  - **延伸思考**：本课是 NCE3 少有的\"人与自然的诗意叙事\"——没有论点、没有说理，只有一个家庭与一条河之间一辈子的故事。注意作者是怎样把\"事实\"写得有温度的：他不说\"我们爱这条河\"，但读者从 *We tell the river all our secrets*、从 *the most exciting of all*、从 *We still think it a miracle* 里感受到了。英语叙事文的灵魂不是形容词，而是**具体细节 + 克制的感情流露**。试着用中文写一段\"你与一个地方的感情\"，然后译成英文——逼自己用具体事例和细节表达感情，而不是直接说\"我爱这里\"。
