<Callout type="info" title="Listen first 听前思考">
  城市还是乡村？这场争论已经持续了几百年。有人说乡村有**宁静、清新、自然**；有人说城市有**便利、丰富、精彩**。本课的作者就是一个"铁杆城里人"——他生在城里、长在城里，对朋友们一提到乡村就"狂喜"的样子完全无法理解。更让他困惑的是：那些明明享受着城市一切便利的人，为什么还要**固执地假装**自己更喜欢乡村？课文的题目叫 *Illusions of pastoral peace*——"田园宁静的**幻觉**"，光看标题你就知道作者站在哪一边了。
  **What particular anxiety spoils the country dweller's visit to the theatre?** 带着这个问题读课文，特别留意作者是如何用一连串**对比 (contrast)** 和**比较结构**来"控诉"乡村、为城市辩护的——以及那个让整篇文章逻辑收束的关键句型：**Why ... is beyond me**。
</Callout>

## The Text 课文

The quiet life of the country has never appealed to me. **City born and city bred**, I have always regarded the country as something you look at through a train window, or something you occasionally visit during the weekend. Most of my friends live in the city, yet they always **go into raptures** at the mere mention of the country. Though they **extol the virtues** of the peaceful life, only one of them has ever gone to live in the country and he was back in town within six months. Even he still lives **under the illusion** that country life is somehow superior to town life. He is forever talking about the friendly people, the clean atmosphere, the closeness to nature and the gentle pace of living. **Nothing can be compared**, he maintains, with the first cockcrow, the twittering of birds at dawn, the sight of the rising sun glinting on the trees and pastures. This idyllic pastoral scene is only part of the picture. My friend fails to mention the long and friendless winter evenings in front of the TV — **virtually the only form of entertainment**. He says nothing about the poor selection of goods in the shops, or about those unfortunate people who have to travel from the country to the city every day to get to work. Why people are prepared to tolerate a four-hour journey each day for the dubious privilege of living in the country **is beyond me**. They could be saved so much misery and expense if they chose to live in the city where they rightly belong.

If you can **do without** the few pastoral pleasures of the country, you will find the city can provide you with the best that life can offer. You never have to travel miles to see your friends. They invariably live nearby and are always available for an informal chat or an evening's entertainment. Some of my acquaintances in the country come up to town once or twice a year to visit the theatre as a special treat. For them this is a major operation which involves considerable planning. As the play **draws to its close**, they wonder whether they will ever catch that last train home. The city dweller never experiences anxieties of this sort. The latest exhibitions, films, or plays are only a short bus ride away. Shopping, too, is always a pleasure. There is so much variety that you never have to **make do with second best**. Country people run wild when they go shopping in the city and stagger home loaded with as many of the exotic items as they can carry. Nor is the city without its moments of beauty. There is something comforting about the warm glow shed by advertisements on cold wet winter nights. **Few things could be more impressive than** the peace that descends on deserted city streets at weekends when the thousands that travel to work every day are tucked away in their homes in the country. It has always been a mystery to me why city dwellers, who appreciate all these things, obstinately pretend that they would prefer to live in the country.

## 课文注释 Notes on the text

<Callout type="info" title="10 个值得停下来嚼一嚼的点">
  1. **City born and city bred** (l.1) — "城里生，城里长"。这是由 **名词 + 过去分词** 构成的合成形容词（compound adjective），此处作**原因状语**，相当于 *As I was born and bred in the city*。同型表达：*London born and London bred; country born and country bred.* 教材 Structure 第 4 题正是考这个结构如何改写为分词短语 *Being born and bred in the city*。

  2. **go into raptures** (l.5) — "变得欣喜若狂起来"。**rapture** 用复数 *raptures*，表"狂喜、欢天喜地"。**at the mere mention of** = "只要一提到"——**mere** 强调"仅仅、光是"：*At the mere mention of his name, she blushed.* 同型：*go into ecstasies / go wild with joy.*

  3. **extol the virtues of ...** (l.6) — "赞美……的优点、颂扬……的种种好处"。**extol** 比 *praise* 正式，含"极力推崇"之意。**virtues** 此处不是"美德"而是"优点"（≈ advantages / merits）。同型：*He never stops extolling the virtues of his new car.*

  4. **under the illusion that ...** (ll.7-8) — "存有……的幻觉/错觉"。**that 引导的是同位语从句**，说明 illusion 的具体内容。注意：**illusion**（错觉、幻觉）≠ **delusion**（妄想、病态错觉）。同型：*He labours under the illusion that money can buy happiness.*

  5. **Nothing can be compared with ...** (ll.9-10) — "没有什么能与……相比"。这是用否定词 + 比较结构来表达**最高级**含义（≈ …is the best / most incomparable）。同型：*Nothing can be compared with the taste of home-cooked food.*

  6. **is beyond me** (l.15) — "我所无法理解的、超越我的理解范围"。**beyond + 人称代词宾格** = "超出某人的理解/能力"。这也是教材 Key structures 的核心句型——把 *I can't understand why...* 改写为 *Why ... is beyond me*（名词性从句作主语）。同型：*How she managed to finish it in one day is beyond me. / It's beyond me why he said that.*

  7. **do without ...** (l.17) — "没有……也行、将就着不用……"（= manage without）。**can/could do without** 口语常用："能凑合着没有……"。**do** 在这里是不及物的"应付、凑合"。同型：*I can do without a car — the bus is fine. / We'll have to do without sugar for a while.*

  8. **draw to a close** (ll.21-22) — "结束、告终"（= come to an end, near its end）。也可用 *draw to an end*。**draw** 这里表"逐渐移向"（向终点靠近）。课文 Vocabulary MCQ 第 11 题正是考它的同义替换 *near its end*。同型：*As the year draws to a close, people look back on what they have achieved.*

  9. **make do with second best** (l.25) — "用二等品来凑合"。**make do (with ...)** = "凑合着用、将就"——在不理想的条件下尽量应付。**second best** = "次好的、二等的东西"，反义是 *the best*。同型：*We didn't have enough chairs, so we had to make do with boxes. / There's no butter — we'll have to make do with jam.*

  10. **Few things could be more impressive than ...** (ll.27-28) — "没有什么能比……更令人难忘"。用 **否定词 few + 比较级** 来表达**最高级/极度强调**的含义（≈ …is one of the most impressive things）。同型：*Few sights could be more beautiful than the sunset over the ocean.* 课文最后正是用这个结构把"城市的美"推向了最高点。
</Callout>

  宁静的乡村生活从来没有吸引过我。我生在城市，长在城市，总认为乡村是透过火车车窗看到的那个样子，或偶尔周末去游玩一下的景象。我的许多朋友都住在城市，但他们只要一提起乡村，马上就会变得欣喜若狂。尽管他们都交口称赞宁静的乡村生活的种种优点，但其中只有一人真去农村住过，而且不足6个月就回来了。即使他也仍存有幻觉，好像乡村生活就是比城市生活优越。他滔滔不绝地大谈友好的农民，清净的空气，贴近大自然的环境和悠闲的生活节奏。他坚持认为，凌晨雄鸡第一声啼叫，黎明时分小鸟吱喳欢叫，冉冉升起的朝阳染红树木、牧场，此番美景无与伦比。但这种田园诗般的乡村风光仅仅是一个侧面。我的朋友没有提到在电视机前度过的漫长寂寞的冬夜——电视是唯一的娱乐形式。他也不说商店货物品种单调，以及那些每天不得不从乡下赶到城里工作的不幸的人们。人们为什么情愿每天在路上奔波4个小时去换取值得怀疑的乡间的优点，我是无法理解的。要是他们愿意住在本来属于他们的城市，则可以让他们省去诸多不便与节约大量开支。

  如果你愿舍弃乡下生活那一点点乐趣的话，那么你会发现城市可以为你提供生活中最美好的东西。你去看朋友根本不用跋涉好几英里，因为他们都住在附近，你随时可以同他们聊天或在晚上一起娱乐。我在乡村有一些熟人，他们每年进城来看一回或几回戏，并把此看作一种特殊的享受。看戏在他们是一件大事，需要精心计划。当戏快演完时，他们又为是否能赶上末班火车回家而犯愁。这种焦虑，城里人是从未体验过的。坐公共汽车几站路，就可看到最新的展览、电影、戏剧。买东西也是一种乐趣。物品品种繁多，从来不必用二等品来凑合。乡里人进城采购欣喜若狂，每次回家时都买足了外来商品，直到拿不动方才罢休，连走路都摇摇晃晃的。城市也并非没有良辰美景。寒冷潮湿的冬夜里，广告灯箱发出的暖光，会给人某种安慰。周末，当成千上万进城上班的人回到了他们的乡间寓所之后，空旷的街市笼罩着一种宁静的气氛，没有什么能比这种宁静更令人难忘了。城里人对这一切心里很明白，却偏要执拗地装出他们喜欢住在乡村的样子，这对我来说一直是个谜。
</Callout>

## 3. 关键句型：名词性从句作主语 & 比较表达

<Callout type="tip" title="一分钟回顾">
  本课是一篇**议论文式的夹叙夹议**——作者借"一个朋友"的故事引出一场城乡对比，最终落到一句直指人心的反问。支撑全篇语法的有三大支柱：

  | 结构 | 课文例句 | 用途 |
  |---|---|---|
  | **Why/How + 从句 + is beyond me** | *Why people are prepared to tolerate... **is beyond me**.* | 名词性从句作主语，表"我无法理解……" |
  | **Nothing can be compared with ...** | *Nothing can be compared with the first cockcrow...* | 否定词 + 比较 = 最高级（"无与伦比"） |
  | **Few things could be more + adj. + than ...** | *Few things could be more impressive than the peace...* | 否定词 + 比较级 = 极度强调（"最……不过了"） |
  | **分词短语作状语** | ***City born and city bred**, I have always regarded...* | 名词+过去分词合成形容词，表原因 |
  | **do 短语** | *do without / make do with second best* | do = "凑合、应付" |

  ### ⚠️ 3 个本课最容易踩的雷区

  1. **Why 从句作主语，谓语用单数**：*Why people ... **is** beyond me*（不是 are）。整个 *Why ...* 从句是一个名词性成分，视为单数。
  2. **do without ≠ give up**：*do without* = "没有……也凑合着过"（暂时应付），不是永久放弃。*make do with* = "拿（次好的东西）凑合着用"。
  3. **beyond me** 里的 **me** 是宾格：不能说 *beyond I* 或 *beyond myself*。结构是 **beyond + 人称代词宾格**（beyond me / him / her / us / them）。
</Callout>

## 4. 难点：do 短语 & beyond 表达 Special difficulties

<Callout type="info" title="教材 Key structures 改写题">
  教材要求：在**不改变意思**的前提下改写下面这句（参照 ll.14-15）：

  > I can't understand why people are prepared to tolerate a four-hour journey each day for the dubious privilege of living in the country.
  > Why people ...

  **解题钥匙**：把 *I can't understand + 宾语从句* 改写成 **名词性从句作主语 + is beyond me**：

  > Why people are prepared to tolerate a four-hour journey each day for the dubious privilege of living in the country **is beyond me**.

  ### beyond + 人称代词宾格：3 种常用模式

  1. **be beyond + sb** = 超出某人的理解/能力：*This math problem is beyond me.*（这数学题我搞不懂。）
  2. **be beyond + 名词** = 超出……的范围：*The pain was beyond description.*（痛苦难以形容。）*The view was beyond compare.*（景色无与伦比。）
  3. **go beyond** = 超越、超出界限：*His behaviour goes beyond what is acceptable.*（他的行为超出了可接受的范围。）

  ### do 的两个"凑合"

  | 短语 | 含义 | 例句 |
  |---|---|---|
  | **do without + n.** | 没有……也将就着过 | *We'll have to do without sugar for a week.* |
  | **make do with + n.** | 拿（次好的）凑合着用 | *No butter? We'll make do with jam.* |

  **do without** 强调的是"缺少也过"（managing despite lack of）；**make do with** 强调的是"用替代品将就"（using a substitute）。两者都带"将就"的意思，但使用场景不同。
</Callout>

## 11. 作文 Composition (约 300 词)

<Callout type="info" title="任务">
  Write a composition in about **300 words** on one of the following:

  **a.** Write an answer to the above passage pointing out the **advantages of living in the country** and the **disadvantages of living in the city**.

  **b.** Which part of your country would you prefer to live in and why?
</Callout>

<Callout type="warning" title="提示骨架（以 a 题为例——反驳课文作者）">
  - **开头（让步 + 表态）**：承认作者说得有一定道理——城市的便利无可否认。但乡村真正的魅力他没有看到。
  - **乡村优势 1——真正的宁静**：没有车流的轰鸣、没有凌晨的警笛。你听到的是鸟鸣、风声、雨打树叶——这种宁静不是"周末空城"那种短暂的假宁静，而是日日可得的。
  - **乡村优势 2——社区与人情**：作者说乡村的冬夜"孤独而漫长"（*long and friendless*）。错——乡村的邻里关系比城市紧密得多。在城里住了三年都不知道对门姓什么；在乡下，你搬家第二天就有人送来自制果酱。
  - **乡村优势 3——空间与自由**：不需要挤地铁、不需要为停车位打架。孩子可以在院子里跑，而不是关在几十平米的公寓里。
  - **城市的缺点 1——压力与成本**：高房价、高物价、通勤挤车、空气污染——作者只说了城市的好，没提这些代价。
  - **城市的缺点 2——匿名与孤独**：城市里 *friends live nearby* 是幸运而非常态。大量城市居民恰恰是孤身一人淹没在人海中。
  - **结尾（平衡）**：城乡各有优劣，没有绝对的"更好"。作者说得对——*obstinately pretending* 不可取。但执拗地假装城市一切都好，同样是一种 illusion。
</Callout>

<Callout type="tip" title="写作脚手架">
  - **时态主线**：用一般现在时陈述观点（这是议论文）。需要举例时用一般过去时或现在完成时。
  - **"乡村优势"词库**：*tranquillity, birdsong, starry skies, fresh air, a slower pace of life, close-knit community, neighbourly spirit, room to breathe, connection to nature, home-grown food*。
  - **"城市劣势"词库**：*noise pollution, rush-hour crush, cramped flats, anonymity, the rat race, sky-high rent, traffic jams, light pollution, stress-related illness*。
  - **结构亮点**：每段开头用一个简短有力的主题句（topic sentence），然后用具体例子支撑（show, don't just tell）。结尾避免"两者都重要"的废话——给出一个有力但不绝对化的结论。
</Callout>

<Callout type="tip" title="参考范文（自己写完再看）">
  The author of *Illusions of pastoral peace* makes a spirited case for city life, and in many ways he is right. The convenience, the choice, the easy access to entertainment — these are real and undeniable. Yet his picture of the country is, by his own admission, only part of the picture. Let me paint the other half.

  First, there is a kind of peace in the country that no city can offer. The author describes the silence of deserted city streets at weekends as though it were the height of tranquillity. But that is a borrowed peace — the peace of absence, not of presence. In the country, you do not have to wait for the weekend. Every morning you wake to birdsong, not traffic. Every night you see stars, not neon. The air smells of grass, not exhaust fumes. This is not an illusion; it is a daily reality for millions.

  Second, the author dismisses country community too quickly. He calls winter evenings "long and friendless", but in a village, the long evening is precisely when neighbours gather. In the city, you may live next door to someone for three years and never learn their name. In the country, you arrive on Monday and by Friday you have been invited to supper, lent a lawnmower and told the entire history of the parish church. The warmth is real; it is the city that can be the lonelier place.

  Third, space and freedom. A child in the country has fields to run in, streams to explore, trees to climb. A child in the city has a balcony if they are lucky.

  I do not pretend the country is perfect. The commute can be brutal, the shops limited, the winters hard. But the author, for all his wit and style, has mistaken a preference for a proof. The city suits him; the country suits others. Neither side has a monopoly on illusion — or on truth. *(约 310 词)*
</Callout>

## 12. 书信写作 Letter writing (约 100 词)

<Callout type="info" title="任务">

<Callout type="tip" title="写作脚手架">
  - **称呼**：*Dear + 名*（朋友间用名字，亲切自然）。
  - **开头**：欣然答应 + 提供客房——*I was so glad to get your letter — of course you can stay! I have a spare guestroom that's all yours.*

## 13. 拓展练习 Going further

<Callout type="tip" title="自学者建议">
  - **影子跟读 (shadowing)**：用第 1 节的三个 SayIt 按钮跟读 3-5 遍。特别注意 *City born and city bred*（分词短语一口气读到底，中间不要停顿）和 *Few things could be more impressive than*（从句一口气读完，感受节奏）。
  - **复述 (retell)**：合上课文，用 2 分钟把本文的逻辑链讲一遍。**三步推进**：作者亮明立场（爱城市）→ 借一个朋友的口吻列出乡村的"美"（再一一拆穿其不完整性）→ 正面列出城市的种种好处（便利、娱乐、购物、美）→ 结尾反问：明知城市好，为什么偏要假装爱乡村？
  - **造句迁移**：用今天的 5 个高价值结构造你自己的句子——
    1. **City born and city bred**（名词+过去分词表出身）——*Beijing born and Beijing bred, I know every corner of this ancient city.*
    2. **Why ... is beyond me**（名词性从句作主语）——*Why students leave their assignments to the last minute is beyond me.*
    3. **do without ...**（没有……也凑合）——*I can do without social media for a week — it's actually quite liberating.*
    4. **make do with ...**（用……将就）——*The printer was broken, so we had to make do with handwritten copies.*
    5. **Few things could be more + adj. + than ...**（双重否定表最高级）——*Few things could be more satisfying than a hot bath after a long day.*
  - **延伸思考**：本课是一篇典型的**"反浪漫化"散文**（anti-romantic essay）。作者的技巧是：先让对手（他那个爱乡村的朋友）尽情发表赞美之词，把乡村描绘成一幅田园诗画——然后作者只轻轻地加了一句 *This idyllic pastoral scene is only part of the picture*，整幅画就碎了。这种"先扬后抑，只补一句"的写法，比全程自己说"乡村不好"高明得多。下次写议论文时不妨试试：**先充分呈现对方的论据，再用一个新的角度或一条对方忽略的事实把它推翻**——读者会觉得你更公平、更有说服力。另外注意标题里的 *illusions* 用的是复数——不是说"一个幻觉"而是"种种幻觉"，说明作者认为人们对乡村的浪漫想象是多方面、系统性的。好标题，一句话就把立场挑明了。
