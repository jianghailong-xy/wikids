  你有没有遇到过这种人——路越烂、坑越大、危险越近，他反而越不当回事？本课的主角 Bruce 就让你见识什么叫"天塌下来当被子盖"。一车乘客紧张得要命，他却始终面不改色，最后在一片欢呼（或者说无语）中，轻描淡写地说出那句让人哭笑不得的话——*there was no oil in the engine!*
  **What was the difference between Bruce's behaviour and that of other people?** 带着这个问题读课文，重点留意全篇如何用 **get sb. to do**（使某人做某事）、**sooner or later**（迟早）、**no matter what**（无论什么）以及一系列 **get** 短语动词来刻画"其他人紧张 vs. Bruce 淡定"的对照。
</Callout>

## The Text 课文

The rough road across the plain soon became so bad that we **tried to get Bruce to drive back** to the village we had come from. Even though the road was littered with boulders and pitted with holes, Bruce was **not in the least perturbed**. Glancing at his map, he informed us that the next village was a mere twenty miles away. It was not that Bruce always underestimated difficulties. He simply **had no sense of danger at all**. No matter what the conditions were, he believed that a car should be driven as fast as it could possibly go.

As we bumped over the dusty track, we swerved to avoid large boulders. The wheels scooped up stones which hammered ominously under the car. We felt sure that **sooner or later** a stone would rip a hole in our petrol tank or damage the engine. Because of this, we kept looking back, wondering if we were leaving a trail of oil and petrol behind us.

What a relief it was when the boulders suddenly disappeared, **giving way to a stretch of plain** where the only obstacles were clumps of bushes. But there was worse to come. Just ahead of us there was a huge fissure. **In response to renewed pleadings**, Bruce stopped. Though we all got out to examine the fissure, he remained in the car. We informed him that the fissure extended for fifty yards and was two feet wide and four feet deep. Even this **had no effect**. Bruce **went into a low gear** and drove at a terrifying speed, keeping the front wheels astride the crack as he followed its zigzag course. Before we had time to worry about what might happen, we were back on the plain again. Bruce consulted the map once more and told us that the village was now only fifteen miles away. Our next obstacle was a shallow pool of water about half a mile across. Bruce charged at it, but in the middle, the car **came to a grinding halt**. A yellow light on the dashboard flashed angrily and Bruce cheerfully announced that there was no oil in the engine!

## 课文注释 Notes on the text

<Callout type="info" title="6 个值得停下来嚼一嚼的点">
  1. **get sb. to do sth.** (ll.1-2) — "说服/让某人做某事"。与 make sb. do sth. 不同：**get** 偏"劝说、设法使……"，语气不如 make 强（make 是\"强迫\"）。同型：*I finally got him to see a doctor.* ⚠️ get 后用 **to do**，make 后用 **不带 to 的不定式**。教材 Key structures 第 1 题正是这个转换。

  2. **not in the least** (l.4) — "一点儿也不，完全不"。**in the least** 意为"丝毫"，用于否定句中强调"完全没"。同型：*He wasn't in the least worried about the exam.* 类似表达：*not at all / not in the slightest*。

  3. **It was not that ... He simply ...** (ll.5-6) — "这并不是说……他不过是……"。一个很地道的"澄清+解释"结构：先把读者的误解否掉，再给出真正原因。同型：*It was not that she was lazy. She simply had too much on her plate.*

  4. **sooner or later** (l.10) — "迟早"。固定短语，作状语。同型：*Sooner or later, you'll have to face the truth.* 教材注释第 4 条专门讲这个。

  5. **giving way to a stretch of plain** (l.13) — "被一片平原所替代"。**give way to** = 让位于 / 被……替代。原义"让路"，引申为"被取代"。同型：*The old town gave way to modern apartment blocks.* 教材注释第 5 条讲解了这个短语。

  6. **came to a grinding halt** (l.21) — "嘎吱一声停了下来"。**grinding** 来自 grind（磨擦），形容金属摩擦的刺耳声；**halt** = 停止。这个短语画面感极强：车冲到水塘正中，一阵刺耳的摩擦声后戛然而停。同型：*Traffic came to a grinding halt after the accident.*
</Callout>

## 3. 关键句型 使役结构与感叹句 Causatives & exclamations

<Callout type="tip" title="一分钟回顾">
  本课是一篇"幽默叙事"——作者通过乘客的紧张与 Bruce 的淡定构成全篇的喜剧张力。语法上重点练两套结构：**使役动词 get / make** 和 **What 感叹句**，外加一系列 **get** 短语动词和间接问句。

  | 结构 | 课文例句 | 用途 |
  |---|---|---|
  | **get sb. to do sth.** | *we tried to **get Bruce to drive** back* | 说服/让某人做某事（to do） |
  | **make sb. do sth.** | *made him tell the truth* → got him to tell... | 强迫/使某人做某事（无 to） |
  | **What a + 名词 + 主 + 谓!** | ***What a relief** it was* | What 引导的感叹句 |
  | **How + 形/副 + 主 + 谓!** | *How relieved we felt* | How 引导的感叹句（同义改写） |
  | **wonder if / when ...** | *wondering **if** we were leaving / I wonder **when** he will arrive* | 间接问句（主语+谓语正序） |
  | **get + 介词/副词** | *get out / get on / get over / get through* | get 短语动词 |

  ### ⚠️ 4 个本课最容易踩的雷区

  1. **get sb. to do** vs **make sb. do**：get 后带 **to**，make 后**不带 to**。改写时互换要记得加减 to。
  2. **What a relief!** vs **How relieved!**：What 接**名词**（What a relief / What a wonderful garden），How 接**形容词/副词**（How relieved / How wonderful）。
  3. **间接问句用正序**：*I wonder **when he will arrive***（不是 when will he arrive）；*wondering **if we were leaving***（不是 if were we leaving）。
  4. **get 短语动词意思各不同**：*get on*（上车/进展）、*get over*（克服/从……恢复）、*get through*（通过/完成/熬过）——三个方向，不要混淆。
</Callout>

## 4. 难点：get 使役与短语动词 Special difficulties

<Callout type="info" title="教材 Key structures 和 Special difficulties 6 项练习">
  本课教材的关键句型与难点练习涵盖 6 项：

  **① get sb. to do ↔ make sb. do（ISD 66）**
  > I made him tell me the truth. → I **got** him **to tell** me the truth.
  > He made me translate the article into English. → He **got** me **to translate** the article into English.

  规则：**make** 后接不带 to 的不定式（强迫），**get** 后接带 to 的不定式（说服/设法）。

  **② suggest / insist + that ...（IKS 63）**
  > He believed that a car should be driven fast.
  > → He **suggested** that a car (should) be driven fast.（建议，虚拟语气 should 可省）
  > → He **insisted** that a car (should) be driven fast.（坚持，虚拟语气 should 可省）

  **③ wonder if / when ...（IKS 63d）**
  > Can he wait a few minutes longer? → I wonder **if** he can wait a few minutes longer.
  > When will he arrive? → I wonder **when** he will arrive.

  规则：间接问句用**陈述语序**（主语在前，谓语在后），不用疑问倒装。

  **④ What 感叹句（ISD 2）**
  > This is a wonderful garden! → **What a wonderful garden** (this is)!
  > It is a terrible day! → **What a terrible day** (it is)!

  规则：**What + (a/an) + (形容词) + 名词 + (主语 + 谓语)!** ——What 后面接名词短语。

  **⑤ get 短语动词（ISD 74a）**
  > **get on**：上车 / 进展 —— *How are you getting on with your work?*
  > **get over**：克服 / 从……恢复 —— *It took her months to get over the illness.*
  > **get through**：通过（考试）/ 熬过 / 完成 —— *I finally got through the exam.*

  **⑥ say vs tell（KS 15）**
  > **say** + (to sb.) + 说的内容 —— *He **said** (that) he was tired. / He **said** to me that he was tired.*
  > **tell** + sb. + 说的内容 —— *He **told** me (that) he was tired.*
  
  规则：**tell 必须有间接宾语**（告诉谁），say 的间接宾语必须通过 to 引入。
</Callout>

## 10. 摘要写作 Summary writing (≤ 80 词)

<Callout type="info" title="任务">
  *In not more than 80 words* describe **what happened from the time when the car got past the boulders to the moment it stopped in the shallow pool**. Use your own words as far as possible. **Do not include anything that is not in the last paragraph.**

  按下面的提示点串成一段：

  1. 大石块消失后的地貌变化（stretch of plain / clumps of bushes）。
  2. 前方出现裂缝——乘客的请求与 Bruce 的回应。
  3. Bruce 如何跨过裂缝（low gear / astride the crack / zigzag course / terrifying speed）。
  4. 下一个障碍：浅水塘——Bruce 冲过去。
  5. 车在水塘正中熄火、仪表盘闪黄灯、Bruce 宣布没油——他的反应如何？
</Callout>

  After the boulders disappeared, the car reached a stretch of plain with only clumps of bushes. Soon a huge fissure appeared. ## 11. 作文 Composition (≤ 250 词)

<Callout type="info" title="任务">
  Write a composition in about **250 words** continuing the passage. Expand the ideas given below into a plan and provide a suitable title. Your composition should be in **four paragraphs**.

  **Ideas:** Got out — tried to push car — impossible — walked to next village — tried to get a taxi — no driver would take us over rough road — paid a large sum of money to rent a jeep — pulled car out of water — found engine badly damaged — Bruce undismayed.
</Callout>

<Callout type="warning" title="提示骨架">
  - **Paragraph 1**：下车，试图推车 —— *We all got out, waded into the water and tried to push, but the car would not budge.*
  - **Paragraph 2**：步行到下一个村子 —— *Left the car and walked the remaining fifteen miles to the village. Exhausted and covered in dust.*
  - **Paragraph 3**：找车救援失败，租吉普 —— *No taxi driver would take us back over that road. We finally paid a heavy sum for a jeep and a driver to tow the car out.*
  - **Paragraph 4**：发动机严重损坏，Bruce 依旧面不改色 —— *The engine was found to be badly damaged — water had got into it. But Bruce, undismayed as ever, simply shrugged and said, "Nothing to worry about. We'll fix it."*
</Callout>

<Callout type="tip" title="写作脚手架">
  - **时态主线**：全篇延续过去时（got out / tried / walked / paid / pulled / found / was）。故事情节所以全程过去时。
  - **句式亮点**：用课文里的结构来写续篇——*tried to get the driver to help us*（get sb. to do）；*no matter how much we offered*（No matter 让步）；*What a struggle it was to push*（What 感叹）。
  - **"Bruce 不为所动"词库**：*undismayed, unperturbed, unfazed, shrugged, cheerfully remarked, not in the least bothered*。
  - **"困难艰辛"词库**：*wade, budge, exhaust, trudge, refuse, charge, tow, damage, struggle*。
</Callout>

<Callout type="tip" title="参考范文（自己写完再看）">
  **Nothing to worry about — continued**

  The moment the grinding noise died away, we all got out and waded into the shallow water. We pushed and heaved against the car, but it would not budge — the wheels seemed to be stuck fast in the muddy bottom. What a hopeless situation it was!

  Leaving Bruce with the car, the rest of us walked the remaining fifteen miles to the village. We arrived exhausted, our clothes soaked and our shoes coated in red dust. The village was tiny — little more than a handful of houses and a small garage.

  We tried to hire a taxi, but no driver would take us back over that terrible track, no matter how much we offered. In the end, we paid an enormous sum to rent a jeep from the garage owner and persuaded him to drive us back. He attached a rope to our car and pulled it out of the water.

  Back on dry land, we opened the bonnet. Water had flooded the engine, which was now badly damaged — it would take days to repair. Bruce, utterly undismayed, leaned against the jeep and cheerfully remarked, 'Nothing to worry about. A bit of water never hurt anyone.' *(约 200 词)*
</Callout>

## 12. 书信写作 Letter writing (约 100 词)

<Callout type="info" title="任务">
  You are on holiday and are **running out of money**. Write an **urgent letter** to your father in about **100 words** in **three paragraphs**, asking him to help you out of your difficulties.
</Callout>

<Callout type="tip" title="写作脚手架">
  - **称呼**：Dear Dad / Dear Father（家人间用亲切称呼）。
  - **Paragraph 1 — 说明现状**：I'm writing this in a bit of a hurry because I've run into a problem. I was sure my money would last, but the holiday has turned out more expensive than I expected.
  - **Paragraph 2 — 具体困难**：车票 / 住宿 / 意外开销 —— *The hotel rates are higher than I thought, and I had to pay for an unexpected repair to my bag.* 表达紧迫感：*I'm really down to my last few pounds.*
  - **Paragraph 3 — 请求帮助 + 致歉 + 保证**：Could you possibly send me some money — say, £50 — to tide me over? I'm sorry to ask, but I honestly can't manage otherwise. I'll pay you back as soon as I get home.
  - **结束语**：Love / Yours / With love + 名字。
  - **语气要点**：坦诚（承认自己考虑不周）+ 礼貌（不是命令，是请求）+ 急迫（urgent / as soon as possible / I hate to ask）。
</Callout>

<Callout type="tip" title="参考范文（自己写完再看）">
  Dear Dad,

  I'm writing this in a bit of a hurry, as I've run into a spot of difficulty. The holiday has turned out far more expensive than I expected, and I'm now seriously short of money. I honestly thought I had brought enough.

  Everything here costs twice what I had budgeted for — the hotel, the meals, even the bus fares. I had to pay for an emergency repair to my suitcase as well, which didn't help. I'm now down to my last few pounds and I still have five days to go.

  Could you possibly send me some money to tide me over? I hate to ask, but I really can't manage otherwise. I'll pay you back the moment I get home, I promise.

  With love,
  Tom *(约 110 词)*
</Callout>

## 13. 拓展练习 Going further

<Callout type="tip" title="自学者建议">
  - **影子跟读 (shadowing)**：用第 1 节的三个 SayIt 按钮跟读 3-5 遍。特别注意 *not in the **least*** 中 least 的清晰发音，以及 *What a **relief** it was* 中 relief 的重音落在第二个音节（re-LIEF）。
  - **复述 (retell)**：合上课文，用 2 分钟把故事讲一遍。**四个阶段**：出发（路烂 + Bruce 毫不在乎）→ 大石块段（乘客紧张 / 石头砸车 / 怕漏油）→ 大裂缝段（恳求无效 / 低档跨缝 / 吓人速度）→ 水塘熄火（黄灯闪烁 / Bruce 兴高采烈宣布没油）。
  - **造句迁移**：用本课的 5 个高价值结构造你自己的句子——
    1. *get sb. to do sth.*（说服某人做）—— *My mother finally got me to clean my room.*
    2. *not in the least*（丝毫不）—— *He wasn't in the least surprised by the news.*
    3. *What a + 名词!*（感叹句）—— *What a beautiful sunset (it is)!*
    4. *sooner or later*（迟早）—— *Sooner or later, you'll have to learn to drive.*
    5. *came to a halt*（停了下来）—— *The conversation came to an awkward halt.*
  - **延伸思考**：本课是一篇典型的"人物性格驱动型"幽默叙事。全文笑话的笑点不在于路有多烂、裂缝有多宽、水塘有多深——而在于 **Bruce 的性格**：一个"No matter what ..."都面不改色的人。写人物时记住这个技巧：**让环境越糟，让人物越淡定，反差越大，效果越强**。试着用这个思路写一段"考试前大家都在疯狂复习，但有一个人……"的小故事。
  - **短语动词 get 专项**：get on / get over / get through / get out / get back ——五个都是本课出现或练习要求的 get 短语动词。拿一个"旅行遇困"的场景，用这五个 get 短语动词各造一句。
</Callout>
