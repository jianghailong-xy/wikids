<Callout type="info" title="Listen first 听前思考">
  什么样的笑话能让你笑？答案可能比你想的更复杂——你长大的地方、你的文化背景，都在悄悄决定你觉得什么好笑。本课先讲幽默与民族的秘密关系，再引入一种让很多人皱眉头的新式幽默——"病态幽默 (sick humour)"。最精彩的是第三段：一个刚摔断右腿出院的人，在派对上喝多了抱怨医院，结果脚下一滑……你猜怎么着？**What is the basis of 'sick humour'?** 带着这个问题读课文，重点留意 **largely depends on / be bound up with / find it difficult to / keep on doing** 这类高频搭配，以及课文是怎么用一个令人哭笑不得的故事来"定义"一种幽默的。
</Callout>

## The Text 课文

Whether we find a joke funny or not **largely depends on** where we have been brought up. The sense of humour is mysteriously **bound up with** national characteristics. A Frenchman, for instance, might find it hard to laugh at a Russian joke. In the same way, a Russian might **fail to see** anything amusing in a joke which would make an Englishman laugh to tears.

Most funny stories are based on comic situations. In spite of national differences, certain funny situations have a **universal appeal**. No matter where you live, you would find it difficult not to laugh at, say, Charlie Chaplin's early films. However, a new type of humour, which **stems largely from** the U.S., has recently **come into fashion**. It is called 'sick humour'. Comedians base their jokes on tragic situations like violent death or serious accidents. Many people find this sort of joke **distasteful**. The following example of 'sick humour' will enable you to **judge for yourself**.

A man who had broken his right leg was taken to hospital a few weeks before Christmas. From the moment he arrived there, he **kept on pestering** his doctor to tell him when he would be able to go home. He **dreaded** having to spend Christmas in hospital. Though the doctor **did his best**, the patient's recovery was slow. On Christmas Day, the man still had his right leg in plaster. He spent a miserable day in bed thinking of all the fun he was missing. The following day, however, the doctor **consoled** him by telling him that his chances of being able to leave hospital in time for New Year celebrations were good. The man **took heart** and, sure enough, on New Year's Eve he was able to **hobble** along to a party. To **compensate for** his unpleasant experiences in hospital, the man drank **a little more than was good for him**. In the process, he enjoyed himself thoroughly and kept telling everybody how much he hated hospitals. He was still **mumbling** something about hospitals at the end of the party when he slipped on a piece of ice and broke his left leg.

## 课文注释 Notes on the text

<Callout type="info" title="8 个值得停下来嚼一嚼的点">
  1. **whether ... or not** 引导主语从句 (l.1) — *Whether we find a joke funny or not* 整个从句作主语，谓语用单数 depends。同型：*Whether he comes or not doesn't matter.* 可简化为 *Whether or not he comes ...*。

  2. **be bound up with** (l.2-3) — "与……紧密相连"。**bound** 是 bind 的过去分词，这里表"捆绑在一起"。同型：*Her happiness is bound up with her family's well-being.*

  3. **fail to see** (l.5) — "未能看出、没能察觉"。比 *cannot see* 更正式，强调"结果就是没看出来"。同型：*I fail to understand why he refused the offer.* —— 注意 fail 后接 **to do**，不是 doing。

  4. **say** 作插入语 (l.10) — "比如说"。课文 *you would find it difficult not to laugh at, **say**, Charlie Chaplin's early films* —— 这里的 say 不是"说"，而是"例如、比如说"（= for example）。口语中常用 *Say you win the lottery, what would you do?*

  5. **stem from** (l.11) — "起源于、来自"。同型：*His fear of water stems from a childhood accident.* 注意 stem 的过去式/过去分词用 **stemmed**。

  6. **come into fashion** (l.11) — "开始流行、时兴起来"。对比：*go out of fashion*（过时）。同型：*Online learning has come into fashion in recent years.*

  7. **take heart** (l.23) — "振作精神、鼓起勇气"。不是字面"拿心"，是习语。对比：*lose heart*（灰心丧气）。同型：*The team took heart from the coach's speech.*

  8. **a little more than was good for him** (l.25-26) — "稍微多喝了一点（对他没好处的量）"。**more than was good for him** = 超过了对他有益的量，than 后面省略了主语 what。这是 NCE3 的经典省略句型。同型：*She spent more money than was necessary.*
</Callout>

## 3. 关键句型 depends on + find it hard to + keep on doing

<Callout type="tip" title="一分钟回顾">
  本课虽是一篇叙事 + 议论，语法上却扎扎实实练了三组高频结构：**动词 + 介词搭配**、**find it + adj + to do**、以及 **keep 短语**。

  | 结构 | 课文例句 | 用途 |
  |---|---|---|
  | **depend on** | *Whether we find a joke funny or not largely **depends on** where we have been brought up.* | "取决于"——on 后接名词/从句 |
  | **be bound up with** | *The sense of humour is mysteriously **bound up with** national characteristics.* | "与……紧密相连" |
  | **find it + adj + to do** | *A Frenchman might **find it hard to laugh** at a Russian joke.* | "觉得做某事……"——it 是形式宾语 |
  | **fail to do** | *a Russian might **fail to see** anything amusing* | "未能……"——比 can't 更正式 |
  | **keep on doing** | *he **kept on pestering** his doctor* | "不停地做……"——强调持续/反复 |
  | **compensate for** | *To **compensate for** his unpleasant experiences* | "补偿……"——for 后接补偿对象 |

  ### ⚠️ 3 个本课最容易踩的雷区

  1. **动词 + 介词的固定搭配不能换**：depend **on**（不是 in/at），bound up **with**（不是 to），compensate **for**（不是 of），stem **from**（不是 of）。这些介词的记忆要靠"整块记 + 大量输入"，不能凭中文逐字翻译。
  2. **find it + adj + to do**：it 是**形式宾语**，不能省。*I found it difficult to understand* ✓ / *I found difficult to understand* ✗。这和 Lesson 55 的 *make it possible to do* 是同一类结构——it 代指后面的不定式。
  3. **keep on doing vs keep doing**：两者都表"持续做"，但 **keep on** 多一层"反复、坚持、甚至令人厌烦"的味道——课文 *kept on pestering*（不断纠缠）正合适。而 *keep off / keep up with / keep out* 中的 keep 意义完全不同，见下面 Special difficulties。
</Callout>

## 4. 难点：laugh / laugh at · amuse / enjoy / entertain · keep 短语 · could / was able to

<Callout type="info" title="教材 Special difficulties 四合一">
  本课 Special difficulties 涉及四个知识点：

  ### 1. laugh vs laugh at (ll.4-5)

  - **laugh** (vi.) = 笑（动作）—— *The joke made everyone laugh.*
  - **laugh at** = 嘲笑；因……而笑 —— *Don't laugh at him.* (嘲笑) / *We all laughed at the joke.* (因笑话而笑)

  区别：laugh 是不及物的"笑"；laugh at 带宾语，可表"嘲笑"也可表"被……逗笑"。

  ### 2. amuse / enjoy / entertain (l.5)

  - **amuse** = 逗乐、使……发笑 —— *The clown amused the children.* → **amusing** (adj.) = 有趣的、好笑的
  - **enjoy** = 享受、喜欢 —— *I enjoyed the film.*（主语体验愉快）
  - **entertain** = 招待、使……快乐 —— *He entertained the guests with stories.*（强调"为他人提供娱乐"）

  课文 *anything **amusing*** = 任何好笑/有趣的东西。amusing 是形容词，修饰 anything（定语后置）。

  ### 3. keep 短语 (l.16)

  | 短语 | 含义 | 例句 |
  |---|---|---|
  | **keep on doing** | 持续/反复做 | *He kept on pestering his doctor.* |
  | **keep off** | 避开、不靠近 | *Keep off the grass.* |
  | **keep up with** | 跟上、不落后 | *She walks so fast I can't keep up with her.* |
  | **keep out** | 挡在外面、不让进入 | *The sign said: Keep out.* |

  ### 4. could vs was able to (l.23)

  - **could** = 过去的一般能力 —— *I could swim when I was five.*（五岁时就会游泳）
  - **was able to** = 过去某次具体场合下"设法做到" —— *He was able to hobble along to a party.*（他那次确实做到了）

  课文用 *was able to* 正因为它强调"这一次他真的做到了"——毕竟腿还没全好，能走到派对实属不易。
</Callout>

## 10. 摘要写作 Summary writing (≤ 80 词)

<Callout type="info" title="任务">
  *In not more than 80 words* relate the story told in the passage. Use your own words as far as possible. Do **not** include anything that is not in the last paragraph.

  按故事情节串成一段：
  1. 谁、怎么了？（摔断右腿，住院，圣诞节前）
  2. 他什么态度？（缠着医生想回家，害怕在医院过圣诞）
  3. 恢复得如何？（慢，圣诞当天还上着石膏，郁郁不乐）
  4. 转折？（医生安慰→新年有望出院→振作精神）
  5. 结局？（除夕瘸着走去派对→多喝→抱怨医院→踩冰滑倒→摔断左腿）
</Callout>

<Callout type="tip" title="参考范文（自己写完再看）">
  A man with a broken right leg was taken to hospital weeks before Christmas. Dreading to spend the holiday there, he kept pestering his doctor about going home. His recovery was slow, and on Christmas Day he lay miserably in bed. Then the doctor told him he could probably leave in time for the New Year. Encouraged, the man hobbled to a party on New Year's Eve, drank more than he should, and kept telling everyone how much he hated hospitals — only to slip on ice and break his left leg. *(80 words)*
</Callout>

## 11. 作文 Composition (≤ 250 词)

<Callout type="info" title="任务">
  Write a composition in about **250 words** telling a funny story you know well. Make out a full plan and provide a suitable title. Your composition should be in **three or four paragraphs**.
</Callout>

<Callout type="warning" title="提示骨架">
  - **标题**：给你的故事起一个能引起好奇心的名字——不要太直白，留点悬念。
  - **第 1 段（背景 + 铺垫）**：who / where / when —— 出场人物和场景。制造一点"一切正常"的假象，让读者放松警惕。
  - **第 2-3 段（事件展开）**：what went wrong —— 关键的误会/巧合/弄巧成拙。注意节奏——细节要有，但不能拖；转折要突然但不牵强。
  - **第 4 段（收尾 punchline）**：the twist —— 最好像课文一样"咚"地砸在最后一句。好的幽默故事，笑点往往在最后一句话悄悄落地。
</Callout>

<Callout type="tip" title="写作脚手架">
  - **时态主线**：一般过去时（叙事）。直接引语穿插可丰富节奏。
  - **故事动词库**：*rush, trip, spill, slip, bump into, end up, turn out, burst out laughing, realise*。
  - **节奏控制**：用 *when / just as / the moment / no sooner ... than* 制造"突然"感；用短句收尾。
  - **幽默策略**（三选一）：① **irony**（反讽——结果与预期相反，如本课）；② **exaggeration**（夸张——把小事写得惊心动魄）；③ **misunderstanding**（误会——两人说的不是同一件事，读者知道但角色不知道）。
</Callout>

<Callout type="tip" title="参考范文（自己写完再看）">
  **The wrong suitcase**

  Last summer I took a train from London to Edinburgh. I had one suitcase — a black one, the most common colour in the world. The train was crowded and I shoved my case onto the rack above my seat, then settled down to read.

  At York, a man got off and took a black suitcase from the rack. I barely looked up. When I reached Edinburgh, I pulled down the remaining case and checked into my hotel. Only when I opened it did I realise my mistake. Inside were not my clothes but a set of oil paints, three blank canvases, and a letter addressed to "The Selection Committee".

  I had exactly forty minutes before the gallery's deadline. The address on the letter was two miles away. I ran — actually ran — through the Edinburgh streets with a stranger's paintings. I arrived sweating and breathless, blurting out my story to a very confused receptionist. She took the case and handed me mine, which the other man had returned ten minutes earlier, equally red-faced.

  The committee wrote to thank me for my "evident enthusiasm". I did not tell them it was the most exercise I had taken in years. *(215 words)*
</Callout>

## 12. 书信写作 Letter writing (约 100 词)

<Callout type="info" title="任务">
  You cannot find your overcoat and think you may have left it at the house of a friend whom you visited recently. Write him a letter of about **100 words** in **three paragraphs** asking him if you did in fact leave your overcoat at his house.
</Callout>

<Callout type="tip" title="写作脚手架">
  - **称呼**：*Dear + 名*（朋友间用名字）。
  - **第 1 段**：寒暄 + 引出问题 —— *I hope you're well. I'm writing because I can't seem to find my overcoat anywhere, and I wonder if I might have left it at your place when I came over the other day.*
  - **第 2 段**：描述外套（帮助对方辨认）+ 不是紧急但如能找到请告知 —— *It's the navy-blue wool one with brown buttons. If you do come across it, could you let me know? No hurry at all.*
  - **第 3 段**：礼貌收尾 + 再次感谢 —— *Thanks so much, and sorry for the trouble. Looking forward to catching up soon.*
  - **结束语**：*Best wishes, / Yours, / [你的名字]*
</Callout>

<Callout type="tip" title="参考范文（自己写完再看）">
  Dear Jack,

  Hope you've been well since we last met. I'm writing with a small request — I can't find my overcoat anywhere, and it occurred to me that I might have left it at your house when I visited last Tuesday. Would you mind having a look?

  It's a dark grey, single-breasted wool coat with black buttons and a burgundy lining. I had it with me when I arrived, but I don't remember putting it back on when I left. If it happens to be there, just let me know — there's absolutely no rush.

  Sorry to trouble you over something so trivial. Hope to see you again soon.

  Yours,

  David *(about 100 words)*
</Callout>

## 13. 拓展练习 Going further

<Callout type="tip" title="自学者建议">
  - **影子跟读 (shadowing)**：用第 1 节的三个 SayIt 按钮跟读 3-5 遍。第一句注意 *largely depends on* 的连读（largely 尾音 /li/ + depends 首音 /dɪ/ 自然过渡）；第三句练长句节奏——*He was still mumbling something about hospitals | at the end of the party | when he slipped on a piece of ice | and broke his left leg.*
  - **复述 (retell)**：合上课文，用 2 分钟用自己的话讲一遍那个"乐极生悲"的故事。**四步叙事链**：住院焦虑（broken leg → pestering → dread）→ 圣诞痛苦（miserable day → missing the fun）→ 转机→乐极（doctor consoles → take heart → party → drinks too much → hates hospitals）→ 生悲（slips on ice → breaks left leg）。
  - **造句迁移**：用今天的 5 个高价值结构造你自己的句子——
    1. *depend on ...*（取决于）—— *Success largely depends on how much effort you put in.*
    2. *find it + adj + to do*（觉得做某事……）—— *She found it impossible to finish the work on time.*
    3. *keep on doing*（持续做）—— *He kept on talking even after the meeting ended.*
    4. *more than was good for him*（超过对他有益的限度）—— *The child ate more ice cream than was good for her.*
    5. *was/were able to*（某次具体场合做到了）—— *Despite the heavy traffic, I was able to catch the train.*
  - **延伸思考**：本课的幽默其实有两层——文章前半段在**论述**幽默（民族性、普遍性、sick humour 的定义），后半段**演绎**了一个 sick humour 的实例。这种"先定义，再举例"的结构是英文说明文的一种经典套路。另外，课文结尾的 punchline——*he slipped on a piece of ice and broke his left leg*——有没有让你想起中文里"福无双至，祸不单行"？不同文化的幽默表达方式不同，但"黑色巧合"这种笑点，似乎全世界的人都能懂。
</Callout>
