  **Why did Harry decide to give up his little game? 哈里为什么决定金盆洗手、不再玩这个小把戏了？** 带着这个问题读课文，重点留意全篇是怎么用 **would（过去的习惯）/ used to / 现在分词短语 / it 作形式宾语 / 过去完成时** 把这个“搬起石头砸自己脚”的故事讲得活灵活现。

## The Text 课文

My cousin, Harry, **keeps a large curiously-shaped bottle on permanent display** in his study. **Despite the fact that** the bottle is tinted a delicate shade of green, an observant visitor would soon notice that it is **filled with what looks like a thick, greyish substance**. If you were to ask Harry what was in the bottle, he would tell you that it contained perfumed mud. If you expressed doubt or surprise, he would immediately invite you to smell it and then to rub some into your skin. This brief experiment would dispel any further doubts you might have. The bottle really does contain perfumed mud. **How Harry came into the possession of this outlandish stuff** makes an interesting story which he is fond of relating. Furthermore, the acquisition of this bottle **cured him of a bad habit** he had been developing for years.

Harry **used to consider it a great joke** to go into expensive cosmetic shops and make outrageous requests for goods that do not exist. He **would invent fanciful names on the spot**. On entering a shop, he would ask for a new perfume called 'Scented Shadow' or for 'insoluble bath cubes'. If a shop assistant told him she had not heard of it, **he would pretend to be considerably put out**. He loved to be told that one of his imaginary products **was temporarily out of stock** and he would faithfully promise to call again at some future date, but of course he never did. **How Harry managed to keep a straight face** during these performances **is quite beyond me**.

Harry does not need to be prompted to explain how he bought his precious bottle of mud. One day, he went to an exclusive shop in London and asked for 'Myrolite'. The shop assistant looked puzzled and Harry repeated the word, **slowly stressing each syllable**. When the woman shook her head **in bewilderment**, Harry **went on to explain that** 'myrolite' was a hard, amber-like substance which could be used to remove freckles. This explanation **evidently conveyed something to the woman** who searched shelf after shelf. She produced all sorts of weird concoctions, but **none of them met with Harry's requirements**. When Harry **put on his act of being mildly annoyed**, the assistant promised to order some for him. **Intoxicated by his success**, Harry then asked for perfumed mud. He expected the assistant to look at him **in blank astonishment**. However, **it was his turn to be surprised**, for the woman's eyes immediately lit up and she fetched several bottles which she placed on the counter for Harry to inspect. For once, **Harry had to admit defeat**. He picked up what seemed to be the smallest bottle and discreetly asked the price. He was glad to **get away with a mere twenty pounds** and he **beat a hasty retreat**, clutching the precious bottle under his arm. From then on, Harry decided that this little game he had invented might prove expensive. The curious bottle, which now **adorns the bookcase** in his study, was his first and last purchase of rare cosmetics.

## 课文注释 Notes on the text

<Callout type="info" title="8 个值得停下来嚼一嚼的点">
  1. **a delicate shade of green** (l.3) — “淡绿色”。**shade** 在这里指“（颜色的）浓淡、色度”，**a shade of + 颜色** = “一种……的色调”；**delicate** 修饰颜色时是“淡雅的、柔和的”。同型：*a deep shade of blue / a pale shade of pink*。
  2. **If you were to ask Harry ..., he would tell you ...** (l.5) — **were to + 动词原形**是虚拟语气，用来表示对**将来不太可能 / 纯假设**情况的设想，比一般条件句更“委婉、更假设”。主句配 **would + 原形**。同型：*If you were to win the lottery, what would you do?*
  3. **come into the possession of ...** (l.9) — “得到、占有……”。固定表达 **come into possession of sth.**（注意 possession 前可加 the）= 获得某物（常指意外或经过一番周折得到）。近义：*get hold of / acquire*。名词形式即课文 l.10 的 **acquisition**（获得）。
  4. **cure sb. of sth.** (l.10) — “治好某人的（病 / 坏毛病）”。介词用 **of**。课文 *cured him of a bad habit* = 治好了他多年养成的坏习惯。同型：*The doctor cured her of her illness. / This experience cured me of my fear of flying.*
  5. **on the spot** (l.13) — “当场、立即”。课文 *invent fanciful names on the spot* = 当场瞎编名字。同型：*He was offered the job on the spot. / She made up an excuse on the spot.*
  6. **put out** (l.15) — “心烦、不快、恼火”。**be / feel put out** 是固定形容词性短语，相当于 *annoyed / upset*。课文 *pretend to be considerably put out* = 假装十分不快。
  7. **out of stock** (l.16) — “缺货、脱销”。反义 **in stock**（有货）。课文 *was temporarily out of stock* = 暂时缺货。这是 Harry 最爱听的话——因为这说明售货员“信”了。
  8. **keep a straight face** (l.17) — “板着脸、忍住不笑、装作一本正经”。固定短语。课文 *How Harry managed to keep a straight face ... is quite beyond me* = 他怎么能在这些表演中忍住不笑，我实在弄不明白。**be beyond sb.** = “某人无法理解”。
</Callout>

## 3. 关键句型 would / used to 与非真实条件句

<Callout type="tip" title="一分钟回顾">
  本课语法主线是**“讲过去的习惯”**——一篇短文里把 *used to* 与 *would* 这两把“回忆的尺子”用了个遍，再配上 **it 作形式宾语 / 形式主语**、**虚拟条件 were to**、**主语从句**等结构。圈出课文的动词，分布是这样的：

  | 结构 / 形式 | 课文例句 | 用途 |
  |---|---|---|
  | **used to + V** | *Harry **used to consider** it a great joke ...* | 过去经常做、现在不做了的习惯 |
  | **would + V（过去习惯）** | *He **would invent** fanciful names ... he **would ask** for ... he **would pretend** ...* | 过去反复发生的动作（不用于静态动词） |
  | **were to + V（虚拟）** | *If you **were to** ask Harry ..., he **would tell** you ...* | 对将来纯假设 / 不太可能情况的设想 |
  | **it 作形式宾语** | *consider **it** a great joke to go ... / thought **it** funny to ask ...* | it 占位，真正宾语是后面的不定式 |
  | **主语从句作主语** | ***How Harry managed to keep a straight face** ... is quite beyond me.* | 从句整体当主语，谓语用单数 |
  | **what 引导的名词性从句** | *filled with **what looks like** a thick substance* | what = the thing that，引导宾语 / 表语从句 |
  | **过去完成时** | *a bad habit he **had been developing** for years* | 过去某点之前一直在持续 |

  ### ⚠️ 4 个本课最容易踩的雷区

  1. **used to vs. be used to**：*used to do sth.* = 过去常做（现已不做）；*be used to **doing** sth.* = 习惯于做（接动名词）——两者天差地别。课文是前者。
  2. **would 表过去习惯**只能接**动作动词**，不能接 *be / have / know* 这类**状态动词**。“他过去很有钱”只能说 *He used to be rich*，不能说 *He would be rich*。
  3. **were to 的虚拟条件**：主语无论单复数都用 **were**（If I were to / If he were to）；主句必须配 **would / could / might + 原形**。
  4. **it 作形式宾语**的固定框架：*consider / think / find / make + **it** + 形容词 / 名词 + to do*。课文 *consider it a great joke to go ...* 里的 it 不能省，真正的宾语是 *to go ...*。
</Callout>

## 4. 难点：would / used to 与短语动词 put out / out of stock

<Callout type="info" title="3 组最容易混的“近义而不同”">
  本课的难点集中在几组**形近 / 义近却用法不同**的表达上。错一个，意思就拐弯：

  | 易混点 | 正确用法 | 常见错误 |
  |---|---|---|
  | **used to / be used to** | *used to **do*** = 过去常做（现已不做）；*be used to **doing*** = 习惯于做 | 把两者混用：~~used to doing~~ / ~~be used to do~~ |
  | **would / used to** | 两者都表过去习惯；但 **would 不接状态动词**，**used to 既可接动作也可接状态** | ~~He would be rich.~~（状态须用 used to be） |
  | **come into possession / take possession** | *come into possession of* = 得到（结果）；*take possession of* = 占有（动作） | 介词错：possession **of**，不是 ~~possession to~~ |

  ### ⚠️ 本课的短语动词小词典

  - **put out**（l.15）= 心烦、恼火（*be put out* = annoyed）。注意 put out 还有“扑灭、伸出、出版”等义，靠语境区分。
  - **out of stock**（l.16）= 缺货 ↔ **in stock** 有货。
  - **go on to do sth.**（l.22）= 接着去做（另一件事）；区别于 *go on **doing*** = 继续做（同一件事）。课文 *went on to explain* = 接着解释。
  - **get away with**（l.29）= （只付出很小代价就）脱身、侥幸过关。课文 *get away with a mere twenty pounds* = 只破费 20 英镑就脱身。
  - **beat a hasty retreat**（l.30）= 急忙撤退、溜之大吉（固定短语）。

  ### 5 个本课常见雷区

  1. *be used to* 后接**名词 / 动名词**（be used to the noise / to getting up early），不接动词原形。
  2. *would* 表过去习惯时常与 *used to* 互换，但描述**过去的状态**（住在、拥有、相信……）只能用 *used to*。
  3. *go on **to do*** vs *go on **doing***：前者“接着做另一件事”，后者“继续做同一件事”——课文是前者。
  4. *put out* 作“恼火”讲时是**被动 / 系表结构**：*be / feel / look put out*，不能说 ~~he put out~~ 表生气。
  5. *get away with* 后接“代价 / 罪行 / 轻罚”：*get away with murder / a small fine / twenty pounds*。
</Callout>

## 11. 作文 Composition (约 300 词)

<Callout type="info" title="任务">
  Write a composition in **about 300 words** on **one** of the following:
  a. *A day's shopping.*（购物的一天）
  b. *The strangest person I have ever met.*（我遇到过的最古怪的人）
</Callout>

<Callout type="warning" title="提示骨架（以 b 为例）">
  - **开头**：一句话立人物——你遇过的最古怪的人是谁？在哪儿遇到的？
  - **外貌 / 第一印象**：用一两个具体细节让人物“立起来”（穿着、神态、说话方式）。
  - **怪在哪里**：选 2-3 个最离奇的行为 / 习惯，分点展开（像 Harry 那样“煞有介事地要根本不存在的东西”）。
  - **一次具体事件**：详写一个场景，用对话 / 动作把“古怪”演出来，而不是干说。
  - **结尾**：你对这个人的总体感受 / 一句点睛（幽默或回味）。
</Callout>

<Callout type="tip" title="写作脚手架">
  - **时态主线**：回忆性叙事以**一般过去时**为骨架；背景 / 当时正在发生用**过去进行时**（*was wearing / was talking*）；更早的事用**过去完成时**（*had never seen / had always been*）；描述此人长期习性可用 *used to / would*（呼应本课语法！）。
  - **“古怪”形容词库**：*eccentric, peculiar, odd, outlandish, bizarre, quirky, unconventional, fanciful*。
  - **“煞有介事”动词库**：*put on an act, keep a straight face, pretend to, make outrageous requests, invent ... on the spot, with mock seriousness*。
  - **结构亮点**：开篇可用 *The strangest person I have ever met was ...*（最高级 + 现在完成时定语从句）；描写习性时用 *He would always ..., and of course he never ...*（would 表过去习惯，正是本课句型）。
</Callout>

<Callout type="tip" title="参考范文（自己写完再看）">
  The strangest person I have ever met was my old neighbour, Mr. Pringle. He used to keep a large, curiously-shaped umbrella by his front door, although, as far as anyone knew, it had never once been opened. Mr. Pringle was a small, neat man who always wore a bow tie and addressed even the postman as 'my dear sir'. What made him truly eccentric, however, was his shopping.

  Every Saturday he would set off for the market with a long handwritten list, and he would solemnly ask the stallholders for things that simply did not exist. I once heard him request 'a pound of silent thunder' and 'three yards of yesterday's sunshine'. When a puzzled trader told him these were out of stock, Mr. Pringle would look considerably put out, sigh deeply, and faithfully promise to call again. Of course, he never bought anything at all.

  For a long time I imagined he was merely confused. Then, one afternoon, I caught him winking at a small boy who had overheard the whole performance, and I realised the truth: the old man knew perfectly well that none of his goods existed. The joke, all along, was that he knew something the rest of us did not.

  After that I began to look forward to his Saturday outings. There was something splendid about a man who could keep so straight a face while turning an ordinary market into a stage. When Mr. Pringle finally moved away, the market seemed a duller, more sensible place — and I have never since met anyone half so wonderfully strange. *(about 270 words)*
</Callout>

## 12. 书信写作 Letter writing

<Callout type="info" title="任务">
  Your house was damaged during a recent thunderstorm. Write a letter of **about 100 words** to a friend telling him **what happened**.
</Callout>

<Callout type="tip" title="写作脚手架">
  - **称呼**：写给朋友用 *Dear + 名字*（如 *Dear Tom,*），结尾用 *Yours / Best wishes / Love,*——比 *Yours sincerely* 更亲切。
  - **开头 (Introduction)**：一句寒暄 + 直接抛出大事——*You'll never guess what happened to us last week.*
  - **主体 (Body)** —— 按时间顺序串：
    1. **天气背景**（过去进行时设景）：*It was pouring with rain and the wind was howling when ...*
    2. **发生了什么**（一般过去 + 过去完成）：*A bolt of lightning struck the chimney, which crashed through the roof ...*
    3. **后果 / 当时反应**：*Water poured into the bedrooms and we spent the whole night ...*
    4. **现在情况**（现在完成 / 现在进行）：*The builders have already started repairs and we are staying with my aunt for now.*
  - **结尾 (Conclusion)**：*Luckily no one was hurt. Do come and see the damage for yourself! Write soon.*
  - **时态混搭**：背景 *was pouring / was howling*（过去进行）；主线 *struck / crashed*（一般过去）；更早 *had already gone to bed*（过去完成）；现状 *have started / are staying*（现在完成 / 现在进行）。
</Callout>

<Callout type="tip" title="参考范文（自己写完再看）">
  Dear Tom,

  You'll never guess what happened to us last Thursday. It was pouring with rain and the wind was howling when, just after we had gone to bed, a tremendous bolt of lightning struck the chimney. It crashed straight through the roof and rain came pouring into the bedrooms. We spent the rest of the night carrying buckets up and down the stairs! Luckily, no one was hurt, but the ceiling is ruined. The builders have already started work, and we are staying with my aunt until the house is dry again.

  Do come and see the mess for yourself!

  Best wishes,

  Jack *(about 105 words)*
</Callout>

## 13. 拓展练习 Going further

<Callout type="tip" title="自学者建议">
  - **影子跟读 (shadowing)**：用第 1 节的三个 SayIt 按钮跟读 3-5 遍。重点感受英式幽默的“一本正经”——把 *consider it a great joke / keep a straight face / it was his turn to be surprised* 这几处读出“面无表情却暗藏笑意”的语气。
  - **复述 (retell)**：合上课文，用 2 分钟把 Harry 的故事讲一遍。**叙事三段**：道具引子（古怪瓶子 + 香水泥）→ 旧习惯（专要不存在的商品、爱听“缺货”、忍住不笑）→ 翻车经过（要 Myrolite → 又要香水泥 → 竟真有好几瓶 → 花 20 镑认输 → 金盆洗手）。
  - **造句迁移**：用今天的 5 个高价值结构造你自己的句子——
    1. *used to + V*（过去的习惯）—— *I used to walk to school every day.*
    2. *would + V*（过去反复的动作）—— *On Sundays, my grandfather would sit by the window and read.*
    3. *If + 主语 + were to + V, 主语 + would + V*（虚拟条件）—— *If you were to ask me, I would say yes.*
    4. *consider / think it + adj + to do*（it 作形式宾语）—— *I find it hard to believe his story.*
    5. *How + 从句 + is beyond me*（主语从句 + be beyond sb.）—— *How she finished it so fast is quite beyond me.*
  - **延伸思考**：本课的语法主角是 **would 与 used to**——两把“回忆的尺子”。下次写英文回忆 / 描述某人的旧习惯时，问自己：“是反复的**动作**吗？→ would 或 used to 都行；是过去的**状态**（住在、拥有、相信）吗？→ 只能用 used to。”另一个值得回味的点：标题 **“Mud is mud”（实事求是）**——Harry 编了一辈子假货名，最后栽在一团“货真价实”的泥上。**这正是幽默的反转结构**：把人物的“小聪明”一路推高，再让现实在最后一刻给他当头一棒。好的叙事，往往就藏在这“搬起石头砸自己脚”的最后一句里。
</Callout>
