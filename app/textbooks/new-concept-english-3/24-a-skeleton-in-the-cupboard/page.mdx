  **Who was Sebastian?** 带着这个问题读课文，留意作者是如何用**叙述 + 对话**的手法，在短短三段文字里把悬念拉到顶点再轻描淡写地解开的——文章倒数第二句那嫣然一笑般的 *'That's Sebastian.'*，就是全文的点睛之笔。
</Callout>

## The Text 课文

We often read in novels how a seemingly respectable person or family has some terrible secret which has been concealed from strangers for years. The English language possesses a vivid saying to describe this sort of situation. The terrible secret is called **'a skeleton in the cupboard'**. At some dramatic moment in the story, the terrible secret becomes known and a reputation is ruined. The reader's **hair stands on end** when he reads in the final pages of the novel that the heroine, a dear old lady who had always been so kind to everybody, had, in her youth, poisoned every one of her five husbands.

It is all very well for such things to occur in fiction. **To varying degrees**, we all have secrets which we do not want even our closest friends to learn, but **few of us have skeletons in the cupboard**. The only person I know who has a skeleton in the cupboard is George Carlton, and he is very proud of the fact. George studied medicine in his youth. Instead of becoming a doctor, however, he became a successful writer of detective stories.

I once spent an uncomfortable weekend which I shall never forget at his house. George showed me to the guestroom which, he said, was rarely used. He told me to unpack my things and then come down to dinner. After I had stacked my shirts and underclothes in two empty drawers, I decided to hang one of the two suits I had brought with me in the cupboard. I opened the cupboard door and then **stood in front of it petrified**. A skeleton was dangling before my eyes. The sudden movement of the door made it sway slightly and it gave me the impression that it was **about to leap out at me**. Dropping my suit, I dashed downstairs to tell George. This was worse than 'a terrible secret'; this was a real skeleton! But George **was unsympathetic**. 'Oh, that,' he said with a smile as if he were talking about an old friend. 'That's Sebastian. You forget that I was a medical student **once upon a time**.'

## 课文注释 Notes on the text

<Callout type="info" title="6 个值得停下来嚼一嚼的点">
  1. **"A skeleton in the cupboard"** (l.4) — 此系一成语，意为"家丑"。字面是"柜中骷髅"，比喻不愿外人知道的秘密或丑事。课文标题一语双关：乔治的柜子里真的有一具骷髅。

  2. **"conceal sth. from sb."** (l.2) — 作"对某人隐瞒某事"解。同型：*He concealed the truth from his parents.* conceal 比 hide 更正式，常用于"隐瞒秘密/真相"。

  3. **"The reader's hair stands on end"** (l.6) — 读者感到毛骨悚然。**hair stands on end** = 毛骨悚然，形容极度恐惧。字面义是"头发竖起来"，一种因恐惧而产生的生理反应。

  4. **"to varying degrees"** (l.10) — 在不同程度上。**varying** 作形容词修饰 degrees，意为"不同的、变化的"。注意此处 varying 是"各不相同"，不是"变化中的"。

  5. **"stood in front of it petrified"** (ll.17-18) — 站在柜前吓呆了。**stand** 此处表示处于某种状态，起系动词的作用，后面常接形容词、介词短语或过去分词，表示主语处于某种状态中的特征，可视作表语。同型：*He stood motionless. / She sat there silent.*

  6. **"the impression that it was about to leap out..."** (l.19) — **that** 引导的从句作 **impression** 的同位语。**be about to do sth.** 表示按照计划即将做的动作（或"眼看就要……"）。同型：*I had the impression that someone was watching me. / The train was about to leave.*
</Callout>

## 3. 关键句型 副词位置与使役结构 Adverb position & causative patterns

<Callout type="tip" title="一分钟回顾">
  本课最核心的句型分为三类：**副词位置**（频率副词在句中放在哪里）、**使役结构**（make + 宾语 + 不带 to 的不定式）、以及**"即将发生"**的表达（be about to do / no sooner ... than ...）。

  | 结构 | 课文例句 | 用途 |
  |---|---|---|
  | **频率副词位置** | *We **often** read in novels ...* (l.1) | 频率副词（often/always/never）放在实义动词之前 |
  | **make + 宾语 + 动词原形** | *The sudden movement of the door **made it sway*** (l.18) | 使役动词 make 后接不带 to 的不定式 |
  | **be about to do** | *it was **about to leap out** at me* (l.19) | 表示"眼看就要……" |
  | **as if + 虚拟语气** | *he said with a smile **as if he were talking** about an old friend* (l.21) | as if 引导方式状语从句，可表虚拟（与事实相反） |
  | **no sooner ... than ...** | （MCQ 第 7 题）*No sooner had I opened the cupboard door than I stood ...* | "一……就……"（主句用过去完成时倒装） |

  ### ⚠️ 6 个本课最容易踩的雷区

  1. **频率副词的位置**：放在**实义动词前**、**be 动词之后**（*We **often read*** / *he **is always** kind*）。**never** 要放在助动词之后（*I have **never** forgotten*），not 与之相反。
  2. **make + 宾语 + 动词原形**：make 后面的动词不能加 to（*made it **sway***，不是 *made it **to sway***）。同理：*let him go / have him wait*。
  3. **be about to do** 表"即将发生"，通常不再加时间状语（不说 *about to do it tomorrow*——因为 about to 本身已有"立刻"的含义）。
  4. **as if + were**：as if 后的 be 动词在虚拟语气中用 **were**（*as if he **were** talking*），无论主语是第几人称。
  5. **no sooner ... than ...**：no sooner 位于句首时，主句要**倒装**——*No sooner **had I** opened ... than ...*；注意搭配是 **than**，不是 when 或 then。
  6. **once upon a time** (l.22) 是故事开头的套语"从前、很久以前"，此处乔治用这个词自带幽默——他把自己的学医岁月说成了"童话里的从前"。
</Callout>

## 4. 难点：want + 宾语 + 不定式 / 动名词作宾语 Special difficulties

<Callout type="info" title="教材 Key structures 改写题">
  教材要求掌握两个重要句型：

  **1. want / teach / allow + 宾语 + 不定式**（ISD 11a）
  - *We do not **want** even our closest friends **to learn***（我们连最亲密的朋友都不愿让他们知道）
  - **want / teach / allow / tell / ask + sb. + to do sth.**
  - 同型：*He taught me **to swim**. / They allowed us **to leave**.*

  **2. few 与 a few；little 与 a little 的区别**（IKS 32b）
  - **few + 可数名词** = "很少（几乎没有）"，含否定意味
  - **a few + 可数名词** = "几个、一些"，含肯定意味
  - **little + 不可数名词** = "很少（几乎没有）"，含否定意味
  - **a little + 不可数名词** = "一点、少量"，含肯定意味

  | 词 | + 名词类型 | 含义 | 语气 |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | **few** | 可数复数 | 很少（几乎没有） | 否定 |
  | **a few** | 可数复数 | 几个（有一些） | 肯定 |
  | **little** | 不可数 | 很少（几乎没有） | 否定 |
  | **a little** | 不可数 | 一点（有一些） | 肯定 |

  课文原句：*but **few of us** have skeletons in the cupboard* (l.11) —— "但我们当中极少人有柜中骷髅"。
</Callout>

## 11. 作文 Composition (约 250 词)

<Callout type="info" title="任务">
  In not more than **250 words** write a continuation of the above passage **in the first person**. Use the ideas given below. Do not write more than four paragraphs.

  **Title:** A Week-end with Sebastian.

  **Introduction:** George Carlton refused to remove skeleton — nowhere to put it.
  **Development:** How I spent the night — very uncomfortable — took Sebastian out of the cupboard — walked around the house with him looking for somewhere to put him — maid just going to bed — saw Sebastian walking around — screamed — Carlton appeared — the scene.
  **Conclusion:** Two years later I read about myself and Sebastian in one of Carlton's detective stories.
</Callout>

<Callout type="warning" title="提示骨架">
  - **第 1 段 (Introduction)**：乔治拒绝把骷髅拿走——"塞巴斯蒂安住那儿，没别的地方放。" 我只好硬着头皮回房。
  - **第 2 段 (Development 1)**：那晚我辗转难眠——总觉得骷髅在晃。半夜我决定把塞巴斯蒂安从柜子里拿出来，抱着他在屋里转，想找个地方"安置"他。
  - **第 3 段 (Development 2)**：女仆正要睡觉，看见"塞巴斯蒂安"走来走去——尖叫——乔治出现——看到我抱着骷髅站在走廊里——笑翻了。
  - **第 4 段 (Conclusion)**：两年后，我在乔治最新的侦探小说里读到了一个"抱着骷髅满屋跑"的角色。
</Callout>

<Callout type="tip" title="写作脚手架">
  - **时态主线**：叙述过去的事，用**一般过去时**为主。描述"当时正在做"的事情用**过去进行时**（*the maid was just going to bed / Sebastian was dangling*）。
  - **第一人称**：用 I / me / my 贯穿全文，保持与课文一致。
  - **幽默笔法**：参考课文本身黑色幽默的语气——乔治的"满不在乎"、作者的"大惊小怪"可以延续。
  - **场景描写**的动词库：*crept out of bed / tiptoed across the room / grabbed the skeleton / clattered down the hallway / let out a scream / burst out laughing / turned as white as a sheet*。
  - **对话插入**：女仆尖叫 → 乔治出现 → 两人的简短对话能活跃节奏。
  - **首尾呼应**：开头说"无处可放"，结尾说明骨头终于"有了用处"——被写进了小说。幽默收尾。
</Callout>

<Callout type="tip" title="参考范文（自己写完再看）">
  **A Week-end with Sebastian**

  When I asked George to remove the skeleton, he just laughed. 'But where would Sebastian go? The cupboard is his home.' I returned to my room with a chill running down my spine. Sleep was impossible. Every creak of the old house made me imagine Sebastian swinging gently in the dark.

  Around midnight, I decided I could bear it no longer. I crept out of bed, opened the cupboard, and carefully took Sebastian down. He was lighter than I expected. I carried him into the hallway, looking for somewhere — anywhere — else to put him. A broom cupboard? Under the stairs? Nothing seemed right.

  As I turned a corner, the maid appeared. She was just going to bed. She saw me — a man in his pyjamas, holding a skeleton in his arms — and let out a scream that could have woken the dead. George rushed out, took one look at the scene, and burst into laughter. 'Sebastian, are you sleepwalking again?' he said to the skeleton.

  Two years later, I picked up George's latest detective novel. There it was — a character who spent a sleepless night carrying a skeleton around a house. George had turned my misery into fiction. I had to admit: it was quite a good story. *(约 230 词)*
</Callout>
## 12. 书信写作 Letter writing (约 100 词)

<Callout type="info" title="任务">
  In not more than **100 words** write a letter of **three paragraphs** to your family doctor (who is also a personal friend) telling him that you have **not been feeling well lately** and asking him if you **could make an appointment**.
</Callout>

<Callout type="tip" title="写作脚手架">
  - **称呼**：*Dear Dr. [姓]*（既是家庭医生又是朋友，用 Dr. + 姓既正式又亲切）。
  - **第 1 段**：问候 + 说明写信缘由（最近身体不适）—— *I hope this letter finds you well. I am writing because I have not been feeling myself lately.*
  - **第 2 段**：具体描述症状—— *I have been feeling unusually tired / suffering from headaches / having trouble sleeping.* 注意用**现在完成进行时**（have been feeling）表示最近一段时间持续不适。
  - **第 3 段**：请求预约 + 表示灵活配合—— *Could I possibly make an appointment to see you this week? I am free any afternoon.*
  - **结束语**：朋友间的亲切语气—— *Looking forward to hearing from you. Best wishes / Yours, [你的名字]。*
  - **时态要点**：描述近来持续的症状用 **现在完成进行时**（*I have been feeling*）；预约请求用 **Could I ...** 委婉语气。
</Callout>

<Callout type="tip" title="参考范文（自己写完再看）">
  Dear Dr. Mitchell,

  I hope you are well. I am writing because I have not been feeling quite myself for the past few weeks. I have been unusually tired and have had trouble sleeping, which is not like me at all.

  I have also been experiencing frequent headaches, especially in the afternoons, and a general sense of low energy that no amount of rest seems to cure. I wonder if it might be related to stress or perhaps something more physical.

  Could I possibly make an appointment to see you this week? I am free most afternoons. Please let me know what time would suit you best.

  With best wishes,

  Paul *(约 110 词)*
</Callout>
## 13. 拓展练习 Going further

<Callout type="tip" title="自学者建议">
  - **影子跟读 (shadowing)**：用第 1 节的三个 SayIt 按钮跟读 3-5 遍。尤其注意第三句（乔治的对话）——模仿他那种**轻描淡写、满不在乎**的语气，跟"说老朋友一样"说骷髅。英语中这种"用日常语气说惊人内容"的反差感，是讲好故事的关键。
  - **复述 (retell)**：合上课文，用 2 分钟把这个故事讲一遍。**三级结构**：成语引入（"家丑"是什么）→ 作者亲身经历（打开柜门、看见骷髅、吓坏了）→ 乔治的反转式结局（"那是塞巴斯蒂安"）。
  - **造句迁移**：用今天的 5 个高价值结构造你自己的句子——
    1. *make + 宾语 + 动词原形*（使……做……）—— *The news made him jump out of his seat.*
    2. *about to do*（眼看就要……）—— *I was about to leave when the phone rang.*
    3. *as if + were + doing*（虚拟语气：好像……）—— *He walked in as if he owned the place.*
    4. *no sooner ... than ...*（一……就……）—— *No sooner had I sat down than the doorbell rang.*
    5. *few of us + 复数谓语*（很少人……）—— *Few of us realise how much we depend on electricity.*
  - **延伸思考**：本课是一篇典型的**"第一人称叙事"**（first-person narrative）——作者既是被吓到的角色，又是讲故事的人。注意观察课文如何用**三段结构**（成语 → 铺垫 → 亲身经历）把悬念一步步推至顶点，然后在最后一句用一句轻描淡写的 *'That's Sebastian'* 把悬念"解"掉。这种**"紧拉慢松"**的叙事手法，在新概念英语第三册中非常经典。下一次你写英文故事时，不妨试试：先用段"宽泛的议论"开头，再落到"我的亲身经历"——把读者从"听说"带入"看见"。
</Callout>
