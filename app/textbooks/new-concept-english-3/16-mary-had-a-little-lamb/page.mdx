</Callout>

## The Text 课文

Mary and her husband Dimitri lived in Perachora, a tiny village in southern Greece. One of Mary's most **prize possessions** was a little white lamb which her husband had given her. She kept it tied to a tree in a field during the day and went to fetch it every evening. One evening, however, the lamb was missing. The rope had been cut, so it was obvious that the lamb had been stolen.

When Dimitri came in from the fields, his wife told him what had happened. Dimitri at once set out to find the thief. He knew it would not prove difficult in such a small village. After telling several of his friends about the theft, Dimitri found out that his neighbour, Aleko, **had suddenly acquired a new lamb**. Dimitri immediately went to Aleko's house and angrily accused him of stealing the lamb. He told him **he had better return it** or he would call the police. Aleko denied taking it and led Dimitri into his backyard. It was true that he had just bought a lamb, he explained, but his lamb was black. Ashamed of having acted so rashly, Dimitri apologized to Aleko for having accused him. While they were talking it began to rain and Dimitri stayed in Aleko's house until the rain stopped. When he went outside half an hour later, he was astonished to find that the little black lamb was almost white. Its wool, which had been dyed black, had been washed clean by the rain!

## 课文注释 Notes on the text

<Callout type="info" title="6 个值得停下来嚼一嚼的点">
  1. **prize possession** (l.2) — \"最珍贵的财产\"。**prize** 在这里是**形容词**，意为\"珍贵的、值得珍藏的\"（不是动词\"获奖\"）。同型：*prize collection / prize specimen / prize pupil*（得意门生）。
  2. **keep it tied to a tree** (l.3) — **keep + 宾语 + 过去分词** 表"使……保持某种状态"。同型：*keep the door **locked** / keep the cat **shut in** / keep me **informed***。这里 *tied* 是被动状态（小羊被拴着）。
  3. **set out to + V** (l.6) — \"出发去做……\"。**set out** 既可指动身（启程），又可指**着手开始**做某事。同型：*She set out to **prove** him wrong. / He set out to **write** a novel.*
  4. **accuse sb. of doing** (l.8) — \"指控某人做了某事\"——介词 **of** 后接 V-ing。这是本课的核心句型之一。同型：*deny / admit / confess to / be guilty of / be ashamed of + V-ing*（IKS 92）。
  5. **had better + V 原形** (l.9) — \"最好……\"。比 should 更**带威胁感**，常用于警告。否定 *had better not + V*。⚠️ 后接 V 原形（不是 to V，不是 V-ing）。
  6. **ashamed of having + V-ed** (l.11) — \"为自己**之前**做过的事感到羞愧\"。**完成式 V-ing (having done)** 表示\"动作在 ashamed 之前发生\"。如果只是 *ashamed of doing*，则不强调先后。
</Callout>

## 3. 关键句型 介词 + V-ing (IKS 92)

<Callout type="tip" title="一分钟回顾">
  本课的核心句型是**介词 + 动名词 (V-ing)**——介词后面绝对不能跟动词原形或 to V，只能跟 V-ing 或名词。课文里集中出现了 4 个：

  - *accused him **of stealing** the lamb*（指控他偷羊）
  - *Aleko denied **taking** it*（否认拿走它）— deny 也接 V-ing
  - *Ashamed **of having acted** so rashly*（为自己鲁莽行事感到羞愧）
  - *apologized to Aleko **for having accused** him*（为指控他道歉）

  ### 高频"介词 + V-ing"搭配速记

  | 搭配 | 含义 | 例句 |
  |---|---|---|
  | **accuse sb. of doing** | 指控某人做了…… | *They accused him of cheating.* |
  | **be ashamed of doing** | 为……感到羞愧 | *I'm ashamed of having lied to you.* |
  | **apologize for doing** | 为……道歉 | *He apologized for being late.* |
  | **be guilty of doing** | 犯了……的罪 | *She was guilty of forging the signature.* |
  | **suspect sb. of doing** | 怀疑某人…… | *I suspected him of taking the money.* |
  | **prevent sb. from doing** | 阻止某人…… | *Nothing can prevent us from going.* |
  | **insist on doing** | 坚持要做…… | *He insisted on paying the bill.* |
  | **succeed in doing** | 成功做到…… | *She succeeded in escaping.* |

  ### ⚠️ 3 个常见雷区

  1. **介词后绝不接 to V**：*accuse him **to steal*** ✗ → *accuse him **of stealing*** ✓。
  2. **完成式动名词 (having + V-ed) 表先后**：*ashamed of **having acted** rashly* = \"为之前鲁莽行事感到羞愧\"——动作在 ashamed 之前。
  3. **deny / admit / suggest / mind / enjoy / finish + V-ing**（不接 to V）——这些动词后只接 V-ing。
</Callout>

## 4. 难点 过去完成时的叙事层

<Callout type="info" title="本课是过去完成时的密集案例">
  课文以\"小羔羊被偷\"为主线时间，**已经发生**的事件全用 had + V-ed：

  | 课文位置 | 原句 | 解读 |
  |---|---|---|
  | l.2 | her husband **had given** her | 给羔羊在故事发生之前 |
  | l.3-4 | The rope **had been cut** ... the lamb **had been stolen** | 发现之前已发生（被动） |
  | l.7 | Aleko **had suddenly acquired** a new lamb | \"得知\"之前阿列科已买羊 |
  | l.10 | he **had just bought** a lamb | 阿列科解释\"刚买\"——发生在被问之前 |
  | l.13 | wool **had been dyed** black, **had been washed** clean | 真相：染色与被冲洗都发生在\"出门看见\"之前 |

  ### ⚠️ 雷区
  1. **被动语态**：had + been + V-ed（had been cut / had been stolen / had been dyed / had been washed）。
  2. **had + V-ed 不是万能**：按顺序发生的事件，用一般过去时排列即可；只有需要\"强调先后\"或\"插入回顾\"时才用过去完成。
  3. **just / already / never / ever / by then** 是过去完成时的高频伴侣副词。
</Callout>

  - **Development**: Confrontation in front of the village elder — the cut rope produced as evidence — Aleko's wife appears with a half-empty bottle of black dye — Aleko goes pale — denies, stutters, finally admits.
  - **Conclusion**: Lamb returned to Mary — Aleko forced to pay damages — the village laughs for weeks about \"the lamb that washed itself white\".
</Callout>

<Callout type="tip" title="参考范文（自己写完再看）">
  As soon as the rain stopped, Dimitri walked home with the half-white lamb under his arm and called for his neighbours. By the time he reached the field, half the village was following, eager to see whose lamb had \"changed colour\". The cut rope still hung from the tree, and Mary identified her lamb at once.

  Aleko, dragged before the village elder, tried his old story: the lamb was his, bought that morning. But the dye had already given him away. To make matters worse, his wife arrived from the house carrying a half-empty bottle of black dye, asking innocently whether she should pour it away. The crowd erupted. Aleko's face went pale; he stammered, denied, then — under the elder's steady gaze — finally admitted that he had cut the rope the night before and dyed the lamb to disguise it.

  The lamb was returned to Mary, and Aleko was made to pay damages. For weeks the village laughed about \"the lamb that washed itself white\". *(190 words)*
</Callout>

<Callout type="tip" title="参考范文">
  Dear Eleni,

  You'll never believe what happened to us last week. One evening I went down to the field and found that my little lamb had been stolen — the rope cut clean through. Dimitri rushed off to find the thief and ended up accusing our neighbour, Aleko. We were so embarrassed when Aleko showed us a *black* lamb! But while Dimitri was sheltering from a sudden rain at Aleko's house, the dye washed off — and the lamb turned white before our eyes. Aleko was the thief after all.

  Mary *(118 words — feel free to tighten)*

## 13. 拓展练习 Going further

<Callout type="tip" title="自学者建议">
  - **影子跟读 (shadowing)**：用第 1 节的三个 SayIt 按钮跟读 3-5 遍。重点感受 *had been cut / had been stolen / had been dyed / had been washed* 这四组**过去完成被动**的连读——had-been 几乎吞成 \"d-b'n\"。
  - **复述 (retell)**：合上课文，把 Mary 的故事用 3 分钟讲一遍。**三段**：背景（玛丽家的白羊）→ 经过（失窃 → 指控 → 黑羊 → 道歉）→ 反转（雨冲掉染料 → 真相）。
  - **造句迁移**：用今天的 5 个高价值结构造句——
    1. **accuse sb. of + V-ing**（指控） — *They accused him of cheating in the exam.*
    2. **deny + V-ing**（否认） — *He denied breaking the window.*
    3. **be ashamed of + having V-ed**（为过去行为羞愧） — *I am ashamed of having spoken to her like that.*
    4. **apologize to sb. for + V-ing**（向某人道歉） — *He apologized to me for being late.*
    5. **had better + V 原形**（最好……） — *You had better tell the truth.*
  - **延伸思考**：这则故事是\"**叙事反转**\"的经典样本——表面证据（黑羊）让指控者羞愧，但被遮蔽的真相（染料）在结尾被自然力量（雨）揭穿。注意结尾的 *Its wool, **which had been dyed black**, **had been washed clean** by the rain!* —— 两个过去完成被动叠加，相当于电影里\"切回案发现场\"的镜头。这正是英文叙事最有力的工具之一。
</Callout>
