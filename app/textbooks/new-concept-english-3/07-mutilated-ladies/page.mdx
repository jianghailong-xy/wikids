## The Text 课文

Has it ever happened to you? Have you ever put your trousers in the washing machine and then remembered there was a large bank note in your back pocket? When you rescued your trousers, did you find the note was whiter than white? People who live in Britain needn't despair when they make mistakes like this (and a lot of people do)! Fortunately for them, the Bank of England has a team called **Mutilated Ladies** which deals with claims from people who fed their money to a machine or to their dog. Dogs, it seems, love to chew up money!

A recent case concerns Jane Butlin **whose** fiancé, John, runs a successful furniture business. John had a very good day and put his wallet **containing** £3,000 into the microwave oven for safekeeping. Then he and Jane went horse-riding. When they got home, Jane cooked their dinner in the microwave oven and without realizing it, cooked her fiancé's wallet as well. Imagine their dismay when they **found a beautifully-cooked wallet and notes turned to ash**! John went to see his bank manager who sent the remains of wallet and the money to the special department of the Bank of England in Newcastle: the Mutilated Ladies! They examined the remains and John got all his money back. 'So long as there's something to identify, we will give people their money back,' said a spokeswoman for the Bank. 'Last year, we paid £1.5m on 21,000 claims.'

> \*Damaged bank notes. The Queen's head appears on English bank notes, and 'lady' refers to this.

## 课文注释 Notes on the text

<Callout type="info" title="6 个值得停下来嚼一嚼的点">
  1. **whiter than white** (l.4) — 英国洗衣粉电视广告里的经典广告语，意为"比白纸还白"。这里是**反讽**：把钱洗成"比白还白"——上面的字迹图案全没了。文学修辞叫 **deliberate irony**（故意反语）。
  2. **fed ... to ... / chew up money** (l.7-8) — *feed sth. to sb./sth.* = 把……喂给…… 。课文里 *fed their money to a machine or to their dog* 是**拟人 + 黑色幽默**：洗衣机和狗被当作"嘴"。**chew up** = 嚼碎、嚼烂（up 表"完全"）。
  3. **whose** (l.9) — **关系代词所有格**，引导定语从句修饰前面的名词。课文 *Jane Butlin **whose** fiancé, John, runs a successful furniture business* = 简・巴特林**的**未婚夫约翰经营一家家具店。whose 既可指人也可指物（the house **whose** roof is broken），是中性的所有格关系词。
  4. **containing £3,000** (l.10) — **现在分词作后置定语**，相当于 *which contained £3,000*。注意 *contain* 是**状态动词**，**不用进行时**：~~was containing~~ → contained / containing。同类状态动词：know / believe / belong / own / consist of。
  5. **for safekeeping** (l.10) — 介词 *for* + 名词，**表目的**："为了妥善保管"。同型：*for sale / for rent / for hire / for fun*。注意这里不能用 *to + V* 的不定式来替换（*to keep it safe* 也对，但要换整个结构）。
  6. **found a beautifully-cooked wallet and notes turned to ash** (l.12) — **find + 宾语 + 宾语补足语**结构。两个宾语：*a wallet* 和 *notes*，两个补足语：*beautifully-cooked*（过去分词作定语）和 *turned to ash*（过去分词短语作宾补）。整句相当于 *found that the wallet had been beautifully cooked and the notes had been turned to ash*——但**用分词更紧凑**。
</Callout>

## 3. 关键句型 Past simple narration 一般过去时叙事

<Callout type="tip" title="一分钟回顾">
  本课的关键句型是 **What happened? 一般过去时叙事**——用过去时把一连串动作按时间顺序串起来。整个第二段就是一条**过去时链**：

  *John **had** a very good day → **put** his wallet ... into the microwave → **went** horse-riding → ... Jane **cooked** their dinner → **cooked** her fiancé's wallet → ... they **found** a beautifully-cooked wallet → John **went** to see his bank manager → who **sent** the remains → They **examined** the remains → John **got** all his money back.*

  ### 一般过去时的 3 大用法

  | 用法 | 例子 |
  |---|---|
  | **过去某一时刻 / 时段的动作** | John **had** a very good day. / He **put** his wallet into the oven. |
  | **过去的习惯**（常配 always / often / used to） | Dogs **love** to chew money.（一般现在时表习惯）vs. *He **used to live** in Britain.*（过去习惯）|
  | **一连串过去动作的叙事** | They **went** home, **cooked** dinner, and **found** the wallet. |

  ### ⚠️ 常见雷区

  - **不规则动词的过去式必须记牢**：*feed → fed / find → found / send → sent / get → got / run → ran / put → put（不变）/ go → went*
  - **状态动词不用进行时**：~~was containing~~ → contained / ~~was knowing~~ → knew
  - **一般过去 vs 现在完成**：明确的过去时间（yesterday, last year, in 1990）配**过去时**；时间不明确或与现在有关联用**完成时**
</Callout>

## 4. 难点：名词 → 形容词的派生 Noun → Adjective derivation

<Callout type="tip" title="6 组本课直接出现 / 课后练习的派生">
  英文常**给名词加后缀**得到形容词。规律性很强，记住几个常用后缀就能"举一反三"：

  | 名词 | 形容词 | 后缀 | 例子 |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | success | **successful** | -ful | He runs a **successful** business. |
  | fool | **foolish** | -ish | He often makes **foolish** decisions. |
  | energy | **energetic** | -etic | She's very **energetic**. |
  | hour | **hourly** | -ly | The clock strikes at **hourly** intervals. |
  | care | **careless / careful** | -less / -ful | You were completely **careless**. |
  | beauty | **beautiful** | -ful | She admired the **beauty** of the countryside. |

  ### 常见后缀总览

  | 后缀 | 含义 | 例子 |
  |---|---|---|
  | **-ful** | 充满……的 | useful, careful, hopeful, beautiful, painful |
  | **-less** | 没有……的（反义） | useless, careless, hopeless, painless, endless |
  | **-ous** | 多……的 | dangerous, famous, generous, mysterious |
  | **-y** | 多……的（口语） | sunny, rainy, dirty, noisy, lucky |
  | **-al** | ……方面的 | natural, national, musical, personal |
  | **-ic / -ical** | ……性质的 | scientific, historic / historical, magical |
  | **-ish** | 略带……的 / 像……的 | childish, foolish, reddish, selfish |
  | **-ly** | 有……特征的（多与时间或方式有关） | friendly, lovely, weekly, monthly, hourly |
  | **-able / -ible** | 可……的 | reliable, comfortable, visible, identifiable |
  | **-ive** | 有……倾向的 | active, creative, sensitive, attractive |

  ### ⚠️ -ly 既可作副词也可作形容词

  - **副词**：quickly / suddenly / carefully（多由形容词 + ly）
  - **形容词**：friendly / lovely / lively / weekly / monthly / hourly（多由名词 + ly）

  这就是为什么“He is a **friendly** man” 用 friendly（形容词），但 “He smiled **kindly**” 用 kindly（副词）。**只有上下文能告诉你**它是形容词还是副词。

## 10. 摘要写作 Summary writing (≤ 80 词)

<Callout type="info" title="任务">
  In not more than **80 words** describe **what happened to John and Jane**. **Do not include anything that is not in the last paragraph**（即只用 l.9 起的内容）。

  按下面 8 个提示点串成一段：

  1. What sort of business does John Butlin run?
  2. What did he do at the end of a very good day?
  3. What did he and his fiancée do then?
  4. What did Jane do after they got home?
  5. What were they dismayed to find?
  6. Who did John go and see?
  7. What did the bank manager do?
  8. What happened after that?
</Callout>

<Callout type="tip" title="参考范文（自己写完再看）">
  John Butlin, who runs a furniture business, ended a profitable day by hiding his wallet containing £3,000 inside his microwave oven. He and his fiancée Jane then went horse-riding. On returning home, Jane unwittingly used the same oven to cook dinner, and was dismayed to find the wallet beautifully cooked and the notes reduced to ash. John consulted his bank manager, who sent the remains to the Mutilated Ladies in Newcastle. They reimbursed him in full. *(78 words)*
</Callout>

## 11. 作文 Composition (约 150 词扩写)

<Callout type="info" title="任务">
  Describe what happened when Jane got the dinner out of the microwave. Expand the skeleton below into a paragraph of about **150 words**.
</Callout>

<Callout type="warning" title="提示骨架（每个省略号都得自己填）">
  - Jane opened the oven door and saw that her meal was **...**
  - She took the food out of the oven and **...**
  - They both rushed to the oven and **...**
  - At first, they didn't know what **...**
  - John wanted to **...** but Jane wouldn't **...**
  - 'It's best not to **...**,' Jane said.
  - 'You can see that all the money **...**'
  - Jane told John to **...**, so the next day **...**
</Callout>

<Callout type="tip" title="写作脚手架">
  - **时态主线**：一般过去时叙事；过去完成时表更早事件（*had cooked / had put*）；被动语态描述钱包的状态（*was beautifully cooked / was charred*）。
  - **打开微波炉的画面词库**：*lifted the lid, peered inside, recoiled, gasped, froze on the spot, dropped the plate, exchanged horrified glances*。
  - **钱包 / 钞票变化的形容词**：*charred, blackened, crispy, brittle, smouldering, reduced to ash, beyond recognition*。
  - **第一反应动词**：*panicked, scooped out, prodded gently, tried to peel apart, hesitated, swallowed hard*。
  - **冷静劝阻**：*Don't touch it — you'll only crumble it further. We need to keep every scrap intact if the bank is ever going to identify it.*
</Callout>

<Callout type="tip" title="参考范文（自己写完再看）">
  Jane opened the oven door and saw that her meal was sitting on top of a small, smoking lump. She took the food out of the oven and, with growing unease, lifted the lump onto a plate. They both rushed to the oven and stared at the blackened object in disbelief. At first, they didn't know what to make of it; then John recognised the burnt buckle of his wallet. John wanted to pull the wallet apart at once, but Jane wouldn't let him touch it. 'It's best not to disturb anything,' Jane said gently. 'You can see that all the money has been reduced to ash, but the corners and serial numbers may still be readable.' Jane told John to put the entire remains, plate and all, into a sealed box, so the next day he could take it straight to the bank manager. *(148 words)*
</Callout>

## 12. 书信写作 Letter writing

<Callout type="info" title="任务">
  Write a letter of **about 80 words** to a friend **thanking him for his hospitality**. Supply a suitable Introduction and Conclusion. Use the following ideas to write the **Purpose**:
  - The pleasure of seeing your friend again
  - His kindness during your stay
  - Things about the visit you will remember for a long time
  - Hope you can return this hospitality one day
</Callout>

<Callout type="tip" title="结构模板：感谢信三段式">
  | 段落 | 作用 | 常用句式 |
  |---|---|---|
  | **Introduction** | 切入主题——表达谢意 | *I am writing to thank you for ... / Just a quick line to say how much I enjoyed ... / I cannot tell you how grateful I am for ...* |
  | **Purpose** | 主体——具体说明被感动的细节 + 难忘的瞬间 | *It was wonderful to see you again after so long. / Your kindness throughout my stay made me feel completely at home. / I shall never forget the evening when we ...* |
  | **Conclusion** | 期待回报 + 结束语 | *I do hope you will let me return the favour next time you are in ... / Once again, my warmest thanks. / Looking forward to seeing you soon.* |
</Callout>

<Callout type="tip" title="参考范文（自己写完再看）">
  **Dear Mark,**

  Just a quick line to say how much I enjoyed staying with you last weekend. After all the years apart, it was lovely to spend time together again.

  Your kindness throughout my visit really touched me — you must have planned every meal and every outing in advance. I shall remember the long walk by the river, and our evening playing cards by the fire, for a very long time indeed.

  Do let me know when you are next in London; I would love to return the favour and put you up for a few days.

  **Yours ever,**
  **Tom** *(85 words)*
</Callout>

<Callout type="warning" title="6 个最常用的感谢信结尾句">
  | # | 结尾句 | 用在什么时候 |
  |---|---|---|
  | 1 | *Once again, my warmest thanks for everything.* | 通用收尾 |
  | 2 | *I do hope I can return the favour one day.* | 表达"以后回请" |
  | 3 | *Please give my love / regards to Sarah and the children.* | 顺便问候家人 |
  | 4 | *Do let me know when you are next in town.* | 期待下次见面 |
  | 5 | *I look forward to seeing you both again soon.* | 等再聚 |
  | 6 | *With many thanks and best wishes.* | 简洁通用 |
</Callout>

## 13. 拓展练习 Going further

<Callout type="tip" title="自学者建议">
  - **影子跟读 (shadowing)**：用第 1 节的三个 SayIt 按钮跟读 3-5 遍。重点感受 *whiter than white* 的**对比节奏**——两个 white 都重读但语调走向不同（第一个升、第二个降）。
  - **复述 (retell)**：合上课文，把 Jane 和 John 的故事用 90 秒讲一遍。**叙事骨架**：背景（残钞鉴别组在干嘛）→ 案例（John 把钱藏微波炉）→ 灾难（Jane 煮饭把钱包一起煮了）→ 转机（送银行经理 → 残钞鉴别组）→ 结局（拿回钱）。
  - **造句迁移**：用今天的 5 个高价值结构造你自己的句子——
    1. *N + containing + 名词短语*（装有……的）—— *a box containing old photos*
    2. *Have you ever V-ed ...?*（你有没有过……）
    3. *Find + 宾语 + V-ed*（发现……处于某状态）—— *I found the door locked.*
    4. *for + V-ing / 名词 表目的*（为了……）—— *for safekeeping / for sale / for hire*
    5. *So long as / Provided ..., S + will ...*（只要……就……）
  - **延伸思考**：本课在风格上和 Lesson 5 形成鲜明对比——Lesson 5 是"严肃媒体的讽刺"（编辑死磕琐碎精确）；Lesson 7 是"温暖的报道笔法"，先用第二人称 *Have you ever ...?* 拉近距离，再讲一个**具体案例**，最后用**机构发言人**的引语收尾。**这是英语新闻“feature story”的标准结构**：hook（钩住） → case study（案例） → quote from authority（权威引语）。下次看 BBC / The Guardian 的非新闻类报道时，对比一下结构。
</Callout>
