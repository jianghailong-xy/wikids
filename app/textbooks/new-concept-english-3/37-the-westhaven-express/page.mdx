## The Text 课文

We have learnt to expect that trains will be **punctual**. After years of **conditioning**, most of us have developed an **unshakable** faith in railway timetables. Ships may be delayed by storms; flights may be **cancelled** because of bad weather; but trains must be on time. Only an **exceptionally** heavy snowfall might temporarily **dislocate** railway services. It is **all too easy** to blame the railway authorities when something goes wrong. The truth is that when mistakes occur, they are more likely to be *ours* than *theirs*.

After **consulting** my railway timetable, I noted with satisfaction that there was an express train to Westhaven. It went direct from my local station and the journey lasted a mere hour and seventeen minutes. When I boarded the train, I **could not help noticing** that a great many local people also got on. At the time, this did not **strike me as odd**. I reflected that there must be a great many people besides myself who wished to **take advantage of** this excellent service. **Neither was I surprised** when the train stopped at Widley, a tiny station a few miles along the line. Even a mighty express train can be held up by signals. But when the train **dawdled** at station after station, I began to wonder. It suddenly **dawned on me** that this express was not roaring down the line at ninety miles an hour, but barely **chugging** along at thirty. One hour and seventeen minutes passed and we had not even covered half the distance. I asked a passenger if this was the Westhaven Express, but he had not even heard of it. He determined to **lodge** a complaint as soon as we arrived. Two hours later, I was talking angrily to the station master at Westhaven. When he denied the train's existence, I asked if I might borrow his copy of the timetable. There was **a note of triumph** in my voice when I told him that the train was there **in black and white**. Glancing at it briefly, he told me I should look again. A tiny **asterisk** conducted me to a footnote at the bottom of the page. It read: 'This service has been suspended.'

## 课文注释 Notes on the text

<Callout type="info" title="8 个值得停下来嚼一嚼的点">

**1. they are more likely to be *ours* than *theirs*** — 这些错误往往是我们而非铁路当局造成的；ours、theirs 用斜体（重读）起强调作用

**2. could not help noticing** — 我不禁注意到……

> can't help doing = cannot resist doing（禁不住做某事）

> I *couldn't help laughing*.（我忍不住笑了出来。）

**3. strike sb as odd** — 使某人感到奇怪

> It *struck me as odd* that no one else seemed worried.（奇怪的是没有其他人似乎担心。）

**4. take advantage of** — 利用

> We should *take advantage of* this excellent service.（我们应该好好利用这项优质服务。）

**5. Neither was I surprised** — 以 neither 开头，需倒装语序

> *Neither* was the station master surprised.（站长也不感到惊讶。）

cf. Neither did he know / Nor did she come — 均为倒装

**6. It suddenly dawned on me** — 我突然明白了……

> *It dawned on* him that something was wrong.（他突然意识到出了什么问题。）

**7. a note of triumph** — 胜利者的口吻/调子

> She announced the result with *a note of triumph*.（她带着一丝得意宣布了结果。）

**8. in black and white** — 白纸黑字，书面写明的

> It was there *in black and white* — the service had been suspended.（白纸黑字写得清清楚楚——这趟车已停运。）

</Callout>

## 3. 关键句型：can't help doing & Neither/Nor倒装 Grammar focus

<Callout type="tip" title="一分钟回顾">

**① can't help doing** — 忍不住做……，禁不住……

> I **couldn't help noticing** that many local people also got on.（我不禁注意到许多当地人也上了车。）

⚠️ 后接**动名词**，不接不定式：
- `I couldn't help to notice` ✗
- `I couldn't help noticing` ✓

---

**② Neither/Nor 开头的倒装句**

当 neither/nor 置于句首，句子须用**助动词 + 主语**的倒装语序：

> **Neither was I surprised** when the train stopped at Widley.
>（火车在威德里停车时，我也不感到惊讶。）

| 正常语序 | 倒装语序 |
|----------|----------|
| I was not surprised either. | **Neither was I** surprised. |
| She didn't know either. | **Neither did she** know. |
| He hadn't arrived either. | **Nor had he** arrived. |

⚠️ 其他引发倒装的词：Never, Hardly, Little, Seldom, Rarely, Only then

> **Never** had I seen such a sight.（我从未见过这样的景象。）

</Callout>

## 4. 难点：borrow / lend & refuse / deny Special difficulties

<Callout type="info" title="本课两组易混词">

**① borrow vs lend**

| 词 | 方向 | 例句 |
|----|------|------|
| borrow | 从别人那里借来（我方借入） | I asked if I might **borrow** his copy of the timetable. |
| lend | 借给别人（我方借出） | He **lent** me his copy of the timetable. |

⚠️ `Can you lend me a pen?` = `Can I borrow a pen from you?`

---

**② refuse vs deny**

| 词 | 含义 | 例句 |
|----|------|------|
| refuse | 拒绝（做某事） | He **refused** to show me the timetable.（拒绝做） |
| deny | 否认（某事存在/发生） | He **denied** the train's existence.（否认事实） |

⚠️ deny 后接名词或动名词，refuse 后接不定式：
- He **denied** having seen it. ✓
- He **refused** to admit it. ✓

</Callout>

## 11. 作文 Composition

<Callout type="info" title="任务">
  用不超过 **250 个单词**，描述一次乘火车的经历：你本想搭慢车，却误上了快车。分四段，自拟标题。

  **素材提示：** Got on train — expected it to stop — prepared to alight — it went straight on — very fast — you asked passengers — learnt it was an express — ticket collector came along — you explained situation — had to pay full fare — journey lasted two hours — arrived miles away from destination — no fast train back — had to board a slow one!
</Callout>

<Callout type="warning" title="提示骨架">
- 第一段：登车经过，期待在某站下车
- 第二段：火车没有停，意识到自己上了快车
- 第三段：与乘务员交涉，不得不补票
- 第四段：到达远离目的地的终点，如何返程
</Callout>

<Callout type="tip" title="参考范文（自己写完再看）">
  *The Wrong Train*

  Last Tuesday, I needed to visit a friend in the small town of Barton, about twenty minutes from my local station. I hopped on the first train I saw, settled into a seat, and waited for the familiar announcement.

  After twenty minutes, the train was still moving at full speed. I couldn't help noticing that the scenery was entirely unfamiliar. When I asked the passenger beside me whether the train stopped at Barton, she laughed. 'This is the London Express,' she said. 'It doesn't stop for another hour.'

  A ticket collector appeared shortly afterwards. I explained my mistake, but he showed little sympathy. I had to pay the full express fare, which was three times what I had expected. The journey lasted well over two hours.

  When I finally arrived in London, there was no fast train back to Barton. I had no choice but to board a slow local service, which stopped at every tiny station along the way. By the time I reached my friend's house, it was nearly dark — and she had already had dinner.
</Callout>

## 12. 书信写作 Letter writing

<Callout type="info" title="任务">
  Write a letter of about **100 words** in three paragraphs congratulating a young couple you know on the birth of their first child — a baby girl. Inquire about the health of the mother and child and say that you hope to be able to visit them soon.
</Callout>

<Callout type="tip" title="写作脚手架">
**结构：**
1. 表达热烈祝贺，为他们感到高兴

**有用表达：**
- I was absolutely delighted to hear that...
- What wonderful news!
- How is [mother's name] feeling after the birth?

- I would love to come and see the new arrival as soon as possible.

## 13. 拓展练习 Going further

<Callout type="tip" title="自学者建议">

**① 影子跟读** — 选课文第 1 段，重点练习强调语气：

> It is **all too easy** to blame the railway authorities when something goes wrong. The truth is that when mistakes occur, they are more likely to be **ours** than **theirs**.

注意斜体词 *ours* / *theirs* 须重读。

**② 倒装造句练习** — 用以下词语各造一个倒装句：

1. `Neither` — （我也不……）
2. `Never` — （我从未……）
3. `Hardly` — （我几乎没……就……）
4. `Only then` — （直到那时才……）

**③ 造句迁移** — 用本课 5 个核心结构各造一个句子：

1. `can't help doing`
2. `it dawned on sb that...`
3. `strike sb as odd`
4. `in black and white`
5. `take advantage of`

**④ 延伸思考** — 课文的最后一句话是神来之笔：作者带着满满的得意，却被一个小星号彻底打脸。你有没有类似“自以为赢了，结果输得更惨”的经历？用英文写 3–5 句。

**⑤ 词汇辨析** — 查找以下词对的区别：

- borrow / lend（借入 vs 借出）
- refuse / deny（拒绝 vs 否认）
- passed / past（动词 vs 介词/形容词）
