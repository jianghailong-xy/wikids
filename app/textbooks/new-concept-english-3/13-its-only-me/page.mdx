## The Text 课文

After her husband had gone to work and the children to school, Mrs. Richards sent for the chimney sweep. As soon as he arrived, Mrs. Richards went upstairs to the bedroom. She was too excited to do any housework, for in the evening **she would be going** to a fancy-dress party with her husband. She intended to dress up as a ghost and as she had made her costume the night before, she was impatient to **try it on**. Though the costume **consisted only of** a sheet, it was very effective. After putting it on, Mrs. Richards went downstairs. She wanted to find out **how comfortable it would be to wear**.

Just as Mrs. Richards was entering the dining room, there was a knock on the front door. She knew it **must be** the baker. She had told him to come straight in if ever she failed to open the door and to leave the bread on the kitchen table. **Not wanting to frighten** the poor man, Mrs. Richards quickly hid in the small storeroom under the stairs. She heard the front door open and heavy footsteps in the hall. Suddenly the door of the storeroom was opened and a man entered. Mrs. Richards realized that it **must be** the man from the Electricity Board who had come to read the metre. She tried to explain the situation, saying 'It's only me', but it was too late. The man let out a cry and jumped back several paces. When Mrs. Richards walked towards him, he fled, slamming the door behind him.

## 课文注释 Notes on the text

<Callout type="info" title="6 个值得停下来嚼一嚼的点">
  1. **she would be going** (l.3) — \"她**将要去**\"。**过去将来进行时 would be V-ing** 用于**已计划好、将持续进行**的未来事件——比 *would go* 更强调"已经安排好"。同型：*He said he **would be flying** to Paris next Monday.* 课文里因为已经做好了戏服，所以这场舞会几乎是板上钉钉，用 would be going 最合适。
  2. **fancy-dress party / dress up as ...** (ll.3-4) — **fancy-dress** = "化装的"，作复合形容词（中间有连字符）；**dress up as + 名词** = "装扮成……"。同型：*The children **dressed up as** pirates for Halloween.* 注意 dress up 不带 as 是"盛装打扮"（穿礼服），加 as 才是"扮成"。
  3. **try it on** (l.5) — \"试穿"——**try on** 是分离式动词短语，宾语是代词时**必须放中间**：try **it** on (✓) / *try on it* (✗)。同型：put **it** on / take **it** off / pick **it** up。
  4. **just as ... if ever ...** (ll.9-11) — *just as* = "正当……的时候"（瞬间衔接，比 when 更紧凑）；*if ever she failed to open the door* = "如果她哪一次没来开门"——**if ever** 强调\"任何一次只要发生\"，比单 if 更口语化。
  5. **must be / it must be ...** (ll.10, 13) — **本课题眼！must 表"推断"** = "（根据眼前线索）一定是……"。课文 l.10 *She knew it **must be** the baker*（根据敲门习惯推断）；l.13 *it **must be** the man from the Electricity Board*（突然推门 + 直奔储藏室——一定是查电表的）。**注意**：表"推断"的 must 用于**现在/将来**用 must + V 原形；推断"过去"则用 **must have V-ed**。
  6. **Not wanting to frighten the poor man, ...** (l.11) — **现在分词短语作目的状语**——主语 Mrs. Richards 是"不想吓唬"的发出者。**否定形式 Not + V-ing 放短语开头**（不是 *Don't wanting*）。同型：*Not knowing what to say, he remained silent.*
</Callout>

## 3. 关键句型 must 的多重用法 (IKS 89)

<Callout type="tip" title="一分钟回顾">
  本课题眼是 **must**。课文 l.10 *She knew it **must be** the baker* 与 l.13 *it **must be** the man from the Electricity Board*——两次都是 **must 表"推断"**（根据眼前线索做出的肯定推测）。但 must 远不止"必须"和"一定"两层含义。

  ### must 的 4 层含义

  | 含义 | 形式 | 例子 |
  |---|---|---|
  | **义务 / 必须** | must + V 原形 | You **must** wear a seatbelt. (法律规定) |
  | **推断 (现在/将来)** | must + V 原形 / must be ... | It **must be** the baker. (课文 l.10) |
  | **推断 (过去)** | **must have + V-ed** | He **must have left** already — the lights are off. |
  | **禁止** | **mustn't** + V 原形 | You **mustn't** smoke here. (= 严禁) |

  ### ⚠️ 4 个高频陷阱

  1. **mustn't ≠ needn't**：
     - *You **mustn't** go.* = **禁止**去（= must not，强制）
     - *You **needn't** go.* = **不必**去（= don't have to，自由选择）
  2. **must 没有过去时**：要表达"过去必须做了"，**用 had to**——*I **had to** stay late yesterday.*（不能说 *I musted*）
  3. **must have V-ed vs ought to have V-ed**：
     - *He **must have** missed the train.* = "他**一定**误了火车"（肯定推断）
     - *He **ought to have** caught the train.* = "他**本应该**赶上火车"（实际上没赶上——遗憾 / 责备）
  4. **must 表推断的否定**：不能用 mustn't（mustn't 是"禁止"！），要用 **can't / couldn't**：
     - *It **must be** John.* → *It **can't be** John.*（不是\"It mustn't be John\"）
</Callout>

## 4. 难点 形容词 + 动词不定式 (It would be comfortable to wear)

<Callout type="info" title="规则速览">
  课文 l.8 *how comfortable it would be **to wear*** 展示了一个高频结构：**形容词 + 动词不定式**——用 *to V* 直接说明 / 解释那个形容词的内容。最常用的几个搭配：

  | 形容词 | 习惯搭配的不定式 | 例句 |
  |---|---|---|
  | **delighted** | to learn / to hear / to see | He was **delighted to learn** that his offer had been accepted. |
  | **glad** | to hear / to know / to see | I was **glad to hear** that he had arrived. |
  | **pleased** | to hear / to meet / to inform | I was **pleased to hear** that you now feel better. |
  | **sorry** | to learn / to hear / to tell | I was **sorry to learn** that he had had an accident. |
  | **anxious** | to leave / to know / to start | He was **anxious to leave** early. |
  | **sad** | to hear / to learn / to see | She was **sad to hear** the news. |
  | **proud** | to be / to say / to announce | I am **proud to be** your teacher. |
  | **shocked** | to find / to discover / to learn | She was **shocked to find** the door open. |

  ### ⚠️ 关键改写规律

  **"形容词 + when / if + 从句"** → 常常可以浓缩成 **"形容词 + to V"**：
  - I was sorry **when I learnt** that he had had an accident. → I was sorry **to learn** that he had had an accident.
  - I was glad **when I heard** that she had come. → I was glad **to hear** that she had come.
  - You will be sad **when you hear** what I have to tell you. → You will be sad **to hear** what I have to tell you.

  这种改写让句子更**简洁、书面**——尤其在写作中很有用。
</Callout>

## 13. 拓展练习 Going further

<Callout type="tip" title="自学者建议">
  - **影子跟读 (shadowing)**：用第 1 节的三个 SayIt 按钮跟读 3-5 遍。重点感受 *must be* 两次出现时的**重读模式**——must 不重读、be 不重读，重音落在后面的名词 (the **BAK**er / the **MAN** from the Electricity Board)。这正是\"推断\"语气的语调特征。
  - **复述 (retell)**：合上课文，把"It's only me"的故事用 2 分钟讲一遍。**叙事三段**：背景（要参加化装舞会 → 试戏服）→ 经过（敲门 → 躲储藏室 → 门被推开 → \"是我，别怕!\"）→ 结局（电力局抄表员吓得砰门逃走）。
  - **造句迁移**：用今天的 5 个高价值结构造你自己的句子——
    1. *It must be ...*（必定是……，根据眼前线索的推断）—— *That noise — it must be the wind.*
    2. *adj + to V*（形容词 + 不定式）—— *I'm pleased to meet you. / He's anxious to leave.*
    3. *dress up as + n.*（装扮成）—— *The children dressed up as pirates.*
    4. *(too late) to V / no time to V*（来不及做……）—— *She had no time to explain.*
    5. *On + V-ing*（一……就……）—— *On seeing the ghost, he let out a cry.*
  - **延伸思考**：本课是\"**误会喜剧**的工业级范本\"——两个人在错误的时间、错误的地点、按各自的合理逻辑行动，碰撞出戏剧。理查兹夫人**根据敲门习惯**推断是面包师 (must be the baker)；电力局的人**根据制度**进门读电表；两条线索本来不相交，可\"楼梯下的储藏室\"恰好既是电表的位置，又是夫人选择的藏身处——于是\"鬼\"与\"陌生人\"在那扇门后正面相遇。下次你写英文叙事时，问自己一句：\"两个角色的**默认假设**是什么？这些假设在哪一秒突然破产？\"——找到那一秒，你的故事就有了**笑点**。
</Callout>
