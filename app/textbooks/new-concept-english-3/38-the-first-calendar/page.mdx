  几千块刻着点、线、奇怪符号的石头、骨头和长毛象的象牙，在博物馆的抽屉里躺了上百年，谁也读不懂。人们一直以为：日历是人类学会种地之后才发明的——因为种地才需要"看天吃饭"。可如果告诉你，早在农业出现的两万年前，那些追着野兽四处迁徙的猎人就已经在记录"日子"了呢？本课就是一桩破译远古密码的"侦探故事"：科学家如何把世界各地的刻痕拼在一起，最终认出——这竟是**最早的一部日历**。
  **What is the importance of the dots, lines, and symbols engraved on stone, bones and ivory?**（刻在石头、骨头和象牙上的那些点、线和符号到底有什么意义？）带着这个问题读课文，重点留意全篇是怎样用 **被动语态 + 现在完成时** 来讲述"一项至今仍在推进的研究"，又怎样用 **as it were / what is more / be faced with** 这类插入语和固定短语把说明文写得既严谨又有温度。
</Callout>

## The Text 课文

Future historians will be in **a unique position** when they come to record the history of our own times. They will hardly know which facts **to select from** the great mass of evidence that steadily accumulates. **What is more**, they will not have to **rely solely on** the written word. Films, videos, CDs and CD-ROMs are just some of the bewildering amount of information they will have. They will be able, **as it were**, to see and hear us in action. But the historian attempting to reconstruct the distant past is always **faced with** a difficult task. He has to **deduce what he can from** the few scanty clues available. Even seemingly insignificant remains can **shed** interesting **light on** the history of early man.

Up to now, historians have assumed that calendars **came into being** with **the advent of** agriculture, for then man was faced with a real need to understand something about the seasons. Recent scientific evidence seems to indicate that this assumption is incorrect.

Historians have long been puzzled by dots, lines and symbols which have been engraved on walls, bones, and the ivory tusks of mammoths. The nomads who made these markings lived by hunting and fishing during the last Ice Age which began about 35,000 B.C. and ended about 10,000 B.C. **By correlating** markings made in various parts of the world, historians have been able to read this difficult code. They have found that it **is connected with** the passage of days and **the phases of the moon**. It is, in fact, **a primitive type of calendar**. It has long been known that the hunting scenes depicted on walls were not simply a form of artistic expression. They had a definite meaning, for they were **as near as early man could get to** writing. It is possible that there is **a definite relation between** these paintings and the markings that sometimes accompany them. It seems that man was making a real effort to understand the seasons 20,000 years earlier than has been supposed.

## 课文注释 Notes on the text

<Callout type="info" title="7 个值得停下来嚼一嚼的点">
  1. **what is more** (l.4) — 作"而且"、"再者"解，是**插入语**，用来追加更重要的一点。课文 *What is more, they will not have to rely solely on the written word*。同型：*He is clever and, what is more, he works hard.*

  2. **CD-ROM** (l.5) — 是 *compact disc read-only memory*（只读光盘）的缩写。课文里 *Films, videos, CDs and CD-ROMs* 并列，泛指未来历史学家能掌握的各种音像资料。

  3. **as it were** (l.7) — 作"可以说"、"在某种意义上"解，也是**插入语**，常用来给一个略带比喻的说法打个"招呼"。课文 *They will be able, as it were, to see and hear us in action*——"可以说"能亲眼看见、亲耳听见我们。注意它是**固定说法**，用的是虚拟语气的 *were*，不随主语变化。

  4. **be faced with** (ll.7-8) — 作"面临"、"面对"解（被动形式）。课文 *the historian ... is always faced with a difficult task* / *man was faced with a real need ...*。同型：*We are faced with a serious problem.*

  5. **shed light on ...** (l.9) — 作"使……清楚地显示出来"、"解释、阐明"解。课文 *Even seemingly insignificant remains can shed ... light on the history of early man*——连看似微不足道的遗物也能让早期人类史"亮"起来。也常说 *throw / cast light on*。

  6. **come into being** (l.10) — 作"产生"、"形成"解（= come into existence）。课文 *calendars came into being with the advent of agriculture*。注意 being 在此是名词，**不能**改成 *come into existing*。

  7. **Ice Age** (l.15) — 冰川时期。课文 *the last Ice Age which began about 35,000 B.C. and ended about 10,000 B.C.*——那些游牧猎人正是生活在末次冰期。
</Callout>

## 3. 关键句型 被动 · 分词 · 插入语 Passive, participles & parentheticals

<Callout type="tip" title="一分钟回顾">
  本课是一篇"层层推证"的说明文，语法上靠三组结构撑起来：**被动语态 + 现在完成时**（讲述一项至今仍在推进的研究）、**分词/名词性从句**（让长句紧凑）、以及 **插入语**（给说明文加上口吻）。

  | 结构 | 课文例句 | 用途 |
  |---|---|---|
  | **被动 + 现在完成** | *symbols which **have been engraved** on walls* | "（至今）已被刻上"——强调结果留存 |
  | **By + doing（方式状语）** | ***By correlating** markings ..., historians have been able to read ...* | "通过做……"，常置句首 |
  | **what 引导名词性从句** | *He has to deduce **what he can** from the clues* | = the things that he can；作宾语 |
  | **as if + 过去式/were** | *It **were as if** they could see us*（由 *as it were* 拓展） | 表"仿佛、可以说"的虚拟 |
  | **there be + 名词** | *there is **a definite relation** between ...* | 表"存在"，主语在 be 之后 |
  | **插入语** | *..., **as it were**, ... / **What is more**, ...* | 补充口吻，前后用逗号隔开 |

  ### ⚠️ 4 个本课最容易踩的雷区

  1. **被动语态的搭配**：engrave 要说 *be engraved **on** walls*；connect 要说 *be connected **with** ...*。介词错了，被动就垮了。
  2. **as it were** 是**固定插入语**（"可以说"），永远用 were，**不能**写成 *as it was*，也不要和真正的 *as if it were*（仿佛）混为一谈。
  3. **come into being** 里 being 是名词，整短语 = come into existence；**不能**说 *come into existing / come to existence*。
  4. **By correlating ...** 句首是分词作方式状语，其**逻辑主语必须是主句主语**（historians）——是"历史学家在对比"，不是"刻痕在对比"，所以后面接 *historians have been able to read*。
</Callout>

## 4. 难点：between vs among & 动词的介词搭配 Special difficulties

<Callout type="info" title="教材难点：between 和 among 的区别 (ISD 76b) + 动词 + with/for (IKS 46)">
  教材这一课的难点练习有两处值得专门拎出来：

  **① between 还是 among？** 课文 l.20：*a definite relation **between** these paintings and the markings*。

  | 用 between | 用 among |
  |---|---|
  | 指**两者之间**，或彼此**对应、区别**的多方之间 | 指**三者或三者以上**笼统的"在……之中" |
  | *the difference **between** A and B* | *He sat **among** the crowd.* |
  | *a relation **between** the paintings and the markings* | *popular **among** young people* |

  小诀窍：能一一点名、讲清"谁和谁"的关系，用 **between**（哪怕超过两方）；只是"夹在一群当中"，用 **among**。

  **② 动词后跟 with 还是 for？** 课文 l.17：*it is connected **with** ...*。常见搭配速记——

  - 跟 **with**：*agree with（赞同某人）、correspond with（通信）、cope with（应付）、quarrel with（与……争吵）、be satisfied with（对……满意）、be faced with（面临）*
  - 跟 **for**：*account for（解释、占）、apologize for（为……道歉）、blame ... for（因……责备）、wait for（等待）、search for（搜寻）*
  - **两可但意义不同**：*agree with sb / agree to a plan*；*mistake A for B（把 A 误当成 B）*。
</Callout>

## 10. 摘要写作 Summary writing (≤ 80 词)

<Callout type="info" title="任务">
  *In not more than 80 words* describe **what historians have learnt from the strange markings made by early man**. Use your own words as far as possible. **Do not include anything that is not in the last paragraph**（只用最后一段的信息）。

  按下面的提示点串成一段：

  1. 这些点、线、符号刻在哪里、是谁刻的？（墙壁、骨头、象牙；冰期的游牧猎人）
  2. 历史学家用什么办法读懂了它们？（对比世界各地的刻痕）
  3. 这些刻痕原来是什么？（与昼夜、月相有关——一种最原始的日历）
  4. 墙上的狩猎图景又说明了什么？（不只是艺术，有确定含义，接近文字，可能与刻痕相关）
  5. 由此得出的惊人结论是什么？（人类了解四季比想象早两万年）
</Callout>

<Callout type="tip" title="参考范文（自己写完再看）">
  By comparing dots, lines and symbols engraved on walls, bones and ivory in many regions, historians have decoded markings left by Ice Age nomads. They record the passage of days and the phases of the moon — a primitive calendar. The hunting scenes painted nearby were not mere art but had a definite meaning, almost a form of writing, and may be linked to the markings. So man tried to understand the seasons 20,000 years earlier than was once supposed. *(75 words)*
</Callout>

## 11. 作文 Composition (约 250 词)

<Callout type="info" title="任务">
  In not more than **250 words** describe some of the things future historians will be able to learn about us. Use the ideas given below. Do not write more than four paragraphs.

  - **Title**: Studying the past.
  - **Introduction**: The future historian's sources — films, video, CDs, CD-ROMs.
  - **Development**: Social and political history — how we dressed, what we ate, what houses we lived in, what our cities were like, the forms of entertainment we enjoyed, the news day by day, the way we fought our wars, great moments in history, leading figures as well as ordinary people.
  - **Conclusion**: Study of history will provide interest and excitement — the past will be brought to life.
</Callout>

<Callout type="warning" title="提示骨架（四段，对应四个提示）">
  - **第 1 段（引入·来源）**：未来的历史学家无须只靠文字——films, videos, CDs, CD-ROMs 让他们能"看见、听见"我们。
  - **第 2 段（社会生活）**：how we dressed / what we ate / the houses and cities we lived in / the entertainment we enjoyed。
  - **第 3 段（政治与大事）**：the news day by day / how we fought our wars / great moments / leading figures as well as ordinary people。
  - **第 4 段（结论·升华）**：研究历史将充满趣味与激动——the past will be brought to life。
</Callout>

<Callout type="tip" title="写作脚手架">
  - **时态主线**：写未来历史学家用 **will be able to + 动词原形**（will be able to watch / hear / see / trace）；提到我们这个时代用一般现在时。
  - **"了解/重现"动词库**：*reconstruct, trace, picture, witness, follow, bring ... to life*。
  - **"资料来源"词库**：*moving images, recordings, archives, a bewildering mass of information, day-to-day footage*。
  - **结构亮点**：用 *No longer will they have to rely solely on the written word ...* 这样的**部分倒装**开篇，呼应课文 *rely solely on the written word*，立刻提气。
</Callout>

<Callout type="tip" title="参考范文（自己写完再看）">
  **Studying the past**

  Future historians will be in a unique position when they come to study us. No longer will they have to rely solely on the written word, for films, videos, CDs and CD-ROMs will preserve a bewildering mass of information. They will be able, as it were, to see and hear us in action, and so to bring our age back to life.

  From these sources they will learn how we lived from day to day: how we dressed, what we ate, the houses we built and the crowded cities we filled. They will watch the games and films that entertained us, and follow, almost minute by minute, the news that shaped our moods.

  They will also trace our political history — the way we fought our wars, the great moments that changed the world, and the leaders who made the headlines. Yet the cameras will capture ordinary people too: faces in a street, children at school, a family at dinner. The famous and the forgotten will stand side by side.

  To study such a past will be a pleasure rather than a labour. Where earlier historians had to deduce what they could from a few scanty clues, our descendants will simply watch and listen. For them the twentieth and twenty-first centuries will not be a silent puzzle but a living world — and that is why studying the past will provide both interest and excitement. *(约 240 词)*
</Callout>

## 12. 书信写作 Letter writing (约 100 词)

<Callout type="info" title="任务">
  For several years you have been writing to a **penfriend** whom you have never met in person. Write him a letter of about **100 words in three paragraphs** telling him that you will be **visiting his country soon** and expressing pleasure that you will be able to **meet each other for the first time**.
</Callout>

<Callout type="tip" title="写作脚手架">
  - **称呼**：*Dear + 名*（笔友间用名字，亲切）。
  - **第 1 段（消息）**：宣布好消息 —— *I have wonderful news: I am finally coming to your country next month!*
  - **第 2 段（细节 + 期待）**：行程 + 想见面 —— *I'll be in ... for two weeks. After all these years of letters, I can hardly believe we will meet at last.*
  - **第 3 段（约定 + 收尾）**：约时间地点 + 盼回信 —— *Do let me know when you are free; I'll bring the photos I promised. I can't wait to meet you face to face.*
  - **时态要点**：将来计划用 **be going to / will be + -ing**（I am coming / I'll be staying）；多年通信用现在完成进行 *we have been writing to each other for years*。
</Callout>

<Callout type="tip" title="参考范文（自己写完再看）">
  Dear Mario,

  I have some wonderful news. After all these years of writing to each other, I am finally coming to Italy! My flight lands in Rome on the 10th of next month, and I shall be staying for two whole weeks.

  I can hardly believe it. We have been penfriends for almost six years, yet we have never once met in person. The thought that we will at last be able to talk face to face, instead of on paper, makes me happier than I can say.

  Do let me know which day suits you best. I shall bring all the photos I promised. See you very soon!

  Warm wishes,

  Daniel *(约 110 词)*
</Callout>

## 13. 拓展练习 Going further

<Callout type="tip" title="自学者建议">
  - **影子跟读 (shadowing)**：用第 1 节的三个 SayIt 按钮各跟读 3-5 遍。注意插入语 *as it were* 前后要略作停顿、降调，把"可以说"的口吻读出来。
  - **复述 (retell)**：合上课文，用 2 分钟讲清本文的**推证链**：未来史家资料丰富（音像）→ 但远古史家只能靠零碎线索推断 → 旧说"日历随农业出现" → 新证据：冰期猎人的刻痕已是日历（与昼夜、月相对应，各地一致）→ 墙上狩猎图也有含义、接近文字 → 结论：人类懂四季比想象早两万年。
  - **造句迁移**：用今天的 5 个高价值结构造你自己的句子——
    1. *be faced with ...*（面临）—— *Every new manager is faced with the same problem.*
    2. *shed light on ...*（阐明）—— *The diary sheds new light on her childhood.*
    3. *come into being*（产生）—— *The club came into being only last year.*
    4. *By + doing ...*（通过做……）—— *By reading aloud every day, she improved her accent.*
    5. *be connected with ...*（与……有关）—— *His illness is closely connected with stress.*
  - **延伸思考**：本课是一篇典型的"**证据推翻成见**"式说明文——先摆出公认的旧说（calendars came with agriculture），再用新证据（the markings are a calendar）推翻它，最后给出令人一震的结论（早两万年）。下次你写英文议论或说明文时，可以借用这条骨架：**旧说 → 反证 → 新结论**。语言上多用 **被动 + 现在完成时**（have been engraved / have been able to read）来表达"一项延续至今、不断有新发现的研究"，会显得格外严谨。
</Callout>
