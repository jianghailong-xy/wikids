  一个"理想仆人"应该是什么样？勤劳、高效、忠诚、从不犯错？本课给你讲一个让人哭笑不得的故事：姨妈哈丽特花了半辈子寻找"完美佣人"，终于碰到一个样样都好的贝西——干活卖力、厨艺精湛，整整三年没出任何差错。可就在姨妈最得意的时候，贝西的"小小弱点"暴露了，而且是用一种极其戏剧化的方式——一盘布丁飞过餐桌。**What was Bessie's 'little weakness'?** 带着这个问题读课文，重点留意全篇如何用 **讽刺与反差 (irony & contrast)** 讲一个"理想幻灭"的故事：作者铺垫了三年的"完美"，只为最后一刻的"崩塌"。同时注意课文里的**分词短语 (After being absent ...)**、**倒装句 (Not only was the meal ...)** 和**过去完成时 (had had / had been)** 的用法。
</Callout>

## The Text 课文

It is a good thing my aunt Harriet died years ago. If she **were alive today** she would not be able to **air her views on** her favourite topic of conversation: domestic servants. Aunt Harriet lived in that leisurely age when servants **were employed to** do housework. She had a huge, rambling country house called 'The Gables'. She was **sentimentally attached to** this house, for even though it was far too big for her needs, she **persisted in** living there long after her husband's death. Before she grew old, Aunt Harriet **used to entertain lavishly**. I often visited The Gables when I was a boy. No matter how many guests were present, the great house was always **immaculate**. The parquet floors shone like mirrors; highly polished silver was displayed in gleaming glass cabinets; even my uncle's huge collection of books was kept miraculously **free from dust**. Aunt Harriet **presided over** an invisible army of servants that continuously scrubbed, cleaned, and polished. She always **referred to them as** 'the shifting population', for they came and went with such frequency that I never even got a chance to learn their names. Though my aunt pursued **what was, in those days, an enlightened policy**, **in that** she never allowed her domestic staff to work more than eight hours a day, she was extremely difficult to please. While she always criticized the fickleness of human nature, she **carried on** an unrelenting search for the ideal servant **to the end of her days**, even after she had been sadly **disillusioned by** Bessie.

Bessie worked for Aunt Harriet for three years. During that time she so gained my aunt's confidence, that she was **put in charge of** the domestic staff. Aunt Harriet **could not find words to praise** Bessie's industriousness and efficiency. In addition to all her other qualifications, Bessie was an expert cook. She acted the role of the perfect servant for three years before Aunt Harriet discovered her 'little weakness'. **After being absent** from The Gables for a week, my aunt unexpectedly returned one afternoon with a party of guests and instructed Bessie to prepare dinner. **Not only was** the meal well below the usual standard, but Bessie seemed unable to walk steadily. She bumped into the furniture and kept mumbling about the guests. When she came in with the last course — a huge pudding — she tripped on the carpet and the pudding went flying through the air, **narrowly missed** my aunt, and crashed on the dining table with considerable force. Though this caused great mirth among the guests, Aunt Harriet was horrified. She **reluctantly came to the conclusion that** Bessie was drunk. The guests had, of course, realized this from the moment Bessie opened the door for them and, **long before** the final catastrophe, **had had** a difficult time trying to conceal their amusement. The poor girl was dismissed instantly. After her departure, Aunt Harriet discovered that there were piles of empty wine bottles of all shapes and sizes neatly stacked in what had once been Bessie's wardrobe. They had **mysteriously found their way there** from the wine cellar!

## 课文注释 Notes on the text

<Callout type="info" title="8 个值得停下来嚼一嚼的点">
  1. **air one's views on ...** (l.2) —— "就……发表意见、大谈特谈"。**air** 在这里是动词，意为"公开发表、诉说"。同型：*He likes to air his views on politics at every dinner party.* 教材 Vocabulary 练习要求解释 *air her views*，不要跟名词"空气"混淆。

  2. **persist in doing sth.** (l.7) —— "坚持做……、执意做……"（往往带有"不顾困难、不顾劝阻"的意味）。课文 *she **persisted in** living there* 说明姨妈不顾房子"太大、不需要"的事实仍然坚持住下去。同型：*He persisted in asking questions long after the lecture ended.* 教材 Vocabulary 要求解释 *persisted in*。

  3. **be sentimentally attached to ...** (l.6) —— "对……有感情上的依恋"。**attach** 本义是"系、附"，引申为情感上的"依恋"。同型：*She is deeply attached to her grandmother's ring.* 课文 Notes 第 2 条特别解释了这个短语。

  4. **what was, in those days, an enlightened policy** (l.14) —— 这里 **what** 引导的名词性从句作 *pursued* 的宾语，意为"在当时算是开明的做法"。**what** = the thing that。中间的 *in those days* 是插入语。整句拆开：*My aunt pursued a policy. This policy was, in those days, enlightened.* 同型：*He achieved what was, at the time, a remarkable success.* 本课 Structure 第 6 题就考这个结构——它是整个 NCE3 教材里 **what** 引导宾语从句的经典例句。

  5. **in that ...** (l.14) —— "因为、在于"（正式书面语，相当于 *because*，但比 because 更书面）。课文解释为什么姨妈的政策算"开明"：*in that she never allowed her domestic staff to work more than eight hours a day* —— "因为她规定佣人每天工作不超过 8 小时"。同型：*The plan was a failure, in that it cost too much and achieved too little.*

  6. **After being absent ... / 分词短语作时间状语** (l.21-22) —— ***After being absent** from The Gables for a week, my aunt unexpectedly returned ...* —— 用"After + 动名词 being"代替从句"After she had been absent ..."，更简洁。这是本课 **Key structures** 的核心改写题：把 *My aunt had been absent ... when she returned* 改成 *After being absent ..., my aunt returned*。同型：*After finishing his work, he went for a walk.* / *Before leaving, she locked all the doors.*

  7. **Not only ... but ... 倒装句** (ll.23-24) —— ***Not only was** the meal well below the usual standard, **but** Bessie seemed unable to walk steadily.* —— Not only 放在句首时，主句要用**部分倒装**（助动词/be 动词提至主语前）。正常语序：*The meal was not only well below the usual standard, but Bessie seemed ...* 同型：*Not only did she arrive late, but she also forgot the documents.*

  8. **long before ...** (l.29) —— "早在……之前就……"。课文：*long before the final catastrophe, (the guests) had had a difficult time* —— 早在最后那场灾祸之前好久，客人们就已经憋笑憋得很辛苦了。对比 **long after**（在……之后很久）。同型：*Long before the meeting ended, everyone had made up their minds.* 本课 Vocabulary MCQ 第 12 题考 *long before* 的同义替换：**well before**。
</Callout>

## 3. 关键句型 what 从句 · 分词短语 · 倒装结构

<Callout type="tip" title="一分钟回顾">
  本课是一篇"回忆叙事"，讲一个"理想幻灭"的故事，语言上靠三组核心结构撑起叙述的节奏与戏剧感：

  | 结构 | 课文例句 | 用途 |
  |---|---|---|
  | **what 引导的名词从句** | *she pursued **what was, in those days, an enlightened policy*** | 用 what (= the thing that) 把整件事压成一个宾语 |
  | **After + 现在分词 / After being + 过去分词** | ***After being absent** from The Gables for a week, she returned ...* | 时间状语紧缩，替代 After she had been absent |
  | **Not only + 倒装, but ...** | ***Not only was** the meal ... below standard, **but** Bessie seemed ...* | Not only 句首 → 部分倒装，加重戏剧对比 |
  | **过去完成时 had + 过去分词** | *had been absent / had had a difficult time* | 过去的过去——\"（姨妈回来之前）已经……\" |
  | **in that ...** | *an enlightened policy, **in that** she never allowed ...* | 正式书面语的"因为"，比 because 更精确 |
  | **persist in + doing** | *she **persisted in living** there* | 坚持/执意做……（不顾现实条件） |

  ### ⚠️ 4 个本课最容易踩的雷区

  1. **what was an enlightened policy**：**what** = the thing that，整串作 *pursued* 的宾语。**不是** 疑问词"什么"——别翻成"她追求什么……"。
  2. **After being absent** ≠ After she was absent（用 she **was** 也对但不如 being 简练）。但**不能说** After absent（介词 after 后面必须接名词/动名词）。
  3. **Not only was the meal ...**：Not only 在句首 → was 提到主语 the meal 前面（**部分倒装**）。正常语序是 *The meal was not only below standard, but ...*。
  4. **had had**（l.29）：第一个 had 是助动词（构成过去完成时），第二个 had 是实义动词 have 的过去分词（*have a difficult time* = 度过一段困难的时光）。两个 had 连写是正确语法，不要回避。
</Callout>

## 4. 难点：过去完成时 vs 一般过去时 Special difficulties

<Callout type="info" title="教材 Key structures 改写题">
  教材要求：在**不改变意思**的前提下改写下面这句（参照 ll.21-23）：

  > My aunt had been absent from The Gables for a week when she unexpectedly returned one afternoon with a party of guests.
  > After ... my aunt ...

  **解题钥匙**：原句用"过去完成时 + when"表达"（在过去某时间点之前）已经……了多久"。改写思路是**用 After + 分词短语**替代从句，同时保持"先离开一周，后回来"的时间顺序：

  > **After being absent** from The Gables for a week, my aunt unexpectedly returned one afternoon with a party of guests.

  （更正式的版本也可：*After having been absent from The Gables for a week, my aunt ...*）

  ### 过去完成时 vs 一般过去时 的 3 条规则

  1. **先发生的用过去完成，后发生的用一般过去**：*She **had left** before he **arrived**.*（先离开 → 后到达）
  2. **when 从句的时间辨别**：*My aunt **had been** absent for a week **when** she returned.* —— 回来是过去，离开一周是"过去的过去"→ 用 had been。
  3. **缩写成 After + 分词短语**时，过去完成时的"先于"含义由 After 本身就表达了：*After being / having been absent, ...*（After 已经表明了先后，所以 being / having been 都可以，前面不再重复 had）。
</Callout>

<Callout type="info" title="任务">
  *In not more than 80 words* describe **what happened after Aunt Harriet unexpectedly returned to The Gables with a party of guests**. Use your own words as far as possible. **Do not include anything that is not in the last paragraph.**

  按下面的提示点串成一段：

  1. 姨妈回来，要贝西准备晚饭。
  2. 饭菜不行，贝西走路不稳（撞家具、咕哝）。
  3. 高潮：布丁飞过空中，砸在桌上——客人大笑，姨妈吓坏。

## 11. 作文 Composition (约 300 词)

<Callout type="info" title="任务">
  Write a composition in about **300 words** on one of the following:

  **a.** Write an imaginary account of **how Bessie's 'little weakness' went undetected for three years**.

  **b.** *"It is a good thing that domestic servants have become a great rarity."* **What is your opinion?**
</Callout>

<Callout type="warning" title="提示骨架（以 a 题为例）">
  - **开头 (设定)**：For three years, Bessie was the perfect servant. How did nobody notice?
  - **第 1 个原因**：The cellar was rarely checked —— Aunt Harriet never went down there; the wine was only brought up for parties, and Bessie was always the one to fetch it.
  - **第 2 个原因**：Bessie's timing was clever —— she drank only when alone, late at night, and never during working hours. She kept a bottle hidden in her wardrobe.
  - **第 3 个原因**：The "invisible army" of servants —— Aunt Harriet cared only about results (a spotless house), not about how the servants spent their free time. No one dared tell tales because Bessie was in charge.
  - **转折 (几乎暴露的时刻)**：Describe one close call —— a guest noticed a half-empty bottle, but Bessie explained it away.
  - **结尾 (呼应课文)**：*For three years, her 'little weakness' was her best-kept secret — until the day she tripped on the carpet.*
</Callout>

<Callout type="tip" title="写作脚手架">
  - **时态主线**：全篇用**一般过去时**叙述（*she hid / she waited / she opened*），偶尔用过去完成时表\"先于\"（*she had been careful*）。
  - **\"偷偷做\"动词库**：*sneak, slip away, conceal, hide, go unnoticed, keep secret, cover one's tracks*。
  - **\"酒/喝\"词库**：*wine bottles, spirits, a sip, a glass, tipsy, drunk, sober, cellar, stash*。
  - **结构亮点**：用 *One reason was ... Another was ...* 逐层解释\"为什么三年没被发现\"，每一点都跟课文细节挂钩（酒窖没人查、贝西统管佣人所以无人告状、姨妈只看结果不关心过程）。
</Callout>

<Callout type="tip" title="参考范文（自己写完再看）">
  For three whole years, Bessie was the ideal servant — industrious, efficient, an expert cook. Yet all the while she was nursing a secret. How did her `little weakness' go undetected for so long?

  One reason was simple geography. The wine cellar, tucked away beneath The Gables, was a place Aunt Harriet never visited. Bottles were brought upstairs only for parties, and it was Bessie, as head of the domestic staff, who always fetched them. No one else had reason to go down; no one else noticed the levels dropping.

  A second reason was Bessie's careful timing. She never drank during working hours. Her"sips` were taken late at night, when the rest of the household was asleep. She kept a single bottle hidden at the back of her wardrobe, wrapped in an old shawl, and replaced it only when it was empty.

  The third reason was the very system Aunt Harriet had created. She presided over an"invisible army` of servants who came and went so fast she never learned their names. So long as the floors shone and the silver gleamed, she asked no questions. The other servants certainly knew — the faint smell of wine on Bessie's breath could not always be hidden — but they were too afraid of the woman who had been put in charge of them all to say a word.

  A close call came and went. One evening a guest, wandering back to the dining room for a forgotten glove, glimpsed a half-empty bottle on a side table. Bessie, quick as ever, smiled and said it was for cooking — a special sauce for tomorrow's dinner. The guest thought nothing of it.

  For three years, Bessie's `little weakness' was her most perfectly kept secret — until the afternoon her aunt came home unexpectedly, and a pudding went flying across the dining table. *(约 300 词)*
</Callout>

## 12. 书信写作 Letter writing (约 100 词)

<Callout type="info" title="任务">
  Write a letter of thanks to an aunt who has sent you **a generous amount of money for your twenty-first birthday**. Say **what you intend to do with the money**. Do not write more than **100 words**.
</Callout>

<Callout type="tip" title="写作脚手架">
  - **称呼**：*Dear Aunt + 名*（亲切）。
  - **开头**：收到礼物 + 感谢 —— *Thank you so much for the generous cheque you sent for my twenty-first birthday. I was truly touched.*
  - **主体（钱的用途）**—— 挑 2-3 点具体说：
    1. *I've decided to put part of it towards a laptop for my university studies.*
    2. *I'm also planning to use some to take a short trip to Edinburgh — my first real travel on my own.*
    3. *The rest I'm putting into savings for the future.*
  - **收尾**：再次致谢 + 邀请/盼望见面 —— *I hope I can thank you in person when you next visit.*
  - **结束语**：*With love, / [你的名字]*。
  - **时态要点**：感谢用一般现在/现在完成（*I'm writing to thank you / I've received ...*）；钱的用途用现在完成时/将来时（*I've decided to ... / I'm going to ... / I plan to ...*）。
</Callout>

<Callout type="tip" title="参考范文（自己写完再看）">
  Dear Aunt Margaret,

  Thank you so much for the wonderfully generous cheque you sent for my twenty-first birthday. I was genuinely touched — both by your kindness and by the note you wrote.

  I've given a good deal of thought to how best to use it. Part of the money will go towards a new laptop for my studies; my old one has been limping along for years.   Emily *(约 100 词)*

## 13. 拓展练习 Going further

<Callout type="tip" title="自学者建议">
  - **影子跟读 (shadowing)**：用第 1 节的三个 SayIt 按钮跟读 3-5 遍。注意 *persisted in living* / *After being absent* / *Not only was the meal* 这类结构中的连读和重音——persisted **in** living（介词 in 弱读，与 living 连读）；Not only **was** the meal（was 提前，读得比正常轻）。
  - **复述 (retell)**：合上课文，用 2 分钟把故事讲一遍。**四段式结构**：姨妈其人（爱聊佣人 + 大宅 + 难伺候）→ 贝西出现（三年完美 + 获得信任）→ 崩塌一刻（姨妈回来 + 醉酒出丑 + 布丁飞桌）→ 事后发现（衣柜空酒瓶 + 从酒窖来）。
  - **造句迁移**：用今天的 5 个高价值结构造你自己的句子——
    1. *persist in doing ...*（执意做……）—— *Despite the doctor's advice, he persisted in working twelve-hour days.*
    2. *After being ...*（在……之后/被……之后）—— *After being ignored for an hour, she decided to leave.*
    3. *Not only ... but ...*（不仅……而且……）—— *Not only did she finish the report ahead of schedule, but she also found three mistakes in the data.*
    4. *what was ...*（……那种/那样的东西）—— *He achieved what was, at the time, an almost impossible goal.*
    5. *long before ...*（早在……之前）—— *Long before the exam began, she had memorized every formula.*
  - **延伸思考**：本课是一篇经典的\"**铺垫→反转**\"叙事——作者用了整整一段半来铺垫\"完美\"（一尘不染的房子、无形的仆人军团、贝西三年来的勤奋高效），只为在最后半段用一顿晚餐把一切砸得粉碎。这种\"**慢铺垫、快崩塌**\"的节奏是英文幽默叙事的经典笔法。另外，课文的\"幽默\"不是靠俏皮话，而是靠**场景的反差**——\"理想\"姨妈找\"理想\"仆人，结果唯一\"理想\"的那个是个酒鬼。再读一遍开头那句 *It is a good thing my aunt Harriet died years ago* ——这哪是庆幸她去世？分明是说\"幸好她没活到今天，不然连个能让她唠叨的话题都没了\"——一种绅士式的、不伤人的调侃。
</Callout>
