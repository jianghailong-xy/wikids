## The Text 课文

Editors of newspapers and magazines often go to extremes to provide their readers with unimportant facts and statistics. Last year a journalist had been instructed by a well-known magazine to write an article on the president's palace in a new African republic. When the article arrived, the editor read the first sentence and then refused to publish it. The article began: 'Hundreds of steps lead to the high wall which surrounds the president's palace.' The editor at once sent the journalist a fax instructing him to find out the exact number of steps and the height of the wall.

The journalist immediately set out to obtain these important facts, but he took a long time to send them. Meanwhile, the editor was getting impatient, for the magazine would soon go to press. He sent the journalist two more faxes, but received no reply. He sent yet another fax informing the journalist that if he did not reply soon he would be fired. When the journalist again failed to reply, the editor reluctantly published the article as it had originally been written. A week later, the editor at last received a fax from the journalist. **Not only had the poor man been arrested, but he had been sent to prison as well.** However, he had at last been allowed to send a fax in which he informed the editor that he had been arrested while counting the 1,084 steps leading to the fifteen-foot wall which surrounded the president's palace.

## 课文注释 Notes on the text

<Callout type="info" title="6 个值得停下来嚼一嚼的点">
  1. **go to extremes (to do sth.)** (l.1) — "走极端、不惜一切去做"。注意 **extremes 必须用复数**（两端嘛），固定搭配。课文里 *go to extremes to provide ... with unimportant facts* = 为了提供无关紧要的事实而走极端。同义说法：*go to great lengths to ...*。
  2. **provide A with B / provide B for A** (l.1-2) — "向 A 提供 B"。两种说法都对，但介词不能混：with 接的是**提供物**，for 接的是**接收方**。错例：*provide readers facts*（缺介词，错）。
  3. **instructed sb. to do sth.** (l.3) — *instruct* 在这里 = **正式命令、指示**（不是"教导"）。结构 *instruct + sb. + to V*。课文里两次出现：*had been instructed by ... to write*（被动） / *a fax instructing him to find out*（现在分词作后置定语，修饰 fax）。
  4. **lead to + 名词** (l.6) — 两条用法都在本课出现：
     - **通向**（实指方向）：*Hundreds of steps lead to the high wall ...* / *the 1,084 steps leading to the fifteen-foot wall* (l.15-16)
     - **导致**（抽象）：*His carelessness led to the accident.*
     两者意思完全不同，但句型完全相同——靠上下文判断。
  5. **go to press** (l.10) — 报刊行业的固定表达："**付印、开印**"。*press* 这里是**印刷机**。同领域常见短语：*meet the deadline*（赶上截稿）/ *hit the newsstands*（上报摊）。
  6. **Not only had ... but ... as well** (l.13-14) — **本课题眼**。否定副词 *not only* 放在句首，后面必须**倒装**（助动词提到主语前）：*Not only **had** the poor man **been arrested**, but he had been sent to prison as well.* 正常语序应是：*The poor man had not only been arrested, but had also been sent to prison.* 倒装结构的作用是**加强语气**，让"不仅……而且……"的递进感更强。
</Callout>

## 3. 关键句型 Negative inversion 否定倒装

<Callout type="tip" title="一分钟回顾">
  本课最值得偷学的句型只有一个：**否定副词置于句首时的倒装**。

  **正常语序**：*The poor man had not only been arrested, but had also been sent to prison.*

  **倒装语序**：*Not only **had** the poor man been arrested, but he had been sent to prison as well.*

  规则：**否定副词 / 副词短语**放在句首时，**主谓必须倒装**（助动词提到主语前，像一般疑问句的语序）。这种倒装**专门用来加强语气**。

  ### 触发倒装的常见否定词

  - **never / seldom / rarely / hardly / scarcely / no sooner**
  - **not only ... but (also)**
  - **not until ...**
  - **little**（很少，作否定意）
  - **on no account / in no way / under no circumstances**

  ### 对比

  | 正常 | 倒装（强调）|
  |---|---|
  | I have **never** seen so many people. | **Never have** I seen so many people. |
  | I had **hardly** finished speaking when the door opened. | **Hardly had** I finished speaking when the door opened. |
  | He **not only** made this mistake, but he will make it again. | **Not only did** he make this mistake, but he will make it again. |
  | I realized what was happening **only then**. | **Only then did** I realize what was happening. |

  ⚠️ **常见雷区**：
  - 一般现在时 / 过去时**没有助动词**时要**借用 do/does/did**：*Never **did** I see ...*（不是 ~~Never saw I~~）
  - 倒装只在**主句**发生，从句不动：*Hardly had I finished speaking **when the door opened**.*
  - **never before / no sooner ... than** 是搭配："**No sooner had** I finished **than** the phone rang."
</Callout>

## 4. 难点：a / an / the / some 冠词与不定数量词

<Callout type="tip" title="3 组本课直接出现的冠词用法">
  ### A. *a / an* — 第一次提到、泛指

  课文里：
  - *Last year **a** journalist had been instructed by **a** well-known magazine to write **an** article on the president's palace in **a** new African republic.*

  全句出现 4 个 *a/an*——每一个都是**首次提到 + 单数可数 + 泛指**：随便哪位记者、随便哪家著名杂志、随便哪篇文章、随便哪个新成立的非洲共和国。第二次再提到时就用 *the*。

  ### B. *the* — 特指、上下文唯一

  - *the article*（上文刚提到的那篇文章）
  - *the editor*（上文那位编辑——一篇杂志只有一位主编，唯一）
  - *the president's palace*（这个国家只有一座总统府）
  - *the high wall which surrounds the president's palace*（带限定从句，明确说明是哪堵墙）

  规则：**已经提到过 / 上下文里唯一 / 后面带限定语**，三种情况都用 *the*。

  ### C. *some* vs. *a* — 量的差别

  - **a / an**：单数可数（指一个）：*a journalist*
  - **some**：不可数 / 复数可数（指一些）：*some facts* / *some information*
  - 课文里的 *Hundreds of steps* = 不定的复数，**不用 some**，因为 *hundreds of* 本身就是表数量的短语。

  ⚠️ **常见雷区**：
  - **information / advice / news / progress** 都是**不可数**，不能用 *a/an*：~~an information~~ → *some information*
  - 抽象河流 / 山脉等**专有名词**：*the Hudson River, the Alps, the Himalayas*（江河山脉前加 the）

## 10. 摘要写作 Summary writing (≤ 80 词)

<Callout type="info" title="任务">
  In not more than **80 words** describe what happened from the time the journalist set out to get the facts. **Do not include anything that is not in the last paragraph** (即只用 l.9 起的内容)。

  按下面 9 个提示点串成一段：

  1. Did the journalist immediately set out to get the facts after receiving instructions from his editor or not?
  2. Did he send them at once or not?
  3. Was the editor getting impatient or not?
  4. How many faxes did the editor send?
  5. What did the editor threaten to do?
  6. Was the last fax answered or not?
  7. Was the article omitted from the magazine, or was it printed in its original form?
  8. When did the journalist send a fax?
  9. Why had he been imprisoned?
</Callout>

<Callout type="tip" title="参考范文（自己写完再看）">
  The journalist set out at once but failed to send the facts quickly. The editor, growing impatient as the magazine was about to go to press, sent two more faxes — and a third threatening to fire him. When no reply came, he reluctantly published the original article. A week later a fax finally arrived: the journalist had been arrested and imprisoned for counting the 1,084 steps and measuring the fifteen-foot wall around the president's palace. *(78 words)*
</Callout>

## 11. 作文 Composition (约 150 词扩写)

<Callout type="info" title="任务">
  Describe **how the journalist was arrested and what happened afterwards**. Expand the following skeleton into a paragraph of about **150 words**.
</Callout>

<Callout type="warning" title="提示骨架（每个省略号都得自己填）">
  - The journalist counted the number of steps as he **...**
  - On arriving outside the main gate, he **...**
  - He then **...** in order to measure the wall.
  - While he was busy measuring the wall, a policeman **...**
  - Though the journalist **...**, the policeman refused to believe him.
  - He was arrested and sent to prison because the police thought that **...**
  - When the journalist **...** he made things worse for himself.
  - This proved to the police that **...**
</Callout>

<Callout type="tip" title="写作脚手架">
  - **时态主线**：一般过去时叙事；过去完成时 (*had counted / had tried to explain*) 表更早事件；**被动语态**用在 *was arrested / was sent to prison*。
  - **动作词库**：*counted, paced out, jotted down, scribbled in his notebook, produced a tape measure, stretched out the tape, leaned against the wall*。
  - **警察来时**：*a policeman approached / called out / demanded to know / seized him by the arm / led him away*。
  - **辩解动词**：*protested, insisted, tried to explain, produced his press card, showed his fax from the editor, swore he was a journalist*。
  - **越解释越糟**：*the more he explained, the more suspicious he seemed; his story sounded so improbable that the police were convinced he was a spy.*
</Callout>

<Callout type="tip" title="参考范文（自己写完再看）">
  The journalist counted the number of steps as he climbed slowly towards the palace, jotting each figure in his notebook. On arriving outside the main gate, he stood looking up at the great wall. He then produced a tape measure and a length of string in order to measure the wall. While he was busy stretching the tape against the stones, a policeman approached, demanded to know what he was doing and seized his notebook. Though the journalist tried to explain that he was merely writing an article, the policeman refused to believe him. He was arrested and sent to prison because the police thought that he was a spy gathering information about the president's residence. When the journalist produced his press card and the editor's fax demanding the exact figures, he made things worse for himself. This proved to the police that he was indeed collecting sensitive military intelligence. *(151 words)*
</Callout>

## 12. 书信写作 Letter writing

<Callout type="info" title="任务">
  Write a letter of **about 80 words** to a friend who has recently got married. You read about the wedding in your local paper. Supply a suitable **Introduction** and **Conclusion**. Use the following ideas to write the **Purpose**:
  - Surprise and pleasure at seeing newspaper report
  - Glad to hear that he and his wife will be staying in your neighbourhood
  - Hope to see them soon
</Callout>

<Callout type="tip" title="结构模板：三段式私信">
  | 段落 | 作用 | 常用句式 |
  |---|---|---|
  | **Introduction** | 自然开场，提到为何写信 | *I was delighted / surprised to read in this morning's paper that ... / I have just heard the wonderful news that ...* |
  | **Purpose** | 信的主体——表达祝贺 + 你的反应 + 共同未来 | *Congratulations on your marriage! / I had no idea that ... / It will be wonderful to have you and ... living so close.* |
  | **Conclusion** | 收尾期待 + 问候 | *Do let me know when you have settled in. / Looking forward to seeing you both soon. / Please give my warmest regards to ...* |
</Callout>

<Callout type="tip" title="参考范文（自己写完再看）">
  **Dear Tom,**

  What a wonderful surprise it was to read in this morning's *Echo* that you and Jane were married last Saturday! I had no idea you were even engaged — you have kept the news very well hidden indeed.

  Even better, the report mentioned that the two of you have just moved into a flat on Elm Road. That makes us almost neighbours! Helen and I would love to have you both over to dinner as soon as you have settled in.

  Once again, congratulations and every good wish for the future.

  **Yours ever,**
  **Peter** *(82 words)*
</Callout>

<Callout type="warning" title="6 个常用的私信结尾句（再补一组）">
  | # | 结尾句 | 用在什么时候 |
  |---|---|---|
  | 1 | *I look forward to hearing from you soon.* | 等对方回信 |
  | 2 | *Do write back when you have the time.* | 不急的约稿 |
  | 3 | *Please give my love / regards to your family.* | 顺便问候家人 |
  | 4 | *Take care of yourself.* | 关心、温柔收尾 |
  | 5 | *Hope to see you both soon.* | 期待再见 |
  | 6 | *Once again, congratulations and every good wish for the future.* | 祝贺信专用 |
</Callout>

## 13. 拓展练习 Going further

<Callout type="tip" title="自学者建议">
  - **影子跟读 (shadowing)**：用第 1 节的三个 SayIt 按钮，跟读 3-5 遍。重点感受 *Not only had the poor man been arrested* 的**重音模式**——*Not only* 的 *only* 重读，*had* 因为提前也要重读。
  - **复述 (retell)**：合上课文，用自己的话讲一遍这个故事（约 2 分钟）。**关键节奏**：起因（编辑要确切数字）→ 经过（多次催稿无果）→ 结局（记者被捕）→ 反转（被关进监狱）。
  - **造句迁移**：用今天的 5 个高价值结构造你自己的句子——
    1. *S + go to extremes to do sth.*（走极端去做）
    2. *S + provide sb. with sth.*（向……提供……）
    3. *S + instruct sb. to do sth.*（指示某人做）
    4. *Not only + 助动词 + S + V, but ... as well*（否定倒装强调）
    5. *S + had been ... while V-ing ...*（过去完成被动 + while + 现在分词）
  - **延伸思考**：本课在讽刺新闻业对**琐碎精确**的痴迷——*hundreds of steps* 和 *1,084 steps* 信息量几乎一样，但记者却为此进了监狱。**英语写作里"精确数字"是双刃剑**：科学论文需要，但散文/通讯里反而可能让读者出戏。这一课讲的不只是倒装语法，也是一种**新闻写作的反思**。
</Callout>
