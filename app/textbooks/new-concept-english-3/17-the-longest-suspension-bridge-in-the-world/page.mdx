  **How is the bridge supported?** 带着这个问题读课文，重点留意全篇**被动语态 (was designed / was named / has been suspended / has been estimated)** 与一连串数字怎样编织出一座"既庞然又轻盈"的工程奇迹。
</Callout>

## The Text 课文

Verrazano, an Italian about whom little is known, sailed into New York Harbour in 1524 and named it Angoulême. He **described it as** 'a very agreeable situation located within two small hills in the midst of which flowed a great river.' Though Verrazano is **by no means** considered to be a great explorer, his name will probably remain immortal, for on November 21st, 1964, the longest suspension bridge in the world **was named after** him.

The Verrazano Bridge, which **was designed by** Othmar Ammann, joins Brooklyn to Staten Island. It has a span of 4,260 feet. The bridge is so long that **the shape of the earth had to be taken into account** by its designer. Two great towers support four huge cables. The towers are built on immense underwater platforms made of steel and concrete. The platforms extend to a depth of over 100 feet under the sea. These alone took sixteen months to build. Above the surface of the water, the towers rise to a height of nearly 700 feet. They support the cables from which **the bridge has been suspended**. Each of the four cables contains 26,108 lengths of wire. **It has been estimated that** if the bridge **were packed with cars**, it would still only be carrying a third of its total capacity. However, size and strength are not the only important things about this bridge. Despite its immensity, it is both simple and elegant, fulfilling its designer's dream to create 'an enormous object drawn as faintly as possible'.

## 课文注释 Notes on the text

<Callout type="info" title="6 个值得停下来嚼一嚼的点">
  1. **describe ... as ...** (l.2) — \"把……描绘成 / 形容为……\"。**as 后接名词 / 形容词 / 名词性短语**。课文 *described it as 'a very agreeable situation ...'* —— as 后是一个引号引出的长名词短语。同型：*regard ... as ... / refer to ... as ... / look upon ... as ...*。
  2. **located within two small hills in the midst of which flowed a great river** (l.3-4) — 嵌套两层定语。**located ...** 是过去分词短语作后置定语，修饰 *situation*；**in the midst of which flowed a great river** 是 *which* 引导的定语从句，**which 指代 two small hills**，从句因 *in the midst of which*（地点状语）置前而**完全倒装**（动词 flowed 提到主语 a great river 前）。这是 NCE3 里典型的"高密度定语"句法。
  3. **by no means** (l.4) — \"决不、绝非\"。**否定意味的强语气**短语；位置可放在 be 动词 / 情态动词后，或句首（句首时主谓**倒装**）。同型：*in no way / under no circumstances / on no account*。课文 *Verrazano is **by no means** considered to be a great explorer* —— 绝不被认为是伟大的探险家。
  4. **be named after sb.** (l.6-7) — \"以某人的名字命名\"。**after 在这里 = 仿照……的样子 / 取其名**（不是\"在……之后\"的时间意思）。同型：*The street was named after a war hero. / The baby is named after his grandfather.*
  5. **be taken into account** (l.9) — \"被考虑到、被纳入考量\"。**take sth. into account** 的被动式。同型：*take sth. into consideration / bear sth. in mind*。课文 *the shape of the earth had to be taken into account* —— 连**地球曲率**都得算进去（"必须"用 had to）。
  6. **It has been estimated that ...** (l.14) — \"据估计……\"。**It 是形式主语，that-从句是真主语**；现在完成被动 *has been estimated* 表示\"这个估算已经被做出且结论沿用至今\"。同型：*It is reported that ... / It has been said that ... / It is believed that ...*——客观、不指明施动者，新闻 / 科技英语高频结构。
</Callout>

## 3. 关键句型 Passive voice 被动语态

<Callout type="tip" title="一分钟回顾">
  本课是英语教材里讲**被动语态**的经典案例——一座桥的"被设计、被命名、被悬吊、被估算"全程**人不出场，物站 C 位**。每次出现 be + V-ed，都把"动作的承受者"推到主语位置：

  - *the longest suspension bridge ... **was named after** him*（被以他的名字命名）
  - *The Verrazano Bridge, which **was designed by** Othmar Ammann, joins ...*（被奥斯马·阿曼设计）
  - *the shape of the earth **had to be taken into account***（地球曲率不得不被考虑）
  - *the bridge **has been suspended***（大桥已被悬挂）
  - *It **has been estimated that** ...*（据估计……）
  - *if the bridge **were packed with cars** ...*（被汽车塞满——虚拟语气里的被动）

  ### 被动语态的 5 种常见时态形式

  | 时态 | 主动 → 被动 | 课文例证 |
  |---|---|---|
  | **一般过去 was/were + V-ed** | designed → was designed | *The bridge was designed by Ammann.* |
  | **现在完成 has/have + been + V-ed** | has suspended → has been suspended | *the bridge has been suspended* |
  | **过去完成被动 had + been + V-ed** | had to take → had to be taken | *had to be taken into account* |
  | **虚拟语气被动 were + V-ed** | packed → were packed | *if the bridge were packed with cars* |
  | **It + has been + V-ed + that 从句**（形式主语） | estimated → It has been estimated that | *It has been estimated that ...* |

  ### ⚠️ 4 个常见雷区

  1. **by 短语不必每次都出现**：当**施动者不重要 / 未知 / 是常识**时，by 完全可以省略——课文里多数被动句都没 by（*was named after / has been suspended*）。
  2. **情态动词 + be + V-ed**：had **to be** taken / must **be** finished / should **be** considered——情态动词后必接 **be**（原形），不是 *was*。
  3. **It is / has been + V-ed + that-从句**：It is said / It has been estimated / It is believed——表"客观、普遍"，新闻和科技英语首选。这里 It 是**形式主语**，that-从句才是真主语。
  4. **如何选时态**：现在仍有效用 has been V-ed；事件发生在过去用 was V-ed；过去之过去用 had been V-ed；虚拟用 were V-ed（不分人称）。
</Callout>

## 4. 难点：Verb + object + to be + complement 动词 + 宾语 + to be + 补足语

<Callout type="info" title="一分钟看懂教材的 Special Difficulties">
  本课课后 *Special Difficulties* 给出一个看似冷门却高频的句法变形：把 *that-从句*改成 **宾语 + to be + 补足语**。

  - *Instead of saying*: I find **that he is** quite unsuitable for the job.
  - *We can say*: I find **him to be** quite unsuitable for the job.

  这种变形的关键：

  1. **动词必须是"思维 / 判断 / 估算"类**——find, believe, consider, declare, know, guess, estimate, suppose, prove, think。
  2. **that 从句的主语**变成**主句的宾语**（he → him，宾格）。
  3. **that 从句的 is/was/are/were** 变成 **to be**。
  4. **从句的其余部分**（表语形容词 / 名词 / 短语）成为**主句的补足语**。

  本课课文 *Verrazano is **by no means considered to be** a great explorer* —— 就是这个结构的**被动形式**（They do not consider Verrazano to be a great explorer → Verrazano is not considered to be a great explorer）。

  ### ⚠️ 3 个常见雷区

  1. **to be 不能省**（在 consider / believe / find 等动词后，正式书面英语里 to be 是必需的；口语里可省，但 NCE3 这种考试型教材里保留 to be 更稳）。
  2. **被动时 to be 仍然保留**：is considered **to be** / was believed **to be** / has been estimated **to be**。
  3. **不是所有动词都能这样变**——\"say / tell / announce / explain\"等\"言语类\"动词通常不能这样变形（We cannot say: \"He told him to be sick\" 来表示 \"He told that he was sick\"）。
</Callout>

## 10. 摘要写作 Summary writing (≤ 80 词)

  **Describe the Verrazano Bridge in not more than 80 words. Do not include anything that is not in the last paragraph.**

  2. What is its span?

  8. What is the bridge suspended from?
  9. How many lengths of wire does each of these cables contain?

</Callout>

<Callout type="warning" title="提示骨架（每个省略号都得自己填）">
  - **First sight**: name + locations it joins + appearance from a distance (slender thread / dark ribbon / arch silhouetted against the sky).
  - **Up close**: material (steel / stone / iron / reinforced concrete) + supports (towers / arches / pylons / suspension cables) + decorative details.
  - **Morning**: what you can see (mist on the river / fishermen casting lines / first commuters cycling across / sun rising over the water).
  - **Day**: change of atmosphere (traffic roaring / horns / trucks rattling the deck).
  - **Night**: lights reflected on water + sounds (lapping water / distant ship's horn / occasional footsteps).
</Callout>

<Callout type="tip" title="参考范文（自己写完再看）">
  The bridge I know best is called the Nanjing Yangtze River Bridge. It joins the southern bank of Nanjing to the northern districts across the great river. From far away it looks like a slender grey thread stretched between two banks, but when you get near, you see a colossal double-deck structure of steel and reinforced concrete. It is made of high-strength steel girders and supported by massive piers which rise from the riverbed.

  If you stand on the bridge early in the morning, you can see fishermen casting their lines and the first commuters pedalling silently across. At this time everything is quiet. During the day, however, lorries thunder along the upper deck while trains rumble below. I enjoy standing on the bridge at night when its long string of lamps comes alive. In the darkness, you can see the lights mirrored on the black water. In the stillness, the lapping river and the distant horn of a passing barge are the only sounds that can be heard. *(168 words)*
</Callout>

## 12. 书信写作 Letter writing

<Callout type="info" title="任务">
  **A friend who is coming to visit you has written to you asking for detailed information on how to get to your house. Write a reply in about 80 words. Supply a suitable Introduction and Conclusion.**

  Use the following information to write the *Purpose*: which train to catch — where to get off — which bus to catch and where — any familiar landmark — where to get off — which road to take — where your house is.
</Callout>

<Callout type="tip" title="参考范文（自己写完再看）">
  *Dear Tom,*

  *It was good to hear that you are coming up next weekend — I'll do my best to make the journey easy for you.*

  *Catch the 9:45 express from King's Cross and get off at Welham Green; the journey takes about forty minutes. Outside the station you'll see the **No. 17 bus** stop. Take any bus heading north and get off at the **old stone clock tower** — you can't miss it. From there, walk down **Mill Road** for two minutes; my house is the second on the left, with a blue door and a small magnolia tree by the gate.*

  *Phone me from the station if anything goes wrong. Looking forward to Saturday!*

  *Yours ever,*

  *Sarah  (84 words in the body)*
</Callout>

<Callout type="info" title="为什么这封信合格？">
  1. **Introduction**：第一段一句话 —— 表达高兴、铺垫接下来给方向。
  2. **Purpose**（核心段，按 7 个要点严格走）：① which train（9:45 from King's Cross）→ ② where to get off（Welham Green）→ ③ which bus and where（No. 17 outside the station）→ ④ familiar landmark（old stone clock tower）→ ⑤ where to get off（at the clock tower）→ ⑥ which road（Mill Road）→ ⑦ where the house is（second on the left, blue door）。
  3. **Conclusion**：留电话兜底 + 期待见面——温度。
  4. **Tone**：私信 → *Dear + 名*, *Yours ever*；用现在时给指示（catch, get off, walk）让指令清晰。
</Callout>

## 13. 拓展练习 Going further

<Callout type="tip" title="自学者建议">
  - **影子跟读 (shadowing)**：用第 1 节的三个 SayIt 按钮跟读 3-5 遍。重点感受 *was named after him* / *had to be taken into account* / *if the bridge were packed* 这些**被动 + 介词 + 代词**的连读——*named after him* 几乎读成 \"name-DAF-tə-rim\"。
  - **复述 (retell)**：合上课文，把 Verrazano Bridge 的故事用 3 分钟讲一遍。**叙事三段**：背景（维拉萨诺 1524 年驶入纽约港 → 命名权）→ 工程（4,260 英尺跨度、2 塔 4 缆、地球曲率、塔基 16 个月）→ 美学（despite immensity, simple and elegant）。
  - **造句迁移**：用今天的 5 个高价值结构造你自己的句子——
    1. *be named after sb.*（以某人名字命名）—— *Our school library was named after a former headmaster.*
    2. *be by no means + adj / V-ed*（绝非……）—— *This problem is by no means easy.*
    3. *be taken into account*（被纳入考虑）—— *Cost must be taken into account before we proceed.*
    4. *It has been estimated that ...*（据估计……）—— *It has been estimated that 60% of users prefer dark mode.*
    5. *despite + n., ... is + adj*（尽管……，仍然……）—— *Despite its small size, the team is highly productive.*
  - **延伸思考**：本课是\"**工程描写的工业级范本**\"——一座桥的故事，因为**被动语态把人推后、把物推前**（was designed / was named / has been suspended / has been estimated），读起来像一段冷静客观的工程史。每个 *was V-ed* 都是\"把动作还给物体\"。下次你写英文描写客观事物时，问自己一句：\"这个动作的真正主角是谁？如果是物，要不要用被动让物站到主语位？\"——答案对了，你的英文就有了**工程师的冷静质感**。
</Callout>
