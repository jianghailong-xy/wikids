## The Text 课文

These days, people who do manual work often receive **far more money** than people who work in offices. People who work in offices are frequently **referred to as** 'white-collar workers' for the simple reason that they usually wear a collar and tie to go to work. Such is human nature, that a great many people are often willing to **sacrifice higher pay for the privilege of** becoming white-collar workers. This can **give rise to** curious situations, as it did in the case of Alfred Bloggs who worked as a dustman for the Ellesmere Corporation.

When he got married, Alf was **too embarrassed to say** anything to his wife about his job. He **simply** told her that he worked for the Corporation. Every morning, he left home dressed in a smart black suit. He then changed into overalls and spent the next eight hours as a dustman. Before returning home at night, he took a shower and changed back into his suit. Alf did this for over two years and his fellow dustmen kept his secret. Alf's wife has never discovered that she married a dustman and she never will, for Alf has just found another job. He will soon be working in an office. He will be **earning only half as much as he used to**, but he feels that his rise in status is **well worth the loss of money**. From now on, he will wear a suit all day and others will call him 'Mr. Bloggs', not 'Alf'.

## 课文注释 Notes on the text

<Callout type="info" title="7 个值得停下来嚼一嚼的点">
  1. **far more money** (l.1) ——「多得多」。**far** 是副词，放在比较级前面加强语气，相当于 **much**。同型：*far easier / far better / far less important*。注意 **far** 只能修饰比较级，不能修饰原级（不能说 *far easy*）。

  2. **refer to ... as ...** (ll.2–3) ——「把……称作……」。等于 **regard ... as ...**。同型：*People often refer to him as a genius. / The area is referred to as Silicon Valley.*。被动语态 *are frequently referred to as* = 常常被称作。注意 **refer to** 本身还有「参考、查阅」的意思：*refer to a dictionary*。

  3. **for the simple reason that ...** (l.3) ——「原因很简单，就是……」——**that** 引导同位语从句，补充说明 **reason** 的具体内容。比 *because* 更正式，多用于书面文体。

  4. **Such is human nature, that ...** (ll.4–5) ——「此乃人之常情，以至……」。**such** 放在句首形成倒装，**that** 引导同位语从句解释 **such** 指什么。正常语序：*Human nature is such that ...*。同型：*Such was his kindness that we'll never forget him.*（= 他如此善良，我们永不会忘。）

  5. **give rise to** (ll.6–7) ——「引起、导致」。正式表达，等于 **lead to / cause / result in**。同型：*Such behaviour might give rise to misunderstandings. / The discovery gave rise to a heated debate.*。注意区别于 **in the case of**（就……而言），不是 **in case of**（万一）。

  6. **too ... to ...** (l.9) ——「太……以致不能……」。**too embarrassed to say anything** = 太尴尬而什么都没说。这是 **too + adj/adv + to do** 的否定含义结构，可转换为 **so ... that ... 否定式**：*so embarrassed that he did not say anything*。同型：*The box is too heavy for me to lift. / It was too late to catch the train.*

  7. **half as much as he used to** (l.15) ——「只有他过去挣的一半」。**倍数 / 分数表达法**：**half / a quarter / twice / three times + as + 形容词原级 + as**。同型：*This room is about three times as large as that one. / She earns twice as much as I do.*。注意 **used to** 后面省略了与前面相同的部分（earn），但当主句谓语是 be 时，**used to** 之后必须保留 be：*the amount they used to be*（教材 Structure 第 8 题考点）。
</Callout>

## 3. 关键句型 现在完成时与一般过去时的对峙 Present Perfect vs Simple Past

<Callout type="tip" title="一分钟回顾">
  本课虽然是一篇叙事文，但时间层次非常讲究。全篇用**一般过去时**讲述 Alf 两年里每天做的事（completed past），但在最关键的两句话——他妻子从未发现、他终于找到了新工作——切换到了**现在完成时**（present relevance）。这是本课语法核心。

  | 时态 | 课文例句 | 用途 |
  |---|---|---|
  | **一般过去时 (Simple Past)** | *Alf **did** this for over two years. / His fellow dustmen **kept** his secret.* | 已结束的、在过去持续了一段时间的动作 |
  | **现在完成时 (Present Perfect)** | *Alf's wife **has never discovered** that she married a dustman.* | 从过去到现在一直如此——至今不知道 |
  | **现在完成时 + just** | *Alf **has just found** another job.* | 刚刚发生的动作，强调对现在的影响 |
  | **will soon be doing** | *He **will soon be working** in an office.* | 不久的将来；将来进行时表计划好的事 |

  ### ⚠️ 3 个本课最容易踩的雷区

  1. **现在完成时 ≠ 一般过去时**。*has never discovered*（至今不知道）≠ *never discovered*（过去不知道，现在可能知道了）。用一般过去时就等于暗示「现在已经知道了」——整篇故事就塌了。
  2. **just 的位置**：现在完成时中 **just** 放在 have/has 之后、过去分词之前——*has **just** found*。
  3. **used to 之后的省略**：*half as much as he used to (earn)* 省略了 earn。但当主句是 be 动词时不可省略：*the amount they used to **be***（不能省 be）。
</Callout>

## 4. 难点：间接宾语与 worth 的用法 Special difficulties

<Callout type="info" title="教材 Special difficulties">
  本课 Special difficulties 含两个独立知识点：

  ### A. 间接宾语（Indirect Objects）

  有些动词**不能直接带「人」作宾语**——必须用 **to + 人** 才正确。记住下面这组「to 类」动词：

  > **explain / describe / say / suggest / confide**

  正确写法：
  - *He explained the difficulty **to me**.*（✔）→ 不能说 *He explained me the difficulty.*（✘）
  - *I described the scene **to my wife**.*（✔）→ 不能说 *I described my wife the scene.*（✘）
  - *He said nothing **to me** about it.*（✔）
  - *Did you suggest this idea **to him**?*（✔）
  - *I confided the secret **to my sister**.*（✔）

  ⚠️ 对比 **give / tell / send / show** 类动词——这些可以直接带「人」作间接宾语：*give me the book / tell her a story / send him a letter*。

  ### B. worth 的用法

  **worth** 只能接**名词或动名词**，不能接**不定式**：
  - *His rise in status is well **worth** the loss of money.*（✔ worth + 名词）
  - *That film is not **worth seeing**.*（✔ worth + 动名词）
  - ✘ *Is this worth **to buy**?*（❌ 错——应用 *Is this worth **buying**?*）

  **be worth** 的否定/疑问用 **be 动词**，不用 do/does：
  - *It is worth five pounds.* → *Is it worth five pounds?*（✔）
  - ✘ *Does it worth five pounds?*（❌——worth 是形容词，不是动词）
</Callout>

## 10. 摘要写作 Summary writing（≤ 80 词）

<Callout type="info" title="任务">
  *In not more than 80 words* describe **how Alfred Bloggs prevented his wife from discovering that he was a dustman**. Use only the **last paragraph** of the passage.

  按下面的提示点串成一段：

  1. 婚后 Alf 对妻子说了什么？
  2. 每天早上他怎么穿？
  3. 到公司后换了什么？
  4. 他在一整天里做什么？
  5. 晚上回家前他做什么？
  6. 这样持续了多久？
  7. 同事有没有替他保密？
  8. 妻子以后会知道吗？
  9. 接下来他要去哪里工作？
</Callout>

<Callout type="tip" title="参考范文（自己写完再看）">
  When Alfred Bloggs got married, he told his wife only that he worked for the Ellesmere Corporation. Every morning he left home in a smart black suit, but on arrival he changed into overalls and worked the next eight hours as a dustman. Before returning home at night, he took a shower and changed back into his suit. He kept this up for over two years, and his fellow dustmen faithfully kept his secret. His wife has never learnt the truth and never will, for Alf has found a white-collar office job. *(80 words)*
</Callout>

## 11. 作文 Composition（约 200 词）

<Callout type="info" title="任务">
  Write a composition of about **200 words** titled **"Nearly caught"**. Use the ideas given below. Do not write more than **three paragraphs**.

  **Title:** Nearly caught

  **Outline:**
  - **Introduction:** Alf and three other dustmen were collecting rubbish — arrived at Mrs. Frost's house.
  - **Development:** Alf's wife was visiting Mrs. Frost — Alf got out of the dustcart — saw his wife leaving — hid in the dustcart — dustmen helped him — his wife talked to Mrs. Frost on the doorstep.
  - **Conclusion:** The dustcart drove away just as Mrs. Bloggs came towards it.
</Callout>

<Callout type="warning" title="提示骨架">
  - **第 1 段（开头）**：交代场景——某天 Alf 和三个清洁工同事在某街区收垃圾，开到 Mrs. Frost 家门口。
  - **第 2 段（发展——最紧张的 30 秒）**：Alf 跳下车刚要搬垃圾桶，突然看见妻子从 Mrs. Frost 家出来。他立刻躲回 dustcart 后面，同事看见这一幕，立刻懂了——赶快把 Mrs. Bloggs 的注意力引向 Mrs. Frost（她们在门口聊了起来），同时用垃圾箱挡住 Alf。Alf 蹲在车后，心几乎要跳出来。
  - **第 3 段（结尾——险中脱身）**：Mrs. Bloggs 往 dustcart 方向走来，就在这千钧一发之际，dustcart 发动引擎开走了。Alf 趴在车里长出一口气。
</Callout>

<Callout type="tip" title="写作脚手架">
  - **时态主线**：全篇用**一般过去时**叙事（arrived / saw / hid / helped / drove away）。
  - **动词库**：*collect rubbish, pull up outside, jump out of, catch sight of, duck / dodge behind, keep sb. talking, cover for sb., breathe a sigh of relief, drive off*。
  - **紧张感写法**：用短句。——*His heart pounded. She was coming closer. The engine roared.*
  - **结构亮点**：用一个 **just as ...**（正当……之时）收束结尾——*Just as Mrs. Bloggs came towards it, the dustcart drove away.*
</Callout>

<Callout type="tip" title="参考范文（自己写完再看）">
  One Thursday morning, Alf and three other dustmen were collecting rubbish in the Ellesmere district when their dustcart pulled up outside Mrs. Frost's house. Alf jumped out and was about to reach for the bin when he froze — his wife was stepping out of Mrs. Frost's front door, laughing about something.

  Without a second thought, Alf ducked behind the dustcart. One of his mates, Joe, saw what was happening and immediately stepped forward. "Morning, Mrs. Bloggs!" he called cheerfully, blocking her view. While Joe and Mrs. Frost kept Alf's wife talking on the doorstep, the others hastily wheeled the bins to the far side of the cart. Alf crouched behind the vehicle, his heart pounding.

  The conversation on the doorstep seemed to last an age. Finally Mrs. Bloggs said goodbye and began walking — straight towards the dustcart. Alf held his breath. The driver, quick as a flash, started the engine. Just as Mrs. Bloggs came within a few yards, the dustcart rumbled away round the corner. Alf lay flat in the back, breathing hard. He had been nearly caught — but his secret was safe for one more day. *(196 words)*
</Callout>

## 12. 书信写作 Letter writing（约 80 词）

<Callout type="info" title="任务">
  Write a letter of about **80 words** to an acquaintance whom you do not know well. You want to **borrow a book** which you know he has. Supply a suitable Introduction and Conclusion. Use the following ideas to write your letter:

  - Ask for the loan of the book — give its title.
  - Say why you want it — how long you will keep it.
  - Promise you will take good care of it.
</Callout>

<Callout type="tip" title="写作脚手架">
  - **称呼**：*Dear Mr. + 姓*（对不太熟的人，正式些）。
  - **开头**：先寒暄一句，再说明来意 —— *I wonder if I might ask a small favour.*
  - **主体**：
    1. 提书名 + 为什么想借（考试 / 写文章 / 研究）。
    2. How long you'll keep it — *two weeks at most*。
    3. Promise to take good care — *I'll make sure it comes back in perfect condition.*
  - **收尾**：*I'd be most grateful if you could spare it. / Many thanks in advance.*
  - **结束语**：*Yours sincerely, / [你的名字]*。
  - **时态要点**：现在时为主（请求 / 说明），将来时表承诺。
</Callout>

<Callout type="tip" title="参考范文（自己写完再看）">
  Dear Mr. Thompson,

  I wonder if I might ask a small favour. I understand you have a copy of *A Short History of English Literature*, and I would be most grateful if you could lend it to me for a week or two.

  I am preparing a paper on eighteenth-century satire, and several colleagues have told me that your edition has excellent notes. I promise to take the greatest care of it and return it by the end of the month.

  Many thanks in advance.

  Yours sincerely,

  David Hall *(78 words)*
</Callout>

## 13. 拓展练习 Going further

<Callout type="tip" title="自学者建议">
  - **影子跟读 (shadowing)**：用第 1 节的三个 SayIt 按钮跟读 3–5 遍。注意 **too embarrassed to say** 的重音落在 embarrassed 上；**half as much as** 整段要读出节奏感——half **AS** much as he **USED** to。
  - **复述 (retell)**：合上课文，用 2 分钟讲一遍 Alf 的故事。**叙述弧线**：社会背景（蓝领挣得多，但人人想当白领）→ Alf 的双重生活（西装→工作服→洗澡→西装回家，两年）→ 结局（换工作、减薪一半、但终于能穿西装被称 Mr. Bloggs）。
  - **造句迁移**：用今天的 5 个高价值结构造你自己的句子——
    1. *far more ... than ...*（多得多）—— *The new job pays far more than the old one.*
    2. *be referred to as ...*（被称作）—— *She is often referred to as the best singer in town.*
    3. *give rise to ...*（引起、导致）—— *His remarks gave rise to a heated discussion.*
    4. *too ... to ...*（太……以致不能）—— *The suitcase was too heavy for the child to carry.*
    5. *half / twice as ... as ...*（倍数比较）—— *My new flat is twice as large as the old one.*
  - **延伸思考**：本课是一篇**社会观察式**叙事文——作者用一个小人物的故事，照出了一整个社会的价值观：**social status matters more than money**。全文没有一句说教，只是把 Alf 的每一天细细道来：西装→工作服→淋浴→西装回家……这个**换装的仪式**重复了两年，本身就成了最锋利的社会评论。写记叙文时记住这一招：**用具体的、重复的日常行为来承载抽象的社会命题**——不要告诉读者「社会很看重地位」，让他们看到 Alf 每天在换衣服就够了。另外，留意最后一句 *Mr. Bloggs, not Alf*——用名字的转变作结，是记者和非虚构写作里的经典收束手法：把大道理浓缩进一个最小的符号里。
</Callout>
