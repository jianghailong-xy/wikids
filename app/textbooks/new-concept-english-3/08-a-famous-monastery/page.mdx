## The Text 课文

The Great St. Bernard Pass connects Switzerland to Italy. At 2,473 metres, it is the highest mountain pass in Europe. The famous monastery of St. Bernard, which was founded in the eleventh century, lies about a mile away. For hundreds of years, St. Bernard dogs **have saved** the lives of travellers crossing the dangerous Pass. These friendly dogs, which were first brought from Asia, **were used** as watchdogs even in Roman times. **Now that** a tunnel has been built through the mountains, the Pass is less dangerous, but each year, the dogs are still sent out into the snow **whenever** a traveller is in difficulty. Despite the new tunnel, there are still a few people who rashly attempt to cross the Pass on foot.

During the summer months, the monastery is very busy, for it is visited by thousands of people who cross the Pass in cars. As there are so many people about, the dogs have to be kept in a special enclosure. In winter, however, life at the monastery is quite different. The temperature drops to −30° and very few people attempt to cross the Pass. **The monks prefer winter to summer for they have more privacy.** The dogs have greater freedom, too, for they are allowed to wander outside their enclosure. The only regular visitors to the monastery in winter are parties of skiers who go there at Christmas and Easter. These young people, who love the peace of the mountains, always receive a warm welcome at St. Bernard's monastery.

## 课文注释 Notes on the text

<Callout type="info" title="6 个值得停下来嚼一嚼的点">
  1. **Now that + 从句** (l.7) — *now that* 是**连词**，相当于 *since*（既然、由于），引导**原因状语从句**，强调"**新出现**的情况"作为后果的依据。课文 *Now that a tunnel **has been built***——用**现在完成时**强调"已经建成"这一新事实。常见搭配：*Now that you mention it, ... / Now that I think about it, ...*。
  2. **whenever** (l.8) — = *every time when / at any time when*，引导**时间状语从句**。课文 *the dogs are sent out into the snow **whenever** a traveller is in difficulty*——只要 / 一旦有旅客遇险。同类 *-ever* 词：*whoever / whatever / wherever / however*——既加强语气也表泛指。
  3. **Despite + 名词** (l.9) — *despite* 后**直接接名词或动名词**（不要 of）：*Despite the new tunnel ...* 不要写成 ~~Despite of the new tunnel~~（**英语里没有 despite of**）。同义：*in spite of + 名词*（in spite of 才有 of）。让步从句要用 *Although* + 完整句。
  4. **prefer A to B** (l.14) — = 喜欢 A 胜过 B。课文 *The monks prefer winter to summer*——修士们更喜欢冬天。to 是**介词**，后接**名词 / 动名词**：*prefer tea to coffee / prefer reading to watching TV*。**不要用 than**（than 是 *would rather A than B* 的搭配）。
  5. **for they have more privacy** (l.14) — *for* 在书面英语里 = *because*，引导**并列分句**（不是从属从句），前面用逗号、不能放句首。同型出现两次：*the monastery is very busy, **for** it is visited by ... / The monks prefer winter to summer **for** they have more privacy.*
  6. **about (副词)** (l.12) — *about* 这里不是介词"关于"，而是**副词**，意思是"**在附近，到处**"，相当于 *around*。课文 *As there are so many people **about***——周围、附近有那么多人。同型：*Don't leave your things lying about. / Is anyone about?*
</Callout>

## 3. 关键句型 Verb forms review 动词时态复习

<Callout type="tip" title="一分钟回顾">
  本课的句子在三套时态间灵活切换，组成一幅"几百年传统 + 当下日常"的全景图：

  | 时态 | 用来表达 | 课文例子 |
  |---|---|---|
  | **一般过去 (what happened)** | 过去的具体事件 | the monastery **was founded** in the eleventh century / These dogs **were first brought** from Asia |
  | **现在完成 (what has happened)** | 过去发生、影响延续到现在 | For hundreds of years, dogs **have saved** the lives of travellers / a tunnel **has been built** through the mountains |
  | **一般现在 (what always happens)** | 习惯、普遍真理、现状 | The Pass **connects** Switzerland to Italy / The monks **prefer** winter to summer / Skiers **always receive** a warm welcome |

  ### ⚠️ 三个最容易混的临界点

  1. **明确过去时间 → 一般过去**：*in the eleventh century / in Roman times / first brought* —— 都有明确历史时点，配过去时。
  2. **不明确时点 + 影响延续 → 现在完成**：*For hundreds of years ... have saved*（持续到现在）/ *a tunnel has been built*（结果还在——隧道现在还在用）。
  3. **永恒事实 / 当下状态 → 一般现在**：*connects, lies, is visited, prefer, are allowed, receive* —— 描述这座修道院"**现在的样子**"。

  ### 常用时间状语 → 时态对应表

  | 状语 | 配的时态 |
  |---|---|
  | yesterday / last week / in 1990 / **ago** | 一般过去 |
  | **for + 时段**（for six months）/ **since + 时点**（since 1988）/ **so far / up to now** | 现在完成 |
  | **always / usually / often / every day** | 一般现在 |
  | **when + 过去时** | 一般过去（叙事）|
  | **now that ...** | 现在完成 / 一般现在 |
</Callout>

## 4. 难点：-ever words 加强语气的连词

<Callout type="tip" title="6 个 -ever 词，5 种用法">
  *-ever* 词（whoever / whatever / whenever / wherever / however / whichever）有**两个角色**：

  ### 角色 A：复合关系代词 / 副词（引导从句）

  = "**无论谁 / 无论什么 / 无论何时……**"，让从句的指代变得**泛指**。

  | 词 | 引导的从句类型 | 例子 |
  |---|---|---|
  | **whoever** | 主语 / 宾语从句 | **Whoever** told you that didn't know what he was talking about. |
  | **whatever** | 主语 / 宾语从句 | He did **whatever** I asked him to do. |
  | **whenever** | 时间状语从句 | I'll tell you **whenever** you make a mistake. |
  | **wherever** | 地点状语从句 | He does **whatever** he pleases. |
  | **however** | 方式 / 让步状语 | **However** hard he tries, he can't succeed. |
  | **whichever** | 主语 / 宾语 + 选择 | Take **whichever** you like. |

  ### 角色 B：和 wh- 一起加强疑问语气

  放在**疑问句**里 = "**究竟、到底**"，相当于 *on earth / in the world*。

  | 一般疑问 | 加强语气 |
  |---|---|
  | What are you doing? | **What ever** / **Whatever** are you doing? |
  | Who told you that? | **Who ever** / **Whoever** told you that? |
  | Where has he gone? | **Where ever** / **Wherever** has he gone? |

  ⚠️ 这种加强语气用法里，*-ever* 可以与 wh- **分写**（What ever）也可**连写**（Whatever）。但作“无论……”讲时**只能连写**。

  ### ⚠️ 常见雷区

  - **whenever vs. when ever**：连写 = 无论何时；分写 = 究竟何时（语气）。
  - **wherever vs. where ever**：同上。
  - **whatever / whoever** 引导**名词性从句**时不能换成 *what / who*——意思会变（无论 vs. 选择性指代）。
</Callout>

## 10. 摘要写作 Summary writing (≤ 80 词)

## 11. 作文 Composition (约 200 词扩写)

<Callout type="info" title="任务">
  Write an **imaginary account** of the way a traveller was rescued on St. Bernard's Pass in winter. Use the ideas below. Three paragraphs, **no more than 200 words**.

  **Title:** *Rescue.*
</Callout>

<Callout type="warning" title="提示骨架（每个省略号都得自己填）">
  - **Introduction**: A monk took two dogs out for exercise — the dogs were restless — a search party was organised.
  - **Development**: The dogs led the monks through the snow — high winds the previous night — now heavy fog — temperature 20° below — they got near — heard cries — a man was trapped under the snow — the dogs dragged him out — he was taken to the monastery on a sledge.
  - **Conclusion**: The man was unconscious — recovered later — told them what had happened the previous night.
</Callout>

<Callout type="tip" title="写作脚手架">
  - **时态主线**：一般过去时叙事；过去完成时表前一天 / 更早事件（*had been blowing / had fallen / had set out*）；被动表受动者状态（*was trapped / was taken / was carried*）。
  - **天气词库**：*howling wind, blinding blizzard, swirling snow, knee-deep drifts, an icy fog hanging over the slopes, visibility down to a few metres*。
  - **狗的动作**：*pricked up their ears, whined uneasily, strained at the leash, sniffed the air, bounded ahead, began to dig frantically*。
  - **救援动作**：*set out at first light, harnessed the sledge, ploughed through, scraped away the snow, dragged him clear, wrapped him in blankets*。
  - **结尾的张力**：让获救者**自己讲述**前一晚的遭遇——一句"He had been trying to cross alone, when the storm caught him just below the summit."就够。
</Callout>

<Callout type="tip" title="参考范文（自己写完再看）">
  Early one February morning, a young monk took two St. Bernard dogs out for their usual exercise. The animals seemed unusually restless, sniffing the air and whining toward the snowy slopes. Sensing that something was wrong, the monk hurried back to the monastery and a small search party was quickly organised.

  The dogs led the monks straight up the Pass through deep snow. High winds the previous night had piled drifts shoulder-high, and a heavy fog now lay over the mountainside. The temperature stood at twenty below. Just as they neared the summit, the dogs began to dig frantically and the monks heard faint cries beneath the snow. A man had been trapped there for hours. With the dogs scraping and the monks shovelling, they dragged him clear and lifted him onto a sledge to be carried down to the monastery.

  The traveller was unconscious when they reached the warmth of the monastery, but, after several hours by the fire, he came round. He told the monks how, the previous evening, he had foolishly set out to cross the Pass alone, only to be caught by the storm and buried in a sudden avalanche just below the ridge. *(198 words)*
</Callout>

## 13. 拓展练习 Going further

<Callout type="tip" title="自学者建议">
  - **影子跟读 (shadowing)**：用第 1 节的三个 SayIt 按钮跟读 3-5 遍。重点感受 *have saved* / *has been built* 的**完成时连读**——have/has 几乎被吞掉，听起来像 "haveSAVED / hasBEENbuilt"。
  - **复述 (retell)**：合上课文，分**夏 / 冬**两幅画面讲修道院的生活。夏：thousands of cars, dogs in enclosure, busy。冬：−30°, few people, monks more privacy, dogs free, only skiers visit。
  - **造句迁移**：用今天的 5 个高价值结构造你自己的句子——
    1. *For + 时段 + S + have/has + V-ed*（持续到现在）—— *For ten years he has lived in Beijing.*
    2. *Now that + 完成时 / 现在时, ...*（既然……）—— *Now that you've finished school, what's next?*
    3. *Despite + 名词, ...*（尽管……）—— *Despite the rain, we set out.*
    4. *Whenever + 主谓, ...*（每当……）—— *Whenever I see her, she smiles.*
    5. *prefer A to B*（更喜欢 A 不愿 B）—— *I prefer tea to coffee.*
  - **延伸思考**：本课文风是“**地理 + 历史 + 当下日常**”的**百科式叙述**，三个时态层层切换——过去时讲历史（创建、引入）、现在完成讲传承（救人 / 建隧道）、一般现在讲日常（连接、参观、欢迎）。这种**多时态调度**正是英语百科类（Wikipedia / National Geographic）文章的标准写法。下次读英文百科条目时，留意作者怎么换时态——你会发现它们的逻辑和本课一模一样。
</Callout>
