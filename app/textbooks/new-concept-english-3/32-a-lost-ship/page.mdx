## The Text 课文

The salvage operation **had been a complete failure**. The small ship, Elkor, which **had been searching** the Barents Sea for weeks, was on its way home. A radio message from the mainland **had been received** by the ship's captain instructing him to **give up the search**. The captain knew that another attempt would be made later, for the sunken ship he was trying to find had been carrying a precious cargo of gold bullion.

Despite the message, the captain of the Elkor decided to try once more. The sea bed **was scoured** with powerful nets and there was tremendous excitement on board when a chest **was raised** from the bottom. Though the crew were at first **under the impression that** the lost ship had been found, the contents of the chest **proved them wrong**. **What they had in fact found** was a ship which had been sunk many years before.

The chest contained the personal belongings of a seaman, Alan Fielding. There were books, clothing and photographs, **together with** letters which the seaman had once received from his wife. The captain of the Elkor ordered his men to salvage **as much as possible** from the wreck. Nothing of value was found, but the numerous items which were brought to the surface proved to **be of great interest**. From a heavy gun that was raised, the captain realized that the ship **must have been** a cruiser. In another chest, which contained the belongings of a ship's officer, there was an unfinished letter which had been written on March 14th, 1943. The captain learnt from the letter that the name of the lost ship was the *Karen*. The most valuable **find** of all was the ship's log book, parts of which **it was still possible** to read. From this the captain was able to **piece together** all the information that had **come to light**. The *Karen* had been sailing in a convoy to Russia when she **was torpedoed** by an enemy submarine. This was later confirmed by a naval official at the Ministry of Defence after the Elkor had returned home. All the items that were found **were sent** to the War Museum.

## 课文注释 Notes on the text

<Callout type="info" title="7 个值得停下来嚼一嚼的点">
  1. **had been searching** (l.2) —— **过去完成进行时**。表达"从过去某个时间点之前一直在做某事"，强调动作的持续性和延续性。课文开头连用过去完成时（had been a complete failure / had been searching / had been received / had been carrying），把叙述的时间锚定在"返航"这个过去时刻，而之前的搜寻、收报、装货都是更早发生的事。同型：*The team had been working on the project for months before they finally succeeded.*

  2. **under the impression that ...** (l.10) —— "以为……"，表达**与事实相反的认识**。that 后接完整的从句。同型：*I was under the impression that you had already left. / He was under the impression that the meeting had been cancelled.* 本文的"反转"靠的就是 **under the impression that ... proved them wrong** 这个搭配——先以为对了，结果证明错了。教材 Structure 第 5 题的 **It was ... that ...** 强调句也呼应此处：*It was a ship ... that they had in fact found*。

  3. **be of great interest** (l.15) —— "非常有趣 / 很有价值"。**be of + 抽象名词** = be + 该名词对应的形容词（be of great interest = be very interesting）。同型：*be of great importance (= be very important) / be of little value (= be almost worthless) / be of no use (= be useless)*。

  4. **must have been** (l.16) —— **对过去的推测**。"（从已掌握的证据来看）一定是……"。船长看到那门重炮（from a heavy gun that was raised），由此推断船"一定曾是一艘巡洋舰"。注意 **must have been** 只表推测，不表义务。同型：*The ground is wet — it must have rained last night. / She looks exhausted — she must have been working all day.*

  5. **parts of which it was still possible to read** (l.19) —— which 指代 the ship's log book。这个非限制性定语从句里，**it** 是形式主语，真正主语是 **to read parts of which**。正常语序为 *it was still possible to read parts of the log book*。换成被动语态会更简洁——教材 Structure 第 7 题正是这个转换：*parts of which **could still be read***。**it is possible to do** 和 **it is still possible to do** 的区别在于后者强调"尽管条件不利，仍然可以"——航海日志虽然沉在海底多年，部分仍可辨识。

  6. **piece together** (l.19-20) —— "拼凑、拼合"。可分开用（piece ... together 或 piece together ...）。同型：*The detective pieced together the clues. / It took years to piece the whole story together.* 与之搭配的 **come to light** (l.20) = "显露出来，浮现"，常指隐藏的信息被披露。同型：*New evidence has come to light. / The truth finally came to light after decades.*

  7. **together with** (l.13) —— "连同……一起"。比简单的 and 更正式，强调"附带着一起"。同型：*He sent the report, together with all the supporting documents. / The captain, together with his crew, was rescued.* 注意：together with 后面的名词不影响主语单复数（主语是 the captain 单数，谓语用 was）。本课 Vocabulary 第 10 题的同义替换正是 **along with**。
</Callout>

## 3. 关键句型 过去完成时与被动语态 Past perfect & passives

<Callout type="tip" title="一分钟回顾">
  本课是一篇**叙事文**，讲的是"一次打捞的意外发现"。讲"已经完成/发生在过去某个时刻之前的事"，英语最自然的做法是**过去完成时（had + 过去分词）**。如果要掩盖"谁做的"、突出"发生了什么"，就用**被动语态（be + 过去分词）**。本文几乎每句都在这两个框架里。

  | 结构 | 课文例句 | 用途 |
  |---|---|---|
  | **had been + doing** | *which **had been searching** the Barents Sea for weeks* | 过去完成进行：过去某时之前一直在做 |
  | **had been + 过去分词** | *A radio message **had been received** by the ship's captain* | 过去完成被动：过去某时之前已被…… |
  | **was/were + 过去分词** | *The sea bed **was scoured** with powerful nets* | 一般过去被动：动作发生在过去，不知/不强调谁做的 |
  | **must have been** | *the ship **must have been** a cruiser* | 对过去的推测：一定曾是…… |
  | **it was still possible to do** | *parts of which **it was still possible** to read* | 形式主语 + 不定式：仍然可以…… |

  ### ⚠️ 4 个本课最容易踩的雷区

  1. **had been searching** 不是“被搜索”（被动），而是"一直在搜索"（过去完成**进行**）。区分：*had been searched*（被动）vs. *had been searching*（主动进行）。
  2. **must have been** 表推测，不是义务。"它一定是巡洋舰"≠"它必须成为巡洋舰"。must + 完成式 = 对过去的推断。
  3. **be of great interest**：不要把 of 丢了写成 *be great interest（错！）。固定搭配是 be of + 抽象名词 = be + 形容词。
  4. **被动语态的时态要配套**：*was scoured*（一般过去被动）vs. *had been received*（过去完成被动）——看哪个动作更早。search 和 receive 都发生在 captain decided to try 之前，所以用过去完成被动；scour 发生在 decided 之后，所以用一般过去被动。
</Callout>

## 4. 难点：易混词辨析 Special difficulties

<Callout type="info" title="本课 Special difficulties 训练点">
  教材 Key structures 和 Special difficulties 共 7 项训练，核心是**时态辨析**和**近义词区分**：

  ### 1. had been doing vs. had been working（过去完成进行）
  **had been doing** 强调"过去某时之前一直在做"（持续/未完成）；**had been working** 同型，只是动词换成了 work。造句练习：
  - *The team **had been working** on the project for two years before the funding ran out.*
  - *She **had been studying** French for five years when she moved to Paris.*

  ### 2. receive vs. take
  **receive** = 收到（被动接受，不涉及有意去拿）；**take** = 拿去、带走（主动获取）。例句：
  - *I **received** a letter from my brother yesterday.*（收到——信是被送到我这儿的）
  - *Could you **take** this package to the post office?*（拿去——你要动手带过去）

  ### 3. cloth / clothes / clothing
  | 词 | 词性 | 意思 | 例句 |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | **cloth** | n. (不可数/可数) | 布，布料；作可数时指"抹布" | *This **cloth** is very soft.*（这块布很软） |
  | **clothes** | n. (永远复数) | 衣服（穿在身上的） | *She bought some new **clothes**.* |
  | **clothing** | n. (不可数，集合名词) | 衣物（总称，正式/泛指） | *We took warm **clothing** for the trip.* |

  ### 4. bring / take / fetch
  | 词 | 方向 | 例句 |
  |---|---|---|
  | **bring** | 带来（往说话人方向） | *Please **bring** your book to class.* |
  | **take** | 带走（离开说话人方向） | *I'll **take** the children to school.* |
  | **fetch** | 去取来（往返一趟） | *Go and **fetch** me a glass of water.* |

  ### 5. realize vs. understand
  **realize** = 意识到、突然明白（之前不知道/没注意的事）；**understand** = 理解、懂（可能一直知道）。例句：
  - *I suddenly **realized** I had left my keys at home.*（恍然大悟）
  - *I **understand** the problem now — you explained it very clearly.*（理解了）

  ### 6. contents 的重音
  教材 SD 93 问：*the contents of the chest* 中 **contents** 怎么重读？——重音在**第一音节**：**CON**tents /ˈkɒntents/。注意不要和 content /kənˈtent/（满足的；内容——单数、后重）混淆。
</Callout>

## 11. 作文 Composition (≤ 250 词)

<Callout type="info" title="任务">
  In **not more than 250 words** write the page of *Karen*'s log book which was dated **March 14th, 1943**. Use the ideas given below. Do not write more than **four paragraphs**.

  **Title:** The Last Day
</Callout>

<Callout type="warning" title="提示骨架">
  - **Introduction**（第 1 段）：Journey has gone well so far — convoy successfully fought off an air attack — early morning — no ships lost.
  - **Development**（第 2 段）：10 a.m. First attack by U-boat — ship ahead, the *Dauntless* sunk — men in sea — *Karen* picked up survivors — 720 men — 50 lives lost — crowded on board.
  - **Further development**（第 3 段）：Attack on U-boat — puts it out of action with depth charges.
  - **Conclusion**（第 4 段）：3.15 p.m. (last entry) — second U-boat attack ...
</Callout>

<Callout type="tip" title="写作脚手架">
  - **时态主线**：航海日志记录的是当天发生的事，用**一般过去时**为主，偶尔用过去完成时交代此前的情况。
  - **航海词汇库**：*convoy*（护航队）、*U-boat*（德军潜艇）、*depth charges*（深水炸弹）、*survivors*（幸存者）、*picked up*（救起）、*put out of action*（使其瘫痪）、*on board*（在船上）、*crowded*（拥挤的）。
  - **结构亮点**：日志体写法——用第一人称复数（we），短句为主（日志不是小说），每段以"时间 + 事件"开头，营造紧迫感。
</Callout>

<Callout type="tip" title="参考范文（自己写完再看）">
  **March 14th, 1943 — The Last Day**

  *0600 hours.* The convoy has made good progress. We successfully fought off an air attack at dawn — the gunners did well and not a single ship was lost. Morale is high.

  *1000 hours.* Disaster. A U-boat struck without warning. The *Dauntless*, sailing just ahead of us, took a torpedo amidships and went down in minutes. We lowered boats at once and managed to pull 720 men from the sea, but 50 souls were lost. The ship is terribly crowded now — men are lying on every deck — but we dare not slow down.

  *1130 hours.* We located the U-boat on sonar and launched depth charges. After the third run, oil and wreckage came to the surface. The submarine is out of action. The men gave a cheer, though it was a grim one.

  *1515 hours.* Second U-boat attack — torpedo approaching from starboard. We are turning hard. God help us.

  *(约 150 词 — 不到 250，保持日志简洁感。)*
</Callout>

## 12. 书信写作 Letter writing (约 100 词)

<Callout type="info" title="任务">
  In about **100 words** write the **unfinished letter** referred to in the passage which was written on **March 14th, 1943** by a **ship's officer**.
</Callout>

<Callout type="tip" title="写作脚手架">
  - **称呼**：*My dearest + 名*（战时的家信，语气深情）。
  - **开头**：报告近况 + 地点模糊化（军人写信不会透露具体位置）—— *We are somewhere in northern waters ...*

## 13. 拓展练习 Going further

<Callout type="tip" title="自学者建议">
  - **影子跟读 (shadowing)**：用第 1 节的三个 SayIt 按钮跟读 3-5 遍。注意 *had been carrying* / *was scoured* / *must have been* 这类过去完成/被动结构中，助动词 had/was/were 通常弱读，过去分词重读。
  - **复述 (retell)**：合上课文，用 2 分钟把故事讲一遍。**三步推演**：寻找黄金船失败 → 意外捞到另一艘 → 拼凑出战争历史。试着用课文里的关键短语串故事：*had been a failure → give up the search → under the impression that → proved them wrong → be of great interest → must have been → piece together → come to light*。
  - **造句迁移**：用今天的 5 个高价值结构造你自己的句子——
    1. *had been doing*（过去完成进行）—— *He had been waiting for over an hour when the train finally arrived.*
    2. *be of great + n.*（= be very + adj.）—— *This document is of great importance to the investigation.*
    3. *must have been*（对过去的推测）—— *The street is wet — it must have rained during the night.*
    4. *under the impression that*（以为……）—— *I was under the impression that the meeting was at three, not two.*
    5. *prove + obj. + adj.*（证明……是……）—— *The test results proved the theory correct.*
  - **延伸思考**：本课是一篇**叙事文**——讲的是"目标落空，却另有收获"的故事。埃尔科号**没有**找到黄金，却揭开了二战一段被遗忘的历史。叙事语言的精妙之处在于**时态管理和被动语态**：过去完成时建立时间层次（先搜后返、先装货后沉没），被动语态让叙述焦点始终落在"物品/事件"上而非"谁做的"（*was scoured / were brought / were sent*）。另一个值得学习的技巧是**反转结构**：*at first under the impression that ... proved them wrong* 是英语叙事里制造转折的经典句式——先建立一种期待，再打破它。
</Callout>
