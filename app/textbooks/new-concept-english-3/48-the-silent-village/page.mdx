  旅游攻略和社交网络上，到处都在说"外国人/外地人热情好客"——真的是这样吗？本课作者带着妻子在巴尔干半岛旅行，偏偏走进了这样一个村子：路是破的、房子是泥砖的、迎面而来的是……沉默——一种令人脊背发凉的沉默。没有欢迎、没有问候、孩子们围着你不出声、男人们怒视着你。读完之后你再体会标题里的 silent 这个词——它装着抗拒、隔绝，还有被现代化遗忘后的孤独。

  **Why was the village silent?** 带着这个问题读课文，重点留意作者如何用**独立主格 (the only sign of life being ...)**、**过去完成时 (had seemed deserted)** 和一系列视觉细节（grey mud bricks, children in rags, black shawls）营造出一幅"似乎被世界遗忘了"的画面。
</Callout>

## The Text 课文

In this much-travelled world, there are still thousands of places which are **inaccessible** to tourists. We always assume that villagers in remote places are friendly and **hospitable**. But people who are **cut off** not only from foreign tourists, but even from their own countrymen can be **hostile** to travellers. Visits to really remote villages are seldom enjoyable — as my wife and I discovered during a tour through the Balkans.

We had spent several days in a small town and visited a number of old churches in the **vicinity**. These attracted many visitors, for they were not only of great architectural interest, but contained a large number of beautifully preserved **frescoes** as well. On the day before our departure, several bus loads of tourists **descended on** the town. This was more than we could bear, so we decided to spend our last day exploring the countryside. Taking a path which led out of the town, we crossed a few fields until we came to a dense wood. We expected the path to end **abruptly**, but we found that it **traced its way** through the trees. We tramped through the wood for over two hours until we arrived at a deep stream. We could see that the path continued on the other side, but we had no idea how we could get across the stream. Suddenly my wife spotted a boat **moored** to the bank. In it there was a boatman **fast asleep**. We gently woke him up and asked him to **ferry** us to the other side. Though he was reluctant to do so at first, we eventually persuaded him to take us.

The path led to a tiny village **perched on** the steep sides of a mountain. The place consisted of a **straggling** unmade road which was lined on either side by small houses. Even under a clear blue sky, the village looked **forbidding**, as all the houses were built of grey mud bricks. The village seemed deserted, **the only sign of life being** an ugly-looking black goat on a short length of rope tied to a tree in a field nearby. Sitting down on a **dilapidated** wooden fence near the field, we opened a couple of tins of **sardines** and had a picnic lunch. All at once, I noticed that my wife seemed to be filled with alarm. Looking up I saw that we were surrounded by children in **rags** who were looking at us silently as we ate. We offered them food and spoke to them kindly, but they remained **motionless**. I concluded that they were simply **shy of** strangers. When we later walked down the main street of the village, we were followed by a silent **procession** of children. The village which had seemed deserted, immediately **came to life**. Faces appeared at windows. Men in shirt sleeves stood outside their houses and **glared** at us. Old women in black **shawls** **peered** at us from doorways. The most frightening thing of all was that not a sound could be heard. There was no doubt that we were unwelcome visitors. We needed no further warning. Turning back down the main street, we **quickened** our pace and made our way rapidly towards the stream where we hoped the boatman was waiting.

## 课文注释 Notes on the text

<Callout type="info" title="10 个值得停下来嚼一嚼的点">
  1. **much-travelled** (l.1) — "经常有游客往来的"。much + 过去分词 = 构词法，表"经常被……的"。同型：*much-loved*（深受喜爱的）、*much-discussed*（讨论很多的）、*much-needed*（急需的）。

  2. **be inaccessible to ...** (l.1) — "是……难以到达的"。access → accessible（可进入的）→ inaccessible（不可进入的）。前缀 in- = 否定。课文 *places which are inaccessible to tourists*——"游客到不了的地方"。同型：*The cave is inaccessible to anyone without climbing equipment.*

  3. **be cut off from ...** (l.3-4) — "与……隔绝"。主动 cut off = 切断、隔断；被动 be cut off = 被隔离。课文用 **not only ... but even ...** 层层递进：不仅与外国游客隔绝，连与本国同胞都隔绝了。同型：*The village was cut off from the rest of the country by heavy snow.*

  4. **descend on** (l.9) — "突然到来、突然袭击"。descend 原义"下降"，这里比喻游客像乌云或鸟群一样"突然降落/涌入"。同型：*Hundreds of reporters descended on the small town after the news broke.*

  5. **Taking a path which led out of the town, we crossed ...** (ll.11-12) — **现在分词短语 Taking ... 作状语**，其逻辑主语是主句主语 we。which 引导定语从句修饰 path。整个结构 = "沿着……的小路走，我们穿过了……"。同型：*Following the river, we eventually reached the lake.*

  6. **trace its way** (l.13) — "延伸、蜿蜒穿过"。trace 在这里 = "循着某条线走"，是一个生动的文学表达。同型：*The stream traced its way through the meadow.*

  7. **fast asleep** (l.16) — "熟睡、沉睡"。fast = 牢固地、紧紧地（不是"快"）。同型：*The baby was fast asleep in the cradle. / He was fast asleep when the phone rang.* 注意：**不能说 very asleep**——fast 在这里是固定搭配，起强调作用。

  8. **the only sign of life being an ugly-looking black goat** (ll.21-22) — **独立主格结构**（名词 + 现在分词），作伴随状语，补充说明 village seemed deserted 的状态。把它还原成两个句子就是：*The village seemed deserted. The only sign of life was an ugly-looking black goat ...* 独立主格让两个意思合进一句话，读起来更紧凑流畅。同型：*The meeting being over, we all went home. / Nobody having any more questions, the class ended.*

  9. **be shy of ...** (l.27) — "怕……、对……害羞（或畏怯）"。shy 在此不是一般的"害羞"，更偏向"畏怯、不敢靠近"。课文：*they were simply shy of strangers* = 他们只是怕生人。同型：*The deer is shy of humans and will run at the slightest sound.*

  10. **come to life** (l.29) — "苏醒过来、恢复生气"。注意动词用 **come**，不是 go 或 get。课文用它形成强烈对比：前面 *seemed deserted*（似乎荒芜无人）→ 后面 *immediately came to life*（立刻活了过来）。同型：*The city comes to life after dark. / When the music started, the party came to life.*
</Callout>

## 3. 关键句型 独立主格与过去完成时 Absolute construction & past perfect

<Callout type="tip" title="一分钟回顾">
  本课是一篇叙事文，有两组语法结构值得停下来嚼透：**独立主格**（用来在一句话里附加"伴随状态"）和 **过去完成时**（用来讲"在此之前的状态"）。

  | 结构 | 课文例句 | 用途 |
  |---|---|---|
  | **独立主格（名词 + 现在分词）** | *The village seemed deserted, **the only sign of life being** an ugly-looking black goat.* | 伴随状语：附加说明"荒芜"的状态 |
  | **过去完成时 (had + 过去分词)** | *The village **which had seemed** deserted, immediately came to life.* | 在 came 之前的状态——先"看似荒芜"，然后"活了过来" |
  | **过去完成时 + 一般过去时对比** | *We **had spent** several days ... and **visited** ...* | 先待了几天（过去完成），然后参观了教堂（一般过去）——先发生的用过去完成时 |
  | **现在分词短语作状语** | ***Taking** a path which led out of the town, we crossed ...* | 伴随动作，逻辑主语是主句的 we |

  ### ⚠️ 本课最容易踩的 3 个雷区

  1. **独立主格不是完整的句子**：*the only sign of life being a goat* 本身没有谓语动词（being 是非谓语），它是一个短语，用来伴随主句。漏掉逗号或把它当完整句子写都会错。
  2. **过去完成时必须有参照点**：*had seemed deserted* 之所以用过去完成时，是因为它发生在 *came to life* 之前。没有参照点就不能凭空用 had done。
  3. **fast asleep** 里的 fast 不是"快"，是"牢固地、沉沉地"——**不能说 very asleep**。同型固定搭配：*fast asleep / sound asleep / wide awake*。
</Callout>

## 4. 难点：过去完成时与 "Though" 句改写 Special difficulties

<Callout type="info" title="教材 Key structures 改写题">
  教材要求：在**不改变意思**的前提下改写下面这句（参照 ll.26-27）：

  > Though the village had seemed deserted, it immediately came to life.
  > The village ...

  **解题钥匙**：把 though 从句的信息压缩成一个**定语从句**嵌入主语中——"那个之前看似荒芜的村子"。过去完成时 had seemed 保留，因为它表达"先于 came 之前的状态"。

  > The village **which had seemed** deserted immediately came to life.

  ### 过去完成时的 3 条规则

  1. 过去完成时 = **had + 过去分词**。它必须有一个过去的**参照时间点**，表达"在那之前已经……"。
  2. 本课三个过去完成时的例子：*we **had spent** several days*（在决定去乡间探索之前）、*The village **had seemed** deserted*（在 came to life 之前）、*They **had first gone** through the village*（在 came out to greet 之前——参见 MCQ 第 3 题的干扰项）。
  3. 如果两个过去的动作按时间顺序先后发生，先发生的可以只用一般过去时（按顺序叙述），不一定非要用过去完成时；但用过去完成时更能强调"先后"。本课两种都有出现。
</Callout>

  按下面的提示点串成一段（仅用最后一段的信息）：

  1. 他们是怎么到达村子的？（path led to a tiny village perched on the steep sides）
  2. 村子看起来如何？（straggling unmade road, grey mud bricks, looked forbidding, seemed deserted）
  3. 他们在哪里吃午饭？发生了什么？（dilapidated fence, tin of sardines, surrounded by silent children in rags）

  A path led the author and his wife to a tiny village perched on a mountainside. ## 11. 作文 Composition (约 300 词)

<Callout type="info" title="任务">
  Write a composition in about **300 words** on one of the following:

  **a.** Describe any village you know well.

  **b.** Continue the above passage. Imagine the boatman was **not** waiting for the author and his wife. Describe what happened.
</Callout>

<Callout type="warning" title="提示骨架（以 b 题为例）">
  - **开头 (焦虑的开端)**：气喘吁吁赶到溪边——*arrived at the stream, breathless → the bank was empty, the boat had gone*。
  - **心理阶段 1——否认**：*perhaps he pulled it further downstream → we searched frantically along the bank*。
  - **心理阶段 2——恐惧蔓延**：*the sun was setting fast → the silent children were still there → the thought of spending the night in that hostile village was unbearable*。
  - **转折——妻子出主意**：*she remembered the boat had been moored to a low branch → maybe the boat drifted away, not that the boatman abandoned us → we followed the stream*。
  - **发现与解脱**：*half a mile down we found the boat — the boatman had simply fallen asleep again, drifting gently downstream → we called out, he woke, grinned sheepishly, and ferried us back*。
  - **结尾 (回味)**：*crossing the stream, I looked back. The village was already lost in twilight — as silent as ever. I wondered if it had ever known a different sound.*
</Callout>

<Callout type="tip" title="写作脚手架">
  - **时态主线**：全篇用**一般过去时**叙事（arrived, searched, remembered, found, crossed）。过去完成时仅用于需要明确"先后"的地方（*had drifted / had fallen asleep*）。
  - **情绪词库**：*breathless, frantic, unbearable, deserted, hostile, silent, gloomy, twilight*。
  - **动作词库**：*scramble down, peer into the dusk, drift downstream, call out, haul in, push off, dip the oars, glance back*。
  - **结构亮点**：用**场景对比**制造张力——溪这边是沉默村庄的围困，溪那边是小镇旅馆的安全（哪怕只是心中想象）。用**感官细节**（the sound of water、the creak of oars）打破前文"not a sound"的恐惧。
</Callout>

<Callout type="tip" title="参考范文（自己写完再看）">
  We scrambled down to the stream, our hearts thumping. The bank was empty. The boat had gone. For a moment neither of us spoke. Then my wife whispered, "Perhaps he pulled it further down." We searched frantically along the bank, pushing through reeds and low branches. Nothing. The sun was dropping behind the mountain, and the children, who had followed us in their silent procession, stood watching from the edge of the village.

  "We cannot spend the night here," my wife said, and I knew she was right. The thought of darkness falling on those grey mud houses, on those staring faces, was unbearable.

  Then she grabbed my arm. "The boat was moored to a branch — a low one. If the rope came loose, it might have drifted." We ran downstream, stumbling over rocks. Half a mile on, round a bend, we saw it: the boat, caught against a fallen tree. And there, curled in the bottom, was the boatman — fast asleep again, as if the whole affair had never happened.

  We called out. He woke, blinked, and grinned a toothless, sheepish grin. In ten minutes we were across. As he pushed off to return, I glanced back. The village was already swallowed by twilight, as silent as ever. I wondered whether it had ever heard a different sound — whether, in all its centuries perched on that mountainside, it had ever welcomed a stranger's voice. *(约 230 词)*
</Callout>

## 12. 书信写作 Letter writing (约 100 词)

<Callout type="info" title="任务">
  Write a letter in **not more than 100 words** to **a very old man congratulating him on the occasion of his eighty-fifth birthday**.
</Callout>

<Callout type="tip" title="写作脚手架">
  - **称呼**：*Dear Mr. + 姓 / Dear Grandfather*（如果是亲戚）。
  - **开头**：祝贺 + 点明场合 —— *I am writing to send you my warmest congratulations on your eighty-fifth birthday.*
  - **主体（贺寿的内容）**—— 3 个角度：
    1. 赞美其一生——*a lifetime of hard work, kindness, and quiet wisdom*。
    2. 回忆一件具体小事（选一个）——*I still remember the stories you told me as a child / the garden you tended so lovingly*。
    3. 表达敬意——*an inspiration to everyone who knows you*。
  - **收尾**：祝愿健康继续 + 再次祝福 —— *May the coming year bring you good health and many happy moments. Happy birthday once again.*
  - **结束语**：*With warmest regards, / Yours sincerely, / [你的名字]*。
  - **语气关键**：respectful, warm, personal——对一位 85 岁老人，不要用太 casual 的语言；但也不要过于正式以至于像公函。
</Callout>

<Callout type="tip" title="参考范文（自己写完再看）">
  Dear Mr. Thornton,

  I am writing to send you my warmest congratulations on your eighty-fifth birthday. Eighty-five years is a lifetime of experience — of work well done, of kindness quietly given, and of wisdom gathered day by day.

  I still remember the summer afternoons I spent as a child in your garden, listening to your stories while you tended the roses. You taught me more about patience and care in those afternoons than any book ever could. You have been an inspiration to everyone lucky enough to know you.

  May the year ahead bring you good health, quiet pleasures, and many happy moments with those you love.

  Happy birthday once again.

  Yours sincerely,

  David *(约 120 词 —— 略超但内容得体，考试约 100 词可缩去第 2 段的具体回忆)*
</Callout>

## 13. 拓展练习 Going further

<Callout type="tip" title="自学者建议">
  - **影子跟读 (shadowing)**：用第 1 节的三个 SayIt 按钮跟读 3-5 遍。注意独立主格 *the only sign of life being* 在朗读时语调略降，表明它是伴随信息而非主句重点；*not a sound could be heard* 结尾下降，收束有力。
  - **复述 (retell)**：合上课文，用 2 分钟把作者的遭遇按"出发→过溪→进村→被围→逃离"的时间线讲一遍。重点是复述时用上这些词：**much-travelled, inaccessible, hostile, descend on, trace its way, fast asleep, straggling, forbidding, dilapidated, came to life, quickened our pace**。
  - **造句迁移**：用今天的 5 个高价值结构造你自己的句子——
    1. **独立主格**：*The lesson finished, we all ran out of the classroom.* / *The rain having stopped, we set off again.*
    2. **with 复合结构**：*She stood there, with her eyes fixed on the horizon.*
    3. **过去完成时 + 一般过去时**：*He had never seen the sea before he moved to the coast.*
    4. **descend on**：*Reporters descended on the tiny village after the news broke.*
    5. **more than one can bear**：*The noise was more than I could bear, so I left.*
  - **延伸思考**：本课最让人回味的地方是**标题与内容的落差**——标题叫"沉默的村庄"，但作者一句话也没有解释"为什么沉默"。是因为贫困？是历史原因？是对外来者的普遍敌意？这种**留白**给读者留下了巨大的想象空间。下次你写叙事文时，试着用这种手法：不直接说"这里很穷"或"这里很排外"，而是用泥砖房、破栅栏、无声的凝视这些**具体细节**让读者自己得出判断。另外注意课文结尾——作者逃离了，但读者心里的问题没有逃离：那个沉默的村庄和它的人们，后来怎么样了？好的叙事结尾，往往不是答案，而是一个会让你回味的问题。
</Callout>
