## The Text 课文

We have been brought up to fear insects. We regard them as unnecessary creatures that **do more harm than good**. We continually **wage war on** them, for they contaminate our food, carry diseases, or devour our crops. They sting or bite **without provocation**; they fly uninvited into our rooms on summer nights, or beat against our lighted windows. We **live in dread** **not only of** unpleasant insects like spiders or wasps, **but of** quite harmless ones like moths. Reading about them increases our understanding without dispelling our fears. Knowing that the industrious ant lives in a highly organized society **does nothing to prevent** us from being filled with revulsion when we find hordes of them crawling over a carefully prepared picnic lunch. No matter how much we like honey, or how much we have read about the uncanny sense of direction which bees possess, we **have a horror of** being stung. Most of our fears are unreasonable, but they are **impossible to erase**. At the same time, however, insects are strangely fascinating. We enjoy reading about them, especially when we find that, like the praying mantis, they lead perfectly horrible lives. We enjoy staring at them, **entranced as they go about their business**, unaware (we hope) of our presence. Who has not stood in awe at the sight of a spider pouncing on a fly, or a column of ants triumphantly bearing home an enormous dead beetle?

Last summer I spent days in the garden watching thousands of ants crawling up the trunk of my prize peach tree. The tree has grown against a warm wall on a sheltered side of the house. I am especially proud of it, **not only because** it has survived several severe winters, **but because** it occasionally produces luscious peaches. During the summer, I noticed that the leaves of the tree were beginning to wither. Clusters of tiny insects called aphids were to be found on the undersides of the leaves. They were visited by a large colony of ants which obtained a sort of honey from them. I immediately **embarked on** an experiment which, **even though it failed** to get rid of the ants, kept me fascinated for twenty-four hours. I bound the base of the tree with sticky tape, **making it impossible for the ants to reach** the aphids. **The tape was so sticky that they did not dare to cross it.** For a long time, I watched them scurrying around the base of the tree in bewilderment. I even went out at midnight with a torch and noted with satisfaction (and surprise) that the ants were still swarming around the sticky tape without being able to do anything about it. I got up early next morning hoping to find that the ants **had given up in despair**. Instead, I saw that they had discovered a new route. They were climbing up the wall of the house and then on to the leaves of the tree. I realized sadly that I **had been completely defeated by their ingenuity**. The ants **had been quick to find an answer** to my thoroughly unscientific methods!

## 课文注释 Notes on the text

<Callout type="info" title="6 个值得停下来嚼一嚼的点">
  1. **do more harm than good** (l.2) — \"弊大于利、害多益少\"。固定对照结构 *more A than B* = \"与其说 B 不如说 A\"。同型：*do more good than harm*（利大于弊）/ *He is more shy than unfriendly.*（与其说他不友好，不如说他害羞）。
  2. **wage war on / against ...** (l.3) — \"向……开战、对……发动战争\"。**wage** 是\"进行、开展（运动 / 战争）\"。课文用它写\"人类对昆虫的持久战\"——夸张又传神。同型：*wage war on poverty / wage a campaign against crime*。
  3. **without provocation** (l.4) — \"无缘无故地、未受任何挑衅地\"。**provocation** 名词\"挑衅、刺激\"，动词 *provoke*。同型：*attack without provocation*（无端攻击）/ *He lost his temper without the slightest provocation.*
  4. **be filled with revulsion** (ll.9-10) — \"满心厌恶、感到强烈反感\"。**revulsion** = 强烈的厌恶（比 dislike 重得多）。课文：*being filled with revulsion when we find hordes of them ...*——看到一大群蚂蚁爬满野餐时那种生理性的反胃。
  5. **go about one's business** (l.14) — \"忙自己的事、各干各的\"。**go about + 名词 / V-ing** = \"着手做、忙于\"。课文 *as they go about their business* = \"在它们各忙各的时候\"。同型：*go about your work / go about doing it the wrong way*。
  6. **stand in awe at the sight of ...** (l.15) — \"看到……而肃然起敬 / 惊叹不已\"。**awe** = 敬畏 / 惊叹；**at the sight of** = 一看到……。这句用**反问** *Who has not ...?* = \"谁没有……过呢？\"（人人都有过）——是很有感染力的修辞。同型：*stand in awe of the great mountains.*
</Callout>

## 3. 关键句型 so ... that 与平行结构 Key structures

<Callout type="tip" title="一分钟回顾">
  本课 Structure 题和 Key structures 改写题，集中在 **\"把两句话合并成一句\"** 的几种手法上。把它们排成一张表，写作时就有了\"长句工具箱\"：

  | 结构 | 课文 / 教材例句 | 用途 |
  |---|---|---|
  | **so + 形容词 + that ...** | *The tape was **so** sticky **that** they did not dare to cross it.* | 把\"程度\"和\"结果\"连成一句（本课改写题答案） |
  | **not only ... but (also) ...** | *I am proud of it **not only because** it has survived ..., **but because** it ... produces luscious peaches.* | 平行并列两个理由 / 两个对象 |
  | **形式宾语 it** | *we find **it** impossible to erase them* | *find / think / make + it + 形容词 + to do*；真正宾语是后面的不定式 |
  | **despite / in spite of + 名词** | *an experiment which, **despite its** failure ..., kept me fascinated* | 把让步从句 (*even though it failed*) 压缩成介词短语 |
  | **省略助动词的平行否定** | *we may increase our understanding but **this will not** (dispel our fears)* | 后半句承前省略 dispel，用 will not 顶上 |
  | **had + been + 形容词** | ***The ants had not been** long to find an answer ...* | 过去完成时改写 *were quick* |

  ### ⚠️ 4 个本课最容易踩的雷区

  1. **so ... that vs such ... that**：**so** 后接**形容词 / 副词**（so sticky / so quickly）；**such** 后接**名词（短语）**（such a sticky tape）。本课是 *so sticky that*。
  2. **形式宾语 it 不能省**：*find impossible to erase them* ✗ → 必须 *find **it** impossible to erase them* ✓。这个 it 指代后面的不定式。
  3. **despite / in spite of 后只接名词，不接从句**：*despite its failure* ✓ / *despite it failed* ✗（要接句子得用 *although / even though*）。
  4. **平行结构两端词性 / 形式要对称**：*not only **because** ... but **because** ...*（两个 because 从句对齐）；*sting **or** bite*；*crawling ... **and** bearing ...*——天平两端必须\"同形\"。
</Callout>

## 4. 难点：cannot help doing 与\"形近词陷阱\" Special difficulties

<Callout type="info" title="一个句型 + 三对最坑的形近词">
  本课 Vocabulary 题考的不是\"生词\"，而是**用词的精确**。两块硬骨头：

  **① cannot help + V-ing = 禁不住、忍不住（做某事）**

  - 教材第 9 题：*we cannot **help being** filled with revulsion ...*（我们禁不住一阵反胃）。
  - 这里的 **help = avoid / prevent**（避免），所以后接**动名词**，不接不定式。
  - 同型：*I couldn't help laughing.*（我忍不住笑了）/ *She can't help worrying about it.*
  - ⚠️ 别和 *help (sb.) to do sth.*（帮忙做）混了——那个 help 是\"帮助\"，后接不定式。

  **② 三对\"长得像、意思差很远\"的形近词——本课全是出题点：**

  | 看着像 | 其实是 | 区别记忆 |
  |---|---|---|
  | **industrious** 勤劳的 | **industrial** 工业的 | 课文 *industrious ant* 是\"勤劳的蚂蚁\"，不是\"工业蚂蚁\" |
  | **ingenuity** 机智 / **ingenious** 巧妙的 | **ingenuous** 天真坦率的 | 第 12 题陷阱：*ingenuous insects* = \"天真的昆虫\"（错），蚂蚁是 **ingenious / inventive** |
  | **considerable** 相当大的 | **considerate** 体贴的 | 程度 vs 性格——一个量大，一个心善 |

  ### ⚠️ 再加两个本课词义辨析

  1. **uncanny** = 神秘的、不可思议的（≈ mysterious / weird）——课文 *uncanny sense of direction* 指蜜蜂\"神乎其神\"的方向感。别理解成 \"can not\" 的字面拆解。
  2. **luscious** = 甘美多汁的（≈ sweet and juicy）——专形容果实 / 食物味道，不是\"新鲜 (fresh)\"也不是\"异国的 (exotic)\"。
</Callout>

## 10. 摘要写作 Summary writing (≤ 80 词)

<Callout type="info" title="任务">
  *In not more than 80 words* describe **what the writer saw and did when he tried to prevent the ants from climbing up his peach tree**. **Use your own words as far as possible. Do not include anything that is not in the last paragraph**（只用最后一段的内容）。

  把最后一段的事件串成一段——按\"动手 → 观察 → 自以为赢 → 翻盘\"的顺序：

  1. He noticed aphids on the leaves, milked by a colony of ants.
  2. What did he wind round the base of the trunk?
  3. Why couldn't the ants cross it?
  4. What did he see when he checked at midnight with a torch?
  5. What did he expect to find next morning?
  6. What had the ants actually done instead?
  7. How did he feel about the result?
</Callout>

<Callout type="tip" title="参考范文（自己写完再看）">
  Noticing aphids on his peach tree, the writer bound the trunk with sticky tape so that the ants could not reach the leaves. The tape was too sticky to cross, and at midnight he found them still swarming helplessly around it. Next morning, expecting them to give up, he was dismayed to see they had found a new route up the wall straight to the leaves. Their ingenuity had completely defeated him. *(70 words)*
</Callout>

## 11. 作文 Composition (约 300 词)

<Callout type="info" title="任务">
  Write a composition in **about 300 words** on one of the following:

  - **a.** Write an account of **any insect you have read about or observed**.
  - **b.** Describe **man's efforts to control pests**.
</Callout>

<Callout type="warning" title="提示骨架（以 b 为例，分三层展开）">
  - **开篇：人与害虫的\"战争\"** —— 为什么人类要 control pests？(contaminate food, spread disease, ruin crops)。
  - **传统手段** —— pesticides / insecticides 喷洒；优点（见效快）+ 代价（污染环境、误杀益虫、农药残留）。
  - **\"军备竞赛\"困境** —— 害虫产生 resistance（抗药性），药越用越猛，问题越治越复杂——呼应本课蚂蚁\"另辟蹊径\"的 ingenuity。
  - **现代 / 生态思路** —— biological control（引入天敌）、crop rotation、integrated pest management；与自然\"共处\"而非\"剿灭\"。
  - **收尾：一点反思** —— 昆虫的适应力提醒我们：彻底\"战胜\"自然几乎不可能，明智的是\"管理\"而非\"消灭\"。
</Callout>

<Callout type="tip" title="写作脚手架">
  - **时态主线**：以**一般现在时**陈述普遍事实（man wages war on pests）；举具体例子 / 历史时用**一般过去**；谈趋势用 *have begun to / are turning to*。
  - **\"防治\"动词库**：*combat, control, eradicate, exterminate, keep ... in check, ward off, repel, deter*。
  - **\"代价\"词库**：*side effects, residue, contamination, upset the balance of nature, build up resistance, harm beneficial insects*。
  - **\"生态\"词库**：*biological control, natural predators, crop rotation, integrated pest management, eco-friendly, sustainable*。
  - **结构亮点**：用 *No matter how ... , ...*（无论……都……）或 *The more we ... , the more they ...*（我们越……它们越……）来写\"军备竞赛\"的循环——既呼应课文 *No matter how much we ...* 的句式，又点出主题。
</Callout>

<Callout type="tip" title="参考范文（自己写完再看）">
  Ever since man began to farm the land, he has waged a relentless war on pests. Insects contaminate our food, spread disease and devour our crops, and so we have always looked for ways to keep them in check. For much of the last century, our chief weapon was the chemical one. Powerful insecticides were sprayed over fields and orchards, and for a time the results seemed miraculous: whole swarms could be wiped out overnight.

  Yet this victory proved costly. The poisons did not stay where they were put. They seeped into rivers, lingered in the soil, and killed the bees and ladybirds that do us nothing but good. Worse still, the pests fought back. No matter how strong the spray, a few survivors always remained, and their offspring inherited a resistance to it. The more chemicals we used, the tougher the insects became — an arms race we could not win.

  Today many farmers are turning to wiser methods. Instead of trying to exterminate every insect, they aim to manage them. Natural predators are encouraged, crops are rotated to break the breeding cycle, and chemicals are used only as a last resort. This approach, known as integrated pest management, works with nature rather than against it.

  The lesson is a humbling one. Insects have survived for millions of years precisely because of their extraordinary adaptability. As the ants on the peach tree showed, block one route and they will simply find another. We may never defeat them outright; the most we can sensibly hope to do is hold them in balance — and that, in the long run, is far cleverer than all-out war. *(about 290 words)*
</Callout>

## 12. 书信写作 Letter writing (约 100 词)

<Callout type="info" title="任务">
  A friend took some amusing photographs of you when you were on holiday together some time ago. Write him a letter of **about 100 words** asking him **what he has been doing since then** and **requesting that he send you copies of the photographs** he took.
</Callout>

<Callout type="tip" title="写作脚手架">
  - **称呼**：写给朋友用名字 —— *Dear Tom,*
  - **开头**：寒暄 + 回忆那次假期 —— *It seems ages since we ... I was just looking back on our holiday in ...*
  - **主体三步**：
    1. **问近况**：*What have you been up to since then?* / *How have things been going with you?*（**现在完成进行 have been doing** 是本信的时态主线）。
    2. **提照片**：*Do you remember those ridiculous photographs you took of me ...?*
    3. **提出请求**（语气要委婉）：*I'd be very grateful if you could send me copies.* / *Would you mind sending me a few prints?*（**request that he (should) send** 是正式说法；朋友间用 could / would mind 更自然）。
  - **结尾**：*Do write back soon. / Looking forward to hearing from you. / Best wishes,*
  - **请求句型库**：*I'd be grateful if ... / I wonder if you could ... / Would you mind ...ing / Could you possibly ...*
</Callout>

<Callout type="tip" title="参考范文（自己写完再看）">
  Dear Tom,

  It seems ages since we got back from Cornwall, and I have been meaning to write to you ever since. What have you been up to all this time? I have started a new job at a bookshop and have been far too busy, but I often think of that wonderful fortnight by the sea.

  Do you remember the ridiculous photographs you took of me falling off the surfboard? I would love to keep them. I'd be really grateful if you could send me a few copies whenever you have a moment.

  Do write back soon and tell me all your news.

  Best wishes,

  Jack *(about 105 words)*
</Callout>

## 13. 拓展练习 Going further

<Callout type="tip" title="自学者建议">
  - **影子跟读 (shadowing)**：用第 1 节的三个 SayIt 按钮跟读 3-5 遍。重点感受作者那种\"半敬畏半好笑\"的语气——尤其是结尾的感叹句 *The ants had been quick to find an answer to my thoroughly unscientific methods!*，*thoroughly* 和 *unscientific* 上要带一点自嘲的重音。
  - **复述 (retell)**：合上课文，用 2 分钟讲一遍\"人虫斗法\"。**叙事三段**：观点（人怕虫，却又觉得虫子着迷）→ 实验（发现蚜虫 → 缠胶带 → 半夜查看 → 自以为赢）→ 翻盘（蚂蚁另找新路 → 我被机智打败）。
  - **造句迁移**：用今天的 5 个核心结构造你自己的句子——
    1. *so + 形容词 + that ...*（如此……以致……）—— *The box was so heavy that I could not lift it.*
    2. *not only ... but (also) ...*（不仅……而且……）—— *She is proud of the house not only because it is old but because it is hers.*
    3. *cannot help + V-ing*（禁不住做……）—— *I couldn't help laughing at his joke.*
    4. *make it impossible for sb. to do ...*（使某人无法做……）—— *The noise made it impossible for me to concentrate.*
    5. *had been + 过去分词 (被动)*（在那之前就已被……）—— *By the time help arrived, the village had been completely destroyed.*
  - **延伸思考**：标题 *Instinct or cleverness?* 是个**没有给出答案的问题**——这正是文章的妙处。蚂蚁绕开胶带，究竟是写进基因的\"本能\"，还是临场的\"机智\"？作者故意不下结论，把判断留给读者。写英文议论 / 记叙文时，**结尾抛一个开放式问题**，往往比把话说尽更有力量。另一个可迁移的点：本文是\"先立观点、再用一个亲历小实验来印证\"的经典结构——**抽象论断 + 具体故事**，让说理既有高度又落地，这是写说明 / 议论文非常好用的骨架。
</Callout>
