## The Text 课文

Small boats **loaded with wares** sped to the great liner as she was entering the harbour. Before she had anchored, the men from the boats had climbed on board and the decks were soon covered with colourful rugs from Persia, silks from India, copper coffee pots, and beautiful handmade silverware. It was difficult not to be tempted. Many of the tourists on board had begun bargaining with the tradesmen, but I decided not to buy anything until I had disembarked.

I **had no sooner got off** the ship than I was assailed by a man who wanted to sell me a diamond ring. I had no intention of buying one, but I could not conceal the fact that I was impressed by the size of the diamonds. Some of them were **as big as marbles**. The man **went to great lengths** to prove that the diamonds were real. As we were walking past a shop, he held a diamond firmly against the window and made a deep impression in the glass. It took me over half an hour **to get rid of him**.

The next man to approach me was selling expensive pens and watches. I examined one of the pens closely. It certainly looked genuine. At the base of the gold cap, the words 'made in the U.S.A.' had been neatly inscribed. The man said that the pen was worth £50, but **as a special favour**, he would let me have it for £30. I shook my head and held up five fingers indicating that I was willing to pay £5. **Gesticulating wildly**, the man acted as if he found my offer outrageous, but he eventually reduced the price to £10. Shrugging my shoulders, I began to walk away when, a moment later, he ran after me and thrust the pen into my hands. Though he kept **throwing up his arms in despair**, he readily accepted the £5 I gave him. I felt especially pleased with my wonderful bargain — until I got back to the ship. **No matter how hard I tried**, it was impossible to fill this beautiful pen with ink and to this day it has never written a single word!

## 课文注释 Notes on the text

<Callout type="info" title="5 个值得停下来嚼一嚼的点">
  1. **no sooner ... than ...** (l.9) —— “一……就……”。no sooner 引导的从句用**过去完成时** (had got off)，than 后面的主句用**一般过去时** (was assailed)。如果 no sooner 放在句首，要**倒装**：*No sooner had I got off the ship than I was assailed ...* 同型：*He had no sooner sat down than the phone rang. / No sooner had we arrived than it began to rain.*

  2. **go to great lengths to do sth.** (l.11) —— “不遗余力做某事、想尽一切办法”。lengths 用复数，表示“程度、地步”。同型：*She went to great lengths to keep the party a secret. / The company went to great lengths to satisfy the customer.*

  3. **get rid of** (l.13) —— “摆脱、甩掉”。rid 是及物动词，过去式 rid / ridded。注意 of 后接被摆脱的对象。同型：*I can't get rid of this headache. / We finally got rid of the old furniture.*

  4. **as a special favour** (l.16) —— “作为一种特殊优惠”。favour 这里的意思是“恩惠、好意”，do sb. a favour（帮某人忙）。小贩用这个说法来制造“我给你面子才便宜”的假象——典型的推销话术。

  5. **throw up one's arms in despair** (l.19) —— “绝望地高举双臂”。throw up 不是“呕吐”而是“猛地举起”；in despair = 绝望地。这个动作是戏精小贩的最后一幕——嘴上说亏死了，手上却痛快地接过了 5 英镑。
</Callout>

## 3. 关键句型 no sooner ... than 与时间 / 比较结构 Narrative time & comparison

<Callout type="tip" title="一分钟回顾">
  本课是一篇**第一人称叙事**——作者用一连串**过去时态**（一般过去 / 过去完成 / 过去进行）把两次“被缠住→脱身”的经历讲得像电影镜头。语法重点有三组：**no sooner ... than**（一……就……）、**as ... as 比较**、以及 **it takes ... to do**（花时间）。

  | 结构 | 课文例句 | 用途 |
  |---|---|---|
  | **no sooner had ... than** | *I **had no sooner got off** the ship **than** I was assailed ...* | “一……就……”，no sooner 从句用过去完成，than 后接一般过去 |
  | **as ... as** | *Some of them were **as big as** marbles.* | 同级比较（= 一样大） |
  | **not as ... as** | *It was **not as expensive as** I thought.* | 否定同级比较（= 不如……） |
  | **While / As + 过去进行** | ***As** we **were walking** past a shop, he held ...* | 过去进行作时间背景："正走着……这时……" |
  | **It takes / took ... to do** | ***It took** me over half an hour **to get rid of** him.* | 花时间：It takes + 人 + 时间 + to do |
  | **No matter how ...** | ***No matter how** hard I tried, it was impossible ...* | 无论怎么……都……（= However hard I tried） |

  ### ⚠️ 3 个本课最容易踩的雷区

  1. **no sooner ... than** 的时态：no sooner 从句必须用**过去完成时**（had done），than 后主句用**一般过去时**。不能说 *No sooner I got off than ...*（漏 had + 语序错）。
  2. **as ... as 只能用原级**：*as big as*（对）、*as bigger as*（错）。否定用 *not as ... as* 或 *not so ... as* 都可以。
  3. **made in / made by / made of / made from** 四个介词各不相同：*made **in** China*（产地）、*made **by** hand*（制造者/方式）、*made **of** wood*（看得出原材料）、*made **from** grapes*（看不出原材料）。
</Callout>

## 4. 难点：no sooner ... than 倒装与 made 短语 Special difficulties

<Callout type="info" title="教材 Key structures 改写题">
  教材要求完成 6 组结构练习：

  ### 1. no sooner ... than 倒装 (ll.9)

  合并句子：*I opened the door. The telephone began to ring.*
  → **I had no sooner opened the door than the telephone began to ring.**
  或倒装：**No sooner had I opened the door than the telephone began to ring.**

  *He finished his speech. Everyone began to clap.*
  → **He had no sooner finished his speech than everyone began to clap.**

  **规则**：no sooner 从句用**过去完成**（had done），than 后接**一般过去**。no sooner 放句首时从句要**部分倒装**（had + 主语 + 过去分词）。

  ### 2. as ... as 与 not as ... as (l.11)

  例句：*The new model is **as fast as** the old one. / This pen is **not as good as** it looks.*
  **规则**：肯定用 **as + 原级 + as**；否定用 **not as / not so + 原级 + as**。

  ### 3. While / As + 过去进行 (l.12)

  *While I was working in the garden, my neighbour called to me.*
  *I was just going into the shop when I met an old friend.*
  **规则**：过去进行（was/were doing）作时间背景，when 引出突然发生的主事件。

  ### 4. It takes / It has taken ... to do (l.13)

  *It takes about an hour to walk to the station.*（一般现在，表常态）
  *It has taken three years to complete the project.*（现在完成，强调"从过去到现在已花的时间"）

  ### 5. made in / made by / made of / made from (l.15)

  | 短语 | 含义 | 例句 |
  |---|---|---|
  | **made in** | 产地（在哪制造） | *This car was made in Germany.* |
  | **made by** | 制造者（谁/什么造的） | *The chair was made by a local carpenter.* |
  | **made of** | 原材料（看得出） | *The table is made of oak.* |
  | **made from** | 原材料（看不出） | *Paper is made from wood.* |

  ### 6. up till now / so far (l.22)

  *I've read five chapters up till now.* / *So far, it hasn't written a single word.*
  **规则**：两者都表示"到目前为止"，常与**现在完成时**连用。
</Callout>

## 10. 摘要写作 Summary writing (≤ 80 词)

<Callout type="info" title="任务">
  *In not more than 80 words* describe **the writer's experiences after he had got rid of the diamond seller**. **Do not include anything that is not in the last paragraph**（只用最后一段的信息——钢笔小贩的故事）。

  按下面的提示点串成一段：

  1. 谁来了？（卖钢笔和手表的）
  2. 作者的初始态度？（仔细查看，觉得像真的）
  3. 小贩的要价与作者的还价？（£50 → £30 → £10 → £5）
  4. 小贩的“表演”？（愤怒 → 降价 → 追上来塞笔 → 高举双臂……但痛快收钱）
  5. 结局？（笔根本不能用！）
</Callout>

<Callout type="tip" title="参考范文（自己写完再看）">
  After getting rid of the diamond seller, the writer was approached by a man selling expensive pens and watches. He examined a pen that looked genuine, with "made in the U.S.A." inscribed on its gold cap. The seller asked £50, then offered it for £30 as a "special favour." The writer indicated he would pay £5. After much gesticulating and pretended outrage, the seller reduced the price to £10 and, when the writer walked away, ran after him and accepted £5. Back on the ship, the writer discovered it was impossible to fill the pen with ink. To this day it has never written a word. *(80 words)*
</Callout>
## 11. 作文 Composition (≤ 250 词)

<Callout type="info" title="任务">
  Write an imaginary account in **not more than 250 words**, **mainly in dialogue form**, of the scene that took place between the writer and the man who sold him the pen. Use the ideas given below.

  **Title:** The bargain

  **Introduction:** Man approached with pens and watches — held them up — writer showed interest.

  **Development and Conclusion:** Writer asked to see a pen — man handed him one — argument about the price — gradually reduced to £10 — writer walked away — man followed — made it clear that he was being robbed but accepted £5. Writer pleased, but man disappeared quickly.
</Callout>

<Callout type="warning" title="提示骨架">
  - **开头（接近）**：用对话开头——小贩的叫卖声，展示商品
  - **发展（看货→讨价）**：作者要看看笔 → 要价 £50 → 作者还 £5 → "你在开玩笑！" → £30 → £10 → "不，就 £5"
  - **高潮（walk away）**：作者耸耸肩走开 → 小贩追上来塞笔 → "你在抢我！"
  - **结尾（成交→消失）**：作者得意付钱 → 小贩迅速消失在人群中
  - **对话比例**：至少 60% 的内容是对白
</Callout>

<Callout type="tip" title="写作脚手架">
  - **时态主线**：全篇用**一般过去时**叙事；对话中用**一般现在/将来/情态动词**（与真实对话一致）。
  - **“砍价”词库**：*offer, bargain, haggle, cut the price, special favour, a fair price, you're robbing me, I'm not giving it away*。
  - **“动作描写”词库**：*hold up, wave about, shrug, walk away, thrust into one's hands, slip away, grin to oneself*。
  - **结构亮点**：对话中穿插动作描写——小贩每说一句价钱都配一个手势，作者每还一句价都配一个表情或动作，形成"台词+舞台提示"的节奏。
</Callout>

<Callout type="tip" title="参考范文（自己写完再看）">
  **The Bargain**

  "Pens! Watches! The finest in all the East, sir — come, take a look!"

  The man held up a handful of shining pens, waving them about so the gold caps caught the sun. I slowed my step. One pen, in particular, caught my eye.

  "May I see that one?"

  "Ah, an excellent choice!" He handed it to me with a little bow. "Made in America, sir. Solid gold cap. Worth fifty pounds anywhere in the world."

  I turned the pen over in my hands. The inscription was neat, the weight felt right. "Fifty? That's far too much. I'll give you five."

  "Five!" His eyes widened and he stepped back as if I had struck him. "You are joking with me, sir. This is a quality instrument. I could not possibly — thirty, then. As a special favour to you."

  "Five," I repeated.

  Gesticulating wildly, he rolled his eyes heavenwards. "Sir, you are robbing me! But very well — ten pounds, and I am losing money."

  I shrugged my shoulders and turned to walk away. I had not gone three steps before I heard his sandals slapping behind me.

  "Wait! Wait, sir!" He thrust the pen into my hands, threw up his arms in despair, and sighed dramatically. "Take it for five. May Allah forgive me — I am a ruined man."

  I handed over the five-pound note, feeling extraordinarily pleased with myself. What a bargain! But before I could even look up to thank him, the man had melted into the crowd, moving with surprising speed for someone who had just been "ruined." I would not understand why until later. *(249 words)*
</Callout>
## 12. 书信写作 Letter writing (约 100 词)

<Callout type="info" title="任务">
  You have heard that a friend of yours wishes to sell his CD player. Write him a letter of about **100 words** in **three paragraphs**. Express interest in the machine and ask him to tell you about its condition and how much he wants.
</Callout>

<Callout type="tip" title="写作脚手架">
  - **称呼**：*Dear + 名*（朋友间用名字，亲切）。
  - **第一段**：听说他要卖 CD 机 + 表达兴趣 —— *I heard from Tom that you're thinking of selling your CD player. I've been looking for one, so I might be interested.*
  - **第二段**：询问状况 —— 用多久了？有没有毛病？什么型号？遥控器、说明书还在吗？ —— *Could you tell me a bit more about it? How old is it? Is everything in working order?*
  - **第三段**：问价格 + 期待回复 —— *And of course — how much are you asking for it? Let me know when it's convenient.*
  - **结束语**：*Best wishes, / All the best, / [你的名字]*。
  - **时态要点**：用一般现在和现在完成（不是过去叙事）。语气友好、自然。
</Callout>

<Callout type="tip" title="参考范文（自己写完再看）">
  Dear Jack,

  I heard from Sarah the other day that you're thinking of selling your CD player. As it happens, I've been meaning to get one for a while, so I might well be interested.

  Before I decide, could you tell me a little more about it? I'd like to know how long you've had it, what model it is, and whether everything is still in good working order. Has it ever needed any repairs, and do you still have the remote control and the original instructions?

  And of course — the all-important question: how much are you hoping to get for it? Just give me a ring or drop me a line when you have a moment.

  All the best,

  Alex *(about 105 words)*
</Callout>
## 13. 拓展练习 Going further

<Callout type="tip" title="自学者建议">
  - **影子跟读 (shadowing)**：用第 1 节的三个 SayIt 按钮跟读 3-5 遍。注意节奏——*I had no sooner got off the ship than ...* 读起来几乎没有停顿，一整个从句一气呵成；*Gesticulating wildly* 开头的句子用分词作状语，读的时候分词短语后面要有一个微停顿，像电影镜头切过去一样。
  - **复述 (retell)**：合上课文，用 2 分钟把这场"港口遇小贩"的经历讲一遍。**三个故事节拍**：①下船被钻石贩子缠住 → ②划玻璃证明 + 花半小时甩掉 → ③钢笔贩子上场 → 从 £50 砍到 £5 → 得意洋洋 → 回到船上发现笔是废的。注意最后的"反转"（直到今天它一个字也没写出过）是全文的笑点和教训。
  - **造句迁移**：用今天的 5 个高价值结构造你自己的句子——
    1. *no sooner ... than ...*（一……就……）—— *She had no sooner hung up the phone than it rang again.*
    2. *go to great lengths to do*（不遗余力做）—— *My parents went to great lengths to give me a good education.*
    3. *get rid of*（摆脱）—— *I finally got rid of the old sofa that had been in the garage for years.*
    4. *as a special favour*（作为一种特别优惠）—— *As a special favour, the landlord agreed to let us stay an extra week.*
    5. *No matter how ...*（无论怎么……）—— *No matter how carefully I pack, something always breaks in transit.*
  - **叙事技巧分析**：本文两次用同一个叙事套路——“被小贩缠住 → 砍价/推拉 → 摆脱/成交”——但一次是**失败**（钻石：花了半小时才甩掉）、一次是**表面胜利、实际失败**（钢笔：砍到了 £5，但笔是假的）。两段形成“反复 + 升级”的结构：第一次讲“怎么都甩不掉的烦”，第二次讲“以为赢了结果被坑”的幽默反转。下次写叙事文，可以考虑用这种“两次事件、一正一反”的结构来制造层次和结尾的 punchline。
</Callout>
