  新年下决心这事儿，谁没做过？每一年我们都咬牙切齿列一张“要做”和“不做”的清单——早起、少吃、多读书、对不喜欢的人笑脸相迎……然后没过两周，一切打回原形。本课作者以自嘲式的幽默，把自己两桩“朴素得不能再朴素”的新年决议从头到尾演给你看：**每天早晨锻炼 11 分钟 + 每晚多读点书**。结果呢？11 分钟缩成零；书摊在膝盖上，人却在电视机前睡着了。
  **What marked the end of the writer's New Year resolutions?** 带着这个问题读课文。读的时候重点留意全篇是怎样用 **过去时叙事 + 现在完成时收尾** 织出“决心→行动→溃败→嘴硬”这段完整弧线的，同时留意那些让你会心一笑的英式自嘲表达：*the same old favourites recur year in year out with monotonous regularity*、*I soon got back to my old bad habit of dozing off in front of the screen*。
</Callout>

## The Text 课文

The New Year is a time for resolutions. Mentally, at least, most of us could compile formidable lists of 'dos' and 'don'ts'. The same old favourites **recur year in year out** with **monotonous regularity**. We resolve to get up earlier each morning, eat less, find more time to play with the children, do a thousand and one jobs about the house, be nice to people we don't like, drive carefully, and take the dog for a walk every day. Past experience has taught us that certain accomplishments are **beyond attainment**. If we remain **inveterate** smokers, it is only because we have so often experienced the frustration that results from failure. Most of us fail in our efforts at **self-improvement** because our schemes are too ambitious and we never have time to **carry them out**. We also make the fundamental error of announcing our resolutions to everybody so that we look even more foolish when we **slip back into** our bad old ways. Aware of these **pitfalls**, this year I attempted to **keep my resolutions to myself**. I limited myself to two **modest** ambitions: to do physical exercises every morning and to read more **of an evening**. An all-night party on New Year's Eve provided me with a good excuse for not carrying out either of these new resolutions on the first day of the year, but on the second, I **applied myself assiduously to** the task.

The daily exercises lasted only eleven minutes and I proposed to do them early in the morning before anyone had got up. The **self-discipline** required to drag myself out of bed eleven minutes earlier than usual was considerable. Nevertheless, I managed to creep down into the living room for two days before anyone found me out. After jumping about on the carpet and twisting the human **frame** into uncomfortable positions, I sat down at the breakfast table in an exhausted condition. **It was this that betrayed me.** The next morning the whole family **trooped in** to watch the performance. That was really **unsettling**, but I **fended off** the **taunts** and **jibes** of the family **good-humouredly** and soon everybody got used to the idea. However, my enthusiasm **waned**. The time I spent at exercises gradually **diminished**. **Little by little** the eleven minutes fell to zero. By January 10th, I was back to where I had started from. I argued that if I spent less time exhausting myself at exercises in the morning, I would keep my mind fresh for reading when I got home from work. Resisting the **hypnotizing** effect of television, I sat in my room for a few evenings with my eyes glued to a book. One night, however, feeling cold and lonely, I went downstairs and sat in front of the television pretending to read. That proved to be my **undoing**, for I soon got back to my old bad habit of **dozing off** in front of the **screen**. I still haven't given up my resolution to do more reading. In fact, I have just bought a book entitled *How to Read a Thousand Words a Minute*. Perhaps it will solve my problem, but I just **haven't had time to read it**!

## 课文注释 Notes on the text

<Callout type="info" title="12 个值得停下来嚼一嚼的点">
  1. **dos and don'ts** (l.2) — “该做的和不该做的事”。**dos** 是 do 的复数，don'ts 是 don't 的复数，口语里做名词用。同型：*Before you travel, learn the local dos and don'ts.* 注意拼写：do 直接 + s → dos；don't + s → don'ts（已有 apostrophe，再加 s，不再加一个）。

  2. **year in year out** (l.3) — “年复一年地”（= year after year, every year without change）。常含“一成不变 / 单调”的意味。同型：*Year in year out, he sits at the same desk doing the same job.*

  3. **a thousand and one** (l.5) — 字面是“一千零一”，实指“非常多的、数不清的”（= countless, a great many）。同型：*I've got a thousand and one things to do before the trip.* 相当于中文“八百件事”。

  4. **beyond attainment** (l.8) — “达不到的、无法实现的”。**beyond + 名词** = 超出……的范围。attainment = 达到、成就。同型：*Perfection is beyond attainment.* / *The task was beyond my ability.*

  5. **carry out** (l.10) — “执行、实施、落实”（a plan / an order / a resolution）。同型：*We must carry out the plan without delay.* / *He failed to carry out his duties.*

  6. **slip back into our bad old ways** (ll.11-12) — “滑回到那些坏的老习惯中去”。**slip back into** 暗含“不知不觉、身不由己地退回去”，比简单说 return to 更形象。同型：*After two smoke-free months, he slipped back into his old habit.*

  7. **keep ... to oneself** (l.12) — “把……保密 / 不告诉别人”。**keep my resolutions to myself** = 把决心埋在自己心里（不对外宣布）。同型：*She keeps her problems to herself.* / *I'd be grateful if you'd keep this to yourself.*

  8. **of an evening** (l.14) — “在晚上”（= **in the evenings**），这个用法带有**习惯性**的意味（“每个晚上习惯性地”），比较老派英式，多见于书面。是教材 Special difficulties 重点句型。同型：*He likes to read of a Sunday.* / *What do you do of an evening?*（你晚上一般干什么？）

  9. **apply oneself to** (l.15) — “专心致志于、全力以赴做……”。同型：*If you apply yourself to your studies, you will pass the exam.* / *He applied himself to learning French.*

  10. **It was this that betrayed me.** (l.21) — “正是这个暴露了我。”这是 **It is / was ... that ... 强调句型**。被强调的 **this** 指的是前面“满头大汗、精疲力竭地坐到早餐桌前”这件事。全句去掉 It was 和 that 还原为：*This betrayed me.* 这种强调句是本课的重点语法。

  11. **fend off** (l.23) — “挡开、避开、抵挡”（攻击 / 问题 / 嘲笑）。同型：*He fended off the reporters' questions with a smile.* / *She managed to fend off the criticism.*

  12. **doze off** (l.29) — “打瞌睡、打盹儿”。比 fall asleep 轻，指“不自觉的小睡”。同型：*I dozed off in the middle of the lecture.* / *She kept dozing off in front of the fire.*
</Callout>

</Callout>

## 3. 关键句型 强调句、习惯表达与连接词 Key structures

<Callout type="tip" title="一分钟回顾">
  本课是一篇幽默自叙文，语法点散落在叙事细节里，但有三样值得专门挑出来练：**It is ... that ... 强调句**（l.21）、**“of a + 时间名词”表习惯**（l.14）、以及 **by + 动名词** 改写 if 从句（l.25-26）。同时教材 Structure 部分还考了比较从句省略（*than we would*）和 **such ... that** 结构。

  | 结构 | 课文例句 | 用途 |
  |---|---|---|
  | **It is / was ... that ...** | *It was this that betrayed me.* | 强调句：突出“正是这个暴露了我” |
  | **of a / an + 时间名词** | *to read more of an evening* | 表习惯性时间（= in the evenings） |
  | **by + 动名词** | *by spending less time ... I would ...* | 代替 if 从句，表“通过……方式” |
  | **than we would** | *look even more foolish than we would* | 比较从句省略（省去重复的 look） |
  | **such ... that** | *twisted myself into such uncomfortable positions that I sat down exhausted* | 如此……以至于 |

  ### ⚠️ 4 个本课最容易踩的雷区

  1. **It was this that betrayed me.** —— “this” 不是指“跳来蹦去”，而是指“满头大汗坐到早餐桌前”这整件事。去掉 It was / that 还原——*This betrayed me.* 判断强调句是否正确的办法：把 It is / was 和 that 同时拿掉，看句子是否依然完整。
  2. **of an evening ≠ of the evening** —— *of an evening* = *in the evenings*（每个晚上，习惯性），是固定用法，不能说 *of the evening*。类似：*of a Sunday* = *on Sundays*。这个用法偏老派英式书面语。
  3. **than we would** 后面省略了什么？—— *we look even more foolish than we would [look] when we slip back.* 省略的是重复的动词 look。这是比较状语从句的常规省略。
  4. **by spending** 改写 if 从句时，“by + doing” = “通过做……”，语气比 if 更直接，主语必须一致：*I argued that by spending less time ..., I would keep my mind fresh.*
</Callout>

## 4. 难点：of a / an 习惯时间表达 & perhaps / may 改写 Special difficulties

<Callout type="info" title="教材 Special difficulties 要点">
  本课 Special difficulties 聚焦两个点：

  ### 1. “of a / an + 时间名词” 表习惯

  - *of an evening* = *in the evenings*（每个晚上习惯性地）
  - *of a Sunday* = *on Sundays*（每个星期天习惯性地）
  - *of a morning* = *in the mornings*
  
  这是比较老派、偏英式书面的表达，表示 **习惯性的、通常的** 时间。不能说 *of the evening*（定冠词错）。注意：它不表“某个特定晚上”。

  ### 2. Perhaps 改写 may

  教材 Key structures 改写题（ll.31-32）：
  > 原句：*It may solve my problem, but I just haven't had time to read it!*
  > 改写：*Perhaps it will solve my problem, but ...*

  规则：**may + 动词原形**（表“也许”）→ **Perhaps + 主语 + will + 动词原形**。may 被副词 Perhaps 替代后，谓语用 will（将来时），不用 may 重复，也不可用一般现在时。

  ### 3. “by + 动名词” 代替 if 从句

  原文：*I argued that if I spent less time exhausting myself ..., I would keep my mind fresh ...*
  改为：*I argued that **by spending** less time exhausting myself ..., I would keep my mind fresh ...*

  **by + doing** 表达“通过做某事（的方式）”，比 if 从句更简洁直接。条件：by 的逻辑主语必须与主句主语一致。
</Callout>

## 11. 作文 Composition (约 300 词)

<Callout type="info" title="任务">
  Write a composition in about **300 words** on one of the following:

  **a.** Write an account of resolutions you have made in the past and failed to keep.

  **b.** Describe New Year celebrations in your country.
</Callout>

<Callout type="warning" title="提示骨架（以 a 题为例）">
  - **开头（hook）**：像课文一样用一个自嘲的句子开场——*Every January 1st, I become a different person for about eleven days.*
  - **第一次决心 + 失败**：……年下的什么决心 → 怎么做的 → 怎么崩的（具体一件小事/一个画面最有效）。
  - **第二次决心 + 失败**：换一个领域（比如学习 / 省钱 / 社交）→ 又是怎么“高开低走”的。
  - **反思**：为什么总失败？是决心太大（*too ambitious*）？还是像课文说的——宣布了出去，然后更难堪（*announcing to everybody*）？还是根本就是 *the same old favourites recur year in year out*？
  - **结尾（callback）**：呼应课文的那种自嘲嘴硬——*Next year, I've decided not to make any resolutions at all. That should be one I can keep.*
</Callout>

<Callout type="tip" title="写作脚手架">
  - **时态主线**：叙事主体用**一般过去时**（做了什么）+ **过去完成时**（决心之前已经……）；反思用一般现在时（谈普遍道理）。
  - **决心词库**：*resolve to ..., make a resolution, swear off (戒掉), take up (开始从事), turn over a new leaf (翻开新的一页), stick to (坚持)*。
  - **失败词库**：*fall by the wayside (半途而废), slip back into, lose steam (泄气), fizzle out (虎头蛇尾收场), within a fortnight (不到两周)*。
  - **结构亮点**：模仿课文的叙事节奏——先用概括段铺背景（像第一段），再选一两个具体场景展开（像第二段），最后来一句点睛的自嘲收尾。
</Callout>

<Callout type="tip" title="参考范文（自己写完再看）">
  Every January 1st, I become a different person — for about eleven days. My resolutions are invariably the same old favourites, and they recur with a regularity that is, if I am honest, more comic than admirable.

  The most memorable was the year I resolved to learn the guitar. I bought an expensive instrument, three method books, and a subscription to an online course. For the first week I practised religiously every evening, my fingers growing raw on the steel strings. By day ten I could almost play three chords. By day fourteen the guitar stood in the corner of my room, silently reproaching me. By February it had acquired a fine layer of dust. I told myself I was simply too busy — the same excuse the writer of our lesson used for not reading his speed-reading book.

  The following year I decided to stop making grand resolutions and chose something modest: drink more water. Six glasses a day. I downloaded an app, set reminders, bought a litre bottle to keep on my desk. For two weeks I was a model of hydration. Then one afternoon I forgot the bottle at home. The next day the app notification annoyed me, so I swiped it away. Within a month the bottle was being used to water the plants.

  Looking back, I suspect my failures follow the exact pattern described in the lesson: my schemes are too ambitious and, even when modest, I lack the self-discipline to carry them out. I also make the fundamental error of telling everyone what I am going to do. So this year I have made only one resolution, and I am keeping it entirely to myself. It is to make no resolutions at all. That, surely, is one I can keep. *(约 285 词)*
</Callout>

## 12. 书信写作 Letter writing (约 100 词)

<Callout type="info" title="任务">
  Before you went abroad, you promised your parents that you would write once a week. Since then, you have failed to keep your promise. Write a letter to them explaining why it has been impossible for you to write more often. **Do not write more than 100 words.**
</Callout>

<Callout type="tip" title="写作脚手架">
  - **称呼**：*Dear Mum and Dad*（给父母，亲切）。
  - **开头（认错 + 缘由）**：点题——我知道我答应过每周写信，但实在没做到。*I know I promised to write once a week, and I am sorry I haven't kept that promise.*
  - **主体（解释原因，2-3 点）**：
    1. 学业 / 工作太忙——*Lectures run from nine till six, and by the time I get home I am too exhausted to put pen to paper.*
    2. 时差 / 周末各种意料之外的事——*Weekends disappear in a blur of laundry, shopping, and trying to explore a bit of the city.*
    3. （可选）每次想写都不知道从哪说起——*Every time I sat down to write, I had so much to say that I didn't know where to begin.*
  - **收尾（保证改进 + 爱意）**：*I will try to do better — starting with this letter. I miss you both very much.*
  - **结束语**：*With love, / [你的名字]*。
  - **时态要点**：承诺用过去完成时 *had promised* / 过去时 *promised*；解释用现在时 / 现在完成时；保证用将来时 *will*。
</Callout>

<Callout type="tip" title="参考范文（自己写完再看）">
  Dear Mum and Dad,

  I know I promised before I left that I would write once a week, and I am truly sorry that I haven't kept my word. Please don't think it means I haven't been thinking of you.

  The truth is that life here has been far busier than I expected. Lectures and assignments fill my days from morning till evening, and by the time I get back to my room I am often too exhausted to put pen to paper. Weekends, which I imagined would be quiet, disappear in a rush of laundry, shopping, and trying to see something of the city. Every time I sat down meaning to write, I found I had so much to say that I didn't know where to begin.

  I will do better — and I am starting with this letter. Write soon and tell me all your news.

  With love,
  Chris *(约 100 词)*
</Callout>

## 13. 拓展练习 Going further

<Callout type="tip" title="自学者建议">
  - **影子跟读 (shadowing)**：用第 1 节的三个 SayIt 按钮跟读 3-5 遍。注意 *monotonous regularity*、*exhausted condition* 这些短语里重音落在名词上，而 *year in year out* 读起来像三个重音节拍——这种英式节奏感正是本课语言的一大特色。
  - **复述 (retell)**：合上课文，用 2 分钟把作者的“决心里程碑”讲一遍。**三步复盘**：下定决心（两项 modest 任务、保密不公开）→ 执行崩盘（锻炼 11 → 0，被家人发现）→ 嘴硬收尾（买了速读书但没时间看）。注意第三人称 + 过去时的转换。
  - **造句迁移**：用今天的 5 个高价值结构造你自己的句子——
    1. *keep ... to oneself*（把……保密）—— *She kept the good news to herself until the party.*
    2. *of an evening*（在晚上，习惯性）—— *Of a summer evening, the old man would sit on his porch and watch the fireflies.*
    3. *It was ... that ...*（强调句）—— *It was his sense of humour that got him through the hardest days.*
    4. *apply oneself to*（全力以赴）—— *If you apply yourself to learning a new language, half an hour a day is enough.*
    5. *slip back into*（不知不觉滑回）—— *After months of healthy eating, he slipped back into his old fast-food habits.*
  - **延伸思考**：这篇课文的厉害之处不在于讲了什么大道理——而是用**自嘲**（self-deprecation）把一件“人人都失败”的小事写成了让人一边读一边笑的好文章。全文的结构暗合一条弧线：**泛论（大家都这样）→ 个论（我也不例外）→ 行动（偷偷摸摸开干）→ 暴露（被家人围观）→ 溃败（归零）→ 嘴硬（买书但没时间看）**。下次你写英文自叙文时，试试这条弧线——在“失败”上收尾时，不必严肃反思；一个自嘲式的 callback（像 *How to Read a Thousand Words a Minute* 这种荒唐的书名 + 没时间看的悖论），往往比“我学到了什么”更打动人。
</Callout>
