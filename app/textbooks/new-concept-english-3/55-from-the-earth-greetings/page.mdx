  **Which life forms are most likely to develop on a distant planet?** 带着这个问题读课文，重点留意全篇是如何用 **虚拟 / 假设语气 (would have to be ...)** 和 **比较结构 (too ... for ... / as ... as / more ... than)** 来"推演"一个我们还没见过的世界。

## The Text 课文

Recent developments in astronomy **have made it possible** to detect planets in our own Milky Way and in other galaxies. This is a major achievement because, **in relative terms**, planets are very small and do not emit light. Finding planets is proving hard enough, but finding life on them will prove **infinitely more difficult**. The first question to answer is whether a planet **can actually support life**. In our own solar system, Venus is **far too hot** and Mars is **too cold** for there to be any life. Only the Earth provides ideal conditions, and even here it has taken more than four billion years for plant and animal life to evolve.

Whether a planet can support life depends on the size and brightness of its star, that is its 'sun'. Imagine a star up to twenty times larger, brighter and hotter than our own sun. A planet would have to be **a very long way from** it to be capable of supporting life. **Alternatively**, if the star were small, the life-supporting planet would have to have a close orbit round it and also provide the perfect conditions for life to develop. But how would we find such a planet? At present, there is **no telescope in existence that is capable of** detecting the presence of life. The development of such a telescope will be one of the great astronomical projects of the twenty-first century.
It is impossible to look for life on another planet **using earth-based telescopes**. Our own warm atmosphere and the heat generated by the telescope would make it impossible to detect objects **as small as** planets. Even a telescope in orbit round the earth, like the very successful Hubble telescope, would not be suitable because of the dust particles in our solar system. A telescope would have to be **as far away as** the planet Jupiter to look for life in outer space, because the dust becomes thinner the further we travel towards the outer edges of our own solar system. Once we detected a planet, we would have to find a way of **blotting out the light** from its star, so that we would be able to 'see' the planet properly and analyse its atmosphere. In the first instance, we would be looking for plant life, **rather than** 'little green men'. The life forms most likely to develop on a planet would be bacteria. **It is bacteria that have generated the oxygen** we breathe on earth. For most of the earth's history they have been the only form of life on our planet. As Earth-dwellers, we always **cherish the hope** that we will be visited by little green men and that we will be able to communicate with them. For the present, this hope is always in the realms of science fiction. If we were able to discover lowly forms of life like bacteria on another planet, it would completely change our view of ourselves. As Daniel Goldin of NASA observed, 'Finding life elsewhere would change everything. **No human endeavour or thought would be unchanged by it.**'

## 课文注释 Notes on the text

<Callout type="info" title="6 个值得停下来嚼一嚼的点">
  1. **make it possible to do ...** (l.1) — \"使做……成为可能\"。这里的 **it** 是**形式宾语**，真正的宾语是后面的 *to detect planets*。同型：*The internet has made it possible to work from home.* 教材 Structure 第 4 题就是这个句式的改写：*It has become possible **through** recent developments ... to ...*（through = 由于、借助）。

  2. **too ... for ... to do** (ll.6-8) — \"太……以至于（某人/某事）不能……\"。课文 *Venus is far too hot and Mars is too cold **for there to be** any life* —— 注意这里 for 后面是 **there to be**（\"there be\" 的不定式形式）。**far / much** 可放在 too 前加强程度。同型：*The box is too heavy for me to lift. / It was too dark for us to see anything.*

  3. **would have to + 动词原形** (ll.11-13) — **虚拟/假设语气**：\"（假如要……）就必须……\"。全篇推演一个还不存在的星球，所以反复用 **would have to be / would have to have**。它表达的是\"理论上的必要条件\"，不是已发生的事实。同型：*To survive there, a plant would have to store water for months.*

  4. **as ... as ...** (l.18) — 同级比较。课文两处：*objects **as small as** planets*（小到行星那种程度的物体）、*A telescope would have to be **as far away as** the planet Jupiter*（远到木星那么远）。结构是 **as + 形容词/副词原级 + as + 比较对象**。

  5. **the + 比较级 ..., the + 比较级 ...** 的变体 (ll.19-20) — *the dust becomes thinner **the further** we travel ...* —— \"我们越往外走，尘埃就越稀薄\"。这是\"越……越……\"句式的倒装变体（正常语序：*the further we travel, the thinner the dust becomes*）。同型：*The harder you work, the luckier you get.*

  6. **It is ... that ...** 强调句 (l.25) — ***It is** bacteria **that** have generated the oxygen we breathe* —— 强调主语 *bacteria*（正是细菌制造了氧气）。把 It is 和 that 去掉，句子依然完整：*Bacteria have generated the oxygen ...*。注意强调的是复数主语，所以从句谓语用 **have**。**rather than**（l.24）= \"而不是\"，连接两个并列成分。
</Callout>
## 参考译文 Chinese reference

<Callout type="tip" title="先读原文，遇到读不懂的句子再回来对照">
  天文学方面的最新发展使我们能够探测到银河系和其他星系中的行星。这是一个重大的成就，因为相对来讲，行星很小，而且自身不发光。寻找行星证明相当困难，但要在行星上发现生命就更加难上加难了。第一个需要回答的问题是：行星上是否有能维持生命的条件。例如，在我们的太阳系里，金星太热，火星又太冷，都不可能有生命。只有地球提供了理想的条件，而即使在地球上，植物界和动物界进化到今天也用了 40 亿年的时间。

  行星上是否有能维持生命的条件取决于其太阳——即恒星的大小和亮度。设想一下，一颗恒星比我们的太阳还要大、亮、热 20 倍。一颗行星就必须离它非常遥远才可能有维持生命的条件。换句话说，如果恒星较小，有维持生命条件的行星就必须有一条紧紧围绕它运行的轨道，并且提供恰到好处的生命发育条件。可是我们怎样才能找到这样一颗行星呢？目前还根本没有一台能探测到生命存在的望远镜。研制这样一台望远镜将成为 21 世纪天文学的伟大工程之一。

  利用陆基望远镜在另一颗行星上寻找生命是不可能的。我们自己温暖的大气层和望远镜散发的热量使我们无法探测到像行星那么小的物体。即便是一台围绕地球运行的望远镜——像非常成功的哈勃望远镜——也不适用，原因是我们太阳系里有尘埃微粒。望远镜必须像木星那么遥远才能到太空中去寻找生命，因为我们越往太阳系外缘走，尘埃就越稀薄。一旦我们探测到一颗行星，就必须设法挡住它的恒星射来的光，这样我们才能真正"看清"这颗行星并分析它的大气。首先，我们要寻找的是植物，而不是"小绿人"。行星上最有可能出现的生命形式将是细菌。正是细菌制造了我们在地球上呼吸的氧气。在地球史的大部分时间里，细菌曾是我们星球上唯一的生命形式。身为地球居民，我们总怀着希望，希望有一天会有"小绿人"来访，希望能与别的行星上像细菌那样的生命交流。但目前，这一希望还停留在科幻小说的境界里。如果我们能在另一颗行星上发现细菌之类的低等生命形式，那将彻底改变我们对自身的看法。正如美国国家航空和航天局的丹尼尔·戈尔丁所说："在其他地方发现生命会改变一切。任何人类的努力和想法都会因之而变。"
</Callout>

## 3. 关键句型 假设语气与比较结构 Hypothesis & comparison

<Callout type="tip" title="一分钟回顾">
  本课是一篇\"推演式\"说明文——作者在**假设**一个还没见过的星球，所以全篇靠两组结构撑起来：**假设语气 (would / would have to)** 和 **比较结构 (too ... to / as ... as / -er than)**。

  | 结构 | 课文例句 | 用途 |
  |---|---|---|
  | **would have to + V** | *A planet **would have to be** a very long way from it.* | 假设条件下\"理论上的必须\" |
  | **if + were, ... would** | *If the star **were** small, the planet **would have to have** a close orbit.* | 与现在/将来事实相反的虚拟条件 |
  | **too + adj + for ... to do** | *Venus is **too hot** ... **for there to be** any life.* | 太……以致不能…… |
  | **as + adj + as** | *objects **as small as** planets / **as far away as** Jupiter* | 同级比较 |
  | **比较级 + than** | *a star ... **larger, brighter and hotter than** our own sun* | 不同级比较 |
  | **the + 比较级 ...** | *the dust becomes thinner **the further** we travel* | 越……越…… |

  ### ⚠️ 4 个本课最容易踩的雷区

  1. **would have to be** 不是\"过去\"，而是**假设**——\"（如果要支持生命）就必须是……\"。它表"理论上的必要条件"，全篇没有一处是在讲已发生的事实。
  2. **too ... for there to be ...**：too + 形容词后，用 *for + 逻辑主语 + to do* 表\"谁/什么\"做不到；这里逻辑主语是虚指的 *there*，所以是 *for **there to be** any life*。
  3. **as small as / as far away as**：同级比较的框架是 **as + 原级 + as**，两个 as 不能少；中间永远是**原级**（small / far away），不是比较级。
  4. **far too hot**：**far / much / a lot** 可放在 too 之前加强语气（\"远远太热\"）；但 **very** 不能修饰 too（不能说 *very too hot*）。
</Callout>

## 4. 难点：as ... as 同级比较改写 Special difficulties

<Callout type="info" title="教材 Key structures 改写题">
  教材要求：在**不改变意思**的前提下改写下面这句（参照 ll.20-21）：

  > A telescope would have to be far away —— for instance round the planet Jupiter —— to look for life in outer space.
  > A telescope would ... as the planet Jupiter ...

  **解题钥匙**：把\"far away ... 比如绕木星那么远\"压缩成**同级比较 as ... as**——\"远到木星那么远\"。课文里原句正是这么写的：

  > A telescope would have to be **as far away as** the planet Jupiter to look for life in outer space.

  ### 同级比较 as ... as 的 3 条规则

  1. 框架是 **as + 形容词/副词原级 + as + 比较对象**；中间永远用**原级**（far away），不是比较级。
  2. 否定时用 **not as / not so + 原级 + as**：*Mars is **not as warm as** the Earth.*
  3. 表\"倍数\"时把倍数词放最前：*twice / three times **as big as***（是……的两倍/三倍大）。
</Callout>

## 10. 摘要写作 Summary writing (≤ 80 词)

<Callout type="info" title="任务">
  *In not more than 80 words* describe **what would have to be done to find life on a distant planet and what kind of life this might be**. **Do not include anything that is not in the last paragraph**（只用最后一段的信息）。

  按下面的提示点串成一段：

  1. 用什么样的望远镜才行？陆基望远镜为什么不行？
  2. 望远镜必须放到多远（为什么——尘埃）？
  3. 找到行星后，还得做什么（挡住恒星的光、分析大气）？
  4. 我们首先要找的是哪种生命（植物 / 细菌，而不是\"小绿人\"）？
  5. 这种生命在地球上扮演了什么角色（制造了氧气）？
</Callout>

<Callout type="tip" title="参考范文（自己写完再看）">
  Earth-based telescopes cannot find life, so a telescope would have to be placed as far away as Jupiter, where the dust is thinner. Once a planet was detected, we would have to blot out the light from its star and analyse its atmosphere. At first we would look not for 'little green men' but for plant life, especially bacteria — the simplest life forms, and the very organisms that generated the oxygen we breathe on earth. *(73 words)*
</Callout>
## 11. 作文 Composition (约 300 词)

<Callout type="info" title="任务">
  Write a composition in about **300 words** on one of the following:

  **a.** Extra-terrestrials（外星人）.

  **b.** Supposing the time came when we could **send pictures of life on earth to intelligent creatures on other planets**. What sorts of pictures do you think would be most interesting to them?
</Callout>

<Callout type="warning" title="提示骨架（以 b 题为例）">
  - **开头 (假设)**：Suppose a day came when we could beam images of earthly life across the galaxy ...
  - **第 1 类图片**：自然界——*the blue planet from space, oceans, forests, a single drop of water teeming with life*。
  - **第 2 类图片**：人类自身——*a newborn child, a crowded city at night, people of every colour and age*。
  - **第 3 类图片**：人类的创造——*music being played, a rocket launch, a library, children at school*。
  - **取舍标准**：哪些图片**不需要语言**也能看懂？哪些能传达\"我们是谁\"\"我们珍视什么\"？
  - **结尾 (升华)**：呼应课文 Goldin 那句——*contact would change everything; the pictures we choose would be our self-portrait to the universe.*
</Callout>

<Callout type="tip" title="写作脚手架">
  - **时态主线**：全篇用**假设语气**——*Suppose ... came; if we could send ...; the pictures would have to ...; they might find ... most interesting*。
  - **\"传递/展示\"动词库**：*beam, transmit, broadcast, send out, reveal, convey, capture, portray*。
  - **\"外星视角\"词库**：*intelligent creatures, distant observers, beings who share none of our senses, a civilization light-years away*。
  - **结构亮点**：用 *Suppose / Imagine + 过去式* 开篇（与课文 *Imagine a star ...* 同款假设笔法），瞬间把读者拉进\"思想实验\"。
</Callout>

<Callout type="tip" title="参考范文（自己写完再看）">
  Suppose the day came when we could beam pictures of life on earth across the galaxy to intelligent creatures on a distant planet. What would we choose to show them? Since they would share none of our languages, the pictures would have to speak for themselves.

  I think the first images should be of the earth itself — the blue planet seen from space, its oceans and forests, and a single drop of pond water swarming with invisible life. Such pictures would say, before any word was spoken, that ours is a living world. Bacteria, after all, are the most likely life forms anywhere, and a distant observer might recognise them at once.

  Next, I would send pictures of ourselves: a newborn child, a market crowded with people of every colour, an old couple holding hands. These would reveal not what we own but what we feel. I would add images of what we have made — a symphony being played, a rocket climbing into the night, a library, a classroom full of children. Together they would show a species that is curious, social and forever reaching beyond itself.

  I would leave out our wars and our waste, not to deceive, but because a self-portrait is a choice. The pictures most interesting to them might not be our skyscrapers at all, but the simplest things — water, light, and a child's face — for these need no translation.

  Whatever we sent, the act itself would change everything. As Daniel Goldin said, no human thought would be unchanged by contact with life elsewhere. The pictures we chose would be our message to the universe, and, just as truly, a mirror held up to ourselves. *(约 290 词)*
</Callout>
## 12. 书信写作 Letter writing (约 100 词)

<Callout type="info" title="任务">
  You borrow from a friend a **travel guide** which you are now returning. Write him or her a letter of about **100 words** **thanking** him or her for having lent it to you and saying **how useful you found it**.
</Callout>

<Callout type="tip" title="写作脚手架">
  - **称呼**：*Dear + 名*（朋友间用名字，亲切）。
  - **开头**：点明缘由 + 致谢 —— *Thank you so much for lending me your travel guide to ... — I'm returning it with this note.*
  - **主体（有用在哪）**——挑 2-3 点具体说：
    1. *The maps saved me from getting lost more than once.*
    2. *Its tips on small, cheap restaurants were far better than anything online.*
    3. *I'd never have found the old harbour without it.*
  - **收尾**：归还 + 再次致谢 + 回报之意 —— *I've slipped it back into your letterbox. Do borrow my camera any time in return.*
  - **结束语**：*Thanks again, / With love, / [你的名字]*。
  - **时态要点**：**thank you for having lent**（动名词完成式，强调\"先借给我\"这件已完成的事）；正文回顾旅途用一般过去时（*saved / found / was*）。
</Callout>

<Callout type="tip" title="参考范文（自己写完再看）">
  Dear Mark,

  Thank you so much for lending me your travel guide to northern Italy — I'm returning it with this note. It turned out to be far more useful than I expected. The maps saved me from getting hopelessly lost in Venice more than once, and its tips on small, family-run restaurants were much better than anything I found online. I would never have stumbled on that quiet harbour town near Genoa without it.

  I've slipped the guide back through your letterbox. Do borrow my camera any time in return.

  Thanks again,

  Anna *(约 100 词)*
</Callout>
## 13. 拓展练习 Going further

<Callout type="tip" title="自学者建议">
  - **影子跟读 (shadowing)**：用第 1 节的三个 SayIt 按钮跟读 3-5 遍。注意 *far too hot* / *as far away as* 这类比较结构里，重音通常落在形容词上（too **HOT** / as far **a**way as）。
  - **复述 (retell)**：合上课文，用 2 分钟把本文的逻辑链讲一遍。**三步推演**：能否维生（取决于与恒星的距离）→ 怎么找（必须放到木星那么远、挡光、分析大气）→ 找什么（细菌等低等生命，而非小绿人）→ 意义（一旦发现，将改变一切）。
  - **造句迁移**：用今天的 5 个高价值结构造你自己的句子——
    1. *make it possible to do ...*（使……成为可能）—— *The bridge made it possible to reach the island by car.*
    2. *too + adj + for ... to do*（太……以致不能）—— *The water was too dirty for the fish to survive.*
    3. *... would have to be ...*（假设条件下的必须）—— *To win, the team would have to score twice in ten minutes.*
    4. *as + adj + as ...*（同级比较）—— *The new model is as light as a feather.*
    5. *It is ... that ...*（强调句）—— *It is practice that makes the difference.*
  - **延伸思考**：本课是一篇\"**思想实验式**\"说明文——作者从头到尾都在\"假设\"一个我们还没见过的世界，所以语言上靠 **would / would have to**（假设）和 **as ... as / too ... to / -er than**（比较）撑起整个推演。下次你写\"如果……会怎样\"的英文议论或说明文时，记住这两把工具：**用 would 锁定\"假设\"的语气，用比较结构把抽象的\"大、远、难\"变得可量化**。另一个值得回味的点：文章结尾借 NASA 局长之口，从\"找细菌\"这件看似冷冰冰的科学小事，一跃升华到\"改变全人类对自身的看法\"——好的说明文，往往在最后一句把\"事实\"推向\"意义\"。
</Callout>
