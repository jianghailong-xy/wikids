<Callout type="info" title="Listen first 听前思考">
  几乎每个人家里都堆着舍不得扔的东西——旧线绳、包装纸、剪下来的报纸……这叫**囤积**。可同样是“收藏”，有人只是越堆越乱，有人却把它变成了一门**学问**：交朋友、长见识、出门旅行、甚至被请去**作演讲**。这两种人到底差在哪？作者说，认真的收藏者能从中得到一样东西——它让人**心满意足，再无无聊之日**。

  **What in particular does a person gain when he or she becomes a serious collector? 当一个人成为认真的收藏者时，他/她究竟能得到什么？** 带着这个问题读课文，重点留意这篇**说明文**是如何用 **对比结构**（indiscriminate collector vs serious collector）+ **the + 比较级 ... the + 比较级**、**so ... that / to such an extent that**、**not only ... but also**、**非限定性定语从句** 把“囤积”与“收藏”一层层讲透的。
</Callout>

## The Text 课文

People tend to **amass possessions**, sometimes without being aware of doing so. Indeed they can have a delightful surprise when they find something useful which they did not know they owned. Those who never have to move house **become indiscriminate collectors** of what can only be described as clutter. They leave unwanted objects in drawers, cupboards and attics for years, **in the belief that** they may one day need just those very things. As they grow old, people also accumulate belongings for two other reasons, lack of physical and mental energy, **both of which are essential** in turning out and throwing away, and sentiment. Things owned for a long time are full of associations with the past, perhaps with relatives who are dead, and so they gradually acquire a value beyond their true worth.

Some things are collected deliberately in the home **in an attempt to avoid waste**. Among these I would list string and brown paper, kept by thrifty people when a parcel has been opened, to save buying these two requisites. Collecting small items can easily **become a mania**. I know someone who always cuts sketches out from newspapers of model clothes that she would like to buy if she had the money. As she is not rich, the chances that she will ever be able to afford such purchases are remote; but she is **never sufficiently strong-minded** to be able to stop the practice. It is a harmless habit, but it litters up her desk **to such an extent that** every time she opens it, loose bits of paper fall out in every direction.

Collecting as a serious hobby is quite different and has many advantages. It provides relaxation for leisure hours, as just looking at one's treasures is always a joy. One does not have to go outside for amusement, since the collection is housed at home. Whatever it consists of, stamps, records, first editions of books, china, glass, antique furniture, pictures, model cars, stuffed birds, toy animals, there is always something to do in connection with it, from finding the right place for the latest addition, to verifying facts in reference books. This hobby educates one **not only** in the chosen subject, **but also** in general matters which **have some bearing on it**. There are also other benefits. One wants to meet **like-minded collectors**, to get advice, to compare notes, to exchange articles, to show off the latest find. So one's circle of friends grows. Soon the hobby leads to travel, perhaps to a meeting in another town, possibly a trip abroad in search of a rare specimen, for collectors **are not confined to** any one country. Over the years, one may well **become an authority on** one's hobby and will very probably be asked to give informal talks to little gatherings and then, if successful, to larger audiences. In this way self-confidence grows, first from mastering a subject, then from being able to talk about it. Collecting, **by occupying spare time so constructively**, makes a person contented, with no time for boredom.

### 想一想 Two kinds of collector

这是一篇**说明文 / 议论文**——没有人物对白，而是把“两种收藏者”摆在一起对照。读完先别急着做题，用中文在心里给这张表填上答案：

<Callout type="tip" title="读后自测：你能区分这两种人吗？">
  | 维度 | 不加选择的囤积者 indiscriminate collector | 认真的收藏者 serious collector |
  |---|---|---|
  | **收藏什么** | clutter（杂物：旧线绳、报纸剪贴……） | stamps, records, antique furniture ...（有主题的珍品） |
  | **动机** | “说不定哪天用得上” + 感情 / 舍不得 | 兴趣、求知、社交 |
  | **价值** | a value **beyond their true worth**（虚高的情感价值） | 真实的知识与社交价值 |
  | **结果** | 桌子堆到 *loose bits of paper fall out* | 交朋友、旅行、成为 authority、作演讲 |
  | **作者态度** | a harmless **but** pointless habit | quite different, **many advantages** |

  抓住这条**主线对比**，全篇的逻辑就立起来了。
</Callout>

## 课文注释 Notes on the text

<Callout type="info" title="7 个值得停下来嚼一嚼的点">
  1. **in the belief that ...** (l.6) — “怀着……的想法 / 以为……”。这是一个**介词短语作状语**，修饰前面的谓语动词 *leave*（他们把东西留着，是“以为”将来用得上）。**that** 引导的是 belief 的**同位语从句**（说明 belief 的内容）。同型：*in the hope that ... / on condition that ... / under the impression that ...*。
  2. **those very things** (l.7) — “正是那些东西”。**very** 在这里**不是“非常”**，而是形容词，起**强调**作用，相当于 *exactly those things*。可译作“正是那个（些）”“正是所要的”。同型：*This is the very book I wanted.（这正是我想要的书。）*
  3. **for two other reasons, lack of ... energy, ... and sentiment** (ll.7-9) — 句子里 *lack of physical and mental energy* 和 *sentiment* 是**两个并列成分，作 reasons 的同位语**（具体说明“两个原因”是什么）；而 *both of which are essential ...* 是 *physical and mental energy* 的**非限定性定语从句**（both of which = 这两者，即体力和精力）。
  4. **beyond their true worth** (l.10) — “超出它们的实际价值”。**beyond** = “超过、超出”。同型：*beyond my reach（够不着）/ beyond doubt（毫无疑问）/ beyond repair（无法修复）*。
  5. **to such an extent that ...** (l.16) — “到了这样的程度，以至于……”。**that** 后面是**结果状语从句**（也可理解为 extent 的同位语从句）。与 *so ... that / such ... that* 同义。同型：*He was tired to such an extent that he fell asleep at his desk.*
  6. **have some bearing on it** (ll.23-24) — “与它有关 / 对它有影响”。**bearing** 这里是名词，*have a bearing on sth.* = 与某事有关、对某事有影响。同型：*Her remarks have no bearing on the matter.（她的话与此事无关。）*
  7. **as just looking at one's treasures is always a joy** (l.19) — **as** 是连词，引导**原因状语从句**，“因为……”。整句解释了“为什么收藏能让人放松”。同型：*As it was getting late, we went home.*
</Callout>

## 参考译文 Chinese reference

<Callout type="tip" title="先读原文，遇到读不懂的句子再回来对照">
  人们喜欢收藏东西，有时并没有意识到自己在这样做。确实，一旦无意中从自己的收藏品中找到某件有用的东西，可以给人一种惊喜的感觉。那些从来不必搬家的人成了一种毫无选择的收藏家。他们把那些只能称作杂物的东西一放就是好几年，留在抽屉里、碗柜中、阁楼上，相信总有一天需要的正好是那些东西。人们年老之后也喜欢收藏东西，不过是出于另外两个原因：一是体力、精力均告不佳，而这二者恰是清除无用之物必不可少的；另一个原因是感情因素。东西保存时间一长，便会充满与过去岁月的联系，比方说与死去的亲戚有关。因此，这些东西慢慢获得了一种超出其本身的价值。

  居家度日，有时刻意地收藏某些东西是为了防止浪费。这些东西中，我想举出线绳和包装纸为例。节俭的人打开包裹后便把这两样必备之物留起来，省得再去买。收藏小东西很容易变成一种癖好。我认识一个人，她总爱从报纸上剪下她想买的时装样图，可她又没钱。她既然不富裕，那么她有朝一日买得起这些东西的可能性十分渺茫。但她又缺乏足够坚强的意志去停止这一收藏活动。这种习惯无害，但她的桌子被搞得乱七八糟，每次一打开抽屉，纸屑便四处飘落。

  作为一种严肃的业余爱好，收藏活动则完全是另外一回事，它有许多益处。它可以使人在闲暇时得到放松，因为欣赏自己收藏的珍品总是一种乐趣。人们不必走出家门去寻欢娱，因为收藏品就存放在家中。不管收藏品是什么——邮票、唱片、头版书籍、瓷器、玻璃器皿、老式家具、绘画、模型汽车、鸟兽标本、玩具动物——总有事可做，从给新增添的藏品寻找恰当的位置，到从参考书中查证事实。这种爱好不仅使人从所选定的专题中受到教育，而且还能从与之有关的一般事物中获得长进。除此之外，还有别的益处。人们想要会会志趣相投的收藏者，以求获得指点、交流心得、交换藏品、炫耀新近觅得之物。这样朋友圈子就不断扩大。用不了多久，有这种爱好的人便开始旅行，也许是去另一个城市参加会议，也可能是出国寻找一件珍品，因为收藏家是不分国籍的。一个人积了多年经验，会成为自己这种爱好的权威，很可能应邀在小型集会上作非正式的讲话。如果讲得好，可能会被请去向更多的人发表演说。这样，你的自信心便不断增强——先是因为掌握了一门学问，接下来是因为能够就此发表见解。收藏活动通过富有建设性地利用业余时间，使人感到心满意足，不再有无聊之日。
</Callout>

## 3. 关键句型 Comparison, result & emphasis 对比、结果与强调结构

<Callout type="tip" title="一分钟回顾">
  这篇说明文靠几组**逻辑结构**把意思讲清楚。把它们记牢，本课的 Structure 4 题就迎刃而解：

  | 结构 | 课文 / 例句 | 用途 |
  |---|---|---|
  | **the + 比较级 ..., the + 比较级 ...** | *The older they get, the more belongings people accumulate.* | “越……，就越……”（两个量同步变化） |
  | **so + much / adj ... that ...** = **to such an extent that ...** | *It litters up her desk **so much that** ... / **to such an extent that** ...* | 表**结果**：到了……程度，以至于…… |
  | **not only ... but also ...** | *educates one **not only** in the chosen subject **but also** in general matters* | 递进并列：不仅……而且…… |
  | **非限定性定语从句 which / both of which** | *... mental energy, **both of which** are essential ...* | 补充说明先行词，前后加逗号 |
  | **泛指人称 one ... one's ...** | ***One** does not have to go outside ... **One's** circle of friends grows.* | 全篇用 one 保持人称一致 |
  | **the right place to do sth.** | *finding the right place **to put** the latest addition* | 不定式作后置定语（表用途） |

  ### ⚠️ 4 个本课最容易踩的雷区

  1. **the + 比较级** 句型里，两个 the 后都跟**比较级**，且常**主谓倒装省略**：*The older they get, the more (belongings) people accumulate.* 不能写成 *As they get older more ...*。
  2. **so 修饰 much / 形容词 / 副词**；**such 修饰名词（短语）**：*so much that ...* ✓ / *such an extent that ...* ✓，但 *so an extent* ✗、*such much* ✗。
  3. **collect / accumulate 是及物动词**，后面要有**宾语**：*People deliberately collect **some things** ...* ✓；只说 *People deliberately collect.* 在这个语境里残缺。
  4. **人称要前后一致**：全段主语是 **one**，所以续接也用 *one is / one's*，不能突然换成 *they will be* 或 *it has been*。
</Callout>

## 4. 难点：用一句话替换从句 Rewrite without changing the meaning

<Callout type="info" title="教材难点：it is likely that ... → S is likely to ...">
  教材 *Key structures and Special difficulties* 给的改写题（参照 ll.27-28）：

  > **改写：** *Over the years, it is quite likely that one will become an authority on one's hobby.*
  > **答案：** *Over the years one **is quite likely to** become an authority on one's hobby.*

  核心规则：**It is + 形容词（likely / certain / sure / probable）+ that 从句**，常可改写成 **主语 + be + 形容词 + to do**（把从句主语提到句首）：

  | It-结构 | 等价的 to-do 结构 |
  |---|---|
  | *It is likely that he will win.* | *He **is likely to** win.* |
  | *It is certain that they will agree.* | *They **are certain to** agree.* |
  | *It is sure that she will pass.* | *She **is sure to** pass.* |

  ⚠️ **易错点**：*probable* **不能**这样改写——只能说 *It is probable that ...*，没有 *He is probable to ...* 的说法。likely / certain / sure 才可以。
</Callout>

## 11. 作文 Composition (250–350 词)

<Callout type="info" title="任务">
  Write a composition on **one** of the following subjects; the length should be **between 250 and 350 words**.

  **a.** If you could choose, in which country would you like to live and why?
  **b.** Write a short story beginning "There was a knock on the window ..."
</Callout>

<Callout type="warning" title="提示骨架（以 a 为例）">
  - **开头**：直接亮出你选择的国家 + 一句话点题（the country I would choose to live in is ...）。
  - **理由一**：生活方式 / 气候 / 自然环境（climate, scenery, pace of life）。
  - **理由二**：文化 / 语言 / 人情（culture, food, friendliness of people）。
  - **理由三**：机会 / 教育 / 工作（career, study, healthcare）。
  - **让步 + 反驳**：承认一个缺点，再说明为何仍然选它（Admittedly ..., but ...）。
  - **结尾**：回扣“if I could choose”，升华为一种对“理想生活”的向往。
</Callout>

<Callout type="tip" title="写作脚手架">
  - **时态主线**：以**一般现在时**描述事实与偏好（*The pace of life is gentle.*）；用**虚拟 / 条件语气**呼应题目（*If I could choose ..., I would settle in ...*）；展望用 *I would be able to ... / I hope to ...*。
  - **“偏好”表达库**：*I would settle in ... / I am drawn to ... / what appeals to me most is ... / I have always been fascinated by ...*。
  - **“理由”连接词**：*for one thing ... for another ... / above all / not only ... but also / what is more / chiefly because*。
  - **“让步”句式**：*Admittedly, the winters are harsh, but ... / It is true that ..., yet ...*。
  - **结构亮点**：开篇用条件句点题，主体三段“总—分”铺理由，结尾用 *If I really could choose, ...* 首尾呼应。
</Callout>

<Callout type="tip" title="参考范文（自己写完再看）">
  If I could choose freely where to spend the rest of my life, I would settle in New Zealand. Several things draw me to those two small islands at the bottom of the world.

  For one thing, the scenery is breathtaking. Within a single day one can drive from snow-capped mountains to empty golden beaches, passing lakes, forests and green sheep pastures on the way. For someone like me, who finds peace in the open air, such variety is a daily gift rather than a holiday treat.

  For another, the pace of life is gentle and unhurried. People seem to value time with family and friends more than the race for money. Cities are clean and small enough to feel friendly, yet they still offer good universities, hospitals and work. I could earn a decent living without surrendering my evenings and weekends to the office.

  Above all, I admire the way New Zealanders treat their environment and one another. Strangers greet you on mountain tracks; the air and rivers are clean; and there is a quiet pride in protecting the land for the future. That sense of responsibility appeals to me far more than the glamour of a great capital.

  Admittedly, the country lies a long way from everywhere else, and a flight home to see relatives would take many hours. Yet in an age of video calls and cheap travel, distance matters less than it once did, and the rewards of living somewhere so peaceful would, for me, far outweigh the inconvenience.

  If I really could choose, then, I would trade the noise and crowds of a big city for the clean air, open spaces and unhurried kindness of New Zealand. It is, I believe, as close to my idea of a good life as any place on earth. *(about 300 words)*
</Callout>

## 12. 书信写作 Letter writing (80–100 词)

<Callout type="info" title="任务">
  Write a letter of **between 80 and 100 words** on **one** of the following subjects. Make the beginning and ending like those of an ordinary letter; the address is **not** counted in the total number of words.

  **a.** A friend is coming to visit you. Write a short letter giving him (or her) **directions for finding your house**.
  **b.** Write a letter to a friend explaining that, **through illness, you will be unable to go to stay** with him (or her) as previously arranged. Say how you are progressing and suggest **alternative arrangements**.
</Callout>

<Callout type="tip" title="写作脚手架（以 a 为例：指路信）">
  - **称呼 + 开头**：*Dear [name], I'm so glad you can come on Saturday! Here is how to find us.*
  - **方位线索 1（出站 / 下车）**：*When you come out of the station, turn left and walk along the High Street.*
  - **方位线索 2（标志物）**：*Carry on until you pass a small church on your right.*
  - **方位线索 3（转弯）**：*Take the second turning on the left into Park Road.*
  - **门牌 / 收尾标志**：*Ours is the third house on the right, number 17, with a red door.*
  - **结尾**：*Phone me if you get lost. See you soon! / Best wishes, / [你的名字]*
  - **指路常用语**：*turn left / right · go straight on · carry on for about 200 metres · opposite the bank · next to the post office · on the corner of ...*。时态用**一般现在时 / 祈使句**（give directions）。
</Callout>

## 13. 拓展练习 Going further

<Callout type="tip" title="自学者建议">
  - **影子跟读 (shadowing)**：用第 1 节的三个 SayIt 按钮各跟读 3-5 遍。重点感受英式长句里**逗号处的轻微停顿**与**结果状语 *to such an extent that ...* 后半句的语调下沉**——把控好停顿，长句就不再喘不上气。
  - **复述 (retell)**：合上课文，用 2 分钟把全文讲一遍。抓住**两段对比结构**：① 不加选择的囤积者（为什么囤 → 价值虚高 → 朋友的剪报案例）→ ② 认真的收藏者（放松 → 长知识 → 交朋友 → 旅行 → 成权威 → 心满意足）。
  - **造句迁移**：用今天的 5 个高价值结构造你自己的句子——
    1. *The + 比较级 ..., the + 比较级 ...*（越……越……）—— *The more you read, the wider your horizons become.*
    2. *... to such an extent that ...*（到了……程度，以至于……）—— *He worked to such an extent that he made himself ill.*
    3. *not only ... but also ...*（不仅……而且……）—— *The trip educated me not only in history but also in art.*
    4. *S is likely to do ...*（很可能……）—— *Prices are likely to rise next year.*
    5. *by + V-ing, S + V*（通过做……）—— *By practising every day, she became fluent in a year.*
  - **延伸思考**：本课是一篇结构清晰的**对比说明文**——作者不直接评判，而是把“囤积”与“收藏”并列摆出，让事实自己说话。读完不妨问问自己：**你的爱好属于哪一种？** 是越堆越多、舍不得扔的 clutter，还是能让你 *meet like-minded people, travel, and become an authority* 的 serious hobby？把一项消遣“认真”起来——加上主题、知识与社交——往往就是它从“消磨时间”升级为“滋养人生”的转折点。
</Callout>
