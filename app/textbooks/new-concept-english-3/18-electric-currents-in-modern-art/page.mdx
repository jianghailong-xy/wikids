## The Text 课文

Modern sculpture rarely surprises us any more. The idea that modern art can only be seen in museums is mistaken. Even people who take no interest in art **cannot have failed to notice** examples of modern sculpture **on display** in public places. Strange forms stand in gardens, and outside buildings and shops. We have got quite used to them. Some so-called 'modern' pieces have been on display for nearly eighty years.

In spite of this, some people — including myself — were surprised by a recent exhibition of modern sculpture. The first thing I saw when I entered the art gallery was a notice which said: 'Do not touch the exhibits. Some of them are dangerous!' The objects on display were pieces of moving sculpture. Oddly shaped forms that are suspended from the ceiling and move **in response to** a gust of wind **are quite familiar to** everybody. These objects, however, were different. **Lined up against** the wall, there were long thin wires **attached to** metal spheres. The spheres had been magnetized and **attracted or repelled** each other all the time. In the centre of the hall, there were a number of tall structures which contained coloured lights. These lights **flickered continuously** like traffic lights which have gone mad. Sparks were emitted from small black boxes and red lamps **flashed on and off** angrily. It was rather like an exhibition of prehistoric electronic equipment. These peculiar forms **not only seemed designed to** shock people emotionally, **but to** give them electric shocks as well!

## 课文注释 Notes on the text

<Callout type="info" title="9 个值得停下来嚼一嚼的点">
  1. **The idea that ... is mistaken** (l.1) —— "……的观点是错误的"。这里的 **that** 引导的是一个**同位语从句**，说明 idea 的具体内容——即"现代艺术只能在博物馆里看到"这个想法。同型：*The belief that money buys happiness is mistaken.*（认为金钱能买来幸福的想法是错误的。）

  2. **take no interest in ...** (l.2) —— "对……不感兴趣"。也可以说 *take (a) great interest in*（对……很感兴趣）。同型：*He takes no interest in politics.* / *She takes a keen interest in photography.*

  3. **cannot have failed to notice** (l.3) —— "不可能没注意到"、"不至于没注意到"。**cannot + have + 过去分词** 表示对过去的事情作**否定推测**（"不可能……"）。而 **fail to do** = "未能做"。所以整句是双重否定表肯定："不可能没注意到" = "一定注意到了"。同型：*You cannot have failed to see the sign — it was right in front of you.*

  4. **on display** (l.4) —— "在展出/陈列中"。同义：*on show / on exhibit*。同型：*The painting will be on display until September.*

  5. **move in response to a gust of wind** (ll.13-14) —— "随风飘荡"。**in response to** = "作为对……的回应/反应"。这里指雕塑品对"一阵风（a gust of wind）"做出反应——风一吹就动。同型：*The dog wagged its tail in response to my voice.*（狗听到我的声音摇了摇尾巴。）

  6. **be familiar to sb.** (l.14) —— "为某人所熟悉"。和 *sb. be familiar with sth.* 区分：*familiar to* = 对某人来说熟悉；*familiar with* = 某人对某事熟悉。比较：*This tune is familiar to me.*（这段旋律我很熟） vs. *I am familiar with this tune.*（我对这段旋律很熟。）

  7. **Lined up against the wall** (l.15) —— "靠墙排列着"。**lined up** 是过去分词短语作**状语**，描述"电线是怎么放的"。类似的还有后面 **attached to metal spheres**（被连在金属球上）、**magnetized**（被磁化）——全篇大量使用过去分词短语来"定格"画面。同型：*Parked outside the house, the car attracted everyone's attention.*

  8. **like traffic lights which have gone mad** (ll.19-20) —— "就像发了疯的红绿灯一样"。**go mad** = 发疯、发狂。这里的 mad 表"失控、不受控"。同型：*The crowd went mad when the band appeared on stage.*

  9. **on and off** (l.21-22) —— "断断续续地、时而……时而不"（= *off and on*）。flashed on and off = 一闪一闪地明灭不定。同型：*It has been raining on and off all day.*（雨断断续续下了一整天。）
</Callout>

## 3. 关键句型 介词搭配 Prepositions

<Callout type="tip" title="一分钟回顾">
  本课的核心语法点是**介词搭配**（对应第 2 册第 94 课关键句型）。课文里密集出现了一串**\"动词/形容词 + 介词\"**的固定搭配，每个都是"换了介词意思就变"的那种：

  | 搭配 | 课文出处 | 含义 |
  |---|---|---|
  | **take no interest in** | l.2 | 对……不感兴趣 |
  | **on display** | l.4 | 在展出中 |
  | **be used to** | l.5-6 | 习惯于（*have got quite used to them*） |
  | **suspend from** | l.13 | 从……悬挂下来 |
  | **in response to** | l.13 | 作为对……的回应 |
  | **be familiar to** | l.14 | 为……所熟悉 |
  | **line up against** | l.15 | 沿着……排列 |
  | **attach to** | l.16 | 连接到……上 |

  ### ⚠️ 4 个本课最易踩的雷区

  1. **be familiar to vs. be familiar with**：*to* = 对某人来说熟悉（主语是\"被熟悉的事物\"）；*with* = 某人对某事熟悉（主语是\"人\"）。*This song is familiar **to** me.* / *I am familiar **with** this song.*
  2. **suspend from** = 从……悬挂（from 表"来源/起点"不是"离开"）：*A lamp suspended **from** the ceiling.* 不是 *suspended **by** the ceiling*。
  3. **in response to** 中的 **to** 是介词（不是不定式符号），后接名词/动名词：*in response **to** a gust of wind*（正确）≠ *in response to move*（错）。
  4. **attach to** 的被动语态：*wires were attached **to** spheres*（be attached to = 被连接到……）。不要漏掉 to。
</Callout>

## 4. 难点：拼写规则 -y 结尾变复数 Special difficulties

<Callout type="info" title="教材 Spelling 练习">
  教材 Special difficulties 讲的是**以 -y 结尾的名词/动词的拼写规则**：

  | 规则 | 示例 | 说明 |
  |---|---|---|
  | **辅音 + y → ies** | *lady → ladies*, *story → stories*, *fry → fries* | 去 y 加 ies |
  | **元音 + y → 直接加 s** | *day → days*, *boy → boys*, *valley → valleys*, *chimney → chimneys*, *way → ways* | y 前是 a/e/o 时保留 y |
  | **动词 -y → -ies（第三人称单数）** | *try → tries*, *marry → marries*, *hurry → hurries*, *bury → buries* | 辅音 + y：去 y + ies |
  | **动词 -y → -ys（元音 + y）** | *say → says*, *enjoy → enjoys*, *buy → buys*, *stay → stays* | 元音 + y：直接加 s |
  | **动词 -y → -ied（过去式）** | *qualify → qualified*, *supply → supplied*, *reply → replied* | 辅音 + y 的动词过去式：去 y + ied |

  **简单记法**：看 y 前是什么——**元音前面站，y 就原样办（+s / +s / +ed）；辅音前面站，y 要变成 i（+es / +ies / +ied）**。唯一的易混词是 **money → monies**（不是 moneys），但那是特例。
</Callout>

## 10. 摘要写作 Summary writing (≤ 80 词)

<Callout type="info" title="任务">
  *In not more than 80 words* describe **what the writer saw from the moment he entered the art gallery**. **Do not include anything that is not in the last paragraph**.

  按下面的提示点串成一段：

  1. What did the writer see when he entered the art gallery? (A notice warning that some exhibits were dangerous.)
  2. What did the exhibition consist of? (Pieces of moving sculpture.)
  3. What did the writer see against the wall? (Long thin wires attached to metal spheres.)
  4. What did the spheres do? (They attracted or repelled each other.)
  5. What did the tall structures in the centre of the hall contain? (Coloured lights.)
  6. What did the coloured lights do? (They flickered continuously like mad traffic lights.)
  7. What was emitted from black boxes? (Sparks.)
  8. Did red lamps go on and off or not? (Yes, they flashed on and off angrily.)
</Callout>

<Callout type="tip" title="参考范文（自己写完再看）">
  When the writer entered the art gallery, he saw a notice warning that some exhibits were dangerous. The exhibition consisted of pieces of moving sculpture. Against the wall, long thin wires attached to metal spheres attracted or repelled each other. In the centre, tall structures contained coloured lights that flickered continuously like traffic lights gone mad. Sparks were emitted from small black boxes, and red lamps flashed on and off angrily. *(71 words)*
</Callout>

## 11. 作文 Composition (≤ 200 词)

<Callout type="info" title="任务">
  Write a composition in **not more than 200 words** on the following. Use the ideas given below. Do not write more than **three paragraphs**.

  **Title: An interesting exhibition.**

  **Introduction:** Work of many artists exhibited — great public interest — you went to the art gallery.

  **Development:** Description of some of the pictures on display — the picture you liked best — the strangest picture of them all.

  **Conclusion:** People's comments overheard — your opinion of the paintings at the exhibition.
</Callout>

<Callout type="warning" title="提示骨架">
  - **第 1 段 (Introduction):** 点明时间、地点、展览名称 + 公众关注度（*long queues / crowded halls / talked about for weeks*）→ 你亲自去看。
  - **第 2 段 (Development):** 挑 3 幅画描述——你最爱的（为什么）、最有争议的、最古怪的（什么内容/什么技法让你停下脚步）。
  - **第 3 段 (Conclusion):** 听到的观众评论（褒/贬各一）+ 你的总体评价——这次展览有没有改变你对现代艺术的看法？
</Callout>

<Callout type="tip" title="写作脚手架">
  - **时态主线**: 一般过去时（叙述经历）+ 过去完成时（*I had never seen anything like it before*）。
  - **描述画的词库**: *canvas, brushstroke, colour palette, abstract, lifelike, thought-provoking, bold, delicate, chaotic, serene*.
  - **表达观感的句型**: *What struck me most was ...* / *I found myself drawn to ...* / *It was the kind of painting that ...* / *One visitor remarked that ...*
</Callout>

<Callout type="tip" title="参考范文（自己写完再看）">
  Last Sunday I visited an exhibition of modern paintings at the City Art Gallery. The work of over thirty young artists was on display, and the public response had been remarkable — queues stretched well into the street.

  The exhibition was a feast of styles. My favourite was a large canvas entitled *Rainy Night*, a swirl of blues and greys that somehow made an empty street feel deeply alive. Right opposite hung the strangest picture of them all: a woman's face painted entirely in jagged red triangles. It was uncomfortable to look at, yet I could not walk past it.

  Around me, visitors were divided. "This isn't art — my five-year-old could do better," a man grumbled, while a young woman nearby whispered, "I've never seen anything so honest." I left the gallery feeling that good modern art does not have to be beautiful — it only has to make you stop and think. And this exhibition had certainly done that. *(175 words)*
</Callout>

## 12. 书信写作 Letter writing (约 80 词)

<Callout type="info" title="任务">
  Write a letter of about **80 words** to a friend **accepting an invitation** to go with him to an exhibition. Supply a suitable Introduction and Conclusion. Use the following information to write the **Purpose**（正文主体）:

  - thank him for the invitation
  - looking forward to meeting him again soon
  - particularly interested in the exhibition — why
  - where you will meet your friend: time and place
</Callout>

<Callout type="tip" title="写作脚手架">
  - **称呼**: *Dear + 名字*（朋友间用名字即可）。
  - **开头**: 收到邀请 + 感谢 —— *Thank you so much for inviting me to ... with you. I would love to come.*
  - **主体 (Purpose)**:
    1. *I have been looking forward to seeing you again*（期待重逢）
    2. *I am particularly interested in this exhibition because ...*（解释为什么感兴趣——题材、艺术家、某个听说过的作品）
    3. 约定时间地点 —— *Shall we meet at the gallery entrance at 10 o'clock on Saturday?*
  - **收尾**: *I can hardly wait!* / *See you there!*
  - **结束语**: *Yours, / Best wishes, / [你的名字]*
</Callout>

<Callout type="tip" title="参考范文（自己写完再看）">
  Dear Tom,

  Thank you so much for inviting me to the modern art exhibition at the Riverside Gallery. I'd love to come!
  
  I have been looking forward to seeing you again ever since your last visit. I am particularly interested in this exhibition because I've heard that it features kinetic sculptures — pieces that actually move — which is exactly the kind of modern art I find most exciting.
  
  Shall we meet at the gallery entrance at 10 o'clock on Saturday morning? I can hardly wait!
  
  Yours,
  
  Jack *(82 words)*
</Callout>

## 13. 拓展练习 Going further

<Callout type="tip" title="自学者建议">
  - **影子跟读 (shadowing)**: 用第 1 节的三个 SayIt 按钮跟读 3-5 遍。注意 *cannot have failed to notice* 中 cannot 和 failed 都要重读（双重否定表强调）；*Lined up against the wall* 中 Lined up 连读成 /laɪnˈdʌp/。
  - **复述 (retell)**: 合上课文，用 2 分钟把作者逛展的经历讲一遍。**四步走**：不再惊讶（现代雕塑无处不在）→ 进门（告示：危险！）→ 看到了什么（磁化金属球互相排斥、彩灯疯闪、火花四溅、红灯明灭）→ 结尾的 punchline（不仅情感上 shock 你，还真给你电击）。
  - **造句迁移**: 用今天的 5 个高价值结构造你自己的句子——
    1. *cannot have failed to do*（不可能没……）—— *You cannot have failed to hear the news — it was on every channel.*
    2. *in response to*（作为对……的回应）—— *She smiled in response to his greeting.*
    3. *be familiar to*（为……所熟悉）—— *The skyline of Paris is familiar to people all over the world.*
    4. *过去分词作状语*—— *Surrounded by noisy children, the teacher struggled to make herself heard.*
    5. *not only ... but ... as well*（不仅……还……）—— *The film not only entertained the audience but moved them to tears as well.*
  - **拼写自查**: 把教材 Special difficulties 的那 18 个 -y 结尾词逐一写出复数或单三形式：*ladies, supplies, valleys, qualifies, stories, days, says, replies, marries, ways, chimneys, hurries, stays, enjoys, buys, bodies, buries, fries.* 写完后对照规则自检——凡错一题，就把对应的那类规则再背一遍。
  - **延伸思考**: 本课的写作秘密藏在**对比**里：开头一段（ll.1-7）讲"我们已经习惯了现代雕塑"——这是全篇的**背景板**；第二段（ll.8-25）才是真正的**事件**。另一个值得品味的技巧是文章的**幽默收尾**——*not only seemed designed to shock people emotionally, but to give them electric shocks as well!*——同一个 shock 用了两遍，一个指心理震撼，一个指物理电击，这叫 **pun（双关语）**。好的叙事文不一定需要宏大主题，有时候一个巧妙的结尾就能让读者会心一笑、合上书还记得。
</Callout>
