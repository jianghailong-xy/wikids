  **Did the writer repair his lawn mower in the end? Why/Why not?** 带着这个问题读课文，重点留意开篇那句震撼的 **So great is our passion ... that ...** 倒装句、贯穿全文的分词状语，以及一连串精准的 DIY 动作词汇。
</Callout>

## The Text 课文

**So great is our passion for** doing things for ourselves, that we are becoming increasingly **less dependent on** specialized labour. No one can **plead ignorance of** a subject any longer, for there are countless do-it-yourself publications. **Armed with** the right tools and materials, newlyweds gaily **embark on** the task of decorating their own homes. Men, **particularly**, spend hours of their leisure time installing their own fireplaces, laying out their own gardens; building garages and making furniture. Some really keen enthusiasts **go so far as to** build their own computers. Shops **cater for** the do-it-yourself craze **not only by** running special advisory services for novices, **but by** offering consumers **bits and pieces** which they can **assemble** at home. Such things provide an excellent **outlet for pent up** creative energy, but unfortunately **not all of us** are born handymen.

Some wives **tend to** believe that their husbands are infinitely **resourceful** and can fix anything. Even men who can hardly **drive a nail in straight** are **supposed to be** born electricians, carpenters, plumbers and mechanics. When lights **fuse**, furniture gets **rickety**, pipes get **clogged**, or vacuum cleaners fail to operate, some women **assume** that their husbands will somehow **put things right**. The worst thing about the do-it-yourself game is that sometimes even men **live under the delusion** that they can do anything, even when they have repeatedly been proved wrong. **It is a question of pride as much as anything else.**

Last spring my wife suggested that I **call in** a man to look at our lawn mower. It had **broken down** the previous summer, and though I promised to repair it, I had never **got round to** it. I **would not hear of** the suggestion and said that I would fix it myself. One Saturday afternoon, I **hauled** the machine into the garden and had a close look at it. **As far as I could see**, it needed only a minor **adjustment**: **a turn of a screw** here, a little **tightening up** there, a drop of oil and it would be **as good as new**. Inevitably the repair job was not quite so simple. The mower firmly **refused to mow**, so I decided to **dismantle** it. The garden was soon **littered with chunks** of metal which had once **made up** a lawn mower. But I was extremely pleased with myself. I had **traced** the cause of the trouble. One of the links in the chain that drives the wheels had **snapped**. After buying a new chain I was **faced with** the **insurmountable** task of putting the **confusing jigsaw puzzle** together again. I was not surprised to find that the machine still refused to work after I had reassembled it, for the simple reason that I was left with several curiously shaped bits of metal which did not seem to fit anywhere. I **gave up in despair**. The weeks passed and the grass grew. When my wife **nagged** me to do something about it, I told her that either I would have to buy a new mower or let the grass grow. **Needless to say** our house is now surrounded by a jungle. Buried somewhere in deep grass there is a **rusting** lawn mower which I have promised to repair one day.

## 课文注释 Notes on the text

<Callout type="info" title="8 个值得停下来嚼一嚼的点">
  1. **So great is our passion ... that ...** (ll.1-2) — 本课最醒目的句式：**so + 形容词** 提到句首引起**主谓倒装**，that 引导结果状语从句。正常语序：*Our passion for doing things for ourselves is so great that we are becoming ...* 把 so great 提前是为了强调"热情之高"。同类例句：*So exhausted was she that she fell asleep at her desk.* ⚠️ 仅当 so/such 修饰的成分被提到句首时才倒装主句，that 从句本身不倒装。

  2. **Armed with the right tools and materials** (l.4) — **过去分词短语作状语**，表示"装备有……"的状态，逻辑主语是主句的 newlyweds。= *equipped with*。同类：*Dressed in a hurry, he rushed out the door. / Exhausted by the journey, they went straight to bed.*

  3. **not only ... but (also) ...** (ll.9-12) — 平行结构，连接两个 **by + 动名词** 短语：*not only **by running** ... but **by offering** ...* 两边结构必须对称。同类：*She not only speaks French but also writes it fluently.* ⚠️ not only 放句首时主句要部分倒装：*Not only do shops run advisory services, but they also offer bits and pieces.*

  4. **not all of us are born handymen** (ll.13-14) — **部分否定**。not all = "并非所有人都……"（不是"所有人都不……"）。全部否定要用 **none of us**。同类：*Not everyone agrees with the plan.*（部分否定：并非所有人都同意）× *Not everyone doesn't agree.*（双重否定混乱）

  5. **Even men who can hardly drive a nail in straight ...** (ll.16-17) — **even** 表"甚至、连……都"，修饰整个名词短语 *men who ...*；**hardly** = "几乎不"（准否定词）。*drive a nail in straight* = 把钉子钉直——连钉子都钉不直的男人，却被认为是天生的电工、木匠、水管工、机械师。四个职业名词并列，营造夸张的喜剧效果。

  6. **put things right** (l.20) — 固定搭配，"把东西修好/恢复正常"。= *fix things / put things in order*。同类：*The mechanic promised to put the car right by Friday.*

  7. **as good as new** (l.32) — = **almost, virtually, practically**，"几乎像新的一样"。**as good as** 是一个固定短语，用于修饰形容词或动词。同类：*The car is as good as new after the repair. / He as good as admitted he was wrong.*（他几乎等于承认了错误。）

  8. **The mower firmly refused to mow** (l.33) — **拟人 (personification)**。割草机"坚决拒绝割草"——这种把无生命物体当作有意志的主体来写的笔法，让全篇从一本正经的社会议论滑入轻松的幽默叙述，是 NCE3 课文常见的"文风切换"技巧。
</Callout>

## 3. 关键句型 So...that... 倒装 + 分词状语 + 平行结构

<Callout type="tip" title="一分钟回顾">
  本课有三把"高级句型工具"，把一篇讲 DIY 修理失败的文章写出了正式议论文的质感：

  | 结构 | 课文例句 | 用途 |
  |---|---|---|
  | **So + adj + 倒装 ..., that ...** | *So great is our passion ... that we are becoming ...* | 强调程度，so 提前→主谓倒装 |
  | **过去分词短语作状语** | *Armed with the right tools and materials, newlyweds gaily embark on ...* | 交代伴随状态/原因，逻辑主语=主句主语 |
  | **not only ... but (also) ...** | *not only by running ... but by offering ...* | 平行递进，两边语法结构必须对称 |
  | **as good as** | *it would be as good as new* | = almost, virtually（几乎，差不多）|
  | **get round to + doing** | *I had never got round to it* | = find time to do（抽出时间做）|
  | **needless to say** | *Needless to say our house is now surrounded ...* | = of course, as you can imagine（不用说）|

  ### ⚠️ 4 个本课最容易踩的雷区

  1. **So great is our passion that ...** — 倒装只发生在主句（so + adj 提前），**that 从句语序正常**。不能说 *So great is our passion that are we becoming ...* ❌
  2. **Armed with ...** — 分词短语的逻辑主语必须是**主句主语**。*Armed with tools, the house was decorated.* ❌（房子不能被 armed）→ *Armed with tools, they decorated the house.* ✅
  3. **not only ... but ...** — 两边必须**平行**：not only **by doing A** but **by doing B**。*not only by running services but also offering pieces* ❌（两边结构不对称）→ *not only by running ... but by offering ...* ✅
  4. **get round to + doing** — to 是**介词**（不是不定式符号），后面跟**动名词**。*get round to repair* ❌ → *get round to repairing* ✅（教材 Structure 第 7 题直接考这个点）
</Callout>

## 4. 难点：动词 / 名词辨析 Special difficulties

<Callout type="info" title="6 组本课最易混淆的近义词">
  **1. plead / beg / entreat / implore**
  - **plead**：以……为借口 / 辩解（中性偏正式）。*He pleaded ignorance of the law.*
  - **beg**：可怜地一再恳求（带情感色彩）。*The child begged for more candy.*
  - **entreat**：反复请求以期说服对方（正式）。*She entreated him to reconsider.*
  - **implore**：迫切、痛苦地恳求（最重）。*He implored her forgiveness.*

  **2. keen / eager / anxious**
  - **keen**：因强烈兴趣而热衷。*He is keen on photography.*
  - **eager**：迫不及待想拥有/做某事。*She is eager to start her new job.*
  - **anxious**：因担忧/焦虑而"渴望"（= worried + eager）。*They were anxious about the exam results.*

  **3. assume / presume**
  - **assume**：主观武断地认为（无充分证据）。*I assumed he was guilty, but I was wrong.*
  - **presume**：基于已知事实的合理推测。*She is missing — we presume she is dead.*

  **4. delusion / illusion**
  - **delusion** 语气更重，常指"妄想"（病态或固执的错误观念）。*live under the delusion that ...*
  - **illusion** 指"错觉、幻象"（常与视觉/认知有关）。*an optical illusion*（视错觉）

  **5. assemble / dismantle**
  - 互为反义词。**assemble** = 组装；**dismantle** = 拆卸。*assemble the furniture ↔ dismantle the machine*

  **6. adjust / adapt**
  - **adjust**：小幅调整使之合适（机械/参数）。*adjust the screw*
  - **adapt**：改变以适应新环境/用途。*adapt to the new climate*
</Callout>

## 10. 摘要写作 Summary writing (≤ 80 词)

<Callout type="info" title="任务">
  *In not more than 80 words* describe **what happened after the writer took the lawn mower into the garden. Do not include anything that is not in the last paragraph.**

  按下面的提示点串成一段：

  1. 作者最初的判断是什么（minor adjustment 就够）？
  2. 修理为什么升级为拆卸（mower refused to work）？
  3. 拆卸后发现了什么毛病（snapped chain link）？
  4. 重新组装遇到了什么困难（confusing jigsaw puzzle + leftover bits）？
  5. 最终结果如何（gave up → grass grew → jungle → rusting mower）？
</Callout>

<Callout type="tip" title="参考范文（自己写完再看）">
  The writer thought the mower needed only a minor adjustment, but it still refused to work, so he dismantled it. He traced the trouble to a snapped chain link and bought a new one. However, reassembling the machine proved an insurmountable task — he was left with several bits of metal that did not seem to fit anywhere. He gave up in despair. The grass grew unchecked, and his house is now surrounded by a jungle. Somewhere in the deep grass lies a rusting mower. *(79 words)*
</Callout>
## 11. 作文 Composition (约 300 词)

<Callout type="info" title="任务">
  Write a composition in about **300 words** on one of the following:

  **a.** Write a short story entitled **"It's only me — I can fix it!"**

  **b.** Describe an occasion when you (or someone you know) tried to repair or make something yourself and it went wrong.
</Callout>

<Callout type="warning" title="提示骨架（以 b 题为例）">
  - **开头（设定场景）**：What was broken / what needed making? Why did you decide to do it yourself rather than call a professional?
  - **中间（行动→翻车）**：Describe your initial confidence (*As far as I could see, it needed only a minor adjustment ...*). What went wrong? What unexpected complications arose? Use NCE3-style precision verbs: *dismantle, assemble, trace the cause, snap, tighten, screw*.
  - **高潮（最糟时刻）**：The moment you realized you were in over your head — leftover parts, a machine that still won't work, a bigger mess than when you started.
  - **结尾（幽默收场）**：How did it end? Borrow the writer's self-deprecating tone. Perhaps *"Needless to say, ..."* or *"I have promised to ... one day."*
</Callout>

<Callout type="tip" title="写作脚手架">
  - **时态主线**：叙事用**一般过去时 + 过去完成时**（had broken down, had promised, had snapped）。
  - **DIY 动作词库**：*dismantle, assemble, tighten, screw, unscrew, adjust, trace, snap, fix, repair, mend, hammer, nail, drill, replace, reassemble*。
  - **"翻车"表达库**：*refuse to work, make things worse, in over one's head, give up in despair, littered with, bits and pieces that don't fit anywhere, a bigger mess than when one started*。
  - **结构亮点**：开头用**过去分词状语**（*Armed with ...* / *Determined to ...*），结尾用 **needless to say** 收束，全文用课文式的"一本正经→幽默翻车"文风。
</Callout>

<Callout type="tip" title="参考范文（自己写完再看）">
  Armed with a YouTube tutorial and a brand-new set of screwdrivers, I sat down one Sunday to fix my laptop's noisy fan. "It's only a matter of cleaning it out," I told myself. "No point paying a repair shop eighty pounds for something I can do in twenty minutes."

  Twenty minutes turned into four hours. The first half-hour went well: I unscrewed the back panel and blasted the fan with compressed air. But when I reassembled the laptop, the screen stayed black. I dismantled it again, traced every cable, tightened every connection, and tried once more — still nothing. The fan, at least, was now perfectly silent.

  By evening my desk was littered with screws of a dozen different sizes, a detached keyboard, and a motherboard that suddenly looked like a confusing jigsaw puzzle. I had been left with three tiny screws which, curiously, did not seem to fit anywhere. That was the moment I knew I had crossed the line from repair to destruction.

  I gave up in despair. The following Monday I walked into the repair shop and placed the laptop — now in a plastic bag, in several pieces — on the counter. The technician raised an eyebrow. "Tried to fix it yourself, did you?"

  Needless to say, the repair cost me a hundred and twenty pounds — forty more than it would have done if I had brought it in on Friday. Somewhere in a drawer I still keep those three leftover screws, and I have promised to find out where they belong one day. *(约 270 词)*
</Callout>
## 12. 拓展练习 Going further

<Callout type="tip" title="自学者建议">
  - **影子跟读 (shadowing)**：用第 1 节的三个 SayIt 按钮跟读 3-5 遍。注意 *So great is our passion* 倒装句中，重音落在 **great** 和 **passion** 上；*Armed with ...* 分词状语要一口气读完，逗号处略微停顿。
  - **复述 (retell)**：合上课文，用 2 分钟把"修割草机翻车"这个故事用自己的话讲一遍。三步走：**动机**（答应修、拖了一年、拒绝请人）→ **行动**（拉进花园、初步判断"小调整"、不奏效、拆开、找到链条断了、买新链条）→ **结果**（装不回去、多余零件、放弃、草长成丛林、生锈的割草机埋在草丛中）。
  - **造句迁移**：用今天的 5 个高价值结构造你自己的句子——
    1. *So + adj + 倒装 ... that ...*（如此……以致……）—— *So excited were the children that they couldn't sleep the night before the trip.*
    2. *过去分词短语作状语* — *Exhausted by the long walk, we collapsed onto the sofa.*
    3. *get round to + doing*（终于抽出时间做）—— *I finally got round to reading that book you recommended.*
    4. *as good as + adj*（几乎）—— *The old car is as good as dead — it won't even start.*
    5. *needless to say*（不用说）—— *He forgot his passport. Needless to say, he missed the flight.*
  - **延伸思考**：本课最妙的一笔是**文风切换**——第一段用正式的议论口吻分析 DIY 社会现象（*So great is our passion ...*），第二段转入半讽刺的人类观察（*Even men who can hardly drive a nail in straight ...*），第三段彻底落入自嘲式的个人叙事（*Needless to say our house is now surrounded by a jungle*）。从"宏观"滑向"微观"，从"一本正经"滑向"幽默无奈"——这种**语调下降 (descending tone)** 的写法，正是 NCE3 很多课文的标志性手法。下次写记叙文时，试试让句子从正式庄重开始，慢慢滑向轻松、自嘲，读者会不知不觉跟着你笑出来。
</Callout>
