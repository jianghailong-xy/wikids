Why?** 带着这个问题读课文，重点留意作者是如何用 **虚拟语气 (If they had only had ... / would have passed unnoticed)** 和 **双重被动/因果链 (were to be replaced / had to be employed)** 来编织一个"福兮祸所伏"的叙事。

## The Text 课文

In democratic countries any efforts to **restrict the freedom of the Press** are rightly condemned. However, this freedom can easily be **abused**. Stories about people often attract far more public attention than political events. Though we may enjoy reading about the lives of others, it is extremely doubtful whether we would equally enjoy reading about ourselves. **Acting on the contention that facts are sacred**, reporters can cause **untold suffering** to individuals by publishing details about their private lives. Newspapers **exert such tremendous influence** that they can not only **bring about** major changes to the lives of ordinary people but can even overthrow a government.

The story of a poor family that **acquired fame and fortune overnight**, dramatically illustrates the power of the press. The family lived in Aberdeen, a small town of 23,000 inhabitants in South Dakota. As the parents had five children, life was a **perpetual struggle** against poverty. They were expecting their sixth child and were faced with even more pressing economic problems. If they had only had one more child, the fact would have passed unnoticed. They would have continued to struggle against economic odds and would have lived **in obscurity**. But they suddenly became the parents of **quintuplets**, four girls and a boy, an event which **radically** changed their lives. The day after the birth of the five children, an aeroplane arrived in Aberdeen bringing sixty reporters and photographers.

**The rise to fame was swift**. Television cameras and newspapers carried the news to everyone in the country. Newspapers and magazines offered the family huge sums for the **exclusive rights** to publish stories and photographs. Gifts **poured in** not only from unknown people, but from baby food and soap manufacturers who wished to advertise their products. The old farmhouse the family lived in was to be replaced by a new $500,000 home. Reporters kept **pressing for** interviews so lawyers had to be employed to act as spokesmen for the family at press conferences. While the five babies were still quietly sleeping in oxygen tents in a hospital **nursery**, their parents were paying the price for fame. It would never again be possible for them to lead normal lives. They had become the victims of **commercialization**, for their names had acquired a market value. Instead of being five new family members, these children had immediately become a **commodity**.

## 课文注释 Notes on the text

<Callout type="info" title="11 个值得停下来嚼一嚼的点">
  1. **restrict the freedom of the Press** (l.1) —— "限制新闻自由"。**the Press** 大写 P，特指"新闻界、报界"（集合名词，= newspapers and journalists collectively）。**restrict** = 限制（restrict sth. to sth. 把……限制在……范围）。同型：*The new law restricts advertising aimed at children.*

  2. **Acting on the contention that facts are sacred** (l.6) —— "按'事实是神圣的'这一论点行事"。**act on** = 按照……行事、依据……采取行动。**contention** = 论点、主张（比 argument 更正式）。同型：*Acting on the doctor's advice, she took a week off.*

  3. **cause untold suffering to ...** (ll.6-7) —— "给……带来无尽的痛苦"。**untold** = 数不尽的、极大的（字面义"说不完的"，untold + 名词 = 无法估量的）。同型：*The war caused untold damage to the region.*

  4. **exert such tremendous influence that ...** (ll.8-9) —— "施加如此巨大的影响，以致……"。**exert influence** 是固定搭配（= 施加影响）；**such ... that ...** 引导结果状语从句。同型：*The teacher exerted such a strong influence on him that he changed his career path.*

  5. **bring about** (l.8) —— "引起、导致"（= cause to happen）。同型：*The internet has brought about a revolution in the way we communicate.*

  6. **perpetual struggle against poverty** (l.12) —— "与贫困的长期斗争"。**perpetual** = 永久的、持续不断的（比 continuous 语气更强，含"没完没了"之意）。**struggle against** = 与……作斗争。同型：*Their life was a perpetual struggle against drought and famine.*

  7. **If they had only had one more child, the fact would have passed unnoticed.** (l.15) —— 这是**与过去事实相反的虚拟条件句**：if 从句用过去完成时 (**had had**)，主句用 **would have + 过去分词**。**passed unnoticed** 中 unnoticed 是过去分词作**主语补足语**（= the fact would have passed and it would have been unnoticed）——"事情就这么过去了，没人会注意"。这是本课最值得背诵的句式。同型：*If he had taken the earlier train, the accident would have been avoided.*

  8. **The rise to fame was swift.** (l.18) —— "很快成名"。这是一个**短句独立成段**，极像新闻标题，在结构上承上启下——从"为什么会出名"过渡到"出名之后发生了什么"。**to fame** 与 **rise** 搭配（= rise to fame 成名）。同型：*Her rise to stardom was even faster.*

  9. **pour in** (l.20) —— "纷纷涌入、源源而来"（= arrive in large quantities）。课文指礼物从四面八方涌来。同型：*Letters of complaint poured in after the programme was broadcast.*

  10. **keep pressing for** (l.22) —— "不断催促、反复要求"。**keep doing** = 反复/持续做；**press for** = 急切要求。同型：*The union kept pressing for better working conditions.* 这也是教材 Vocabulary 第 10 题的考点（made repeated requests for）。

  11. **commodity** (l.26) —— "商品"（可买卖的东西）。课文用它收尾是一个刻意的反差——前面叫 **five new family members**（五个新家庭成员），后面叫 **a commodity**（一件商品）——温暖的人称→冰冷的经济术语，整篇文章的论点全压在这个词上。这也是教材 Vocabulary 第 12 题的考点。
</Callout>

## 3. 关键句型 虚拟语气与被动语态 Subjunctive & passive voice

<Callout type="tip" title="一分钟回顾">
  本课是一篇"论证 + 例证"式议论文——第 1 段立论（新闻自由可以被滥用），第 2-3 段用一个真实故事证明论点。语法上靠两组结构撑起全文：**虚拟语气（与过去事实相反）** 和 **被动语态 / be + to do 表将来安排**。

  | 结构 | 课文例句 | 用途 |
  |---|---|---|
  | **If + had done, would have done** | *If they **had only had** one more child, the fact **would have passed** unnoticed.* | 与过去事实相反的虚拟条件 |
  | **过去分词作主语补足语** | *the fact would have passed **unnoticed*** | 表示伴随状态 |
  | **was/were to be + 过去分词** | *The old farmhouse **was to be replaced** by a new home.* | 表"计划/注定要被……" |
  | **被动语态 + 不定式目的** | *Lawyers **had to be employed** to act as spokesmen.* | 被动的不定式表"不得不被……" |
  | **主动 ↔ 被动改写** | *Newspapers offered the family huge sums. ↔ The family were offered huge sums.* | 双宾语动词的两种被动形式 |

  ### ⚠️ 4 个本课最容易踩的雷区

  1. **If they had only had one more child**——第一个 had 是过去完成时的助动词，第二个 had 是实义动词"有"的过去分词。**had had** 不是重复错误，是过去完成时！（= if they had possessed one more child）
  2. **passed unnoticed**——unnoticed 是过去分词作**主语补足语**（= the fact passed, and was unnoticed）。不是被动语态（the fact was not "unnoticed" by someone），而是分词表伴随状态。
  3. **was to be replaced**——**be + to do** 表"按计划/注定将要"（= was destined to be replaced）。不是 was replaced（已被取代），而是"将被取代"。
  4. **The family were offered...**——英式英语中 **family / team / press** 等集合名词常用**复数谓语**（were, not was），因为强调"家庭成员"。改写为被动时，双宾语动词 give/offer/send 有两种被动：*They were offered huge sums*（间接宾语变主语）或 *Huge sums were offered to them*（直接宾语变主语）。
</Callout>

## 4. 难点：双宾语动词的被动改写 Special difficulties

<Callout type="info" title="教材 Key structures 改写题">
  教材要求：在**不改变意思**的前提下改写下面这句（参照 ll.19-20）：

  > The family were offered huge sums by newspapers and magazines.
  > Newspapers and magazines ...

  **解题钥匙**：把被动句还原为主动句。原句是被动语态，by 后面是动作的执行者（newspapers and magazines），将其变为主语，动词改为主动语态——**offered**（一般过去时）。

  > Newspapers and magazines **offered the family huge sums**.

  ### 双宾语动词（give / offer / send / lend / tell 等）的两种被动

  1. **间接宾语变主语**（更常用）：*The family were offered huge sums.*
  2. **直接宾语变主语**：*Huge sums were offered to the family.*（注意加 to）

  主动 → 被动时要判断：原句用的是哪种被动形式？还原时直接宾语（huge sums）和间接宾语（the family）都不能丢。
</Callout>

## 10. 摘要写作 Summary writing (≤ 80 词)

<Callout type="info" title="任务">
  *In not more than 80 words* describe **how the birth of the five children affected the family and the town**. Use your own words as far as possible. **Do not include anything that is not in the last paragraph**（只用最后一段的信息）。

  按下面的提示点串成一段：

  1. 成名有多快？（电视 + 报纸在全国传播消息）
  2. 报社和杂志做了什么？（出高价买独家版权）
  3. 哪些人送来了礼物？（陌生人 + 婴儿食品/肥皂制造商——他们想要什么？）
  4. 旧农舍后来怎样了？（将被价值 50 万美元的新房取代）
  5. 记者不断要求采访→家庭怎么应对？（聘请律师在记者招待会上充当发言人）
  6. 结尾的代价是什么？（再也无法过正常生活、成了商业化的牺牲品、孩子变成了商品）
</Callout>

<Callout type="tip" title="参考范文（自己写完再看）">
  The family's rise to fame was swift: television and newspapers spread the news across the country. Newspapers and magazines paid large sums for exclusive rights, while gifts poured in from strangers and from manufacturers eager to advertise. The old farmhouse was to be replaced by a $500,000 home. Reporters pressed so hard for interviews that lawyers had to be hired as spokesmen. In the end, the parents could never lead normal lives again — they had become victims of commercialization, and their children had become a commodity. *(76 words)*
</Callout>

## 11. 作文 Composition (约 300 词)

<Callout type="info" title="任务">
  Write a composition in about **300 words** on one of the following:

  **a.** Write a 'human interest' story about a person who suddenly acquired fame because he won a lot of money from football pools.

  **b.** Describe the newspaper you most enjoy reading.
</Callout>

<Callout type="warning" title="提示骨架（以 a 题为例）">
  - **开头（一个普通人）**：描写这个人的平凡生活——*He was the kind of man nobody noticed: a clerk in a shipping office, a face in the crowd*。
  - **转折（中奖那刻）**：*Then one Saturday evening, the numbers came up. Half a million pounds. His name was in every paper.*
  - **成名之后**：记者蜂拥而至（参照本课课文）——*Photographers at his door, old friends suddenly appearing, charities and strangers writing letters.*
  - **生活的改变**：新房子、辞掉工作、亲朋态度变了——哪些变好？哪些变坏？
  - **反思 / 代价**：*He had wanted money all his life, but fame was a different thing. He found himself lonely and watched.* —— 呼应本课"paying the price for fame"。
  - **结尾（回到"人"）**：用一句有分量的话收束——胜似课文的 *a commodity*。
</Callout>

<Callout type="tip" title="写作脚手架">
  - **时态主线**：叙事用一般过去时，反思用过去完成时/虚拟语气（*He had never imagined ... / If he had known ...*）。
  - **"名利"词库**：*fame, fortune, a windfall, overnight celebrity, the lottery of life, a stranger to himself*。
  - **"媒体围堵"词库**：*camp on his doorstep, flashbulbs, besiege with questions, have no peace, every move reported*。
  - **结构亮点**：模仿本课的"立论→故事→代价"三段论——第 1 段写这个人中奖前的平凡（"before"），第 2 段写中奖后的疯狂变化（"the rise"），第 3 段写代价与反思（"the price"）。开头和结尾形成前后呼应。
</Callout>

<Callout type="tip" title="参考范文（自己写完再看）">
  George Timms was the kind of man nobody noticed. For thirty-two years he had sat at the same desk in a Birmingham shipping office, stamping invoices and eating cheese sandwiches alone at lunch. On Saturday afternoons he filled in his football pools coupon with the same quiet hope he had always had — and one Saturday evening in November, every number came up. Half a million pounds. By Monday morning his name was in every national newspaper.

  The rise to fame was even swifter than the money reaching his bank account. A photograph of George, taken from his niece's wedding, appeared on the front pages. Reporters camped on his doorstep; old school friends he had not seen in forty years rang to congratulate him; charities, strangers, and distant cousins wrote letters by the sackful. A television crew followed him for three days. He bought a big house with a long drive, gave up his job, and tried to enjoy being somebody at last.

  Yet George soon discovered what the papers never printed. The friends who reappeared wanted loans, not lunch. His sister stopped speaking to him over a gift he had not thought to make. Everywhere he went — the pub, the post office, even the park — he felt watched, appraised, no longer free to be ordinary. The man who had wanted money all his life found that fame was a harder thing to spend.

  If you had asked George Timms, a year later, whether he would do it again, he would have looked at his big empty house and said, very quietly, that half a million pounds was a great deal of money, but invisibility was worth more. *(约 290 词)*
</Callout>

## 12. 书信写作 Letter writing (约 100 词)

<Callout type="info" title="任务">
  Write a letter of about **100 words** to the **Editor** of a newspaper **complaining about an article** that was published recently. Begin *"Dear Sir"* and end *"Yours faithfully"*.
</Callout>

<Callout type="tip" title="写作脚手架">
  - **格式**：正式信函——*Dear Sir* → 正文 → *Yours faithfully* → 签名。没有收信人姓名时用 Dear Sir，结束用 Yours faithfully。
  - **开头**：说明写信目的——*I am writing to complain about an article which appeared in your newspaper on [date] entitled "…"*
  - **主体（投诉什么）**——挑 2-3 个具体问题：
    1. 事实有误——*The article stated that ... In fact, ...*
    2. 侵犯隐私——*It published private details about ... without their consent.*
    3. 误导读者——*The headline was completely misleading ...*
  - **要求**：*I would ask you to publish a correction / an apology.*
  - **时态要点**：描述文章内容用一般过去时；陈述事实/提要求用一般现在时。
</Callout>

<Callout type="tip" title="参考范文（自己写完再看）">
  Dear Sir,

  I am writing to complain about an article entitled "Lottery Winner's Dark Past", which appeared in your newspaper on 15th June.

  The article stated that Mr. David Clarke had been dismissed from his previous job for dishonesty. This is completely untrue. Mr. Clarke left that position voluntarily four years ago to care for his elderly mother, a fact your reporter could easily have verified. Furthermore, the article published his home address and a photograph of his young children, taken without permission. This is a clear invasion of privacy and has caused the family considerable distress.

  I would ask you to publish a full correction and an apology at the earliest opportunity.

  Yours faithfully,

  James Horton *(约 100 词)*
</Callout>

## 13. 拓展练习 Going further

<Callout type="tip" title="自学者建议">
  - **影子跟读 (shadowing)**：用第 1 节的三个 SayIt 按钮跟读 3-5 遍。注意 *If they had only had one more child* 中两个 had 的弱读（第一个 had 轻、第二个 had 略重）；*swift* 最后的 /t/ 要轻而准。
  - **复述 (retell)**：合上课文，用 2 分钟把本文的故事讲一遍。**三步叙事**：他们是谁（穷、五个孩子、等着第六个）→ 发生了什么（变成五胞胎父母 → 记者蜂拥 → 一夜富裕）→ 代价是什么（再也无法过正常生活、成了商业化的牺牲品、孩子成了商品）。
  - **造句迁移**：用今天的 5 个高价值结构造你自己的句子——
    1. *restrict ... to ...*（限制）—— *The school restricts phone use to lunch breaks only.*
    2. *act on the contention that ...*（按照"……"的论点行事）—— *The company acted on the contention that customer trust is more important than short-term profit.*
    3. *bring about*（引起、导致）—— *The new policy brought about a dramatic improvement in air quality.*
    4. *If ... had done, ... would have done*（与过去相反的虚拟）—— *If she had studied medicine, she would have become a doctor.*
    5. *was to be replaced by ...*（"注定/计划要被……取代"）—— *The old bridge was to be replaced by a modern suspension bridge.*
  - **延伸思考**：本课最厉害的地方不在用词，而在**结构设计**：第 1 段是"总论点"（新闻自由可以被滥用、报纸影响力大到可以推翻政府），第 2-3 段是"一个例证"（一个家庭如何在一夜之间从默默无闻变成牺牲品）。这是经典的**"论点 + 例证"英文议论文结构**（thesis + exemplification）。如果你要写一篇议论文——比如"社交媒体对青少年的影响"——试试套这个框架：先写一段总论点（社交媒体的影响力有多强、可能怎样被滥用），再用一个真实/虚构的小故事"戏剧性地说明"（dramatically illustrate）你的论点。另一个值得学的技巧是本课的**收尾**——最后一句 *Instead of being five new family members, these children had immediately become a commodity.* 用一个强烈的前后对比（温暖→冰冷），不需要再多说一个字，全文的立场已经一目了然。好的议论文结尾不是"总结"，而是"一击必中"。
</Callout>
