## The Text 课文

Most of us have **formed an unrealistic picture** of life on a desert island. We sometimes **imagine a desert island to be** a sort of paradise where the sun always shines. Life there is simple and good. Ripe fruit falls from the trees and you never have to work. The other side of the picture is **quite the opposite**. Life on a desert island is wretched. You either **starve to death** or live like Robinson Crusoe, waiting for a boat which never comes. Perhaps there is **an element of truth** in both these pictures, but **few of us have had the opportunity to find out**.

Two men who recently spent five days on a coral island **wished they had stayed there longer**. They were taking a badly damaged boat from the Virgin Islands to Miami **to have it repaired**. During the journey, their boat began to sink. They quickly loaded a small rubber dinghy with food, matches, and cans of beer and rowed for a few miles across the Caribbean until they arrived at a tiny coral island. There were hardly any trees on the island and there was no water, but this **did not prove to be a problem**. The men collected rainwater in the rubber dinghy. **As they had brought a spear gun with them**, they had plenty to eat. They caught lobster and fish every day, and, **as one of them put it** 'ate like kings'. When a passing tanker rescued them five days later, both men were **genuinely sorry that they had to leave**.

## 课文注释 Notes on the text

<Callout type="info" title="5 个值得停下来嚼一嚼的点">
  1. **imagine ... to be ...** (l.2) — “想像……是……”“设想……是……”。不定式 **to be** 引起的短语在这里作**宾语补足语**（imagine + 宾语 + to be + 补足语）。同型：*We imagined the journey to be easy.*（我们设想这趟旅程很轻松。）口语里也常省略 to be：*We imagined it (to be) a paradise.*

  2. **quite the opposite** (l.5) — “完全相反，截然不同”。**quite** 在这里是“完全、十足”的意思（修饰 the opposite 这个名词性短语），不是“相当、有点”。同型：*I expected him to be rude, but he was quite the opposite — charming and kind.*

  3. **starve to death** (l.6) — “饿死”。**动词 + to death** 表示“……死、……到死的地步”：*freeze to death*（冻死）、*be bored to death*（无聊死了）、*be frightened to death*（吓得要死）。

  4. **wished they had stayed there longer** (l.9) — “……还真希望在那里多呆些日子。”**wish** 引起的宾语从句常用**虚拟语气**：对过去的“但愿”用**过去完成时 (had + 过去分词)**。言下之意是“当时没多呆，有点遗憾”。同型：*I wish I had studied harder.*（真希望当初用功些。）参见第 4 节难点 **Wish and If only**。

  5. **as one of them put it** (l.15) — “正如其中一位所说的”。**put** 在这里 = “表达、说”（put it = 这么说/这么表述）。这是一个插入语，常用来引出别人的原话或妙语。同型：*As the saying puts it, time is money. / To put it simply, we were lucky.*
</Callout>

## 3. 关键句型 条件句与虚拟语气 Conditionals & the unreal past

<Callout type="tip" title="一分钟回顾">
  本课的语法主线是 **if 条件句 (IKS 88)** 与 **wish 引出的虚拟语气**——两者其实是一家人，核心都是“**用过去的时态表示与现实相反的假设**”。

  | 类型 | 结构 | 课文/例句 | 表达什么 |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | **真实条件** | If + 现在时, will + 动原 | *You will be disappointed if you go.* | 将来很可能发生 |
  | **与现在相反** | If + 过去式, would + 动原 | *If I were you, I would stay.* | 与现在事实相反的假设 |
  | **与过去相反** | If + had done, would have done | *If you had told me earlier, I would have come.* | 对过去“本可以……”的假设 |
  | **wish + 过去式** | wish (that) + 过去式 | *I wish I had more time.* | 但愿“现在”是另一种样子 |
  | **wish + had done** | wish (that) + had + 过去分词 | *They wished they had stayed longer.* (l.9) | 对“过去”的遗憾、但愿 |

  ### ⚠️ 4 个本课最容易踩的雷区

  1. **be 动词在虚拟里一律用 were**：与现在相反的假设，不论主语单复数，都用 **were**——*If I **were** you* / *If only the weather **were** fine*（口语里有时用 was，但考试和书面语用 were 最稳）。
  2. **“与过去相反”要用过去完成时**：*wished they **had stayed*** = 当时没多呆（已经过去）；写成 *wished they stayed* 就丢了“对过去的遗憾”这层意思。
  3. **主句别再用 will**：一旦是假设（与现在/过去相反），主句必须用 **would / could / might**，不能用 will——这是最常见的“一半虚拟一半真实”混搭错误。
  4. **after + 动名词**：表“做完某事之后”，after 后接 **doing**——课文同款句式 *After **rowing** a few miles, they arrived ...*（教材 Structure 第 6 题），不要写成 after rowed / after to row。
</Callout>

## 4. 难点：Wish 与 If only Special difficulties

<Callout type="info" title="教材难点讲解：Wish and If only">
  在英语中，表示愿望可用动词 **wish** 或短语 **if only**。其中 **wish** 常指尚有可能发生的事情，而 **if only** 则**强调所希望的状况并不存在**（语气更强烈，常带感叹）。在 wish 和 if only 后面：

  | 想表达 | 用什么时态 | 例句 |
  |---|---|---|
  | **与现在相反** | 用**过去时** | *I wish she **could** see me now. / If only the weather **would** change.* |
  | **与过去相反** | 用**过去完成时** | *I wish I **hadn't said** anything about it. / If only we **could have gone** to the party!* |
  | **希望对方改变行为** | 用 **would** | *I wish you **would** do as you are told. / If only you **would** try a little harder!* |

  一句话记牢：**wish / if only 后面，“用过去时表示现在，用过去完成时表示过去。”**
</Callout>

## 10. 摘要写作 Summary writing (≤ 80 词)

<Callout type="info" title="任务">
  *In not more than 80 words* explain **how the two men came to spend five days on a desert island and say what they did there**. **Do not include anything that is not in the last paragraph**（只用最后一段的信息）。

  按下面 10 个提示点串成一段（教材给定）：

  1. 他们的船是好是坏？ 2. 他们要把船开去哪里？ 3. 途中船出了什么事？
  4. 他们往橡皮艇上装了什么？ 5. 他们划向何方？ 6. 他们到了哪里？
  7. 在岛上他们靠什么取水？ 8. 他们怎样捕到鱼和龙虾？
  9. 他们 5 天里是不是都“吃得像国王”？ 10. 他们最后怎样获救？
</Callout>

<Callout type="tip" title="参考范文（自己写完再看）">
  Two men were taking a badly damaged boat to Miami to be repaired when it began to sink. They loaded a rubber dinghy with food, matches and beer and rowed to a tiny coral island. Though it had no water, they collected rainwater in the dinghy, and, having brought a spear gun, caught lobster and fish every day, so they ate like kings. After five days a passing tanker rescued them. *(70 words)*
</Callout>

## 11. 作文 Composition (≤ 200 词)

<Callout type="info" title="任务">
  Imagine spending **two weeks on an uninhabited desert island**. In not more than **200 words**, describe what you did there. Use the ideas given below. **Do not write more than three paragraphs.** *Title: Shipwrecked.*
</Callout>

<Callout type="warning" title="提示骨架（教材给定）">
  - **Introduction（引子）**：Shipwreck — everybody drowned — I clung to a plank — washed up on island.（海难——其他人都淹死了——我抱住一块木板——被冲上岛。）
  - **Development（展开）**：Slept for a long time — woke up — hungry and thirsty — explored island — uninhabited — found plenty of fruit — freshwater spring — tried to hunt wild animals — failed to catch anything — spent days swimming, lying in sun.
  - **Conclusion（结尾）**：Boat on horizon — signalled and shouted — rescued.
</Callout>

<Callout type="tip" title="写作脚手架">
  - **时态主线**：全篇是过去发生的经历，用**一般过去时**为主（was / woke / explored / found / failed），背景铺垫可用过去完成时（the ship **had sunk**）。
  - **“海难求生”动词库**：*be shipwrecked, cling to a plank, be washed ashore, explore, hunt, signal, wave, be rescued*。
  - **“岛上风物”词库**：*a freshwater spring, ripe fruit, a sandy beach, the burning sun, the horizon*。
  - **结构亮点**：三段对应 引子→展开→结尾，正好三个画面；结尾用“地平线上出现一条船”制造转折与释然，呼应课文“获救”的母题。
</Callout>

<Callout type="tip" title="参考范文（自己写完再看）">
  **Shipwrecked**

  Our ship went down in a storm one black night. Everybody else drowned; I survived only because I clung to a wooden plank and was washed up, half dead, on a strange shore.

  I slept for a long time on the warm sand. When I woke, I was desperately hungry and thirsty, so I explored the island. It was completely uninhabited, but I soon found plenty of ripe fruit and, to my great joy, a freshwater spring. For food I tried to hunt the wild animals I glimpsed among the trees, but I was clumsy and failed to catch anything. In the end I gave up hunting and spent my days swimming in the warm sea and lying in the sun, living quite happily on fruit and spring water.

  Two weeks later, I saw a boat on the horizon. I signalled with my shirt, shouted until my voice cracked, and lit a smoky fire. To my enormous relief, the crew saw me, rowed ashore, and rescued me at last. *(约 175 词)*
</Callout>

## 12. 书信写作 Letter writing (≤ 60 词)

<Callout type="info" title="任务">
  In not more than **60 words**, write a suitable *Purpose* and *Conclusion* to follow this introductory paragraph（续写一段“目的 + 结尾”，承接下面这段开头）：

  > Dear Judy,
  >
  > We arrived here late last night and are staying at a charming little hotel by the sea. The weather is perfect and I am sure we are going to enjoy our holiday.
</Callout>

<Callout type="tip" title="写作脚手架">
  - **Purpose（写信目的）**：度假写信通常是想**邀请对方来玩**或**请对方帮个小忙**——挑一个写。本范文取“邀请你来同住几天”。
  - **常用句**：*The real reason I'm writing is to ask whether ... / Why don't you come and join us for a few days?*
  - **Conclusion（结尾）**：给出具体安排 + 期待回复 —— *There's a spare room with your name on it. Do let me know which train you can catch.*
  - **结束语**：*Love, / Best wishes, + 名字*。
</Callout>

<Callout type="tip" title="参考范文（自己写完再看）">
  The real reason I'm writing is to tempt you into joining us. There is a spare room at the hotel with your name on it, and the beach is far too lovely to enjoy alone. Why not catch the Friday train and spend a week with us? Do let me know soon.

  Love,

  Anna *(约 55 词)*
</Callout>

## 13. 拓展练习 Going further

<Callout type="tip" title="自学者建议">
  - **影子跟读 (shadowing)**：用第 1 节的三个 SayIt 按钮跟读 3-5 遍。注意 *starve to **death*** / *ate like **kings*** 这类短语里，重音通常落在最后那个实词上；*had brought* 里的 had 要读得轻而快。
  - **复述 (retell)**：合上课文，用 2 分钟把故事讲一遍。**四步链条**：弃船（boat began to sink）→ 上艇逃生（loaded the dinghy, rowed to a coral island）→ 自给自足（rainwater in the dinghy, spear gun for food, ate like kings）→ 获救却不舍（a tanker rescued them, genuinely sorry to leave）。
  - **造句迁移**：用今天的 5 个高价值结构造你自己的句子——
    1. *form an unrealistic picture of ...*（对……有不切实际的想象）—— *Many tourists form an unrealistic picture of city life.*
    2. *quite the opposite*（恰恰相反）—— *I thought he was shy; in fact he is quite the opposite.*
    3. *have + 物 + done*（让某物被……）—— *I must have my bike repaired this weekend.*
    4. *wish + had done*（但愿当初……）—— *I wish I had learned to swim as a child.*
    5. *as ... put it*（正如……所说）—— *As my teacher put it, practice makes perfect.*
  - **延伸思考**：本课是一则“**反差叙事**”——开篇先立两幅极端的“想象”（天堂 vs. 地狱），再用一桩真事告诉你现实往往落在两者之间，甚至出人意料地美好。这种“**先立刻板印象，再用事实打破它**”的写法，很适合用在记叙文或议论文的开头。语言上，留意作者如何用 **wish / waiting for a boat which never comes** 等虚拟、假设的笔触渲染“想象”，又用 **过去完成时 (had brought)** 把“先因后果”讲得井井有条——下次你写“计划之外的意外收获”这类故事时，这两件工具都用得上。
</Callout>
