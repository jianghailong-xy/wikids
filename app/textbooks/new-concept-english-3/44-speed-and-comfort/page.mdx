  火车、汽车、轮船还是飞机——你长途旅行会选哪一种？本课作者把四种交通方式从头到脚比了一遍：火车憋闷催睡，汽车比火车还难受，轮船倒是舒服可又慢又容易晕，只有飞机才是 **又快捷又舒适（speed and comfort）** 的王牌。这是一篇经典的"对比论证"说明文，作者用 **so...that...** 结果状语从句和一系列 **比较结构** 把四个选项的优劣逐一"称量"，最后的结论干脆利落——下了飞机，你精神抖擞、衣冠整齐，根本不用花几天"缓过来"。**Which type of transport does the writer prefer, do you think?** 带着这个问题读课文，重点体会作者如何层层递进地"淘汰"前三项、用 **nothing can match...for...** 把飞机推到最高点。
</Callout>

## The Text 课文

People travelling long distances frequently have to decide whether they would prefer to go by land, sea, or air. Hardly anyone can positively enjoy sitting in a train for more than a few hours. Train compartments soon get cramped and stuffy. It is almost impossible to **take your mind off** the journey. Reading is only a partial solution, for the monotonous rhythm of the wheels clicking on the rails soon lulls you to sleep. During the day, sleep comes **in snatches**. At night, when you really wish to go to sleep, you rarely manage to do so. If you are lucky enough to get a sleeper, you spend half the night staring at the small blue light in the ceiling, or fumbling to find your ticket for inspection. Inevitably you arrive at your destination almost exhausted. Long car journeys are even less pleasant, for it is quite impossible even to read. On motorways you can, at least, travel fairly safely at high speeds, but **more often than not**, the greater part of the journey is spent on roads with few service stations and too much traffic. **By comparison**, ferry trips or cruises offer a great variety of civilized comforts. You can stretch your legs on the spacious decks, play games, meet interesting people and enjoy good food — always assuming, of course, that the sea is calm. If it is not, and you are likely to get seasick, no form of transport could be worse. Even if you travel in ideal weather, sea journeys take a long time. Relatively few people are prepared to sacrifice holiday time for the pleasure of travelling by sea.

Aeroplanes have the reputation of being dangerous and even hardened travellers are intimidated by them. They also have the disadvantage of being an expensive form of transport. But **nothing can match them for speed and comfort**. Travelling at a height of 30,000 feet, far above the clouds, and at over 500 miles an hour is an exhilarating experience. You do not have to devise ways of taking your mind off the journey, for an aeroplane gets you to your destination rapidly. For a few hours, you settle back in a deep armchair to enjoy the flight. The real escapist can watch a film and sip champagne on some services. But even when such refinements are not available, there is plenty to **keep you occupied**. An aeroplane offers you an unusual and breathtaking view of the world. You soar effortlessly over high mountains and deep valleys. You really see the shape of the land. If the landscape is hidden from view, you can enjoy the extraordinary sight of unbroken cloud plains that stretch out for miles before you, while the sun shines brilliantly in a clear sky. The journey is **so smooth that** there is nothing to prevent you from reading or sleeping. However you decide to spend your time, one thing is certain: you will arrive at your destination **fresh and uncrumpled**. You will not have to spend the next few days **recovering from** a long and arduous journey.

## 课文注释 Notes on the text

<Callout type="info" title="7 个值得停下来嚼一嚼的点">
  1. **take your mind off the journey** (l.5) — "把心思从旅途上移开"。**take one's mind off sth.** 是一个很常用的短语，意思是"使某人不再想某事"。同型：*A good film can take your mind off your worries.* 反义表达是 **keep one's mind on sth.**（把心思集中在……上）。

  2. **in snatches** (l.7) — "断断续续地"。**snatch** 本身作名词意思是"片刻、短时的一段"，in snatches 描述的是一种不连续的状态。同型：*He slept in snatches during the long flight.* 也可以用 **in fits and starts** 表达类似意思。对比下文的 *manage to do so*（第 8 行），强调"入睡"这件事做着有多难。

  3. **more often than not** (l.11) — "多半、通常"。这是一个高频书面短语，等于 **usually / as often as not**。用它来淡化绝对语气。同型：*More often than not, the cheapest option turns out to be the most expensive in the long run.*

  4. **By comparison** (l.12) — "相比之下、比较起来"。放在句首，引出与前文（火车 / 汽车）完全相反的另一种体验（轮船）。写作中 **By comparison / In contrast / On the other hand** 都是段落转折的标志词。这里作者用对比来推进论证："前两种已经很糟了，轮船好歹……但……"——先扬后抑。

  5. **nothing can match them for speed and comfort** (ll.19-20) — "就速度和舒适度而言，没有任何交通工具能与飞机相比"。这是全文的**结论句**，也是课文标题 *Speed and comfort* 的直接出处。结构是 **match + 宾语 + for + 在某方面**，意思是"在……方面比得上"。for 引出比较的维度。同型：*No one can match her for patience. / This city cannot match Paris for romance.*

  6. **keep you occupied** (l.24) — "使你有事可做、让你不无聊"。**keep + 宾语 + occupied**（过去分词作宾补），意思是"让某人一直有事可做"。反过来，飞机上不会"无事可做"。同型：*The puzzles kept the children occupied for hours. / He keeps himself occupied with gardening at weekends.*

  7. **The journey is so smooth that there is nothing to prevent you from reading or sleeping.** (l.28) — 这是本课的**语法核心——so...that... 结果状语从句**。"旅途极其平稳，以至于没有什么能阻止你阅读或睡觉。"结构：**so + 形容词/副词 + that + 结果从句**。教材 *Key structures* 把原句 *You travel so smoothly that...* 改写为 *The journey is so smooth that...*，把副词 smoothly 转成形容词 smooth，让主语变成 journey（更自然地统领全句）。同型：*She was so tired that she fell asleep at her desk. / The music was so loud that we couldn't hear each other.*
</Callout>

## 3. 关键句型 so...that... 结果状语从句与比较结构

<Callout type="tip" title="一分钟回顾">
  本课有两组语法工具值得整块拿下：**so...that... 结果状语从句**（教材 Key structures 改写题的核心考点）和贯穿全文的**比较结构**（作者靠它在四种交通方式之间做"淘汰赛"式的论证）。

  | 结构 | 课文例句 | 用途 |
  |---|---|---|
  | **so + adj./adv. + that...** | *The journey is **so smooth that** there is nothing to prevent you from reading or sleeping.* | "如此……以至于……"——表结果 |
  | **such (+ article) + adj. + n. + that...** | *...enjoy the extraordinary sight of **such** unbroken cloud plains **that** stretch out for miles...* | so 修饰形容词/副词，such 修饰名词 |
  | **nothing can match ... for ...** | *Nothing can match them **for** speed and comfort.* | "在……方面无与伦比"——最高级比较 |
  | **more often than not** | *...but **more often than not**, the greater part of the journey is spent on roads...* | 固定短语："多半、通常" |
  | **By comparison** | ***By comparison**, ferry trips or cruises offer a great variety of civilized comforts.* | 段落转折："相比之下" |

  ### ⚠️ 本课 4 个最容易踩的雷区

  1. **so...that ≠ so that**：**so that**（连在一起）引导目的状语（"为了……"），而 **so + adj./adv. + that** 引导结果状语（"如此……以至于"）。本课考的是后者。
  2. **so 与 such 不分**：记住口诀——**so + 形/副，such + 名**。*so smooth that...*（smooth 是形容词）√；*such a smooth journey that...*（smooth 修饰名词 journey）√。不能说 *such smooth that...* ✗。
  3. **nothing can match ... for ...**：这里的 **for** 不是"为了"，而是"在……方面"（= in terms of / as regards）。同型：*No city can match Paris for romance.*
  4. **"so...that" 从句中的 that 可以省略**（非正式），但考试改写题通常要求保留 that，以保持从句的完整性和形式感。
</Callout>

## 4. 难点：match / equal 近义辨析 Special difficulties

<Callout type="info" title="教材 Vocabulary MCQ 第 9 题引出的考点">
  课文 l.19-20：*But nothing can match them for speed and comfort.* 教材 Vocabulary MCQ 第 9 题考你 **match** 和 **equal** 在句中的替换——正确答案是 **equal**（及物动词，不加介词）。

  ### match 与 equal 的使用规则

  | 用法 | 例句 | 备注 |
  |---|---|---|
  | **match + 宾语 + for + 方面** | *Nothing can **match** aeroplanes **for** speed.* | for = 在……方面 |
  | **equal + 宾语 + in + 方面** | *Nothing can **equal** aeroplanes **in** speed.* | in = 在……方面（更常见） |
  | **equal** (及物, 无介词) | *Nothing can **equal** aeroplanes.* | "比得上"（不具体说哪方面） |
  | **be equal to** (形容词短语) | *No other transport **is equal to** aeroplanes.* | 书面语，带 to |

  ### 常见错误

  1. ✗ *match **with** aeroplanes* — match 在"比得上"这个意义上**是及物动词**，不加 with。
  2. ✗ *equal **with** aeroplanes* — equal 作及物动词也不加 with；形容词形式才是 equal to。
  3. ✗ *equal **to** aeroplanes for speed* — equal 作及物动词时直接加宾语；作形容词时才说 *is equal to*。

  其他本课需辨析的近义表达：**by comparison**（相比之下）vs **in contrast**（与之相反——更强调反差）vs **on the other hand**（另一方面——引出对立观点）。

## 11. 作文 Composition (约 300 词)

<Callout type="info" title="任务">
  Write a composition in about **300 words** on one of the following:

  **a.** Which form of transport do you prefer for long distance journeys: train, car, or ship?

  **b.** The disadvantages of travelling by air.
</Callout>

<Callout type="warning" title="提示骨架（以 a 题为例）">
  - **开头**：直接表态——*For long-distance journeys, I would choose ... without hesitation.* 给出两个理由。
  - **第 2 段**：排除第一个选项——为什么 train / car / ship 不如你的选择？用课文里的论据：cramped and stuffy、can't read、seasick、too slow……
  - **第 3 段**：正面论证你的选择——速度、舒适、自由（train 可以看风景 → car 随时停 → ship 有社交空间）。至少两个具体例子。
  - **第 4 段**：承认缺点但回击——*It is true that ... However, ...*——学习课文 also 先承认后反转的写法。
  - **结尾**：呼应开头，简洁收束。
</Callout>

<Callout type="tip" title="写作脚手架">
  - **时态主线**：全篇**一般现在时**（论述个人偏好 + 交通方式的一般特征）。
  - **"比较级 + 转折"词库**：*by comparison, even less, far more, nothing can match, however, on the other hand, admittedly, it is true that ... but ...*
  - **"交通体验"词库**：*compartment, cramped, stuffy, motorway, service station, deck, cabin, terminal, boarding, legroom, aisle, window seat*。
  - **结构亮点**：模仿课文用 **more often than not / by comparison / nothing can match ... for ...** 等短语，让论证有教材的味道。
</Callout>

<Callout type="tip" title="参考范文（自己写完再看）">
  For long-distance journeys, I would choose the train without hesitation — not because it is perfect, but because it strikes the best balance between comfort, cost and experience.

  By comparison, the car is even less pleasant. On a motorway you can travel swiftly, but more often than not you end up stuck in traffic on winding roads with no service stations for miles. Worse still, the driver cannot even glance at the scenery, let alone read a book. As for ships, they do offer spacious decks, good food and interesting company — assuming, of course, that the sea is calm. If it is not, no form of transport could be worse.

  The train, by contrast, hands you the landscape on a plate. Sitting by the window, you watch villages and fields roll by in a silent film. You can read, provided the rhythm of the wheels does not lull you to sleep — but then a nap on a train is not time wasted; it is part of the journey. And at night, if you are lucky enough to get a sleeper, you wake up in a new city as if by magic.

  It is true that trains can be cramped and stuffy, especially during holidays, and they cannot match aeroplanes for speed. However, air travel strips the journey of all texture — you step into a metal tube and step out somewhere else, with nothing in between but a screen and a plastic cup. Trains, for all their faults, remind you that the distance you cover is real, made of earth, stone and sky.

  In the end, I would rather arrive a little tired and full of what I saw than land fresh and remember nothing at all. *(约 290 词)*
</Callout>

## 12. 书信写作 Letter writing (约 100 词)

<Callout type="info" title="任务">
  You had bought a ticket to the theatre but now find that you will be unable to go. Write a letter of about **100 words** to a friend enclosing the ticket and explaining why you are sending it to him.
</Callout>

<Callout type="tip" title="写作脚手架">
  - **称呼**：*Dear + 名*（朋友之间用名字或昵称，亲切直接）。
  - **开头**：点明夹寄了话剧票——*I am enclosing a theatre ticket with this note, because something has come up and I simply cannot make it to the play on Saturday.*
  - **解释原因**——择 1-2 点，简短自然即可（不要说太多）：*My parents are coming to town unexpectedly that weekend, and I have not seen them in months.*
  - **鼓励对方去 + 简要介绍剧**——不让票浪费：*It is a comedy at the Grand Theatre — I have heard terrific things about the cast. You will enjoy it far more than I would have.*
  - **收尾**——希望对方喜欢 + 下次再约：*Let me know how you find it — and let us plan a theatre night together soon.*
  - **结束语**：*Yours, / Best, / Love,* + 你的名字。
  - **时态要点**：写信时用一般现在时（I'm enclosing / I can't make it）；原因用一般现在/将来（parents are coming / I haven't seen them）；交代剧的信息用一般现在（It is a comedy / the cast is terrific）。
</Callout>

<Callout type="tip" title="参考范文（自己写完再看）">
  Dear Emma,

  I am enclosing the theatre ticket I bought for Saturday evening, because I have just heard that my parents are coming to town that weekend and I have not seen them in months. I would hate for the ticket to go to waste, and I immediately thought of you.

  It is a comedy at the Grand Theatre. I have heard wonderful things about the cast, and the reviews say it is one of the funniest plays of the year. I honestly think you will enjoy it far more than I would have.

  Do let me know how you find it — and let us plan a theatre evening together soon.

  Yours,

  Tom *(约 100 词)*
</Callout>

## 13. 拓展练习 Going further

<Callout type="tip" title="自学者建议">
  - **影子跟读 (shadowing)**：用第 1 节的三个 SayIt 按钮跟读 3-5 遍。注意 *nothing can match them for speed and comfort* 中 match 和 for 之间有一个微停顿——这是 **match ... for ...** 结构的自然节奏。另一句 *so smooth that there is nothing to prevent you from...* 要一口气读完 that 从句，体验"结果"的延伸感。
  - **复述 (retell)**：合上课文，用 2 分钟把四种交通方式的对比串一遍。**四步淘汰赛**：火车（憋闷→催睡→睡不好→筋疲力尽）→ 汽车（更惨→书都看不了→大半路程堵在路上）→ 轮船（本来不错→但晕船+太慢→没人愿为\"坐船\"牺牲假期）→ **飞机**（虽贵且怕→但 speed + comfort = 无与伦比→高空体验→不无聊→到达 fresh and uncrumpled→无需恢复）。
  - **造句迁移**：用今天的 5 个高价值结构造你自己的句子——
    1. **hardly anyone can positively enjoy...**（几乎没有人能真正喜欢）—— *Hardly anyone can positively enjoy going to the dentist.*
    2. **more often than not**（多半、通常）—— *More often than not, the simplest explanation is the right one.*
    3. **nothing can match ... for ...**（在……方面无与伦比）—— *Nothing can match a handwritten letter for warmth and sincerity.*
    4. **so + adj. + that...**（如此……以至于）—— *The lecture was so boring that half the audience fell asleep.*
    5. **keep + sb. + occupied**（使某人有事可做）—— *The puzzle kept the children occupied all afternoon.*
  - **延伸思考**：本课是一篇\"对比淘汰式\"的典范说明文——作者不靠说\"飞机好\"（那太单薄），而是先把火车、汽车、轮船各自的"硬伤"逐一摆出来，再让飞机以"全科最优"的姿态登场。下次写\"三点式\"对比文章时，试试这个套路：**① 把每一个选项的平均水平写出来 → ② 指出每个选项在\"方便/舒适/速度/成本\"四个维度上有什么致命短板 → ③ 最后你的推荐是唯一一个四个维度都过关的。**另外值得注意的一点：全篇用一般现在时——说明文用一般现在时陈述普遍事实，是最稳妥的选择。不要一写"旅行"就跳到过去时（那是记叙文，不是说明文）。
</Callout>
