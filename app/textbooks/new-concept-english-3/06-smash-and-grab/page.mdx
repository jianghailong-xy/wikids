  **How did Mr. Taylor try to stop the thieves?** 带着这个问题读课文，重点留意全篇是如何用 **一般过去时 (what happened)** 与 **过去进行时 (what was happening)** 的交错，把“静态的清晨→突然的袭击→混乱的对抗→飞速的逃逸”这场三分钟抢劫讲得像一镜到底的电影。

## The Text 课文

The expensive shops in a famous arcade near Piccadilly were just opening. At this time of the morning, the arcade was almost empty. Mr. Taylor, the owner of a jewellery shop was admiring a new window display. Two of his assistants **had been working busily** since eight o'clock and had only just finished. Diamond necklaces and rings had been beautifully arranged on a background of black velvet. After **gazing at** the display for several minutes, Mr. Taylor went back into his shop.

The silence was suddenly broken when a large car, **with its headlights on and its horn blaring**, roared down the arcade. It came to a stop outside the jeweller's. One man **stayed at the wheel** while two others **with black stockings over their faces** jumped out and **smashed the window of the shop** with iron bars. **While this was going on**, Mr. Taylor was upstairs. He and his staff began **throwing furniture out of the window**. Chairs and tables **went flying** into the arcade. One of the thieves was struck by a heavy statue, but he was **too busy helping himself to diamonds to notice any pain**. The raid was all over **in three minutes**, for the men **scrambled back** into the car and it **moved off at a fantastic speed**. Just as it was leaving, Mr. Taylor **rushed out** and ran after it throwing ashtrays and vases, but **it was impossible to stop the thieves**. They had **got away with** thousands of pounds worth of diamonds.

## 课文注释 Notes on the text

<Callout type="info" title="6 个值得停下来嚼一嚼的点">
  1. **with + 名词 + 现在分词/介词短语** (ll.8-10) — with 复合结构，表“伴随状况”，让句子密度极高。课文两处：*a large car, **with its headlights on and its horn blaring***（开着前灯、喇叭大作的车）、*two others **with black stockings over their faces***（脸上蒙着黑色长筒袜的另外两人）。结构是 **with + 名词 + ①形容词/介词短语 或 ②现在分词（主动）或 ③过去分词（被动）**。同型：*He stood there with his hands in his pockets. / She fell asleep with the TV still on. / The thief ran away with the police chasing him.*

  2. **too busy doing ... to do ...** (l.13) — "忙着做……以致于没有……"。课文 *too busy **helping himself to** diamonds **to notice** any pain* —— 注意 too 后面是 **busy + 现在分词**（忙着干什么），to 后面是 **动词原形**（结果没做到什么）。中间嵌了一个关键短语 **help oneself to sth.** = "擅自取用、自取"（不请自拿）。同型：*He was too busy playing games to finish his homework. / The children helped themselves to the cake while no one was looking.*

  3. **一般过去时 vs 过去进行时** (全篇) — 本课的核心语法：**一般过去时** 讲“发生了什么 (what happened)”，**过去进行时** 讲“当时正在发生什么 (what was happening)”，两者交错织出事件层次感。例如：*While this **was going on**（背景进行中）, Mr. Taylor **was** upstairs. He and his staff **began** throwing furniture（突然动作）... Chairs and tables **went** flying（结果动作）.* 教材 Key Structures 练习 A 就是让你识别这两种时态在文中的交替使用。

  4. **go + 现在分词** (l.12) — "……飞了出去"。课文 *Chairs and tables **went flying** into the arcade* —— go + 现在分词强调“以某种方式移动或变化”。同型：*The ball went bouncing down the stairs. / The story went spreading through the town. / Everything went wrong.*

  5. **just as** (l.14) — "正当……的时候"，强调两个动作几乎同时发生。课文 *Just as it was leaving, Mr. Taylor rushed out* —— 车刚要走的那个瞬间，Taylor 冲了出来。注意 **just as** 引导的从句里常用进行时（was leaving），主句用一般过去时（rushed out），两者形成“背景被突然中断”的节奏。同型：*Just as I was about to leave, the phone rang. / Just as he opened the door, the lights went out.*

  6. **It was impossible to stop the thieves** (l.16) — "要拦住那些劫匪是不可能的"。**impossible**（不可能的）= im- + possible。本课 Word building 专门讲如何用 **dis-, in-, im-, un-, il-, ir-** 前缀构成反义词。同型：*It was impossible to see anything in the dark. / Don't be so impatient — the train will arrive soon.*
</Callout>

## 3. 关键句型 一般过去时 × 过去进行时 Simple Past & Past Continuous

<Callout type="tip" title="一分钟回顾">
  本课讲的是一个“三分钟抢劫”的故事，但作者像电影导演一样**切镜头**——一会儿给“大背景”（**过去进行时**），一会儿给“突发动作”（**一般过去时**），让三分钟的事件有快有慢、有气氛有爆发。

  | 时态 | 功能 | 课文例句 |
  |---|---|---|
  | **过去进行时** (was/were + V-ing) | **背景/氛围** —— 当时正在持续的状况 | *Mr. Taylor **was admiring** a new window display.* / *While this **was going on**, Mr. Taylor **was** upstairs.* |
  | **一般过去时** (V-ed) | **突发动作** —— 打破背景的"那一下" | *The silence **was suddenly broken**...* / *Two others **jumped out** and **smashed** the window.* |
  | **过去完成进行时** (had been + V-ing) | **早前一直在做的事** —— 在抢劫之前就已持续的动作 | *Two of his assistants **had been working** busily since eight o'clock.* |
  | **过去完成时** (had + V-ed) | **先于过去的完成** —— 在“抢劫前”就已搞定的事 | *Diamond necklaces and rings **had been** beautifully arranged.* |
  | **just as + 过去进行时, 一般过去时** | **"正当……的时候突然……"** —— 背景被瞬间打断 | *Just as it **was leaving**, Mr. Taylor **rushed out**...* |

  ### ⚠️ 3 个本课最容易踩的雷区

  1. **had been working (过去完成进行时) ≠ were working (过去进行时)**：前者强调“从八点开始一直在忙”（持续+先于过去），后者只是“当时在忙”（背景）。原文 *had been working busily since eight o'clock* —— since 是选择完成时的关键信号。
  2. **went flying ≠ were flying**：*Chairs and tables **went flying*** —— **go + 现在分词** 是固定结构，强调“猛烈地/不可控地移动”，不是“当时在飞”。类似：*go crashing / go tumbling / go spinning*。
  3. **just as** 不是“就像”，而是**"正当"**（= at the very moment when）。它后面的从句多用进行时，制造“正要……突然……”的张力。**just as / while / when** 三者的区别：*while* 强调“持续期间”；*when* 最泛；*just as* 强调“那个精确时刻”。
</Callout>

## 4. 难点：构词法 — 反义前缀 dis- / in- / im- / un- / il- / ir- Word building

<Callout type="info" title="教材 Special difficulties 原题">
  课文最后一句 *It was **impossible** to stop the thieves* 里，**impossible**（不可能的）= im- + possible。英语里许多词可以通过加前缀变成反义词。常见的有六种：

  | 前缀 | 用法规则 | 例词 |
  |---|---|---|
  | **un-** | 最通用，多用于形容词和过去分词 | *locked → **un**locked, usual → **un**usual* |
  | **in-** | 多用于拉丁语源形容词 | *accurate → **in**accurate, complete → **in**complete* |
  | **im-** | 用在 p, b, m 开头的词前 | *possible → **im**possible, patient → **im**patient, polite → **im**polite* |
  | **il-** | 用在 l 开头的词前 | *legible → **il**legible, legal → **il**legal* |
  | **ir-** | 用在 r 开头的词前 | *regular → **ir**regular, responsible → **ir**responsible* |
  | **dis-** | 多用于动词和少数形容词 | *agree → **dis**agree, appear → **dis**appear, honest → **dis**honest* |

  教材练习：写出下列斜体词的正确反义词：
  1. He was extremely **polite**. → **impolite**
  2. I **agree** with you. → **disagree**
  3. His handwriting is quite **legible**. → **illegible**
  4. This report is **accurate**. → **inaccurate**
  5. Have you **locked** the door? → **unlocked**
  6. Have you learnt these **regular** verbs? → **irregular**
</Callout>

## 10. 摘要写作 Summary writing (≤ 80 词)

<Callout type="info" title="任务">
  *In not more than 80 words* write an account of the smash-and-grab raid. **Do not include anything that is not in the last paragraph**（只用最后一段的信息——从 *The silence was suddenly broken ...* 到 *... thousands of pounds worth of diamonds*）。

  按下面的提示点串成一段：

  1. 一辆大轿车怎样开进了拱廊街？（开着前灯、响着喇叭）
  2. 车停在了哪里？几个人下了车？他们做了什么？
  3. 此时 Taylor 先生在哪里？他和店员做了什么？
  4. 一个盗贼被什么击中了？他的反应是什么？
  5. 这次抢劫持续了多久？劫匪怎样逃跑的？
  6. Taylor 先生做了什么？结果如何？

<Callout type="tip" title="参考范文（自己写完再看）">
  A large car, with its headlights on and its horn blaring, roared down the arcade and stopped outside the jeweller's. ## 11. 作文 Composition (≤ 200 词)

<Callout type="info" title="任务">
  *In not more than 200 words* continue the above passage using the ideas given below. **Do not write more than three paragraphs.**

  **Title:** They got away.

  **Introduction:** The thieves' car joined the traffic — Mr. Taylor took a taxi — followed the thieves' car.

  **Development:** A mad chase through the streets — the thieves' car hit another car — did not stop — the police chased both taxi and thieves — Mr. Taylor's taxi stopped at traffic lights — the thieves got away — taxi driver was stopped by the police — he had been speeding — Mr. Taylor explained the situation.

  **Conclusion:** The thieves' car was found ten minutes later — side street — abandoned — the thieves escaped on foot.
</Callout>

<Callout type="warning" title="提示骨架">
  - **第一段——街头的追逐**：劫匪的车汇入车流——看似平静，实则暗潮汹涌。Taylor 拦下一辆出租车——"Follow that car!"——司机犹豫了一下，但看到 Taylor 满脸焦急，还是踩了油门。
  - **第二段——疯狂的追车**：劫匪发现被跟踪后开始在城市街道上飙车——他们闯红灯、擦过一辆停着的车，撞坏了一辆路边的轿车——但没有停。警笛响起。此时 Taylor 的出租车却在一处红绿灯前被迫停下——劫匪趁机甩掉了他们。警察拦下了出租车——"你严重超速！"——Taylor 不得不解释这不是一次普通的超速。
  - **第三段——弃车而逃**：十分钟后，警察在一条小街上发现了劫匪的车——车门大开，人去车空。那些钻石再也没有被找到。
</Callout>

<Callout type="tip" title="写作脚手架">
  - **时态主线**：全篇用**一般过去时**和**过去进行时**交替，就像本课课文一样——用过去进行时造气氛，用一般过去时推情节。
  - **"追"的动词库**：*follow, chase, pursue, speed after, race through, keep up with, lose sight of*。
  - **"逃"的动词库**：*escape, flee, get away, slip away, disappear, abandon the car*。
  - **结构亮点**：用 **Just as ...** 和 **While ... was/were ...** 制造蒙太奇式的画面切换——*Just as the taxi screeched to a halt, the thieves' car vanished round the corner.* / *While the policeman was questioning the driver, Mr. Taylor kept staring down the empty street.*
</Callout>

  The thieves' car shot out of the arcade and joined the early morning traffic. Mr. Taylor, still breathless, ran to the kerb, flagged down a taxi and shouted, "Follow that car!" The driver, startled, obeyed.

  What followed was a mad chase through the streets of central London. The thieves, realizing they were being followed, sped through red lights and, at one corner, hit a parked car — but did not stop. A police car joined the pursuit, its siren wailing. Then, at a busy crossing, Taylor's taxi was forced to stop at a red light. The thieves' car slipped away. When the light turned green, the taxi shot forward — only to be pulled over seconds later by a police motorcyclist who had seen it speeding. Mr. Taylor jumped out and hurriedly explained the situation.

  Ten minutes later, the thieves' car was found abandoned in a narrow side street, its doors wide open. The men had escaped on foot. The diamonds — thousands of pounds' worth — were never recovered. *(约 195 词)*
</Callout>

## 12. 书信写作 Letter writing (约 80 词)

<Callout type="info" title="任务">
  Suppose that you had witnessed an incident similar to the one described in the passage. Write a letter of about **80 words** to a friend describing what you saw. Supply a suitable **Introduction** and **Conclusion**. Use the following ideas to write the **Purpose**:

  **Tuesday morning** — **busy street** — a man smashed the window of an **antique shop** — chased by **passers-by** — you joined in — the man was caught.
</Callout>

<Callout type="tip" title="写作脚手架">
  - **称呼**：*Dear + 名*（给朋友写信）。
  - **开头（Introduction）**：一两句话引出你要讲的"怪事"——*You won't believe what I saw this morning!* / *I must tell you about something that happened right before my eyes on Tuesday.*
  - **主体（Purpose）**——按提示串起来：
    1. 时间地点：*Tuesday morning, on a busy street near my office*
    2. 事件：*a man suddenly smashed the window of an antique shop with a hammer*
    3. 目击者反应：*passers-by started shouting and chasing him*
    4. 你的参与：*I joined in without a second thought*
    5. 结局：*ran him down and held him until the police arrived*
  - **收尾（Conclusion）**：一两句感慨——*I've never been so close to a crime before. I'm still shaking!*
  - **时态要点**：全篇用**一般过去时** + 穿插**过去进行时**制造气氛——本课核心语法，活用到你自己的写作里。注意“日期 + 时间 + 地点”交代的简洁度，不啰嗦。
</Callout>

<Callout type="tip" title="参考范文（自己写完再看）">
  Dear Tom,

  You will never guess what happened to me on Tuesday morning. I was walking down the High Street, which was already busy with shoppers, when I heard a loud crash. A man had smashed the window of an antique shop with an iron bar and was grabbing whatever he could reach.

  Several passers-by, including myself, began chasing him. He darted down a side street, pushing people aside, but one man managed to trip him. We caught up and held him there until the police arrived.

  I am still shaking as I write this. I never thought I would actually see a crime like this — let alone help catch the thief!

  Yours,
  David *(约 80 词)*
</Callout>

## 13. 拓展练习 Going further

<Callout type="tip" title="自学者建议">
  - **影子跟读 (shadowing)**：用第 1 节的三个 SayIt 按钮跟读 3-5 遍。注意 *with its headlights on and its horn blaring* 这句里，blaring 和 roaring 连续出现**头韵**（b-l-a-r- / r-o-a-r），读快了像汽车引擎声——作者在用声音模仿内容（拟声/头韵）。
  - **复述 (retell)**：合上课文，用 2 分钟把这场三分钟抢劫讲一遍。**镜头顺序**：清晨宁静的拱廊街→汽车冲入→砸窗→店员扔家具反击→劫匪被雕像砸中浑不在意→飞速逃走→Taylor 追出。注意用**一般过去时**推情节，用**过去进行时**给“背景”。
  - **造句迁移**：用今天的 5 个高价值结构造你自己的句子——
    1. *with + 名词 + 分词/介词*（伴随状况）—— *He sat on the bench, with his eyes closed and his head resting against the wall.*
    2. *had been doing*（过去完成进行时）—— *I had been waiting for over an hour when the train finally arrived.*
    3. *too busy doing ... to do*（忙得顾不上）—— *She was too busy texting to notice the bus had already left.*
    4. *just as ... was/were doing, ... did*（正当……突然……）—— *Just as we were sitting down to dinner, the doorbell rang.*
    5. *go + V-ing*（猛地……起来）—— *The glass slipped from her hand and went crashing to the floor.*
  - **构词法迁移**：用 dis-, in-, im-, un-, il-, ir- 前缀，各造一个含对立义词的句子，把今天的 Word building 内容即刻变成能用的工具。
  - **延伸思考**：这篇课文不到 200 词，却讲完了一场完整的罪案——含铺垫（清晨街景）、起势（汽车冲入）、冲突（扔家具反击）、细节（被砸中却不觉疼）、高潮（飞速逃逸）和结尾（拦不住）。NCE3 教你的是"叙述密度"——用最少的词塞最多的信息。下次写英文记叙文时，试试像本文一样，用 **with 复合结构**把自己塞进名词短语（*a large car, with its headlights on and its horn blaring*），让一个句子等于别人三句。
</Callout>
