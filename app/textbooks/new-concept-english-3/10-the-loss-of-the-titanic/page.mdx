## The Text 课文

The great ship, *Titanic*, sailed for New York from Southampton on April 10th, 1912. She was carrying 1,316 passengers and a crew of 891. Even by modern standards, the 46,000 ton *Titanic* was a colossal ship. At that time, however, she was not only the largest ship that **had ever been built**, but was regarded as unsinkable, for she had sixteen watertight compartments. Even if two of these were flooded, she would still be able to float. The tragic sinking of this great liner will always be remembered, for she went down on her first voyage with heavy loss of life.

Four days after setting out, while the *Titanic* was sailing across the icy waters of the North Atlantic, a huge iceberg was suddenly spotted by a lookout. **After the alarm had been given, the great ship turned sharply** to avoid a direct collision. The *Titanic* turned just in time, narrowly missing the immense wall of ice which rose over 100 feet out of the water beside her. Suddenly, there was a slight trembling sound from below, and the captain went down to see **what had happened**. The noise **had been so faint** that no one thought that the ship **had been damaged**. Below, the captain realized to his horror that the *Titanic* was sinking rapidly, for five of her sixteen watertight compartments **had already been flooded**! The order to abandon ship was given and hundreds of people plunged into the icy water. As there were not enough lifeboats for everybody, 1,500 lives were lost.

## 课文注释 Notes on the text

<Callout type="info" title="6 个值得停下来嚼一嚼的点">
  1. **sail for + 目的地** (l.1) — \"驶往……\"。**for** 在这里表示**目的地方向**（不是 \"为了\"）。同型：*The plane took off **for** Tokyo. / The ship is bound **for** Hong Kong.*
  2. **even by modern standards** (l.3) — \"即便按照现代标准来衡量\"。**by** 在这里 = \"按照、依照\"（标准）。同型：*by my watch / by Chinese law / by any measure*。常用来**让步**——\"即便用最严格的尺子量，她也是巨轮\"。
  3. **regard ... as ...** (l.5) — \"把……视作 / 被认为是……\"。课文 *was regarded as unsinkable* = 被认为是不会沉没的（被动）。**as 后接名词 / 形容词**（不接 to be——*regard as to be* 是中式英语）。同型：*regard him as a friend / see him as a hero / look upon him as ...*。
  4. **on her first voyage** (l.8) — \"在她的首次航行中\"。**maiden voyage** 是\"处女航\"的固定说法，更书面 / 更哀婉。船在英语里用 **she/her**（人格化指代），是航海传统。
  5. **what had happened** (l.13) — **宾语从句中的过去完成时**——\"船长下去看**之前已发生**的事\"。两个动作都在过去，**先发生**的用过去完成（had happened），**后发生**的用一般过去（went down）。这是本课的题眼。
  6. **realized to his horror that ...** (l.14) — \"惊恐地意识到……\"。**to one's + 情绪名词** 是一个高频结构：*to his horror / to her surprise / to my delight / to our amazement*。常作**插入语**或**伴随状语**，让叙事更有戏剧性。
</Callout>

## 3. 关键句型 Past perfect 过去完成时

<Callout type="tip" title="一分钟回顾">
  本课是英语教材里讲**过去完成时**的经典案例——一个**过去时间点**（船长走下舱）+ 一连串**更早发生**的事件。每次出现 had + V-ed，都指向\"那个时间点之前已经发生的事\"：

  - *the largest ship that **had ever been built***（**那时**之前所有曾被建造的船里）
  - *After the alarm **had been given**, the great ship turned sharply*（警报先响，转弯在后）
  - *the captain went down to see **what had happened***（先发生事 → 船长才下去看）
  - *The noise **had been so faint** that no one thought that the ship **had been damaged***（声音之前是那么轻 → 当时无人想到船受损）
  - *five of her sixteen watertight compartments **had already been flooded***（船长意识到时，进水已是既成事实）

  ### 过去完成时的 3 大触发条件

  | 触发 | 例子 |
  |---|---|
  | **过去更早 (before another past)** | He **had locked** the door before he went out. |
  | **过去经验 (by then)** | She **had never seen** snow before she came to Beijing. |
  | **过去完成 + by + 过去时点** | By 1912, he **had sailed** the Atlantic many times. |

  ### ⚠️ 4 个常见雷区

  1. **不要无脑乱用**：一连串按时间顺序发生的过去事件，**用一般过去时按序排列**即可。只有需要\"强调先后\"或\"插入回顾\"时才用 had。
  2. **被动语态**：had + been + V-ed（had been built / had been given / had been flooded）。
  3. **常配的副词**：*already, just, never, ever, by then, before, after, when*。
  4. **after / before / when 引导的从句**：因为这些连词本身已经表明先后，**主从句之间用一般过去时**也完全可以——只在需要强调时才用过去完成。课文 *After the alarm **had been given**, the great ship **turned*** 用了过去完成是因为被动语态读起来更紧凑。
</Callout>

## 4. 难点：Word building 构词法（动词 / 形容词 → 名词）

<Callout type="tip" title="5 个常用的名词后缀">
  课文的 *Special Difficulties* 部分讲了 5 个**名词化**后缀。掌握它们，单词量瞬间翻倍：

  | 后缀 | 加在什么词上 | 含义 | 例子 |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | **-er / -or** | 动词 → 人 | 做某事的人 | work → **worker** / build → **builder** / sail → **sailor** / visit → **visitor** |
  | **-ist** | 名词 → 人 | 从事 / 信奉某事的人 | violin → **violinist** / piano → **pianist** / physics → **physicist** / Marx → **Marxist** |
  | **-ness** | 形容词 → 抽象名词 | ……的状态 / 性质 | careless → **carelessness** / kind → **kindness** / happy → **happiness** / dark → **darkness** |
  | **-ion / -tion / -sion** | 动词 → 抽象名词 | 动作或结果 | explain → **explanation** / collide → **collision** / discuss → **discussion** / impress → **impression** |
  | **-ity / -ty** | 形容词 → 抽象名词 | ……的性质 / 程度 | responsible → **responsibility** / similar → **similarity** / human → **humanity** / original → **originality** |

  ### ⚠️ 拼写变化几条规律

  1. **去 y 加 i**：happy → happiness / busy → business / lazy → laziness。
  2. **重复辅音**：sad → sadness 不变；但 hot 仅作形容词。
  3. **-tion 常伴去 e 加 ation**：explain → explanation（保留 a）/ create → creation / inform → information。

## 11. 作文 Composition (约 200 词扩写)

<Callout type="info" title="任务">
  Write an **imaginary account** of what happened on the *Titanic* immediately after the order to abandon ship was given. **No more than 200 words, three paragraphs.**

  **Title:** *Abandon ship.*
</Callout>

<Callout type="warning" title="提示骨架（每个省略号都得自己填）">
  - **Introduction**: Order to abandon ship unexpected — everybody unprepared.
  - **Development**: Immediate effect — panic and confusion — people rushing in all directions — crew came up from below — lifeboats lowered — people jumped overboard — struggle to get into lifeboats — lifeboats full.
  - **Conclusion**: *Titanic* sank rapidly — people in water — cries of despair — lifeboats moved away.
</Callout>

<Callout type="tip" title="写作脚手架">
  - **时态主线**：一般过去时叙事；过去完成时表更早事件（*had been given / had been damaged*）；过去进行时表背景（*was sinking / was rising*）。
  - **混乱场面词库**：*chaos broke out, panic spread like wildfire, families scrambled across the decks, women and children first, husbands torn from wives, life jackets thrust into trembling hands*。
  - **救生艇动作**：*lifeboats were lowered, davits creaked, ropes squealed, the boat swung dangerously, oars splashed clumsily*。
  - **水中惨象**：*the freezing sea / numb within minutes / cries echoing across the water / hundreds bobbing in the dark / silence settling over the wreck*。
  - **结尾的张力**：让最后一只救生艇在远处停下，看着 Titanic 滑入海底——一句 *and silence at last fell over the empty Atlantic* 就够。
</Callout>

<Callout type="tip" title="参考范文（自己写完再看）">
  The order to abandon ship came so suddenly that no one was prepared. Most passengers had already gone to bed; some had been sitting in the lounges, sipping their nightcaps. Now bells rang, officers shouted, and crewmen hammered on cabin doors.

  Panic and confusion spread instantly. Half-dressed families came rushing in all directions, dragging children and clutching life-jackets they barely knew how to fasten. Crewmen scrambled up from the boiler rooms, soaked to the waist. The lifeboats were lowered one by one, but most were only half full when they touched the water. Husbands were torn from wives; some men leapt overboard rather than wait. As each boat pulled away, others fought to climb in, only to be turned back — the lifeboats were already dangerously low in the water.

  The *Titanic* began to sink with terrifying speed. Hundreds of passengers and crew who had not found a place plunged into the freezing sea. Their cries of despair rose from every direction as the lifeboats, themselves overcrowded, rowed steadily away from the dying ship. Within minutes the great liner tilted, slid forward, and disappeared. A vast silence settled over the empty Atlantic. *(192 words)*
</Callout>

## 12. 书信写作 Letter writing

<Callout type="info" title="任务">
  Which of the following addresses is **correct**?

  | A | B | C |
  |---|---|---|
  | 19 Kingsley Ave. | 19 Kingsley Ave., | 19 Kingsley Ave., |
  | Sandford Park, | Sandford Park, | Sandford Park, |
  | London, N.W.8, | London, N.W.8, | London, N.W.8, |
  | England. | England. | England |
  | August 24th, 19__ | 24th Aug., 19__ | Aug. 24th, 19__ |
</Callout>

<Callout type="tip" title="正确答案：B（24th Aug., 19__）">
  英式私信里的**信头地址 + 日期**的标准写法：

  1. **第一行：门牌号 + 街名**——用逗号结尾（街名缩写如 Ave. / Rd. / St. 末尾的句号保留）。
  2. **第二行：区或附属地名**——用逗号结尾。
  3. **第三行：城市 + 邮区代号**（如 N.W.8）——用逗号结尾。
  4. **第四行：国家**——用**句号**结尾（这是整个地址段的末尾）。
  5. **第五行：日期**——**日 + 月份缩写 + 年**（24th Aug., 19__）。**月份用缩写**，并以逗号隔开。

  ### A / C 为什么错？

  - **A 错**：日期 *August 24th* 把**月份写全了**——英式信头里月份通常**缩写**（Aug. / Sept. / Oct.）。
  - **C 错**：第四行 *England* **少了句号**；且日期 *Aug. 24th* 是**美式**\"月 + 日\"顺序——英式应是\"日 + 月\"。

  ### 美式 vs 英式日期写法

  | 风格 | 写法 | 读法 |
  |---|---|---|
  | **英式** | 24th August, 2026 / 24/08/2026 | the twenty-fourth of August |
  | **美式** | August 24th, 2026 / 08/24/2026 | August twenty-fourth |
</Callout>

## 13. 拓展练习 Going further

<Callout type="tip" title="自学者建议">
  - **影子跟读 (shadowing)**：用第 1 节的三个 SayIt 按钮跟读 3-5 遍。重点感受 *had ever been built* / *had already been flooded* 这类**三层叠时态**的连读——had / been 都被吞成 \"d / b'n\"，整体像 \"had-EVER-been-BUILT\"。
  - **复述 (retell)**：合上课文，把 Titanic 的故事用 3 分钟讲一遍。**叙事三段**：背景（设计与首航）→ 经过（冰山 → 转弯 → 颤音 → 5 舱进水）→ 结局（弃船 → 1500 人丧生）。
  - **造句迁移**：用今天的 5 个高价值结构造你自己的句子——
    1. *S + had + V-ed + before / by + 过去时间*（过去之过去）—— *He had left before I arrived.*
    2. *the largest / first / only ... that had ever been V-ed*（曾经……的最……）—— *the kindest person that had ever helped me*
    3. *be regarded as + adj/n*（被认为是）—— *He is regarded as a hero.*
    4. *Even if + 一般过去, would + V 原形*（即便假设）—— *Even if I knew, I wouldn't tell you.*
    5. *to one's + 情绪名词*（令某人……地）—— *to her delight / to his amazement*
  - **延伸思考**：本课是\"**叙事的工业级范本**\"——一个简单的过去事件，因为**多层时态嵌套**（一般过去 + 过去完成 + 过去进行 + 过去完成被动），读起来有电影的画面感。每个 *had been V-ed* 都是\"插入的回顾镜头\"。下次你写英文叙事时，问自己一句：\"这个动作发生在主线时间之前吗？如果是，要不要用过去完成时强调？\"——答案对了，你的英文就有了**层次感**。
</Callout>
