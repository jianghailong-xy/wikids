<Callout type="info" title="Listen first 听前思考">
  上一课我们跟"我"一起去看了一场激动人心的汽车比赛——**1995 年**的盛大比赛，Billy Stewart 驾驶**第 15 号车**获胜，回家路上妻子一句 *Don't drive so quickly!* 让人忍俊不禁。在那篇课文里，你第一次遇见了 **be 动词的过去式 was / were** 和 **There was / There were**。

  到了本课，轮到你自己开口说：**他们是什么时候在那里的？**——一场覆盖 **介词三兄弟 at / on / in** 的总复习。全书给出四大块替换操练：
  - **第一块（星期 × 文具店）**：练「at + 地点 + on + 星期」——从 **Monday** 一路问到 **Saturday**。
  - **第二块（日期 × 面包店/肉店）**：练「at + 地点 + on + 日期」——从 **January 21st** 跨到 **June 26th**，顺带复习序数词 21st–26th。
  - **第三块（月份 × 国家）**：练「in + 地点 + in + 月份」——从 **July** 到 **December**，后半年的六个月。
  - **第四块（年份 × 国家）**：练「in + 地点 + in + 年份」——跨十年，1985、1987、1990、1994、1996。

  本课是 Lesson 69 的**句型操练课（双数课）**。核心语法一句话：**过去时间 be 动词用 was/were；在某地＋在何时用 at/on/in 三个介词——小地点用 at，具体某天用 on，大范围（月/季/年/国家）用 in。**

  先看四块替换图，每块在心里用英文问一句 **When were they at/in ...?**，再答一句 **They were at/in ... on/in ...**。然后翻到本课末尾的**生词表**和**书面练习**——A 部分烤你 **at / on / in 三选一填坑**，B 部分要你**自己造问句和答句**。两题练完，过去时＋介词的组合就刻进肌肉记忆了。
</Callout>

## The Drills 句型操练

### Part 1 — at the stationer's · on + 星期

第一块操练：时间用**星期几**，地点用**文具店**。问 When were **they** at the stationer's?，答 They were at the stationer's **on** + 星期：

| No. | 星期 | Q | A |
|-----|------|---|---|
| 1 | Monday | When were they at the stationer's? | They were at the stationer's **on Monday**. |
| 2 | Tuesday | When were they at the stationer's? | They were at the stationer's **on Tuesday**. |
| 3 | Wednesday | When were they at the stationer's? | They were at the stationer's **on Wednesday**. |
| 4 | Thursday | When were they at the stationer's? | They were at the stationer's **on Thursday**. |
| 5 | Friday | When were they at the stationer's? | They were at the stationer's **on Friday**. |
| 6 | Saturday | When were they at the stationer's? | They were at the stationer's **on Saturday**. |

**规律**：星期前面永远用 **on**；文具店前用 **at the stationer's**（店铺 's 结构，加 the）。主语 they 是复数，be 动词过去式用 **were**。

### Part 2 — at the baker's / at the butcher's · on + 日期

第二块操练：时间升级为**具体日期**，地点交替出现**面包店**和**肉店**。问 When were they at the baker's / the butcher's?，答 They were at ... on + 日期：

| No. | 日期 | 地点 | Q | A |
|-----|------|------|---|---|
| 1 | January 21st | the baker's | When were they at the baker's? | They were at the baker's **on January 21st**. |
| 2 | February 22nd | the butcher's | When were they at the butcher's? | They were at the butcher's **on February 22nd**. |
| 3 | March 23rd | the baker's | When were they at the baker's? | They were at the baker's **on March 23rd**. |
| 4 | April 24th | the butcher's | When were they at the butcher's? | They were at the butcher's **on April 24th**. |
| 5 | May 25th | the baker's | When were they at the baker's? | They were at the baker's **on May 25th**. |
| 6 | June 26th | the butcher's | When were they at the butcher's? | They were at the butcher's **on June 26th**. |

**规律**：具体日期前面用 **on**；baker's / butcher's 都是店铺 's 结构，必须加 **the**（*at the baker's*、*at the butcher's*）。

### Part 3 — in Denmark · in + 月份

第三块操练：地点换成**国家**，时间换成**月份**。介词对子从 at/on 切换为 **in/in**——地点大范围用 in，时间大于一天也用 in：

| No. | 月份 | Q | A |
|-----|------|---|---|
| 1 | July | When were they in Denmark? | They were in Denmark **in July**. |
| 2 | August | When were they in Denmark? | They were in Denmark **in August**. |
| 3 | September | When were they in Denmark? | They were in Denmark **in September**. |
| 4 | October | When were they in Denmark? | They were in Denmark **in October**. |
| 5 | November | When were they in Denmark? | They were in Denmark **in November**. |
| 6 | December | When were they in Denmark? | They were in Denmark **in December**. |

**规律**：国家前面用 **in**（*in Denmark*），月份前面也用 **in**（*in July*）。比一天大的时间单位（月、季、年、世纪）统统用 in。

### Part 4 — in Australia · in + 年份

第四块操练：时间进一步放大为**年份**，地点换为另一个国家。介词全部用 **in**：

| No. | 年份 | Q | A |
|-----|------|---|---|
| 1 | 1985 | When were they in Australia? | They were in Australia **in 1985**. |
| 2 | 1987 | When were they in Australia? | They were in Australia **in 1987**. |
| 3 | 1990 | When were they in Australia? | They were in Australia **in 1990**. |
| 4 | 1994 | When were they in Australia? | They were in Australia **in 1994**. |
| 5 | 1996 | When were they in Australia? | They were in Australia **in 1996**. |

**规律**：年份前面也用 **in**（*in 1985*）。四大块的介词规律到此全出：**at + 小地点（店铺/建筑），on + 一天（星期/日期），in + 大范围（国家/月份/年份）**。

## 课文注释 Notes on the text

<Callout type="info" title="8 个值得停下来嚼一嚼的点">
  1. **When were they at the stationer's?**（本课第一块核心问句）——「他们什么时候在文具店？」特殊疑问句的结构是 **疑问词 + were + 主语 + 地点/时间**。本课全用 **were**（主语 they 是复数），如果是单数则用 **was**：*When was he at the stationer's?*

  2. **at the stationer's**（本课地点）——「在文具店」。**stationer** = 文具商；**the stationer's** = the stationer's shop 的省略——文具店。在 Lesson 69 里你学了 *at the butcher's / at the baker's*，这里同理：店铺 's 结构，前面加 **the**，介词用 **at**。

  3. **介词三兄弟：at / on / in 的总复习**——本课四大块是对全书介词的终极梳理：
     - **at + 小地点**（店铺、建筑物、家以外的小场所）：*at the stationer's / at the baker's / at the butcher's / at the office / at school / at home*
     - **on + 一天**（星期几、几月几日）：*on Monday / on January 21st / on June 26th*
     - **in + 大范围**（国家、城市、月份、季节、年份、世纪）：*in Denmark / in Australia / in July / in spring / in 1990*
     这是本课最核心的语法点——也是 Written Exercise A 要你反复练的。

  4. **denmark**（生词）——「丹麦」。国家名前不加 the（除了少数特例：*the United States / the United Kingdom*）。介国家用 **in**：*in Denmark / in Australia / in Canada*。

  5. **stationer**（生词）——「文具商」。本课把 *stationer* 用作店铺名（the stationer's）。注意读音 /ˈsteɪʃənə/——第一个音节 *sta* 读如 *stay*。

  6. **日期里藏着的序数词 21st–26th**——本课第二块操练里的日期从 January 21st 到 June 26th，正好练 21st–26th 六个序数词：*twenty-first (21st) / twenty-second (22nd) / twenty-third (23rd) / twenty-fourth (24th) / twenty-fifth (25th) / twenty-sixth (26th)*。日期的读写规则：月 + the + 序数词（*January the twenty-first*），或月 + 序数词（*January twenty-first*），书写时用 *January 21st*。

  7. **具体时间点用 at**（Written Exercise A, No.2）——「在四点」用 *at four o'clock*。**at** 除了接小地点，还接**具体时刻**（*at three o'clock / at half past two*）。与 on（一天）和 in（月/季/年）形成三级对比：**at 时刻 / on 天 / in 月季年**。

  8. **in spring**（Written Exercise A, No.4）——「在春天」。季节前面用 **in**（*in spring / in summer / in autumn / in winter*）。季节属于"比一天大的时间范围"，介词用 in——和月份、年份一样。
</Callout>

## 3. 关键句型：was/were + 介词三兄弟 at / on / in

<Callout type="tip" title="一分钟回顾：一张表吃透 be 动词过去式 + 介词三兄弟">
  本课把 Lesson 69 的 **was / were** 和本课四大块操练的 **at / on / in** 合在一起，是整个 NCE1 前半册的「地基总复习」——你以后说任何「某人过去什么时候在哪儿」，靠的全是这套组合。

  ### 第一轨：be 动词过去式的开关 — was vs were

  | 主语 | be 动词过去式 | 本课例 |
  |---|---|---|
  | I / He / She / It | **was** | *When **was** he at the stationer's?* |
  | You / We / They | **were** | *When **were** they at the stationer's?* |

  ### 第二轨：介词三兄弟的势力范围 — at vs on vs in

  | 介词 | 接什么 | 本课例 |
  |---|---|---|
  | **at** | 小地点（店铺、建筑）+ 具体时刻 | *at the stationer's / at the baker's / at four o'clock* |
  | **on** | 一天（星期、日期） | *on Monday / on January 21st / on June 26th* |
  | **in** | 大范围（国家、城市、月、季、年） | *in Denmark / in Australia / in July / in spring / in 1990* |

  ### ⚠️ 4 个本课最容易踩的雷区

  1. **星期和日期前面用 on，不是 in**：*on Monday* ✓ / *in Monday* ✗；*on January 21st* ✓ / *in January 21st* ✗。on = 具体某一天；in = 月份或比天大的范围。
  2. **国家和城市前面用 in，不是 at**：*in Australia* ✓ / *at Australia* ✗。对比：*at the stationer's*（小店铺，at）↔ *in Denmark*（国家，in）。
  3. **店铺 's 前面加 the**：*at the butcher's* ✓ / *at butcher's* ✗。the butcher's = the butcher's shop 的省略，the 不能省。
  4. **具体时刻用 at**：*at four o'clock* ✓ / *in four o'clock* ✗ / *on four o'clock* ✗。时刻是「点」，用 at——形成记忆链条：**at 时刻 → on 天 → in 月/季/年**。
</Callout>

## 4. 难点：at / on / in — 把三个介词刻进记忆

<Callout type="info" title="at/on/in 三级层级——错一个介词，老外立刻感到别扭">
  英语里说「什么时候在哪儿」，最核心的就是 **at / on / in** 三个介词的选择。本课四大块操练 + Written Exercise A 总共用了 30+ 个介词填空——目的就是让你在这些精确搭配中形成肌肉记忆。

  ### 一张图概括三级系统

  | 层级 | 介词 | 接什么 | 教材例 |
  |------|------|--------|--------|
  | **点** | at | 时刻、小地点（店铺/建筑） | *at four o'clock / at the stationer's* |
  | **天** | on | 星期、具体日期 | *on Monday / on January 21st* |
  | **范围** | in | 月、季、年、世纪、国家、城市 | *in July / in spring / in 1990 / in Denmark* |

  **记忆法**：
  - **at = 一个点**（钟点、店铺门口那个点）：钟表上的一个点用 at，地图上的一个店铺小点也用 at。
  - **on = 一个面/一天**（日历上那个日期）：星期几、几月几日——日历上一个具体的格子，用 on。
  - **in = 一个容器/范围**（这个月里、这个国家里、这一年里）：一个月、一个季节、一个年份、一个国家——你被"装"在里面，用 in。

  ### Written Exercise A 全解析

  教材 Written Exercise A 专练 at / on / in ——下面 6 题覆盖了三个介词的所有用法：

  1. *We were ___ the stationer's ___ Monday.* → at / on（小店铺 at；星期 on）
  2. *We were there ___ four o'clock.* → at（具体时刻 at）
  3. *They were ___ Australia ___ September.* → in / in（国家 in；月份 in）
  4. *They were there ___ spring.* → in（季节 in）
  5. *___ November 25th, they were ___ Canada.* → On / in（具体日期 On 句首大写；国家 in）
  6. *They were there ___ 1990.* → in（年份 in）

  6 道题，5 个 in、3 个 at、2 个 on——**in 用在大范围（国家、月、季、年），at 用在小地点和时刻，on 用在具体某一天。** 写完这 6 题还搞混，就把这张表抄三遍。
</Callout>

## 8. Written exercises 书面练习

<Callout type="info" title="Exercise A — Complete using at, on or in">
  *Complete these sentences using **at**, **on** or **in**. 用 at、on 或 in 填空。*

  1. We were ___ the stationer's ___ Monday.
  2. We were there ___ four o'clock.
  3. They were ___ Australia ___ September.
  4. They were there ___ spring.
  5. ___ November 25th, they were ___ Canada.
  6. They were there ___ 1990.
</Callout>

<Callout type="tip" title="Exercise A 参考答案（自己写完再看）">
  1. We were **at** the stationer's **on** Monday.
  2. We were there **at** four o'clock.
  3. They were **in** Australia **in** September.
  4. They were there **in** spring.
  5. **On** November 25th, they were **in** Canada.
  6. They were there **in** 1990.
</Callout>

<Callout type="info" title="Exercise B — Write questions and answers">
  *Write questions and answers using **we/they** and **at/in/on**. 仿照例句，用 we 或 they 以及 at、in、on 写出问句和答句。*

  **Examples（例句）：**

  Sam and Penny / the stationer's / Monday
  → **When were Sam and Penny at the stationer's?** / **They were at the stationer's on Monday.**

  you and Penny / Australia / July
  → **When were you and Penny in Australia?** / **We were in Australia in July.**

  1. you and Susan / the office / March 23rd
  2. Sam and Penny / India / 1986
  3. you and Penny / the baker's / Saturday
  4. Sam and Penny / Canada / 1993
  5. you and Penny / Austria / August
  6. Sam and Penny / home / May 25th
  7. you and Penny / Finland / December
  8. you and Sam / school / February 22nd
</Callout>

<Callout type="tip" title="Exercise B 参考答案（自己写完再看）">
  1. **When were you and Susan at the office?** — We were at the office on March 23rd.
  2. **When were Sam and Penny in India?** — They were in India in 1986.
  3. **When were you and Penny at the baker's?** — We were at the baker's on Saturday.
  4. **When were Sam and Penny in Canada?** — They were in Canada in 1993.
  5. **When were you and Penny in Austria?** — We were in Austria in August.
  6. **When were Sam and Penny at home?** — They were at home on May 25th.
  7. **When were you and Penny in Finland?** — We were in Finland in December.
  8. **When were you and Sam at school?** — We were at school on February 22nd.

  **关键提示**：
  - 问句中的主语如果是 **you and ...**（包括"我"），回答时用 **we**；如果主语是 **Sam and Penny**（第三方），回答时用 **they**。
  - 店铺/建筑（office, baker's, home, school）前用 **at**；国家（India, Canada, Austria, Finland）前用 **in**。
  - 具体日期/星期（March 23rd, Saturday, May 25th, February 22nd）前用 **on**；年份/月份（1986, 1993, August, December）前用 **in**。

## 10. 拓展练习 Going further

<Callout type="tip" title="自学者建议">
  - **影子跟读 (shadowing)**：用第 1 节的三个 SayIt 按钮各跟读 3-5 遍。重点练**介词粘连**——*at the stationer's* 要读得像一个词、*on Monday* 不能断开；以及 **was/were 的弱读**——*were* 在自然语流中常弱读为 /wə/，*they were* 读得像 *they wə*。
  - **看图说话**：句型操练课的终极训练——不看文字，只盯四块替换图在心里过：星期 × 文具店（on Monday at the stationer's）→ 日期 × 面包店/肉店（on Jan 21st at the baker's）→ 月份 × 丹麦（in July in Denmark）→ 年份 × 澳大利亚（in 1990 in Australia）。眼睛看图、嘴巴出句，不靠文字提示。
  - **造句迁移**：用今天学的 5 个结构造你自己的句子——
    1. **at + 店铺 + on + 星期**：*I was at the supermarket on Sunday. / Mum was at the hairdresser's on Friday.*
    2. **at + 店铺 + on + 日期**：*We were at the restaurant on March 15th. / He was at the cinema on my birthday.*
    3. **in + 国家 + in + 月份**：*They were in China in October. / I was in Japan in April.*
    4. **in + 国家 + in + 年份**：*My parents were in France in 2005. / She was in England in 2018.*
    5. **When were ... at/in ...?** 问遍身边的人：*When were you at the doctor's? When were they in Singapore? When was she at the library?*
  - **介词总复习——全书 at/on/in 一网打尽**：拿出笔记本，画三列：**at / on / in**。翻回前面学过的双数课（Lesson 2, 4, 6 ... 直到 68），把每课出现的 at/on/in 搭配写进对应列。例：*at school / at church / at the butcher's / on the left / on the right / on Sunday / in the crowd / in the country / in July / in 1990*。写完后，这张表就是你随身可查的「全册介词词典」。
  - **介词口诀**：把本课的"三级记忆法"背下来——**at 一个点，时刻小地都用它；on 一张面，星期日期都归它；in 一个大容器，国家月季年全装下。**
  - **Written Exercise B 口头练习**：不看答案，用 Exercise B 的 8 组提示词，先口头说出问句，再口头说出答句——每句都要同时过两个开关：① 人称 → was 还是 were？② 地点/时间 → at、on 还是 in？两个开关都拨对了，才算过关。
  - **Lesson 69 联动**：回看 Lesson 69 课文，把里面每一处 **was / were / at / on / in** 标出来。你会发现 69 课是 70 课的"实战版"——69 在真实叙事里用 was/were + 介词，70 把它们拆出来系统操练。两条线一起吃透，过去时态 + 介词搭配就永远过关了。
