## The Drills 句型操练

本课 6 组替换操练——每组一张插图 + 一句 if 条件句。核心公式：**If + 一般现在时, 主句用 will/can + 动词原形**。

| No. | 插图场景 | If 条件句 |
|-----|---------|-----------|
| 1 | 男孩踢球砸碎了窗户 | If you **break** this window, you**'ll have to pay for** it! |
| 2 | 两人冲向即将关门的列车 | If you **don't hurry**, we**'ll miss** the train. |
| 3 | 男子在冰面上滑倒 | If he **falls**, he**'ll hurt** himself. |
| 4 | 窗外大雨，一家人看着海边海报叹气 | If it **rains** tomorrow, we **won't go** to the seaside. |
| 5 | 病人从床上坐起，精神好转 | If you **feel better**, you **can get up**. |
| 6 | 男子擦车，旁边站着买家 | If he **sells** that car, he **can buy** a new one. |

注意每组 if 从句里的动词都是**一般现在时**——就算说的是将来的事，if 后面也绝不用 will。主句用 **will + 动词原形**（第 1-4 组）或 **can + 动词原形**（第 5-6 组，表示「能够、可以」）。第 4 组还出现了**否定主句 won't**（= will not），第 2 组出现了**否定从句 don't**。

## 课文注释 Notes on the text

<Callout type="info" title="5 个值得停下来嚼一嚼的点">
  1. **if 条件句（第一条件句）：If + 一般现在时, 主句用 will + 动词原形** —— 这是全课的核心语法。if 从句说出「条件」（先发生的事），主句说出「结果」（后发生的事）。**铁律：从句永远用一般现在时，主句用一般将来时（will + 动词原形）。** 例：*If you **break** this window, you**'ll have to pay for** it!*（如果你打碎这扇窗户，你就得赔！）—— break 是现在时，you'll have to pay = you will have to pay 是将来时。⚠️ 最容易踩的雷区：在 if 从句里用 will —— *If you will break ...* ✗ 绝对错误！

  2. **can 在 if 条件句的主句中**（本课第 5、6 组）—— 不仅 will 可以出现在主句中，**can**（能够、可以）也可以。当条件满足时，can 表示「就能够 / 就可以做某事」。例：*If you **feel better**, you **can get up**.*（如果你觉得好些了，你就可以起来了。）/ *If he **sells** that car, he **can buy** a new one.*（如果他卖了那辆车，他就可以买一辆新的。）注意 can 后面同样是接**动词原形**，与 will 规则一致。

  3. **if 条件句的否定**（本课第 2、4 组）—— 否定可以出现在三处：① **从句否定**（don't / doesn't）：*If you **don't hurry**, we'll miss the train.*（如果你不赶紧，我们会错过火车。）② **主句否定**（won't = will not）：*If it rains tomorrow, we **won't go** to the seaside.*（如果明天下雨，我们就不去海边了。）③ **两者都否定**：*If you don't study, you won't pass the exam.*（如果你不学习，你就通不过考试。）

  4. **have to** —— 「不得不、必须」—— 出现在第 1 组主句 *you'll **have to pay for** it* 中。**have to** 表示客观上的必须（规章、后果），比 must（主观意愿）更常用于谈「后果」。将来时写法：**will have to + 动词原形**。缩写：you'll have to。例：*If you break it, you'll **have to** pay for it.*（如果你打碎它，你就得赔。）

  5. **pay for** —— 「赔偿、为……付钱」—— 第 1 组出现。**pay for sth.** = 为某物付钱 / 赔偿某物。同型：*I paid £5 for this book.*（我花 5 英镑买了这本书。）/ *Who's going to pay for the broken window?*（谁来赔打碎的窗户？）注意区分：**pay sb.**（付钱给某人）—— *I paid the taxi driver.*（我付钱给的士司机）；**pay for sth.**（为某物付钱）—— *I paid for the meal.*（我付了饭钱）。两者可以合并：*I paid the waiter for the meal.*（我付了饭钱给服务员。）
</Callout>

## 3. 关键句型：第一条件句（First Conditional with if）

<Callout type="tip" title="一分钟回顾：if 引导的条件句是怎么搭起来的？">
  本课练的就是英语里最常用的条件句——**第一条件句（First Conditional）**。公式极简：

  > **If + 一般现在时, 主句用 will / can + 动词原形**

  拆开来看：

  | 部分 | 时态 | 说明 | 例 |
  |------|------|------|-----|
  | **if 从句**（条件） | **一般现在时** | 绝不用 will！ | If you **break** this window, ... |
  | **主句**（结果） | **will / can + 原形** | 也可用 won't 否定 | ... you**'ll have to pay for** it! |

  6 组操练按主句类型分为两组：

  **A 组：will 类**（第 1-4 组）—— 主句用 **will / 'll / won't**
  | No. | if 从句（现在时） | 主句（will + 原形） |
  |-----|-------------------|---------------------|
  | 1 | If you **break** this window, | you**'ll have to pay for** it! |
  | 2 | If you **don't hurry**, | we**'ll miss** the train. |
  | 3 | If he **falls**, | he**'ll hurt** himself. |
  | 4 | If it **rains** tomorrow, | we **won't go** to the seaside. |

  **B 组：can 类**（第 5-6 组）—— 主句用 **can + 动词原形**（表示「就可以、就能够」）
  | No. | if 从句（现在时） | 主句（can + 原形） |
  |-----|-------------------|--------------------|
  | 5 | If you **feel better**, | you **can get up**. |
  | 6 | If he **sells** that car, | he **can buy** a new one. |

  ### ⚠️ 5 个本课最容易踩的雷区

  1. **if 从句里绝不用 will** —— 就算说的是将来的事！*If it **will rain** tomorrow ...* ✗ —— 正确：*If it **rains** tomorrow ...* ✓。这是初学者最容易犯的头号错误。
  2. **主语是 he / she / it 时，if 从句的动词要加 -s** —— *If he **fall** ...* ✗ —— 正确：*If he **falls**, he'll hurt himself.* ✓（一般现在时，第三人称单数要加 -s）。
  3. **won't = will not** —— *we **won't go*** = we will not go。注意拼写是 won't，不是 willn't。同样 can't = cannot。
  4. **if 从句在前还是主句在前？** —— 两种都可以：*If you break this window, you'll have to pay for it.* ✓ / *You'll have to pay for it if you break this window.* ✓。区别：if 从句在前时，**从句后面要加逗号**；主句在前时，不要逗号。
  5. **can 和 will 不能叠用** —— *If you feel better, you **will can get up**.* ✗ —— 正确：*you **can get up*** 或 *you **will be able to get up***（can 没有将来式 will can，要表达「将来能够」用 will be able to）。
</Callout>

本课书面练习分为三部分：**A 部分**（回顾 Lesson 137 对话）、**B 部分**（if ... will 句型操练）、**C 部分**（... can ... if ... 句型操练）。

### Exercise A：回顾 Lesson 137

重读第 137 课的对话，然后回答以下问题：

> 1. What is Brian doing?
> 2. Has Brian ever won anything on the football pools?
> 3. What will Brian buy his wife if he wins a lot of money?
> 4. She doesn't want a mink coat, does she?
> 5. What does Julie want instead of a mink coat?
> 6. What will Brian do if he spends all the money?
> 7. It's only a dream, isn't it?
> 8. What does it all depend on?

<Callout type="tip" title="参考答案（自己做完再看）">
  1. **He is doing the football pools.**（他正在填足球赛赌注单。）
  2. **No, he has never won anything.**（没有，他从未赢过。Julie 说 *we never win anything*。）
  3. **He'll buy her a mink coat.**（他会给她买一件貂皮大衣。）
  4. **No, she doesn't.**（不，她不要。她说 *I don't want a mink coat!*）
  5. **She wants to see the world.**（她想见世面/环游世界。她说 *I want to see the world.*）
  6. **He'll try and win the football pools again.**（他会设法再赢一次足球赛赌注。）
  7. **Yes, it is.**（是的，只是一个梦。Julie 说 *It's a pleasant dream ...*）
  8. **It all depends on 'if'.**（一切都取决于那个「如果」。*everything depends on 'if'!*）
</Callout>

### Exercise B：回答 if 条件句

模仿例句回答以下问题——把提示词组转换为完整的 if 条件句：

> **例：** What will you do if you win a lot of money? → Stay at the best hotels.
> **→ If I win a lot of money, I'll stay at the best hotels.**

| 题号 | 问题 | 提示 | 你的回答 |
|------|------|------|----------|
| 1 | What will he do if he misses the bus? | Take a taxi. | **If he misses the bus, he'll take a taxi.** |
| 2 | What will he do if he doesn't sell his old car? | He won't buy a new one. | **If he doesn't sell his old car, he won't buy a new one.** |
| 3 | What will you do if they offer you more money? | Work less. | **If they offer me more money, I'll work less.** |
| 4 | What will he do if she doesn't type the letter? | Type it himself. | **If she doesn't type the letter, he'll type it himself.** |
| 5 | What will the children do if they come home early? | Play in the garden. | **If the children come home early, they'll play in the garden.** |
| 6 | What will you do if you are ill tomorrow? | I won't go to work. | **If I'm ill tomorrow, I won't go to work.** |
| 7 | What will you do if you go to the party? | Enjoy myself. | **If I go to the party, I'll enjoy myself.** |
| 8 | What will you do if he asks you? | Tell him the truth. | **If he asks me, I'll tell him the truth.** |
| 9 | What will they do if it rains tomorrow? | Stay at home. | **If it rains tomorrow, they'll stay at home.** |

### Exercise C：用 ... can ... if ... 改写句子

## 9. 拓展练习 Going further

<Callout type="tip" title="自学者建议">
  - **影子跟读 (shadowing)**：用第 1 节的三个 SayIt 各跟读 3-5 遍。本课 6 组句子节奏统一（if 从句引路 → 主句给出结果），重点练**从句结尾处的微升 + 逗号停顿**——说完 if 条件后要稍顿一下，让听者准备好接收「结果」。第 4 组的 **won't** 要重读，因为它是整句的转折点（原计划去海边，下雨 = 不去了）。

  - **看图说 if**：合上书，看着 6 幅插图的描述（不要看句子），用 *If ..., ... will/can ...* 口头说出完整条件句。先练 6 组原句，再换主语/动词造新句：
    1. (break window) → *If my little brother breaks the vase, my mum will be very angry.*
    2. (don't hurry) → *If I don't hurry, I'll be late for school.*
    3. (falls) → *If the baby falls, he'll cry.*
    4. (rains) → *If it rains this afternoon, we won't have the picnic.*
    5. (feel better) → *If I feel better tomorrow, I can go to the party.*
    6. (sells car) → *If my dad sells his old bike, he can buy a new one.*

  - **造句迁移**：用今天学的 5 个结构造你自己的句子——
    1. *If + 现在时, ... will ...*（未来的确定结果）—— *If I study hard, I'll pass the exam.*
    2. *If + 现在时, ... won't ...*（未来的否定结果）—— *If you eat too much, you won't feel well.*
    3. *If + 现在时, ... can ...*（条件满足就可以做）—— *If you finish your homework, you can watch TV.*
    4. *... can ... if ... is/are rich*（有钱就能……）—— *I can buy a big house if I am rich.*
    5. *If ... don't/doesn't ...*（否定条件）—— *If he doesn't come, we'll start without him.*

  - **if 从句语序灵活练习**：把下面 3 句的主句和从句位置互换，注意逗号的加减：
    1. *If you break it, you'll have to pay for it.* → *You'll have to pay for it if you break it.*（去掉逗号）
    2. *If it rains, we won't go out.* → *We won't go out if it rains.*（去掉逗号）
    3. *He can buy a new car if he sells the old one.* → *If he sells the old one, he can buy a new car.*（加上逗号）
    规则：从句在前 → 加逗号；主句在前 → 不加逗号。

  - **语法意识**：本课的三个「铁律」刻进肌肉记忆：
    ① **if 从句永远用一般现在时，绝不用 will / can**——不管事情发生在多远的将来。这是全世界英语学习者最容易犯的头号错误！
    ② **主语是 he / she / it 时，if 从句动词要加 -s**（一般现在时第三人称单数）：*If he **falls**, ...* / *If she **sells**, ...* ✓
    ③ **will / can 后面永远用动词原形**——不加 -s、不加 -ed、不加 -ing：*will miss* ✓ / *will misses* ✗ / *will can* ✗

  - **与 Lesson 137 联动**：回看 Lesson 137 的 6 句 if 条件句——*If I win a lot of money I'll buy you a mink coat.* / *If we win a lot of money we'll travel round the world.* / *If we spend all that money we'll be poor again.* ...——试试把这 6 句里的主句和从句位置互换，练习逗号的加减。然后再用 Lesson 137 的词汇（win / travel / spend / poor / depend on），套进本课的两种句型里各造一句。

  - **跨文化小知识**：**football pools**（足球赛赌注）——在 Lesson 137 里 Brian 在填这个，本课 Written Exercise A 又问了 8 道回顾题。**the pools** 是英国最经典的「平民博彩」之一，每周填一张表预测球赛比分，全中者可赢巨额奖金（有时高达百万英镑）。它和 lottery（乐透、摇号抽奖）不同——pools 需要「足球知识 + 运气」。英国有一句老笑话：*The football pools — the only way a working man can dream of being a millionaire.*（足球赌注——工人阶级做百万富翁梦的唯一途径。）这恰恰是 Lesson 137 那句 *It's a pleasant dream but everything depends on 'if'!* 的文化背景。
