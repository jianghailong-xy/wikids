  **Can Mr. Frith buy the television on instalments? How does it work? 弗里斯先生能分期付款买那台电视机吗？分期是怎么分的？** 带着这个问题听录音、读课文。本课的语法重点是 **多音节形容词的比较级与最高级（more expensive / the most expensive）** 和 **同级比较 not as ... as（不如……）**，全藏在买电视的讨价还价里。

## The Text 课文

**MR. FRITH:** I like this television very much. **How much does it cost?**

**ASSISTANT:** It's **the most expensive model** in the shop. **It costs** five hundred pounds.

**MRS. FRITH:** That's **too expensive for us**. We **can't afford** all that money.

**ASSISTANT:** This model's **less expensive than** that one. It's only three hundred pounds. But, of course, **it's not as good as** the expensive one.

**MR. FRITH:** I don't like this model. The other model's **more expensive**, but **it's worth the money**.

**MR. FRITH:** Can we **buy it on instalments**?

**ASSISTANT:** Of course. You can **pay a deposit** of thirty pounds, and then fourteen pounds a month for three years.

**MR. FRITH:** Do you like it, dear?

**MRS. FRITH:** I certainly do, but I don't like the price. You always want the best, but we can't afford it. Sometimes you think you're **a millionaire**!

**MR. FRITH:** Millionaires don't buy things on instalments!

## 课文注释 Notes on the text

<Callout type="info" title="7 个值得停下来嚼一嚼的点">
  1. **How much does it cost?** — "它多少钱？"这是询问价格的**最常用句式**。*cost* 在这里是不及物动词"值（多少钱）、花费"。也可说 *How much is it?* 同型：*How much does this book cost?*（这本书多少钱？）

  2. **It costs five hundred pounds.** — "售价 500 英镑。"注意**百位数**的读法：*five hundred*，hundred 后面不加 -s。同理：*three hundred*（300），*six hundred*（600）。如果要说"500 英镑"，hundred 和 pounds 之间不加 and。

  3. **We can't afford all that money.** — "我们花不起那么多钱。"**afford** 常与 can / can't / could / couldn't 连用，表示"有经济能力做某事"。句型：*can/can't afford + 名词* 或 *can/can't afford + to do*。例：*I can't afford a new car.*（我买不起新车。）/= *I can't afford **to buy** a new car.*

  4. **less expensive than** — "比……便宜"。**less + 形容词原级 + than** 是多音节形容词比较级的**降级比较**（"不那么……"），和 *cheaper than*（更便宜）是两种说法——*less expensive* = 不那么贵（偏书面），*cheaper* = 更便宜（偏口语）。同型：*less interesting than*（不如……有趣）、*less difficult than*（不如……难）。

  5. **not as good as** — "不如……好"。这是**同级比较的否定形式**。结构：*not as + 形容词原级 + as*，表示"前者不如后者……"。课文 *it's not as good as the expensive one* = 它不如贵的那台好。肯定形式是 *as good as*（和……一样好）。同型：*This book is not as interesting as that one.*（这本书不如那本有趣。）

  6. **buy it on instalments** — "分期付款购买"。**instalment** 是"分期付款的一期"，on instalments 是固定短语，表示"以分期付款的方式"。美式拼 *installment*。例：*We bought the car on instalments.*（我们用分期付款买了车。）

  7. **pay a deposit** — "付定金"。**deposit** 在这里指买东西时的"预付定金、首付"，不是"存款"。例：*You need to pay a deposit of 10%.*（你需要付 10% 的定金。）
</Callout>

## 3. 关键句型：多音节形容词的比较级与最高级 + not as ... as

<Callout type="tip" title="一分钟回顾：长的形容词前面加 more / most，而不是在后面加 -er / -est">
  第 107 课学了单音节和 -y 结尾双音节形容词的比较，规则是**词尾加 -er / -est**（small → smaller → smallest）。本课更进一步，学**多音节**（两音节及以上，且不以 -y 结尾）的形容词怎么比较：

  | 原级 Positive | 比较级 Comparative | 最高级 Superlative | 规则 |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | **expensive** 贵的 | **more** expensive 更贵的 | **the most** expensive 最贵的 | 三音节及以上：前面加 more / most |
  | **beautiful** 漂亮的 | **more** beautiful 更漂亮的 | **the most** beautiful 最漂亮的 | 同上 |
  | **interesting** 有趣的 | **more** interesting 更有趣的 | **the most** interesting 最有趣的 | 同上 |

  **核心规则**：长词不折腾词尾，只在前头加 more / most。一条判断标准：**音节 ≥ 3 的形容词、以及大多数不以 -y 结尾的双音节词（如 careful → more careful），一律用 more / most，不加 -er / -est。**

  ### 三级比较的四种表达（本课一个不落地全出场了）

  1. **比较级（升级）：more + 原级** → *The other model's more expensive, but it's worth the money.*
  2. **比较级（降级）：less + 原级** → *This model's less expensive than that one.*
  3. **最高级：the most + 原级** → *It's the most expensive model in the shop.*
  4. **同级比较否定：not as + 原级 + as** → *It's not as good as the expensive one.*

  ### ⚠️ 5 个本课最容易踩的雷区

  1. **长词别在后面加 -er / -est**：*more expensive* ✓ / *expensiver* ✗ —— 三音节词不可能加 -er！
  2. **最高级前必须有 the**：*the most expensive model* ✓ / *most expensive model* ✗ —— 最高级几乎永远带 the。
  3. **less + 原级 ≠ more + 原级的反义**：*less expensive* = 没那么贵（但可能还是贵）；*cheaper* = 更便宜。两个说法都对，但 *less expensive* 更委婉。
  4. **not as ... as 中间放形容词原级，不能放比较级**：*not as good as* ✓ / *not as better as* ✗ —— as...as 之间永远是原级！
  5. **表示"值（多少钱）"用 be worth**：*It's worth the money.* ✓ / *It's worthy the money.* ✗ —— *worth* 后面直接跟名词或动名词，不加 to。
</Callout>

## 8. 拓展练习 Going further

<Callout type="tip" title="自学者建议">
  - **影子跟读 (shadowing)**：用第 1 节的三个 SayIt 按钮各跟读 3-5 遍。这句对话节奏分明，重点练：店员报价时的冷静陈述调（*It's the most expensive model in the shop*）、介绍便宜机型时的先扬后抑（*But, of course, it's not as good as ...*），以及弗里斯先生最后那句俏皮的反驳（*Millionaires don't buy things on instalments!*），要带一点得意和幽默。
  - **角色扮演**：三人一组（Mr. Frith / Mrs. Frith / Assistant），把这个"买电视"的场景演一遍，重点体会弗里斯先生"非要贵的"的固执、夫人"精打细算"的务实，以及店员"两边不伤害"的职业话术。
  - **造句迁移**：用今天学的 5 个结构造你自己的句子——
    1. *主语 + can't afford + 名词 / to do*（买不起 / 负担不起）：*I can't afford a holiday this year.*
    2. *more + 多音节形容词 + than*（更……）：*This phone is more expensive than mine.*
    3. *the most + 多音节形容词*（最……）：*That was the most interesting film I've ever seen.*
    4. *not as + 原级 + as*（不如……）：*My car is not as fast as yours.*
    5. *less + 原级 + than*（不那么……）：*This book is less difficult than I expected.*
  - **比较级实战**：拿身边三样东西（手机、电脑、耳机……），分别用 *more expensive / the most expensive / less expensive / not as good as* 各造一句比较，把本课的四种比较结构都练一遍。
  - **分期付款算一算**：课文中的方案——首付 £30，月供 £14×36 月 = £504，总付款 £534，比一次付清 £500 多出 £34。用英语把这个计算写出来：*If they pay a deposit of £30, then £14 a month for three years, the total is £534 — £34 more than paying £500 at once.*
  - **延伸场景**：把课文场景换一换——不买电视了，改成"买车""买手机""买电脑"。选一样，仿照课文写一段 6-8 句的购物对话，必须用上 *the most expensive / can't afford / less ... than / not as ... as / on instalments / pay a deposit*。
