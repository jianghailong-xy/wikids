## The Drill 句型操练

Listen to the tape and look at the pictures. Answer each question using **have to / has to / had to** or **don’t need to / doesn’t need to**. 听录音，看图，根据图片里的回答提示，用 have to / has to / had to 或 don’t need to / doesn’t need to 作出回答：

### 1. Going now?

**Do you have to go now?**
Yes, I **have to** leave immediately.

**Did you have to take a taxi?**
I’m afraid I **had to**. I couldn’t get a bus.

### 2. Water the garden?

**Do you have to water the garden?**
No, I **don’t need to** water it now. It’s going to rain.

### 3. Get up early?

**Do you have to get up early tomorrow morning?**
Yes, I **have to** get up at six o’clock.

### 4. Waiting for a friend?

**How long have you had to wait?**
I’ve **had to** wait for two hours!

### 5. Walk to the station?

**Do we have to walk to the station?**
No, we **don’t need to**. We can catch a bus.

<Callout type="tip" title="操练提示">
  五组小场景练习了 **have to 的三种时态**：
  - **现在**：*Do you have to ...? — Yes, I have to ... / No, I don’t need to ...*
  - **过去**：*Did you have to ...? — Yes, I had to ... / No, I didn’t need to ...*
  - **完成**：*How long have you had to ...? — I’ve had to ...*

  操练时注意三样东西：
  - 第三人称单数要用 **has to**（如 *Does she have to ...? — Yes, she has to.*）
  - **don’t need to**（不必）≠ **mustn’t**（禁止）——这是本课最容易踩的雷
  - 回答要完整——不光说 *Yes, I have to.*，还要补上做什么：*Yes, I have to leave **immediately**.*
</Callout>

## 课文注释 Notes on the drill

<Callout type="info" title="6 个值得停下来嚼一嚼的点">
  1. **have to** (必须、不得不) — 表示由 **客观情况决定** 的必要性（“被事情逼的”），区别于 **must** 更多表达主观意愿或命令。*I have to leave immediately.*（我得马上走——有事等不了）。have to 可以用于任何时态：现在 *have to / has to*，过去 *had to*，将来 *will have to*，完成 *have had to*。

  2. **don’t need to / doesn’t need to** (不必、不需要) — 意思是“做这件事不是必需的，可以不做”。注意它 **不是** *mustn’t*（禁止/不准）。*No, I don’t need to water it now. It’s going to rain.*（不用浇——因为快下雨了）。这里的 *don’t need to* 也可以在英式英语中说成 **needn’t**（后接动词原形，不加 to）：*I needn’t water it now.* 美式英语更多用 *don’t need to*。

  3. **immediately** (/ɪˈmiːdiətli/) (p.259) — “立即、马上”，副词，修饰动作发生得快。*I have to leave immediately.* = 我得马上走。同义说法：*at once / right away*。形容词是 **immediate**。

  4. **I’m afraid I had to.** (p.259) — “恐怕我不得不（那样做）。” **I’m afraid** 在这里是口语里表示遗憾/抱歉的“软化”说法，不是“害怕”。后面接 **省略句 I had to**（= I had to take a taxi），把重复的动作省掉了——这是口语中非常自然的省略。

  5. **have to 的疑问句** — 因为 have to 在这里当实义动词（不是助动词），所以提问要借助 **do/does/did**：*Do you have to go? / Does she have to go? / Did you have to go?* 完成时直接用完成时助动词：*How long have you had to wait?*

  6. **had to / have had to / have to** — 三笔“时间账”：
  - *I had to* wait.（过去某个时刻不得不等）
  - *I’ve had to* wait for two hours!（从过去某个点一直等到现在）
  - *I have to* leave now.（现在必须走）
  把这三个读出声对比一遍，脑子里就拨清了 **have to 的时态“钟表”**。
</Callout>

## 3. 关键句型：have to / must / don’t need to / needn’t

<Callout type="tip" title="一分钟回顾：四个“兄弟”，两个“家”">
  本课要打通的两扇门：

  **第一扇门：「必须做」怎么说？—— have to 和 must**

  | 表达 | 含义 | 语气 | 例句 |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | **have to** | 必须、不得不 | 客观需要（被事情逼的） | *I have to get up at six o’clock. The bus leaves at 6:30.* |
  | **must** | 必须、一定 | 主观意志（说话人的命令/强烈的自我要求） | *You must come and see us.*（命令/热情邀请） |
  | **had to** | （过去）不得不 | 过去时态的 have to——must 没有过去式！ | *I had to take a taxi. I couldn’t get a bus.* |

  **第二扇门：「不必做」怎么说？—— don’t need to、needn’t 和 mustn’t 的“雷区”**

  | 表达 | 含义 | ⚠️ 别搞混 | 例句 |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | **don’t need to / doesn’t need to** | 不必、不需要（做不做随你） | 不是“禁止”！ | *I don’t need to water it now. It’s going to rain.* |
  | **needn’t** | 不必（英式更多用） | = don’t need to，语气更“英”一点 | *You needn’t hurry. We have plenty of time.* |
  | **mustn’t** | 不准、禁止、绝对不能 | **≠ don’t need to！** 它是在说“你绝不能做” | *You mustn’t smoke here.*（这里不准抽烟。） |

  ### ⚠️ 5 个本课最容易踩的雷区

  1. **must 没有过去式**：要说“过去必须”，只能用 **had to**。*I **must** go now.* → 过去 → *I **had to** go then.* ✓ / *I musted go.* ✗（没有这个说法）。
  2. **have to 的疑问句借 do**：*Do you have to go?* ✓ / *Have you to go?* ✗——have to 当实义动词处理，疑问句和否定句都要借助 do/does/did。
  3. **must 的否定回答 ≠ mustn’t**：*Must I go now? — No, you **needn’t** / you **don’t have to**.* ✓ / *No, you mustn’t.* ✗——**mustn’t 是“禁止”**，不是“不必”。答 Must I...? 的 No 要用 needn’t 或 don’t have to。
  4. **don’t need to 后面跟动词原形（带 to）**：*I don’t need to **water** it.* ✓ / *I don’t need to **watering** it.* ✗
  5. **第三人称单数：has to / doesn’t need to**：*She **has to** leave.* ✓ / *She **have to** leave.* ✗；*She **doesn’t need to** go.* ✓ / *She **don’t need to** go.* ✗
</Callout>

## 6. 书面练习 Written exercises

<Callout type="info" title="任务">
  *Do these three exercises.* **完成以下三项书面练习。** 这三项练习把本课的三个语法点——**have to 的疑问式、must → have to 转换、don’t need to / needn’t 否定回答**——各练一遍。
</Callout>

### A. 写问句并回答 Write questions and answers

<Callout type="info" title="练习 A">
  模仿例句，把下列句子变成**疑问句**，并做出回答。注意：have to 的句子用 **Do/Does ... have to ...?** 提问，否定答句用 **don’t/doesn’t need to**；must 的句子用 **Must ...?** 提问，否定答句用 **needn’t**。

  **Examples 例句：**
  - You have to leave early. → **Do you have to leave early?** You **don’t need to**.
  - She must leave early. → **Must she leave early?** She **needn’t**.

  | # | 原句 | 你的问句 + 回答 |
  |---|---|---|
  | 1 | She has to decide immediately. | |
  | 2 | She must decide immediately. | |
  | 3 | We have to take a taxi. | |
  | 4 | We must take a taxi. | |
</Callout>

<Callout type="tip" title="参考答案（写完再看）">
  1. **Does she have to decide immediately?** She **doesn’t need to**.
  2. **Must she decide immediately?** She **needn’t**.
  3. **Do we have to take a taxi?** We **don’t need to**.
  4. **Must we take a taxi?** We **needn’t**.

  核心规律：
  - *have to* 疑问 → 借 **do/does**，否定答 → **don’t/doesn’t need to**
  - *must* 疑问 → 把 **must 提前**，否定答 → **needn’t**（不是 mustn’t！）
</Callout>

### B. 模仿例句回答 Answer these questions

<Callout type="info" title="练习 B">
  使用 **have to / has to** 或 **don’t / doesn’t need to** 回答下列问题。

  **Example 例句：**
  - I must go now. What about you? → **I have to go, too.**

  | # | 原句 | 你的回答 |
  |---|---|---|
  | 1 | I must telephone him. What about you? | |
  | 2 | Mary must wait for him. What about Mary? | |
  | 3 | I must meet her. What about you? | |
  | 4 | They must travel by ship. What about Tom and Mary? | |
</Callout>

<Callout type="tip" title="参考答案（写完再看）">
  1. I **have to** telephone him, too.
  2. She **has to** wait for him, too.
  3. I **have to** meet her, too.
  4. They **have to** travel by ship, too.

  注意：“What about + 人？”改写回答时要**把主语换成对应代词**（Mary → she；Tom and Mary → they），同时 **have to 跟着变**（I → have to；She → has to；They → have to）。
</Callout>

### C. 写问句 Write questions

<Callout type="info" title="练习 C">
  模仿例句，把下列句子变成 **have to 的疑问句**。把 must 替换成 have to，并用 Do/Does 来提问。

  **Example 例句：**
  - I must go now. → **Do I have to go now?**

  | # | 原句 | 你的问句 |
  |---|---|---|
  | 1 | I must telephone him. | |
  | 2 | Mary must wait for him. | |
  | 3 | I must meet her. | |
  | 4 | Tom and Mary must travel by ship. | |
</Callout>

<Callout type="tip" title="参考答案（写完再看）">
  1. **Do I have to** telephone him?
  2. **Does Mary have to** wait for him?
  3. **Do I have to** meet her?
  4. **Do Tom and Mary have to** travel by ship?

  must → have to 转换的两步：
  1. 把 must 换成 have to（注意第三人称用 **has to**）
  2. 用 Do/Does 提问（Mary she → **Does she have to ...?**）
</Callout>

## 10. 拓展练习 Going further

<Callout type="tip" title="自学者建议">
  - **影子跟读 (shadowing)**：用第 1 节的三个 SayIt 按钮各跟读 3-5 遍。重点练：
    - 问句的上扬声调：*Do you have to \`go\` now?* ↗
    - have to 的吞音连读：/ˈhæf tə/（have to 在快速口语中常读成 *hafta*）
    - 感叹句的语气：*I’ve had to wait for two \`hours\`!*（压着点不耐烦）
  - **替换操练 (substitution drill)**：拿书面练习 A 的 4 句话从头过一遍——合上书，嘴巴说出问句和否定回答。重点分两条路线练：
    - *She has to ...* → *Does she have to ...?* — *She doesn’t need to.*
    - *She must ...* → *Must she ...?* — *She needn’t.*
  - **造句迁移**：用本课的结构造你自己的句子——
    1. *I have to + 动词原形*（我不得不……）— *I have to finish this report before 5 p.m.*
    2. *I don’t need to + 动词原形 + because ...*（我不必……因为……）— *I don’t need to rush because I have plenty of time.*
    3. *Must I ...? — No, you needn’t.*（我必须……吗？不，不必。）— *Must I wear a tie? — No, you needn’t.*
    4. *How long have you had to ...?*（你不得不……多久了？）— *How long have you had to study for this exam?*
    5. *Do we have to ...? — No, we don’t need to. We can ... instead.*（我们非得……吗？不，不必。我们可以……）— *Do we have to drive? — No, we don’t need to. We can walk.*
  - **mustn’t vs. needn’t 对比记忆**：这是本课乃至整个 NCE1 最经典的“一对反义词不是反义词”：
    - *You **mustn’t** smoke here.* = 这里不准抽烟。（禁止）
    - *You **needn’t** smoke.* = 你不必抽烟。（你可以不抽，但你要抽的话我也不拦着）
    - 记住了：**mustn’t = 禁令（红线），needn’t = 松绑（绿灯）**
  - **提前预习**：下一课 Lesson 127 *A famous actress* 是一篇对话课，你将听到“认出一个名人但不敢确定”的精彩对话——用到 **can’t be**（不可能是）和 **must be**（肯定是）来推测。本课学的 **must（必须）** 到那里会“变脸”成另一个意思——**must（一定、肯定）**。
</Callout>
