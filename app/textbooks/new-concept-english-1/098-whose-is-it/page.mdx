  上节课（Lesson 97），霍尔先生在火车上丢了一只蓝色小箱子，到失物招领处认领时，服务员问了一句 *Is this case yours?*（这箱子是你的吗？），霍尔先生看了看说 *No, that's not mine.*（不，那不是我的。）——最后发现服务员给错了箱子，霍尔先生急了：*This case doesn't belong to me!*（这箱子不是我的！）

  yours、mine、belong to……这一串"你的、我的、属于……"今天要一口气练透。**所有格代词（possessive pronouns）** 是英语里表达"归属"的捷径——不说 *It is my book*，直接说 ***It is mine.** *（它是我的。）；不说 *They are her keys*，直接说 ***They are hers.** *（它们是她的。）。mine、yours、his、hers、ours、theirs 这 6 个词最大特点：**独立使用，后面绝不跟名词**——它们自己就是"（某人的）东西"。

  本课是**句型操练课**：12 幅插图，全部围绕两套句型——*Does this belong to ...? / Is this ...?*（单数）和 *Do these belong to ...? / Are these ...?*（复数）。读完这课，你就能在 *This is my book. = This book is mine. = This book belongs to me.* 三种说法之间自由切换。

  **Listen to the tape then answer the questions. 听录音，然后回答问题。** 每幅插图配有一组问答——*Does this belong to me? Is this mine?* 听录音时注意：**belong to 后面跟宾格代词（me/you/him/her/us/them）**，而 **Is this ...? 后面跟所有格代词（mine/yours/his/hers/ours/theirs）**——两个句型各用各的尾巴，别串。
</Callout>

## The Pattern Drills 句型操练

下面 12 组句型是本课全部操练内容。前 7 组是"母句型"——从 *Does this belong to me? / Is this mine?* 到 *Do these belong to them? / Are these theirs?*——涵盖了全部 6 种人称的单数和复数；后 5 组是综合操练。

朗读时注意两条铁律：
1. **belong to 后面永远跟宾格代词**（me, you, him, her, us, them），绝对不能跟所有格代词（× belong to mine / hers / theirs）。
2. **Is this ...? / Are these ...? 后面永远跟所有格代词**（mine, yours, his, hers, ours, theirs），这些词后面不能再加名词。

---

**单数物品 → Does this belong to ...? / Is this ...?**

1. **Does this belong to me?** — Is this mine? — No, it doesn't belong to you. It's not yours. It belongs to me. It's mine.

2. **Does this belong to you?** — Is this yours? — No, it doesn't belong to me. It's not mine. It belongs to you. It's yours.

3. **Does this belong to him?** — Is this his? — Yes, it belongs to him. It's his.

4. **Does this belong to her?** — Is this hers? — Yes, it belongs to her. It's hers.

---

**复数物品 → Do these belong to ...? / Are these ...?**

5. **Do these belong to us?** — Are these ours? — Yes, they belong to us. They're ours.

6. **Do these belong to you?** — Are these yours? — Yes, they belong to you. They're yours.

7. **Do these belong to them?** — Are these theirs? — Yes, they belong to them. They're theirs.

---

**综合操练（单数 vs 复数混练）**

8. **Does this suitcase belong to you?** — Is this yours? — No, it doesn't belong to me. It's not mine. It belongs to my brother. It's his.

9. **Do these keys belong to her?** — Are these hers? — Yes, they belong to her. They're hers.

10. **Does this watch belong to him?** — Is this his? — No, it doesn't belong to him. It's not his. It belongs to Jane. It's hers.

11. **Do these tickets belong to us?** — Are these ours? — No, they don't belong to us. They're not ours. They belong to them. They're theirs.

12. **Does this umbrella belong to me?** — Is this mine? — Yes, it belongs to you. It's yours.

## 句型讲解 Notes on the pattern

<Callout type="info" title="7 个值得停下来嚼一嚼的点">
  1. **belong to + 宾格代词，不是所有格代词** — *This case belongs to **me**.* ✓ / *This case belongs to **mine**.* ✗ —— belong to 是"属于（某人）"，to 后面要接"人"，所以用宾格代词（me, you, him, her, us, them）。所有格代词 mine / yours / his / hers 代表的是"某人的**东西**"，不是"某人"，不能放在 belong to 后面。

  2. **所有格代词 = 形容词性物主代词 + 名词，独立使用不加名词** — *This book is **mine**.*（mine = my book）✓ / *This is **mine** book.* ✗ —— mine / yours / his / hers / ours / theirs 已经是"（某人的）那个东西"了，后面不能再加任何名词。对比：**形容词性物主代词**（my / your / his / her / our / their）后面**必须**跟名词：*This is **my** book.* ✓ / *This is **my**.* ✗。一表总结：

  | 人称 | 形容词性（+名词） | 名词性（独立） | 宾格（belong to 后） |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | 我 | my | mine | me |
  | 你 | your | yours | you |
  | 他 | his | his | him |
  | 她 | her | hers | her |
  | 我们 | our | ours | us |
  | 你们 | your | yours | you |
  | 他们 | their | theirs | them |

  3. **his 既是形容词性又是名词性** — *This is **his** book.*（his = 他的，形容词性）→ *This book is **his**.*（his = 他的（东西），名词性）——his 是唯一一个"形、名同形"的所有格，两种用法长相一样，但后面跟不跟名词决定了它是哪种身份。hers 比 her 多一个 s，其他所有格代词都比形容词性多一个 s（除了 mine——my → mine 是特殊的）。

  4. **单数用 Is this ...? / Does this belong to ...?；复数用 Are these ...? / Do these belong to ...?** — 判断单复看物品：一个 item → *Is this **yours**?*；多个 items → *Are these **yours**?*。注意 *yours* 既可以指"你的（单数）"也可以指"你们的（复数）"，本身不区分单复——单复由动词（is / are）和指示代词（this / these）来标记。

  5. **否定形式：doesn't belong to / don't belong to** — 第三人称单数用 **doesn't** + 动词原形 belong：*It **doesn't belong** to me.*（它不属于我。）。非单三用 **don't**：*They **don't belong** to us.*（它们不属于我们。）。belong 是实义动词，否定和疑问需要借用 do / does / did——这与情态动词 can 完全不同（can 自己就能变否定和疑问，不需要借 do）。

  6. **简略否定：It's not mine. / They're not ours.** — 两种否定方式：① ***It doesn't belong to me.** *（完整句，否定 belong）；② ***It's not mine.** *（简略，否定所有格代词）。两种说法日常生活中都在用，belong to 否定更正式，not + 所有格代词更口语。

  7. **Whose ...? 提问归属** — *Whose is this?*（这是谁的？）→ *It's mine.* / *Whose are these?*（这些是谁的？）→ *They're hers.* —— **whose** 是提问"归属"的特殊疑问词，回答用所有格代词。*Whose book is this?*（whose 后跟名词 = 形容词性用法）→ *It's my book. / It's mine.* —— whose 和所有格代词一样，也有"带名词"和"独立使用"两种场合。
</Callout>

## 3. 关键句型：所有格代词 Possessive pronouns

<Callout type="tip" title="一分钟回顾：所有格代词 =「某人的东西」，不跟名词，独立站">
  英语里表达"归属"有三条路，都通向同一个意思：

  | 方式 | 结构 | 例句 |
  |---|---|---|
  | ① 形容词性物主代词 + 名词 | my / your / his / her / our / their + **名词** | This is **my book**. |
  | ② 名词性物主代词（所有格代词） | mine / yours / his / hers / ours / theirs（**独立**） | This book is **mine**. |
  | ③ belong to + 宾格代词 | belong(s) to + me / you / him / her / us / them | This book **belongs to me**. |

  三条路的换算公式：
  - ① → ②：去名词，加 s（my → mine, your → yours, her → hers, our → ours, their → theirs；his 不变）
  - ① / ② → ③：所有格代词 → 宾格代词 + belong(s) to（mine → belongs to me, hers → belongs to her）

  ### ⚠️ 6 个本课最容易踩的雷区

  1. **belong to 后面跟宾格，不是所有格代词**：*belongs to **me**.* ✓ / *belongs to **mine**.* ✗ —— to 后面要接"人"，用宾格代词（me/him/her/us/them）。mine / yours / his 这些代表的是"东西"，不是"人"。

  2. **所有格代词后面不加名词**：*It's **mine**.* ✓ / *It's **mine** book.* ✗ —— mine 本身就是 my book 的"压缩包"，已经包含了"东西"，再加名词就重复了。

  3. **belong 的疑问和否定需要借 do / does / did**：***Does** this belong to you?* ✓ / ***Is** this belong to you?* ✗ —— belong 是实义动词（不是情态动词，也不是 be 动词），否定/疑问需要助动词 do/does/did 帮忙。don't belong / doesn't belong / didn't belong。

  4. **人称转换要一致**：*Does this belong to **me**? → Yes, it belongs to **you**.* —— 问句主语是 me（我），肯定回答要把人称转成 you（你），因为"这个东西属于我"从答话人嘴里说出来就变成了"这个东西属于你"。my → you, you → me, your → mine……人称的进/出转换是英语会话的基本功。

  5. **his 形名同形，但 hers 比 her 多 s**：*This is **his** book.* → *This book is **his**.*（不变）；*This is **her** book.* → *This book is **hers**.*（加 s）——his 是唯一"形名同形"的；her → hers 是最容易漏 s 的（因为读音上差别很细微）。

  6. **所有格代词的单复数不由自身决定，由动词和指示代词决定**：*Is this **yours**?*（单数） vs. *Are these **yours**?*（复数）——yours 这个词本身单复同形，到底是"你的（一个）"还是"你们的（多个）"，取决于前面的 is/are 和 this/these。
</Callout>

## 4. 进阶：三种说法自由转换

<Callout type="info" title="从「只有一种说法」到「三种说法随心换」">
  本课的核心是打通归属性表达的"任督二脉"——同一句话有三种说法：

  > 这是我的书。
  > ① This is **my book**.（形容词性物主代词 + 名词）
  > ② This book is **mine**.（所有格代词独立作表语）
  > ③ This book **belongs to me**.（belong to + 宾格代词）

  日常对话里，① 最基础（介绍物品用），② 最简洁（一句话搞定归属），③ 最正式（强调"所有权"）。三种说法能自由切换，才算真正学会了本课。
</Callout>

## 8. 拓展练习 Going further

<Callout type="tip" title="自学者建议">
  - **影子跟读 (shadowing)**：用第 1 节的三组 SayIt 各跟读 3-5 遍。重点练 **belong to** 的节奏——*It be-LONGS to ME.* —— belong 重读，to 弱读，me/him/her 轻读。*Is this YOURS?* 的疑问语调要上扬，yours 尾音 /z/ 不要吞掉。

  - **人称转换对练**：合上课本，把 Written exercise B 的 9 组问答各练一遍——核心是**问句的人称 → 答句的人称的自然转换**：
    1. *Is this your ...? → Yes, it's mine. It belongs to me.*（your → mine / me）
    2. *Is this John's ...? → Yes, it's his. It belongs to him.*（John's → his / him）
    3. *Is this Jane's ...? → Yes, it's hers. It belongs to her.*（Jane's → hers / her）
    4. *Are these their ...? → Yes, they're theirs. They belong to them.*（their → theirs / them）
    5. *Is this my ...? → Yes, it's yours. It belongs to you.*（my → yours / you）
    6. *Is this our ...? → Yes, it's yours. It belongs to you.*（our → yours / you）
    7. *Are these her ...? → Yes, they're hers. They belong to her.*（her → hers / her）
    8. *Are these Frank's and Gary's ...? → Yes, they're theirs. They belong to them.*（两人物主 → theirs / them）
    9. *Are these the children's ...? → Yes, they're theirs. They belong to them.*（children's → theirs / them）

  - **三种说法自由切换**：拿身边 5 个物品，每个用三种说法说一遍：
    1. 手机：*This is my phone. / This phone is mine. / This phone belongs to me.*
    2. 钥匙：*These are my keys. / These keys are mine. / These keys belong to me.*
    3. 书包：*This is your bag. / This bag is yours. / This bag belongs to you.*
    4. 同事的笔：*This is his pen. / This pen is his. / This pen belongs to him.*
    5. 朋友的车票：*These are her tickets. / These tickets are hers. / These tickets belong to her.*

  - **造句迁移**：用本课学的 belong to + 所有格代词造 5 个关于你自己家里的句子：
    1. *The blue suitcase belongs to my father. It's his.*
    2. *The red coat belongs to my mother. It's hers.*
    3. *These books belong to my sister and me. They're ours.*
    4. *That bicycle belongs to my brother. It's his.*
    5. *Those keys on the table belong to you. They're yours.*

  - **两条铁律默写**：合上课本，把本课的两条铁律默写一遍——① **belong to 后面永远跟宾格代词**（me/you/him/her/us/them），绝对不跟所有格代词（mine/yours/his/hers/ours/theirs）；② **所有格代词后面永远不跟名词**——mine = my + 名词，已经"打包"好了。每次想说"属于……"或"……的"之前，先在脑子里过这两条——错了其中任何一条，整句就是 broken。

  - **小测试**：把下面这 6 个中文说法的英文写出来，再对照答案：
    1. 它是我的。→ *It's mine.*
    2. 它是你的。→ *It's yours.*
    3. 它是他的。→ *It's his.*
    4. 它是她的。→ *It's hers.*
    5. 它是我们的。→ *It's ours.*
    6. 它是他们的。→ *It's theirs.*
    
    注意第 3 个 his 不变化，第 4 个 hers 比 her 多 s——这两道最容易被"形式坑"绊倒。
</Callout>
