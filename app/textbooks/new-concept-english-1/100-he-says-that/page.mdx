## The Pattern Drills 句型操练

下面是本课的替换表——左侧是"谁说 / 怎么说的 + that"，中间是"他／她／他们"，右侧是谓语 + 补语。朗读时注意：每个完整句都是 **He + 转述动词 (says/thinks ...) + that + he/she/they + 谓语 + 补语** 的框架。主句用一般现在时（says/thinks 等），从句用相应的现在时形式。

| He<br/>She<br/>They | says<br/>say<br/>thinks<br/>believes<br/>knows<br/>understands<br/>is afraid<br/>is sorry<br/>is sure | that | he<br/>she<br/>they | is/are<br/>feel(s)<br/>has/have (got)<br/>need(s)<br/>want(s)<br/>can<br/>must<br/>will | ... |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|

配合 16 幅插图里的补语，你可以造出上百个不同的句子——例如：

**He says that he feels ill.** 他说他觉得不舒服。

**She thinks that she has got a cold.** 她觉得自己感冒了。

**He says that he needs a haircut.** 他说他需要理发。

**They say that they must wait for a bus.** 他们说他们必须等公共汽车。

**He hopes that he will sell his house.** 他希望自己能卖掉房子。

**She is afraid that she has got an earache.** 她怕自己得了耳痛。

**He is sorry that he feels tired.** 他很抱歉——他觉得很累。

**He believes that he needs an X-ray.** 他相信自己需要拍 X 光片。

下面 16 幅插图的补语依次是：

1. **ill** — 不舒服、生病了
2. **tired** — 累了
3. **thirsty** — 渴了
4. **cold** — 冷
5. **a cold** — （得了）感冒
6. **a headache** — 头痛
7. **an earache** — 耳痛
8. **a toothache** — 牙痛
9. **a haircut** — 理发
10. **a licence** — 执照
11. **an X-ray** — X 光（检查）
12. **some money** — 一些钱
13. **catch a bus** — 赶公共汽车
14. **wait for a bus** — 等公共汽车
15. **repair** — 修理
16. **sell his house** — 卖（他的）房子

## 句型讲解 Notes on the pattern

<Callout type="info" title="7 个值得停下来嚼一嚼的点">
  1. **间接引语（现在时主句）的基本结构：He says that + 从句** — 主句是一般现在时（He says / He thinks / He knows ...），从句的时态**不需要往回推**（这一点与主句是过去时 He said ... 不同）。从句的人称要从"我"变成"他／她"：*I feel ill.* → *He says that **he feels** ill.*（I → he, feel → feels）。**that 是一个"连接词"**——它在口语里常常被省略（就说 *He says he feels ill.*），但本课作为间接引语的第一课，**先练带 that 的完整版**，把结构搭稳。

  2. **转述动词不止 says 一种** — 除了 **says**（说），本课替换表还提供了 **thinks**（认为）、**believes**（相信）、**knows**（知道）、**understands**（理解／明白）、**is afraid**（恐怕／担心）、**is sorry**（抱歉）、**is sure**（确定）——用不同的转述动词，可以把同一件事从"陈述"变成"推测""担忧""确信"等不同语气。例：*He says that he has got a cold.*（他说他感冒了）→ *He thinks that he has got a cold.*（他觉得自己感冒了）→ *He is afraid that he has got a cold.*（他担心自己感冒了）。

  3. **人称转换的铁律** — 间接引语里从句的主语不再是"我 (I)"而是转述对象"他／她 (he/she)"。从句的动词也必须跟这个新主语保持一致：*I **have** got a cold.* → *He says that he **has** got a cold.*（have → has）；*I **am** thirsty.* → *She says that she **is** thirsty.*（am → is）；*We **want** some money.* → *They say that they **want** some money.*（we → they，但 want 不变——因为 they 不触发三单 -s）。

  4. **has got = has（"有"，英式口语尤其常用 have got）** — *He says that he **has got** a cold.* = *He says that he **has** a cold.* 身体不适类名词（a cold, a headache, an earache, a toothache）前面用 **a** 或 **an**，因为它们是可数名词的一次具体发作。同型：*have a fever / have a sore throat / have a stomach ache*。

  5. **need + 名词 vs need to + 动词** — *He says that he **needs a haircut**.*（need + 名词：需要理发）/*He says that he **needs to have a haircut**.*（need to + 动词：需要去理个发）。本课练的是 need + 名词（a haircut / a licence / an X-ray / some money）。同型：*I need some rest. / She needs a new coat.* 注意：**some money** 中的 some 用于不可数名词 money 前——不能说 *a money*。

  6. **will / must / can 后接动词原形，永远不变** — *He hopes that he **will sell** his house.* / *He knows that he **must wait** for a bus.* / *He says that he **can repair** it.* 情态动词（will, must, can）后面永远接动词原形，不受主语人称影响——*he will sell*（✓），*he wills sells*（✗）。must 表"必须、不得不"，can 表"能够"，will 表"将会"。

  7. **-ache 类词的拼写与发音** — **headache** /ˈhedeɪk/（头痛）、**earache** /ˈɪəreɪk/（耳痛）、**toothache** /ˈtuːθeɪk/（牙痛）。三个要点：① -ache 发 /eɪk/（与字母 A 的发音一样）；② toothache 里 tooth 保留 h，不要写成 *tootache*；③ earache 以元音音素 /ɪ/ 开头，冠词用 **an earache**。
</Callout>

## 3. 关键句型：间接引语（现在时主句 · 带 that）Reported Speech with Present Tense Main Clause

<Callout type="tip" title="一分钟回顾：现在时主句的间接引语，that 是桥梁，人称要变、时态不变">
  英语里转述别人说的话有两种情况：
  - **主句是现在时**（He **says** / He **thinks** ...）→ 从句时态**不用往回推**，只需调整人称。用 **that** 把主句和从句连起来——这是间接引语的"完整版"（口语里 that 常省略）。
  - **主句是过去时**（He **said** / He **thought** ...）→ 从句时态要**往回推一格**（will → would, can → could, is → was 等）。这是 NCE1 后半册（Lesson 133+）的内容，本课先专攻第一种。

  | 直接引语 (Direct Speech) | 间接引语 (Reported Speech) | 变化 |
  |---|---|---|
  | I feel ill. | He says that **he feels** ill. | I → he, feel → feels |
  | I have got a cold. | She says that **she has got** a cold. | I → she, have → has |
  | I need a haircut. | He says that **he needs** a haircut. | I → he, need → needs |
  | I can repair it. | She says that **she can repair** it. | I → she（can 不变） |
  | I will sell it. | He says that **he will sell** it. | I → he（will 不变） |

  核心规则只有一条：**把第一人称"我"换成说话人的"他／她"，谓语动词在人称和数上与主语保持一致，that 把前后连起来。**

  ### ⚠️ 5 个本课最容易踩的雷区

  1. **人称不换**：*He says that I feel ill.* ✗（I 还是"我"，没变成"他"）→ *He says that **he feels** ill.* ✓
  2. **忘记第三人称单数 -s**：*She says that she **need** a haircut.* ✗ → *She says that she **needs** a haircut.* ✓（she 是三单，动词加 -s）
  3. **主句动词忘加 -s**：*He **say** that he feels ill.* ✗ → *He **says** that he feels ill.* ✓
  4. **have → has 忘变**：*She says that she **have got** a cold.* ✗ → *She says that she **has got** a cold.* ✓
  5. **情态动词后多加了东西**：*He knows that he **musts** wait.* ✗ / *He knows that he **must to** wait.* ✗ → *He knows that he **must** wait.* ✓
</Callout>

## 9. 拓展练习 Going further

<Callout type="tip" title="自学者建议">
  - **影子跟读 (shadowing)**：用第 1 节的三组 SayIt 各跟读 3-5 遍。重点练 **that 的轻读**——*He says that he feels ill.* 中的 that 念得很快、很轻（/ðət/，几乎只有 /ðt/），不要重读它。整个句子的重音落在 **says**、**feels**、**ill** 三个实词上，that 和 he 都是"滑过去的"。
  - **看图转述**：合上书，回忆这 16 幅图，每幅图用 *He says that he ... / She says that she ... / They say that they ...* 造一句。**三步走**：① 挑主句主语（He / She / They）+ 转述动词（says? thinks? hopes? is afraid?）→ ② 加 that → ③ 人称转换 + 加补语。16 句下来，*... says that ...* 的语感就定型了。
  - **替换表自由玩**：打开替换表，从第一列挑主语（He / She / They）+ 转述动词（says / thinks / believes / knows / understands / is afraid / is sorry / is sure），固定加 that，从第二列挑从句主语（he / she / they）+ 谓语（is/are / feel(s) / has/have got / need(s) / want(s) / can / must / will），再从 16 幅插图里挑一个补语——造出一个你自己的句子。8×8×16 = 好几百个组合！试着挑你平时不常用的（比如 *He is sorry that he needs some money. / She understands that she must wait.*），让大脑**真正"组装"英语而不是背诵英语**。
  - **that 的去留意识**：本课是间接引语的第一课，**先练带 that 的完整版**，把结构搭稳。日常生活中 that 经常被省略——*He says he feels ill.*（不加 that 也对）。练熟本课后，试着把 that 去掉，说同样的话，感觉一下省略 that 后的语流变化。后续 Lesson 102 就会正式练"不加 that"的省略版。
  - **造句迁移**：用今天学的 5 个核心结构造你自己的句子——
    1. *He says that he feels ...*（他说他觉得……）—— *He says that he feels hungry. / She says that she feels nervous.*
    2. *I think that I have got ...*（我觉得我得了……）—— *I think that I have got a fever. / I think that I have got a sore throat.*
    3. *He hopes that he will ...*（他希望自己会……）—— *He hopes that he will find a good job. / She hopes that she will pass the exam.*
    4. *She is afraid that she ...*（她担心她……）—— *She is afraid that she will be late. / He is afraid that he has lost his wallet.*
    5. *I know that I must ...*（我知道我必须……）—— *I know that I must study harder. / I know that I must get up early tomorrow.*
  - **间接引语进阶意识**：本节课你练的是**主句现在时的间接引语**（人称变、时态不变）。后续第 133-136 课会学到**主句过去时的间接引语**（人称变、时态也要往回推——*He said that he felt ill.*）。现在先把这个"that 连接 + 人称转换 + 三单 -s"的基本功练熟，为后面的进阶铺好路。
  - **-ache 词汇扩展**：本课学了 headache, earache, toothache——英语里以 -ache（/eɪk/ 痛）结尾的病痛还有：**backache**（背痛）、**stomach ache**（胃痛／肚子痛，分两词写）、**heartache**（心痛，多指情感上）。试着用本课句型各造一句：*He says that he has got a backache. / She says that she has got a stomach ache.*
  - **与 Lesson 99 串起来**：Lesson 99 的课文就是一个"间接引语实战现场"——安迪摔了、露西转述 *The doctor says that he will come at once.*（医生说他马上就来。）和 *I'm sure that you need an X-ray.*（我确定你需要拍 X 光片。）本课的句型就是从这里抽出来的。回到 Lesson 99 的课文，把安迪和露西的对话用本课的替换表造出更多版本——*Andy says that he has hurt his back. Lucy thinks that Andy needs a doctor. The doctor says that he will come at once.*
</Callout>
