  先看第一块 12 幅钟面，每幅在心里用英文说出时间；再看第二块 10 组日期地点图，每幅问一句 **When was/were ... at ...?**，再答一句 **... was/were at ... on ...**。然后翻到本课末尾的**生词表**和**书面练习**，把时间和地点的表达刻进肌肉记忆。

## The Drills 句型操练

### Part 1 — What's the time? 几点钟？

下面是本课第一块 **12 幅钟面替换操练**——每幅钟面配同一问一答的格式。先问 **What's the time?**，再按指针位置说出时间。前 6 幅用「分 past 时」，第 7 幅是 half past，后 5 幅用「分 to (下一时)」：

| No. | Q | A | 规律 |
|-----|---|---|------|
| 1 | What's the time? | It's **one o'clock**.（1:00） | 整点 |
| 2 | What's the time? | It's **five past one**.（1:05） | 分≤30 → past |
| 3 | What's the time? | It's **ten past one**.（1:10） | past |
| 4 | What's the time? | It's **a quarter past one**.（1:15） | quarter = 一刻 |
| 5 | What's the time? | It's **twenty past one**.（1:20） | past |
| 6 | What's the time? | It's **twenty-five past one**.（1:25） | past |
| 7 | What's the time? | It's **half past one**.（1:30） | half = 半 |
| 8 | What's the time? | It's **twenty-five to two**.（1:35） | 分>30 → to (下一时) |
| 9 | What's the time? | It's **twenty to two**.（1:40） | to |
| 10 | What's the time? | It's **a quarter to two**.（1:45） | quarter to |
| 11 | What's the time? | It's **ten to two**.（1:50） | to |
| 12 | What's the time? | It's **five to two**.（1:55） | to |

**口诀**：分 ≤ 30 → **分** past **时**；分 > 30 → **(60−分)** to **(时+1)**。quarter 替 15 分，half 替 30 分。整点用 **时 o'clock**。

### Part 2 — When were you at ...? 你什么时候在……？

下面是本课第二块 **10 组日期＋地点操练**——每组配同一个问答模板。用 **When was / were ... at ...?** 提问，用 **... was / were at ... on ...** 回答。注意主语不同时 be 动词用 **was**（I / he / she / it）还是 **were**（you / we / they）：

| No. | 日期 | 地点 | 人物 | Q | A |
|-----|------|------|------|---|---|
| 1 | Sunday, January 1st | church | you | When **were** you at church? | I **was** at church **on** Sunday. |
| 2 | Monday, February 2nd | school | you | When **were** you at school? | I **was** at school **on** Monday. |
| 3 | Tuesday, March 3rd | the office | Miss Grey | When **was** Miss Grey at the office? | She **was** at the office **on** Tuesday. |
| 4 | Wednesday, April 4th | the butcher's | Mrs. Jones | When **was** Mrs. Jones at the butcher's? | She **was** at the butcher's **on** Wednesday. |
| 5 | Thursday, May 5th | the hairdresser's | Tom | When **was** Tom at the hairdresser's? | He **was** at the hairdresser's **on** Thursday. |
| 6 | Friday, June 6th | the baker's | Mary | When **was** Mary at the baker's? | She **was** at the baker's **on** Friday. |
| 7 | Saturday, July 7th | the dairy | you | When **were** you at the dairy? | I **was** at the dairy **on** Saturday. |
| 8 | Sunday, August 8th | home | you | When **were** you at home? | I **was** at home **on** Sunday. |
| 9 | Monday, September 9th | the grocer's | Mrs. Williams | When **was** Mrs. Williams at the grocer's? | She **was** at the grocer's **on** Monday. |
| 10 | Tuesday, October 10th | the greengrocer's | Mrs. Jones | When **was** Mrs. Jones at the greengrocer's? | She **was** at the greengrocer's **on** Tuesday. |

**这些日期里藏着一个细节**：1月1日 (Jan 1st)、2月2日 (Feb 2nd)、3月3日 (Mar 3rd) … 一直到 10月10日 (Oct 10th)——月份和日期数字完全一样，帮你同时记住 ordinal numbers（序数词）1st–10th。

## 课文注释 Notes on the text

<Callout type="info" title="8 个值得停下来嚼一嚼的点">
  1. **What's the time?**（本课第一块核心问句）——「几点钟了？」等于 *What time is it?*。回答永远用 **It's + 时间**。**整点**用 *时 + o'clock*（*It's one o'clock.*）；**分钟 ≤ 30** 用 *分 + past + 时*（*It's five past one.* = 一点过五分）；**分钟 > 30** 用 *(60−分) + to + (时+1)*（*It's twenty-five to two.* = 差二十五分到两点，即一点三十五分）。特殊分钟用 **quarter**（一刻，15 分）和 **half**（半，30 分）。

  2. **was / were**（本课第二块核心动词）——be 动词的**过去式**。主语 I / he / she / it 用 **was**；主语 you / we / they 用 **were**。本课所有问答都在练这个——*I was at church.* / *She was at the office.* / *He was at the hairdresser's.* 否定式：*was not (wasn't) / were not (weren't)*。疑问式：把 was / were 提到主语前——*Was he at school? → Yes, he was. / No, he wasn't.* → *When were you at the dairy?*（特殊疑问句：疑问词 + was/were + 主语 + …?）

  3. **at + 地点**——「在（某地）」。本课大量地点前都用 **at**：*at church / at school / at the office / at the butcher's / at home*。**at 后面是地点，on 后面是日期**——这两个介词分工明确。对比：*I was **at** the baker's **on** Friday.*（先地点、后时间）

  4. **on + 星期 / 日期**——「在星期几 / 在几月几日」。*on Sunday / on Monday / on Friday.* 记住了：具体某一天前面用 **on**。月份也同理：*on January 1st / on May 5th*。对比：at 后面接具体时刻（*at three o'clock*），in 后面接月份/年份（*in January / in 2025*）。

  5. **church / school / home 前面为什么不加 the？**（本课 No.1-2, 8）——英语里，当你说到某个场所并强调它**本身的功能**（去教堂做礼拜、去学校上学、在家里待着），这些词前面**不加冠词**。*at church* = 在教堂（做礼拜）；*at school* = 在学校（上学）；*at home* = 在家。但如果强调的是「建筑物本身」或「某个具体的场所」则要加 the：*at the office*（办公室——不是去办公室的功能，而是那个具体的地方）。

  6. **店铺名称的 's 结构**（本课 No.4-7, 9-10）——*the butcher's / the hairdresser's / the baker's / the dairy / the grocer's / the greengrocer's*。这些是**名词所有格的省略形式**，全称是 *the butcher's shop / the baker's shop* 等。口语里只说 's 就够了。注意：**dairy**（乳品店）是完整名词，不是 's 省略，所以不加 's。这些店铺前面通通要加 **the**（*at the butcher's*, *at the grocer's*）。

  7. **序数词 1st–10th**（本课日期栏）——1月1日到10月10日正好对应 first 到 tenth。拼写：*first (1st) / second (2nd) / third (3rd) / fourth (4th) / fifth (5th) / sixth (6th) / seventh (7th) / eighth (8th) / ninth (9th) / tenth (10th)*。日期读法：*January the first* 或 *January first*；写作 *January 1st*。

  8. **country**（来自 Lesson 67 生词，在本课 Written Exercise 中复现）——*in the country* = 在乡下（不是「在国家」）。注意前面有 **the**。对比 Lesson 67 中 Mrs. Johnson 说的 *We're going to spend three days in the country.* ——正是本课练习 *My daughter was in the country on Friday.* 的来源。
</Callout>

## 3. 关键句型：时间表达 + was/were + at/on 双轨

<Callout type="tip" title="一分钟回顾：时间表达的铁律 + was/were 的开关">
  本课其实在练**两套表达**。把它们拆开、吃透，英语的「时间」和「过去地点」就通了。

  ### 第一轨：几点钟？—— past / to 分界线

  英语时间表达只有三条规则：

  | 分钟位置 | 结构 | 例 |
  |----------|------|-----|
  | 整点 | *时 + o'clock* | *one o'clock* |
  | 分 ≤ 30 | *分 + past + 时* | *twenty past one*（1:20）|
  | 分 > 30 | *(60−分) + to + (时+1)* | *twenty to two*（1:40）|

  **特例**：15 分钟用 **a quarter**（不是 fifteen），30 分钟用 **half**（不是 thirty）。

  ### ⚠️ 时间表达 4 个最容易踩的雷区

  1. **half past 后不加 o'clock**：*half past one* ✓ / *half past one o'clock* ✗
  2. **quarter 前面必有 a**：*a quarter past one* ✓ / *quarter past one* ✗（口语偶尔省 a，但书写必须加）
  3. **to 后面是「下一时」，不是「当前时」**：1:40 = *twenty **to two*** ✓ / *twenty to one* ✗（那样就是 12:40）
  4. **不要混用 past 和 to**：超过 30 分就必须换 to，不能说 *thirty-five past one*

  ### 第二轨：什么时候在哪儿？—— was/were + at + on

  | 句子成分 | 用什么 | 例 |
  |----------|--------|-----|
  | 主语 I/he/she/it | **was** | *He was at school.* |
  | 主语 you/we/they | **were** | *You were at church.* |
  | 地点 | **at + 地点** | *at the butcher's / at home / at school* |
  | 日期/星期 | **on + 日期/星期** | *on Sunday / on January 1st* |

  问过去的事，把 was / were 提到主语前面：*He was at school.* → *Was he at school?* → *When was he at school?*
</Callout>

## 4. 难点：at church / at school / at home 不加 the 的秘密

<Callout type="info" title="什么时候不加 the">
  本课第二块操练里有一个反复出现、但很容易被忽略的细节——No.1 *at church*、No.2 *at school*、No.8 *at home* 前面都**没加 the**；但 No.3 *at **the** office*、No.4 *at **the** butcher's* 以及所有店铺前面又都**加了 the**。这不是随意省略。

  英语里有一条规则：当你提到一个场所，并且强调的是它**本身的功能**（去那里干它设计好的事），**不加冠词**：
  - *at church* = 在教堂（去做礼拜）
  - *at school* = 在学校（去上学）
  - *at home* = 在家（在家待着）
  - *in bed* = 在床上（睡觉/休息）
  - *at work* = 在上班

  当你强调的是**建筑物本身**或**某个具体的地点**，**要加 the**：
  - *at **the** office*（办公室——强调的是那个具体工作场所）
  - *at **the** butcher's*（肉店——那个具体的店铺）
  - *at **the** hairdresser's / baker's / grocer's*（同理，都是具体店铺）

  这条规则在 Written Exercise A 里被专门练到——**7 个句子，3 个不加 the（church / school / home），4 个加 the（office / butcher's / grocer's / country）**。学完这个练习，你就吃透了英语里最基础的「零冠词」用法。
</Callout>

## 10. 拓展练习 Going further

<Callout type="tip" title="自学者建议">
  - **影子跟读 (shadowing)**：用第 1 节的三个 SayIt 按钮各跟读 3-5 遍。重点练**时间的节奏**——*a quarter past one* 要把 quarter 读准（/ˈkwɔːtə/，两个音节），*half past one* 要读流畅；**was/were 的弱读**——在自然语流中 *was* 常弱读为 /wəz/，*were* 常弱读为 /wə/；以及 **on + 星期** 的粘连——*on Sunday* 读得像 *onSunday*一样顺。
  - **看图说话**：第一块操练的终极训练——不看文字，只盯着 12 幅钟面（在心里想象），每幅先问 *What's the time?*，再自己回答。第二块——看日期＋地点的配图，自己用 *When was/were ... at ...?* 提问、再用 *... was/were at ... on ...* 回答。这才是「句型操练课」的精髓——眼睛看图、嘴巴出句，不靠文字提示。
  - **造句迁移**：用今天学的 5 个结构造你自己的句子——
    1. **时间 past/to**：找个钟或手表，把每个整点、每个 quarter、每个 half 都说一遍——*It's twenty past three. It's a quarter to seven. It's half past twelve.*
    2. **was/were + at + 地点 + on + 星期**：回顾过去一周，你每天在哪儿？—— *I was at work on Monday. I was at the supermarket on Wednesday. I was at home on Sunday.*
    3. **When was/were ...?** 问家人/朋友：*When was Mum at the hairdresser's? — She was at the hairdresser's on Saturday. When were you at the cinema? — I was at the cinema on Friday.*
    4. **不加 the 的场所**：练习说 *at school / at church / at home / at work / in bed*——这些都是「功能用法」，不能加 the。
    5. **序数词 1st–10th**：把每个月的 1 号到 10 号对应的英文序数词念一遍，再反过来看着 1st–10th 说出全称——*first, second, third, fourth, fifth, sixth, seventh, eighth, ninth, tenth*。
  - **语法意识：两个「开关」**——① 时间表达的 **past / to 开关**：分钟 ≤ 30 → past，分钟 > 30 → to + 下一时。② be 动词过去式的 **was / were 开关**：I/he/she/it → was，you/we/they → were。每次开口说过去的事之前，先在脑子里过一下这两个开关——时间是要用 past 还是 to？be 是要用 was 还是 were？开关拨对了，句子就立住了。
  - **Lesson 67 联动**：把 Lesson 67 课文里每个 *was / were* 标出来——*Were you at the butcher's? / Yes, I was. / No, I wasn't. / I was at the greengrocer's. / Was he absent from school? / Yes, he was. / He was absent on Monday, Tuesday, Wednesday and Thursday.* 全部都是本课第二块操练的「实战版」——67 课在真实的对话中用 was/were，68 课把 was/were 拆出来系统操练。两条线一起吃透，过去时态 be 动词就过关了。
  - **实际场景**：你能用英文说出以下时间吗？（1）你每天早上几点起床？（2）你昨天下午三点在哪儿？（3）你上个星期六去了什么地方？（4）用 *was / were + at + on* 的结构，把「我 （人）星期 （几）在 （地方）」这个模版填满 7 天。
</Callout>
