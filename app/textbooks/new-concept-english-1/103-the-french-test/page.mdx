## The Text 课文

**GARY:** **How was the exam**, Richard?

**RICHARD:** **Not too bad.** I think **I passed in** English and Mathematics. The questions were **very easy**. **How about you**, Gary?

**GARY:** The English and Maths papers weren't **easy enough for me**. I hope **I haven't failed.**

**RICHARD:** I think **I failed the French paper**. I could **answer** sixteen of the **questions**. They were very easy. But I couldn't answer **the rest**. They were **too difficult for me**.

**GARY:** **French tests are awful, aren't they?**

**RICHARD:** **I hate them.** I'm sure **I've got a low mark.**

**GARY:** Oh, **cheer up!** Perhaps **we didn't do too badly.** The guy **next to me** wrote his name **at the top of** the paper.

**RICHARD:** **Yes?**

**GARY:** Then he sat there and looked at it **for three hours**! He didn't write a word!

## 课文注释 Notes on the text

<Callout type="info" title="6 个值得停下来嚼一嚼的点">
  1. **I passed in English and Mathematics.** (l.2) — “我英语和数学及格了。”注意：说“通过某个考试”直接用 **pass + the exam**；说“通过某一科目”则用 **pass in + 科目**，如 *pass in English*、*pass in French*。这里的 **pass** 是不及物用法，后面跟介词 in。

  2. **the rest** (l.6) — “其余的（题目）、剩余部分。”课文中 Richard 说 *I couldn't answer the rest.* = “我答不出剩下的题。”**rest** 作“剩余部分”讲时，前面要加定冠词 **the**：*the rest of the ...*。例：*Three of us will go; the rest are staying.*（我们三个人去，其余的留下。）

  3. **at the top of** (l.9) — “在……顶端。”Gary 说那个同学 *wrote his name at the top of the paper*（在试卷顶端写了名字）。反义词组为 **at the bottom of**（在……底部/末端）。例：*Sign your name at the bottom of the page.*（在页面底部签名。）注意这里的 **paper** 不是“纸”，而是可数名词“考卷 / 试卷”——*English and Maths papers* = 英语和数学试卷。

  4. **next to** (l.9) — “邻近、贴近。”*The guy next to me* = 坐在我旁边的那个人。**next to** 既可以指空间上的“挨着”（*sit next to sb.*），也可以引申为“仅次于”（*next to impossible* = 几乎不可能）。

  5. **cheer up** (l.9) — “振作起来、别灰心。”Gary 看到 Richard 闷闷不乐，用 **cheer up** 来安慰他。这是一个常用的安慰语，口语里也说 *Come on!*（加油！）。本课另一常用安慰口吻是 *Not too bad.*（不算太坏 / 还行。）

  6. **French tests are awful, aren't they?** (l.7) — “法语考试太可怕了，你说呢？”这是个**反意疑问句 (tag question)**：前面是肯定句，后面就挂一个否定式的短问句。中文里类似“……是吧？”/“……对不对？”，用来确认或引发对方回应。回答时注意：**事实是肯定的就说 Yes，事实是否定的就说 No**，不要被中文“对，我不……”套住。例：*You like coffee, don't you?* — *Yes, I do.* / *No, I don't.*
</Callout>

## 3. 关键语法：too 与 enough — “太”与“够”

<Callout type="tip" title="一分钟回顾：一对反义词，用对立刻让英语变自然">
  本课对话里反复出现两个词——**too** 和 **enough**——它们是一对“程度反义词”，都用来评价“够不够”，但方向相反：

  | 说法 | 含义 | 位置 | 课文原句 |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | **too + 形容词** | 太……（否定：超过） | too 放在形容词**前面** | *They were **too difficult** for me.* |
  | **形容词 + enough** | 足够……（肯定：达标） | enough 放在形容词**后面** | *The papers weren't **easy enough** for me.* |
  | **enough + 名词** | 足够的……（做定语） | enough 放在名词**前面** | *I don't have **enough time**.* |

  **一句话记住：too 在前表“过”、enough 在后表“够”。**

  ### too... to... vs. enough... to...（本课→第 104 课的核心对比）

  | 结构 | 含义 | 例句 |
  |---|---|---|
  | **too + adj. + to do** | 太……而不能…… | *He is too tired to study.*（他太累了，学不了。） |
  | **adj. + enough + to do** | 足够……可以…… | *He is strong enough to lift it.*（他够强壮，举得动。） |

  ### ⚠️ 4 个本课最容易踩的雷区

  1. **enough 放形容词后面，绝不是前面**：*easy enough* ✓ / *enough easy* ✗。但修饰名词时 enough 放前面：*enough money* ✓ / *money enough* 罕见且偏旧。
  2. **too 不等于 very**：*The questions were very easy.* = 很容易（客观）。*The questions were too easy.* = 太容易了（太容易反倒没挑战，含否定）。
  3. **too + adj. + for sb.**：*too difficult for me* ✓ / *too difficult to me* ✗——介词用 **for** 不是 to。
  4. **enough 后的 to 不能丢**：*not easy enough to pass* ✓ / *not easy enough pass* ✗。
</Callout>

## 8. 拓展练习 Going further

<Callout type="tip" title="自学者建议">
  - **影子跟读 (shadowing)**：用第 1 节的三个 SayIt 按钮各跟读 3-5 遍。重点模仿 *How was the exam?* 的自然语调、*French tests are awful, aren't they?* 的反意疑问上扬，以及 Gary 讲“别人更惨”那段——*He didn't write a word!* 要把那种惊讶又好笑的口吻读出来。
  - **复述 (retell)**：合上课文，用 1 分钟把这段“考后吐槽”讲一遍。**三步走**：两人互相问考得怎样（英语数学还行）→ Richard 担心法语挂了（只答出 16 题）→ Gary 讲冷笑话安慰（旁边的人只写名字发呆三小时）。
  - **造句迁移**：用今天学的 5 个结构造你自己的句子——
    1. *主语 + too + 形容词 + for sb.* —— *The bag is too heavy for the little boy.*
    2. *主语 + (not) + 形容词 + enough + for sb.* —— *This room isn't big enough for five people.*
    3. *too ... to ...*（太……而不能……）—— *She is too young to drive a car.*
    4. *... enough ... to ...*（够……可以……）—— *He is old enough to go to school.*
    5. *反意疑问句（肯定 + 否定）* —— *It's a nice day, isn't it?*
  - **too 与 enough 转换**：拿本课生词造句，把 *too* 句换成 *not ... enough* 句——
    - too difficult → not easy enough: *The French paper was too difficult. = The French paper wasn't easy enough.*
    - too low → not high enough: *My mark is too low. = My mark isn't high enough.*
  - **考试口语串烧**：把本课“考试用语”串一遍：*take an exam → pass in English → the English paper → answer the questions → get a low mark → fail the exam → cheer up!* ——你已经有了一套完整的“考后对话工具箱”。
</Callout>
