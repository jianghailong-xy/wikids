  第 139 课里，格雷厄姆·特纳拨通电话，开口就问 *Is that you, John?*，结果闹了个大乌龙——电话那头确实是一位 John Smith，但不是他以为的那位「海外工程公司的工程师约翰·史密斯」，而是一位 **修电话线的电话工程师约翰·史密斯**！整段对话里，特纳说了不少话——叫对方带话给玛丽、解释自己今晚要加班、问玛丽要不要帮忙——这些内容在这节课都要被转成「他想要知道……」「他问……」「他说……」的形式。这就是本课的核心语法：**宾语从句（object clause）**——把一个问句嵌进另一句话里，用 **if**（是否）、**why**（为什么）、**what**（什么）、**when**（什么时候）来引导。

  **本课目标：学会把直接问句转换成「He wants to know if/why/what/when ...」的间接形式。** 带着这个目标看句型操练、做书面练习。
</Callout>

## The Patterns 句型操练

本课是句型操练课。下面三组操练，每组先给一句 **直接问句**，再把它变成 **「他想知道……」（He wants to know ...）** 的宾语从句。请跟着录音逐句跟读，重点体会 **if / why / what / when** 的用法区别和宾语从句的 **陈述语序（主语 + 谓语，不倒装）**。

### 第一组：if 还是 why？—— 是否 vs 为什么

<div className="grid grid-cols-1 md:grid-cols-2 gap-4 not-prose">

<div>

**画面 1**

— Are you tired?（你累了吗？）

— What does he want to know?（他想知道什么？）

— **He wants to know if you are tired.**（他想知道你是否累了。）

</div>

<div>

**画面 2**

— Why are you tired?（你为什么累？）

— What does he want to know?（他想知道什么？）

— **He wants to know why you are tired.**（他想知道你为什么累。）

</div>

</div>

<Callout type="tip" title="一分钟对比">
  - **if** = 是否：原问句是 yes/no 问句（Are you tired?），嵌进宾语从句后变成 *He wants to know **if** you are tired.*
  - **why** = 为什么：原问句是 wh- 问句（Why are you tired?），嵌进后变成 *He wants to know **why** you are tired.*
  - ⚠️ 关键：宾语从句里 **语序不再是疑问倒装**，而是 **主语 + 谓语** 的陈述语序：*you are*（不是 *are you*）。
</Callout>

### 第二组：if 还是 what？—— 是否 vs 什么

<div className="grid grid-cols-1 md:grid-cols-2 gap-4 not-prose">

<div>

**画面 3**

— Are you reading?（你在看书吗？）

— What does he want to know?（他想知道什么？）

— **He wants to know if you are reading.**（他想知道你是否在看书。）

</div>

<div>

**画面 4**

— What are you reading?（你在看什么？）

— What does he want to know?（他想知道什么？）

— **He wants to know what you are reading.**（他想知道你在看什么。）

</div>

</div>

<Callout type="tip" title="一分钟对比">
  - **if** = 是否：Yes/No 问句 *Are you reading?* → 宾语从句 *He wants to know **if** you are reading.*
  - **what** = 什么：wh- 问句 *What are you reading?* → 宾语从句 *He wants to know **what** you are reading.*
  - ⚠️ *what* 在从句中作宾语（*you are reading what*），语序仍是「主 + 谓 + 宾」，不是疑问语序。
</Callout>

### 第三组：if 还是 when？—— 是否 vs 什么时候

<div className="grid grid-cols-1 md:grid-cols-2 gap-4 not-prose">

<div>

**画面 5**

— Does Tom always do his homework?（汤姆总是做作业吗？）

— What does he want to know?（他想知道什么？）

— **He wants to know if Tom always does his homework.**（他想知道汤姆是否总是做作业。）

</div>

<div>

**画面 6**

— When does Tom do his homework?（汤姆什么时候做作业？）

— What does he want to know?（他想知道什么？）

— **He wants to know when Tom does his homework.**（他想知道汤姆什么时候做作业。）

</div>

</div>

<Callout type="tip" title="一分钟对比">
  - **if** = 是否：第三人称单数的 does 问句 *Does Tom always do ...?* → 宾语从句中 does 消失，动词恢复原形：*He wants to know **if** Tom always **does** his homework.*
  - **when** = 什么时候：*When does Tom do ...?* → *He wants to know **when** Tom **does** his homework.*
  - ⚠️ do/does/did 在宾语从句中**不会出现**——它们只在直接问句里当助动词，转间接后就直接用动词本身。
</Callout>

## 课文注释 Notes on the text

<Callout type="info" title="5 个值得停下来嚼一嚼的点">
  1. **He wants to know if you are tired.** — 宾语从句中的 **if** = 是否，引导原来是一个 yes/no 问句。注意从句的语序：**you are**（主语 + 谓语），不是 *are you*。初学者最容易犯的错就是把从句仍写成疑问语序：~~*He wants to know if are you tired.*~~ ✗

  2. **He wants to know why you are tired.** — **why** = 为什么。原问句是 *Why are you tired?*，转成宾语从句后，why 保留在从句开头，但后面的语序从倒装 **are you** 恢复为陈述 **you are**。同类：*I don't know when I'll finish.*（第 139 课）、*My wife wants to know if Mary needs any help.*（第 139 课）

  3. **He wants to know what you are reading.** — **what** 在从句里作宾语（你在读 → 什么 = *you are reading what*）。what 提前到从句开头，语序保持「主语 + 谓语 + ...」。同类：*I don't know what you're talking about.*（第 139 课）

  4. **He wants to know when Tom does his homework.** — 原问句 *When **does** Tom **do** his homework?*，转间接后 **does 消失**，动词 do 恢复为 **does**（主语 Tom 是第三人称单数）。这是整个第 139–140 课最容易丢分的细节：do/does/did 是直接问句的「脚手架」，转成宾语从句就拆掉，动词自己上。

  5. **Tell me if / why / what / when ...** — 书面练习里反复出现的替代句型。**Tell me** 后面同样是宾语从句，规则完全一样：*Tell me why you are tired. / Tell me what you are reading. / Tell me when Tom went to bed.* 比 *He wants to know* 更口语、更直接，日常用来请对方告诉你一件事。
</Callout>

## 3. 关键句型：宾语从句 Object clauses with if / why / what / when

<Callout type="tip" title="一分钟回顾：把问句嵌进句子里，语序要从「问」变「陈」">
  本课的核心语法——**宾语从句（object clause）**——是把你想要表达的「想知道 / 问」的内容用一个完整句子来充当宾语。引导词分两类：

  | 原问句类型 | 引导词 | 例句 |
  |---|---|---|
  | Yes/No 问句 | **if**（是否） | *He wants to know **if** you are tired.* |
  | Wh- 问句（why） | **why**（为什么） | *He wants to know **why** you are tired.* |
  | Wh- 问句（what） | **what**（什么） | *He wants to know **what** you are reading.* |
  | Wh- 问句（when） | **when**（什么时侯） | *He wants to know **when** Tom does his homework.* |

  ### ⚠️ 3 个本课最容易踩的雷区

  1. **宾语从句用陈述语序，不颠倒主谓**：*He wants to know if **you are** tired.* ✓ / ~~*He wants to know if **are you** tired.*~~ ✗——这是 No.1 高频错误：把从句写成直接问句的倒装语序。

  2. **do / does / did 在从句里消失**：直接问句 *Does Tom always do his homework?* 转成从句后，does 这个「脚手架」拆除，动词恢复人称变位：*He wants to know if Tom always **does** his homework.*。同样，*When does Tom do his homework?* → *He wants to know when Tom **does** his homework.*——does 没有消失，而是从助动词位置移到了动词位置（主语第三人称单数，do → does）。

  3. **if 还是 whether？**：在宾语从句中 if = whether，都表「是否」。日常口语里 if 更常见；正式文体偏 whether。二者在 *He wants to know if/whether ...* 中可以互换。

  本课学过的「想知道……」「告诉……」句式都可以接宾语从句：
  - *He wants to know if/why/what/when ...*
  - *Tell me if/why/what/when ...*
  - *I don't know if/why/what/when ...*
  - *My wife wants to know if ...*（第 139 课）
</Callout>

## 8. 摘要写作 Summary writing

把第 139 课那通电话的「信息」用宾语从句重述一遍——模仿 *He wants to know ... / He said that ... / He doesn't know ...* 的间接形式：

  *Answer these questions in not more than 60 words.* 回答下列问题，把答案串成 **一个段落**，总词数不超过 60：

  1. Who did Graham Turner telephone?
  2. Did he speak to the right John Smith?
  3. What did he tell John to do?（Tell Mary ...）
  4. Why did he say they would be late?
  5. What doesn't Graham know?
  6. What does his wife want to know?

## 9. 拓展练习 Going further

<Callout type="tip" title="自学者建议">
  - **影子跟读 (shadowing)**：用第 1 节三组句型操练的 SayIt 各跟读 3-5 遍。重点练**宾语从句的语调节奏**——主句 *He wants to know* 略平，从句 *if/why/what/when ...* 自然接上，不要在每个词之间停顿。
  - **句型替换操练**：拿出第 139 课对话，把格雷厄姆说过的每一句相关内容都「转述」一遍：
    - *Graham said (that) they would be late for dinner.*
    - *Graham told John (that) Mary had invited Charlotte and him to dinner.*
    - *Graham said (that) he would be at John's house at six o'clock.*
    - *Graham said (that) the boss wanted him to do some extra work.*
    - *Graham said (that) he didn't know when he would finish.*
    - *Graham said (that) his wife wanted to know if Mary needed any help.*
  - **造句迁移**：用今天学的 4 个引导词各造 3 句你自己的宾语从句——
    1. **if**：*I want to know if it will rain tomorrow. / Can you tell me if the shop is open? / She asked if I was hungry.*
    2. **why**：*I don't know why he is late. / Tell me why you didn't call. / He wants to know why she quit her job.*
    3. **what**：*I don't understand what you mean. / Tell me what you bought. / She wants to know what he said.*
    4. **when**：*Do you know when the train arrives? / I want to know when you'll be back. / He asked when the meeting would end.*
  - **口语转换**：把「直接问句」快速转成「间接问句」——找个搭档或自言自语：
    - A: *Are you tired?* → B: *He wants to know if I'm tired.*
    - A: *What are you reading?* → B: *He wants to know what I'm reading.*
    - A: *When do you go to bed?* → B: *He wants to know when I go to bed.*
  - **易错警醒**：每次写宾语从句前，默念三条铁律——① 语序陈述不颠倒 ② do/does/did 消失 ③ if = 是否（不跟 or not 连用也可以）。把这三条练成肌肉记忆，今后的宾语从句就不会写错了。
</Callout>
