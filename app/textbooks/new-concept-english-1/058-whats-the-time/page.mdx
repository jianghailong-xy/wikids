<Callout type="info" title="Listen first 听前思考">
  上节课（Lesson 57）我们看到了索耶一家人 **很不平常的一天（An unusual day）**——从早晨 8 点到晚上 9 点，家里每个人都在做着和平时**完全不一样**的事：孩子们没坐车，走路上学；妈妈没呆在家，去了商店；爸爸没看报，读起了一本有趣的书。每句话里都有一条清晰的对比线：**usually + 一般现在时**（平时）↔ **today/this morning + 现在进行时**（今天/此刻）。

  本课是 **句型操练课**：10 幅插图，5 组对比——每组包含两张图，左边画着「平时 (usually)」做的事，右边画着「今天 (today)」做的事。你的任务就是看着左边的图说出**一般现在时的习惯句**，再看着右边的图说出**现在进行时的「反常」句**。核心句型：**What's the time?**（几点钟？）+ **What do/does ... usually do?**（……通常做什么？）+ **What is/are ... doing today?**（……今天在做什么？）。

  *Listen to the tape and answer the questions.* **听录音，然后回答问题。** 看到每对插图，先看钟面问自己几点了（*What's the time?*），再看左边图用 *usually* 描述习惯，最后看右边图用 *today/now* 描述正在发生的动作。特别注意：**问句里 Does/Is/Are 已经包揽了变化，后面的动词要恢复原形或保持 -ing 形式。**
</Callout>

## The Pattern Drills 句型操练

下面是本课 **10 幅替换操练插图**，组成 5 组「平时 vs 今天」对比。每组由 **时间（几点钟）+ 平时动词** 和 **今天动词** 组成提示，你先看钟面说出时间，再看图说出两句完整对比句。朗读时注意：**usually** 放主语后、动词前；**现在进行时 = be (am/is/are) + -ing**。

### 第 1 组 — 8 点钟 · 上学 vs 步行

| 图号 | 时间 | 平时 (Simple Present) | 今天 (Present Continuous) |
|------|------|----------------------|--------------------------|
| 13th | 8:00 | They usually **go to school by car** every day. | but today, they **are going to school on foot**. |
| 14th | 7:00 | He usually **shaves** at seven o'clock every day. | but today, he **is shaving** at eight o'clock. |

### 第 2 组 — 10 点钟 · 喝茶 vs 喝咖啡 / 在家 vs 去商店

| 图号 | 时间 | 平时 (Simple Present) | 今天 (Present Continuous) |
|------|------|----------------------|--------------------------|
| 15th | 10:00 | She usually **drinks tea** in the morning. | but this morning, she **is drinking coffee**. |
| 16th | 10:00 | Mrs. Sawyer usually **stays at home** in the morning. | but this morning, she **is going to the shops**. |

### 第 3 组 — 4 点钟 · 花园玩 vs 河里游泳

| 图号 | 时间 | 平时 (Simple Present) | 今天 (Present Continuous) |
|------|------|----------------------|--------------------------|
| 17th | 4:00 | Mrs. Sawyer usually **drinks tea in the living room** in the afternoon. | but this afternoon, she **is drinking tea in the garden**. |

### 第 4 组 — 6 点钟 · 做作业 vs 花园玩

| 图号 | 时间 | 平时 (Simple Present) | 今天 (Present Continuous) |
|------|------|----------------------|--------------------------|
| 19th | 6:00 | The children usually **do their homework** in the evening. | but this evening, they **are not doing their homework**. At the moment, they **are playing in the garden**. |
| 20th | 6:00 | I usually **cook a meal** in the evening. | but this evening, I **am reading a book**. |

### 第 5 组 — 9 点钟 · 看报 vs 读书 / 看电视 vs 听音响

| 图号 | 时间 | 平时 (Simple Present) | 今天 (Present Continuous) |

| 21st | 9:00 | Mr. Sawyer usually **reads his newspaper** at night. | but tonight, he **is reading an interesting book**. |

## 句型讲解 Notes on the pattern

<Callout type="info" title="8 个值得停下来嚼一嚼的点">
  1. **一般现在时 vs. 现在进行时：两条时间线** — 这是本课全 10 幅插图的语法核心。**一般现在时** 描述「习惯、常规、反复发生的事」——时间状语是 *every day, usually, in the morning* 等，动词用原形 / 三单 -s。**现在进行时** 描述「此刻、今天正在发生的事」——时间标志是 *today, this morning, this afternoon, this evening, tonight, at the moment*，结构是 **be (am/is/are) + 动词-ing**。两条线的对照：*She usually **drinks** tea in the morning. → This morning she **is drinking** coffee.* —— 同一个主语、同一个时间概念，时态一变，意思就从一个「习惯」变成一个「此刻正在发生的动作」。

  2. **三单问句：Does ... do?** — 第三人称单数的疑问句用 **Does + 主语 + 动词原形**：*What **does** she usually **do** in the morning?*（注意 does 后面的 do 是实义动词「做」）。规则：**does 已经把 -s 抢走了，动词恢复原形**。回答：*She usually drinks tea.* ✓（不是 *She usually drink tea.* 也不是 *She usually does drink tea.*）。同类对比：*What **do** they usually **do**?*（复数主语用 do...do，第一个 do 是助动词，第二个 do 是实义动词「做」。）

  3. **进行时问句：Is/Are ... doing?** — 现在进行时问句把 **be 动词提到主语前面**：***Is** she **drinking** coffee?* / ***Are** they **going** to school on foot?* 回答用 yes/no：*Yes, she is. / No, they aren't.* 问「正在做什么」用 **What is/are ... doing?**：*What **is** she **doing** this morning?*（is 后面的 doing 是动词 do 的 -ing 形式。）

  4. **频率副词 usually 的位置** — *usually*（通常）放在 **主语之后、行为动词之前**：*She **usually** drinks tea.* ✓ *She drinks **usually** tea.* ✗。如果谓语是 be 动词，则放在 be 动词之后：*She **is usually** at home.* 本课所有 10 组操练都属于行为动词类，记住「主 + usually + 谓」即可。

  5. **时间状语的位置** — 本课出现了多种时间短语：① **at + 钟点**（at seven o'clock / at eight o'clock）② **in the + 时间段**（in the morning / in the afternoon / in the evening）③ **at night**（在夜里，注意介词用 at）④ **every day**（每天）。这些时间状语通常放在**句末**：*He usually shaves **at seven o'clock**.* 也可放到句首：***Every day** they go to school by car.* —— 但不能插在主语和动词之间：*They every day go to school by car.* ✗

  6. **交通方式：by car vs. on foot** — 表示「乘坐……交通工具」用 **by + 名词**（名词前不加冠词、保持原形）：*by car*（坐车）、*by bus*（坐公共汽车）、*by bike*（骑自行车）。表示「步行」用 **on foot**（注意是 on 不是 by）：*go to school on foot*。比较：*take a bus / take a car* 也能表示乘坐交通工具，但前面要加冠词。

  7. **否定进行时：is/are not + -ing** — 现在进行时的否定形式在 be 动词后加 **not**，动词保持 -ing：*They **are not doing** their homework.* *He **is not reading** his newspaper.* 口语中常缩写为 isn't / aren't。对比：一般现在时的否定用 **don't / doesn't + 动词原形**：*They **don't do** their homework.*（他们平时不做作业）↔ *They **are not doing** their homework.*（他们此刻没在做作业）。两套否定、两套时间含义，不能混用。

  8. **at the moment = now** — *at the moment*（此刻）是现在进行时的标志词，相当于 *now*。在 6 点钟和 9 点钟的段中，都是先用 *this evening / tonight* 给出大时间范围，再用 *at the moment* 把镜头拉近到「说话的这一刻」：*At the moment, they are playing in the garden.* —— 这里的画面感是「他们不是在做作业……看，此刻他们正在花园里玩呢」。
</Callout>

## 3. 关键句型：一般现在时 vs. 现在进行时 Simple present vs. Present continuous

<Callout type="tip" title="一分钟回顾：英语里怎样表达「平时 vs 今天」">
  本课和 Lesson 57 一起，构成了 NCE1 中 **一般现在时 ↔ 现在进行时对比** 的核心单元。两组时态共用一个主语和同一个时间概念，却讲出两个完全不同的故事：

  | | 一般现在时 (Simple Present) | 现在进行时 (Present Continuous) |
  |---|---|---|
  | **表达什么** | 习惯、常规、反复发生的事 | 此刻、今天正在发生的事 |
  | **时间标志** | every day, usually, in the morning, at night | today, this morning, this afternoon, this evening, tonight, at the moment |
  | **结构** | 主语 + 动词（三单 + -s） | 主语 + am/is/are + 动词-ing |
  | **否定** | don't / doesn't + 动词原形 | am/is/are + not + 动词-ing |
  | **疑问** | Do/Does + 主语 + 动词原形? | Am/Is/Are + 主语 + 动词-ing? |
  | **例（she）** | She usually **drinks** tea. | She **is drinking** coffee this morning. |
  | **例（they）** | They **play** in the garden. | They **are swimming** in the river. |
  | **例（I）** | I **cook** a meal every evening. | I **am reading** a book this evening. |

  ### ⚠️ 5 个本课最容易踩的雷区

  1. **usually + 进行时？绝对不行！**：*She usually is drinking tea.* ✗ → *She usually **drinks** tea.* ✓ —— usually 是习惯标志，必须配一般现在时。
  2. **today + 一般现在时？看情况：** *Today they **go** to school on foot.* 语法上能成立（陈述一个事实），但本课强调「此刻正进行的动作」与「平时习惯」的对比，所以要用 **are going** 才能凸显「反常」。
  3. **三单问句动词忘还原：** *What does she usually **drinks**?* ✗ → *What does she usually **drink**?* ✓ —— does 已抢走 -s，动词必须原形。
  4. **进行时漏了 be 动词：** *She drinking coffee.* ✗ → *She **is** drinking coffee.* ✓ —— 现在进行时 = be + -ing，缺 be 就是残缺句。
  5. **时间状语插在主谓之间：** *They every day go to school by car.* ✗ → *They go to school by car **every day**.* ✓（或 *Every day they go to school by car.* ✓）
</Callout>

## 6. Written Exercise 书面练习

<Callout type="info" title="A. 完成下列句子 Complete these sentences using is/are + -ing.">
  在横线上填入现在进行时的正确形式（am/is/are + 动词-ing）。回忆规则：**现在进行时 = be (am/is/are) + 动词-ing**。

  **例**：He usually shaves at 7.00 o'clock, but today, he **is shaving** at 8.00.

  1. She usually drinks tea in the morning, but this morning, she **__________** coffee. (drink)
  2. They usually play in the garden in the afternoon, but this afternoon, they **__________** in the park. (play)
  3. He usually washes the dishes at night, but tonight he **__________** clothes. (wash)
</Callout>

<Callout type="tip" title="A 部分答案（自己写完再看）">
  1. She usually drinks tea in the morning, but this morning, she **is drinking** coffee.
  2. They usually play in the garden in the afternoon, but this afternoon, they **are playing** in the park.
  3. He usually washes the dishes at night, but tonight he **is washing** clothes.
</Callout>

<Callout type="info" title="B. 模仿例句写出问句和答句 Write questions and answers following the pattern in the example.">
  每组给你两个时间点——一个是「平时（every day / morning / afternoon / evening / night）」的提示词（主语 + 时间 + 动词 + 地点/宾语），另一个是「今天（today / this morning / tonight）」的提示词（时间 + 动词 + 地点/宾语）。

  格式：
  - 用 **What do/does ... usually do ...?** 问平时（答句用一般现在时）
  - 用 **What is/are ... doing ...?** 问今天（答句用现在进行时）

  **例**：they / every day go / to school by car ｜ today go / to school on foot
  → **What do they usually do every day?** They usually go to school by car every day.
  → **What are they doing today?** They are going to school on foot today.

  1. she / morning drink / tea ｜ morning drink / coffee
  2. they / afternoon play / in the garden ｜ afternoon swim / in the river
  3. I / evening cook / a meal ｜ evening read / a book
  4. we / night watch / television ｜ tonight listen to / the stereo
</Callout>

<Callout type="tip" title="B 部分答案（自己写完再看）">
  **1.** What does she usually do in the morning? She usually drinks tea in the morning.
  What is she doing this morning? She is drinking coffee this morning.

  **2.** What do they usually do in the afternoon? They usually play in the garden in the afternoon.
  What are they doing this afternoon? They are swimming in the river this afternoon.

  **3.** What do you usually do in the evening? I usually cook a meal in the evening.
  What are you doing this evening? I am reading a book this evening.

  **4.** What do you usually do at night? We usually watch television at night.
  What are you doing tonight? We are listening to the stereo tonight.
</Callout>

## 8. 拓展练习 Going further

<Callout type="tip" title="自学者建议">
  - **影子跟读 (shadowing)**：用第 1 节的三个 SayIt 按钮各跟读 3-5 遍。重点练两个层次——① 前半句「习惯」的**平铺节奏**（*They usually go to school by car every day*——一口气顺下来，不拖不抢）；② 后半句「转折 + 反常」的**but 停顿 + 降调重音**（*BUT today, they are going to school on FOOT*——but 后微顿，on foot 把脚踩实）。
  - **复述 (retell)**：合上课本，看着这 5 组时间表（8:00 → 10:00 → 4:00 → 6:00 → 9:00），用 1 分钟把索耶一家人「反常的一天」讲一遍。**三步走**：几点钟 → 平时谁做什么 → 今天/此刻谁在做（没在做）什么。每讲完一个人，检查动词——三单（he/she）→ -s/-es，复数（they/we）→ 原形；进行时 → be + -ing 搭齐。
  - **造句迁移**：用今天学的 5 个结构造你自己的句子——
    1. *主语 + usually + 一般现在时 ... but today, 主语 + 现在进行时 ...* — *I usually drink tea in the morning, but today, I am drinking coffee.*
    2. *主语 + usually + 动词三单 + at + 时间 ... but this morning/afternoon/evening, 主语 + 现在进行时 ...* — *My father usually reads the newspaper at night, but tonight, he is reading a novel.*
    3. *At the moment, 主语 + am/is/are + 动词-ing ...* — *At the moment, my sister is playing the piano.*
    4. *主语 + be + not + 动词-ing ...*（否定进行时）— *They are not watching TV. They are doing their homework.*
    5. *What do/does ... usually do ...? What is/are ... doing ...?* — *What does your mother usually do on Sundays? What is she doing today?*
  - **时态标签系统**：以后写句子前，先问自己——这句话说的是「习惯」还是「此刻」？**习惯** → 打上「usually / every day」标签 → 用一般现在时。**此刻** → 打上「today / now / at the moment」标签 → 用现在进行时。这个「标签法」能帮你彻底告别时态混用。
  - **时间表达总复习**：本课把所有时间表达串了一遍——整点用 **... o'clock**（*eight o'clock*），时间段用 **in the morning / in the afternoon / in the evening**，夜里用 **at night**。试着从早到晚列一份你真实的「一日作息」，每个时间点配上一个动作，再为每个动作写出「平时」和「今天」两个版本。你就把整课的句型框架变成了自己的生活英语。
  - **上连 Lesson 57**：回到《很不平常的一天》的正文，圈出每一组对比句里的 **时态信号词**（usually / every day / today / this morning / this afternoon / this evening / tonight / at the moment）和 **动词时态形式**（一般现在 / 现在进行）。你会发现 Lesson 57 里的 5 段正文，正好是 Lesson 58 里 10 幅操练图的文字版——一个是用来读的短文，一个是用来练的句型。
</Callout>
